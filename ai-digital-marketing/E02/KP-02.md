## 1. E-E-A-T — The Foundation of AI-Citable Content

### 1.1 What is E-E-A-T?

**E-E-A-T** stands for **Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness**. It is a framework originally developed by Google to evaluate the quality of web content for its human quality raters, and it has become the de facto standard for assessing whether content will be trusted and cited by AI systems.

The four components are defined as follows:

| Component | Definition | Example Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Experience (Pengalaman)** | The content creator has first-hand, real-world experience with the topic. | A café owner writing a guide to "how to source halal-certified ingredients in JB" — drawing on their personal supplier relationships |
| **Expertise (Kepakaran)** | The content creator has formal or demonstrated knowledge in the subject area. | A content piece signed by a certified digital marketing practitioner with credentials listed |
| **Authoritativeness (Autoriti)** | The website or author is recognised as an authority on the topic by other credible sources. | Other websites link to your article; industry bodies cite your work; your Google Business Profile is verified |
| **Trustworthiness (Kebolehpercayaan)** | The content is accurate, transparent, and honest. The website has clear contact information, privacy policy, and is not deceptive. | Website has an HTTPS certificate; contact details are consistent across all platforms; claims are supported by cited sources |

> **NOTA PENTING:** E-E-A-T is not a direct ranking algorithm — it cannot be "hacked" by inserting the words "Expert says…" into your content. It is a holistic assessment of your entire digital presence. AI systems infer E-E-A-T from structural signals, third-party references, and content quality.

### 1.2 Why E-E-A-T Matters for AI Systems

When an AI search engine such as Google AI Overviews or Perplexity AI selects which sources to cite in a generated answer, it is essentially performing an automated E-E-A-T assessment. It asks:

- **Can I verify this claim from multiple sources?** (Trustworthiness)
- **Is this source recognised as authoritative on this topic?** (Authoritativeness)
- **Does this content reflect real experience or is it generic?** (Experience)
- **Is the author or organisation credible in this domain?** (Expertise)

Content that scores highly on all four dimensions is significantly more likely to be cited.

### 1.3 Applying E-E-A-T to Malaysian SME Content

Most Malaysian SME websites currently fail on multiple E-E-A-T dimensions. The table below maps common failures to specific remediation actions:

| E-E-A-T Failure (Kegagalan) | Common Cause | Remediation Action |
|---|---|---|
| No author or business credentials listed | Content published without authorship attribution | Add an "About Us" page with the owner's name, years of experience, and relevant credentials (e.g., "Certified by JAKIM for halal handling") |
| Generic content with no specific facts or examples | Content copied from templates or AI-generated without personalisation | Rewrite with specific local details: prices in RM, specific JB neighbourhoods, named local suppliers |
| Contact information inconsistent across platforms | Business listed differently on Google Maps, Facebook, and website | Conduct a NAP audit (Name, Address, Phone) and standardise across all platforms |
| No third-party mentions or backlinks | Business has not engaged with bloggers, journalists, or directories | Submit to Malaysia directory sites (e.g., Laman Web SME Corp, Tourism Johor directory); engage a local food blogger for a review |
| Website lacks HTTPS | Hosting plan does not include SSL certificate | Upgrade hosting to include SSL, or install Let's Encrypt free SSL certificate |
| Claims are unsubstantiated | Content says "best café in JB" without evidence | Replace superlatives with verifiable facts: "Rated 4.8/5 on Google with 240 reviews (as of June 2026)" |

## 2. Content Formats That AI Systems Prefer

### 2.1 The Definition-First Paragraph (Perenggan Takrif-Dahulu)

AI search engines are trained to extract the most direct and complete answer to a user's question. Research from Princeton University's GEO study (2023) found that content structured with a **clear definition or direct answer in the first sentence** is significantly more likely to be selected and cited than content that buries the key information.

**Definition-first writing principle:** When you write about any topic, open with a concise, complete definition or answer — then expand with supporting detail.

**Example — Before (AI-unfriendly format):**

> *"Many people often wonder about the differences between various types of coffee beverages. Coffee culture in Malaysia has grown tremendously over the past decade. At Makan Moments Cafe in JB, we pride ourselves on serving a wide range of beverages…"*

This paragraph does not give the AI a clear, extractable answer to any specific question. The key information is buried after generic scene-setting.

**Example — After (definition-first, AI-friendly format):**

> *"Makan Moments Cafe is a Malaysian-owned café located in Johor Bahru, Johor, specialising in local Malaysian cuisine and artisan coffee. The café serves traditional dishes including nasi lemak, roti canai, and kaya toast alongside espresso-based beverages and local favourites such as teh tarik and cendol. It is halal-certified and operates daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM."*

This paragraph gives an AI system everything it needs to cite Makan Moments Cafe in response to questions such as: "What does Makan Moments Cafe serve?", "Is Makan Moments Cafe halal?", "What are the operating hours of Makan Moments Cafe?"

### 2.2 FAQ Format (Format Soalan Lazim)

The **FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions / Soalan Lazim)** format is the single most effective content structure for AI extraction. This is because:

- FAQs mirror the conversational question format that users type into AI search tools
- Each FAQ question-answer pair is self-contained and extractable as a unit
- FAQ schema markup (covered in Section 3) signals to search engines that this is structured Q&A content
- AI systems are trained on Q&A data formats and process them with high comprehension

**Writing effective FAQ content for AI search:**

1. **Write questions as users would ask them** — not as marketing copy
   - Poor: "What makes our café special?" (self-promotional, not how users search)
   - Good: "Is Makan Moments Cafe in Johor Bahru halal-certified?" (matches real user query)

2. **Write answers that are complete within 2–4 sentences** — AI systems prefer concise, complete answers they can extract intact

3. **Include location, category, and factual detail** in every answer — these are the signals AI uses to associate your content with specific queries

4. **Target the exact questions your customers ask** — review your Google Business Profile questions, WhatsApp enquiries, and social media comments for the real questions people ask

**Sample FAQ for Southern Homestay, KSL D'Esplanade JB:**

| Question | AI-Optimised Answer |
|---|---|
| Where is Southern Homestay located? | Southern Homestay is located at KSL D'Esplanade, Jalan Seladang, Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia — directly within the KSL D'Esplanade residential and commercial complex, approximately 500 metres from KSL City Mall. |
| How many guests can Southern Homestay accommodate? | Southern Homestay accommodates up to 6 guests in a fully furnished serviced apartment with 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a fully equipped kitchen, and a living room. It is suitable for families, group travellers, and corporate stays. |
| Is Southern Homestay suitable for Singapore travellers? | Yes. Southern Homestay is located approximately 15 minutes by car from the Johor-Singapore Causeway (Tambak Johor) and 20 minutes from the Second Link (CIQ Tuas). Free parking is available for Singapore-registered vehicles. |
| What amenities does Southern Homestay provide? | Southern Homestay provides WiFi, air-conditioning in all rooms, a washing machine, full kitchen equipment, Smart TV, and daily housekeeping on request. Towels and toiletries are included. |
| How do I book Southern Homestay? | Southern Homestay can be booked via Airbnb, Booking.com, or directly via WhatsApp at +60 XX-XXXX XXXX. Direct booking rates are available. Check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. |

### 2.3 Cited Statistics and Verifiable Claims

AI systems are trained to prefer content that includes **specific, verifiable facts** over generic claims. When your content cites statistics, names sources, and uses precise figures, AI systems treat it as more authoritative and are more likely to extract it.

**Principles for using cited statistics in AI-optimised content:**

- **Always cite the source and year:** "According to MCMC's Internet Users Survey 2025, 92% of Malaysian internet users access the internet via mobile devices."
- **Use precise figures, not approximations:** "4.8 out of 5 stars from 312 Google reviews" is more citable than "highly rated by customers"
- **Include the date of measurement:** AI systems deprioritise claims without temporal context
- **Use government and recognised institutional sources where possible:** MCMC, DOSM, Tourism Malaysia, Bank Negara Malaysia — these are high-authority sources that AI systems trust

**Example application for Makan Moments Cafe:**

Instead of writing: *"Our café is very popular among locals and tourists."*

Write: *"Makan Moments Cafe has received 4.7 stars from 185 Google reviews as of July 2026, with reviewers frequently mentioning the nasi lemak and local kopi. The café is listed in the Johor Tourism Board's recommended F&B directory."*

The second version gives an AI system four citable data points: the rating, the review count, the date, and the directory listing.

## 3. Technical Optimisation Signals

### 3.1 What is Schema Markup / Structured Data?

**Schema markup (penandaan skema)** — also called **structured data (data berstruktur)** — is a standardised way of adding machine-readable labels to the content on your website. It tells search engines and AI systems: "This piece of text is the business name. This other piece is the address. This is the opening hour for Monday."

Without schema markup, AI systems must infer what your content means from context. With schema markup, you explicitly tell them — removing ambiguity and increasing the probability of accurate citation.

Schema markup is defined by Schema.org — an open vocabulary maintained by Google, Microsoft, Apple, and Yandex. It is implemented on websites using **JSON-LD (JavaScript Object Notation for Linked Data)**, which is the format recommended by Google and most accessible for beginners.

### 3.2 JSON-LD Organisation Schema — Step-by-Step

The **Organisation schema** tells AI systems and search engines the fundamental facts about a business: its name, type, address, contact details, and web presence. It is the most important schema for any Malaysian SME to implement first.

**How to implement JSON-LD Organisation schema:**

JSON-LD is added to your website's HTML inside a `<script>` tag in the `<head>` section of the page. Below is a complete, annotated example for Makan Moments Cafe:

```json
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "CafeOrCoffeeShop",
  "name": "Makan Moments Cafe",
  "alternateName": "Makan Moments",
  "url": "https://www.makanmoments.com.my",
  "telephone": "+60-XX-XXXX-XXXX",
  "email": "hello@makanmoments.com.my",
  "description": "A Malaysian-owned café in Johor Bahru serving authentic local cuisine including nasi lemak, roti canai, and kaya toast alongside artisan coffee and traditional beverages. Halal-certified.",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "[Street Address]",
    "addressLocality": "Johor Bahru",
    "addressRegion": "Johor",
    "postalCode": "[Postcode]",
    "addressCountry": "MY"
  },
  "geo": {
    "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
    "latitude": "[Latitude]",
    "longitude": "[Longitude]"
  },
  "openingHoursSpecification": [
    {
      "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification",
      "dayOfWeek": ["Monday","Tuesday","Wednesday","Thursday","Friday","Saturday","Sunday"],
      "opens": "08:00",
      "closes": "22:00"
    }
  ],
  "servesCuisine": ["Malaysian", "Local", "Halal"],
  "priceRange": "RM",
  "hasMap": "https://maps.google.com/?q=Makan+Moments+Cafe+JB",
  "sameAs": [
    "https://www.facebook.com/makanmoments",
    "https://www.instagram.com/makanmoments",
    "https://g.co/kgs/[GoogleBusinessID]"
  ]
}
</script>
```

**Key fields explained:**

| Field | Purpose | AI Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| `@type` | Tells the AI what kind of entity this is (CafeOrCoffeeShop, LodgingBusiness, LocalBusiness, etc.) | Enables category-specific queries: "best café in JB" |
| `name` | Official business name — must be identical across all platforms | NAP consistency check by AI systems |
| `description` | Plain-language description of the business | Direct source for AI-generated business summaries |
| `address` | Full structured address with PostalAddress sub-type | Powers local search and location-based AI queries |
| `openingHoursSpecification` | Machine-readable opening hours | AI can answer "Is Makan Moments Cafe open on Sunday?" accurately |
| `servesCuisine` | Array of cuisine types | Powers query: "Malaysian cafés in JB that serve nasi lemak" |
| `sameAs` | Links to your social media profiles and Google Business entry | Cross-platform identity verification for AI |

### 3.3 FAQ Schema — JSON-LD Implementation

The **FAQ schema** marks up your FAQ content so that search engines can display it as expandable rich results in traditional search, and so AI systems know to treat these question-answer pairs as structured, extractable data.

**JSON-LD FAQ schema example for Southern Homestay:**

```json
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "Where is Southern Homestay located in Johor Bahru?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Southern Homestay is located at KSL D'Esplanade, Jalan Seladang, Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia. It is within walking distance of KSL City Mall and approximately 15 minutes by car from the Johor-Singapore Causeway."
      }
    },
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "How many guests can stay at Southern Homestay?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Southern Homestay accommodates up to 6 guests. The unit is a fully furnished serviced apartment with 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a living room, and a fully equipped kitchen."
      }
    },
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "Is free parking available at Southern Homestay?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Yes. Complimentary parking is available within the KSL D'Esplanade compound. The parking facility is covered and accessible to guests throughout their stay."
      }
    }
  ]
}
</script>
```

**Rules for FAQ schema:**

1. Each question must have exactly one accepted answer
2. Questions must reflect what real users genuinely ask — do not fabricate questions for SEO purposes
3. The schema must match the visible FAQ content on the page exactly — if the schema says one thing and the visible page says another, search engines will flag this as structured data spam
4. Limit to 10–20 FAQ items per page for best results

### 3.5 What is llms.txt?

**llms.txt** is a plain-text file placed at the root of a website (e.g., `www.yourwebsite.com/llms.txt`) that provides AI language model crawlers with a structured, human-readable guide to the most important content on the website. It is analogous to `robots.txt` (which guides traditional search crawlers) but is specifically designed for LLM-based AI systems.

The llms.txt format was proposed in 2024 by Jeremy Howard (fast.ai) and has been adopted by a growing number of organisations as AI crawlers (including GPTBot from OpenAI, ClaudeBot from Anthropic, and PerplexityBot) have become mainstream.

**Why llms.txt matters:**

- AI crawlers typically have limited context windows — they cannot read an entire website in one pass
- llms.txt helps the AI crawler prioritise which pages contain the most authoritative and complete information
- It prevents AI systems from over-indexing thin or irrelevant pages (e.g., shopping cart pages, account login pages)
- It signals to AI systems which pages contain FAQs, product/service descriptions, team credentials, and other high-value content

### 3.6 Writing an llms.txt File

The llms.txt format is simple and human-readable. It begins with a brief description of the website and organisation, followed by markdown links to the most important pages, organised by section.

**llms.txt example for Makan Moments Cafe:**

```markdown
# Makan Moments Cafe

> Makan Moments Cafe is a halal-certified Malaysian café in Johor Bahru serving local cuisine (nasi lemak, roti canai, kaya toast) and artisan coffee. Established in 2019. Located at [Address], Johor Bahru.

## About

- [About Makan Moments Cafe](https://www.makanmoments.com.my/about): History, ownership, mission, halal certification details
- [Our Team](https://www.makanmoments.com.my/team): Owner profile and head chef credentials

## Menu and Offerings

- [Full Menu](https://www.makanmoments.com.my/menu): Complete food and beverage menu with prices in RM
- [Halal Certification](https://www.makanmoments.com.my/halal): JAKIM halal certificate details and renewal dates

## Location and Hours

- [Find Us](https://www.makanmoments.com.my/location): Address, map, parking information, public transport directions
- [Opening Hours](https://www.makanmoments.com.my/hours): Daily operating hours including public holiday schedule

## Customer Information

- [FAQ](https://www.makanmoments.com.my/faq): Answers to common customer questions
- [Reviews](https://www.makanmoments.com.my/reviews): Curated customer testimonials and Google review summary

## Contact

- [Contact Us](https://www.makanmoments.com.my/contact): Phone, WhatsApp, email, and social media links
```

**llms.txt rules and best practices:**

| Rule | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Place the file at the root domain: `/llms.txt` | AI crawlers look for it at this standard location |
| Write the introductory description in 2–3 sentences, factual and specific | This is the single most important section — AI systems read this first |
| Link only to pages with substantive content | Linking to thin pages wastes the AI's context and reduces your authority signal |
| Use descriptive link text that explains what the linked page contains | AI systems use the link text to decide which pages to prioritise |
| Keep the file under 2,000 words | AI context windows are finite; a focused llms.txt is more effective than an exhaustive one |
| Update the file when you add major new pages | Outdated llms.txt files may cause AI systems to miss your best content |

## 4. Practical Content Audit Workflow

### 4.1 The GEO Content Audit Framework

Before implementing any GEO improvements, a systematic content audit is required. The following five-step audit framework can be applied to any Malaysian SME's digital presence:

**Step 1 — Inventory (Inventori)**
List all public-facing digital content assets: website pages, Google Business Profile, Airbnb/Booking.com listings, Facebook About section, Instagram bio. Record the URL, content length, and last update date for each.

**Step 2 — NAP Consistency Check (Semakan Konsistensi NAP)**
Verify that the business Name, Address, and Phone number are identical across all platforms. Any discrepancy reduces AI confidence in the business's identity.

**Step 3 — E-E-A-T Assessment (Penilaian E-E-A-T)**
For each major content asset, rate it on a simple 1–3 scale for each E-E-A-T dimension:
- 1 = No signal present
- 2 = Partial signal
- 3 = Strong signal

**Step 4 — Content Format Assessment (Penilaian Format Kandungan)**
Check whether the website has: (a) a definition-first About page, (b) a FAQ section, (c) cited statistics with sources, (d) specific local details (address, operating hours, prices in RM).

**Step 5 — Technical Signal Assessment (Penilaian Isyarat Teknikal)**
Check whether the website has: (a) HTTPS, (b) Organisation schema, (c) FAQ schema (if FAQ page exists), (d) llms.txt file.

### 4.2 Prioritising GEO Improvements

Not all improvements have equal impact. The following priority matrix guides Malaysian SMEs on where to focus their effort:

| Priority | Action | Estimated Time | Expected Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Highest** | Write/rewrite the About page using definition-first format with specific facts | 2–4 hours | High — About pages are the most frequently extracted by AI |
| **Highest** | Create a FAQ page with 10–15 real customer questions, answered in 2–4 sentences each | 4–6 hours | High — FAQ format directly matches AI query-answer extraction |
| **High** | Implement Organisation JSON-LD schema | 1–2 hours | High — machine-readable identity signals significantly improve citation accuracy |
| **High** | Standardise NAP across all platforms | 2–3 hours | High — inconsistency is a major reason AI systems avoid citing a business |
| **Medium** | Add FAQ JSON-LD schema to FAQ page | 1 hour | Medium — amplifies the FAQ page but requires the page to exist first |
| **Medium** | Create llms.txt file | 30 minutes | Medium — growing importance as AI crawlers become more sophisticated |
| **Lower** | Seek a mention/review on a credible third-party website | Ongoing | Medium-High — third-party authority signals require external relationships |
