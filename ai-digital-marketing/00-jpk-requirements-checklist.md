## How to Use This Checklist

JPK evaluators score each item using the COPTPA rubric:

| Score | Malay Description | Meaning | Pass? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | Tiada | Not present; does not exist | FAIL |
| **1** | Ada, dilaksanakan sebahagian (<50%) | Exists but less than half implemented | FAIL |
| **2** | Ada, dilaksanakan sebahagian (50–90%) | Exists and mostly implemented | BORDERLINE — aim higher |
| **3** | Ada dan dilaksanakan sepenuhnya (>90%) | Fully present and fully implemented | PASS |

**Target:** Score **3** on all items. Score **2** is the minimum acceptable. Any **0 or 1** risks failing accreditation.
