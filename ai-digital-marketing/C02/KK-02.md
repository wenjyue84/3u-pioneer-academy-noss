1. Use AI to generate a series of social media captions for a single campaign across multiple platforms.
2. Adapt brand voice and tone appropriately for each platform's audience and content culture.
3. Produce multilingual caption variants (English and Bahasa Malaysia minimum) using AI assistance.
4. Evaluate and edit AI-generated captions for cultural appropriateness and brand alignment.

---

## Work Activity Title

Create a 5-Post Social Media Caption Series for a Malaysian Brand Campaign Using AI

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:
- Produced 5 campaign captions adapted for 2 platforms (10 total captions)
- Applied platform-specific formatting (hooks, hashtags, length, CTA)
- Created multilingual variants for at least 2 captions (BM and English)
- Evaluated all captions against a brand voice and cultural sensitivity checklist

## Safety Precautions

- All captions must be reviewed for cultural and religious sensitivity before submission
- Do not include competitor brand names in captions without explicit approval
- Captions making product claims (e.g., "best in Malaysia") must be verifiable — remove superlatives if not substantiated
- WhatsApp broadcast content may only be sent to opted-in contacts (PDPA compliance)

## Campaign Brief

Use the following campaign brief for this activity, OR your trainer may provide an alternative:

---

**Client:** Bloom Naturals — a halal-certified, plant-based skincare brand based in Shah Alam, Selangor

**New Product Launch:** "Hydra Glow Serum" — a lightweight, fragrance-free facial serum suitable for all skin types

**Key Messages:**
- 100% plant-based and halal certified
- Suitable for sensitive skin, including users with hijab (reduces under-mask skin issues)
- Affordable: RM 89 / 30ml (free shipping on Shopee for orders above RM100)
- Available: Shopee, official website, and selected pharmacies

**Campaign Theme:** "Your skin, naturally glowing" (BM: "Kulit anda, berseri secara semula jadi")

**Target Audience:** Muslim women aged 22–38, Malaysia, conscious about halal beauty and ingredient transparency

**Platforms:** Instagram and TikTok (primary); Facebook (secondary)

**Campaign Duration:** 2 weeks (Posts: Monday, Wednesday, Friday across 2 weeks = 6 posts; you will produce 5 of these)

**Brand Voice:** Warm, trustworthy, knowledgeable but not clinical; uses inclusive BM/English mix; celebrates Malaysian beauty culture

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Define the 5 Post Topics (5 minutes)

Before generating captions, decide the topic/theme for each of the 5 posts. The campaign needs variety — not all posts should be direct product promotion.

Use these campaign content pillars as guidance:

| Post # | Recommended Pillar | Suggested Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Post 1 | Education | What makes a serum "truly" halal? Ingredient transparency |
| Post 2 | Promotion | Product launch announcement — key benefits + buy link |
| Post 3 | Testimonial / Social Proof | Before/after skin story (fictional for exercise purposes) |
| Post 4 | Community / Relatable | Under-mask skin struggles — connecting with hijab-wearing audience |
| Post 5 | Engagement | Interactive post — poll or question about skincare habits |

Trainer note: students may modify post topics with trainer approval.

---

### Step 2: Generate Instagram Captions (20 minutes)

Enter the following prompt into ChatGPT or Claude for **each post** (modify for each topic):

```
You are writing social media captions for Bloom Naturals, a halal plant-based skincare brand in Malaysia.

Brand voice: warm, trustworthy, inclusive; uses a natural mix of BM and English phrases; celebrates Malaysian Muslim women.

Product: Hydra Glow Serum — plant-based, halal-certified, RM89, fragrance-free, for sensitive skin.

Write an Instagram caption for Post [number]: [post topic/theme]

Requirements:
- First line: a scroll-stopping hook (under 15 words)
- Body: 3–5 lines of supporting content relevant to topic
- CTA: specific action (shop link in bio / comment / tag a friend / answer the question)
- Hashtags: 8–12 hashtags — mix of BM, English, and niche skincare hashtags relevant to Malaysia
- Total caption length: 150–250 words
```

Generate one caption per post. Record all 5 in your Answer Sheet under **Section A: Instagram Captions**.

---

### Step 3: Adapt for TikTok (15 minutes)

TikTok captions are shorter — the video carries the main content. Adapt each Instagram caption into a TikTok-optimised version.

Enter this follow-up prompt for each post:
```
Now adapt this Instagram caption for TikTok. 
Requirements:
- Keep only the hook (first line) and the CTA
- Add a 3-word "sticker text" that would overlay on the video thumbnail
- Suggest one trending sound category appropriate for this post (e.g., upbeat pop, emotional ballad, voiceover-style)
- Maximum 3 hashtags for TikTok (more hurts reach on this platform)
- Total length: under 100 characters for the caption itself
```

Record all 5 TikTok adaptations in **Section B: TikTok Captions**.

---

### Step 4: Multilingual Variants (15 minutes)

For **Post 1 (Education)** and **Post 4 (Community/Relatable)**, create Bahasa Malaysia versions.

Enter:
```
Translate and culturally adapt this Instagram caption into natural Bahasa Malaysia.
- Do not translate word-for-word — make it sound like something a Malaysian brand would actually post
- Keep the brand voice: warm, inclusive, Malaysian
- Adapt the CTA to BM ("Klik link di bio" / "Tag kawan kamu" etc.)
- Replace English hashtags with BM equivalents where they exist
- Keep product name "Hydra Glow Serum" in English (it's the brand name)
```

Record BM variants in **Section C: Bahasa Malaysia Captions**.

---

### Step 5: Cultural Sensitivity and Brand Voice Review (15 minutes)

Before finalising, review all 10 captions (5 Instagram + 5 TikTok) using this checklist:

**Cultural Sensitivity Checklist:**

| Check | All Pass? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| No food references that may be non-halal for a halal brand | ☐ | |
| Festival references (if any) are accurate and appropriate | ☐ | |
| No stereotyping of any ethnic or religious group | ☐ | |
| Inclusive language — captions welcome all skin types and backgrounds | ☐ | |
| Price in RM (not USD or other currency) | ☐ | |
| No unsubstantiated superlatives ("best in Malaysia", "#1 serum") | ☐ | |

**Brand Voice Checklist:**

| Check | All Pass? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tone is warm, not clinical or cold | ☐ | |
| Language feels natural — not obviously AI-generated | ☐ | |
| BM/English mix is balanced and natural (not forced) | ☐ | |
| Each post has a distinct hook — no two hooks sound the same | ☐ | |
| Every post has a clear, specific CTA | ☐ | |

For any item that does not pass, revise the caption before final submission.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook Quality** | Every hook is compelling, specific, and platform-appropriate | Most hooks are effective | Hooks are present but generic | Weak or missing hooks |
| **Platform Adaptation** | Instagram and TikTok captions are distinctly different; format correct | Some platform differences visible | Captions are similar across platforms | Same caption copied to both platforms |
| **Brand Voice Consistency** | All captions sound like Bloom Naturals — warm, trustworthy, inclusive | Most captions on-brand | Some off-brand captions | Brand voice inconsistent or absent |
| **BM Quality** | BM captions are natural, culturally appropriate, not a literal translation | Mostly natural; minor awkward phrases | Reads like a translation | Clearly machine-translated |
| **Checklists** | Both checklists complete; all flagged items revised | Most items checked; minor issues | Checklists partially completed | Not completed |

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. Which of the 5 posts do you think would perform best on Instagram, and why? Which would perform best on TikTok?
2. Compare your English and BM Instagram captions for Post 4. What specific changes did you make beyond direct translation, and why?
3. The brand voice for Bloom Naturals is "warm and trustworthy." Give one example from your captions where you felt the AI got this right, and one example where you had to rewrite to achieve the right tone.
4. You notice a competitor brand using the exact same hashtags as your campaign. What would you do to differentiate your content's discoverability?
