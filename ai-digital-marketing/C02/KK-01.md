1. Use AI tools to research a keyword and generate a complete SEO blog article outline and draft.
2. Edit AI-generated blog content to meet readability, accuracy, and brand standards.
3. Apply on-page SEO elements including H1/H2 structure, meta description, and keyword placement.

---

## Work Activity Title

Write a Publication-Ready SEO Blog Post Using AI Tools for a Malaysian Business

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:
- Selected a target keyword with clear search intent
- Used an AI tool to generate a full blog article outline and draft
- Edited the AI draft for accuracy, local relevance, and brand voice
- Produced a final article with proper H1/H2 structure, meta description, and internal link suggestions

---

## Tools & Materials Required

| Item | Purpose | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Computer / laptop with internet | Primary workspace | Lab / personal device |
| ChatGPT or Claude | Article outline and draft generation | chat.openai.com / claude.ai |
| Google Trends | Keyword validation | trends.google.com |
| AnswerThePublic | Question keyword research | answerthepublic.com |
| Microsoft Word / Google Docs | Final article editing and formatting | Lab or personal |
| Answer Sheet (provided) | Record keyword research steps | Printed / digital |

---

## Safety Precautions

- Do not publish any AI-generated article without human editing and fact-checking
- Do not use statistics from the AI draft without verifying against a credible source
- Ensure all content is appropriate for the Malaysian market (cultural and religious sensitivity)
- Respect copyright — do not reproduce third-party content even via AI paraphrase

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Choose Your Business Scenario and Topic (10 minutes)

Your trainer will assign a business scenario, or you may use one from this list:

| Option | Business | Suggested Blog Topic Area |
|---|---|---|
| A | F&B / Café | How to Market a Café on Social Media |
| B | Online Fashion | How to Build an Online Fashion Brand in Malaysia |
| C | Tuition Centre | How to Choose the Right Tutor for SPM Students |
| D | Fitness Studio | Beginner's Guide to Starting a Fitness Routine in Malaysia |
| E | AI services | How AI is Changing Digital Marketing for Malaysian SMEs |

My business scenario: _______________________________
My chosen topic area: _______________________________

---

### Step 2: Keyword Research (15 minutes)

**2a. Generate keyword ideas with AI**

Enter this prompt into ChatGPT:
```
I am writing a blog post for a [business type] in Malaysia.
Topic area: [your topic area].
Target audience: [describe audience].

Suggest:
1. One primary long-tail keyword (3–5 words) that Malaysian users are likely to search
2. Three related secondary keywords to use naturally in the article
3. The likely search intent (informational / commercial / navigational)
4. Estimated competition level (low / medium / high) for a new Malaysian website
```

Record your chosen **primary keyword**: _______________________________
**Search intent**: _______________________________

**2b. Validate on Google Trends**
- Go to trends.google.com
- Search your primary keyword
- Change region to **Malaysia**
- Note the 12-month trend: Rising / Stable / Declining? _______________________________

**2c. Check AnswerThePublic**
- Go to answerthepublic.com
- Enter your primary keyword
- List **3 question-based keywords** you could address in the article:
  1. _______________________________
  2. _______________________________
  3. _______________________________

---

### Step 3: Generate Article Outline (10 minutes)

Enter the following prompt into ChatGPT or Claude:
```
Create a detailed SEO blog article outline for the following:

Primary keyword: [your keyword]
Business: [your business type]
Target audience: [audience description] in Malaysia
Search intent: Informational
Word count: 800 words

Provide:
- One H1 title containing the keyword
- An alternative H1 title (for A/B testing)
- 5 H2 section headings
- 3 bullet point sub-topics under each H2
- One suggested CTA for the end of the article
- A 155-character meta description
```

Record your chosen **H1 title**: _______________________________
**Meta description** (verify it is under 158 characters): _______________________________

---

### Step 4: Generate Full Article Draft (10 minutes)

Enter:
```
Using the outline above, write a complete 800-word blog article.

Requirements:
- Include the keyword "[your keyword]" in the H1, within the first 100 words, in at least 2 H2 subheadings, and in the conclusion
- Use a conversational tone appropriate for Malaysian readers
- Include at least one Malaysia-specific example (mention a Malaysian brand, city, or cultural context)
- End with a clear call to action
- Do not include placeholder text — write complete sentences throughout
```

Copy the AI draft into your Google Docs / Word document.

---

### Step 5: Human Editing Round (25 minutes)

Work through the AI draft systematically using the editing checklist below. For each item, make the actual edits in your document.

**Editing Checklist:**

| # | Check | Done? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fact-check: verify every statistic or data point | ☐ | |
| 2 | Add at least one local Malaysian example not generated by AI | ☐ | |
| 3 | Remove or rewrite any sentence that sounds robotic or overly formal | ☐ | |
| 4 | Shorten any sentence over 30 words | ☐ | |
| 5 | Confirm keyword appears in H1, first paragraph, and conclusion | ☐ | |
| 6 | Add 2 suggested internal links (note: "link to [page topic] here") | ☐ | |
| 7 | Rewrite the CTA to sound more specific and compelling | ☐ | |
| 8 | Ensure article has a clear introduction with a hook sentence | ☐ | |
| 9 | Check for cultural sensitivity (food, religion, community references) | ☐ | |
| 10 | Word count is between 700 and 900 words | ☐ | |

---

### Step 6: Format and Finalise (10 minutes)

In your document, format the article as follows:

1. **Title (H1):** Bold, large font
2. **Meta description:** In a text box or highlighted in yellow at the top (for reference)
3. **H2 subheadings:** Bold
4. **Introduction:** No heading; 2–3 paragraphs
5. **Body sections:** Each starts with an H2 heading
6. **Conclusion:** H2 heading ("Conclusion" or "Summary")
7. **CTA:** Final paragraph, bold the key action

Save as: `[YourName]-BlogPost-[Keyword]-[Date].docx`

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Keyword Usage** | Keyword in H1, intro, 2+ H2s, conclusion; natural placement | Keyword in H1 and intro; 1 H2 | Keyword in H1 only | Keyword missing or forced |
| **Content Quality** | Well-edited; no AI artifacts; local examples; engaging | Mostly edited; one local example | Partially edited; generic | Raw AI output with minimal changes |
| **Accuracy** | All claims verified; sources cited or removed | Most claims checked | Some verification | No fact-checking evident |
| **Structure** | Clear H1/H2 hierarchy; intro-body-conclusion-CTA | H1/H2 present; CTA present | Some structure present | No clear structure |
| **Meta Description** | Under 158 chars; contains keyword; compelling | Under 158 chars; keyword present | Present but too long or generic | Missing or incorrect |

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. Compare the AI-generated draft with your final edited version. What percentage of the content did you change, and what types of changes did you make most often?
2. What was the most significant factual error or questionable claim you found in the AI draft? How did you resolve it?
3. How does adding a Malaysia-specific example improve the article's usefulness to the target reader?
4. If a client asked you to produce 10 blog posts per month using this workflow, how would you structure your time? What would be the bottleneck?
