## Competency Unit: Create AI-Assisted Written Content (DM-001-3:2026-C02)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Duration | 2 hours 30 minutes |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Passing Mark | 60% (60 marks) |
| Work Activities Covered | WA1, WA2, WA3 |

---

## Instructions to Candidates

1. This performance assessment consists of THREE (3) practical tasks
2. Complete ALL tasks within the allocated time
3. All written content must be produced using AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, or equivalent) as a starting point — demonstrate editing and refinement
4. You will be assessed on PROCESS, OUTPUT, ATTITUDE, SAFETY/ETHICS, and ENVIRONMENT
5. Submitting raw, unedited AI output as final work is an ethical violation and results in automatic failure
6. Equipment provided: computer/laptop with internet access and active accounts for ChatGPT or Claude

---

## TASK 1: SEO Blog Post using AI (35 marks)
**Time Allocation: 60 minutes**
**Reference: WA1, WA2 — KK-01**

You are assigned a Malaysian brand and product/service topic (provided by assessor). Produce a complete, publish-ready SEO blog post. You must:

1. Perform keyword research using an AI tool; identify ONE (1) primary keyword and TWO (2) secondary keywords with estimated search intent (5 marks)
2. Prompt the AI to generate a blog post outline (minimum 5 sections); document the prompt and AI output (5 marks)
3. Generate a full blog post draft of 700–900 words using AI — document the generation prompt (8 marks)
4. Edit and humanise the draft: improve flow, add local Malaysian context, remove generic filler phrases — show tracked changes or a before/after excerpt (7 marks)
5. Write a meta title (50–60 characters) and meta description (140–160 characters) optimised for the primary keyword (5 marks)
6. Complete an SEO checklist for the final post: keyword in title, keyword in first paragraph, at least one H2/H3, internal link placeholder, image alt text placeholder (5 marks)

---

## TASK 2: Bilingual Social Media Caption Series (35 marks)
**Time Allocation: 50 minutes**
**Reference: WA3 — KK-02**

Create a series of FIVE (5) social media captions for the assigned Malaysian brand — each caption must be produced in BOTH English and Bahasa Malaysia. You must:

1. Prompt the AI to generate five caption concepts aligned to the brand's tone; document the prompt (5 marks)
2. Produce five caption pairs (EN + BM) covering these content types, one per post: (20 marks)
   - Product/service highlight (4 marks)
   - Customer testimonial or social proof (4 marks)
   - Educational tip related to the product/service (4 marks)
   - Promotional or limited-time offer (4 marks)
   - Brand story or behind-the-scenes (4 marks)
3. Each caption must include: a hook (first line), body (2–3 sentences), call-to-action, and 3–5 relevant hashtags (5 marks)
4. Verify BM captions for grammatical accuracy and appropriate register (formal/informal); note any corrections made to the AI output (5 marks)

---

## TASK 3: Fact-Check, Humanise, and Hallucination Detection (30 marks)
**Time Allocation: 40 minutes**
**Reference: WA1, WA2 — KK-01, KK-02**

The assessor will provide a sample AI-generated marketing article (approximately 400 words) containing deliberate factual errors and hallucinations. You must:

1. Read the provided article and identify ALL factual claims that require verification (5 marks)
2. Verify a minimum of FOUR (4) claims using credible sources (government websites, established Malaysian news outlets, official brand websites); record the source URL and verdict (Accurate / Inaccurate / Unverifiable) for each claim (10 marks)
3. Rewrite the article correcting all identified errors; maintain the original word count (±10%); preserve the marketing tone (10 marks)
4. Write a short hallucination report (100–150 words) summarising: how many errors were found, what types of errors they were (factual fabrication, outdated data, wrong statistics), and what verification method was used (5 marks)
