## Competency Unit: Generate AI Visual & Graphic Content (DM-001-3:2026-C03)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Duration | 2 hours 30 minutes |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Passing Mark | 60% (60 marks) |
| Work Activities Covered | WA1, WA2, WA3 |

---

## Instructions to Candidates

1. This performance assessment consists of THREE (3) practical tasks
2. Complete ALL tasks within the allocated time
3. All AI images must be generated using Lovart AI, Canva AI Image Generator, Adobe Firefly, or equivalent AI image tools — document the tool and prompts used
4. You will be assessed on PROCESS, OUTPUT, ATTITUDE, SAFETY/ETHICS, and ENVIRONMENT
5. Generating images depicting real identifiable persons without consent, or images containing culturally insensitive content, is an ethical violation and results in automatic failure
6. Equipment provided: computer/laptop with internet access; active accounts for Lovart AI or Canva (Pro/Teams) provided by centre

---

## TASK 1: AI Image Generation — Product & Lifestyle Shots (30 marks)
**Time Allocation: 50 minutes**
**Reference: WA1 — KK-01**

You are assigned a Malaysian brand and product category (provided by assessor). Generate SIX (6) AI images — THREE (3) product shots and THREE (3) lifestyle shots. You must:

1. Write structured image prompts for each of the six images; each prompt must include: subject, style, lighting, background, mood, and any cultural/local context (10 marks)
   - Document all six prompts and the corresponding generated image (submit as screenshots or exported files)
2. Generate the three product shots: clean background, product clearly visible, professional quality (6 marks)
3. Generate the three lifestyle shots: show product in use or in a Malaysian lifestyle context (6 marks)
4. Evaluate each image for cultural appropriateness for a Malaysian Muslim-majority audience — note any regenerations required and the reason (5 marks)
5. Select the BEST image from each category (1 product + 1 lifestyle) and write a 50-word justification for each selection (3 marks)

---

## TASK 2: Brand Kit Setup and Visual Identity Application (30 marks)
**Time Allocation: 40 minutes**
**Reference: WA2 — KK-02**

Using Canva or equivalent design tool, set up a Brand Kit for the assigned brand and apply it consistently. You must:

1. Set up a Brand Kit containing: (10 marks)
   - Primary colour (hex code), Secondary colour (hex code), Accent colour (hex code) (3 marks)
   - Primary font (headings) and Secondary font (body text) — must be a matched pair (3 marks)
   - Upload or create a logo placeholder (2 marks)
   - Define brand tone in two sentences (formal/casual, playful/serious, etc.) (2 marks)
2. Apply the Brand Kit to produce TWO (2) branded social media post templates (not the final five posts — these are reusable blank templates): (12 marks)
   - Template 1: Image-dominant post (landscape or square) (6 marks)
   - Template 2: Text-dominant post with branded colour background (6 marks)
3. Export both templates as PNG at 1080×1080 px; verify dimensions and file format before submission (5 marks)
4. Write a brand consistency checklist (minimum 5 items) that will be applied to all five posts in Task 3 (3 marks)

---

## TASK 3: Instagram Feed Series — 5 Posts at Platform Specifications (40 marks)
**Time Allocation: 60 minutes**
**Reference: WA3 — KK-01, KK-02**

Design a cohesive five-post Instagram feed series for the assigned brand using the Brand Kit from Task 2 and AI-generated images from Task 1. You must:

1. Plan the five-post series with a feed layout overview — explain the visual flow and colour cohesion across the five posts (5 marks)
2. Design FIVE (5) Instagram posts, each incorporating: (25 marks)
   - At least one AI-generated image (from Task 1 or newly generated) (2 marks per post × 5 = 10 marks)
   - Brand Kit colours, fonts, and logo applied consistently (2 marks per post × 5 = 10 marks)
   - Post number and content type labelled (e.g., Post 1 — Product Highlight) (1 mark per post × 5 = 5 marks)
3. Export all five posts as PNG files at correct Instagram feed specification: 1080×1080 px (square) or 1080×1350 px (portrait); verify dimensions (5 marks)
4. Present the five posts as a mock feed grid (screenshot or montage) showing how they appear together on an Instagram profile (5 marks)
