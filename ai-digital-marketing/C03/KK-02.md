## Work Activity Title

Design a 5-Post Instagram Feed Series for a Malaysian Brand Using Canva AI

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:
- Produced 5 visually consistent Instagram posts in Canva using AI-assisted features
- Applied brand colours, fonts, and logo across all posts
- Used at least two Canva AI features (Magic Media, Magic Write, or Magic Design)
- Exported all posts at Instagram-correct resolution

## Safety Precautions

- Verify all product claims in post copy before including in designs (no unsubstantiated "best" or "#1" claims)
- Do not use third-party images, logos, or assets without permission — use only AI-generated or provided brand assets
- Ensure designs are culturally appropriate for the Malaysian market
- Save your Canva project regularly (Canva auto-saves, but confirm before closing)

## Brand Brief — Design Reference

**Brand:** FreshBrew — a Malaysian artisan cold brew coffee brand
**Location:** Bangsar, Kuala Lumpur (ships nationwide via Shopee)
**Product:** Premium cold brew coffee concentrate, available in Original, Chocolate Hazelnut, and Coconut Latte flavours
**Price:** RM 45 for 500ml; RM 120 for 1.5L (Shopee free shipping above RM 100)
**Target Audience:** Urban professionals and coffee enthusiasts aged 22–38; both genders; KL, Selangor, Penang
**Campaign:** "Brew Your Day" — weekly 5-post Instagram feed series

**Brand Colours:**
- Primary: Deep Espresso Brown (#3B1F0A)
- Secondary: Cream (#F7F0E6)
- Accent: Warm Caramel (#C68642)

**Fonts (suggest in Canva):**
- Heading: Playfair Display (serif — premium feel)
- Body: Lato or Montserrat (clean sans-serif)

**Visual Style:** Rich, warm, premium café aesthetic; close-up coffee photography; wood and marble textures; lifestyle shots of coffee in beautiful settings; minimal and elegant layout

**Post Series Topics:**
1. Product introduction / hero post — "Meet FreshBrew Cold Brew"
2. Education post — "Why Cold Brew is Different from Iced Coffee"
3. Product variant spotlight — "Introducing: Coconut Latte"
4. Lifestyle/social proof post — "Your Monday, Brewed Better"
5. Offer/CTA post — "Buy 3, Get Free Shipping + RM10 Off"

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Set Up Your Canva Workspace (10 minutes)

**1a.** Open Canva → New Design → Custom Size: **1080 × 1080 px** (for Posts 1, 2, 4, 5)
Create a second template at **1080 × 1350 px** (for Post 3 — portrait format for better reach)

**1b.** Set up your brand palette (all users):
- Go to any design → left sidebar → Brand (or use colour picker)
- Add the three HEX colour codes: #3B1F0A, #F7F0E6, #C68642
- Note these down — you will apply them manually to each design

**1c.** Set up font reference:
- Primary heading: Playfair Display, Bold
- Body/subheading: Lato, Regular or SemiBold

> **Canva Pro users:** Set up Brand Kit now with logo, colours, and fonts for one-click brand application.

---

### Step 2: Create Post 1 — Hero Introduction (20 minutes)

**Goal:** Make a strong first impression; introduce the brand and core product.

**Design approach:** Premium, dark background; product as hero; gold/cream text; minimal copy.

**Step 2a:** Use **Magic Design** or start from a template:
- Search Canva templates for "dark coffee product post"
- Select one closest to the brand feel → Duplicate to your workspace

**Step 2b:** Replace template content:
- Background: Change to brand Deep Espresso Brown (#3B1F0A) or use Magic Media to generate a dark coffee texture background
  - Magic Media prompt: "Close-up macro shot of cold brew coffee in a glass, dark and moody lighting, rich brown tones, condensation droplets, premium café aesthetic"
- Heading: "FreshBrew Cold Brew" — Playfair Display Bold, Cream (#F7F0E6), large
- Subheading: "Premium Malaysian Cold Brew, Delivered to Your Door" — Lato Regular, smaller
- Add logo in bottom-right corner (or use text logo "FB" if no logo file available)
- CTA text: "Shop on Shopee — Link in Bio" — Caramel (#C68642), small, near bottom

**Step 2c:** Check visual hierarchy:
- Brand name reads first? ✓/✗
- Subheading reads second? ✓/✗
- CTA is visible but not dominant? ✓/✗

---

### Step 3: Create Posts 2, 3, 4, 5 (40 minutes — 10 minutes each)

For each post, follow the same brand application workflow:
1. Duplicate the Post 1 canvas (to maintain consistent dimensions)
2. Change the background (keep the visual style consistent but vary the layout)
3. Use Magic Write or type new copy appropriate to each post's topic
4. Maintain brand colours and fonts throughout
5. Check design principles for each post (see evaluation criteria)

**Post-specific design guidance:**

**Post 2 — Education (1080 × 1080 px):**
- Split layout: left side text, right side image
- Magic Media prompt: "Comparison of cold brew and iced coffee in two glasses, clean white marble background, top-down shot, warm cream tones"
- Heading: "Cold Brew vs Iced Coffee" — the difference matters
- Use a simple 2-column comparison table as a graphic element

**Post 3 — Variant Spotlight (1080 × 1350 px — portrait):**
- Product-hero layout; feature image fills 60% of canvas
- Magic Media prompt: "Coconut latte cold brew in a tall glass with coconut slices and straw, warm golden lighting, tropical but premium aesthetic"
- Heading: "New Flavour" (large, accent colour)
- Product name: "Coconut Latte Cold Brew" (large, cream)
- Short description: 2–3 words max on graphic; full copy goes in caption

**Post 4 — Lifestyle (1080 × 1080 px):**
- Person-centred layout; warm, aspirational mood
- Magic Media prompt: "Young Malaysian professional woman in her late 20s working at a bright café, smiling, holding a cold brew coffee glass, natural morning light, warm and productive atmosphere"
- Minimal text overlay: "Brew Your Day" as headline only
- Mood: optimistic, energising

**Post 5 — Offer/CTA (1080 × 1080 px):**
- High contrast; offer must be unmissable
- Background: keep Deep Espresso Brown
- Large bold text: "BUY 3, GET FREE SHIPPING" — Playfair Display, Cream, very large
- Sub text: "+ RM10 OFF with code FRESHBREW10"
- Product image in corner
- CTA button: Canva shape with Caramel fill + "Shop Now on Shopee" text

---

### Step 4: Consistency Audit (10 minutes)

View all 5 posts side by side (Canva allows this in the left panel) and check:

| Consistency Check | All 5 Posts? |
|---|---|
| Brand brown (#3B1F0A) used as primary background or accent on all posts | ☐ |
| Cream (#F7F0E6) used for primary text on all posts | ☐ |
| Caramel (#C68642) used as accent/CTA colour on all posts | ☐ |
| Playfair Display used for all headings | ☐ |
| Lato or Montserrat used for all body text | ☐ |
| Logo or "FreshBrew" brand name present on all posts | ☐ |
| Visual style is cohesive — posts look like they belong to the same brand | ☐ |

Fix any inconsistencies before proceeding.

---

### Step 5: Export All Posts (5 minutes)

For each post:
- Download → PNG → "2x" quality if available (or standard PNG)
- Rename files:
  - `FreshBrew-Post01-Hero.png`
  - `FreshBrew-Post02-Education.png`
  - `FreshBrew-Post03-Variant.png`
  - `FreshBrew-Post04-Lifestyle.png`
  - `FreshBrew-Post05-Offer.png`

Verify: all files are PNG; Posts 1,2,4,5 are 1080×1080px; Post 3 is 1080×1350px.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Brand Consistency** | All 3 colours, both fonts, logo on all 5 posts; cohesive visual identity | Mostly consistent; 1–2 minor variations | 2–3 elements inconsistent | Inconsistent; posts look unrelated |
| **Design Principles** | Hierarchy, contrast, alignment, white space all well-applied | 3 principles well-applied | 2 principles applied | Design fundamentals not applied |
| **AI Feature Use** | 2+ Canva AI features used effectively across posts | 2 features used | 1 feature used | No AI features used |
| **Technical Specs** | All files correct resolution, format, and naming convention | 4/5 posts correct spec | 3/5 posts correct | Incorrect specs throughout |
| **Design Quality** | Posts are professional, attractive, and publication-ready | Posts are clean and correct | Posts are functional but not polished | Rough or incomplete designs |

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. Which of the five posts took the most iterations to get right, and what was the main challenge?
2. Did you use Magic Media, Magic Write, or Magic Design? Describe one specific instance where the AI output was helpful and one where it needed significant correction.
3. Look at your 5 posts as a grid (as they would appear on an Instagram feed). Does the overall feed look cohesive and professional? What would you change if you had more time?
4. A client reviews your Post 5 (offer post) and says "it doesn't feel premium enough for our brand." What specific design changes would you make to elevate the perceived quality of the post?
