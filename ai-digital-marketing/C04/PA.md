## Instructions to Candidates

1. This performance assessment consists of THREE (3) practical tasks
2. Complete ALL tasks within the allocated time
3. Video must be produced using CapCut (desktop or mobile); voiceover must be produced using ElevenLabs or equivalent AI voice generation tool
4. You will be assessed on PROCESS, OUTPUT, ATTITUDE, SAFETY/ETHICS, and ENVIRONMENT
5. Using copyrighted music without a licence or using AI voices to impersonate real persons is an ethical violation and results in automatic failure
6. Equipment provided: computer/laptop with internet access; smartphone (for filming if required); active CapCut account; ElevenLabs account (Starter tier) provided by centre

---

## TASK 1: AI-Assisted Video Script — Hook-Body-CTA Structure (25 marks)
**Time Allocation: 35 minutes**
**Reference: WA3 — KK-01**

You are assigned a Malaysian brand and promotional objective (provided by assessor). Write a complete 60-second video script using AI tools. You must:

1. Prompt an AI tool to generate a video script brief; document the prompt and AI output (4 marks)
2. Produce a final 60-second script in three-section structure: (15 marks)
   - **HOOK (0–5 seconds):** attention-grabbing opening line or visual action cue; must be platform-native for TikTok or Instagram Reels (5 marks)
   - **BODY (5–50 seconds):** product/service benefit, social proof element, and one supporting fact or statistic; include on-screen text direction cues (6 marks)
   - **CTA (50–60 seconds):** clear call-to-action with urgency trigger; state the exact CTA text to appear on screen (4 marks)
3. Format the script in a two-column table: VISUAL / ACTION (left column) and VOICEOVER / TEXT (right column) (4 marks)
4. State the target platform (TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts) and justify why the hook is appropriate for that platform's audience behaviour (2 marks)

---

## TASK 2: 60-Second Promotional Video with CapCut AI (45 marks)
**Time Allocation: 70 minutes**
**Reference: WA1 — KK-02**

Using the script from Task 1, produce a complete 60-second promotional video in CapCut. You must:

1. Film or import a minimum of FIVE (5) raw video clips relevant to the brand and script; each clip must be at least 5 seconds (8 marks)
2. Apply CapCut AI Smart Cut to auto-trim and sequence the clips — document which AI editing features were used (6 marks)
3. Add Auto Captions using CapCut's AI captioning feature; review and correct a minimum of TWO (2) captioning errors before export (8 marks)
4. Add background music from CapCut's royalty-free library — music must match the video's pace and brand tone (5 marks)
5. Add a minimum of THREE (3) on-screen text overlays aligned with the Hook, Body, and CTA sections of the script (6 marks)
6. Apply ONE (1) consistent colour grading filter or LUT throughout the video (3 marks)
7. Export the final video at 1080×1920 px (9:16 vertical), MP4 format, minimum 720p resolution (9 marks)
   - File named: `[BrandName]_60sec_promo_[Date].mp4`
   - Submit the exported file and a screenshot of the CapCut project timeline

---

## TASK 3: 30-Second AI Voiceover with Background Music (30 marks)
**Time Allocation: 45 minutes**
**Reference: WA2 — KK-01, KK-02**

Using ElevenLabs (or equivalent AI voice generation tool), produce a 30-second promotional voiceover. You must:

1. Write a 30-second voiceover script (approximately 75–90 words at natural speaking pace) for the assigned brand — this may be an edited extract from the Task 1 script (5 marks)
2. Generate the voiceover in ElevenLabs — test a minimum of THREE (3) different voice profiles; record the voice name and your assessment of suitability for each (10 marks)
   - Assessment criteria for each voice: accent appropriateness for Malaysian audience, clarity, pace, and brand tone match
3. Select the BEST voice and re-generate the final voiceover with the selected voice; export as MP3 (5 marks)
4. Import the voiceover MP3 into CapCut or any audio editor; add one royalty-free background music track at a volume level of 20–30% relative to the voiceover (5 marks)
5. Export the final mixed audio as MP3; submit alongside a voice selection log (table: Voice Name | Accent | Clarity Rating /5 | Tone Match /5 | Selected Y/N) (5 marks)
