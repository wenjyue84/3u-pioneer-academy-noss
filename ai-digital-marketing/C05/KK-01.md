## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions

### Method A: Using Framer AI

**Step 1: Create a Framer Account**
1. Open Google Chrome and go to `framer.com`
2. Click **Start for free** and sign up with your email address
3. Verify your email and log in to the Framer dashboard

**Step 2: Start a New AI Project**
1. Click **+ New project**
2. Select **Start with AI**
3. You will see a text prompt box — this is where you describe your business

**Step 3: Enter Your Business Prompt**
Use the business brief provided in Section 5. Structure your prompt as follows:
```
Create a professional landing page for [Business Name], a [type of business]
located in [City], Malaysia. We offer [main services/products].
Our target customers are [target audience]. Our unique selling point is
[USP]. Include a call-to-action to [desired action: WhatsApp us / book now / contact us].
Colour scheme: [preferred colours]. Tone: [professional / friendly / modern].
```

**Step 4: Review AI-Generated Layout**
1. Framer AI will generate a full landing page in 15–30 seconds
2. Review all sections: hero, features, testimonials, footer
3. Note which sections need customisation (AI-generated text is a starting point only)

**Step 5: Customise Content**
1. Click on any text element to edit it directly
2. Replace placeholder business name, tagline, and contact details with brief-specific content
3. Update the hero image: click the image → Replace → Upload or use Unsplash integration
4. Adjust colours to match brand: Settings → Colours → Edit palette
5. Ensure the contact section has a working WhatsApp link:
   - Format: `https://wa.me/60XXXXXXXXX?text=Hello,%20I%20found%20you%20on%20your%20website`

**Step 6: Check Mobile Responsiveness**
1. Click the **Device preview** icon (top toolbar)
2. Switch between Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile views
3. Fix any text overflow or image cropping issues by adjusting padding in mobile view

**Step 7: Publish the Page**
1. Click **Publish** (top right)
2. Choose a free Framer subdomain (e.g., `yourbusiness.framer.website`)
3. Click **Publish to web**
4. Copy the published URL

**Step 8: Test Page Speed**
1. Go to `pagespeed.web.dev`
2. Paste your landing page URL
3. Click **Analyse**
4. Note the Mobile and Desktop scores
5. If mobile score is below 70, identify the top recommendation and implement it

### Method B: Using 10Web AI (WordPress Alternative)

**Step 1:** Go to `10web.io` → Start free trial → Create account

**Step 2:** Choose **AI Website Builder** → Enter business description

**Step 3:** 10Web generates a full WordPress site — review the homepage sections

**Step 4:** Use the built-in editor to customise text, images, and colours

**Step 5:** Add a contact form using the WPForms plugin (pre-installed)

**Step 6:** Preview the site → Publish to the 10Web subdomain

## 5. Student Hands-On Task

**Scenario:** You have been hired as a digital marketing specialist by the following client. Build their landing page using Framer AI or 10Web AI.

---

**Client Brief — Trainer assigns ONE of the following:**

**Brief A — Wellness Business:**
> *Harmony Wellness Studio* is a new wellness centre in Subang Jaya offering yoga classes, reflexology, and meditation sessions. Target customers are working adults aged 25–45. USP: "First class free for new members." Desired action: WhatsApp to book a free trial class. Preferred colours: green and white.

**Brief B — F&B Business:**
> *Uncle Tan's Noodle House* is a traditional Chinese noodle restaurant in Ipoh, Perak. Specialises in Ipoh-style curry mee and wonton noodles. Target customers: local residents and food tourists. USP: "Original recipe since 1972." Desired action: View digital menu via QR link. Preferred colours: red and gold.

**Brief C — Professional Services:**
> *SmartBooks Accounting* is a cloud accounting firm in Kuala Lumpur serving Malaysian SMEs. Offers monthly bookkeeping, tax filing, and SST compliance. Target customers: SME owners. USP: "First month bookkeeping free." Desired action: Book a free consultation call. Preferred colours: blue and white.
