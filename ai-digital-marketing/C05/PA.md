## Business Brief (Trainer assigns ONE — do not open until assessment begins)

**Brief A — Beauty & Wellness:**
> *Glow Studio* is a new facial and skincare centre in Petaling Jaya, Selangor. Services: hydra-facial, LED therapy, eyebrow embroidery. Target: working women aged 25–40. USP: "First facial FREE for new members." Desired action: WhatsApp to book. Colours: rose gold and white.

**Brief B — Food & Beverage:**
> *Mak Cik Ros Kitchen* is a home-cooked Malay food delivery service in Johor Bahru. Specialities: nasi lemak, rendang, kuah lodeh. Target: local families and office workers. USP: "Like mak's cooking, delivered in 45 minutes." Desired action: WhatsApp order. Colours: green and cream.

**Brief C — Professional Services:**
> *TaxEasy Accounting* is a cloud accounting and tax filing firm in Kuala Lumpur serving Malaysian SMEs. Services: monthly bookkeeping, SST filing, income tax (Form B/C). USP: "First month free." Desired action: Book consultation. Colours: navy blue and white.

## TASK 1: Build a Business Landing Page Using AI (35 marks)
**Time Allocation: 60 minutes**
**Reference: WA1 — KK-01**

Using the assigned business brief, build and publish a business landing page using Framer AI or 10Web AI (WordPress). You must:

1. Create a Framer AI or 10Web account and start a new AI-generated project using a structured prompt based on the business brief (5 marks)
2. Review and customise all AI-generated sections — replace all placeholder text with brief-specific content: business name, tagline, services, and contact details (6 marks)
3. Ensure the page includes ALL five required sections: hero (business name + tagline + image), benefits/features (minimum 3 points), social proof element (testimonial placeholder or badge), CTA button (WhatsApp or booking form), and contact/footer section (8 marks)
4. Add a functional WhatsApp link in the correct format: `https://wa.me/60XXXXXXXXX?text=Hello,...` (4 marks)
5. Verify mobile responsiveness using the device preview — fix any text overflow or image cropping (5 marks)
6. Publish the page to a live or preview URL and run a Google PageSpeed Insights test; note the mobile score (4 marks)
7. Take and submit a screenshot of: (a) desktop view, (b) mobile view, (c) PageSpeed results (3 marks)

---

## TASK 2: Set Up and Optimise a Google Business Profile (40 marks)
**Time Allocation: 60 minutes**
**Reference: WA2 & WA3 — KK-02**

Using the same assigned business brief, set up and fully optimise a Google Business Profile. You must:

1. Access `business.google.com`, create or claim a GBP listing for the business, and complete all basic fields: business name, category, address, service area, phone number, website URL (6 marks)
2. Use ChatGPT or Gemini to generate an AI business description (maximum 750 characters); customise the output to remove any hallucinated claims before publishing (8 marks)
3. Set business hours including at least one Malaysian public holiday special hour entry (4 marks)
4. Add a minimum of 2 products or services with AI-generated descriptions (max 200 characters each) (6 marks)
5. Upload a minimum of 5 photos across at least 3 categories: logo, exterior, interior, product/service, or team (6 marks)
6. Pre-populate 3 Q&A pairs using AI-generated questions and professional answers; publish all 3 (6 marks)
7. Create and publish one GBP Post (type: What's New) with a Canva-designed 1200×900px image and an AI-generated caption with a CTA button linking to the website (4 marks)

---

## TASK 3: Digital Presence Audit and Improvement Report (25 marks)
**Time Allocation: 30 minutes**
**Reference: WA2 & WA3 — Integrative**

Conduct a digital presence audit for the assigned business and produce a structured improvement report. You must:

1. Check NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across the landing page and GBP listing — note any discrepancies (5 marks)
2. Review social media completeness: assess whether the business has a Facebook Page and/or Instagram account with complete bio, profile photo, and contact button; note any missing elements (5 marks)
3. Use ChatGPT or Gemini to generate an AI-assisted audit summary using the following prompt structure: platform, completeness score (0–100%), key gaps, and one priority action per platform (6 marks)
4. Produce a written improvement report (minimum 200 words) covering: current digital presence status, top 3 weaknesses identified, and a 30-day priority action plan (6 marks)
5. Verify all published content complies with PDPA 2010 — confirm no real customer personal data (IC numbers, phone numbers, photos of identifiable individuals without consent) is published on any platform (3 marks)
