## Competency Unit: Plan Digital Marketing Strategy with AI (DM-001-3:2026-C01)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Duration | 2 hours 30 minutes |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Passing Mark | 60% (60 marks) |

## Instructions to Candidates

1. This performance assessment consists of THREE (3) practical tasks
2. Complete ALL tasks within the allocated time
3. All outputs must be produced using AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, or equivalent) — manual-only submissions will not be accepted
4. You will be assessed on PROCESS, OUTPUT, ATTITUDE, SAFETY/ETHICS, and ENVIRONMENT
5. Fabricating AI outputs or submitting pre-generated content is an academic integrity violation and results in automatic failure
6. Equipment provided: computer/laptop with internet access and active accounts for ChatGPT or Claude

---

## TASK 1: AI-Assisted Market Research — SWOT & Competitor Analysis (30 marks)
**Time Allocation: 45 minutes**
**Reference: WA1, WA2 — KK-01**

You are assigned a Malaysian SME business brief (provided by assessor at start of assessment). Using AI tools, produce a structured market research report. You must:

1. Craft and document at least THREE (3) AI prompts used for research — show prompt text and AI response (5 marks)
2. Produce a SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) relevant to the assigned SME (8 marks)
3. Identify THREE (3) direct or indirect competitors operating in Malaysia; provide name, product/service, and digital marketing channel for each (7 marks)
4. Summarise the competitive landscape in a paragraph of not less than 100 words, citing insights drawn from AI responses (5 marks)
5. State TWO (2) limitations of AI-generated research and how you verified or cross-checked the information (5 marks)

---

## TASK 2: Build a Target Audience Persona with AI (30 marks)
**Time Allocation: 40 minutes**
**Reference: WA2 — KK-02**

Using AI tools, build ONE (1) detailed buyer persona for the assigned SME's primary target audience. You must:

1. Prompt the AI to generate a buyer persona; document the prompt and full AI output (5 marks)
2. Complete the following persona sections (using AI output as a base, refined with your own judgment): (15 marks)
   - Name, age, gender, location, occupation, income bracket (3 marks)
   - Goals and motivations relevant to the SME's product/service (3 marks)
   - Pain points and challenges (3 marks)
   - Preferred digital platforms and content types (3 marks)
   - Buying behaviour and decision triggers (3 marks)
3. Present the persona in a structured one-page format (table or visual layout acceptable) (5 marks)
4. Write a targeting recommendation paragraph — which platforms and content formats would best reach this persona and why (5 marks)

---

## TASK 3: 30-Day AI-Assisted Content Calendar (40 marks)
**Time Allocation: 65 minutes**
**Reference: WA3, WA4 — KK-01, KK-02**

Using AI tools, produce a 30-day content calendar for the assigned SME integrating the SWOT findings and buyer persona from Tasks 1 and 2. You must:

1. Define FIVE (5) content pillars appropriate for the SME; for each pillar provide a name, purpose, and example content topic (10 marks)
2. Generate a 30-day calendar (minimum 20 content entries across the 30 days) using AI — document at least TWO (2) AI prompts used (15 marks)
   - Each entry must include: Date / Day, Platform, Content Pillar, Content Type (video/image/text), Caption Hook / Title
3. Format the completed calendar in a spreadsheet or table (not free-form text) (5 marks)
4. Select TWO (2) calendar entries and write a brief rationale (50–80 words each) explaining why that content, platform, and timing was chosen for the target persona (6 marks)
5. Identify ONE (1) key performance indicator (KPI) for each content pillar and state how it will be measured (4 marks)
