## Work Activity Title

Build a 30-Day Social Media Content Calendar Using AI for a Malaysian Business

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:
- Used AI to generate a month-long content plan with daily post ideas
- Applied content pillars (tiang kandungan) to organise content variety
- Produced a calendar document in a professional spreadsheet format
- Demonstrated ability to adapt AI output to Malaysian cultural context

## Safety Precautions

- Ensure all planned content is culturally sensitive to Malaysia's multi-ethnic, multi-religious context
- Avoid scheduling insensitive content during religious observance periods (e.g., Ramadan, major festivals)
- Verify AI-suggested trending topics are currently relevant — AI training data may be outdated
- Do not plan content that makes unsubstantiated health, financial, or legal claims

---

## Background: Content Pillars (Tiang Kandungan)

A **content pillar** is a core theme that all your social media content is organised around. For a balanced content strategy, most brands use 4–6 pillars to ensure variety and prevent audience fatigue.

**Recommended pillar mix for most Malaysian SME brands:**

| Pillar | Type | Percentage | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Education (Pendidikan)** | Inform | 30% | Tips, how-to guides, industry facts |
| **Entertainment (Hiburan)** | Engage | 20% | Memes, relatable content, fun polls |
| **Inspiration (Inspirasi)** | Motivate | 15% | Success stories, testimonials, quotes |
| **Promotion (Promosi)** | Sell | 20% | Product features, offers, launches |
| **Community (Komuniti)** | Connect | 15% | Behind the scenes, team, UGC repost |

### Step 2: Generate Content Pillars with AI (10 minutes)

Enter the following prompt into ChatGPT or Claude:

```
I am creating a 30-day social media content calendar for [business type] in Malaysia.
Target audience: [paste persona name and key traits from KK-01].

1. Suggest 5 content pillars suitable for this business.
2. For each pillar, give 3 example post ideas relevant to the Malaysian market.
3. Recommend the ideal posting frequency per week.
4. Flag any Malaysian public holidays or cultural events in [month] I should plan content around.
```

### Step 3: Generate 30 Post Ideas (20 minutes)

Enter the following prompt:

```
Based on these 5 content pillars for [business], generate 30 social media post ideas for [month] on [platform].

For each post idea, provide:
- Day number (Day 1 to Day 30)
- Content pillar
- Post type (single image / carousel / video / story / reel / text)
- Caption hook (first line of the caption — must grab attention)
- Hashtag category (3–5 relevant hashtags for Malaysian audience)

Ensure variety — no two consecutive posts should use the same pillar or format.
Include 2–3 posts timed around [mention any festivals or events in the month].
```

> **Note:** If the AI output is missing some days or lacks variety, follow up with: "The calendar has gaps on Days [X, Y, Z]. Please fill these with [pillar] posts using a [format] format."

---

### Step 4: Build the Calendar in Google Sheets (25 minutes)

Open Google Sheets and create the following column headers in Row 1:

| A | B | C | D | E | F | G | H |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day | Date | Platform | Pillar | Post Type | Caption Hook | Hashtags | Status |

Fill in all 30 rows using the AI-generated post ideas.

**Formatting tips:**
- Colour-code rows by pillar (use Google Sheets fill colour)
- Mark festival/special event days with bold text and a different row colour
- Status column: leave blank (will be filled as "Scheduled / Posted / Skipped" during real use)

**Save the file as:** `[BusinessName]-ContentCalendar-[Month][Year].xlsx`

---

### Step 5: Validate and Refine (10 minutes)

Review your completed calendar and check:

| Check | Yes / No / Needs Fix |
|---|---|

| At least 3 different post formats are used (image, video, carousel, etc.) | |
| Festival/event posts are timed correctly | |
| All caption hooks are in the correct brand tone | |
| Hashtags are relevant and in appropriate language (BM/EN) | |
| No post makes an unsubstantiated claim | |

For any "Needs Fix" items, refine those rows using an AI follow-up prompt or your own judgment.

---

### Step 6: Present Your Calendar (5 minutes)

Prepare to present your calendar to the trainer or class. Be ready to explain:
1. Your choice of content pillars and the reasoning
2. One post you are most proud of and why
3. One change you made to the AI output and why you changed it

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pillar Balance** | All 5 pillars well-distributed; ideal mix percentages applied | 4–5 pillars present; reasonable distribution | 3 pillars used; some imbalance | 1–2 pillars dominate calendar |
| **Content Variety** | 4+ formats used; no consecutive repetition | 3 formats; some repetition managed | 2 formats; noticeable repetition | Single format throughout |
| **Local Relevance** | Strong Malaysian context; festival posts; BM/EN mix | Some local context; festival awareness | Generic content; minimal local context | No local relevance |
| **Caption Hooks** | All hooks are compelling, specific, and in brand voice | Most hooks are effective | Hooks are present but generic | Missing or ineffective hooks |
| **Spreadsheet Quality** | Colour-coded, complete, professional presentation | All columns filled; some formatting | Most columns filled; minimal formatting | Incomplete or unformatted |

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. Which content pillar do you think will perform best for your chosen business, and why?
2. The AI suggested a post for a festival you were not aware of. How would you verify whether this is a genuine Malaysian public holiday?
3. A client asks you to add more promotional posts, increasing the "Promotion" pillar to 50% of all posts. What would you advise them and why?
4. How would you adapt this content calendar if the client wanted to post on TikTok instead of Instagram? What would change?
