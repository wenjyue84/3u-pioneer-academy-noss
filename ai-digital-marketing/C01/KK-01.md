1. Use AI tools to conduct structured market and competitor research for a given business scenario.

3. Compile research findings into a professional market research summary report.

---

## Work Activity Title

Conduct AI-Assisted Market Research for a Real or Simulated Malaysian Business

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:
- Completed a full AI-assisted market research workflow
- Produced a SWOT analysis for a given business
- Created one buyer persona with demographic, psychographic, and behavioural data
- Written a 1-page market research summary suitable for a client presentation

---

## Tools & Materials Required

| Item | Purpose | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Computer / laptop with internet | Primary workspace | Lab / personal device |
| ChatGPT (free account) | AI research and drafting | chat.openai.com |
| Perplexity AI | Real-time web research | perplexity.ai |
| Google Trends | Search trend data | trends.google.com |
| Microsoft Word / Google Docs | Final document production | Lab or personal |
| Worksheet Answer Sheet (provided) | Record responses | Printed / digital |

---

## Safety Precautions

- Do not enter real personal data (names, phone numbers, IC numbers) of real individuals into AI tools
- Do not enter confidential client business data into public AI tools
- Verify all statistics generated by AI before including in your report — AI may hallucinate figures
- Save your work every 15 minutes

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Receive Your Business Scenario (5 minutes)

Your trainer will assign you **one** of the following business scenarios, or you may propose your own (subject to trainer approval):

| Option | Business |
|---|---|
| A | A new halal skincare brand targeting Muslim women in Klang Valley |
| B | An online Mandarin tutoring platform for primary school children in Malaysia |
| C | A home-based baking business wanting to scale via Shopee and Instagram |
| D | A co-working space in George Town, Penang targeting digital nomads and freelancers |

Record your assigned scenario: _______________________________________________

---

### Step 2: Industry and Market Overview (15 minutes)

Open **Perplexity AI** (perplexity.ai) and enter the following prompt (adapt to your scenario):

```
I am planning to launch [your business]. Give me:
1. Overview of this industry in Malaysia (market size, growth trend, key players)
2. Top 3 consumer trends shaping this market in 2025-2026
3. Key challenges businesses in this space face in Malaysia
Include citations for statistics where possible.
```

Record the key findings in your Answer Sheet under **Section 1: Industry Overview**.

> **Validation step:** Check at least one statistic against a credible source (Statista, DOSM, Nielsen, industry association). Note any discrepancy.

---

### Step 3: Competitor Research (20 minutes)

Open **ChatGPT** and enter:

```
Act as a business analyst. For [your business scenario] in Malaysia:
1. Identify 5 direct competitors (businesses offering the same product/service)
2. For each competitor, describe: their main product/service, estimated price range, social media presence, and one unique selling point
3. Identify 3 content gaps or underserved customer needs that a new entrant could address
Format as a structured table for the competitor comparison.
```

Then manually visit **2 of the competitors' social media pages** (Instagram or TikTok) and note:
- Number of followers
- Posting frequency (posts per week)
- Most popular content type (video / image / carousel)
- Average likes per post (estimate from recent 5 posts)

Record under **Section 2: Competitor Analysis**.

---

### Step 4: SWOT Analysis (15 minutes)

Using ChatGPT, enter:

```
Based on the following information about my business:
- Business: [your scenario]
- Strengths I have identified: [list 2–3 real advantages based on the scenario]
- Market context: [paste 2–3 key findings from Step 2]

Generate a detailed SWOT analysis with 4 points per quadrant. 
Then suggest one SO strategy, one WO strategy, one ST strategy, and one WT strategy.
```

Review the output. Cross out any SWOT points that are too generic or inaccurate. Add at least two of your own points from your research.

Record the finalised SWOT in **Section 3: SWOT Analysis**.

---

### Step 5: Buyer Persona Creation (20 minutes)

Enter the following prompt in ChatGPT:

```
Create a detailed buyer persona for [your business scenario] in Malaysia.
The persona should include:
- Fictional name, age, location, occupation, income bracket (B40/M40/T20)
- Family status and lifestyle
- Goals related to [your product/service]
- Top 3 pain points with current options
- Where they spend time online (platforms, time of day)
- What type of content they engage with
- What would motivate them to purchase
- What would stop them from purchasing

Make the persona realistic for the Malaysian market.
```

Give your persona a Malaysian name and add any additional details based on your own knowledge of the local market.

Record the completed persona in **Section 4: Buyer Persona**.

---

### Step 6: Compile Market Research Summary (15 minutes)

Using Microsoft Word or Google Docs, create a 1-page market research summary containing:

1. **Business Concept** (2 sentences)
2. **Market Overview** (3 bullet points from Step 2)
3. **Top Competitor** (1 paragraph describing the strongest competitor and their strategy)
4. **SWOT Snapshot** (condensed version — 2 points per quadrant)
5. **Target Customer** (persona name + 5-sentence description)
6. **Recommended Primary Digital Channel** (with one-sentence justification)

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Prompt Quality** | Prompts are specific, structured, use CRAFT framework | Prompts are clear with most elements | Prompts are basic but functional | Prompts are vague or copied verbatim from this sheet |
| **Research Depth** | Findings are detailed, validated, and locally relevant | Most findings are accurate with some local context | Findings present but mostly generic | Findings are superficial or unvalidated |
| **SWOT Quality** | All 4 quadrants complete, realistic, with strategies | 3 quadrants strong; strategies present | SWOT complete but generic | Incomplete or inaccurate |
| **Persona Completeness** | All fields complete with local Malaysian details | Most fields complete | Core fields present | Skeletal or fictional |
| **Summary Report** | Professional format, all 6 sections, well-written | 5 sections, mostly professional | 4 sections, readable | Incomplete or poorly structured |

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. What was the most useful AI tool you used in this activity, and why?
2. Did the AI generate any information that you knew to be incorrect or questionable? How did you handle it?
3. How would this research workflow be different without AI tools? Estimate how much additional time it would take.
4. What additional information would you want to gather if you had more time, and which AI tool would you use to get it?
