## 1. Marketing Analytics Fundamentals

### 1.1 Data vs. Metrics vs. KPIs

These three terms are often used interchangeably but refer to distinct concepts. Confusing them leads to poor decision-making in campaign management.

| Term | Definition | Example in Digital Marketing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Data (Data)** | Raw, unprocessed numbers or facts collected by a system | "3,412 people clicked the ad on Tuesday" |
| **Metric (Metrik)** | A calculated measurement derived from data | Click-through rate (CTR) = 3,412 clicks ÷ 142,167 impressions = 2.4% |
| **KPI (Petunjuk Prestasi Utama)** | A metric specifically chosen to measure progress toward a business objective | "Our campaign KPI is to achieve a CTR ≥ 2.0% and CPL ≤ RM 25" |

**Key Rule:** Not all metrics are KPIs. A metric becomes a KPI only when it is directly tied to a business goal and has a defined target.

### 1.2 Vanity Metrics vs. Actionable Metrics

| Vanity Metric (Metrik Hiasan) | Why It Is Misleading | Actionable Alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total Impressions (Tayangan) | High impressions with zero conversions = wasted spend | Impressions + Conversion Rate together |
| Total Page Likes (Suka Halaman) | Likes do not equal customers or revenue | Lead count or purchase revenue |
| Video Views (Tontonan Video) | Views counted at 3 seconds — user may have ignored the ad | Video completion rate (watched >75%) |
| Reach (Jangkauan) | Large reach to the wrong audience is worthless | Reach × Conversion Rate = qualified leads |
| Engagement (Penglibatan) | Likes and comments from non-buyers inflate numbers | Engagement from target demographic only |

> **Practitioner Principle:** Always work backwards from the business goal. If the goal is revenue, the primary KPI must be ROAS or revenue from ads, not impressions or likes.

### 1.3 The Marketing Analytics Stack for Malaysian Businesses

A complete analytics setup for a Malaysian SME typically involves three interconnected layers:

```
Layer 1 — Platform Analytics (where the ad ran)
├── Meta Ads Manager → campaign, audience, creative performance
└── TikTok Ads Manager → video performance, ad group results

Layer 2 — Website Analytics (what happened after the click)
└── Google Analytics 4 (GA4) → sessions, events, conversions, user behaviour

Layer 3 — Business Intelligence (financial outcome)
├── Looker Studio → combined dashboard from all sources
├── CRM / POS data → actual revenue and customer records
└── AI Report (ChatGPT) → narrative insights for stakeholders
```

## 2. Meta Ads Manager Dashboard — Full Navigation

### 2.1 Dashboard Overview

When logged into Meta Ads Manager (business.facebook.com/adsmanager), the default view shows all active campaigns. Key navigation areas:

| Section | Location | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaigns tab | Top navigation | View all campaigns; switch between Campaign / Ad Set / Ad views |
| Columns | Right side of table header | Select which metrics columns are displayed |
| Filters | Top left | Filter by campaign status, objective, date range |
| Breakdown | Top right of data table | Slice data by age, gender, placement, device, time of day |
| Charts | Above data table | Graphical view of selected metric over time |
| AI Insights | Bell icon / sidebar | AI-generated alerts and recommendations |
| Export | Top right | Download data as CSV or Excel for further analysis |

### 2.2 Core Metrics — Definitions and Warning Thresholds

| Metric | Definition | Formula | Warning Signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reach (Jangkauan) | Unique individuals who saw the ad at least once | Platform-tracked | Below 1,000 after 3 days = audience too narrow or budget too low |
| Impressions (Tayangan) | Total times the ad was displayed | Platform-tracked | Impressions ÷ Reach > 5 = excessive frequency |
| Frequency (Frekuensi) | Average times each person saw the ad | Impressions ÷ Reach | >3.0 = creative fatigue risk; >5.0 = refresh required immediately |
| CTR (Link) | % of impressions resulting in a link click | Link Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100 | Below 0.5% = poor creative or wrong audience |
| CPC (Link) | Cost per link click | Spend ÷ Link Clicks | Above RM 3 for most niches = optimisation needed |
| CPM | Cost per 1,000 impressions | Spend ÷ Impressions × 1,000 | Above RM 35 = audience too narrow or high competition period |
| Result Rate | % of impressions achieving the objective | Results ÷ Impressions × 100 | Below 1% for leads; below 0.5% for purchases = review offer |
| Cost Per Result | Cost per objective achieved | Spend ÷ Results | Compare to predetermined target CPL or CPA |
| ROAS | Revenue generated per RM1 of ad spend | Revenue ÷ Ad Spend | Below 1.0 = campaign is losing money; target ≥ 3× for most SMEs |
| Amount Spent | Total campaign expenditure | Platform-tracked | Overpacing: >115% of planned daily budget on day 1 = adjust bid |

### 2.3 The AI Insights Panel — Understanding Meta's Automated Alerts

Meta Ads Manager generates automatic AI-driven alerts based on performance anomalies and pattern recognition:

| Alert Type | Example Message | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creative Fatigue (Keletihan Kreatif) | "Ad ID 12345 has been shown to the same audience an average of 4.2 times. Performance is declining." | Upload 2–3 new creatives; refresh headline and body text |
| Audience Saturation (Ketepuan Khalayak) | "You have reached 82% of your target audience. Consider expanding your audience or refreshing creatives." | Broaden age range; add new interest layers; try Advantage+ Audience |
| Budget Under-delivery | "Your campaign is only spending 43% of your daily budget due to a narrow audience." | Expand targeting; reduce bid cap; switch to Advantage+ Placements |
| Budget Over-pacing | "Your campaign is spending 35% above your daily budget projection." | Reduce daily budget; add bid cap; narrow audience if reach is too broad |
| Winning Creative Identified | "Creative B is outperforming Creative A by 62% on cost per result." | Pause Creative A; allocate full budget to Creative B; create similar variants |
| Pixel Event Not Firing | "Your Purchase event has not recorded any conversions in 3 days." | Check Meta Pixel installation; verify event setup in Events Manager |

### 2.4 Breakdown Analysis — Finding Performance Patterns

The Breakdown tool allows slicing campaign data to identify hidden patterns. Access: Data table → Breakdown button → Select dimension.

| Breakdown Dimension | Insight Uncovered | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Age (Umur) | Which age group has lowest CPL | Increase budget allocation to winning age bracket; exclude unprofitable brackets |
| Gender (Jantina) | Whether male or female converts at lower cost | Create gender-specific creatives if >30% performance difference |
| Placement (Penempatan) | Facebook Feed vs. Instagram Feed vs. Reels vs. Stories | Allocate manual placement to top-performing placements; exclude poor ones |
| Device (Peranti) | iOS vs. Android; mobile vs. desktop | iOS users typically convert at different rates due to Apple ATT privacy changes |
| Time of Day (Masa) | Which hours have lowest CPL | Schedule ads to run during high-performance windows |
| Region (Wilayah) | Which Malaysian states have best performance | Create state-specific campaigns with localised creative |

## 3. TikTok Ads Dashboard — Key Metrics and Differences

### 3.1 TikTok-Specific Video Metrics

TikTok advertising is video-only, so it includes video engagement metrics not available in Meta:

| Metric | Definition | Target (Malaysia) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impressions | Total video displays | Scale metric — higher is better when CPM is controlled |
| Video Views (Tontonan Video) | Counted at 2 seconds with 50% screen visibility | Baseline view metric |
| 6-Second Views | Users who watched at least 6 seconds | >60% of 2-second views indicates strong hook |
| Video Completion Rate (Kadar Siap Video) | % who watched the full video | >35% is good; <15% = weak content or wrong audience |
| Average Watch Time | Mean seconds watched per view | >10 seconds for 30s video = good content retention |
| Engagement Rate | (Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) ÷ Views × 100 | >3% is strong for paid ads |
| CTR | Clicks on CTA button ÷ Impressions | 0.8–2.0% for Malaysia; >2% is excellent |
| CPA (Cost Per Action) | Ad Spend ÷ Conversions (purchases, leads) | Benchmark varies by industry — see Section 5 |

### 3.2 Comparing TikTok and Meta Dashboard Metrics

| Dimension | Meta Ads Manager | TikTok Ads Manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| View counting threshold | 1 second (impressions) | 2 seconds with 50% visibility |
| Video completion metric | ThruPlay (video watched to 97% completion or 15s) | Video completion rate (100% watched) |
| Placement breakdown | Feed, Stories, Reels, Messenger, Audience Network | For You Page, Search, Profile, Pangle |
| Pixel technology | Meta Pixel + Conversions API | TikTok Pixel + Events API |
| Data delay | Near real-time (2–4 hour delay) | 24–48 hour delay for some conversion data |
| Attribution window default | 7-day click, 1-day view | 7-day click, 1-day view (adjustable) |
| AI insights | Detailed automated alerts | Less detailed; improving in 2026 |

## 4. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) with AI Features

### 4.1 Why GA4 Is Necessary Alongside Ad Platform Data

Meta Ads Manager and TikTok Ads Manager only show what happens inside their platforms — clicks, impressions, and estimated conversions. GA4 shows what actually happens after the user clicks the ad and arrives at the website.

| Question | Platform Dashboard Answers? | GA4 Answers? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did the user click the ad? | Yes | Yes (via UTM parameters) |
| Did the user land on the correct page? | No | Yes (Landing page report) |
| How long did the user stay on the site? | No | Yes (Engagement time) |
| Did the user visit multiple pages? | No | Yes (Pages per session) |
| Did the user complete a conversion? | Estimated (pixel-based) | Confirmed (event-based) |
| Which device and browser did they use? | Partial | Full detail |
| Did the user return to the site later? | No | Yes (Retention report) |

### 4.2 Key GA4 Reports for Campaign Analysis

| Report | Location in GA4 | What to Look For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acquisition Overview | Reports → Acquisition → Overview | % of sessions from Paid Social, Organic Search, Direct |
| Traffic Acquisition | Reports → Acquisition → Traffic Acquisition | Sessions per channel; compare Paid Social vs. Organic |
| Conversions | Reports → Engagement → Conversions | Number and rate of key conversion events (Lead, Purchase, Contact) |
| Landing Pages | Reports → Engagement → Landing Pages | Bounce rate and conversion rate per ad landing page |
| Demographics | Reports → User → User Attributes → Demographics | Age, gender, and location of actual website visitors from ads |
| User Explorer | Reports → Explore → User Explorer | Individual user journey from ad click to conversion |

### 4.3 GA4 AI-Powered Features

GA4 uses Google's machine learning to surface insights that would take human analysts hours to find manually:

| AI Feature | Function | How to Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automated Insights | AI detects significant changes (e.g., "Sessions increased 45% vs last week") and surfaces them proactively | GA4 home → Insights cards at top of dashboard |
| Anomaly Detection (Pengesanan Anomali) | AI flags unusual spikes or drops in traffic and conversions | Insights → View all insights → Anomalies tab |
| Predictive Metrics | AI predicts: Purchase Probability (likelihood to buy in 7 days), Churn Probability, Predicted Revenue | Audiences → Create Audience → Predictive |
| Gemini in GA4 | Ask questions in plain English: "Which campaign had the lowest CPA last month?" | Search bar in GA4 → Natural language query |
| Exploration / Funnel Analysis | AI-assisted path analysis showing where users drop off in the conversion funnel | Explore → Funnel Exploration |

### 4.4 Connecting GA4 to Meta Ads — UTM Parameters

Without UTM parameters (Urchin Tracking Module), GA4 cannot attribute website traffic to specific ad campaigns. All ad links must include UTM tags.

**UTM Parameter Structure:**
```
https://yourwebsite.com/landing-page
  ?utm_source=facebook
  &utm_medium=paid_social
  &utm_campaign=ramadan-2026-promo
  &utm_content=video-ad-a
  &utm_term=women-25-35-kl
```

| UTM Parameter | What It Identifies | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| utm_source | Platform where the ad ran | facebook, tiktok, google |
| utm_medium | Type of marketing | paid_social, paid_search, email |
| utm_campaign | Campaign name | ramadan-2026, hari-raya-sale |
| utm_content | Creative variant (for A/B testing) | video-a, image-b, carousel-c |
| utm_term | Audience or keyword | women-25-35, lookalike-1pct |

**Setup Steps:**
1. Generate UTM URLs using Google's Campaign URL Builder (ga-dev-tools.web.app/campaign-url-builder/)
2. Paste the UTM URL as the destination URL in each Meta or TikTok ad
3. In GA4, go to Reports → Acquisition → Traffic Acquisition → Group by "Session campaign"
4. Each campaign's traffic will appear separately, linked to GA4 conversion data

## 5. AI-Powered Campaign Analysis Workflow

### 5.1 Weekly Analysis Routine

Consistent weekly analysis prevents campaign decay and ensures continuous improvement:

| Day | Action | Tool | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Review AI Insights alerts from weekend; check for anomalies | Meta Ads Manager / TikTok Ads Manager | List of issues requiring action |
| Monday | Check GA4 Automated Insights for week-on-week traffic changes | GA4 | Note any landing page or conversion anomalies |
| Tuesday | Run Breakdown analysis: age × placement × device | Meta Ads Manager Breakdown | Identify best and worst-performing segments |
| Wednesday | Compare TikTok video completion rates and watch time against benchmarks | TikTok Ads Manager | Flag videos with <20% completion for replacement |
| Thursday | Make optimisation decisions: pause, scale, or refresh | Both platforms | Updated campaign settings |
| Friday | Export data; draft weekly client report using AI | ChatGPT + Looker Studio | Client-ready performance summary |

### 5.2 ChatGPT Prompt Templates for Campaign Analysis

**Prompt 1 — Weekly Performance Summary:**
```
You are a digital marketing analyst. I am providing campaign performance data 
for the week ending [date]. 

Campaign data:
[Paste data table from Ads Manager: campaign name, spend, impressions, CTR, 
CPL/CPA, ROAS, top 3 performing ads, bottom 3 performing ads]

Write a professional campaign performance summary including:
1. Executive summary (3 sentences: what happened, key win, key issue)
2. Top performing ad — why it worked (based on the data)
3. Underperforming ad — possible reasons and recommendation
4. Three specific optimisation actions for next week
5. Overall campaign health assessment: Green (on track) / Yellow (needs attention) / Red (urgent action required)

Tone: professional, concise. Audience: business owner with no marketing background.
Length: 350–450 words.
```

**Prompt 2 — Competitive Benchmark Analysis:**
```
My Facebook campaign for a [business type] in Malaysia achieved the following 
this month: CTR [x]%, CPM RM [x], CPL RM [x], ROAS [x]×.

Compare these results to typical Malaysian benchmarks for this industry.
For each metric that is below benchmark, suggest one specific improvement action.
For each metric above benchmark, suggest one way to scale.
Present as a table: Metric | My Result | Benchmark | Assessment | Action.
```

### 5.3 Identifying and Acting on AI-Detected Optimisation Opportunities

| AI-Detected Issue | Root Cause Analysis | Optimisation Action | Timeline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High CPM (>RM 35), low reach | Audience too narrow; high competition period | Expand Lookalike to 3–5%; switch to Advantage+ Audience | Immediate |
| Low CTR (<0.5%) | Creative not compelling; wrong audience | Test 3 new ad creatives with different hooks; review audience relevance | 3–5 days |
| High CTR but low conversion rate (<1%) | Landing page irrelevant or slow; offer mismatch | Check page load speed (<3 seconds); align landing page message with ad copy | Immediate |
| High frequency (>4) | Audience exhausted by repeated exposure | Refresh all ad creatives; expand audience; create exclusion list for converters | Immediate |
| Good leads but low sales conversion | Lead quality issue; sales process problem | Narrow targeting to higher-intent signals; review lead qualification process | 1 week |
| ROAS declining week-on-week | Seasonality; competition increasing; product-market fit | Check competitor activity; test new audiences; review product pricing and offer | 1–2 weeks |
| Spike in CPC during festive season | More advertisers competing in same auction | Pre-build campaign assets before festive period; use Lifetime Budget during peak |  Plan 2 weeks ahead |

## 7. Reporting for Stakeholders

### 7.1 Campaign Report Structure

A professional monthly campaign performance report should follow this structure for non-marketing stakeholders:

| Section | Content | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Executive Summary | Campaign period, total spend, headline results (ROAS or CPL), overall assessment | 3–5 sentences |
| 2. Campaign Overview Table | All campaigns: spend, impressions, CTR, CPL/ROAS — in a single comparison table | 1 table |
| 3. Key Wins | Top 2–3 achievements with supporting data | Bullet points |
| 4. Areas of Concern | Issues identified and their root cause | Bullet points |
| 5. Creative Performance | Best and worst-performing ads with screenshots/descriptions | Images + captions |
| 6. Audience Insights | Which demographics/segments converted best | 1 table or chart |
| 7. Recommendations | Top 3 actions for next month with expected impact | Numbered list |
| 8. Next Month Plan | Proposed budget allocation and campaign changes | 1 table |

### 7.2 Setting Up Looker Studio for Automated Reporting

Looker Studio (lookerstudio.google.com) is a free Google tool that creates live, auto-updating dashboards connected to multiple data sources.

**Setup Steps:**
1. Go to `lookerstudio.google.com` → Create → Report → Add Data Source
2. Connect **Google Analytics 4** using the native GA4 connector (free)
3. Connect **Meta Ads** using Meta's official Looker Studio connector or Supermetrics (paid, ~USD 29/month)
4. Connect **TikTok Ads** using Supermetrics or Windsor.ai connector
5. Design the dashboard with these essential scorecard tiles:
   - Total Ad Spend (this month vs. last month)
   - Total Leads / Conversions
   - Average CPL / CPA
   - Overall ROAS
   - Website Sessions from Paid Social
6. Add a time-series chart: daily Spend vs. Conversions
7. Add a breakdown table: Campaign | Spend | Impressions | CTR | CPL | ROAS
8. Share the dashboard URL with the client — data updates every 24 hours automatically

**Looker Studio Advantages:**
- Client can view results anytime without requesting a manual report
- Eliminates the risk of data transcription errors in manual reports
- AI summaries can be added via Google Gemini integration in Looker Studio 2025+
- Multiple data sources in one view enables cross-platform comparison
