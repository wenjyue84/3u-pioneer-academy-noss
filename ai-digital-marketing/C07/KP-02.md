## 1. Definition and Strategic Scope of Community Management

### 1.1 What Community Management Is

Community management (Pengurusan Komuniti) is the ongoing practice of building, nurturing, moderating, and growing a brand's online audience. It is fundamentally a human relationship function that operates at scale — replacing one-to-one customer service interactions with one-to-many (and many-to-many) digital conversations.

**Community Management vs. Content Creation:**

| Dimension | Content Creation (Penciptaan Kandungan) | Community Management (Pengurusan Komuniti) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | One-to-many: brand publishes to audience | Two-way: brand responds to and engages individual followers |
| Timing | Scheduled in advance | Reactive; must respond in real time |
| Skill set | Writing, design, video production | Communication, empathy, crisis judgement |
| AI application | Generate captions, images, scripts | Draft responses, classify comments, detect crises |
| Measured by | Reach, impressions, follower growth | Engagement rate, response time, CSAT, community growth rate |
| Business outcome | Awareness and consideration | Loyalty, retention, word-of-mouth, conversion |

### 1.2 Why Community Management Matters for Malaysian Businesses

| Business Impact | Statistic / Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Higher engagement = higher organic reach | Facebook algorithm rewards posts with high early engagement by showing them to more followers for free |
| Response speed drives conversion | Businesses that respond to social media DMs within 1 hour are 60% more likely to convert the enquirer |
| Customer retention through community | Customers who receive personalised responses on social media are 71% more likely to recommend the brand |
| Brand sentiment management | Unanswered negative comments on a business page reduce conversion rate among new page visitors by up to 30% |
| Community as a research asset | Regular community engagement generates real-time customer insights at zero cost — no survey required |

## 2. Classification of Community Interactions

### 2.1 The Six Interaction Types

Every message, comment, or mention a brand receives can be classified into one of six types. Classification determines the appropriate response time, tone, and level of human escalation required.

| Type | Examples | Priority | Response Time Standard | Tone | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Purchase Inquiry (Pertanyaan Pembelian) | "How much is this?" "Do you deliver to Sabah?" "Is this available in size L?" | HIGH — potential revenue | Within 1 hour | Helpful, specific, enthusiastic | Answer question; add CTA to purchase |
| 2. Complaint (Aduan) | "I received a damaged item" "My order hasn't arrived" "The staff was rude" | CRITICAL — reputation risk | Within 30–60 minutes | Empathetic, non-defensive | Acknowledge; move to DM; apply HEARD framework |
| 3. Positive Comment / Compliment (Pujian) | "Love your service!" "Best laksa in JB!" "Will definitely come back!" | MEDIUM — engagement opportunity | Within 2–4 hours | Warm, genuine, personal | Thank specifically; invite return; share/pin if exceptional |
| 4. General Question (Soalan Umum) | "What are your opening hours?" "Do you have parking?" "Are you halal-certified?" | MEDIUM — FAQ automation candidate | Within 2 hours (or instant via chatbot) | Informative, friendly | Answer directly; add to chatbot FAQ database |
| 5. Spam / Irrelevant (Spam) | Bot comments ("Win iPhone here!"), competitor promotions, off-topic content | LOW — housekeeping | Within 24 hours (batch) | N/A — no response needed | Hide or delete; block repeat spammers |
| 6. Crisis / Viral Negative (Krisis) | Widespread criticism, shared negative post, media inquiry, food safety/injury allegation | CRITICAL — immediate | Within 15–30 minutes | Professional, calm — NO AI-only response | Escalate to human immediately; apply Level 3/4 crisis protocol |

### 2.2 Interaction Priority Matrix

```
         HIGH URGENCY
              ↑
COMPLAINT ●  PURCHASE INQUIRY ●
              │
CRISIS ●      │              POSITIVE COMMENT ●
──────────────┼──────────────────────────────────
              │              GENERAL QUESTION ●
              │
         SPAM ●
              ↓
         LOW URGENCY
(Left = High Reputation Risk, Right = Low Reputation Risk)
```

## 3. AI Tools for Community Management

### 3.1 Meta Business Suite — Built-In AI Features

Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com) is the free, centralised management platform for all Facebook and Instagram business activity. Its AI capabilities have expanded significantly in 2025–2026.

| Feature | Where to Find | How It Works | Setup Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unified Inbox | Inbox tab | Combines Facebook DMs, Instagram DMs, and all post comments in one feed; AI prioritises high-urgency items | None — available by default |
| AI Reply Suggestions | Within each message thread | AI analyses the message and suggests 2–3 possible replies; human clicks to approve or edit | Enable in Inbox settings |
| Instant Reply Automation | Inbox → Automations → Instant Reply | Auto-response sent when a DM arrives during off-hours; AI-written greeting + FAQ | Requires setup per page |
| Saved Replies Library | Inbox → Saved Replies | Store pre-written responses for common inquiries; access with "/" shortcut | Create replies manually; AI suggests improvements |
| Comment Moderation | Content settings → Keyword moderation | AI filters comments containing blocked keywords; auto-hides or deletes | Define blocked keyword list |
| Response Time Badge | Visible on Business Page | Shows "Typically responds within [X hours]" — affects trust signals | No setup required; improves with consistent response times |

**Setting Up Instant Reply (Recommended for All Malaysian Businesses):**
1. Meta Business Suite → Inbox → Automations → Instant Reply
2. Toggle ON → Click "Customise message"
3. Paste AI-generated greeting text (see prompt below)
4. Set delivery: "Send when you're away" or "Always"
5. Save → Test by sending a DM to the page from a personal account

**AI Prompt for Instant Reply Message:**
```
Write an instant WhatsApp/Facebook DM auto-reply for [Business Name],
a [business type] based in [city], Malaysia.

Requirements:
- Acknowledge their message warmly
- State operating hours: [hours]
- Tell them when they can expect a human response
- Offer one self-service option (FAQ link or WhatsApp number for urgent matters)
- Friendly and professional tone
- Maximum 100 words
- Language: [English / BM / both]
```

**ManyChat Core Features:**

## 4. Engagement Strategy with AI

### 4.1 Understanding Engagement Rate

**Engagement Rate Formula (Kadar Penglibatan):**
```
Engagement Rate = (Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) ÷ Reach × 100%
```

**Malaysian Benchmarks by Platform:**

| Platform | Excellent | Good | Average | Below Average |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram (Feed) | >5% | 3–5% | 1–3% | <1% |
| Facebook (Page) | >3% | 1.5–3% | 0.5–1.5% | <0.5% |
| TikTok (Video) | >8% | 4–8% | 2–4% | <2% |
| YouTube | >5% | 2–5% | 1–2% | <1% |

> **Note:** Engagement rate norms differ by follower count. A page with 500 followers achieving 8% engagement is normal; a page with 500,000 followers achieving 8% is exceptional.

### 4.2 AI-Driven Engagement Tactics — Implementation Guide

| Tactic | Implementation | AI Assistance | Expected Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question posts (Siaran Soalan) | Post asking followers for opinions: "Which flavour should we launch next?" | ChatGPT: generate 10 question post ideas per week by product category | +150–300% comments vs. standard posts |
| Polls (Undian) | Facebook/Instagram native polls (2 options); TikTok poll sticker | AI suggests poll topic and options based on current trends | High engagement; no negative sentiment risk |
| Caption-ending question | Every caption ends with a specific question: "Which one would you choose — A or B?" | ChatGPT: add engagement hook to every caption draft | +50–80% comment rate |
| User-Generated Content (UGC) resharing | Reshare customer photos/videos tagging the brand; ask permission first | Metricool or Meta Business Suite identifies tagged content automatically | Builds community; social proof for non-followers |
| Reply to every comment | Human or AI-assisted response to every public comment within 2–4 hours | Meta Business Suite AI suggests replies; human approves and posts | Increased reach via algorithm reward |
| Pinned positive comment | After posting, pin the most engaging comment to keep conversation visible | AI identifies the most interesting reply; pin manually | Extends post lifespan in feeds |
| Giveaway / Contest | "Like this post, follow us, and tag 2 friends to enter" — announce winner publicly | AI generates giveaway mechanics, T&Cs, and winner selection prompt | Rapid follower growth; viral potential |

## 6. Crisis Management — Negative Comment Handling

### 6.1 The Comment Crisis Spectrum

Community crises develop rapidly in the social media environment. The speed of response and the appropriateness of the response are the two critical factors that determine whether a crisis is contained or escalated.

| Level | Example | Sharing / Spread | Response Time | Who Responds | AI Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 — Individual Complaint | Single comment: "Food was bad today" | Not shared; minimal visibility | Within 2 hours | Community Manager | AI drafts HEARD response; human approves |
| Level 2 — Repeated Pattern | 5+ similar complaints in 48 hours about the same issue | Limited sharing; page regulars notice | Within 1 hour per comment | CM + Department Head | AI batch-drafts responses; human reviews each; manager investigates root cause |
| Level 3 — Viral Negative | Post shared 50–200 times; appears in competitor pages; news site picks up | Significant public visibility | Within 30 minutes | Business Owner / Director | AI provides draft for reference ONLY; human writes final response; legal review if required |
| Level 4 — Legal / Regulatory | Injury claim, food safety allegation, fraud accusation, racial/religious incident | Potential media involvement | Immediately | Owner + Legal Counsel + PR | No AI involvement; all communications reviewed by lawyer before posting |

### 6.2 HEARD Framework — Detailed Step Guide

The HEARD framework (introduced in KP-01 of this CU) is applied in detail here with specific guidance for public comment responses:

**H — HEAR (Dengar):** Demonstrate you have read and understood the complaint.
- "Thank you for bringing this to our attention..."
- "We hear you and we are genuinely sorry to read about your experience..."
- Avoid: "We're sorry you feel this way" (implies their feeling is the problem, not the incident)

**E — EMPATHISE (Berempati):** Show you understand the emotional impact.
- "We completely understand how frustrating it must be to wait 45 minutes for your order..."
- "This is not the experience we want anyone to have at [Business Name]..."
- Avoid: "But usually our service is very fast" (deflects responsibility)

**A — APOLOGISE (Minta Maaf):** Apologise for the experience without necessarily admitting legal fault.
- "We sincerely apologise that your experience fell short of the standard you deserve..."
- Avoid: "We're sorry you weren't satisfied" (conditional; sounds insincere)
- Note: In Level 3+ crises, the wording of the apology should be reviewed by legal counsel

**R — RESOLVE (Selesaikan):** Offer a concrete next step — OFFLINE.
- "Please reach us directly at [WhatsApp number] or [email] so we can make this right..."
- "Please DM us your order number and contact details — our manager will call you within 24 hours."
- NEVER offer discounts, vouchers, or refunds in a public comment — this invites other customers to complain publicly to receive the same benefit

**D — DIAGNOSE (Selidik):** Signal that the business is investigating and improving.
- "We are reviewing our [service procedure / kitchen workflow / staff training] to ensure this is addressed."
- "Your feedback will be shared directly with our team as part of our quality improvement process."
- Avoid: "This was an isolated incident" (dismisses the customer's experience as unusual)

### 6.3 Prohibited Response Actions in a Crisis

| Prohibited Action | Risk | What to Do Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delete the negative comment | Screenshot spreads faster than the original; signals cover-up mentality | Leave the comment; respond professionally using HEARD |
| Argue publicly with the customer | Screenshots go viral; perceived as confrontational and unprofessional | Acknowledge; offer to resolve offline |
| Offer a public refund or voucher | Sets precedent; encourages other customers to complain publicly for compensation | DM the customer with a resolution; never in public |
| Use AI auto-response without human review for Level 2+ | AI may generate tone-deaf, legally risky, or incorrect responses | AI drafts; human reviews and edits EVERY response for Level 2+ |
| Go silent for more than 24 hours | Perceived as ignoring the issue; existing followers interpret silence as guilt | Even an interim "We're looking into this" response shows accountability |
| Make promises you cannot keep | Future failure to deliver will compound the crisis | Only commit to what you can actually deliver |

## 7. Community Management Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### 7.1 Response Time Standards

| Channel | Interaction Type | Maximum Response Time | After-Hours Protocol |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook DM | Any message | 1 hour (business hours) | Instant Reply auto-response; human follow-up next morning |
| Instagram DM | Any message | 1 hour (business hours) | Instant Reply; human follow-up |
| Post Comment (Inquiry) | Purchase inquiry | 2 hours | Batch review twice daily; ManyChat for keyword inquiries |
| Post Comment (Complaint) | Complaint | 30–60 minutes | Emergency contact of duty CM; escalate to manager if Level 2+ |
| WhatsApp Business | Any message | 30 minutes (business hours) | ManyChat away message; chatbot handles FAQ automatically |
| Google Review | Any review | 24–48 hours | No automation — all responses human-written |

### 7.2 Approval Workflow for Sensitive Responses

Not all community management responses should be posted immediately. A tiered approval workflow prevents errors and ensures brand consistency:

| Response Type | Requires Approval From | Turnaround |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard positive comment reply | None — CM posts directly | Immediate |
| Standard purchase enquiry reply | None — CM posts directly using saved replies | Immediate |
| Complaint response (Level 1) | None — CM posts HEARD draft; self-review before posting | Within 1 hour |
| Complaint response (Level 2) | Department head or senior manager | Within 1 hour of approval request |
| Crisis response (Level 3) | Business owner or director | Within 30 minutes of escalation |
| Legal or regulatory issue (Level 4) | Legal counsel + business owner | No timeline — do not post without clearance |

### 7.3 Escalation Matrix

```
COMMUNITY MANAGER receives interaction
        ↓
Is it a COMPLAINT or NEGATIVE?
   NO → Handle per response type (inquiry, compliment, question)
   YES ↓
Level 1 (single complaint)?
   YES → CM applies HEARD; posts within 1 hour
   NO ↓
Level 2 (repeated pattern)?
   YES → CM alerts DEPARTMENT HEAD → root cause investigation → CM posts batch responses after approval
   NO ↓
Level 3 (viral / significant spread)?
   YES → CM escalates to BUSINESS OWNER immediately → Owner drafts response → Legal review if needed
   NO ↓
Level 4 (legal / regulatory)?
   YES → STOP all public responses → Contact LEGAL COUNSEL → Await clearance before any post
```

### 7.4 Human Handoff Procedure from AI/Chatbot to Live Agent

When a community management chatbot (ManyChat or WhatsApp Business API) detects a situation requiring human intervention, the handoff must be seamless to prevent customer frustration.

**Triggers for Automatic Human Handoff:**

| Trigger | Detection Method | Handoff Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer types "agent", "human", "person", "talk to someone" | Keyword trigger in ManyChat | Route to Live Chat; notify duty CM via mobile alert |
| Customer expresses anger (detected by AI sentiment) | Sentiment keyword list: "angry", "marah", "complaint", "rubbish", "scam" | AI flags and pauses automation; CM notified immediately |
| Complaint about a specific order or booking | Order number mentioned in chat | Route to fulfilment team; pull order record before responding |
| Payment or refund request | Keywords: "refund", "bayar balik", "wrong charge" | Route to accounts team; do not let chatbot engage on financial matters |
| Legal or sensitive content | Keywords: "lawyer", "saman", "polis", "media", "report" | Immediately escalate to management; stop chatbot |
| Customer has been looping in the same flow > 3 times | Flow loop counter in ManyChat | Auto-escalate with message: "Let me connect you with our team right away!" |

**Seamless Handoff Message Template (AI-generated):**
```
Hi [Name]! Thank you for your patience. I'm connecting you with [Team Member Name / 
"one of our team members"] right now to help you with this. 
They'll be with you in the next [5–15] minutes during business hours 
([hours, days]). For urgent matters, call us at [number]. 
Thank you for choosing [Business Name]! 🙏
```
