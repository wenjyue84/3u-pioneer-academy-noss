## 1. AI Chatbot Technology Overview

### 1.1 What an AI Chatbot Is

An AI chatbot (Bot Sembang AI) is a software application that simulates human conversation through a digital interface — typically WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, a website chat widget, or a mobile app. The term "AI chatbot" encompasses a range of technologies from simple automated menus to sophisticated natural language understanding systems.

**How Chatbots Fit into the Customer Journey:**
```
Customer has a question / need
        ↓
Opens WhatsApp / Facebook / Website
        ↓
Chatbot greets and presents menu options
        ↓
Customer selects option OR types a question
        ↓
Chatbot provides answer from FAQ database
        ↓
[If resolved] → Customer satisfied → Optional review request
        ↓
[If not resolved] → Human handoff → Live agent takes over
```

### 1.2 Three Types of AI Chatbots

| Type | Technology | How It Understands Input | Best For | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rule-Based (Berasaskan Peraturan) | Decision tree / keyword matching | Exact or near-exact keyword match only | Structured menus, FAQs with predictable questions | Fails when customer phrases questions unexpectedly |
| NLP-Powered (Berteraskan NLP) | Natural Language Processing; machine learning models | Understands intent behind varied phrasings ("how much?", "what's the price?", "berapa harga?" all trigger same answer) | Open-ended customer service; complex enquiry handling | Requires training data; may misunderstand ambiguous messages |
| Hybrid (Hibrid) | Rule-based structure + NLP for open-ended slots | Follows structured menu; uses NLP when customer deviates from menu | Most Malaysian SME use cases — balanced automation and flexibility | Slightly more complex to configure than rule-based alone |

### 1.3 How Transformer-Based AI Powers Modern Chatbots

Modern AI chatbots like ManyChat's AI Step (powered by GPT-4o) use transformer neural networks. Understanding the basics helps practitioners configure chatbots correctly and set realistic expectations.

**Simplified Explanation:**
1. The user sends a message: "Saya nak tanya pasal harga pakej kahwin"
2. The transformer model converts the text into mathematical vectors (embeddings)
3. The model compares the vectors against a vast training corpus to infer intent: "wedding package pricing inquiry"
4. The model retrieves the most relevant answer from the system prompt / knowledge base
5. The model generates a natural language response in the user's language

**Key Implication for Practitioners:** The quality of the chatbot's responses depends on the quality of the system prompt and FAQ database provided. Garbage in = garbage out. A well-crafted system prompt with comprehensive FAQ content produces a dramatically better chatbot than a vague one.

## 3. WhatsApp Business Policy and PDPA 2010 Compliance

### 3.1 WhatsApp Business Policy — Key Rules for Malaysian Businesses

WhatsApp Business API (which powers ManyChat, Wati, Respond.io, and other platforms) operates under WhatsApp's Business Policy. Violations can result in phone number ban and loss of all chatbot investment.

**Critical WhatsApp Business Policy Rules:**

| Rule | What It Means | What Happens If Violated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opt-in Requirement | Businesses may only send proactive messages to users who have explicitly opted in to receive messages from the business | Phone number permanently banned from WhatsApp Business API |
| Message Template Approval | All proactive messages (sent first by the business, not in reply) must use pre-approved Message Templates | Non-template messages are blocked; account suspended |
| 24-Hour Conversation Window | Free-form replies are only permitted within 24 hours of the customer's last message | After 24 hours, must use approved Template to re-engage |
| No Spam / Bulk Unsolicited Messages | Broadcasting to contacts who have not opted in constitutes spam | Immediate account suspension; no appeal |
| Prohibited Content | Cannot send adult content, weapons, drugs, gambling, or copyright-infringing material | Permanent ban |
| Commerce Policy | Products and services sold via WhatsApp must comply with WhatsApp Commerce Policy | Product listings removed; account restricted |

### 3.2 Opt-In Requirements — PDPA 2010 Alignment

Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) requires that personal data (including a customer's phone number) be processed only with the data subject's consent and only for the stated purpose.

**PDPA 2010 Key Provisions Applicable to Chatbots:**

| Provision | Requirement | Practical Application for Chatbot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Section 6 — Consent | Must obtain explicit consent before collecting or processing personal data | Opt-in checkbox or WhatsApp opt-in message before adding to chatbot list |
| Section 7 — Notice | Must inform the data subject of the purpose of data collection | State in the opt-in message: "Your data will be used to send you updates from [Business Name]" |
| Section 8 — Disclosure | Cannot share personal data with third parties without consent | Do not share chatbot subscriber list with external parties |
| Section 10 — Right to Access and Correction | Data subjects may request their data or request corrections | Provide a contact method for data access requests in privacy policy |
| Section 12 — Right to Withdraw Consent | Customers must be able to opt out at any time | Include "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" in all broadcast messages |

**PDPA-Compliant WhatsApp Opt-In Message Template:**
```
Hi! 👋 Thank you for contacting [Business Name].

To send you updates, promotions, and service notifications via WhatsApp, 
we need your permission.

By replying "YES", you agree to:
✅ Receive messages from [Business Name]
✅ Your phone number being stored in our customer system
✅ Our Privacy Policy: [link]

You may unsubscribe at any time by replying "STOP".

Reply YES to continue, or NO to opt out.
```

### 3.3 WhatsApp Message Template Approval Process

Before a business can send proactive messages via WhatsApp Business API, each message template must be submitted to Meta for approval. This process typically takes 24–48 hours.

**Template Categories:**

| Category | Use Case | Cost per Message (approx.) | Approval Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Utility | Appointment reminders, order confirmations, delivery updates | ~USD 0.004 | Easy — functional messages |
| Authentication | OTP / verification codes | ~USD 0.003 | Easy |
| Marketing | Promotions, offers, new product announcements | ~USD 0.008 | Moderate — must meet content policy |
| Service (within 24h window) | Replies to customer-initiated conversations | Free | N/A — free-form text allowed |

**Steps to Submit a Template:**
1. WhatsApp Business Manager → Message Templates → Create Template
2. Select category (Utility / Authentication / Marketing)
3. Write template with variable placeholders: `Hello {{1}}, your booking for {{2}} on {{3}} is confirmed.`
4. Submit for Meta review — await approval (24–48 hours)
5. Once approved, use the template in ManyChat, Wati, or Respond.io for proactive messages

## 4. Designing a Chatbot Conversation Flow

### 4.1 Principles of Good Chatbot Conversation Design

| Principle | Application | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear entry point (Titik Masuk Jelas) | Every conversation starts with a greeting and limited menu options | "Hi! What can I help you with today? 1) Products 2) Pricing 3) Contact Us" |
| One question per message (Satu Soalan Setiap Mesej) | Never ask two questions in the same message | BAD: "What's your name and email?" GOOD: "What's your name?" → wait → "Thank you! What's your email?" |
| Short messages (Mesej Pendek) | Maximum 3–4 lines per message on mobile screens | Long messages feel overwhelming; split into multiple sequential messages |
| Quick Reply buttons (Butang Balas Pantas) | Offer 2–4 options as buttons instead of asking open questions | Reduces fallback rate; guides customer to intended path |
| Graceful fallback (Sandaran Mesra) | When AI does not understand: offer help without making customer feel stuck | "I'm not sure I understood that 😊 Here's what I can help with: [buttons]" |
| Human handoff always available | Never trap the customer in a loop; offer human escalation at every stage | "Type AGENT anytime to speak with our team" |
| Language flexibility (Fleksibiliti Bahasa) | Ask language preference at the start or detect from first message | "Which language do you prefer? 🇲🇾 Bahasa Malaysia / 🇬🇧 English / 🇨🇳 中文" |

## 5. FAQ Database Development

### 5.1 Building a Comprehensive FAQ Database

The FAQ database (Pangkalan Data Soalan Lazim) is the knowledge foundation of the chatbot. A poorly built FAQ database results in high fallback rates, frustrated customers, and frequent human escalation. A comprehensive FAQ database allows the chatbot to handle 60–80% of all enquiries autonomously.

**Information Gathering Process:**

| Source | How to Extract FAQ Content | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical WhatsApp chats | Export chat history; identify most common recurring questions | Highest — real customer language |
| Facebook DM inbox | Review last 3 months of messages; categorise by topic | High |
| Email enquiries | Search for question words: "how", "what", "when", "where", "can I" | High |
| Staff knowledge | Interview frontline staff: "What do customers ask most often?" | High |
| Competitor FAQ pages | Review competitor websites for common industry questions | Medium |
| Google "People Also Ask" | Search your product/service type in Google; note suggested questions | Medium |
| Customer review analysis | Use ChatGPT sentiment analysis (KP-01) to identify information gaps | Medium |

### 5.2 AI-Assisted FAQ Generation Prompt

Once source materials are gathered, AI accelerates FAQ content creation:

```
I am building a WhatsApp chatbot for a [business type] called [Business Name]
in [city], Malaysia.

Step 1: Generate 25 common customer questions with professional answers.
Cover these topics: pricing, operating hours, location and parking, 
payment methods, booking / ordering process, cancellation and refund policy,
product/service quality and specifications, delivery or service area,
promotions and loyalty programme, and staff qualifications.

Step 2: For each Q&A pair:
- Write the answer in natural, conversational language (not stiff or formal)
- Keep answers under 80 words each (WhatsApp message length limit consideration)
- Where helpful, suggest if this answer should include a button option (e.g., "Book Now", "See More")

Step 3: Provide the complete FAQ in this format:
Q: [question in English]
Q_BM: [question in Bahasa Malaysia]  
Q_ZH: [question in Mandarin Chinese]
A: [answer — English]
A_BM: [answer — BM]
A_ZH: [answer — ZH]
BUTTON: [Yes/No — button label if applicable]
```

### 5.3 FAQ Categorisation and Structure

Organise FAQ content into categories before entering into the chatbot platform. This enables the main menu structure and makes future updates easier.

| Category | Typical Questions | Number of Q&A Pairs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing and Packages | "How much?", "Do you have packages?", "Is there a deposit?" | 5–8 |
| Operating Hours and Location | "When are you open?", "Where are you located?", "Is there parking?" | 3–5 |
| Booking / Ordering Process | "How do I book?", "Can I book online?", "How long in advance?" | 4–6 |
| Products / Services | "What services do you offer?", "Do you have X?", "What is included?" | 6–10 |
| Payment Methods | "Do you accept credit card?", "Can I pay via QR code?", "DuitNow accepted?" | 3–5 |
| Cancellation and Refund | "Can I cancel?", "What is the refund policy?", "Can I reschedule?" | 3–5 |
| Delivery and Service Area | "Do you deliver to X?", "How long is delivery?", "Free delivery?" | 4–6 |
| Promotions and Loyalty | "Any promotions?", "Do you have a loyalty card?", "Student discount?" | 3–5 |
| Staff / Credentials | "Are your staff certified?", "How many years of experience?" | 2–4 |
| TOTAL | | 33–54 Q&A pairs per language |

## 6. Chatbot Performance Metrics and Optimisation

### 6.1 Key Chatbot Performance KPIs

| Metric | Definition | Formula | Target (Malaysian SME) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Containment Rate (Kadar Penahanan) | % of conversations fully resolved by chatbot without human handoff | (Conversations resolved by bot ÷ Total conversations) × 100 | ≥60% |
| Response Time (Masa Tindak Balas) | Time from user message to first chatbot reply | Platform-tracked | <5 seconds (instant) |
| Fallback Rate (Kadar Sandaran) | % of messages the bot could not understand and had to fall back | (Fallback events ÷ Total messages) × 100 | <15% |
| Human Handoff Rate (Kadar Serah Manusia) | % of conversations escalated to a human agent | (Handoffs ÷ Total conversations) × 100 | <40% |
| Conversation Completion Rate | % of conversations that reach a natural conclusion (question answered or booking made) | (Completed conversations ÷ Started conversations) × 100 | >70% |
| Lead Conversion Rate | % of chatbot conversations that result in a booking, order, or qualified lead | (Bookings / Leads ÷ Total conversations) × 100 | >10% |
| Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) | Rating given by users at end of chatbot interaction (1–5 stars) | Average of all ratings | >4.0 / 5.0 |
| Average Messages per Conversation | Mean number of messages exchanged per chatbot session | Total messages ÷ Total conversations | 4–8 (fewer = efficient; more = friction) |

### 6.2 When to Escalate to a Human Agent

The chatbot must be programmed to recognise situations that exceed its capability. Failure to escalate appropriately frustrates customers and damages brand perception.

| Trigger Situation | Detection Method | Escalation Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer expresses anger or frustration | Sentiment keywords: "angry", "marah", "fed up", "ridiculous", "scam", "lawsuit" | Immediate handoff; priority alert to duty agent |
| Legal or compliance question | Keywords: "saman", "lawyer", "peguam", "sue", "report to authority", "MCMC" | Stop chatbot; alert management; do not attempt AI response |
| Custom quotation required | Keywords: "special request", "custom", "unique", "different from the menu" | Route to sales team for personalised quotation |
| Payment, billing, or refund issue | Keywords: "payment failed", "double charge", "refund", "bayar balik", "wrong amount" | Route to accounts team; retrieve transaction record |
| Specific order complaint | Order number or specific date mentioned with a complaint | Route to fulfilment; pull record before agent responds |
| Medical, safety, or technical emergency | Any message indicating immediate harm or danger | Provide emergency contacts immediately; no chatbot delay |
| Customer explicitly requests human | "I want to talk to a person", "nak cakap dengan manusia", "connect to agent" | Honour immediately; do not attempt chatbot alternatives first |
| Conversation loop detected (3+ repetitions) | Flow counter in ManyChat or Respond.io | Auto-escalate with apologetic message: "Let me connect you to our team!" |

### 6.3 Monthly Chatbot Review and Optimisation Process

The chatbot is not a "set and forget" tool — it requires monthly review to continuously improve performance and reduce fallback rates.

**Step-by-Step Monthly Review:**

**Week 1 of each month:**

1. Export conversation logs from ManyChat / Tidio / Respond.io (CSV export)
2. Run ChatGPT analysis:
   ```
   Here are [number] chatbot conversation logs from [month].
   Each conversation shows the user messages and bot responses.
   
   Please identify:
   1. TOP 5 FAQ TOPICS: What questions came up most often?
   2. TOP 3 FAILURE POINTS: Where did the bot say it didn't understand 
      or fall back to human? What was the user trying to ask?
   3. UNANSWERED QUESTION PATTERNS: What topics are customers asking 
      about that the bot has no answer for?
   4. SUCCESSFUL FLOW ANALYSIS: Which flow paths had highest completion rates?
   5. LANGUAGE DISTRIBUTION: What % of conversations were EN / BM / ZH?
   6. THREE SPECIFIC RECOMMENDATIONS to reduce the fallback rate.
   
   [Paste conversation logs — anonymise customer phone numbers first]
   ```
3. Document findings in a chatbot performance log

**Week 2 of each month:**

4. Update FAQ database: Add Q&A pairs for the top unanswered question patterns
5. Add new keyword triggers for the top failure-point phrases
6. Revise the fallback message if the current one is not guiding customers effectively
7. Update the system prompt in the AI Step with any new business information (new products, changed hours, new promotions)
8. Test all changes using ManyChat's built-in preview / test mode

**Week 3–4:**

9. Monitor the updated chatbot's fallback rate — compare to previous month
10. Track CSAT scores if the platform supports end-of-chat ratings
11. Set improvement target for next month (e.g., "Reduce fallback rate from 22% to 15%")

## 7. Human Handoff Procedures and CRM Integration

### 7.1 Seamless Handoff Protocol

A poor human handoff experience can undo the goodwill built by an effective chatbot. The transition must be seamless from the customer's perspective.

**Seamless Handoff Sequence:**

```
[Handoff triggered — customer requested human or crisis detected]
          ↓
CHATBOT SENDS TRANSITION MESSAGE:
"I'm connecting you with [Team Name] right now! 😊
Our team will be with you in approximately [X] minutes.
Business hours: [hours].
For urgent matters: [phone number]

While you wait, your query has been noted:
'[Summary of customer's issue in 1 sentence]'"
          ↓
[Notification sent to duty agent via mobile alert, email, or CRM]
          ↓
AGENT RECEIVES CONTEXT:
- Customer name (if collected)
- Summary of conversation so far
- Customer's primary need
- Urgency level (based on trigger type)
          ↓
AGENT GREETS CUSTOMER:
"Hi [Name]! This is [Agent Name] from [Business Name].
I can see you were asking about [topic]. I'm here to help!
[Continue conversation from where bot left off]"
          ↓
[Issue resolved or escalated further per crisis protocol]
```

### 7.2 CRM Integration for Complete Customer View

A Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system connected to the chatbot enables the human agent to see the complete customer history before responding — eliminating the frustrating experience of having to repeat information.

| CRM System | Chatbot Integration | Monthly Cost | Key Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HubSpot CRM (Free) | Connects with ManyChat and Tidio | Free (basic) | Contact history, deal tracking, email log |
| Zoho CRM | Connects with Wati and Respond.io | USD 14+/user | Malaysian market popular; BM support |
| Freshdesk | Native integration with Freshchat chatbot | Free–USD 18+/agent | Ticketing system; SLA tracking |
| Notion | Manual log; AI-assisted summary from chatbot | Free–USD 10/month | Suitable for very small teams |
| Google Sheets + Zapier | ManyChat → Zapier → Google Sheets | Zapier: USD 20/month | Low-tech; works for early-stage businesses |

### 7.3 After-Handoff Quality Assurance

Once a human agent resolves a conversation that was escalated from the chatbot, the interaction should be reviewed for quality assurance and chatbot improvement:

**Post-Handoff Checklist:**
- [ ] Was the customer's issue fully resolved?
- [ ] Did the chatbot correctly identify the customer's need before escalating?
- [ ] Was the escalation trigger appropriate (not too early or too late)?
- [ ] Is there a new FAQ pair that could have allowed the chatbot to handle this without escalation?
- [ ] Was the resolution time within the stated SLA?
- [ ] Should a review request be sent to this customer? (If resolved positively)
