## 3. Safety and Ethics Precautions

| # | Precaution | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Always include a human handoff option in every chatbot flow | Customers must never feel trapped with no way to reach a real person |
| 2 | Never programme the chatbot to deny being a bot if a customer sincerely asks | Disclosure of AI identity is an ethical requirement; deception erodes trust |
| 3 | Do not store customer data collected by the chatbot (name, phone, email) without a privacy notice | PDPA 2010 — personal data collection requires disclosure and consent |
| 4 | Test all flows before going live — a broken chatbot is worse than no chatbot | A looping or error-prone bot drives customers away permanently |
| 5 | Configure fallback message for unrecognised inputs — never let the bot go silent | Silent chatbots appear broken; always provide a fallback response |
| 6 | Ensure the AI step (GPT integration) has a defined scope — it must not answer off-topic questions | Liability risk if AI gives incorrect medical, legal, or financial advice |

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions

### Phase 1: Configure WhatsApp Business Profile

**Step 1: Set Up WhatsApp Business App**
1. Install **WhatsApp Business** on your smartphone
2. Register with a dedicated business phone number (separate from personal WhatsApp)
3. Go to **Settings → Business Profile** and complete:
   - Business name
   - Category (select from dropdown)
   - Description (use ChatGPT to generate a 150-character description)
   - Address
   - Email
   - Website URL (landing page from C05/KK-01)
   - Opening hours

**Step 2: Configure Automated Messages**

Go to **Settings → Business Tools → Away Message:**
```
[Generate with ChatGPT prompt:]
Write a WhatsApp Business away message for [Business Name], a [business type].
Operating hours: [hours]. Language: English. Friendly and professional.
Include: acknowledgement that we're offline + expected reply time + alternative contact.
Maximum 100 words.
```

Go to **Settings → Business Tools → Greeting Message:**
```
[Generate with ChatGPT prompt:]
Write a WhatsApp Business greeting message for [Business Name]'s first-time contacts.
Include: warm welcome + brief description of what we offer + prompt to ask their question.
Maximum 80 words. Add 1 emoji. Language: English with "Selamat datang" opening.
```

**Step 3: Set Up Quick Replies**
1. Go to **Settings → Business Tools → Quick Replies**
2. Create quick replies for the 5 most common messages:

| Shortcut | Message | When to Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/price` | Full pricing message | When customer asks for price |
| `/hours` | Operating hours + address | When customer asks when open |
| `/order` | How to order instructions | When customer wants to buy |
| `/location` | Address + Google Maps link | When customer asks location |
| `/thanks` | Thank you for your order message | After confirming orders |

### Phase 2: Build the ManyChat Chatbot Flow

**Step 4: Create ManyChat Account and Connect Channel**
1. Go to `manychat.com` → Sign up with Google account
2. Select channel: **Instagram** (for training without API verification) or **WhatsApp** (if Meta Business verified)
3. Follow the connection wizard to authorise ManyChat to manage the channel

**Step 5: Build the Main Menu Flow**

In ManyChat Flow Builder:

1. Click **+ New Flow** → Name: `Main Menu`
2. Add a **Message** block with this content:
   ```
   Hi {{first name}}! 👋 Welcome to [Business Name]!

   I'm your virtual assistant. How can I help you today?

   Please choose an option:
   ```
3. Add **Quick Reply buttons** (maximum 3 per message row, 2 rows = 6 options):
   - 📋 Our Services
   - 💰 Pricing
   - 📅 Book / Order
   - 📍 Location & Hours
   - ❓ Ask a Question
   - 👤 Talk to Our Team

4. Connect each button to a separate **sub-flow** (create one for each)

**Step 6: Build Three Branch Flows**

**Branch 1 — Services Flow:**
1. New Flow → Name: `Services Info`
2. Message block: List your top 3–5 services with brief descriptions (use ChatGPT to generate)
3. End with Quick Reply: `[Back to Menu]` → connects back to Main Menu

**Branch 2 — Pricing Flow:**
1. New Flow → Name: `Pricing Info`
2. Message block: Price list formatted clearly (generate with ChatGPT)
3. Add: "All prices are subject to change. Contact us for custom packages."
4. Quick Reply: `[Book Now]` → connects to Booking Flow; `[Back to Menu]`

**Branch 3 — Booking / Order Flow:**
1. New Flow → Name: `Booking`
2. Message: "Great! To complete your booking, I'll need a few details."
3. Add **User Input** blocks to collect:
   - Full name: "What is your full name?"
   - Date/time: "What date and time would you prefer?"
   - Service: "Which service are you interested in?"
4. Final message: "Thank you! We've noted your request. Our team will confirm within 1 hour during business hours (Mon–Sat 9am–6pm). 🙏"
5. Set up **email notification** to send collected data to the business email

**Step 7: Add AI Step for Open-Ended Questions**

For the "Ask a Question" branch:
1. New Flow → Name: `AI Assistant`
2. Add **User Input** block: "Go ahead — ask me anything about [Business Name]! 😊"
3. Add **AI Step** (requires OpenAI API key — trainer may provide for training):
   - System prompt:
     ```
     You are a helpful customer service assistant for [Business Name],
     a [business type] in [City], Malaysia.
     Only answer questions about our products, services, pricing, and policies.
     If you don't know the answer, say: "That's a great question — let me connect you
     with our team who can answer this properly!"
     Keep responses under 80 words. Use a friendly, professional tone.
     Language: match the customer's language (English or Malay).
     ```
4. After AI response: add Quick Reply `[Ask another question]` / `[Talk to our team]`

**Step 8: Configure Human Handoff**

1. New Flow → Name: `Human Handoff`
2. Message: "No problem! Let me connect you with our team 👤
   Our team is available: Mon–Sat 9am–6pm
   📞 Call us: [phone number]
   We'll reply to this chat as soon as possible!"
3. Set up **Keyword trigger:** when user types "agent", "human", "staff", "help" or "manusia" → trigger this flow
4. Optionally: send internal notification to team WhatsApp group via Zapier integration

## 7. Reflection Questions

1. During testing, which scenario was most difficult to handle correctly (T1–T8)? What did you learn about chatbot design from this failure?
2. The AI step (GPT integration) sometimes gives slightly inaccurate answers about tax rates or SST compliance. What safeguard would you put in place to prevent the chatbot from providing incorrect financial information to a client?
3. A SmartBooks client sends a message at 11pm saying "I am very stressed about my tax filing deadline tomorrow and need help immediately." The chatbot responds with the standard away message. Is this an acceptable outcome? How would you redesign the flow to better handle urgent after-hours messages?
4. Six months after launching the chatbot, you review the conversation logs and find that 45% of conversations end with the human handoff option. Is this containment rate acceptable? What does a high handoff rate tell you about the chatbot design, and what would you do to improve it?
