## Business Scenario (applies to all three tasks)

**Client:** *Mama Zaiton's Homecook Kitchen* — Johor Bahru, home-cooked Malay food delivery service via WhatsApp. Specialities: nasi lemak, rendang, kuah lodeh, bubur lambuk. Operating hours: Mon–Sat 10am–7pm. Average order value: RM 25–35. Delivery radius: 10 km from Taman Molek, JB.

## TASK 1: Design a Review Acquisition and Response System (35 marks)
**Time Allocation: 60 minutes**
**Reference: WA1 — KK-01**

Build a complete review acquisition and management system for Mama Zaiton's Kitchen using AI tools. You must:

1. Generate three review request messages — one per customer persona — using ChatGPT with the prescribed prompt for each persona. Document each prompt used. (9 marks)
   - **Persona A (English):** Young professional, 25–35, urban, first-time customer. Casual and friendly. Max 80 words. Include thank-you, reason reviews help small businesses, and Google review link placeholder `[LINK]`. 1–2 emojis acceptable.
   - **Persona B (Bahasa Malaysia):** Local community customer, 35–55. Warm, respectful, formal. Max 80 words. Use "sudi" and "kami amat menghargai." Include review link `[PAUTAN]`.
   - **Persona C (Mandarin Chinese):** Chinese-speaking customer, any age. Polite, appreciative, concise. Max 80 words. Include review link `[链接]`. 1 emoji.
   - Each message must be customised after AI generation — replace all placeholders with Mama Zaiton's Kitchen details

2. Generate five review response templates using ChatGPT, one for each of the following reviews from the dataset. Document each prompt used. (16 marks)
   - **Response 1 (Positive — Detailed):** Respond to Review #1 (Ahmad Fadzil, 5 stars, nasi lemak and delivery). Warm, grateful, professional. Max 100 words. Mention reviewer by name. Do NOT use "Thank you for your feedback."
   - **Response 2 (Positive — Brief):** Respond to Review #4 (Lee Chee Weng, 5 stars, no detailed text). Appreciative, max 40 words. Include one Malay phrase of thanks.
   - **Response 3 (Neutral):** Respond to Review #5 (Mohd Hafiz, 3 stars, food good but waited 1.5 hours). Appreciative, address the concern subtly. Invite DM for specific feedback. Max 80 words.
   - **Response 4 (Negative — HEARD):** Respond to Review #6 (Rachel Tan, 2 stars, cold food, poor packaging). Apply HEARD framework. Max 120 words. Do NOT offer public refund or discount. End with "Please DM us or call [number] so we can make this right."
   - **Response 5 (Negative — HEARD):** Respond to Review #8 (Kevin Ng, 2 stars, late delivery both times). Apply HEARD framework. Max 120 words. Same HEARD rules apply.

3. Run an AI sentiment analysis on all 10 provided reviews using the full prescribed ChatGPT prompt (paste all 10 reviews; request sentiment, main theme, keywords, overall score, top 3 positive themes, top 3 negative themes, and one operational recommendation). Submit the raw AI output and your summarised findings. (6 marks)
4. Write one operational improvement action plan (minimum 150 words) based on the most recurring complaint identified in the sentiment analysis — specify the complaint, likely root cause, specific corrective action, and one measurable KPI to verify improvement. (4 marks)

## TASK 2: Handle Community Engagement Scenarios (25 marks)
**Time Allocation: 30 minutes**
**Reference: WA2 — Integrative**

Using ChatGPT or Gemini as an AI writing assistant, produce professional written responses for the six customer interaction scenarios below. Each response must be appropriate to the channel (WhatsApp / Facebook comment / Google review public reply) and the situation type. Document the AI prompt used for each response, and show your edited final version.

**Scenario 1 — WhatsApp Inquiry (Routine):**
A new customer sends: "Hi, nak tanya boleh order untuk esok pagi? Untuk 10 orang. Ada set lunch tak?" — Respond in Bahasa Malaysia. Confirm availability, describe what is available, state order cut-off time, and provide the ordering steps. (4 marks)

**Scenario 2 — Facebook Comment (Positive Engagement):**
A customer comments on a Facebook post: "Tried Mama Zaiton's for the first time last week — the rendang was AMAZING. My husband wants to order every week now! 😍" — Respond publicly. Warm and personal. Invite them back. Tag them by name if shown. Max 60 words. (3 marks)

**Scenario 3 — WhatsApp Complaint (Service Failure):**
A customer messages: "I ordered at 12pm and it's now 2:30pm. Still haven't received my food. This is unacceptable. I want a refund." — Use the HEARD framework in a WhatsApp message. Apologise, offer to follow up, do NOT confirm a public refund policy — escalate to owner. Max 120 words. (5 marks)

**Scenario 4 — Facebook Comment (Negative, Public):**
A customer posts on the Facebook page: "Ordered twice already — BOTH times the food was cold by the time it arrived. Won't be ordering again." — Respond publicly using HEARD. Invite to DM. Do NOT argue. Do NOT offer refund publicly. Max 100 words. (5 marks)

**Scenario 5 — WhatsApp Crisis (Food Safety Concern):**
A customer messages: "My daughter had an upset stomach after eating the nasi lemak yesterday. I'm very worried. Is the food safe?" — Respond with empathy and professionalism. Do NOT admit liability. Express concern, ask clarifying questions, escalate immediately to owner. Advise seeking medical attention if needed. Max 120 words. (5 marks)

**Scenario 6 — WhatsApp Inquiry (Pricing Negotiation):**
A customer messages: "Boleh bagi discount tak kalau order untuk 20 orang? Nak order untuk majlis kami minggu depan." — Respond professionally in Bahasa Malaysia. Express appreciation, confirm you will check with the owner on bulk pricing, ask for their contact and event date to follow up. Max 80 words. (3 marks)

## TASK 3: Build a WhatsApp Business Chatbot Flow in ManyChat (40 marks)
**Time Allocation: 60 minutes**
**Reference: WA3 — KK-02**

Build a functional WhatsApp Business chatbot for Mama Zaiton's Kitchen in ManyChat (use Instagram channel if WhatsApp API is not verified). You must:

1. Configure the WhatsApp Business profile (or ManyChat channel settings): complete all business fields — name, category, description (AI-generated, max 150 chars, customised), address, hours, website/link (4 marks)
2. Configure greeting message and away message using ChatGPT-generated content; customise both to Mama Zaiton's Kitchen; set away hours for Mon–Sat outside 10am–7pm and all day Sunday (4 marks)
3. Set up 5 quick replies with shortcuts `/menu`, `/price`, `/order`, `/location`, `/thanks` — each with an appropriate AI-generated and customised message (5 marks)
4. Build the Main Menu flow with minimum 5 buttons: Our Menu, Pricing, Place Order, Location & Hours, Talk to Our Team; connect each button to its own sub-flow (6 marks)
5. Build THREE branch flows — all must be logically complete with an end action (return to menu or human handoff): (10 marks)
   - **Menu/Services Branch:** List Mama Zaiton's top 5 dishes with brief descriptions (use ChatGPT to draft, then customise); include a "Place Order" quick reply button
   - **Pricing Branch:** Display a simple price list for nasi lemak (individual), family pack, and bulk order tiers (generated with ChatGPT); include "Order Now" and "Back to Menu" buttons
   - **Booking/Order Branch:** Collect three inputs — customer name, delivery address, and items ordered — then send a confirmation message with 1-hour response time caveat
6. Add an AI step to the "Ask a Question" branch with a system prompt that scopes the bot to Mama Zaiton's menu, pricing, and ordering — include a fallback phrase for out-of-scope questions (5 marks)
7. Configure human handoff flow with keyword triggers in English ("agent", "human", "staff", "help") and Malay ("tolong", "manusia", "staf"); flow must display business hours and a phone number (3 marks)
8. Complete the 8-scenario test report; document: Test #, Input, Expected Response, Actual Response, Pass/Fail — all 8 must be tested (3 marks)
