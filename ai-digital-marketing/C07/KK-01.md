## 1. Work Activity and Outcomes

**Work Activity:** Build a complete review acquisition and response system using AI, covering review request messages (in three languages/personas), response templates, and sentiment analysis of a provided review dataset.

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Three review request messages (English, Bahasa Malaysia, Mandarin Chinese) — one per customer persona
- Five review response templates: 2 positive, 1 neutral, 2 negative — all using HEARD framework for negatives
- AI sentiment analysis of 10 provided reviews with theme classification
- One operational improvement recommendation derived from sentiment analysis
- All AI prompts documented for trainer review

## 3. Safety and Ethics Precautions

| # | Precaution | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Review request messages must be sent only to real, verified customers | Sending to non-customers constitutes solicitation fraud |
| 2 | Never instruct customers to write a specific rating or specific content | This constitutes review manipulation under Google's policies |
| 3 | Review response templates must be customised before posting — never post identical responses to multiple reviews | Google detects and flags templated responses as spam |
| 4 | Do not use generated review requests to pressure or harass customers | Consumer Protection Act 1999 — harassment in commercial context |
| 5 | Label AI-generated content clearly in your deliverables (show prompts used) | Professional transparency and assessment integrity |
| 6 | Sentiment analysis results from real customer reviews must be handled confidentially | PDPA 2010 — customer feedback is personal data |

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions

### Phase 1: Generate Multi-Persona Review Request Messages

**Step 1: Define the Three Customer Personas**

Before generating messages, define who each persona is. Use the business brief in Section 5.

| Persona | Demographics | Language | Communication Style |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Persona A | Young professional, 25–35, urban | English | Casual, friendly, brief |
| Persona B | Local community customer, 35–55 | Bahasa Malaysia | Warm, respectful, formal |
| Persona C | Chinese-speaking customer, any age | Mandarin Chinese | Polite, appreciative, concise |

**Step 2: Generate Persona A — English Review Request**

Use ChatGPT with this prompt:
```
Write a WhatsApp review request message for [Business Name], a [business type] in [City].
Target: young professional aged 25–35, English-speaking, first-time customer.
Tone: casual, friendly, modern.
Include: a thank-you, a reason why reviews help, a direct Google review link placeholder [LINK].
Add 1–2 relevant emojis. Maximum 80 words.
```

Document the prompt and output. Then customise the output:
- Replace business name placeholder
- Verify the review link format: `https://g.page/r/[PLACE_ID]/review`
- Ensure emoji use is appropriate and professional

**Step 3: Generate Persona B — Bahasa Malaysia Review Request**

```
Tulis mesej permintaan ulasan WhatsApp untuk [Nama Perniagaan], sebuah [jenis perniagaan] di [Bandar].
Sasaran: pelanggan tempatan berumur 35–55 tahun, berbahasa Melayu.
Nada: mesra, hormat, ikhlas.
Sertakan: ucapan terima kasih, sebab mengapa ulasan membantu perniagaan kecil, pautan ulasan [PAUTAN].
Maksimum 80 patah perkataan. Gunakan "sudi" dan "kami amat menghargai" untuk nada yang lebih sopan.
```

**Step 4: Generate Persona C — Mandarin Chinese Review Request**

```
为[商家名称]（位于马来西亚[城市]的[业务类型]）写一条WhatsApp好评请求消息。
目标客户：说华语的顾客，任何年龄段。
语气：礼貌、感激、简洁。
包含：感谢语、说明评价对小商家的重要性、评价链接[链接]。
最多80字。加入1个表情符号。
```

---

### Phase 2: Create Review Response Templates

**Step 5: Generate Positive Review Response (Template 1 — Detailed)**

```
Write a Google review response for [Business Name] to this positive review:
"[Paste a positive review from the dataset]"
Tone: warm, grateful, professional.
Include: thank the reviewer by name, acknowledge the specific praise, invite them to return.
Maximum 100 words. Language: English.
Do NOT use generic phrases like "Thank you for your feedback."
```

**Step 6: Generate Positive Review Response (Template 2 — Brief)**

```
Write a short Google review response for [Business Name] to this 5-star review with no text.
Tone: appreciative, friendly.
Maximum 40 words. Include a warm invitation to return.
Language: English with one Malay phrase of thanks.
```

**Step 7: Generate Negative Review Response (HEARD Framework)**

For each negative review in the dataset:
```
A customer left this negative Google review for [Business Name]:
"[Paste negative review]"

Write a public response using the HEARD framework:
H — Hear: Acknowledge their experience
E — Empathise: Show understanding
A — Apologise: Apologise for the experience (not admitting fault)
R — Resolve: Offer to resolve via DM/phone
D — Diagnose: Invite them to contact us directly

Requirements:
- Maximum 120 words
- Do NOT offer a discount or refund publicly
- End with: "Please DM us or call [number] so we can make this right."
- Language: [English / Malay]
- Tone: professional, empathetic, not defensive
```

**Step 8: Generate Neutral Review Response**

```
Write a Google review response for [Business Name] to this 3-star review:
"[Paste neutral review]"
Tone: appreciative but subtly address the implicit concern.
Invite them to give specific feedback via DM so we can improve.
Maximum 80 words. Language: English.
```

---

### Phase 3: AI Sentiment Analysis

**Step 9: Conduct Sentiment Analysis on Review Dataset**

Copy all 10 reviews from Section 5 and use this prompt:

```
Analyse the following 10 customer reviews for [Business Name].
For each review provide:
1. Sentiment: Positive / Neutral / Negative
2. Main theme (e.g., service speed, food quality, pricing, cleanliness, staff attitude)
3. Specific keywords that indicate sentiment

Then provide a summary:
- Overall sentiment score (out of 10)
- Top 3 positive themes (most mentioned)
- Top 3 negative themes (most mentioned)
- One specific operational recommendation based on the most common complaint

Reviews:
[Paste all 10 reviews]
```

**Step 10: Extract One Actionable Improvement**

From the AI sentiment analysis output, identify the single most critical operational issue and write a brief improvement action plan (150 words) explaining:
- What the recurring complaint is
- Why it is happening (likely root cause)
- What specific action the business should take
- How to verify the improvement has worked (measurable outcome)
