## Summary

Malaysia’s education system is shaped by **ethnic diversity** and colonial legacy. The government manages it through the **Ministry of Education** and **Ministry of Higher Education**, and uses **SLDN (Sistem Latihan Dual Nasional)** to strengthen **technical and vocational education**. The country aims to be a global **education hub**, using **credit transfer**, **branch campuses**, and **MQA accreditation** for quality and internationalisation.
