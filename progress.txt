### Patterns discovered
- Inline BM translations in KP files use (*italic*) format immediately after the EN term — e.g. "body thermal therapy (*terapi haba badan*)" — this is the standard pattern to match with regex
- Glossary table parser must strip bold markers (**), split on ( to ignore parenthetical notes in EN column, and skip header/separator rows
- 137 term pairs found, some inconsistencies exist in existing KP files (e.g. jangkitan silang vs canonical Kontaminasi silang for cross-contamination) — these are correctly flagged by the validator

### Gotchas
- Glossary entries with parenthetical qualifiers like "Contraindication (absolute)" and "Contraindication (relative)" both parse as "contraindication" after splitting on ( — results in last-write-wins for canonical BM term. Avoid duplicate EN root terms in the glossary or use distinct EN roots
- Very short terms (< 5 chars) should be skipped in the validator to avoid false-positive matches
- Windows: open() must use encoding='utf-8' — prd.json and .md files contain unicode characters
