## 禁忌五穴 — Five Forbidden Acupoints During Pregnancy

The following five acupoints are **absolutely contraindicated** during pregnancy at all trimesters. Stimulation of these points (needling, deep acupressure, strong tuina manipulation) may induce uterine contractions, cause fetal distress, or precipitate miscarriage/preterm labour.

| # | Point Code | Chinese Name | Pinyin | Location | Clinical Rationale | Alternative Point/Technique | Regulatory Citation |
|---|------------|-------------|--------|----------|--------------------|-----------------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **LI-4** | 合谷 | Hégǔ | Dorsum of hand, between 1st and 2nd metacarpal bones | Strong descending Qi action; historically used to induce labour ("四总穴之首"). Classical texts: "孕妇泻之，立堕" (Zhenjiu Dacheng). | LI-11 (曲池) or LI-10 (手三里) with light pressure for distal upper limb symptoms | T&CM Act 2013 Section 28; MOH Code of Ethics 2007 Article 6 |
| 2 | **SP-6** | 三阴交 | Sānyīnjiāo | Medial leg, 3 cun proximal to medial malleolus, posterior tibial border | Intersection of three Yin channels (Spleen, Liver, Kidney); strongly activates Blood and Uterus; induces strong uterine contractions. Classical texts: "三阴交孕妇犯之，胎堕" | ST-36 (足三里) for general Qi tonification; SP-9 (阴陵泉) for dampness (light pressure only) | T&CM Act 2013 Section 28; MOH Code of Ethics 2007 Article 6 |
| 3 | **GB-21** | 肩井 | Jiānjǐng | Midpoint of line connecting GV-14 (大椎) and the acromion | Strongly descends Qi and Blood; clinical association with uterine descent and threatened miscarriage. Classical texts: "妊娠犯之，胎动不安" (Yizong Jinjian) | GB-20 (风池), BL-10 (天柱), or TE-15 (天髎) for neck-shoulder tension | T&CM Act 2013 Section 28; MOH Code of Ethics 2007 Article 6 |
| 4 | **BL-32** | 次髎 | Cìliáo | Sacral region, in the 2nd posterior sacral foramen | Direct proximity to uterosacral ligaments and sacral nerve roots (S2); strong stimulation can trigger uterine contractions via sacral plexus | BL-23 (肾俞) with very light effleurage only; avoid all sacral foramen points | T&CM Act 2013 Section 28; MOH Code of Ethics 2007 Article 6 |
| 5 | **BL-33** | 中髎 | Zhōngliáo | Sacral region, in the 3rd posterior sacral foramen | Direct proximity to S3 nerve root; strong stimulation risks inducing uterine contractions; anatomical risk of pelvic floor destabilisation | BL-23 (肾俞) with very light effleurage only; avoid all sacral foramen points | T&CM Act 2013 Section 28; MOH Code of Ethics 2007 Article 6 |

## Regulatory Framework / 法规框架

### T&CM Act 2013 (Act 775)
- **Section 28** — Registered T&CM practitioners must exercise professional competence and shall not perform treatments that pose undue risk to the patient. Applying contraindicated stimulation to a pregnant patient constitutes a breach of Section 28.
- **Section 34** — Practitioners may face disciplinary action, suspension, or deregistration for breaches of professional conduct.

### MOH Code of Ethics for T&CM Practitioners 2007
- **Article 6** — Duty of care: practitioners must identify and respect absolute contraindications before commencing treatment.
- **Article 9** — Informed consent: patients must be informed of all contraindications relevant to their condition.

## Clinical Summary for Tutors / 讲师临床摘要

1. **All three trimesters**: Absolute prohibition — no exceptions regardless of pressure level or technique.
2. **Documentation**: Record client pregnancy status in consultation form before every session.
3. **Alternative technique**: When treating areas near forbidden points, use superficial effleurage (轻推法) only, redirecting to the alternative points listed above.
4. **Emergency protocol**: If a pregnant client develops sudden abdominal cramping, spotting, or reduced fetal movement after a session — stop immediately and refer to A&E.
5. **Assessment**: Students must correctly identify all five forbidden points in the Knowledge Assessment (KA) and correctly exclude them in the Performance Assessment (PA).
