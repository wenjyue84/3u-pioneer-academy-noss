# SKIM TBT 2026 — PTPK TVET Integrated Financing Scheme (Reference Summary)

> Source: `IKLAN-TAWARAN-SKIM-TBT-TAHUN-2026.pdf` (20 pages, scanned) · Official: https://smart.ptpk.gov.my/WebContent/doc/IKLAN%20TAWARAN%20SKIM%20TBT%20TAHUN%202026.pdf
> Issued by: **Perbadanan Tabung Pembangunan Kemahiran (PTPK)**, Kementerian Sumber Manusia. Signed by Ketua Eksekutif Mohd Fathullah bin Mustafa.

## What it is
**Skim Pembiayaan Program Pendidikan dan Latihan Teknikal dan Vokasional (TVET) Bersepadu Tahun 2026** — an integrated financing scheme (in the form of a financial **grant / geran bantuan kewangan**) to fund TVET skills-training programmes.

## Beneficiaries (penerima manfaat)
TVET graduates (Sijil → Bachelor's degree), existing TVET students, or eligible individuals joining an approved programme.

## Two delivery models
1. **Penempatan dan Latih (Place and Train)** — placed with an employer first, trained on the job.
2. **Latih dan Penempatan (Train and Place)** — trained first, then placed into employment.

## Priority fields (bidang keutamaan) — AI is explicitly listed
1. Robotik / **Kecerdasan Buatan (Artificial Intelligence)** — CREST
2. Keselamatan Siber (Cybersecurity) — NACSA
3. **Kecerdasan Buatan (Artificial Intelligence)** — MOSTI + KPT
4. Teknologi Elektronik dan Bahan Termaju — CREST
5. Mekanisasi Ladang
6. Automasi Pertanian dan Penternakan

> ✅ **Relevance to our course:** "AI-Powered Digital Marketing Specialist" aligns with the **Kecerdasan Buatan (Artificial Intelligence)** priority field.

## What the funding covers (domain pembiayaan)
- **Yuran latihan** (training fees)
- **Kos Sara Hidup (KSH)** — living allowance for trainees

## Training providers (Penyedia Latihan)
Receiving agencies identify, list and recommend eligible Penyedia Latihan to deliver funded programmes. Scope & criteria per agency = Lampiran 3. QR/online-application costs = Lampiran 2.

## Key dates & application
- **Application closing:** 31 July 2026, 12:00 noon
- **Portal:** https://myperkasa.ptpk.gov.my/portal/
- Contact: Sekretariat Majlis TVET Negara (MTVET)

## How we use this
- Position the AI Digital Marketing course as eligible under the AI priority field of SKIM TBT 2026.
- Funding covers fees + living allowance → strong recruitment message.
- Full JPK/COPTPA accreditation requirements apply to the long (12-month ADI / full cert) track; short 7/14-day intensive courses are governed less strictly.
