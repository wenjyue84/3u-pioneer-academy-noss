1. Protocol Comprehension 
1.1 Definition of protocol by Merriam -Webster dictionary is a system of rules that 
explain the correct conduct and procedures to be followed in formal situations. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Figure 1.1 : Example of protocol definition 
 
1.2 Definition of comprehend by Merriam -Webster dictionary is to understand 
(something such as a difficult or complex subject). 
1.3 In today’s business environment, it is vitally important to be aware of business 
protocol, corporate courtesy and good business etiq uette whilst keeping the need to 
understand and appreciate cultural differences in mind. 
1.4 A lack of awareness of an appreciation for social protocols and etiquette can 
undermine effective working relationships, as it may be construed as rudeness, 
disrespect or an intolerance of people of different cultures and persuasions. 
1.5 Protocol has been developed as a guide to assist industry and business in 
recruitment, induction and retainment of employees.  The employment offers many 
opportunities and benefits for bus inesses and be rewarding experience for both 
parties not just from an economic perspective but also from cultural and social 
viewpoints. 
1.6 It is important for an employer to understand the history and background of the 
region / person they are employing as well as gain knowledge of the appropriate 
cultural protocols in order to generate positive relationships in the community and 
between the employer and employee.

4. The Advantages Of Having A Protocol 
4.1 It enables all procedures to be undertaken in a standard manner. 
4.2        It should lead to inter -operator independence, any member of staff should 
produce same / similar results. 
4.3 It enables new staff to familiarize themselves with studies quickly. 
4.4       It enables all staff to perform  less frequently performed studies safely wi thout 
relying on memory. 
4.5 It provides a consistent presentation of data. 
4.6 It encourage confidence in results. 
4.7       Providing written instructions enables minor disciplinary / corrective actions to 
be taken for deviations from the accepted department procedure. 
4.8 It enables audit procedure. 
4.9 It prevents all / some errors.
