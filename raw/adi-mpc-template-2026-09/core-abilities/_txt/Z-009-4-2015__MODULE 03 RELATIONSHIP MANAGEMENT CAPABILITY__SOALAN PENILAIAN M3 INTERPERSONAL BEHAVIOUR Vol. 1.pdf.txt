c.  Components of Empathy 
Empathy can be divided into two major components. 
  i) Affective Empathy 
- Empathic Concern 
 Sympathy and compassion for others in response to their suffering. 
- Personal Distress 
 Self-centered feelings of discomfort and anxiety in response to another's  
 suffering.   
  ii) Cognitive Empathy 
Cognitive empathy can be subdivided into the following scales. 
- Perspective Taking 
The tendency to spontaneously adopt others' psychological perspectives. 
- Fantasy 
The tendency to identify with ﬁctional characters.
