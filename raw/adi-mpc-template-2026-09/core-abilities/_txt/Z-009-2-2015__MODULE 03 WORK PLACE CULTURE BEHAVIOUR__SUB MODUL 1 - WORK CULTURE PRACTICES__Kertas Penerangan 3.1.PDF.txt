PENERANGAN/INFORMATION :  
 
Most workplaces today employ people from diverse cultural backgrounds.  Some workers 
may have specific cultural needs or requirements which should be taken into account such 
as dress, religious practices, customs, social values, family obligations and non-verbal 
behavior. 
 
1. Custom and culture 
A custom is anything which lots of people do, and have done for a long time.  
Usually, the people come from the same country, culture, time or religion. 
The meaning of culture is similar to the meaning of custom. A custom is more about 
practices, while culture is more about ideas or a group of customs.  A custom 
overall is just similar to culture and culture means the way of life of a people. 
It is important to cultivate a positive culture right from the beginning because it is 
difficult to change behaviors and attitudes that have become the norm.

Workplace culture 
1.1 
Every workplace consists of its own culture.  Workplace culture tends to 
evolve over time, although employer can take measures to change it if 
they deem it necessary. 
1.2 
Workplace culture can be shaped by the employer and the types of policy 
and procedures they set as well as the types of people they hire.  Things 
such as dress code, job titles, behavioral policies and even the gender or 
ethnicity of the work force play roles in shaping the culture. 
1.3 
Your company culture reflects the beliefs, values and behaviors of 
everyone in your workforce.  As a result, company culture has a 
significant effect on the success of a business. 
1.4 
The culture of a workplace can play a significant role in its productivity.  
For example, if the culture is one where employees are encouraged to 
help one another, it may foster the development of teamwork.  On the 
other hand, if the culture is one where workers tend to keep to 
themselves, it may be difficult for a new employee to fit in. 
1.5 
Employees and employers should also consider cultural differences as 
possible reasons for problem or misunderstandings in the workplace.  
Treating people unfairly at work because of their cultural differences may 
be unlawful under equal opportunity laws. 
1.6 
Employers are responsible for their worker’s physical and psychological 
health and well-being and should encourage tolerance and respect for 
cultural differences in the workplace.

4. Social interaction skills

Social skills and success in life are both correlated.  People have good social 
interaction skills feel more comfortable to interact with other people.  They 
make better relationships, good friends and achieve their goals easily.  The 
most effective way to build social interaction skills are:- 
 
4.1 Extrovert 
Try to develop ability to interact with others by meeting new people, 
having conversation with them and build strong relations with them. 
4.2 Take risks 
Risk means expressing your thoughts and feelings and that must 
support your behavior.  Behaviors during interaction with others show 
your attitude towards them. 
4.3 Take feedback 
Taking feedback from people with whom you interact is best way to 
improve your social interaction skills.  Other people can give idea about 
your strength and weakness related to positive and negative side of 
your personality.

organization, business sector or economy.  The professional discipline and 
business function that oversees an organization’s human resources is called

training of current employees, employee benefits and retention.

6. Communication skills  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Figure 6.1 : Example of communication skills 
 
 
6.1 
The definition of verbal communication is how and what words are used to 
communicate with individuals.  It is the ability to communicate through words 
with the correct tone and manner.  The definition of non-verbal 
communication is which consists of facial expressions, body language and 
hand gestures. 
 
Two-way communication is essential when developing a set of excellent 
interpersonal skills. 
 
6.2 
The importance of communication skills 
So what are the specific communication skills which are most important?  To 
understand this, it may be helpful to understand basic communication 
patterns which are already occurring at work.  Pay attention to what is said 
when talking to others and pay equal attention to the person’s response.  
Don’t ignore non-verbal cues such as yawning, crossed arms and other signs 
of boredom or frustration.  These are signs that communication needs to be 
enhanced.
