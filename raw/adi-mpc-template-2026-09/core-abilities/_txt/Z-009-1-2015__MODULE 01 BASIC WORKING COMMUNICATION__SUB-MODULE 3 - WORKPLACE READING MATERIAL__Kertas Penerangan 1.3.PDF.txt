PENERANGAN/INFORMATION :  
 
1. Types Of Reading Materials 
  The ability to read and understand reading material is a necessary requirement in most  
   workplaces. Examples of reading material relevant to the workplace include procedure  
   manuals, SOP, operating instructions, research material, formal correspondence, road  
   maps, computer documents and email. This reading materials that written in easy  
   working  language may be able to assist a person to understanding the information and    
   instructions  relevant to the workplace by breaking down complex information and   
   explaining it in simple language, without the use of jargon or technical terms. 
         
 
Figure 1. Signboard. 
 
  1.1 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) 
Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) can be defined as an important aspect of a 
quality system is to work according to unambiguous Standard Operating 
Procedures (SOPs). In fact the whole process from sampling to the filing of the 
analytical result should be described by a continuous series of SOPs. The purpose

A SOP is a compulsory instruction. If deviations from this instruction are allowed, 
the conditions for these should be documented including who can give permission 
for this and what exactly the complete procedure will be. The original should rest at 
a secure place while working copies should be authenticated with stamps and/or 
signatures of authorized persons. 
 
Several categories and types of SOPs can be distinguished. The name "SOP" may 
not always be appropriate, e.g., the description of situations or other matters may 
better designated protocols, instructions or simply registration form. Also 
worksheets    belonging to an analytical procedure have to be standardized (to 
avoid jotting down readings and calculations on odd pieces of paper). The 
term standard SOP, is used in a variety of different contexts, including healthcare, 
aviation, engineering, education, industry and military. Such procedures are a set of 
step-by-step instructions to achieve a predictable, standardized, desired result often 
within the context of a longer overall process. 
 
  1.2 Working Manual  
Guide book or leaflet with instruction written working guide for employees. The 
purpose of convening a working manual to provide simple information or an 
overview of consecutive work how to do the job.  
 
Custom work done with the presentation of the information concise and simple  
language for easy to understand.  There are also some illustrative photos loaded  
to facilitate more understanding. While there is more work manual photo row give  
evidence for a description how to work.

2. Content Of Workplace Reading Materials 
 
The content of reading materials is to provide an explanation before work. They are     
roduced with simple but precise language for understanding all. 
It should be made short and simple language especially for new workers or for those  
had difficulties in reading understanding. For them, reading to learn from content area 
materials can be a struggle.
