## Key terms (cabling management — L4)

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Cabling standards | Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a for copper; fibre types and connectors for optical. Cable runs, bend radius, and separation from power cables must follow TIA/EIA or organisational standard. Non-compliance can cause signal loss, crosstalk, or failure during certification. |
| Labelling | Consistent scheme for patch panel ports, wall outlets, and assets (e.g. room-rack-port). Labels are updated when changes are made so that troubleshooting and moves/adds/changes can be done quickly. Inconsistent or missing labels increase downtime. |
| Cable records and diagrams | Rack layout, patch schedule, and floor plan showing cable routes and endpoints. Records are kept current after every change for troubleshooting and capacity planning. Outdated diagrams lead to wrong disconnects and rework. |
| Coordination | Liaison with facilities for conduits, cable trays, and access; with contractors for installation, termination, and testing. Schedules and handover criteria must be agreed and documented. Poor coordination causes delays and rework. |
