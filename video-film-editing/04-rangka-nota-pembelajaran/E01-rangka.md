# Rangka Nota Pembelajaran — CU E01: Shooting Composition (IT-072-3:2012-E01)

**NOSS:** IT-072-3:2012 Video / Film (Editing) Level 3 · **CU:** Shooting Composition (Elektif)

**Rule of thumb:** 1 WA = 1 Nota Pembelajaran. CU E01 mempunyai 5 WA → 5 Nota Pembelajaran (E01-W01 … E01-W05).

---

## WA1: Plan required shot

**Proses Kerja:** P12 langkah 1 — Rancang shot list bersama Director; sahkan komposisi shot.
**Outcome pembelajaran (CoCU):** Determine composition type; Create shooting steps or guideline.

Bab (= Related Knowledge, verbatim):
- Bab 1: Camera shot requirement (close up, wide angle)
  - Jenis shot asas (ECU, CU, MCU, MS, MLS, LS, ELS)
  - Fungsi setiap shot dalam menyampaikan naratif
- Bab 2: Composition type
  - Rule of thirds, framing, leading lines, headroom/looking room
  - Kaitan komposisi dengan emosi dan penekanan Director

---

## WA2: Identify props according to scene

**Proses Kerja:** P12 langkah 2 — Kenal pasti & susun props ikut scene.
**Outcome pembelajaran (CoCU):** Determine props for scene; Produce realistic scene made up of props.

Bab:
- Bab 1: Scene requirements
  - Membaca skrip/shooting board untuk keperluan scene
- Bab 2: Shooting style (props as background / foreground)
  - Kedalaman visual, depth of field, penempatan props
- Bab 3: Props design
  - Estetika dan kesesuaian tema/period/genre
- Bab 4: Props supply
  - Sumber (beli/sewa/buat sendiri), senarai bekalan

---

## WA3: Determine light position

**Proses Kerja:** P12 langkah 3 — Tentukan kedudukan lampu, suhu warna & mood.
**Outcome pembelajaran (CoCU):** Supply sufficient lighting.

Bab:
- Bab 1: Type of Lighting (2K / 4K, Tungsten, HMI)
- Bab 2: Basic concepts of lighting
  - Colour temperature, intensity, hard vs soft light
- Bab 3: Lighting setup (Key light, Fill Light, Back Light)
  - Three-point lighting, lighting ratio, mood lighting

---

## WA4: Execute camera work

**Proses Kerja:** P12 langkah 4 — Laksanakan camera work ikut arahan Director.
**Outcome pembelajaran (CoCU):** Create excellent shot.

Bab:
- Bab 1: Camera specification
- Bab 2: Camera operation instruction
- Bab 3: Camera and tripod assembly
- Bab 4: Assembly other camera accessories
  - Panning, tilting, eyeline match, screen direction

---

## WA5: Identify continuity shot

**Proses Kerja:** P12 langkah 5 — Kenal pasti & rakam pick-up shot untuk continuity.
**Outcome pembelajaran (CoCU):** Produce smooth storyline.

Bab:
- Bab 1: Scene requirements
- Bab 2: Sufficient pick up shot
- Bab 3: Shooting board sequence
