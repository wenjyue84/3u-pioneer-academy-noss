## WA1 — Analyse visual editing project script (IT-072-3:2012-C01-W01)

**Proses Kerja syarikat yang melindungi:** P01 — Terima job brief & analisis skrip projek
**Jam Pengetahuan (Related Knowledge):** 18 jam (Kuliah)
**Hasil Pembelajaran (dari CoCU):** Trainee dapat menyenaraikan jenis audio, mengesahkan jenis output projek, dan menentukan jenis media, selepas menganalisis skrip projek mengikut keperluan projek.

Bab (ikut Related Knowledge, tertib CoCU):
1. Types of script
2. Project concept (Storyline, Message, Theme, Genre)
3. Type of audio (Dialogue, Voice Over, Music, Ambience)
4. Type of language version (Single/Bi/Multi language)
5. Project media output (Web, Tape, Film, Hardisk, DVD/Blu-ray, 3D Stereoscopic)
6. Project duration (Script requirement, Man hour requirement, Machine availability)
7. Editing technique in theory (Continuity cut, Jump cut, Parallel cut, Cross cut, Slow cut, Fast cut, Elliptical cut, Montage, Overlapping editing, Transition, Rhythmic editing, Graphical editing, Temporal editing, Spatial editing)

## WA2 — Analyse visual editing project concept (IT-072-3:2012-C01-W02)

**Proses Kerja syarikat yang melindungi:** P01 — Terima job brief & analisis skrip projek
**Jam Pengetahuan:** 36 jam (Kuliah)
**Hasil Pembelajaran:** Trainee dapat menentukan editing technique, menilai konsep visual effect, dan menentukan background music serta sound effect, selepas menganalisis konsep projek mengikut skrip.

Bab:
1. Category of genre (Action, Adventure, Comedy, Crime, Documentary, Fantasy, Historical, Horror, Mystery, dll.)
2. Types of genre — sub-genre penuh ikut kategori (Comedy, Action, Adventure, Crime, Documentary, Fantasy, Historical, Horror, Mystery, Paranoid, Philosophical, Political, Romance, Saga, Satire, Science fiction, Slice of life, Speculative, Thriller, Urban)
3. Category of theme (Life, Society, Human nature)
4. Visual effect (VFX) elements (concept design, creation, digital art assets, animation)
5. Sound effect (Hard sound, Background sound, Foley sound, Sound design)
6. Background music (Antecedents, Incidental, Furniture, Elevator, Ambient, Foreground)
7. Visualise editing technique (sama senarai teknik seperti WA1, digunakan dalam konteks konsep kreatif)

## WA3 — Classify visual editing format and sources (IT-072-3:2012-C01-W03)

**Proses Kerja syarikat yang melindungi:** P02 — Semak format sumber & spesifikasi output projek
**Jam Pengetahuan:** 18 jam (Kuliah)
**Hasil Pembelajaran:** Trainee dapat menentukan jenis media dan menentukan frame dimension, selepas mengklasifikasikan format dan sumber editing visual.

Bab:
1. Project material (Tape, High Definition, Film, Stills/graphic, Tapeless Format, Archive)
2. Frame rate (24fps, 25fps, 50i, 29.97, 30fps, 23.976, 60i, HD Universal Timecode)
3. Scanning lines (Interlaced, Progressive)
4. Aspect ratio (4:3, 14:9, 16:9, Cinemascope, Anamorphic, Flat screen, Letter box, Pillar box, Wide screen)
5. Frame Dimension (1920x1080, 1440x1080, 1270x720, 720x576, 720x480)

## WA4 — Preview footage (IT-072-3:2012-C01-W04)

**Proses Kerja syarikat yang melindungi:** P02 — Semak format sumber & spesifikasi output projek
**Jam Pengetahuan:** 24 jam (Kuliah)
**Hasil Pembelajaran:** Trainee dapat mengonversi format footage dan menghasilkan senarai semak bahan projek, selepas melakukan pratonton footage.
