**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C06/KP(3/7) | **WA 3:** Salon Inventory | **Paper:** WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain inventory management principles including FIFO and LILO.
2. Describe inventory checking, updating and reporting.

## 1.0 Inventory Management Principles
- **FIFO (First-In, First-Out)** — Use oldest stock first. Standard for perishable salon products.
- **LILO (Last-In, Last-Out)** — Use newest stock first. Rarely used in salon context.
- **Par level / reorder point** — Minimum stock quantity that triggers reorder.
- **Safety stock** — Buffer stock to prevent stockouts.

## 2.0 Inventory Documents
| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Inventory checklist | Physical count of all items |
| Stock cards | Track individual product movements |
| Purchase orders | Request new stock |
| Delivery notes | Verify received goods |
| Invoices/bills | Financial records |

## 3.0 Inventory Process
1. Obtain inventory checklist. 2. Obtain supporting documents (invoices, bills, stationery records). 3. Conduct physical count. 4. Compare with records. 5. Investigate discrepancies. 6. Update and replenish. 7. Prepare inventory report for superior.
