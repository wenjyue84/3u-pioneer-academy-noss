# KERTAS KERJA — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KK(3/7) | WA 3: Salon Inventory | BLUE

## Objectives: Check, update, replenish and report salon inventory.
## Procedure
1. Obtain inventory checklist. 2. Gather documents (product records, invoices, bills, stationery records). 3. Conduct physical count of all salon inventory. 4. Compare physical count with stock card records. 5. Investigate discrepancies. 6. Apply FIFO rotation; check expiry dates. 7. Update stock cards. 8. Replenish items below reorder level. 9. Prepare inventory report. 10. Submit report to superior.
