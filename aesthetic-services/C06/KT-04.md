# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KT(4/7) | Ref: KP(4/7) | WA 4: Administration | PINK

**Q1:** List FOUR aspects of staff performance that should be monitored. **Q2 (MCQ):** Client grievances should be: A. Ignored B. Attended to promptly C. Referred to police D. Posted on social media **Q3 (T/F):** a) Client feedback analysis helps identify popular services. b) Performance reviews are unnecessary for experienced staff. **Q4:** What should a salon administration report contain? **Q5:** How should client consultation records be stored?

## Answers
**A1:** Punctuality, service quality, client satisfaction, sales performance. **A2:** B. **A3:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — All staff need regular reviews. **A4:** Staff performance, client records management, feedback analysis, grievances handled, recommendations. **A5:** Filed systematically, stored securely per PDPA, accessible for continuity of care.
