# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KT(3/7) | Ref: KP(3/7) | WA 3: Inventory | PINK

**Q1:** Define FIFO and explain why it is used in salons. **Q2 (MCQ):** A par level is: A. Maximum stock B. Minimum stock triggering reorder C. Total stock D. Daily usage **Q3:** List FOUR inventory documents used in a salon. **Q4 (T/F):** a) LILO is the standard rotation for perishable salon products. b) Discrepancies between physical count and records should be investigated. **Q5:** Describe the inventory process in FIVE steps.

## Answers
**A1:** First-In, First-Out: oldest stock used first. Used because salon products have expiry dates; prevents waste. **A2:** B. **A3:** Inventory checklist, stock cards, purchase orders, delivery notes, invoices. **A4:** a) FALSE — FIFO is standard. b) TRUE. **A5:** Obtain checklist → physical count → compare records → investigate discrepancies → update and report.
