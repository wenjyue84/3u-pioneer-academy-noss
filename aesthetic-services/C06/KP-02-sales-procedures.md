## Objectives
1. Describe sales procedures and techniques for salon services.
2. Explain payment processing and documentation.

## 1.0 Sales Procedures
- **Product knowledge** — Staff must know all products/services offered, benefits, prices.
- **Client needs assessment** — Identify what the client needs, not just wants.
- **Recommendation** — Suggest appropriate treatments/products based on consultation.
- **Upselling and cross-selling** — Offer complementary services or products.

## 2.0 Sale Execution
1. Determine products/services client wants.
2. Process sale: calculate cost, apply discounts if applicable.
3. Accept payment: cash, credit/debit card, e-wallet, voucher.
4. Issue official receipt.
5. Book next appointment if applicable.
6. Document sale in sales log/system.

## 3.0 Documentation
- Update appointment book.
- Record products and services sold.
- End-of-day sales reconciliation.
