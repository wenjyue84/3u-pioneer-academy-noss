# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET
**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C06/KP(4/7) | **WA 4:** Salon Administration | **Paper:** WHITE

## Objectives
1. Describe staff performance monitoring and development.
2. Explain client record management and feedback analysis.

## 1.0 Staff Performance Monitoring
- Monitor staff performance: punctuality, service quality, client satisfaction, sales performance.
- Identify staff for promotions or further training.
- Conduct regular performance reviews.
- File and store consultation records and appointment books systematically.

## 2.0 Client Feedback Analysis
- Analyse client feedback: satisfaction surveys, verbal feedback, complaints.
- Identify patterns (recurring issues, popular services).
- Attend to grievances promptly and professionally.
- Report findings to superior with improvement recommendations.

## 3.0 Administration Reporting
- Prepare salon administration report: staff performance, client records, feedback analysis, grievances handled.
- Submit to superior on scheduled basis (weekly/monthly).
