**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C06/KP(1/7) | **WA 1:** Cost Saving Practice | **Paper:** WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain salon cost management and expense control methods.
2. Describe how to determine product usage volumes and staff workload.

## 1.0 Salon Cost Saving
A supervisor must manage salon overhead costs including:
- **Product usage control** — Determine correct volume per treatment to avoid waste. Use measuring dispensers.
- **Staff workload management** — Monitor number of staff vs. clients, unproductive hours, overtime frequency.
- **Daily expenses tracking** — Petty cash, invoices, utility bills, supplier payments.
- **Checklist approach** — Use daily/weekly cost checklists covering all expense categories.

## 2.0 Proposals for Cost Saving
Based on analysis, the supervisor prepares proposals:
- Renegotiate supplier contracts for bulk discounts.
- Adjust staff schedules to minimise unproductive hours.
- Standardise product usage per treatment (e.g., 15ml oil per massage).
- Implement energy-saving measures (LED lighting, timer switches).
- Review subscription/membership costs.
