# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KT(5/7) | Ref: KP(5/7) | WA 5: Work Schedule | PINK

**Q1:** List FOUR factors to consider when preparing a staff work schedule. **Q2 (MCQ):** The completed staff schedule must be: A. Kept secret B. Submitted to superior C. Only shown to senior staff D. Destroyed weekly **Q3 (T/F):** a) Peak hours require fewer staff than off-peak. b) Staff skills should be considered in duty allocation. **Q4:** Describe the work schedule preparation process in FOUR steps. **Q5:** What employment law considerations apply to scheduling?

## Answers
**A1:** Number of staff, services available, working hours, positions/duties, staff skills, leave days. **A2:** B. **A3:** a) FALSE. b) TRUE. **A4:** Obtain template → determine staff/services/hours → allocate shifts → submit to superior. **A5:** Working hours limits, mandatory rest days, overtime regulations per Employment Act 1955.
