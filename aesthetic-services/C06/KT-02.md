# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KT(2/7) | Ref: KP(2/7) | WA 2: Sales | PINK

**Q1:** List FOUR payment modes accepted in a salon. **Q2:** Describe the sales execution process in FIVE steps. **Q3 (T/F):** a) Upselling means offering cheaper alternatives. b) An official receipt must be issued for every sale. **Q4 (MCQ):** End-of-day sales reconciliation involves: A. Counting client complaints B. Matching sales records to cash/payments C. Ordering new products D. Scheduling staff **Q5:** What documentation is required after completing a sale?

## Answers
**A1:** Cash, credit/debit card, e-wallet, voucher. **A2:** Determine products/services → calculate cost → accept payment → issue receipt → book appointment → document sale. **A3:** a) FALSE — Upselling offers complementary/enhanced services. b) TRUE. **A4:** B. **A5:** Sales log updated, appointment book updated, receipt issued.
