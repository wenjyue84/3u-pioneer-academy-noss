# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KT(1/7) | Ref: KP(1/7) | WA 1: Cost Saving | PINK

**Q1:** List THREE salon expense categories a supervisor must monitor. **Q2 (MCQ):** Standardising product volume per treatment helps: A. Increase waste B. Control costs C. Reduce quality D. Confuse staff **Q3 (T/F):** a) A supervisor should never question overtime costs. b) Measuring dispensers help control product usage. **Q4:** Describe TWO cost saving proposals a supervisor could make. **Q5:** What is the purpose of a daily cost checklist?

## Answers
**A1:** Product usage, staff workload/overtime, daily expenses (petty cash, invoices, utilities). **A2:** B. **A3:** a) FALSE. b) TRUE. **A4:** Bulk supplier discounts, adjust schedules to reduce unproductive hours, standardise product usage, energy saving measures. **A5:** Systematic tracking of all expense categories to identify areas for cost reduction.
