# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET
**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C06/KP(7/7) | **WA 7:** Salon Safety & Hygiene | **Paper:** WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain risk assessment procedures for salon operations.
2. Describe safety and hygiene monitoring responsibilities.

## 1.0 Risk Assessment
A risk assessment identifies hazards and evaluates the risk they pose.

### 1.1 Areas of Risk
| Area | Hazards |
|------|---------|
| Workplace ergonomic | Repetitive strain, poor posture, couch height |
| Services/machines | Electrical shock, burns, chemical exposure |
| Client changing room | Slippery floor, privacy breach |
| Treatment room | Equipment malfunction, product spill |
| Hygiene/waste management | Cross-contamination, improper waste disposal |

### 1.2 Risk Assessment Process
1. Obtain risk assessment checklist/form from management.
2. Inspect each area systematically.
3. Identify hazards and assess likelihood/severity.
4. Record findings on the risk assessment form.
5. Recommend corrective actions.
6. Submit report to superior.

## 2.0 Safety Monitoring
- Regular inspections (daily walkthrough, weekly detailed).
- Ensure OSHA 1994 compliance.
- Fire safety equipment checked monthly.
- First aid kit restocked.
- Emergency procedures displayed and practised.
