# KERTAS KERJA — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KK(7/7) | WA 7: Safety & Hygiene | BLUE

## Objectives: Conduct risk assessment, inspect salon areas, prepare and submit report.
## Procedure
1. Obtain risk assessment checklist and form. 2. Identify areas of risk: workplace ergonomic, services/machines, changing room, treatment room, hygiene/waste. 3. Inspect each area using the risk assessment form. 4. Identify hazards (slippery floors, electrical risks, chemical exposure, waste handling). 5. Assess likelihood and severity of each hazard. 6. Record findings. 7. Recommend corrective actions for each risk. 8. Submit risk assessment report to superior.
