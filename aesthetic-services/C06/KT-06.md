# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KT(6/7) | Ref: KP(6/7) | WA 6: In-House Training | PINK

**Q1:** Define TNA and TNI. **Q2 (MCQ):** Which is NOT a type of in-house training? A. Retraining B. Upskilling C. Customer complaints D. New staff training **Q3:** List FOUR methods to identify training needs. **Q4 (T/F):** a) Training feedback should be collected from participants. b) Training records do not need to be updated. **Q5:** Describe the in-house training process in SIX steps.

## Answers
**A1:** TNA: Systematic assessment of skills gaps across the team. TNI: Identifying specific training needs per individual staff. **A2:** C. **A3:** Observation, client feedback, performance reviews, new product/equipment introductions. **A4:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — Must be updated. **A5:** Obtain procedures → TNA/TNI → determine requirements → prepare plan → conduct training → collect feedback → record/update.
