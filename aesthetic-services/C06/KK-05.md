# KERTAS KERJA — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KK(5/7) | WA 5: Work Schedule | BLUE

## Objectives: Prepare and submit staff work schedule.
## Procedure
1. Obtain schedule format/template. 2. Determine: number of staff, services available, working hours, positions. 3. Allocate shifts ensuring coverage for peak/off-peak. 4. Consider staff skills and qualifications. 5. Account for leave and training days. 6. Prepare schedule document. 7. Submit to superior for approval. 8. Post approved schedule for staff.
