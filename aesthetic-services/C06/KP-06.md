# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET
**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C06/KP(6/7) | **WA 6:** In-House Training | **Paper:** WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain Training Need Analysis (TNA) and Training Need Identification (TNI).
2. Describe the process of conducting in-house training.

## 1.0 TNA and TNI
- **TNA (Training Need Analysis)** — Systematic assessment of skills gaps across the team.
- **TNI (Training Need Identification)** — Identifying specific training needs per individual staff member.
- Methods: observation, client feedback, performance reviews, new product introductions, regulatory changes.

## 2.0 Types of In-House Training
| Type | When Applied |
|------|-------------|
| New staff training (*latihan pekerja baru*) | Onboarding new hires |
| Retraining (*latihan semula*) | Performance gaps identified |
| Upskilling (*peningkatan kemahiran*) | New techniques, products, equipment |
| Discipline (*disiplin*) | Policy/procedure compliance |

## 3.0 Training Process
1. Obtain company training procedures. 2. Conduct TNA/TNI. 3. Determine training requirements per staff. 4. Prepare training plan/materials. 5. Conduct training sessions. 6. Collect training feedback from participants. 7. Record all training activities. 8. Update training records.
