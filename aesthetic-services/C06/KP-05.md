# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET
**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C06/KP(5/7) | **WA 5:** Work Schedule | **Paper:** WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain work scheduling principles for salon staff.
2. Describe duty allocation and schedule preparation.

## 1.0 Scheduling Principles
- Match staff numbers to expected client volume.
- Ensure all services are covered by qualified staff.
- Comply with employment law (working hours, rest days, overtime).
- Consider: staff skills, client preferences, leave/training days.

## 2.0 Schedule Preparation
1. Obtain staff schedule format/template from management.
2. Determine: number of staff, services available, working hours (opening/closing), positions/duties.
3. Allocate shifts, ensuring adequate coverage for peak and off-peak hours.
4. Post schedule for staff visibility.
5. Submit to superior for approval.
