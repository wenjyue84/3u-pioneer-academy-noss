# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-C06/KT(7/7) | Ref: KP(7/7) | WA 7: Safety & Hygiene | PINK

**Q1:** List FIVE areas of risk in a salon. **Q2 (MCQ):** OSHA 1994 governs: A. Data protection B. Environmental waste C. Occupational safety and health D. Product quality **Q3:** Describe the risk assessment process in FIVE steps. **Q4 (T/F):** a) Fire safety equipment should be checked monthly. b) Risk assessment is only needed when an accident occurs. **Q5:** What should a risk assessment report contain?

## Answers
**A1:** Workplace ergonomic, services/machines, client changing room, treatment room, hygiene/waste management. **A2:** C. **A3:** Obtain checklist → inspect areas → identify hazards → assess risk → record findings → recommend actions → submit report. **A4:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — Regular scheduled assessments required. **A5:** Areas inspected, hazards identified, risk level assessed, corrective actions recommended, date/inspector signature.
