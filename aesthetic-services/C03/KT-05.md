# KERTAS TUGASAN / ASSIGNMENT SHEET

**Paper Colour:** PINK

---

## Questions

**Q1:** List FIVE (5) after care advice points for a client following manual body massage.

**Q2 (MCQ):** Post-massage muscle soreness typically lasts:
A. 1-2 hours B. 6-12 hours C. 24-48 hours D. 5-7 days

**Q3 (T/F):**
a) Heavy exercise is recommended after massage to maximise benefits. b) Mild muscle soreness is a normal post-massage response. c) Client feedback should only be recorded if negative.

**Q4:** Name TWO (2) home care products suitable for massage clients and state their purpose.

**Q5:** List FOUR (4) items that should be recorded on the consultation card after a massage session.

---

## Answer Scheme
**A1:** Any 5: Drink plenty of water, rest if drowsy, avoid strenuous exercise 24h, mild soreness is normal (24-48h), take warm bath, avoid heavy meals, note continued discomfort for next session.
**A2:** C. 24-48 hours.
**A3:** a) FALSE — Strenuous exercise should be avoided for 24 hours. b) TRUE. c) FALSE — All feedback (positive and negative) should be recorded.
**A4:** Any 2: Body moisturiser (maintain skin softness), muscle rub/balm (residual tension), Epsom salt for bath (muscle relaxation).
**A5:** Any 4: Treatment performed, areas worked, massage medium used, client response/feedback, after care advice given, pressure used, any adverse reactions, date/signature.
