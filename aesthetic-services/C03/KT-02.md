## Questions

A. Coconut oil B. Grapeseed oil C. Sweet almond oil D. Massage cream

## Answer Scheme
**A1:** Any 5: Massage couch (client positioning), bolsters (joint support), trolley (organise supplies), towels (draping/wiping), massage oil/cream (lubricant), tissues (wiping excess), hand sanitiser (hygiene).
**A2:** B. Grapeseed oil — light, non-greasy, easily absorbed.
**A3:** a) FALSE — Therapist must have no jewellery on hands/wrists to prevent scratching. b) TRUE. c) TRUE.
**A4:** Any 3: Couch stable, no tripping hazards, adequate lighting, therapist nails short/filed, calm environment, adequate temperature.
**A5:** 1-C, 2-A, 3-B.
