## Questions

**Q1 (Matching):** Match the classical movement with its description:
| Movement | | Description |
|----------|---|-------------|
| 1. Effleurage | | A. Rhythmic striking/tapping movements |
| 2. Petrissage | | B. Deep circular movements using thumbs |
| 3. Tapotement | | C. Fine trembling transmitted through fingertips |
| 4. Vibration | | D. Long gliding strokes toward the heart |
| 5. Friction | | E. Kneading, squeezing, rolling of tissue |

**Q2:** Describe the effects of effleurage on the body. List at least FOUR (4) effects.

**Q3 (MCQ):** Which classical massage movement should be avoided over bony prominences and the kidney area?
A. Effleurage B. Petrissage C. Tapotement D. Vibration

**Q4:** Explain FOUR (4) principles of proper body mechanics for the massage therapist.

**Q5:** Describe the difference between rhythm, rate, pressure and continuity in massage.

**Q6 (T/F):**
a) Effleurage strokes should move away from the heart. b) Friction requires minimal or no massage medium. c) Hands should lose contact with the client between movements for hygiene. d) Weight transfer from the body is preferred over arm strength for applying pressure.

**Q7:** Outline the standard full-body massage flow sequence, listing at least EIGHT (8) body areas in order.

---

## Answer Scheme
**A1:** 1-D, 2-E, 3-A, 4-C, 5-B.
**A2:** Any 4: Warms tissue, spreads oil, relaxes client, improves venous return/circulation, introduces touch, links between deeper movements.
**A3:** C. Tapotement.
**A4:** Any 4: Feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent, back straight, shoulders relaxed, elbows slightly bent, wrists neutral, use body weight not arm strength, adjust couch height.
**A5:** Rhythm = regularity/pattern of movements; Rate = speed of movements; Pressure = force applied; Continuity = maintaining unbroken contact.
**A6:** a) FALSE — Towards the heart. b) TRUE — Friction works best on dry skin or minimal medium. c) FALSE — Hands should maintain continuous contact. d) TRUE.
**A7:** Example order: 1. Back, 2. Gluteals, 3. Posterior legs, 4. Feet (posterior), 5. [Turn client] Anterior legs, 6. Feet (anterior), 7. Arms/hands, 8. Abdomen, 9. Chest/decollete, 10. Neck/shoulders.
