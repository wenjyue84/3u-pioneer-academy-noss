# KERTAS TUGASAN / ASSIGNMENT SHEET

---

## Questions

**Q1 (MCQ):** Which is an absolute contraindication for manual body massage?
A. Controlled epilepsy B. Varicose veins C. Thrombosis D. Mild hypertension

**Q2:** List FOUR (4) absolute contraindications for manual body massage.

**Q3 (T/F with justification):**
a) Pregnancy is an absolute contraindication for all massage. b) The client must provide written consent before massage. c) Pressure preference should be documented on the consultation card.

**Q4:** What FIVE (5) additional items should be assessed during a massage-specific consultation beyond the general body consultation?

**Q5 (MCQ):** A client with well-controlled type 2 diabetes can receive massage with which precaution?
A. No precautions needed B. Monitor skin response, use lighter pressure C. Only massage the upper body D. Refuse treatment

---

## Answer Scheme
**A1:** C. Thrombosis.
**A2:** Any 4: Fever/acute infection, contagious skin conditions, thrombosis/DVT, cancer (without clearance), recent acute fractures/sprains, open wounds/burns, severe osteoporosis, acute inflammation/swelling.
**A3:** a) FALSE — Pregnancy is a relative contraindication; modified positioning and avoidance of abdomen with GP approval. b) TRUE. c) TRUE.
**A4:** Primary reason for massage, areas of concern/pain points, pressure preference, previous massage experience, specific muscle/joint conditions.
**A5:** B. Monitor skin response, use lighter pressure.
