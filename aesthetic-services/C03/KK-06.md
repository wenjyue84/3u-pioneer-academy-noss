# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C03/KK(6/6)
**CU:** C03 — Manual Body Massage | **WA 6:** Upkeep Work Area
**Paper Colour:** BLUE

---

## Objectives
1. Clean and sterilise massage tools and equipment.
2. Store products correctly.
3. Replenish materials and manage inventory.
4. Dispose of waste per regulations.

## Equipment: Disinfectant, cloths, replacement towels/products, stock cards, waste bins, gloves.

## Procedure
1. Remove used linen; send to laundry (60 C).
2. Wipe massage couch and face hole with disinfectant.
3. Clean bolster covers; wipe trolley.
4. Wash hands thoroughly.
5. Store massage oils in cool, dark location; check expiry dates.
6. Store essential oils in dark glass bottles away from heat.
7. Replenish: oils, towels, tissues, sanitiser, disposable supplies.
8. Apply FIFO rotation.
9. Conduct physical inventory count; update stock cards.
10. Dispose waste: tissues → general, gloves → clinical (yellow), clean empty bottles → recycling.
11. Final check: area clean, stocked, ready for next session.
