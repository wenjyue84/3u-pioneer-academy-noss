# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C03/KK(3/6)
**CU:** C03 — Manual Body Massage | **WA 3:** Client Preparation
**Paper Colour:** BLUE

---

## Objectives
1. Prepare client for massage including PPE, change and belongings.
2. Position client correctly (prone/supine).
3. Perform professional draping.

## Equipment
PPE (apron), client gown, lockable locker, slippers, antiseptic foot spray, towels (4), bolsters, hair clip.

## Procedure
1. Don PPE (apron, closed-toe shoes).
2. Usher client to change area; provide instructions.
3. Store belongings securely; provide key.
4. Sanitise client's feet.
5. Guide client to couch.
6. Position client: prone (bolster under ankles) or supine (bolster under knees, pillow under head).
7. Drape client with towels — full body covered; only expose area to be worked.
8. Secure client's hair with clip; protect from oil.
9. Confirm client comfort.
