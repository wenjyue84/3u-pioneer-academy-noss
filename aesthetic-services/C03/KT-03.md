## Questions

**Q1:** Describe the prone and supine positions. For each, state what bolster support is required.

**Q2 (MCQ):** When should side-lying position be used for massage?
A. For all back massage B. For pregnant clients C. For arm massage D. For scalp massage

**Q3:** State FOUR (4) principles of professional draping during massage.

**Q4 (T/F):**
a) Both legs may be uncovered simultaneously during massage for efficiency. b) Gloves are always mandatory during massage. c) The client should be asked permission before uncovering any body area.

**Q5:** Describe the draping technique for massaging the posterior legs.

---

## Answer Scheme
**A1:** Prone: Client face down, face in face hole, bolster under ankles. Supine: Client face up, pillow under head, bolster under knees.
**A2:** B. For pregnant clients (and hip/lateral body work).
**A3:** Any 4: Only expose area being worked, tuck towels securely, always cover genitals and breasts, ask client permission before uncovering.
**A4:** a) FALSE — Only one leg should be exposed at a time. b) FALSE — Gloves are not typically worn; only when necessary (therapist cuts, client conditions). c) TRUE.
**A5:** Client prone; fold towel to expose one leg from hip to foot, keeping other leg fully covered. Tuck towel edge securely at hip and between legs. Work on exposed leg; re-cover; repeat on other side.
