## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this work sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Prepare the consultation area to professional hygiene standards.
2. Record client profiles for massage per PDPA requirements.
3. Check massage-specific contraindications systematically.
4. Obtain informed written consent before commencing treatment.

## Safety Precautions (Langkah Keselamatan)

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Proceed only after completing a full contraindication check. Never carry out massage on a client with an absolute contraindication — refer to GP immediately.

1. Wash hands before handling any client materials or documentation.
2. Wear disposable gloves when handling client items or during physical assessment.
3. Do not use shared pens without disinfecting between clients.
4. Maintain client data confidentiality at all times (PDPA 2010).
5. Do not proceed if an absolute contraindication is identified.

## Procedure (Prosedur)

### Step 1: Personal Preparation (Persediaan Peribadi)
1.1 Wash hands thoroughly with antibacterial soap for at least 20 seconds.
1.2 Put on clean apron and don disposable gloves.
1.3 Check personal hygiene: clean uniform, hair tied back, no strong perfume, minimal jewellery.

### Step 2: Prepare Consultation Area (Persediaan Kawasan Konsultasi)
2.1 Spray quaternary ammonium compound (QAC) disinfectant on the consultation couch and trolley surfaces; allow contact time as per label (typically 60 seconds).
2.2 Wipe surfaces with a clean cloth in ONE DIRECTION only; do not go back over already-wiped areas; discard used cloth; allow to air dry completely.
2.3 Wipe clipboard surface and pen barrel with 70% isopropyl alcohol; allow to dry.
2.4 Place a fresh bed sheet on the couch; fold towels neatly at the foot and head.
2.5 Place disinfected consultation card, consent form, pen and clipboard on the trolley.
2.6 Apply hand sanitiser after setup is complete.

### Step 3: Client Reception and Preparation (Penerimaan dan Persediaan Klien)
3.1 Greet the client warmly and introduce yourself.
3.2 Escort the client to the consultation area; offer them a seat.
3.3 Explain the purpose of the consultation and what information will be recorded.
3.4 Provide the client gown/wrap and direct them to the changing area.
3.5 Allow the client privacy to change; knock before re-entering.

### Step 4: Record Client Profile (Rekod Profil Klien)
4.1 Retrieve or create the client consultation card.
4.2 Record full name, contact, date of birth, occupation.
4.3 Ask about and record: current medical conditions, recent surgery, medications.
4.4 Ask about: reason for massage, areas of concern, pressure preference, previous massage experience.
4.5 Record any known allergies (including nut-based carrier oils).

### Step 5: Check Contraindications (Semakan Kontraindikasi)
5.1 Systematically check all absolute contraindications (fever, infectious skin disease, thrombosis, cancer, recent surgery, open wounds, anticoagulant medication, acute inflammation).
5.2 Check all relative/local contraindications (varicose veins, mild hypertension, pregnancy, recent scar tissue, areas of bruising).
5.3 Record all findings clearly on the consultation card.
5.4 If absolute contraindication identified: inform client professionally, decline treatment, recommend GP referral.

### Step 6: Obtain Informed Consent (Perolehan Persetujuan Termaklum)
6.1 Explain the massage procedure, expected outcomes, and possible after-effects.
6.2 Present the consent form; allow the client time to read in full.
6.3 Answer all questions honestly.
6.4 Obtain client signature on the consent form.
6.5 Countersign as witness; record date.

### Step 7: Documentation (Dokumentasi)
7.1 Update the consultation card with all findings and recommendations.
7.2 File the signed consent form with the consultation card.
7.3 Date, sign, and initial all entries.
