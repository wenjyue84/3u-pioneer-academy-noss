## Objectives
1. Record client profiles for massage per PDPA.
2. Check massage-specific contraindications.
3. Determine client requirements and obtain consent.

## Equipment: Consultation card, consent form, pen, clipboard.

## Procedure
1. Retrieve/create client consultation card.
2. Review medical history, allergies.
3. Ask about: reason for massage, areas of concern, pressure preference, previous experience.
4. Check absolute and relative contraindications systematically.
5. Record all massage-specific findings.
6. Explain procedure, expected benefits and possible after-effects.
7. Obtain written informed consent.
8. Date and sign.
