## Objectives
1. Select and apply appropriate massage medium.
2. Perform the five classical massage movements on the body.
3. Demonstrate proper body mechanics, rhythm, rate, pressure and continuity.
4. Monitor client response and adjust technique.
5. Complete post-massage medium removal and client transition.

## Equipment
Massage medium (oil/cream), warm towels (4), dry towels (2), drinking water, client consultation card, pen.

## Safety Precautions
1. Check client comfort and pressure regularly.
2. Avoid contraindicated areas (as per consultation).
3. Never apply tapotement over bony areas, spine or kidneys.
4. Maintain hygiene; re-sanitise hands if needed.
5. Respond immediately to any sign of client distress.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Apply Massage Medium
1.1 Warm oil/cream between palms. 1.2 Apply to treatment area with light effleurage.

### Step 2: Perform Full-Body Massage (Prone — ~30 min)
2.1 **Back:** Begin with effleurage (long strokes from sacrum to shoulders). Apply petrissage (kneading) along erector spinae and trapezius. Apply tapotement (hacking, cupping) on fleshy areas. Use friction on tension knots. Close with effleurage.
2.2 **Gluteals** (if appropriate): Petrissage, tapotement.
2.3 **Posterior legs (right then left):** Effleurage from ankle to thigh. Petrissage on calf and hamstrings. Tapotement on thigh. Close with effleurage.
2.4 Re-drape as you transition between areas.

### Step 3: Turn Client (Prone to Supine)
3.1 Hold towel as screen while client turns. 3.2 Reposition bolster under knees. 3.3 Re-drape.

### Step 4: Continue Massage (Supine — ~30 min)
4.1 **Anterior legs:** Effleurage, petrissage on quadriceps and tibialis.
4.2 **Feet:** Thumb pressure on plantar surface, effleurage.
4.3 **Arms and hands:** Effleurage, petrissage on biceps/forearms. Thumb work on palms.
4.4 **Abdomen** (if appropriate): Gentle clockwise effleurage only.
4.5 **Neck and shoulders:** Effleurage, petrissage, friction on trapezius.
4.6 Close entire massage with slow, light effleurage strokes to signal completion.

### Step 5: Monitor Throughout
5.1 Ask about pressure at beginning and periodically: "Is this pressure comfortable?"
5.2 Observe facial expression, muscle guarding. 5.3 Adjust technique as needed.

### Step 6: Post-Massage Completion
6.1 Remove excess oil with warm towels. 6.2 Allow client to rest 2-3 minutes.
6.3 Assist client to sit up slowly. 6.4 Offer water.
6.5 Usher to change; return belongings.
6.6 Record: areas worked, medium used, duration, client response.
