# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C03/KK(5/6)
**CU:** C03 — Manual Body Massage | **WA 5:** Post-Massage Advice
**Paper Colour:** BLUE

---

## Objectives
1. Interpret and record client feedback.
2. Deliver after care and home care advice.
3. Schedule next appointment.

## Equipment: Consultation card, home care samples, appointment system, pen.

## Procedure
1. Ask client for feedback (how they feel, pressure, areas of discomfort).
2. Record feedback on consultation card.
3. Deliver after care advice: drink water, rest, avoid strenuous exercise 24h, expect mild soreness.
4. Recommend home care products (moisturiser, muscle balm).
5. Provide written instructions.
6. Update consultation record: treatment details, medium used, response, advice given.
7. Schedule next appointment per service plan.
8. Provide confirmation; inform of cancellation policy.
9. Return belongings; escort client out.
