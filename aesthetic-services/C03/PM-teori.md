## Instructional Plan

### Session 1: KP(1/6) + KT(1/6) — Massage Consultation and Contraindications (90 min)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | State objectives. Link to C01 consultation skills. | 10 min |
| Penyampaian | Deliver KP(1/6): Massage-specific profiling, contraindications (muscular, skeletal, circulatory), consent. | 40 min |
| Penggunaan | Complete KT(1/6). | 30 min |
| Pengesahan | Review answers. | 10 min |

### Session 2: KP(2/6) + KT(2/6) — Massage Work Area Setup and Equipment (90 min)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Introduce massage equipment. | 10 min |
| Penyampaian | Deliver KP(2/6): Massage couch setup, bolsters, towels, oils, safety, equipment testing, sterilisation. | 40 min |
| Penggunaan | Complete KT(2/6). | 30 min |
| Pengesahan | Review. | 10 min |

### Session 3: KP(3/6) + KT(3/6) — Client Preparation, Positioning and Draping (90 min)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Discuss importance of client comfort and modesty. | 10 min |
| Penyampaian | Deliver KP(3/6): PPE, change procedure, feet sanitisation, prone/supine positioning, bolster placement, professional draping techniques. | 40 min |
| Penggunaan | Complete KT(3/6). | 30 min |
| Pengesahan | Review. | 10 min |

### Session 4: KP(4/6) + KT(4/6) — Classical Massage Movements and Body Mechanics (120 min)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Introduce A&P of muscular system. | 15 min |
| Penyampaian | Deliver KP(4/6): Five classical movements (effleurage, petrissage, tapotement, vibration, friction), massage medium selection, body mechanics, rhythm, rate, pressure, continuity, massage flow sequence. | 55 min |
| Penggunaan | Complete KT(4/6). | 35 min |
| Pengesahan | Review. | 15 min |

### Session 5: KP(5/6) + KT(5/6) — Post-Massage Advice and Product Recommendation (80 min)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Discuss post-massage physiological responses. | 10 min |
| Penyampaian | Deliver KP(5/6): Client feedback, after care, home care products, consultation update, appointment scheduling. | 35 min |
| Penggunaan | Complete KT(5/6). | 25 min |
| Pengesahan | Review. | 10 min |

### Session 6: KP(6/6) + KT(6/6) — Sterilisation, Storage and Inventory (80 min)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Link to C01 sterilisation knowledge. | 10 min |
| Penyampaian | Deliver KP(6/6): Cleaning, sterilisation, storage, product replacement, inventory, waste disposal. | 35 min |
| Penggunaan | Complete KT(6/6). | 25 min |
| Pengesahan | Review. Summarise CU. | 10 min |
