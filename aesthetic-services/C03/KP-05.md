---

## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Explain how to interpret and record client feedback after massage.
2. Describe after care advice specific to manual body massage.
3. Explain home care product recommendations for massage clients.

---

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Advise the client to seek medical attention if they experience unusual pain, swelling, or discomfort lasting more than 48 hours after treatment. Do not diagnose — refer to a qualified medical practitioner.

## 1.0 Client Feedback Interpretation

After massage, ask the client:
- How do they feel? (relaxed, refreshed, sore)
- Was the pressure appropriate?
- Were there any areas of discomfort?
- Do they have any questions?

Record feedback on consultation card. Common post-massage responses:
- Feeling relaxed and light
- Mild muscle soreness (normal; lasts 24-48 hours)
- Increased thirst
- Emotional release (occasional)
- Improved range of motion

---

## 2.0 After Care Advice

| Advice | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| Drink plenty of water (8+ glasses) | Flush metabolic waste released from tissues |
| Rest if feeling drowsy | Allow body to heal and process |
| Avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours | Muscles need recovery time |
| Mild muscle soreness is normal (24-48 hours) | Tissue response to deep work |
| Take a warm (not hot) bath if desired | Soothes muscles without overstimulating |
| Avoid heavy meals immediately after | Body is in relaxation/recovery mode |
| Note any areas of continued discomfort | Report at next session |

---

## 3.0 Home Care Product Advice

| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Body moisturiser | Maintain skin softness from massage oil benefits |
| Muscle rub/balm (*balsam otot*) | For areas of residual tension |
| Epsom salt (for bath) | Magnesium absorption; muscle relaxation |
| Stretching exercises | Maintain flexibility gained from massage |

---

## 4.0 Recording and Scheduling

- Update consultation card with: treatment performed, areas worked, medium used, client response, after care advice given.
- Schedule next appointment based on service plan.
- Provide appointment confirmation.
