# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C03/KP(6/6)
**Competency Unit:** C03 — Manual Body Massage
**Work Activity 6:** Upkeep Manual Body Massage Work Area
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Describe cleaning and sterilisation of massage tools and equipment.
2. Explain proper storage of tools, equipment, materials and products.
3. Describe product replenishment and inventory management.
4. Explain waste disposal per local authority requirements.

---

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Used massage oils and creams are considered contaminated once decanted. **Never return used product to the original container** — this causes cross-contamination. Dispose of unused product from a session's decanted portion as general waste.

## 1.0 Cleaning and Sterilisation

| Item | Method |
|------|--------|
| Massage couch | Wipe with disinfectant; replace linen |
| Face hole/cradle | Disinfect after each client |
| Bolsters/cushions | Wipe covers; launder removable covers weekly |
| Towels | Launder at 60 C minimum; store in closed cupboard |
| Oil/cream containers | Wipe exterior; never return used product to container |
| Therapist hands | Wash with antibacterial soap after each client |

---

## 2.0 Storage

- Store tools and equipment at designated locations.
- Massage oils stored in cool, dark place (prevent rancidity).
- Essential oils stored in dark glass bottles away from heat.
- Towels in closed linen cupboard.
- Couch folded or covered when not in use.

---

## 3.0 Replenishment and Inventory

- Replenish massage oils/creams, towels, tissues, sanitiser.
- Check expiry dates on all massage products.
- Apply FIFO rotation.
- Update stock cards; submit inventory report.

---

## 4.0 Waste Disposal

- Used tissues/cotton — general waste.
- Used disposable gloves — clinical waste (yellow bag).
- Empty oil bottles — recyclable if cleaned.
- Follow salon waste segregation policy.
