# PELAN MENGAJAR AMALI / PRACTICAL LESSON PLAN

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C03
**CU:** C03 — Manual Body Massage | **Practical Duration:** 24 hours | **Work Sheets:** 6
**Paper Colour:** YELLOW

---

## Instructional Plan

### Session 1: KK(1/6) — Massage Consultation (3 hrs)
Demonstrate and practise massage-specific consultation, contraindication checking, consent.

### Session 2: KK(2/6) — Work Area Setup (3 hrs)
Demonstrate and practise couch setup, oil selection, safety checks.

### Session 3: KK(3/6) — Client Preparation (3 hrs)
Demonstrate and practise positioning (prone, supine, side-lying), draping techniques.

### Session 4-5: KK(4/6) — Manual Body Massage (8 hrs)
Demonstrate each classical movement individually, then full-body massage flow. Trainees practise on models/peers. Focus on body mechanics, rhythm, pressure, continuity.

### Session 6: KK(5/6) — Post-Massage Advice (3 hrs)
Role-play feedback interpretation, after care delivery, recording, scheduling.

### Session 7: KK(6/6) — Work Area Upkeep (4 hrs)
Practise cleaning, sterilisation, oil storage, inventory, waste disposal.

Each session follows the 4-step method: Persediaan, Penyampaian, Penggunaan, Pengesahan.
