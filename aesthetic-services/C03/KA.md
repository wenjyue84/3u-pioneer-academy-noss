### Section B
**11.** FALSE — Friction requires minimal or no medium. **12.** TRUE. **13.** FALSE — Knees should be slightly bent. **14.** FALSE — Consent needed for every session. **15.** TRUE.

### Section C
**16.** 3 marks per movement: name, technique description, effects. **17.** 1 mark each: wide stance, bent knees, straight back, relaxed shoulders, body weight transfer. **18.** Start effleurage (warm tissue), petrissage on trapezius/rhomboids (release tension), friction on specific knots (break adhesions), tapotement if desired (stimulate), close effleurage (calm). Justify each choice.

### Section D
**19a.** Varicose veins (left calf): Relative. Avoid direct pressure on affected area; massage above/below; use light strokes over area. **19b.** Sweet almond oil — light, hypoallergenic, good slip for relaxation massage. **19c.** Standard flow but avoid deep pressure/tapotement on left calf; additional focus on neck/shoulders with petrissage and friction. **19d.** Any 5: drink water, rest, avoid exercise 24h, expect mild soreness, warm bath, avoid heavy meals, note continued discomfort. **19e.** Treatment performed, areas worked (including modification for varicose veins), medium used, client response, pressure used, after care given, date/signature.
