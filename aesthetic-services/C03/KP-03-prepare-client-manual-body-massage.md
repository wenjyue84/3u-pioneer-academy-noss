## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Explain correct PPE usage for massage services.
2. Describe client positioning techniques (prone and supine).
3. Explain professional draping methods for body massage.

---

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Maintain professional draping standards at ALL times. Client dignity and privacy must be upheld throughout the session. Only expose the specific body area being treated. Any breach of draping standards constitutes professional misconduct and a violation of client rights under the Consumer Protection Act 1999.

## 1.0 PPE for Massage

| PPE | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| Apron | Protect uniform from oils/creams |
| Disposable gloves (if required) | Open cuts on therapist hands; client skin conditions |
| Closed-toe shoes | Safety and hygiene |

Note: Gloves are not typically worn during massage as they reduce tactile sensitivity. They are used only when necessary.

---

## 2.0 Client Change and Preparation

1. Usher client to change area; provide service attire or instruct to undress to comfort level.
2. Store belongings in lockable locker.
3. Sanitise client's feet.
4. Guide client to massage couch.

---

## 3.0 Client Positioning

### 3.1 Prone Position (Kedudukan Meniarap)
- Client lies face down.
- Face in face hole/cradle (ensure comfort).
- Arms at sides or hanging off couch.
- Bolster under ankles to relieve lower back pressure.
- Used for: back, legs (posterior), gluteals, shoulders.

### 3.2 Supine Position (Kedudukan Menerlentang)
- Client lies face up.
- Small pillow/bolster under head.
- Bolster under knees to relieve lower back.
- Arms at sides.
- Used for: legs (anterior), abdomen, arms, chest, neck.

### 3.3 Side-Lying Position (Kedudukan Sisi)
- Client lies on one side.
- Pillow between knees.
- Used for: pregnant clients, hip/lateral body work.

---

## 4.0 Professional Draping Techniques

### 4.1 Principles
- Only expose the body area being massaged.
- Tuck towels securely so they do not slip during movement.
- Always maintain genital and breast coverage.
- Ask client permission before uncovering any area.

### 4.2 Draping Methods
| Area Being Worked | Draping Technique |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Back | Fold towel down to waist; tuck at sides |
| Legs (posterior) | Fold towel to expose one leg; other leg covered |
| Legs (anterior) | Same technique, supine position |
| Arms | Unfold side of towel to expose arm |
| Abdomen | Fold chest drape up, hip drape down; maintain modesty |

---

## 5.0 Hair Draping
- Secure client's hair with clip or band.
- Protect from oil contact with a towel or headband.
