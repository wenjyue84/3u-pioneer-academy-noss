## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Explain the consultation process specific to manual body massage.
2. Identify contraindications for manual body massage.
3. Describe the informed consent requirements for massage services.

---

## 1.0 Massage Consultation

Building on C01 body consultation, the massage consultation additionally determines:
- Client's primary reason for massage (relaxation, pain relief, circulation improvement, muscle tension release, cellulite reduction)
- Areas of concern / pain points
- Pressure preference (light, medium, firm)
- Previous massage experience and preferences
- Specific muscle or joint conditions

---

## 2.0 Contraindications for Manual Body Massage

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** NEVER proceed with massage if any absolute contraindication is present. For relative contraindications, obtain written GP/specialist clearance where stated and document on the consultation card before commencing treatment.

Contraindications are classified as **systemic** (whole-body; massage must not proceed) or **local** (specific area; massage may proceed but must avoid the site).

### 2.1 Absolute Contraindications (Systemic — No Treatment)
| Condition | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Fever / acute infection (*demam / jangkitan akut*) | Risk of spreading infection; body under systemic stress |
| Contagious skin conditions (*penyakit kulit berjangkit*) | Cross-contamination risk to therapist and other clients |
| Thrombosis / DVT (*trombosis / trombosis urat dalam*) | Risk of dislodging clot → pulmonary embolism |
| Cancer / malignancy (without oncologist clearance) | Risk of aiding metastatic spread via lymph stimulation |
| Recent fractures / sprains (acute stage < 72 hrs) | Risk of further tissue damage |
| Severe osteoporosis (*osteoporosis teruk*) | Pathological fracture risk under pressure |
| Acute inflammation / swelling (*keradangan akut*) | Massage increases prostaglandin release; exacerbates condition |
| Alcohol or drug intoxication (*mabuk alkohol / dadah*) | Impaired pain perception; consent not valid |
| Anti-coagulant medication (e.g. warfarin, heparin) | Elevated bruising and internal bleeding risk |
| Acute mental health crisis / psychosis | Inability to give valid consent; unpredictable response |

### 2.2 Local Contraindications (Avoid Area Only)
| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Open wounds / burns | Do not massage over affected area |
| Varicose veins (*varikos vena*) | Avoid direct pressure; work proximal and distal only |
| Undiagnosed lumps / swellings | Avoid until medically cleared |
| Bruising (*lebam*) | Avoid bruised area; work surrounding tissue gently |
| Recent scar tissue (< 6 weeks) | Avoid until scar is fully closed |

### 2.3 Relative Contraindications (Proceed with Caution)
| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Pregnancy (*hamil*) | Avoid abdomen in first trimester; use side-lying position; GP approval required |
| Diabetes (*kencing manis*) | Monitor skin response; reduce pressure; check for neuropathy |
| Epilepsy (controlled) | Proceed with caution; avoid tapotement; have emergency plan |
| High/low blood pressure (*tekanan darah tinggi/rendah*) | Monitor; slow gentle movements; avoid sudden position changes |
| Skin conditions (eczema, psoriasis) | Avoid affected areas; use hypoallergenic medium |
| Recent surgery (> 6 weeks post-op) | Avoid surgical area; require GP clearance |

## 3.0 Informed Consent

The consent process must include:
- Explanation of the massage procedure and areas to be treated
- Expected benefits and possible after-effects (soreness, increased urination)
- Client's right to stop treatment at any time
- Confirmation of pressure preference
- Written consent with signature

---

## 4.0 Recording Consultation Findings

Update client consultation card with:
- Massage-specific concerns and goals
- Pressure preference noted
- Contraindications reviewed
- Areas to avoid
- Consent obtained (date and signature)
