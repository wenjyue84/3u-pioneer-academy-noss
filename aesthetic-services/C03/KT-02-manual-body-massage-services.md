## Questions

**Q1:** List FIVE (5) tools/equipment needed for manual body massage and state the purpose of each.

**Q2 (MCQ):** Which massage medium is most suitable for a client with oily skin?
A. Coconut oil B. Grapeseed oil C. Sweet almond oil D. Massage cream

**Q3 (T/F):**
a) The therapist should wear rings during massage for better grip. b) Room temperature should be maintained at 24-26 C. c) Massage couch height should be adjusted so the therapist can work with a straight back.

**Q4:** State THREE (3) safety precautions for a massage work area.

**Q5 (Matching):**
| Medium | | Property |
|--------|---|---------|
| 1. Sweet almond oil | | A. Rich, moisturising, for dry skin |
| 2. Coconut oil | | B. Controlled friction, for deep tissue |
| 3. Massage cream | | C. Light, hypoallergenic, for most skin types |

---

## Answer Scheme
**A1:** Any 5: Massage couch (client positioning), bolsters (joint support), trolley (organise supplies), towels (draping/wiping), massage oil/cream (lubricant), tissues (wiping excess), hand sanitiser (hygiene).
**A2:** B. Grapeseed oil — light, non-greasy, easily absorbed.
**A3:** a) FALSE — Therapist must have no jewellery on hands/wrists to prevent scratching. b) TRUE. c) TRUE.
**A4:** Any 3: Couch stable, no tripping hazards, adequate lighting, therapist nails short/filed, calm environment, adequate temperature.
**A5:** 1-C, 2-A, 3-B.
