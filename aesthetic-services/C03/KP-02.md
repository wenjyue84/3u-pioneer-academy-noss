# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C03/KP(2/6)
**Competency Unit:** C03 — Manual Body Massage
**Work Activity 2:** Prepare Manual Body Massage Work Area
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Describe the tools, equipment and materials required for manual body massage.
2. Explain safety precautions for the massage work area.
3. Describe equipment testing and sterilisation procedures.

---

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Always conduct a patch test for massage oils and creams if a new product is being used, or if the client reports known allergies. Carrier oils containing nut derivatives (e.g. sweet almond, grapeseed) are contraindicated for nut allergy sufferers. Record all allergy information on the consultation card before use.

## 1.0 Tools, Equipment and Materials

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Massage couch (*katil urutan*) | Client positioning; adjustable height preferred |
| Bolsters / cushions (*sokongan*) | Support under knees (supine) or ankles (prone) |
| Trolley | Organise oils, creams, towels |
| Towels (large and small) | Draping, wiping, warmth |
| Massage oil / cream (*minyak/krim urutan*) | Lubricant for massage movements |
| Tissue / cotton pads | Wiping excess oil |
| Hand sanitiser | Therapist hand hygiene |
| Essential oils (optional) | Aromatherapy enhancement |
| Heat pack (optional) | Pre-warming muscles |

### 1.1 Massage Medium Selection
| Medium | Properties | Suitable For |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Sweet almond oil (*minyak badam manis*) | Light, good slip, hypoallergenic | Most skin types; general massage |
| Grapeseed oil (*minyak biji anggur*) | Light, non-greasy, easily absorbed | Oily skin; shorter friction massage |
| Coconut oil (*minyak kelapa*) | Rich, moisturising | Dry skin; longer massage sessions |
| Massage cream | Controlled friction, less slip | Deep tissue work; larger body areas |
| Massage lotion | Medium slip, absorbs well | Combination skin |

---

## 2.0 Safety Precautions
- Couch must be stable; check legs, locks, and fabric condition.
- Ensure no tripping hazards (cords, rugs).
- Adequate lighting (adjustable; dim for relaxation).
- Room temperature 24-26 C.
- Calm environment (soft music optional; no loud interruptions).
- Therapist must have short, filed nails; no jewellery on hands/wrists.

---

## 3.0 Equipment Testing and Sterilisation
- Test couch adjustments (height, face hole, back angle).
- Check bolster integrity (no tears, clean covers).
- Sterilise reusable tools per standard protocols.
- Launder all towels at 60 C minimum.
- Disinfect couch surface between clients.
