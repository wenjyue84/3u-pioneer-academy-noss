## Questions

**Q1:** Describe the cleaning method for a massage couch between clients.

**Q2 (T/F):**
a) Used massage oil can be returned to the container if not contaminated. b) Massage oils should be stored in a cool, dark place. c) Empty oil bottles should be placed in clinical waste bags.

**Q3:** List FOUR (4) items that must be replenished after a massage session.

**Q4 (MCQ):** Essential oils should be stored in:
A. Clear glass bottles in sunlight B. Dark glass bottles away from heat C. Plastic containers in the refrigerator D. Any container is acceptable

**Q5:** Describe the correct waste disposal for: a) used tissues, b) used disposable gloves, c) empty product bottles.

---

## Answer Scheme
**A1:** Wipe entire surface with approved disinfectant; replace linen with fresh sheets/towels; ensure face hole cleaned.
**A2:** a) FALSE — Used product must never be returned to the container. b) TRUE. c) FALSE — Empty clean bottles go in recycling, not clinical waste.
**A3:** Any 4: Massage oils/creams, clean towels, tissues, sanitiser, fresh linen, disposable gloves.
**A4:** B. Dark glass bottles away from heat.
**A5:** a) General waste bin. b) Yellow clinical waste bag. c) Recycling bin (if cleaned).
