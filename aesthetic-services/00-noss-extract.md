## 3. Competency Profile (CP)

### CU1: Body Consultation (S960-002-3:2020-C01)

**CU Descriptor:** Body Consultation describes the competency of analysing body posture, specific figure faults, body types, skin and muscular conditions, adipose and cellulite conditions, measuring of height and weight, calculation of body mass index (BMI), recording of consultation information on body consultation card/form, designing and recommending a complete salon service plan and home care.

**Competent person should be able to:** Prepare body consultation work area, prepare client for body consultation, carry out body consultation, carry out post body consultation advice and upkeep body consultation work area.

**Outcomes:** Body consultation and body posture analysed, specific figure faults and body types identified, skin, muscular, adipose and cellulite conditions analysed, height and weight measuring techniques adhered, body mass index (BMI) calculated, methods of recording body consultation information followed, salon service plan and home care designed and recommended.

| WA | Work Activity | Performance Criteria |
|----|---------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Prepare body consultation work area | 1.1 Service area hygiene and safety practices assured. 1.2 Tools, equipment and materials identified and selected. 1.3 Tools, equipment and materials arranged and set up. |
| 2 | Prepare client for body consultation | 2.1 Client requirements identified. 2.2 Client personal belongings kept safely. 2.3 Client ushered to change into service attire. |
| 3 | Carry out body consultation | 3.1 Client personal profile (name, age, gender, occupation, medical history) recorded per Data Protection Act. 3.2 Client contraindications (health condition, allergies, medical history) checked. 3.3 Body types and posture analysed. 3.4 Specific figure faults identified. 3.5 Skin, muscular, adipose and cellulite conditions analysed. 3.6 Height and weight measurements taken. 3.7 Body mass index (BMI) calculated. 3.8 Methods of recording body consultation information adhered. 3.9 Salon service plan designed. 3.10 Salon service plan recommended. 3.11 Home care recommended. |
| 4 | Carry out post body consultation advice | 4.1 After care advised. 4.2 Home care advised. 4.3 Body consultation post advised recorded. 4.4 Client next service appointment scheduled. |
| 5 | Upkeep body consultation work area | 5.1 Tools and equipment sterilised, sanitised and stored. 5.2 Materials and products replenished. 5.3 Stock inventory updated and completed. 5.4 Waste identified and disposed per local authority requirements. |
