Describe the recommended daily home care routine for a body consultation client. Include morning and evening steps.

## Answer Scheme (Skema Jawapan)

### Answer 1
**C. 8 glasses daily**

### Answer 2
Any THREE of the following for Grade 2 cellulite:
1. Dry body brushing towards the heart to stimulate circulation
2. Increased water intake to aid detoxification
3. Reduce caffeine and alcohol consumption
4. Regular cardiovascular exercise (e.g., walking, swimming)
5. Apply anti-cellulite cream as recommended
6. Body exfoliation 1-2 times per week
7. Reduce intake of processed foods and excessive salt

### Answer 3
a) **FALSE** — Written instructions should be provided in addition to verbal advice so the client can refer to them at home.
b) **FALSE** — Treatment frequency is based on the service plan, client availability, therapist availability, and seasonal considerations.
c) **TRUE** — A reminder 24-48 hours before the appointment helps reduce no-shows and allows time for rescheduling.
d) **TRUE** — All post-consultation advice must be documented on the client's consultation card for continuity and duty of care.
e) **FALSE** — Anti-cellulite cream is typically applied in the evening routine; the morning routine focuses on moisturising and sun protection.

### Answer 4
Four reasons for recording post-consultation advice:
1. Creates a reference for subsequent visits — therapist can review what was previously recommended.
2. Demonstrates duty of care — evidence that proper advice was given.
3. Enables tracking of client compliance — identify if the client is following advice.
4. Supports continuity — if the client sees a different therapist, the new therapist can review previous advice.

### Answer 5
1-C, 2-D, 3-A, 4-E, 5-B

### Answer 6
**Morning routine:**
1. Cleanse body (shower)
2. Apply body moisturiser to hydrate skin
3. Apply sunscreen (SPF 30+) on exposed body areas

**Evening routine:**
1. Cleanse body (shower)
2. Apply treatment product as recommended (e.g., anti-cellulite cream, firming serum)

**Weekly additions:**
- Exfoliate 1-2 times per week using body scrub
- Dry body brush before shower (optional, recommended for cellulite)
