## Instructions (Arahan)

## Questions (Soalan)

### Question 1 — Multiple Choice (Soalan Aneka Pilihan)

The Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) in Malaysia requires salons to:

A. Share client data freely with business partners for marketing
B. Obtain client consent before collecting personal data
C. Store client records for a minimum of 20 years
D. Only collect data from returning clients

### Question 2 — Short Answer (Jawapan Pendek)

List FOUR (4) items that a client may need to store safely before a body consultation session.

### Question 3 — True or False (Betul atau Salah)

State TRUE or FALSE with justification:

a) A returning client does not require any update to their consultation card.

b) A lockable locker with a numbered key is an acceptable method for safekeeping client belongings.

c) The aesthetician should handle the client's personal belongings without asking permission to save time.

d) Disposable body wraps are considered more hygienic than reusable salon gowns for single use.

### Question 4 — Short Answer (Jawapan Pendek)

Explain THREE (3) key principles of the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and how each applies to a salon setting.

### Question 5 — Multiple Choice (Soalan Aneka Pilihan)

Which of the following is NOT a common reason clients seek body consultation?

A. Weight management
B. Cellulite treatment
C. Dental whitening
D. Body contouring

### Question 6 — Short Answer (Jawapan Pendek)

Describe the requirements for a client change area in a body consultation setting. List at least FOUR (4) features.

---

## Answer Scheme (Skema Jawapan)

### Answer 1
**B. Obtain client consent before collecting personal data**

### Answer 2
Any FOUR of the following:
1. Handbag / wallet
2. Jewellery and accessories
3. Mobile phone
4. Outerwear / clothing
5. Keys
6. Spectacles / sunglasses

### Answer 3
a) **FALSE** — A returning client's consultation card must be retrieved and updated with any changes since their last visit (health, medication, condition changes).
b) **TRUE** — A lockable locker with a numbered key is an accepted secure method for safekeeping client belongings.
c) **FALSE** — The aesthetician must obtain the client's permission before handling their personal belongings.
d) **TRUE** — Disposable body wraps are single-use and therefore eliminate cross-contamination risks associated with reusable attire.

### Answer 4
Any THREE of the following principles with salon application:

| Principle | Salon Application |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Notice and Choice** | Client is informed what data is collected and why; consent is obtained |
| **Security** | Data stored in locked cabinets or password-protected digital systems |
| **Disclosure** | Client data not shared with third parties without consent |
| **Retention** | Data kept only as long as necessary for the business purpose |
| **Access** | Client can request to view or correct their own data |
| **Integrity** | Data must be accurate and kept up to date |
| **General Principle** | Data must be processed lawfully and fairly |

### Answer 5
**C. Dental whitening** — This is a dental procedure, not an aesthetic body service.

### Answer 6
A client change area must have (any FOUR):
1. Private, enclosed area with a lockable door or curtain
2. Mirror available for the client
3. Hooks for hanging clothing
4. Clear instructions on how to wear the service attire (verbal or signage)
5. Slippers provided for hygiene
