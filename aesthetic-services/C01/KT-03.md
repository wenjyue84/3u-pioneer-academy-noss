## Answer Scheme (Skema Jawapan)

### Answer 1
**C. Endomorph**

### Answer 2
BMI = Weight (kg) / Height (m)^2
BMI = 72 / (1.65 x 1.65)
BMI = 72 / 2.7225
**BMI = 26.4**

Classification (Asian BMI): **Obese I** (25.0 - 29.9)

### Answer 3
a) **Kyphosis** (*kifosis*) — Excessive outward curvature of the thoracic spine; presents as round shoulders or hunchback appearance.
b) **Lordosis** (*lordosis*) — Excessive inward curvature of the lumbar spine; presents as protruding abdomen and pronounced lower back curve.
c) **Scoliosis** (*skoliosis*) — Lateral (sideways) curvature of the spine; presents as uneven shoulders and/or hips.

### Answer 4
Any FOUR of the following:
1. Cancer / malignant tumours
2. Thrombosis / embolism
3. Fever / high temperature
4. Contagious skin diseases
5. Uncontrolled epilepsy
6. Recent surgery (within 6 months)

### Answer 5
1-B, 2-C, 3-A

### Answer 6
- **Android** (apple shape): Fat concentrated around the abdomen (central/upper body). Higher health risk because visceral fat around abdominal organs increases risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, and metabolic syndrome.
- **Gynoid** (pear shape): Fat concentrated on hips, thighs, and buttocks (lower body). Lower health risk as subcutaneous fat in these areas is less metabolically active.

### Answer 7
a) **FALSE** — BMI does not distinguish between muscle mass and fat mass. Athletes with high muscle mass may have a high BMI without being overweight.
b) **FALSE** — Pregnancy is a relative contraindication, not absolute. Modified treatments may proceed with GP approval.
c) **TRUE** — Posture analysis requires observation from anterior (front), posterior (back), and lateral (side) views for complete assessment.
d) **FALSE** — An ectomorph is characterised by a lean, linear build with long limbs. The athletic, muscular build describes a mesomorph.

### Answer 8
Any FIVE of the following:
1. Client personal details (name, age, gender, contact)
2. Medical history and current medications
3. Body type classification
4. Posture assessment findings
5. Figure fault identification
6. Skin, muscular, adipose conditions
7. Height, weight and BMI
8. Skinfold measurements
9. Body measurements (bust, waist, hips)
10. Photographs (with consent)
11. Lifestyle factors (exercise, diet, smoking)
12. Allergies
