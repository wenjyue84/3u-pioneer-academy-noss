# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C01/KK(3/5)
**Competency Unit:** C01 — Body Consultation / Konsultasi Badan
**Work Activity 3:** Carry Out Body Consultation
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** BLUE / BIRU

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this work sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Record client personal profiles in compliance with data protection requirements.
2. Check and identify client contraindications.
3. Perform body type analysis, posture assessment, and figure fault identification.
4. Analyse skin, muscular, adipose and cellulite conditions.
5. Measure height/weight and calculate BMI accurately.
6. Design and recommend a salon service plan.

## Safety Precautions (Langkah Keselamatan)

1. Wear disposable gloves when conducting physical examination.
2. Maintain client modesty with appropriate draping throughout.
3. If any absolute contraindication is identified, STOP the consultation and refer the client to a medical professional.
4. Handle the magnifying lamp carefully to avoid burns from the bulb.
5. Ensure client is stable and safe during height/weight measurement.

---

## Procedure (Prosedur)

### Step 1: Record Client Personal Profile
1.1 Seat the client comfortably.
1.2 Record on the consultation card: full name, age, gender, date of birth, occupation, contact details.
1.3 Record medical history: current medications, past surgeries, chronic conditions.
1.4 Record lifestyle factors: exercise habits, diet, smoking, alcohol, stress levels.
1.5 Record allergies: products, ingredients, materials.
1.6 Record previous aesthetic treatments and outcomes.
1.7 Ensure all data collection complies with PDPA.

### Step 2: Check Contraindications
2.1 Systematically check for absolute contraindications:
- Cancer / malignant tumours
- Thrombosis / embolism
- Fever
- Contagious skin diseases
- Uncontrolled epilepsy
- Recent surgery (< 6 months)

2.2 Check for relative contraindications:
- Pregnancy
- Diabetes
- Varicose veins
- Skin sensitivity / eczema
- High / low blood pressure
- Metal implants

2.3 Record all contraindications on the consultation card.
2.4 If an absolute contraindication is found, explain to the client that treatment cannot proceed and recommend medical consultation.

### Step 3: Analyse Body Types and Posture
3.1 Ask the client to stand in a natural, relaxed posture (barefoot).
3.2 Observe from the **anterior (front)** view: symmetry of shoulders, chest, hips.
3.3 Observe from the **posterior (back)** view: spine alignment, shoulder blade position, hip level.
3.4 Observe from the **lateral (side)** view: head position, spinal curves, pelvic tilt.
3.5 Identify the client's body type: ectomorph, mesomorph, endomorph, or combination.
3.6 Identify postural deviations: kyphosis, lordosis, scoliosis, flat back, winged scapulae.
3.7 Record body type and postural findings with sketches/diagrams on the consultation card.

### Step 4: Identify Specific Figure Faults
4.1 Visually assess the client for:
- Excess adipose tissue (location and extent)
- Cellulite (location and grade)
- Fluid retention / oedema
- Poor muscle tone
- Stretch marks / striae
- Uneven body contour
4.2 Perform the pinch test for cellulite grading (Grade 1, 2, or 3).
4.3 Record all figure faults on the consultation card.

### Step 5: Analyse Skin, Muscular, Adipose and Cellulite Conditions
5.1 Use the magnifying lamp to examine skin condition on body areas.
5.2 Classify skin type: normal, dry, oily, sensitive, dehydrated, mature.
5.3 Palpate muscles to assess tone: hypertonic, hypotonic, atrophied, normal.
5.4 Assess adipose tissue: distribution pattern (android/gynoid), firmness.
5.5 Use the calliper to measure skinfold thickness at standard sites (triceps, subscapular, suprailiac, abdomen) if applicable.
5.6 Confirm cellulite grade and extent.
5.7 Record all findings on the consultation card.

### Step 6: Measure Height and Weight
6.1 Ask the client to stand barefoot on the calibrated weighing scale.
6.2 Record weight to the nearest 0.1 kg.
6.3 Ask the client to stand against the stadiometer, feet together, head in Frankfort plane.
6.4 Lower the headpiece to the crown of the head.
6.5 Record height to the nearest 0.1 cm.

### Step 7: Calculate BMI
7.1 Apply formula: BMI = Weight (kg) / Height (m)^2.
7.2 Record the BMI value on the consultation card.
7.3 Classify using Asian BMI chart: Underweight / Normal / Overweight / Obese I / Obese II.
7.4 Record classification.

### Step 8: Take Body Measurements (if applicable)
8.1 Using the measuring tape, measure and record: bust, waist, hips, thighs, upper arms.
8.2 Ensure consistent measurement points for future comparison.
8.3 Record all measurements on the consultation card.

### Step 9: Design and Recommend Salon Service Plan
9.1 Based on all consultation findings, design a service plan including:
- Recommended treatments (thermal therapy, massage, electrotherapy, body wrap)
- Frequency and duration of each treatment
- Home care product recommendations
- Lifestyle recommendations
- Expected outcomes and timeline
- Cost estimate

9.2 Explain the service plan to the client clearly.
9.3 Answer any client questions.
9.4 Obtain client agreement to the proposed plan.
9.5 Record the agreed service plan on the consultation card.
