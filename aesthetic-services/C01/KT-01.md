## Answer Scheme (Skema Jawapan)

### Answer 1
**B. 24-26 C**

### Answer 2
a) **FALSE** — The weighing scale should be calibrated to zero before EACH use, not once a week.
b) **TRUE** — Fresh towels and linen are required for every client to maintain hygiene standards.
c) **FALSE** — The fire extinguisher must be accessible within the salon premises, not necessarily inside the treatment room.
d) **TRUE** — All electrical equipment must be Portable Appliance Testing (PAT) compliant.

### Answer 3
Any FIVE of the following:
1. Surface disinfection — wipe all surfaces with approved disinfectant before and after each client
2. Hand hygiene — wash hands with antibacterial soap; use hand sanitiser
3. Fresh linen management — clean towels, gowns and sheets for each client
4. Air quality maintenance — adequate ventilation
5. Floor cleanliness — swept and mopped daily; no debris or spillage
6. Soiled linen placed in covered bins
7. Waste bins lined and covered

### Answer 4
1-B, 2-D, 3-E, 4-A, 5-C

### Answer 5
The body consultation work station layout follows this flow:
1. **Entrance zone** — Client arrives and is greeted by the aesthetician.
2. **Change area** — Client changes into service attire behind a privacy screen.
3. **Consultation zone** — Couch, trolley with tools, therapist stool for the examination.
4. **Documentation area** — Desk or clipboard for recording consultation findings.

(Accept any logical 4-zone flow that covers arrival, changing, consultation and documentation.)

### Answer 6
**B. Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994**
