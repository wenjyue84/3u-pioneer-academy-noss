## Learning Objectives (Objektif Pembelajaran)

Upon completion of this practical unit, trainees shall be able to:
1. Prepare a body consultation work area to industry hygiene and safety standards.
2. Prepare a client for body consultation including belongings management and service attire.
3. Perform a complete body consultation including body type analysis, posture assessment, BMI calculation and service plan design.
4. Deliver post-consultation advice and schedule follow-up appointments.
5. Carry out upkeep of the work area including sterilisation, inventory and waste disposal.

---

## Instructional Plan (Kaedah Pengajaran 4 Langkah)

### Session 1: KK(1/5) — Prepare Body Consultation Work Area (3 hours)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan (Preparation)** | State objectives. Review hygiene/safety standards from KP(1/5). Show demonstration area. Check trainee PPE readiness. | 15 min |
| **2. Penyampaian (Presentation)** | Instructor demonstrates: surface cleaning, linen laying, trolley arrangement, equipment testing, privacy screen placement, documentation setup. Explain each step. | 45 min |
| **3. Penggunaan (Application)** | Trainees practise setting up consultation work area individually. Instructor observes and corrects. Trainees complete KK(1/5). | 90 min |
| **4. Pengesahan (Confirmation)** | Instructor assesses each station using KK checklist. Provide feedback. Trainees clean up. | 30 min |

### Session 2: KK(2/5) — Prepare Client for Body Consultation (3 hours)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Review client preparation principles from KP(2/5). Pair trainees (one as client, one as therapist). | 15 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Instructor demonstrates: greeting, requirement identification, belongings safekeeping, change area guidance, PDPA consent form. | 45 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees practise in pairs (role-play). Switch roles. Complete KK(2/5). | 90 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Instructor assesses using KK checklist. Feedback on professionalism and communication. | 30 min |

### Session 3: KK(3/5) — Carry Out Body Consultation (5 hours)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Review body analysis theory from KP(3/5). Distribute consultation forms. Prepare model/volunteer. | 20 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Instructor demonstrates: recording personal profile, checking contraindications, body type identification, posture analysis, figure fault assessment, skin/muscular/adipose/cellulite analysis, height/weight measurement, BMI calculation, service plan design. | 80 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees practise on models/peers. Complete full consultation card. Design a service plan. Complete KK(3/5). | 140 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Instructor reviews consultation cards. Assesses body type/posture accuracy. Provides feedback. | 60 min |

### Session 4: KK(4/5) — Carry Out Post Body Consultation Advice (3 hours)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Review post-consultation advice from KP(4/5). Prepare sample home care products. | 15 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Instructor demonstrates: delivering after care advice, recommending home care products, recording advice, scheduling next appointment using booking system. | 45 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees practise (role-play with scenario cards). Complete KK(4/5). | 90 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Instructor assesses advice delivery. Review recorded documentation. Feedback. | 30 min |

### Session 5: KK(5/5) — Upkeep Body Consultation Work Area (4 hours)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Review sterilisation methods and waste disposal from KP(5/5). Show sterilisation equipment. | 15 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Instructor demonstrates: tool sterilisation (autoclave, UV cabinet), surface sanitisation, stock counting, inventory documentation, waste segregation and disposal. | 60 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees practise sterilisation, inventory count, waste segregation. Complete KK(5/5). | 120 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Instructor assesses using KK checklist. Verify sterilisation technique and waste handling. Summarise entire CU practical. | 45 min |
