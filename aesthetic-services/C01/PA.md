## Instructions to Trainee (Arahan kepada Pelatih)

1. This is a practical assessment covering ALL work activities in CU C01 Body Consultation.
2. You will perform a COMPLETE body consultation on a model/volunteer.
3. You are assessed on process, output, attitude, safety and environment.
4. All tools, equipment and materials will be provided.
5. Maintain professionalism and client modesty at all times.
6. You have 2 hours to complete the entire assessment.

---

## Scenario (Senario)

A new client has arrived at the salon for a body consultation. They are concerned about weight management and wish to explore body treatment options. Perform a complete body consultation from work area preparation to post-consultation advice and upkeep.

---

## Task Breakdown (Pecahan Tugasan)

### Task 1: Prepare Body Consultation Work Area (15 marks, ~20 min)

Prepare the consultation area to professional standards including hygiene, tool arrangement, equipment testing and privacy setup.

### Task 2: Prepare Client for Body Consultation (10 marks, ~10 min)

Greet the model/volunteer, identify requirements, obtain consent, safekeep belongings, and usher to change into service attire.

### Task 3: Carry Out Body Consultation (40 marks, ~50 min)

Perform a complete body consultation:
- Record personal profile
- Check contraindications
- Analyse body type and posture
- Identify figure faults
- Analyse skin, muscular, adipose and cellulite conditions
- Measure height and weight
- Calculate BMI
- Design and recommend a salon service plan

### Task 4: Carry Out Post Body Consultation Advice (15 marks, ~15 min)

Deliver after care advice, recommend home care products, record post-consultation advice, and schedule next appointment.

### Task 5: Upkeep Body Consultation Work Area (20 marks, ~25 min)

Sterilise/sanitise tools, replenish materials, complete inventory count, and dispose of waste correctly.
