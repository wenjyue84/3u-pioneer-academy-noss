# PENILAIAN PRESTASI / PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C01/PA
**Competency Unit:** C01 — Body Consultation / *Konsultasi Badan*
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** LIGHT BLUE / BIRU MUDA
**Duration / Tempoh:** 60 Minutes / 60 Minit (3 × 20 min scenarios)
**Total Marks / Jumlah Markah:** 100
**Passing Mark / Markah Lulus:** 70/100 (minimum Score 3/5 on ALL rubric criteria)

---

## Arahan / Instructions

1. You will assess THREE (3) client consultation scenarios in sequence.
2. Each scenario is 15–20 minutes and is assessed independently.
3. For each scenario, score the candidate on FOUR (4) rubric criteria using a 5-point scale.
4. A score of **3 or above** on each criterion is required to PASS that criterion.
5. Any criterion scored **2 or below** = NOT YET COMPETENT for that scenario.
6. Candidate must pass ALL criteria in at least 2 of 3 scenarios to be declared COMPETENT.
7. Record marks in the tables below. Provide written feedback where scoring ≤ 2.

## SENARIO 1 — Saringan Sejarah Kesihatan / Health History Screening

**Client Profile:** Madam Rosnah, 42 years old, female office manager. Presents with persistent fatigue
and mild lower-back ache. She has hypertension (on medication) and a known shellfish allergy.
She has never received body therapy services before.

**Task:** Conduct a full body consultation from work area preparation through contraindication screening,
BMI calculation, and post-consultation advice. Duration: 15–20 minutes.

**NOSS Reference:** WA 1 (1.1–1.3), WA 2 (2.1–2.3), WA 3 (3.1–3.2, 3.6–3.7, 3.9–3.11), WA 4 (4.1–4.4)

| **S1-A Communication Effectiveness** (*Keberkesanan Komunikasi*) | Greets professionally; explains all steps clearly; uses empathetic, positive language; checks client understanding throughout; bilingual where appropriate | Greets and explains most steps; mostly clear communication; minor lapses in rapport | Adequate greeting and explanation; client understood key steps; basic professional conduct maintained | Communication partial; some steps not explained; client visibly confused at least once | No professional greeting; steps unexplained; client confused or uncomfortable throughout | /5 |
| **S1-B Health & Allergy Data Collection** (*Pengumpulan Data Kesihatan & Alahan*) | ALL fields in personal profile recorded; hypertension medication AND shellfish allergy both documented; PDPA consent obtained; contraindications checklist fully completed and verified | Hypertension and allergy recorded; PDPA obtained; 1–2 minor fields missed | Hypertension OR allergy recorded; PDPA obtained; consultation card mostly complete | One major contraindication missed (hypertension or allergy not recorded) | Both contraindications missed; PDPA not obtained | /5 |

## SENARIO 2 — Analisis Kulit & Komposisi Badan / Skin & Body Composition Analysis

**Client Profile:** Ms. Adilah, 28 years old, female, post-pregnancy (8 months postpartum). She is
concerned about stretch marks (*striae*) on her abdomen and thighs, loose skin, and excess adipose
around the waist. No known allergies. Currently breastfeeding.

**Task:** Perform detailed skin condition analysis, body composition assessment, BMI calculation,
and design a safe service plan appropriate for postpartum and breastfeeding status.
Duration: 15–20 minutes.

**NOSS Reference:** WA 3 (3.3–3.8), WA 4 (4.1–4.2)

### Rubrik Penilaian — Senario 2 / Assessment Rubric — Scenario 2

| Kriteria / Criteria | 5 — Cemerlang | 4 — Memuaskan | 3 — Kompeten | 2 — Sedang Membangun | 1 — Tidak Kompeten | Markah |
|---------------------|---------------|----------------|--------------|----------------------|--------------------|--------|
| **S2-A Communication Effectiveness** (*Keberkesanan Komunikasi*) | Sensitively addresses postpartum concerns; affirming language used; explains each assessment step; maintains client modesty; breastfeeding status acknowledged compassionately | Good communication; postpartum context addressed; modesty maintained; minor lapses | Adequate; breastfeeding flagged; assessment steps explained; client not distressed | Communication awkward; some steps unexplained; client modesty lapse once | Insensitive language; modesty not maintained; breastfeeding status not acknowledged | /5 |
| **S2-B Health & Allergy Data Collection** (*Pengumpulan Data Kesihatan & Alahan*) | Breastfeeding status documented; postpartum timeline recorded; no allergy confirmed; Data Protection Act consent obtained; contraindication form complete | Breastfeeding and timeline recorded; PDPA obtained; 1 minor field missed | Breastfeeding flagged as contraindication for certain modalities; consultation card mostly complete | Breastfeeding status not documented; contraindications partially addressed | Breastfeeding status missed entirely; contraindication form not completed | /5 |
| **S2-C Skin Condition Assessment Accuracy** (*Ketepatan Penilaian Kulit*) | Stretch marks (*striae*) correctly classified (mature/immature); loose skin elasticity graded; adipose and cellulite assessed and graded (Grade 1–4); BMI correctly calculated and classified | Striae classified; adipose/cellulite graded; BMI calculated; 1 minor classification error | Skin, adipose and cellulite assessed; BMI calculated; at least 3 of 5 components correct | Skin assessment partial; BMI incorrect or not calculated; cellulite grade not stated | No structured skin or body analysis; BMI not attempted | /5 |
| **S2-D Recommendation Appropriateness** (*Kesesuaian Cadangan*) | Non-electrotherapy plan recommended (breastfeeding contraindication); suitable manual/thermal alternatives offered; timeline for postpartum clearance stated; safe home care products (no harsh actives on breastfeeding client) | Appropriate safe treatments offered; breastfeeding contraindication addressed; 1 minor oversight | Safe manual alternatives recommended; breastfeeding contraindication acknowledged; home care provided | Service plan partially appropriate; one potentially unsafe recommendation | Electrotherapy recommended despite breastfeeding; unsafe treatment or products suggested | /5 |

## SENARIO 3 — Cadangan Pelan Rawatan / Treatment Recommendation

**Client Profile:** Mr. Hafiz, 35 years old, male executive. BMI = 30.2 (obese class I). He presents
with moderate cellulite on thighs and buttocks (Grade 2–3), requests fat-reduction treatment,
and wants a structured 12-week salon service plan. He has no medical conditions or allergies.
He is highly motivated but has misconceptions about "slimming machines removing fat permanently."

**Task:** Address client misconceptions, carry out full consultation, complete BMI classification,
design and present a realistic evidence-based 12-week service plan including salon and home care.
Duration: 15–20 minutes.

**NOSS Reference:** WA 3 (3.9–3.11), WA 4 (4.1–4.4)

### Rubrik Penilaian — Senario 3 / Assessment Rubric — Scenario 3

| Kriteria / Criteria | 5 — Cemerlang | 4 — Memuaskan | 3 — Kompeten | 2 — Sedang Membangun | 1 — Tidak Kompeten | Markah |
|---------------------|---------------|----------------|--------------|----------------------|--------------------|--------|
| **S3-A Communication Effectiveness** (*Keberkesanan Komunikasi*) | Misconception corrected diplomatically with scientific rationale; realistic expectations set; service plan explained clearly with visual aids or consultation card; client leaves informed and confident | Misconception corrected; plan explained; mostly clear; minor gap in expectations-setting | Misconception addressed; plan communicated; client generally understands outcomes | Misconception not fully corrected; service plan unclear; client may have inflated expectations | Misconception reinforced or not addressed; plan not explained; unprofessional communication | /5 |
| **S3-B Health & Allergy Data Collection** (*Pengumpulan Data Kesihatan & Alahan*) | Full profile recorded (no conditions, no allergies confirmed); PDPA obtained; lifestyle factors (diet, exercise, hydration) documented to inform plan; realistic timeline agreed and recorded | Profile and PDPA complete; lifestyle noted; 1 minor field missed | Profile and PDPA complete; contraindications confirmed clear; consultation card filled | Profile mostly complete; lifestyle factors not documented; PDPA obtained | Profile incomplete; contraindications not checked; PDPA not obtained | /5 |
| **S3-C Skin Condition Assessment Accuracy** (*Ketepatan Penilaian Kulit*) | Cellulite correctly graded (Grade 2–3 confirmed with pinch test and visual inspection); adipose distribution mapped; BMI 30.2 classified as Obese Class I; body type identified; posture assessed | Cellulite graded; adipose mapped; BMI classified correctly; posture done | Cellulite graded; BMI classified; at least 3 of 5 body analysis components completed | Cellulite grade incorrect or not confirmed; BMI classification wrong | No cellulite assessment; BMI not calculated or misclassified | /5 |
| **S3-D Recommendation Appropriateness** (*Kesesuaian Cadangan*) | 12-week plan includes: weekly modality sequence (electrotherapy → manual → wrap), rest intervals, lifestyle advice; home care (diet, hydration, exfoliation) included; measurable outcomes (waist circumference, weight) stated; follow-up appointment scheduled | 12-week plan structured; lifestyle advice included; measurable outcomes mentioned; 1 minor gap | Salon service plan designed with at least 3 treatment types; home care included; follow-up scheduled | Plan presented but incomplete (fewer than 3 treatment types or no home care); outcomes not measurable | No structured service plan; home care not recommended; no follow-up | /5 |
