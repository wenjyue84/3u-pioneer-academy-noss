# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C01/KP(3/5)
**Competency Unit:** C01 — Body Consultation / Konsultasi Badan
**Work Activity 3:** Carry Out Body Consultation
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Explain the methods of recording client personal profiles in compliance with data protection requirements.
2. Identify client contraindications for body treatments.
3. Describe body types, posture analysis, and specific figure faults.
4. Explain skin, muscular, adipose and cellulite conditions.
5. Calculate Body Mass Index (BMI) and interpret the results.

---

## 1.0 Introduction (Pengenalan)

Body consultation (*konsultasi badan*) is the systematic process of analysing the client's body to determine their current physical condition and design an appropriate salon service plan. It involves visual assessment, manual palpation, measurements, and detailed recording of findings. This is the most comprehensive work activity in the consultation process.

---

## 2.0 Recording Client Personal Profiles (Merekod Profil Peribadi Klien)

### 2.1 Information to Record

The body consultation card/form (*kad/borang konsultasi badan*) must capture:

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Personal details | Full name, age, gender, date of birth, occupation, contact number, address |
| Medical history (*sejarah perubatan*) | Current medications, past surgeries, chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, heart disease) |
| Lifestyle factors | Exercise habits, diet, smoking, alcohol consumption, stress levels |
| Previous treatments | Past aesthetic treatments, results, any adverse reactions |
| Allergies (*alahan*) | Known allergies to products, ingredients, materials |

### 2.2 Data Protection Compliance
- Record all information per the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).
- Obtain the client's informed consent before recording.
- Ensure confidentiality (*kerahsiaan*) of all recorded data.

## 3.0 Client Contraindications (Kontraindikasi Klien)

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Always complete the contraindication screening (*tapisan kontraindikasi*) BEFORE proceeding with any consultation assessment or treatment. Proceeding without screening may expose the client to serious risk of injury. If a client discloses a medical condition, refer to a medical practitioner (*pengamal perubatan*) before continuing. Document contraindication checks with date and therapist signature.

Contraindications (*kontraindikasi*) are conditions that make certain treatments inadvisable or dangerous.

### 3.1 Absolute Contraindications (Total — Treatment NOT permitted)

| Contraindication | Reason |
|-----------------|--------|
| Cancer / malignant tumours (*kanser / tumor malignan*) | Risk of spreading |
| Thrombosis / embolism (*trombosis / embolisme*) | Risk of dislodging blood clots |
| Fever / high temperature (*demam*) | Body already stressed |
| Contagious skin diseases (*penyakit kulit berjangkit*) | Cross-contamination risk |
| Epilepsy (uncontrolled) (*epilepsi*) | Risk of seizure during treatment |
| Recent surgery (< 6 months) | Healing tissue at risk |

### 3.2 Relative Contraindications (Partial — Treatment may proceed with caution/GP approval)

| Contraindication | Action Required |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Pregnancy (*kehamilan*) | GP letter; modified treatment only |
| Diabetes (*kencing manis*) | Monitor skin sensation; avoid heat treatments |
| Varicose veins (*urat varikos*) | Avoid direct pressure on affected area |
| Skin sensitivity / eczema (*ekzema*) | Patch test required; use hypoallergenic products |
| High / low blood pressure | Monitor client; gentle movements |
| Metal implants / pins | Avoid electrical treatments on area |

## 4.0 Body Types (Jenis Badan)

### 4.1 The Three Somatotypes

| Body Type | Characteristics | Typical Figure |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Ectomorph** (*ektomorf*) | Lean, long limbs, narrow shoulders/hips, low body fat, fast metabolism | Slim, linear build |
| **Mesomorph** (*mesomorf*) | Athletic, muscular, broad shoulders, medium frame, efficient metabolism | V-shaped or hourglass |
| **Endomorph** (*endomorf*) | Rounded, wider hips, shorter limbs, higher body fat, slower metabolism | Pear or apple shape |

Most individuals are a **combination** of two types (e.g., ecto-mesomorph).

### 4.2 Body Type Significance for Treatment Planning
- **Ectomorphs** — Focus on toning, skin nourishment, relaxation therapies.
- **Mesomorphs** — Maintenance treatments, muscle recovery, body contouring.
- **Endomorphs** — Weight management, cellulite reduction, detoxification, thermal therapies.

## 5.0 Posture Analysis (Analisis Postur)

### 5.1 Ideal Posture (Postur Ideal)

Viewed from the side, a plumb line should pass through:
- Ear lobe
- Centre of shoulder joint
- Centre of hip joint
- Just behind the patella (kneecap)
- Just in front of the lateral malleolus (ankle bone)

### 5.2 Common Postural Deviations

| Deviation | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Kyphosis** (*kifosis*) | Excessive outward curvature of the thoracic spine; round shoulders |
| **Lordosis** (*lordosis*) | Excessive inward curvature of the lumbar spine; protruding abdomen |
| **Scoliosis** (*skoliosis*) | Lateral curvature of the spine; uneven shoulders/hips |
| **Flat back** (*belakang rata*) | Loss of normal lumbar curve |
| **Winged scapulae** (*skapula bersayap*) | Shoulder blades protruding from the back |

### 5.3 Postural Assessment Method
1. Client stands in natural posture, barefoot.
2. Observe from anterior (front), posterior (back), and lateral (side) views.
3. Check alignment of ears, shoulders, hips, knees, and ankles.
4. Note any asymmetry or deviation.
5. Record findings on the consultation card with diagrams.

## 6.0 Specific Figure Faults (Kecacatan Figura Spesifik)

| Figure Fault | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Excess adipose tissue (*tisu adipos berlebihan*) | Localised fat deposits on abdomen, thighs, upper arms, flanks |
| Cellulite (*selulit*) | Dimpled, orange-peel texture skin; commonly on thighs and buttocks |
| Fluid retention / oedema (*pengekalan cecair*) | Swelling, particularly in lower limbs |
| Poor muscle tone (*tonus otot lemah*) | Flabby, slack muscles due to lack of exercise |
| Stretch marks / striae (*tanda regangan*) | Silvery or red lines from rapid weight change or pregnancy |
| Uneven contour (*kontur tidak sekata*) | Asymmetry in body shape |

## 7.0 Skin, Muscular, Adipose and Cellulite Conditions

### 7.1 Skin Conditions (Keadaan Kulit)

| Condition | Characteristics |
|-----------|----------------|
| Normal skin (*kulit normal*) | Smooth, even texture, good elasticity |
| Dry skin (*kulit kering*) | Flaky, tight, may crack |
| Oily skin (*kulit berminyak*) | Shiny, enlarged pores, prone to congestion |
| Sensitive skin (*kulit sensitif*) | Easily irritated, redness, reactive to products |
| Dehydrated skin (*kulit dehidrasi*) | Lacks water content; fine lines, taut feeling |
| Mature / ageing skin (*kulit matang*) | Loss of elasticity, thinning, sagging |

### 7.2 Muscular Conditions (Keadaan Otot)

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Hypertonic (*hipertonik*) | Muscles are excessively tight or in spasm |
| Hypotonic (*hipotonik*) | Muscles are weak and lacking tone |
| Atrophied (*atrofi*) | Muscle wasting from disuse or illness |
| Normal tone (*tonus normal*) | Good firmness and elasticity |

### 7.3 Adipose Tissue (Tisu Adipos)

Adipose tissue (*tisu adipos*) is body fat. Assessment involves:
- **Visual inspection** — Distribution pattern of fat deposits.
- **Palpation** — Firmness or softness of fat deposits.
- **Calliper measurement** — Skinfold thickness at standard anatomical sites.

**Calliper Skinfold Measurement Sites** (*Tapak Pengukuran Lipatan Kulit*):

| Site | Location | Client Position |
|------|----------|----------------|
| Triceps (*trisep*) | Mid-point of back of upper arm, between elbow and shoulder | Standing, arm relaxed at side |
| Subscapular (*subskapular*) | 2 cm below tip of right scapula, at 45° angle | Standing, arm relaxed |
| Suprailiac (*suprailiak*) | 1 cm above iliac crest, mid-axillary line | Standing |
| Abdominal (*perut*) | 3 cm to the right of the navel, horizontal fold | Supine or standing |

Procedure: Pinch the fold firmly with thumb and forefinger; apply calliper at 90° to fold; read within 2 seconds; repeat 3 times and take the mean.

Types of fat distribution:
- **Android** (*android*) — Apple shape; fat concentrated around abdomen. Higher health risk.
- **Gynoid** (*ginoid*) — Pear shape; fat concentrated on hips, thighs, buttocks.

### 7.4 Cellulite Classification (Klasifikasi Selulit)

| Grade | Description | Visual/Manual Test |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| Grade 1 (*Gred 1*) | No visible cellulite when standing or lying; dimpling only when skin is pinched | Pinch test positive |
| Grade 2 (*Gred 2*) | Cellulite visible when standing but not when lying down | Visible standing |
| Grade 3 (*Gred 3*) | Cellulite visible both standing and lying; may be painful to touch | Visible always; nodules palpable |

## 8.0 Height and Weight Measurement (Pengukuran Ketinggian dan Berat Badan)

### 8.1 Height Measurement
1. Client stands barefoot, back against the stadiometer.
2. Feet together, heels touching the base.
3. Head in Frankfort plane (lower eye orbit level with top of ear canal).
4. Lower the headpiece to the crown of the head.
5. Record to the nearest 0.1 cm.

### 8.2 Weight Measurement
1. Calibrate the digital scale to zero before each use.
2. Client stands barefoot, wearing minimal/service attire.
3. Stand still, weight distributed evenly on both feet.
4. Record to the nearest 0.1 kg.

## 9.0 Body Mass Index (BMI) Calculation (Pengiraan Indeks Jisim Badan)

### 9.1 Formula

**BMI = Weight (kg) / Height (m)^2**

Example: Client weighing 65 kg and 1.60 m tall:
BMI = 65 / (1.60 x 1.60) = 65 / 2.56 = **25.4**

### 9.2 BMI Classification (WHO / Asian Cut-offs)

| Classification | BMI Range (Asian) |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Underweight (*kurang berat badan*) | < 18.5 |
| Normal (*normal*) | 18.5 – 22.9 |
| Overweight (*berat badan berlebihan*) | 23.0 – 24.9 |
| Obese I (*obes I*) | 25.0 – 29.9 |
| Obese II (*obes II*) | ≥ 30.0 |

> **Note:** Asian BMI cut-offs are lower than general WHO cut-offs due to higher metabolic risk at lower BMI in Asian populations.

### 9.3 Waist-Hip Ratio (WHR) — Nisbah Pinggang-Pinggul

WHR is a supplementary anthropometric measure that indicates abdominal fat distribution and associated health risk.

**Formula:** WHR = Waist circumference (cm) ÷ Hip circumference (cm)

**Measurement technique:**
1. Waist: Measure at the narrowest point between the lower rib and iliac crest; client breathes normally.
2. Hip: Measure at the widest point of the buttocks.

| Risk Level | WHR — Women | WHR — Men |
|-----------|------------|----------|
| Low risk (*risiko rendah*) | < 0.80 | < 0.90 |
| Moderate risk (*risiko sederhana*) | 0.80 – 0.85 | 0.90 – 0.95 |
| High risk (*risiko tinggi*) | > 0.85 | > 0.95 |

### 9.4 Limitations of BMI
- Does not distinguish between muscle mass and fat mass.
- May not accurately represent body composition in athletes.
- Supplementary measures (waist circumference, WHR, body fat percentage via calliper) are recommended.
- **Lean Meat Index (LMI)** may be used as a supplementary measure.

## 10.0 Recording Consultation Information (Merekod Maklumat Konsultasi)

### 10.1 Body Consultation Card/Form

The card/form should contain:
1. Client personal details (as per Section 2.0)
2. Body type classification
3. Postural assessment findings (with diagrams)
4. Figure fault identification
5. Skin, muscular, and adipose condition notes
6. Height, weight, and BMI
7. Skinfold measurements (if taken)
8. Body measurements (bust, waist, hips, thighs, upper arms)
9. Photographs (with client consent)

### 10.2 Designing and Recommending a Salon Service Plan

Based on the consultation findings, the aesthetician designs a service plan (*pelan perkhidmatan salon*) that may include:
- Recommended treatments (thermal therapy, massage, electrotherapy, body wrap)
- Frequency and duration of treatments
- Home care products
- Lifestyle recommendations (diet, exercise)
- Expected outcomes and timeline
- Cost estimate

The plan must be explained clearly to the client, and their agreement obtained before proceeding.

---

## 11.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Body consultation is a comprehensive assessment process that includes client profiling, contraindication checking, body type analysis, posture assessment, figure fault identification, condition analysis, anthropometric measurements, BMI calculation, and the design of a tailored salon service plan. All findings must be accurately recorded and the client must provide informed consent.
