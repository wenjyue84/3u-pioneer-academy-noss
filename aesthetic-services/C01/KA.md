**BM:** Manakah antara berikut adalah kontraindikasi mutlak bagi rawatan badan?
A. Mild varicose veins  B. Low blood pressure  C. Contagious skin disease  D. Pregnancy | **Answer / Jawapan:** C

**Q3 (Short Answer / Jawapan Pendek):**
**EN:** List THREE (3) key items recorded on a body consultation card.
**BM:** Senaraikan TIGA (3) perkara utama yang direkodkan pada kad konsultasi badan.

**Answer / Jawapan:** Relative contraindication. Perform patch test; use hypoallergenic products; avoid affected skin areas; proceed with caution.

## Marking Scheme (Skema Pemarkahan)

### Section A (2 marks each)

### Section B (4 marks each)
**11.** FALSE — BMI does not distinguish between muscle mass and fat mass. It is a ratio of weight to height only.
**12.** TRUE — Pregnancy is a relative contraindication; modified treatments can proceed with written GP approval.
**13.** FALSE — Sanitisation reduces microorganisms to a safe level. Sterilisation is the complete destruction of all microorganisms including spores.
**14.** TRUE — Android (apple shape) fat is concentrated around the abdomen (visceral fat), which increases risk of cardiovascular disease and metabolic syndrome.
**15.** FALSE — Under the PDPA 2010, client personal data cannot be shared with third parties without the client's explicit consent.

### Section C
**16.** (3 marks each somatotype)
- Ectomorph: Lean build, long limbs, narrow shoulders/hips, low body fat, fast metabolism
- Mesomorph: Athletic, muscular build, broad shoulders, medium frame, efficient metabolism
- Endomorph: Rounded build, wider hips, shorter limbs, higher body fat, slower metabolism

**17.** (3 marks per view)
- Anterior (front): Check symmetry of shoulders, chest, hips, knees
- Posterior (back): Check spine alignment, shoulder blade position, hip level
- Lateral (side): Check head position, spinal curves (kyphosis/lordosis), pelvic tilt

**18.** BMI = 80 / (1.72)^2 = 80 / 2.9584 = 27.0 (3 marks for calculation, 3 marks for classification: Obese I per Asian BMI chart, 25.0-29.9)

**19.** (approx. 1.2 marks each — award full marks for any 5 correct)
- General waste: general bin, local authority collection
- Clinical waste: yellow bag, licensed contractor
- Sharps: puncture-proof container, licensed disposal
- Chemical waste: separate container, per Environmental Quality Act
- Recyclable waste: recycling bin, local recycling programme

### Section D
**20a.** (1 mark each, max 8): Name, age, gender, occupation, contact details, medical history (eczema, low BP medication), allergies, lifestyle (exercise/diet), previous treatments, body measurements, height/weight, BMI, current concern (cellulite, toning)

**20b.** (3 marks each contraindication)
- Eczema: Relative contraindication — perform patch test; use hypoallergenic products; avoid affected areas or proceed with caution
- Low blood pressure: Relative contraindication — monitor client during treatment; use gentle movements; avoid sudden position changes

**20c.** (6 marks)
- Visual inspection of thighs standing and lying (1 mark)
- Pinch test on thigh area (1 mark)
- Grading: Grade 1 = only visible when pinched, Grade 2 = visible standing not lying, Grade 3 = visible standing and lying with nodules (3 marks)
- Record grade on consultation card (1 mark)

**20d.** (2 marks each treatment with rationale, max 3)
- Manual body massage: improve circulation, stimulate lymphatic drainage to reduce cellulite appearance
- Body wrap therapy (warm/herbal): detoxification and fluid reduction in thigh area
- Body electrotherapy (vacuum suction): targeted lymphatic drainage and cellulite reduction
- Avoid heat-intensive treatments on eczema areas

**20e.** (1 mark each, max 4)
- Dry body brushing towards the heart daily
- Increase water intake (8+ glasses)
- Use hypoallergenic moisturiser on eczema areas
- Light cardiovascular exercise 3-5 times per week
- Reduce salt and caffeine intake
- Apply prescribed eczema medication as directed by doctor
