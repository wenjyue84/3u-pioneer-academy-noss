## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this work sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Demonstrate proper hygiene and safety practices for a body consultation service area.
2. Identify, select and arrange tools, equipment and materials for body consultation.
3. Set up a complete body consultation work station ready for client service.

## Safety Precautions (Langkah Keselamatan)

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Use only approved disinfectant solutions at correct dilution. Never mix different chemical disinfectants — toxic fumes can result. Wear gloves when handling cleaning chemicals.

1. Wear appropriate PPE (apron, disposable gloves) during cleaning and setup.
2. Ensure no wet floors or tripping hazards.
3. Check electrical equipment cords for damage before plugging in.
4. Do not overload electrical sockets.
5. Ensure fire extinguisher and first aid kit are accessible.

## Procedure (Prosedur)

### Step 1: Personal Preparation and Client Readiness (Persediaan Peribadi dan Persediaan Klien)
1.1 Wash hands thoroughly with antibacterial soap for at least 20 seconds.
1.2 Put on clean apron and don disposable gloves.
1.3 Ensure personal hygiene standards are met (clean uniform, hair tied, minimal jewellery).
1.4 Prepare client gown/wrap, slippers and towels so they are ready before the client arrives.

### Step 2: Clean, Disinfect and Sterilise (Pembersihan, Penyahkuman dan Pensterilan)
2.1 Spray approved disinfectant solution on the consultation couch surface.
2.2 Wipe thoroughly with a clean cloth in one direction; discard used cloth.
2.3 Repeat for trolley top, stool, and all counter surfaces.
2.4 Wipe measuring instruments (calliper, measuring tape) with 70% isopropyl alcohol.
2.5 Allow all surfaces and instruments to air dry completely before placing items on them.

### Step 3: Lay Fresh Linen
3.1 Place a fresh bed sheet on the consultation couch, tucking corners securely.
3.2 Place one large towel folded at the foot of the couch.
3.3 Place one large towel folded at the head of the couch (for client draping).
3.4 Place small/hand towels on the trolley.

### Step 4: Arrange Tools on Trolley
4.1 Top shelf: Measuring tape, body fat calliper, BMI chart/calculator, pen, clipboard.
4.2 Middle shelf: Disposable gloves, tissues, hand sanitiser, consultation cards.
4.3 Bottom shelf: Client gown/wrap, slippers, spare towels.
4.4 Ensure items are arranged in order of use for efficient workflow.

### Step 5: Set Up Equipment
5.1 Position the weighing scale on a flat, hard surface near the couch.
5.2 Calibrate the digital scale to zero.
5.3 Position the stadiometer near the weighing scale.
5.4 Position the magnifying lamp near the head of the couch; check bulb is working.
5.5 Plug in and test all electrical equipment.

### Step 6: Set Privacy
6.1 Position the privacy screen or ensure the curtain is properly hung.
6.2 Check that the change area is enclosed and provides adequate privacy.
6.3 Place hooks for client clothing in the change area.

### Step 7: Prepare Documentation
7.1 Place 5 blank consultation cards/forms on the clipboard or desk.
7.2 Ensure pens are working.
7.3 Have the client record file/folder accessible.

### Step 8: Final Check
8.1 Stand back and survey the entire work area.
8.2 Check room temperature (24-26 C) and adjust if needed.
8.3 Ensure adequate lighting.
8.4 Confirm all items are in place and the area is presentable.
