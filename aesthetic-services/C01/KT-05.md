## Answer Scheme (Skema Jawapan)

### Answer 1
**C. Autoclave**

### Answer 2
1-B, 2-E, 3-D, 4-C, 5-A

### Answer 3
a) **Sterilisation** (*sterilisasi*) — The complete destruction of ALL microorganisms, including bacterial spores, using methods such as autoclaving, dry heat, or chemicals.
b) **Sanitisation** (*sanitasi*) — The reduction of microorganisms to a safe level, as determined by public health standards. It does not destroy all microorganisms.
c) **Disinfection** (*penyahjangkitan*) — The destruction of most pathogenic (disease-causing) organisms, but not bacterial spores. Uses chemical agents or physical methods.

### Answer 4
a) **FALSE** — FIFO means the OLDEST stock should be used first (First-In, First-Out), not the newest.
b) **TRUE** — Tools lose their sterile status over time when exposed to the environment and should be re-sterilised after 24 hours.
c) **FALSE** — Clinical/contaminated waste must NEVER be placed in general waste. It must go in yellow clinical waste bags and be collected by a licensed waste contractor.
d) **TRUE** — A stock card is specifically designed to track individual product quantities with in and out movements recorded.
e) **TRUE** — The Environmental Quality Act 1974 (Act 127) governs environmental protection and waste disposal in Malaysia.

### Answer 6
