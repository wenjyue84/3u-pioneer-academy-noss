## Learning Objectives (Objektif Pembelajaran)

Upon completion of this theory unit, trainees shall be able to:
1. Explain hygiene, safety and work area preparation requirements for body consultation.
2. Describe client preparation procedures and Data Protection Act compliance.
3. Analyse body types, posture, figure faults, BMI, skin, muscular, adipose and cellulite conditions.
4. Explain post-consultation advice, after care and home care recommendations.
5. Describe sterilisation, sanitisation, inventory management and waste disposal procedures.

---

## Instructional Plan (Kaedah Pengajaran 4 Langkah)

### Session 1: KP(1/5) + KT(1/5) — Hygiene, Safety and Work Area Preparation

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan (Preparation)** | State learning objectives. Relate to salon safety standards. Check trainee prior knowledge of hygiene practices. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian (Presentation)** | Deliver KP(1/5): Hygiene practices, safety standards, identification and selection of tools/equipment/materials, arrangement of work area. Use visual aids showing proper setup. | 40 min |
| **3. Penggunaan (Application)** | Trainees complete KT(1/5): Answer questions on hygiene, safety and work area preparation. | 30 min |
| **4. Pengesahan (Confirmation)** | Review KT answers. Discuss common errors. Confirm understanding through Q&A. | 10 min |

### Session 2: KP(2/5) + KT(2/5) — Client Preparation and Data Protection

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Recap previous session. State objectives for client preparation. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(2/5): Client requirements identification, safekeeping personal belongings, service attire protocols, Data Protection Act compliance. | 40 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(2/5). | 30 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review and confirm understanding. | 10 min |

### Session 3: KP(3/5) + KT(3/5) — Body Analysis

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Introduce body analysis concepts. Link to anatomy and physiology knowledge. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(3/5): Body types (ectomorph, mesomorph, endomorph), posture analysis, figure faults, skin/muscular/adipose/cellulite conditions, height/weight measurement, BMI calculation, recording methods, service plan design. Use anatomical diagrams. | 50 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(3/5). | 30 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review answers. Clarify body type classifications. | 10 min |

### Session 4: KP(4/5) + KT(4/5) — Post-Consultation Advice

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Discuss importance of post-consultation communication. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(4/5): After care advice, home care recommendations, recording post-consultation advice, scheduling next appointment. | 35 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(4/5). | 30 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review and confirm. | 10 min |

### Session 5: KP(5/5) + KT(5/5) — Sterilisation, Inventory and Waste Disposal

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Link to salon hygiene standards and local authority waste regulations. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(5/5): Sterilisation and sanitisation methods, product replenishment, stock inventory procedures, waste identification and disposal per local authority requirements. | 35 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(5/5). | 30 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review. Summarise entire CU theory component. | 15 min |
