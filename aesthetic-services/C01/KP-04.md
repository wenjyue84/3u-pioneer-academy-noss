# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C01/KP(4/5)
**Competency Unit:** C01 — Body Consultation / Konsultasi Badan
**Work Activity 4:** Carry Out Post Body Consultation Advice
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Explain the purpose and content of after care advice following body consultation.
2. Describe home care recommendations suitable for different client conditions.
3. Explain the importance of recording post-consultation advice.
4. Describe the procedure for scheduling a client's next service appointment.

---

## 1.0 Introduction (Pengenalan)

Post body consultation advice (*nasihat selepas konsultasi badan*) is the final client-facing step in the consultation process. It bridges the gap between the assessment findings and the treatment plan, providing the client with immediate guidance while the full service plan is implemented over time.

Effective post-consultation advice enhances client satisfaction, demonstrates professionalism, and encourages treatment compliance.

---

## 2.0 After Care Advice (Nasihat Penjagaan Selepas)

After care advice is given immediately following the body consultation. It addresses:

### 2.1 General After Care Guidelines

| Area | Advice |
|------|--------|
| Hydration (*penghidratan*) | Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily to support skin hydration and detoxification |
| Diet (*pemakanan*) | Balanced diet with fruits, vegetables, lean proteins; reduce processed foods, sugar, excessive salt |
| Physical activity (*aktiviti fizikal*) | Regular moderate exercise (30 minutes, 3-5 times per week) to improve circulation and muscle tone |
| Rest (*rehat*) | Adequate sleep (7-8 hours) for tissue repair and stress management |
| Posture awareness (*kesedaran postur*) | Be mindful of sitting and standing posture; ergonomic adjustments at work |

### 2.2 Condition-Specific After Care

| Condition | After Care Advice |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Cellulite | Dry body brushing, increased water intake, reduce caffeine and alcohol |
| Fluid retention | Reduce salt intake, elevate legs when resting, light exercise |
| Poor muscle tone | Resistance exercises targeting affected areas |
| Excess adipose tissue | Calorie-controlled diet, cardiovascular and resistance exercise |
| Dry/dehydrated skin | Use hydrating body lotion, avoid very hot showers, drink adequate water |

---

## 3.0 Home Care Advice (Nasihat Penjagaan di Rumah)

Home care (*penjagaan di rumah*) refers to products and routines the client follows at home between salon visits to support and maintain treatment results.

### 3.1 Home Care Product Recommendations

| Product Type | Purpose | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Body scrub / exfoliant (*pengelupas kulit*) | Remove dead skin cells, improve texture | Sugar scrub, salt scrub (1-2 times per week) |
| Body moisturiser (*pelembap badan*) | Maintain skin hydration and elasticity | Body lotion, body butter |
| Anti-cellulite cream (*krim anti-selulit*) | Reduce cellulite appearance | Caffeine-based cream, retinol cream |
| Firming serum (*serum pengencangan*) | Improve skin firmness | Collagen serum, elastin serum |
| Sunscreen (*pelindung matahari*) | Protect skin from UV damage | SPF 30+ for exposed areas |

### 3.2 Home Care Routine
1. **Morning** — Cleanse body, apply moisturiser, apply sunscreen on exposed areas.
2. **Evening** — Cleanse body, apply treatment product (anti-cellulite/firming as needed).
3. **Weekly** — Exfoliate 1-2 times per week; dry body brush before shower.

### 3.3 Advising the Client
- Use simple, clear language.
- Demonstrate product application if possible.
- Provide written instructions (*arahan bertulis*) for the client to take home.
- Explain expected timeframe for results.
- Advise on products to avoid (those that may conflict with salon treatments).

---

## 4.0 Recording Post-Consultation Advice (Merekod Nasihat Pasca-Konsultasi)

### 4.1 Why Record?
- Creates a reference for subsequent visits.
- Demonstrates duty of care (*tanggungjawab penjagaan*).
- Enables tracking of client compliance.
- Supports continuity if the client sees a different therapist.

### 4.2 What to Record
| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| After care advice given | Specific advice points communicated to the client |
| Home care products recommended | Product names, application instructions, frequency |
| Client response | Client's questions, concerns, agreement |
| Follow-up actions | Any referrals (e.g., GP for medical conditions) |
| Date and therapist signature | For accountability and audit trail |

### 4.3 Storage
- Record on the client's body consultation card/form.
- File securely per Data Protection Act requirements.
- Update digital records if the salon uses an electronic system.

---

## 5.0 Scheduling Next Service Appointment (Penjadualan Temujanji Perkhidmatan Seterusnya)

### 5.1 Appointment Scheduling Principles
- **Treatment frequency** — Based on the service plan (e.g., weekly, fortnightly, monthly).
- **Client availability** — Work around the client's schedule.
- **Therapist availability** — Ensure the same therapist is available for continuity.
- **Seasonal considerations** — Some treatments are more suitable in certain seasons (e.g., body wraps in cooler weather).

### 5.2 Confirmation Methods
- **Appointment card** (*kad temujanji*) — Physical card given to the client.
- **SMS / WhatsApp reminder** — Sent 24-48 hours before the appointment.
- **Digital booking system** — Online booking confirmation.
- **Phone call** — For clients who prefer verbal confirmation.

### 5.3 No-Show / Cancellation Policy
- Inform the client of the salon's cancellation policy.
- Request at least 24 hours notice for cancellation or rescheduling.
- Record cancellations and follow up to reschedule.

---

## 6.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Post body consultation advice encompasses after care advice, home care recommendations, thorough documentation of advice given, and scheduling the next appointment. This process ensures the client receives holistic support beyond the salon visit and establishes a continuous care pathway.
