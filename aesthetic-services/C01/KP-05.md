# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C01/KP(5/5)
**Competency Unit:** C01 — Body Consultation / Konsultasi Badan
**Work Activity 5:** Upkeep Body Consultation Work Area
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Describe the sterilisation and sanitisation methods for tools and equipment.
2. Explain the process of replenishing materials and products.
3. Describe stock inventory procedures for salon operations.
4. Identify waste types and explain disposal methods per local authority requirements.

---

## 1.0 Introduction (Pengenalan)

Upkeeping the body consultation work area (*penyelenggaraan kawasan kerja konsultasi badan*) is essential for maintaining hygiene standards, ensuring tools are ready for the next client, and complying with health and safety regulations. This is a continuous responsibility that must be performed after every consultation session.

---

## 2.0 Sterilisation and Sanitisation (Sterilisasi dan Sanitasi)

### 2.1 Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Sterilisation** (*sterilisasi*) | Complete destruction of all microorganisms including spores |
| **Sanitisation** (*sanitasi*) | Reduction of microorganisms to a safe level |
| **Disinfection** (*penyahjangkitan*) | Destruction of most pathogenic organisms (not spores) |

### 2.2 Methods of Sterilisation

| Method | Equipment/Agent | Suitable For | Duration |
|--------|----------------|-------------|----------|
| Autoclave (*autoklaf*) | Steam under pressure (121 C, 15 psi) | Metal tools, glass | 15-20 min |
| UV cabinet (*kabinet UV*) | Ultraviolet light | Storing sterilised items; surface treatment | 20-30 min per side |
| Chemical sterilisation | Glutaraldehyde solution | Tools that cannot be autoclaved | As per manufacturer |
| Hot air oven (*ketuhar udara panas*) | Dry heat (160-180 C) | Metal instruments, glass | 60-120 min |

### 2.3 Sanitisation Procedures

| Item | Method |
|------|--------|
| Consultation couch | Wipe with approved disinfectant spray/solution |
| Trolley and surfaces | Wipe with disinfectant after each client |
| Magnifying lamp | Wipe lens and handle with disinfectant |
| Weighing scale | Wipe platform with disinfectant |
| Therapist hands | Wash with antibacterial soap; apply hand sanitiser |

### 2.4 Storage of Sterilised Tools
- Store in UV cabinet or sealed, clean container.
- Label with date of sterilisation.
- Do not handle sterilised tools with bare, unwashed hands.
- Tools have a sterilised shelf life — re-sterilise if not used within 24 hours.

## 3.0 Replenishing Materials and Products (Penambahan Bahan dan Produk)

### 3.1 Daily Replenishment Checks

After each consultation session, check and replenish:

| Item | Check |
|------|-------|
| Disposable gloves | Adequate stock in the treatment room |
| Tissues and cotton pads | Refill dispensers |
| Sanitiser / disinfectant spray | Refill if below half-full |
| Consultation cards/forms | Minimum 10 blank forms available |
| Client gowns / disposable wraps | At least 5 in stock |
| Towels and linen | Send used linen to laundry; replace with clean stock |

### 3.2 Reordering Protocol
- When stock reaches the **reorder level** (*tahap pesanan semula*), inform the supervisor.
- Use FIFO (First-In, First-Out) rotation — use oldest stock first.
- Check expiry dates on products; remove expired items.
- Record all stock movements in the inventory system.

---

## 4.0 Stock Inventory Management (Pengurusan Inventori Stok)

### 4.1 Purpose of Inventory Control
- Prevent stock shortages during service.
- Avoid overstocking and product wastage.
- Track consumption rates for budgeting.
- Ensure product quality through proper rotation.

### 4.2 Inventory Documentation

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Stock card (*kad stok*) | Track individual product quantities, in/out movements |
| Inventory checklist (*senarai semak inventori*) | Daily/weekly physical count of items |
| Purchase order (*pesanan pembelian*) | Request for new stock from supplier |
| Delivery note (*nota penghantaran*) | Verify received goods against order |
| Wastage log (*log pembaziran*) | Record expired/damaged products disposed |

### 4.3 Inventory Count Procedure
1. Count all items physically at end of day or as scheduled.
2. Compare physical count with stock card records.
3. Investigate and record any discrepancies.
4. Update stock cards with current figures.
5. Submit inventory report to the supervisor.

---

## 5.0 Waste Identification and Disposal (Pengenalpastian dan Pelupusan Sisa)

### 5.1 Categories of Salon Waste

| Category | Examples | Disposal Method |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| **General waste** (*sisa umum*) | Paper, packaging, food waste | General waste bin; collected by local authority |
| **Clinical / contaminated waste** (*sisa klinikal*) | Used gloves, blood-stained cotton, used wax strips | Yellow clinical waste bag; licensed waste contractor |
| **Sharps** (*benda tajam*) | Needles, broken glass, razor blades | Sharps container (puncture-proof, labelled); licensed disposal |
| **Chemical waste** (*sisa kimia*) | Expired products, chemical solutions | Separate container; disposed per Environmental Quality Act |
| **Recyclable waste** (*sisa boleh kitar semula*) | Clean plastic bottles, cardboard, glass containers | Recycling bin; local recycling programme |

### 5.2 Legal Requirements

Waste disposal must comply with:
- **Environmental Quality Act 1974** — Akta Kualiti Alam Sekeliling 1974
- **Local authority bylaws** — Undang-undang kecil pihak berkuasa tempatan
- **Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994** — for workplace waste management

### 5.3 Best Practices
- Segregate waste at point of generation.
- Never overfill waste bins.
- Wear gloves when handling waste.
- Ensure waste bins are lined, covered, and labelled.
- Maintain a waste disposal log.
- Schedule regular waste collection with licensed contractors for clinical waste.

---

## 6.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Upkeeping the body consultation work area involves four key activities: sterilisation and sanitisation of tools and equipment, replenishment of materials and products, maintenance of stock inventory records, and proper identification and disposal of waste. These activities ensure continuous hygiene compliance, operational readiness, and adherence to local authority regulations.
