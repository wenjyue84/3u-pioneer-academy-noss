# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C01/KK(2/5)
**Competency Unit:** C01 — Body Consultation / Konsultasi Badan
**Work Activity 2:** Prepare Client for Body Consultation
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** BLUE / BIRU

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this work sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Demonstrate proper client greeting and requirement identification.
2. Perform secure safekeeping of client personal belongings.
3. Usher the client to change into service attire with appropriate guidance.

---

## Safety Precautions (Langkah Keselamatan)

1. Ensure change area is private and secure.
2. Do not handle client belongings without permission.
3. Maintain professional communication at all times.
4. Respect client modesty throughout the process.

---

## Procedure (Prosedur)

### Step 1: Greet the Client
1.1 Welcome the client warmly and professionally.
1.2 Introduce yourself by name and role.
1.3 Confirm the client's name and appointment.
1.4 Offer the client a seat if waiting is required.

### Step 2: Identify Client Requirements
2.1 Ask the client the reason for their visit / primary concern.
2.2 Determine if the client is a first-time or returning client.
2.3 For returning clients: Retrieve existing consultation card from file.
2.4 For new clients: Prepare a blank consultation card.
2.5 Note any immediate concerns or questions the client has.

### Step 3: Obtain Consent
3.1 Explain to the client what personal data will be collected and why.
3.2 Present the PDPA consent form.
3.3 Allow the client time to read the form.
3.4 Answer any questions about data handling.
3.5 Obtain the client's signature on the consent form.

### Step 4: Safekeep Client Personal Belongings
4.1 Show the client the lockable locker or storage basket.
4.2 Ask the client to place their personal belongings (handbag, jewellery, phone) in the locker.
4.3 Provide the numbered key to the client (if locker system).
4.4 Confirm with the client that all valuables are secured.

### Step 5: Usher Client to Change Area
5.1 Guide the client to the private change area.
5.2 Provide the client gown / disposable wrap and slippers.
5.3 Explain clearly how to wear the service attire.
5.4 Instruct the client to hang their clothing on the provided hooks.
5.5 Inform the client to let you know when they are ready.
5.6 Step out and close the curtain/door for privacy.

### Step 6: Confirm Client Readiness
6.1 Wait for the client to signal readiness.
6.2 Ask permission before entering/opening the curtain.
6.3 Check the client is wearing the service attire correctly.
6.4 Guide the client to the consultation area.
