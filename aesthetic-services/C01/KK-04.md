# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C01/KK(4/5)
**Competency Unit:** C01 — Body Consultation / Konsultasi Badan
**Work Activity 4:** Carry Out Post Body Consultation Advice
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** BLUE / BIRU

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this work sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Deliver appropriate after care advice to the client.
2. Recommend suitable home care products and routines.
3. Record post-consultation advice on the client card.
4. Schedule the client's next service appointment.

---

| Pen | 1 |

---

## Safety Precautions (Langkah Keselamatan)

1. Do not recommend products the client is allergic to (check consultation card).
2. Always refer to a medical professional if health concerns arise during advice.
3. Provide accurate product information; do not make medical claims.

---

## Procedure (Prosedur)

### Step 1: Deliver After Care Advice
1.1 Seat the client comfortably in a consultation area.
1.2 Review the consultation findings with the client.
1.3 Provide general after care advice:
- Hydration: drink at least 8 glasses of water daily
- Balanced diet: increase fruits, vegetables, lean protein
- Physical activity: 30 minutes moderate exercise, 3-5 times per week
- Adequate sleep: 7-8 hours per night
- Posture awareness in daily activities
1.4 Provide condition-specific advice based on findings (e.g., dry brushing for cellulite, salt reduction for fluid retention).

### Step 2: Recommend Home Care Products
2.1 Based on the client's skin type and body conditions, select appropriate home care products.
2.2 Show product samples to the client.
2.3 Explain each product: purpose, application method, frequency.
2.4 Demonstrate application technique if possible.
2.5 Advise on products to avoid (those that may conflict with salon treatments).
2.6 Provide written home care instructions (leaflet or written card).

### Step 3: Record Post-Consultation Advice
3.1 On the client's consultation card/form, record:
- After care advice given (specific points)
- Home care products recommended (names and application instructions)
- Client's response and any questions raised
- Any referrals made (e.g., GP referral)
3.2 Sign and date the entry.
3.3 Ensure the card is filed securely.

### Step 4: Schedule Next Service Appointment
4.1 Based on the agreed service plan, determine the next appointment date.
4.2 Check therapist and room availability.
4.3 Offer the client suitable date/time options.
4.4 Confirm the appointment and record it in the booking system.
4.5 Provide the client with an appointment card or digital confirmation.
4.6 Inform the client of the cancellation policy (24 hours notice).

### Step 5: Farewell
5.1 Thank the client for their visit.
5.2 Ensure the client has collected all personal belongings.
5.3 Escort the client to the reception/exit area.
5.4 Confirm the client is satisfied and has no further questions.
