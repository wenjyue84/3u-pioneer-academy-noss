# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C01/KK(5/5)
**Competency Unit:** C01 — Body Consultation / Konsultasi Badan
**Work Activity 5:** Upkeep Body Consultation Work Area
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** BLUE / BIRU

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this work sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Perform sterilisation and sanitisation of tools and equipment.
2. Replenish materials and products after a consultation session.
3. Carry out stock inventory count and documentation.
4. Identify waste categories and dispose of waste per local authority requirements.

---

## Safety Precautions (Langkah Keselamatan)

1. Wear disposable gloves when handling contaminated tools and waste.
2. Do not touch autoclave immediately after cycle — allow cooling.
3. Handle sharps with extreme care; use sharps container.
4. Do not mix clinical waste with general waste.
5. Wash hands after waste handling.

---

## Procedure (Prosedur)

### Step 1: Sterilise and Sanitise Tools and Equipment
1.1 Collect all used tools (callipers, measuring tape handles if applicable).
1.2 Clean tools with soap and water to remove visible debris.
1.3 Load metal/glass tools into the autoclave.
1.4 Set autoclave to 121 C, 15 psi, 15-20 minutes.
1.5 Once complete and cooled, transfer sterilised tools to the UV cabinet for storage.
1.6 Wipe the magnifying lamp lens and handle with disinfectant.
1.7 Wipe the weighing scale platform with disinfectant.
1.8 Wipe the consultation couch, trolley and stool with disinfectant solution.
1.9 Label sterilised items with the date.

### Step 2: Replenish Materials and Products
2.1 Check and replenish:
- Disposable gloves (restock to standard level)
- Tissues and cotton pads (refill dispensers)
- Hand sanitiser (refill if below half)
- Blank consultation cards/forms (minimum 10 available)
- Client gowns / disposable wraps (minimum 5 in stock)
- Slippers
2.2 Send used towels and linen to laundry.
2.3 Replace with clean towels and linen from the linen cupboard.
2.4 Check product expiry dates; remove any expired items.
2.5 Apply FIFO rotation — place newer stock behind older stock.

### Step 3: Update Stock Inventory
3.1 Count all items physically.
3.2 Record current quantities on the stock card / inventory sheet.
3.3 Compare with previous records; note any discrepancies.
3.4 Identify items below reorder level; flag for supervisor.
3.5 Complete the inventory sheet with date and signature.

### Step 4: Identify and Dispose of Waste
4.1 Segregate waste at point of generation:
- **General waste** (paper, packaging) — general waste bin with liner
- **Clinical waste** (used gloves, contaminated items) — yellow clinical waste bag
- **Sharps** (broken glass, blades) — puncture-proof sharps container
- **Chemical waste** (expired products) — separate chemical waste container
- **Recyclable waste** (clean plastic bottles, cardboard) — recycling bin
4.2 Seal full waste bags securely.
4.3 Label clinical waste bags with date.
4.4 Place sealed bags at the designated collection point.
4.5 Record waste disposal in the waste disposal log.
4.6 Ensure hands are washed thoroughly after waste handling.

### Step 5: Final Inspection
5.1 Survey the entire work area.
5.2 Confirm all surfaces are clean and dry.
5.3 Confirm all tools are sterilised and stored.
5.4 Confirm stock is replenished.
5.5 Confirm all waste has been disposed of correctly.
5.6 The work area is now ready for the next session.
