## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Explain the hygiene and safety practices required for a body consultation service area.
2. Identify the tools, equipment and materials used in body consultation.
3. Describe the proper arrangement and setup of a body consultation work area.

---

## 1.0 Introduction (Pengenalan)

Body consultation is the foundation of all aesthetic services. Before any treatment can be recommended or performed, a thorough body consultation must be conducted in a properly prepared work area. The work area must comply with hygiene and safety standards to ensure both client comfort and therapist safety.

A well-prepared consultation area reflects professionalism and instills confidence in the client. The aesthetician (*pakar estetik*) must ensure the area is clean, organised, and equipped with all necessary tools before the client arrives.

---

## 2.0 Hygiene and Safety Practices (Amalan Kebersihan dan Keselamatan)

### 2.1 Salon Hygiene Standards

Hygiene (*kebersihan*) in the salon is governed by:
- **Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 (OSHA)** — Akta Keselamatan dan Kesihatan Pekerjaan 1994
- **Local authority regulations** — Peraturan pihak berkuasa tempatan
- **Ministry of Health guidelines** — Garis panduan Kementerian Kesihatan

Key hygiene practices include:

| Practice | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Surface disinfection (*penyahjangkitan permukaan*) | All surfaces wiped with approved disinfectant before and after each client |
| Hand hygiene (*kebersihan tangan*) | Hand washing with antibacterial soap; use of hand sanitiser |
| Linen management (*pengurusan linen*) | Fresh towels, gowns and sheets for each client; soiled linen in covered bins |
| Air quality (*kualiti udara*) | Adequate ventilation; air purifier if required |
| Floor cleanliness (*kebersihan lantai*) | Swept and mopped daily; no debris or spillage |

### 2.2 Safety Practices

- Ensure no wet floors or tripping hazards in the consultation area.
- Electrical equipment must be PAT (Portable Appliance Testing) compliant.
- Fire extinguisher accessible within the salon premises.
- Emergency exits clearly marked.
- First aid kit (*peti pertolongan cemas*) available and stocked.

## 3.0 Tools, Equipment and Materials (Peralatan, Kelengkapan dan Bahan)

### 3.1 Tools (Peralatan)

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Measuring tape (*pita ukur*) | Body measurements |
| Weighing scale (*penimbang*) | Weight measurement |
| Height rod / stadiometer (*pengukur ketinggian*) | Height measurement |
| Magnifying lamp (*lampu pembesar*) | Skin examination |
| Body fat calliper (*kaliper lemak badan*) | Skinfold measurement |
| BMI chart / calculator | Body mass index calculation |

### 3.2 Equipment (Kelengkapan)

| Equipment | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Consultation couch/bed (*katil konsultasi*) | Client positioning for examination |
| Trolley (*troli*) | Organising tools and materials |
| Privacy screen/curtain (*skrin privasi*) | Client modesty |
| Stool (*bangku*) | Therapist seating |

### 3.3 Materials (Bahan)

| Material | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Body consultation card/form (*kad/borang konsultasi badan*) | Recording client information |
| Disposable gloves (*sarung tangan pakai buang*) | Hygiene during examination |
| Tissues (*tisu*) | General use |
| Sanitiser (*penyahjangkit*) | Hand and surface hygiene |
| Pen / clipboard | Documentation |
| Client gown / service attire (*pakaian perkhidmatan*) | Client modesty |

## 4.0 Arrangement and Setup of Work Area (Penyusunan Kawasan Kerja)

### 4.1 Pre-Session Preparation

1. **Clean all surfaces** — Wipe consultation couch, trolley, and counter surfaces with disinfectant.
2. **Lay fresh linen** — Place clean sheet on consultation couch; prepare fresh towels.
3. **Arrange trolley** — Place all tools (measuring tape, calliper, calculator, forms) on the trolley in order of use.
4. **Test equipment** — Confirm weighing scale is calibrated; magnifying lamp functioning.
5. **Set privacy** — Ensure curtain or screen is in place.
6. **Prepare documentation** — Have blank consultation cards/forms, pens, and client record folder ready.
7. **Check room temperature** — Maintain comfortable temperature (24-26 C) for client comfort.

### 4.2 Work Station Layout

The consultation area should follow a logical flow:
- **Entrance zone** — Client arrives and is greeted
- **Change area** — Client changes into service attire (behind screen)
- **Consultation zone** — Couch, trolley with tools, therapist stool
- **Documentation area** — Desk or clipboard for recording

---

## 5.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Proper preparation of the body consultation work area ensures:
- Compliance with hygiene and safety regulations
- Professional presentation to the client
- All necessary tools and materials are at hand
- Efficient consultation workflow
- Client comfort and confidence

The aesthetician must perform this preparation before every consultation session without exception.
