# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C01/KP(2/5)
**Competency Unit:** C01 — Body Consultation / Konsultasi Badan
**Work Activity 2:** Prepare Client for Body Consultation
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Identify client requirements for body consultation.
2. Explain procedures for safekeeping client personal belongings.
3. Describe protocols for ushering the client to change into service attire.
4. State the requirements of the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) relevant to client information.

---

## 1.0 Introduction (Pengenalan)

Preparing the client for body consultation is a critical step that establishes trust and ensures the consultation process runs smoothly. The aesthetician must handle the client with professionalism, courtesy and sensitivity. This includes identifying client requirements, securing personal belongings, and ensuring the client is appropriately attired for the consultation.

---

## 2.0 Identifying Client Requirements (Mengenal Pasti Keperluan Klien)

### 2.1 Initial Client Communication

Upon arrival, the aesthetician should:
- **Greet the client** warmly and professionally.
- **Confirm the appointment** and the type of service booked.
- **Ask about the client's primary concern** — What brings them for body consultation? Common reasons include:
  - Weight management (*pengurusan berat badan*)
  - Body contouring (*pembentukan badan*)
  - Skin conditions (*keadaan kulit*)
  - Posture concerns (*masalah postur*)
  - Cellulite treatment (*rawatan selulit*)
  - General wellness (*kesejahteraan umum*)

### 2.2 First-Time vs Returning Clients

| Client Type | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| First-time client (*klien baru*) | Complete new consultation card/form; full body analysis required |
| Returning client (*klien tetap*) | Retrieve existing consultation card; update any changes since last visit |

## 3.0 Safekeeping Client Personal Belongings (Penyimpanan Barangan Peribadi Klien)

The aesthetician must provide a secure system for client belongings:

### 3.1 Items to Secure
- Handbag / wallet (*beg tangan / dompet*)
- Jewellery and accessories (*barang kemas dan aksesori*)
- Mobile phone (*telefon bimbit*)
- Outerwear (*pakaian luar*)

### 3.2 Safekeeping Methods
- **Lockable locker** (*loker berkunci*) — Provide the client with a numbered key.
- **Personal belongings basket** with lid — For use within the treatment room.
- **Secure storage area** — Visible to the client or within the consultation room.

### 3.3 Best Practices
- Always inform the client where their belongings are stored.
- Do not handle the client's belongings unless given permission.
- Ensure the client retrieves all belongings upon completion.
- The salon is responsible for the security of client belongings while on premises.

## 4.0 Service Attire Protocols (Protokol Pakaian Perkhidmatan)

### 4.1 Purpose of Service Attire
Service attire (*pakaian perkhidmatan*) allows the aesthetician to:
- Conduct a thorough body examination without obstruction.
- Maintain client modesty (*ketertiban klien*) throughout the process.
- Prevent damage to client's personal clothing.

### 4.2 Types of Service Attire
| Attire | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Disposable body wrap (*pembalut badan pakai buang*) | Single-use, hygienic |
| Salon gown / robe (*jubah salon*) | Reusable, laundered after each use |
| Paper shorts (*seluar pendek kertas*) | Disposable, for lower body modesty |
| Breast cover (*penutup dada*) | Disposable, for female clients during body consultation |

### 4.3 Change Area Requirements
- Private, enclosed area with a lockable door or curtain.
- Mirror available.
- Hooks for hanging clothing.
- Clear instructions (verbal or signage) on how to wear the service attire.
- Slippers provided for hygiene.

## 5.0 Data Protection Act Compliance (Pematuhan Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi)

### 5.1 Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) — Malaysia

The PDPA governs the collection, use and storage of personal data in commercial transactions. In body consultation, the following client data is collected:
- Full name, age, gender, occupation
- Contact details (phone, email, address)
- Medical history and health conditions
- Body measurements and analysis results

### 5.2 Key Principles

| Principle | Application in Salon |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **General Principle** | Data processed lawfully and fairly |
| **Notice and Choice** | Client informed what data is collected and why; consent obtained |
| **Disclosure** | Data not shared with third parties without consent |
| **Security** | Data stored securely (locked cabinet / password-protected digital system) |
| **Retention** | Data kept only as long as necessary |
| **Access** | Client can request to view or correct their data |
| **Integrity** | Data must be accurate and up to date |

### 5.3 Practical Steps
1. Explain to the client what information will be recorded and why.
2. Obtain written consent (*persetujuan bertulis*) before collecting personal data.
3. Store physical consultation cards in a locked filing cabinet.
4. Digital records must be password-protected.
5. Never discuss client information with unauthorised persons.

---

## 6.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Preparing the client for body consultation involves:
- Identifying their specific requirements and concerns
- Securely storing personal belongings
- Providing appropriate service attire with privacy
- Complying with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010

These steps establish professionalism, trust and legal compliance from the first interaction with the client.
