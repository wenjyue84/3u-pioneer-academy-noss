## Q5

**Type:** Scenario-Based Technique Selection | **Marks:** 10 | **CoCU Ref:** S960-002-3:2020-C03 WA1 (1.1, 1.2), WA4 (4.4, 4.5)

**Scenario Context / Konteks Senario:**
Puan Ramlah, 45, an office administrator, presents for a full-body massage. During consultation she reports chronic tension in the upper trapezius and rhomboid area with occasional headaches. She has no diagnosed medical conditions and takes no medication. Patch test is clear.

Puan Ramlah, 45 tahun, seorang pentadbir pejabat, datang untuk urutan badan penuh. Semasa konsultasi, beliau melaporkan ketegangan kronik di kawasan trapezius atas dan romboid dengan sakit kepala sekali-sekala. Tiada kondisi perubatan dan tiada ubatan. Ujian tampalan tiada reaksi.

**Question / Soalan:**
(a) Select the THREE (3) most appropriate classical massage movements for Puan Ramlah's upper back/shoulder area and justify each choice.
(b) State the correct direction of effleurage strokes over the back, and explain why this direction is important physiologically.
(c) Describe ONE contraindication check you would perform before commencing treatment.

**Expected Answer / Jawapan Dijangka:**
(a) Recommended techniques:
- **Effleurage** (*eflurasi*): Opening stroke to warm tissue, assess tension areas, spread medium — calm and non-invasive.
- **Petrissage** (*petrissaj*): Kneading/wringing on upper trapezius and rhomboids to release muscle tension, improve circulation, and break down minor adhesions.
- **Friction** (*geseran*): Deep circular or cross-fibre friction on specific tension nodules/trigger points in rhomboids/paraspinal — breaks down fibrous adhesions.

(b) Effleurage direction: **Towards the heart** (centripetal / *arah sentripetal*) — aids venous return (*aliran balik vena*) and lymphatic drainage (*saliran limfa*). Return stroke is feather-light or off-body.

(c) Contraindication check: Verbal review of consultation card for absolute contraindications (e.g., fever, DVT, recent fractures, anticoagulants). Visually inspect upper back/neck for open wounds, bruising, or undiagnosed lumps before applying any technique.

**Rubric Points / Markah Rubrik:**
- **3 points (Advanced):** Correct techniques identified with physiological justification; centripetal direction explained with venous/lymphatic rationale; contraindication check described specifically.
- **2 points (Intermediate):** 2 techniques correctly justified; direction stated without full physiological explanation; contraindication check mentioned.
- **1 point (Basic):** 1 technique identified; direction stated; minimal contraindication awareness.
- **0 points:** Incorrect or irrelevant response.

**Bilingual Terms / Istilah Dwibahasa:**
- EN: Effleurage | BM: Eflurasi
- EN: Petrissage | BM: Petrissaj
- EN: Friction | BM: Geseran
- EN: Centripetal (towards heart) | BM: Arah sentripetal (ke arah jantung)
- EN: Venous return | BM: Aliran balik vena
- EN: Lymphatic drainage | BM: Saliran limfa
- EN: Trigger point / tension nodule | BM: Nodul ketegangan

## Q6

**Type:** Scenario-Based Technique Selection | **Marks:** 10 | **CoCU Ref:** S960-002-3:2020-C03 WA1 (1.1, 1.2), WA4 (4.4, 4.8)

**Scenario Context / Konteks Senario:**
Encik Hafiz, 38, a warehouse supervisor, requests a full-body relaxation massage. During consultation you note varicose veins (*varikos vena*) on his left calf. He also reports mild eczema (*ekzema ringan*) on his right forearm, currently not inflamed. His blood pressure is within normal range.

Encik Hafiz, 38 tahun, penyelia gudang, meminta urutan badan penuh untuk relaksasi. Semasa konsultasi, anda mendapati varikos vena di betis kiri beliau. Beliau juga melaporkan ekzema ringan di lengan bawah kanan, kini tidak meradang. Tekanan darahnya dalam lingkungan normal.

**Question / Soalan:**
(a) Classify each condition (varicose veins, mild eczema) as absolute, relative, or local contraindication. Justify each classification.
(b) Describe how you would modify the full-body massage for EACH condition.
(c) Which massage movement should be avoided directly over varicose veins and why?

**Expected Answer / Jawapan Dijangka:**
(a) Classification:
- **Varicose veins** — **Local contraindication** (*Kontraindikasi Tempatan*): Affects only specific area (left calf). Full-body massage may proceed, avoiding the affected site.
- **Mild eczema (non-inflamed)** — **Local contraindication** (*Kontraindikasi Tempatan*): Avoid affected area; use hypoallergenic medium (*medium hipoalergenik*) on other areas.

(b) Modifications:
- Left calf (varicose veins): Work proximal (above) and distal (below) the affected area with very light effleurage; no petrissage or tapotement over visible veins; avoid deep pressure.
- Right forearm (eczema): Avoid direct contact with eczema patch; apply hypoallergenic medium (e.g., grapeseed oil) to surrounding areas; do not massage over affected skin.

(c) **Tapotement** (*tapotemen*) and deep **petrissage** (*petrissaj*) must be avoided directly over varicose veins because percussion and compression can damage fragile, dilated vein walls and risk thrombosis formation or rupture.

**Rubric Points / Markah Rubrik:**
- **3 points (Advanced):** Both conditions correctly classified with sound physiological justification; specific modifications described for each; correct technique contraindicated with anatomical reason.
- **2 points (Intermediate):** Correct classifications; modifications stated at general level; contraindicated technique identified.
- **1 point (Basic):** One condition correctly handled; modifications vague; technique named without explanation.
- **0 points:** Misclassification of absolute vs. local; no modification described.

**Bilingual Terms / Istilah Dwibahasa:**
- EN: Varicose veins | BM: Varikos vena
- EN: Eczema | BM: Ekzema
- EN: Local contraindication | BM: Kontraindikasi Tempatan
- EN: Hypoallergenic medium | BM: Medium hipoalergenik
- EN: Tapotement | BM: Tapotemen
- EN: Proximal / Distal | BM: Proksimal / Distal

## Q7

**Type:** Scenario-Based Technique Selection | **Marks:** 10 | **CoCU Ref:** S960-002-3:2020-C03 WA1 (1.1), WA4 (4.4, 4.6, 4.8)

**Scenario Context / Konteks Senario:**
Puan Siti, 32, is 20 weeks pregnant and requests a full-body relaxation massage. She brings a GP clearance letter. She reports lower back discomfort and swollen ankles (*buku lali bengkak*). Patch test is clear.

Puan Siti, 32 tahun, mengandung 20 minggu dan meminta urutan badan penuh untuk relaksasi. Beliau membawa surat kebenaran doktor. Beliau melaporkan ketidakselesaan belakang bawah dan buku lali bengkak. Ujian tampalan tiada reaksi.

**Question / Soalan:**
(a) State the contraindication classification for pregnancy and the action required before treatment.
(b) Describe the client positioning for this massage and explain why this position is selected.
(c) Identify TWO (2) areas to avoid during massage and provide the anatomical/clinical reason for each.
(d) Select ONE massage movement appropriate for lower back discomfort in this client and justify your choice.

**Expected Answer / Jawapan Dijangka:**
(a) **Relative contraindication** (*Kontraindikasi Relatif*): Massage may proceed with caution. GP clearance (*kebenaran doktor*) is required and must be documented on the consultation card. Avoid abdomen in first trimester; at 20 weeks, gentle abdominal effleurage may be done only if specifically requested and with extreme caution.

(b) **Side-lying position** (*posisi berbaring miring*): Reduces pressure on the vena cava and aorta (supine hypotensive syndrome risk); relieves pressure on lower back; comfortable for enlarged abdomen. Use bolsters/cushions to support the abdomen and between the knees.

(c) Areas to avoid:
- **Abdomen** (*abdomen*): Risk of uterine stimulation; avoid deep pressure on abdomen especially in first trimester.
- **Swollen ankles / lower limb** (*buku lali bengkak*): Oedema (*oedema*) may indicate circulatory issue; avoid deep petrissage over oedematous areas; use only very light effleurage if at all; refer if severe.

(d) **Light effleurage** (*eflurasi ringan*) over lower back: Non-invasive, warming, relaxing; does not risk joint compression or nerve irritation; safe for lumbar area in pregnancy. Petrissage should be used with caution and reduced pressure.

**Rubric Points / Markah Rubrik:**
- **3 points (Advanced):** Correct contraindication class, GP clearance documented, side-lying rationale includes vena cava reference, 2 areas with clinical reasons, appropriate technique with justification.
- **2 points (Intermediate):** Correct class, positioning stated with reason, 1 area with reason, appropriate technique.
- **1 point (Basic):** Relative contraindication identified, side-lying stated, technique identified without full justification.
- **0 points:** Incorrect classification, wrong positioning, or unsafe technique recommended.

**Bilingual Terms / Istilah Dwibahasa:**
- EN: Relative contraindication | BM: Kontraindikasi Relatif
- EN: GP clearance | BM: Kebenaran doktor
- EN: Side-lying position | BM: Posisi berbaring miring
- EN: Vena cava | BM: Vena kava
- EN: Oedema | BM: Oedema / bengkak
- EN: Abdomen | BM: Abdomen / perut

## Q9

**Type:** Contraindication Identification | **Marks:** 10 | **CoCU Ref:** S960-002-3:2020-C03 WA1 (1.1, 1.2)

**Question / Soalan:**
A client arrives for her massage appointment. During consultation you discover she is taking warfarin (*warfarin*) for a heart condition and has controlled epilepsy (*epilepsi terkawal*). She also has a small undiagnosed lump (*ketuat tidak terdiagnosis*) on her left shoulder blade area.

(a) For EACH finding, state the contraindication type and the appropriate therapist action.
(b) Explain the specific risk associated with massaging a client on warfarin.

Seorang pelanggan tiba untuk temujanji urutan. Semasa konsultasi anda mendapati beliau mengambil warfarin untuk kondisi jantung dan mempunyai epilepsi terkawal. Beliau juga mempunyai ketuat kecil yang tidak terdiagnosis di kawasan tulang belikat kiri.

**Expected Answer / Jawapan Dijangka:**
(a)
- **Warfarin (anticoagulant / antikoagulan):** **Absolute contraindication** — Do NOT proceed without medical clearance. Warfarin significantly reduces blood clotting ability; massage risks causing serious internal bruising or haematoma.
- **Controlled epilepsy (*epilepsi terkawal*):** **Relative contraindication** — May proceed with caution; avoid tapotement (*tapotemen*); maintain calm environment; have emergency plan in place.
- **Undiagnosed lump (*ketuat tidak terdiagnosis*):** **Local contraindication** — Avoid the area completely until medically cleared; do not massage over or around the lump.

(b) **Warfarin risk:** Warfarin inhibits Vitamin K-dependent clotting factors, reducing the blood's ability to coagulate. Even moderate massage pressure can rupture superficial capillaries (*kapilari superfisial*), leading to extensive bruising (*lebam teruk*), subcutaneous haematoma (*hematoma subkutan*), or in severe cases, dangerous internal bleeding. Deep techniques (friction, petrissage) are especially risky.

**Rubric Points / Markah Rubrik:**
- **Advanced (9–10):** All 3 conditions correctly classified with actions; warfarin mechanism explained accurately including coagulation pathway reference.
- **Intermediate (6–8):** 2 correct classifications; warfarin risk stated without mechanism.
- **Basic (3–5):** 1 correct; warfarin identified as risky without explanation.
- **Below basic (0–2):** Misclassification of warfarin as relative; no risk explanation.

**Bilingual Terms / Istilah Dwibahasa:**
- EN: Anticoagulant | BM: Antikoagulan
- EN: Warfarin | BM: Warfarin
- EN: Epilepsy (controlled) | BM: Epilepsi terkawal
- EN: Undiagnosed lump | BM: Ketuat tidak terdiagnosis
- EN: Haematoma | BM: Hematoma
- EN: Capillaries | BM: Kapilari

## Q10

**Type:** Safety Procedure | **Marks:** 10 | **CoCU Ref:** S960-002-3:2020-C03 WA2 (2.2, 2.3, 2.4), WA4 (4.6, 4.8)

**Question / Soalan:**
Describe FIVE (5) safety procedures a massage therapist must follow throughout a full-body massage session to ensure client safety and professional standards. For each procedure, state the reason it is important.

Huraikan LIMA (5) prosedur keselamatan yang perlu diikuti oleh terapis urutan sepanjang sesi urutan badan penuh bagi memastikan keselamatan pelanggan dan piawaian profesional. Bagi setiap prosedur, nyatakan kepentingannya.

**Expected Answer / Jawapan Dijangka:**
Any 5 of the following (2 marks each: 1 for procedure, 1 for reason):

| Safety Procedure | Reason / Kepentingan |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| 1. Verify consultation card and confirm contraindications verbally before commencing | Ensures no undisclosed conditions are missed; client safety |
| 2. Obtain written informed consent (*persetujuan bertulis*) | Legally protects therapist; confirms client understands procedure |
| 3. Check couch stability, leg locks, and draping (*draperi*) before positioning client | Prevents falls and injury; ensures modesty and client comfort |
| 4. Monitor client response throughout (*respon pelanggan*) — check pressure verbally, observe facial cues | Enables immediate adjustment; prevents over-pressure injury |
| 5. Maintain proper body mechanics (*mekanik badan*) — wide stance, bent knees, straight back | Protects therapist from repetitive strain; maintains consistent technique |
| 6. Never apply direct pressure on the spine (*tulang belakang*) or bony prominences (*bahagian tulang menonjol*) | Risk of injury to spinous processes and periosteum |
| 7. Conduct patch test (*ujian tampalan*) for new products or clients reporting allergies | Prevents allergic reaction (*tindak balas alergi*) during treatment |
| 8. Launder all towels and linen at minimum 60°C | Prevents cross-contamination and pathogen transmission |
| 9. Wash hands with antibacterial soap after each client | Infection control (*kawalan jangkitan*) |
| 10. Document all findings and modifications on consultation card after treatment | Provides clinical record; protects against complaints |

**Rubric Points / Markah Rubrik:**
- **Advanced (9–10):** 5 procedures clearly stated with accurate and specific reasons; professional language used; NOSS-aligned terminology.
- **Intermediate (6–8):** 4 procedures with reasons; reasons may be general.
- **Basic (3–5):** 3 procedures identified; reasons brief or partially correct.
- **Below basic (0–2):** Fewer than 3 procedures; vague or incorrect responses.
