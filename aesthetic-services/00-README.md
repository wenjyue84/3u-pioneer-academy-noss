## Training Prerequisites (Prasyarat Latihan)

- Completed Malaysian Skills Certificate (MSC) S960-002-2:2020 Beauty Therapy Services
- Able to read and write in Bahasa Malaysia and/or English
- Physically fit
- Age 18 years and above
