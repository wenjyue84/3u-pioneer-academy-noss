# KERTAS PENERANGAN — S960-002-3:2020-C07/KP(2/4) | WA 2: Client Needs & Requirements | WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain market trend analysis and demographic research.
2. Describe methods for determining client purchasing power and patterns.

## 1.0 Market Analysis
- **Market trends** — Influences affecting beauty industry (social media, wellness trends, organic products, sustainability).
- **Demographics** — Population, age groups, gender distribution in the salon's catchment area.
- **Purchasing power** — Average spending capacity of target clients.
- **Purchasing patterns** — Frequency of visits, preferred services, seasonal variations.

## 2.0 Recording Findings
Document: market trend observations, demographic statistics, purchasing analysis. Use this data to guide promotional strategy.
