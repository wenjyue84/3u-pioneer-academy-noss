# KERTAS PENERANGAN — S960-002-3:2020-C07/KP(3/4) | WA 3: Periodical Sales & Promotions | WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain how to plan and execute periodical sales and promotional activities.

## 1.0 Planning Promotions
Determine dates: seasonal (Chinese New Year, Hari Raya, Christmas, Year-End), festivals, salon anniversary, client birthdays.

Select: products/services for promotion, festive themes, terms and conditions.

## 2.0 Materials
- Flyers, catalogues, social media posts, website banners.
- Obtain final approval from superior.

## 3.0 Execution Methods
| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Calls | Phone calls to existing clients |
| Email | Email campaigns with offers |
| Social media | Facebook, Instagram, TikTok promotions |
| Face-to-face | In-salon promotion, events, demonstrations |
| SMS/WhatsApp | Direct messaging to client database |

## 4.0 Submission
Periodical sales and promotional plan submitted to superior for approval before execution.
