**Q1:** List THREE categories of resources needed for promotional activities. **Q2 (MCQ):** Which is a sales mode? A. Risk assessment B. Voucher/redemption C. TNA D. Sterilisation **Q3:** List FOUR types of promotional materials. **Q4 (T/F):** a) A promotional budget must be approved before execution. b) Staff allocation is unnecessary for promotions. **Q5:** What must be included in a promotional budget proposal?

## Answers: **A1:** Staff, materials, sales modes. **A2:** B. **A3:** Banners, website, samples, starter kits, flyers, catalogues. **A4:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE. **A5:** Staffing costs, material production, venue, samples, printing.
