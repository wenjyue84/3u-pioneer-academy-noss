## Objectives
1. Explain how to determine staffing, materials and modes for sales/promotional activities.

## 1.0 Budgeting for Promotions
The supervisor determines:
- **Staff allocation** — Number of staff needed for promotional activities (counter, events, demonstrations).
- **Materials** — Banners, website content, venue rental, product samples, starter kits, flyers, catalogues.
- **Sales modes** — Vouchers, redemption cards, reward purchase programs, loyalty schemes.

## 2.0 Budget Proposal
Prepare a budget covering: staffing costs, material production costs, venue costs, sample product costs, printing costs. Submit to management for approval before proceeding.
