# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-C07/KT(4/4) | Ref: KP(4/4) | WA 4 | PINK

**Q1:** List FOUR methods for gathering feedback on promotional activities. **Q2 (MCQ):** A promotional report should include: A. Staff personal details B. Sales figures and feedback analysis C. Client medical history D. Equipment specifications **Q3 (T/F):** a) Client feedback analysis identifies what worked well. b) Improvement proposals are unnecessary if the promotion was successful. **Q4:** What should a promotional report contain? List FIVE items. **Q5:** Why is public feedback important in addition to client feedback?

## Answers: **A1:** Questionnaires, face-to-face interviews, online chat, review platforms. **A2:** B. **A3:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — Continuous improvement applies always. **A4:** Promotion details, participation numbers, sales figures, client/public feedback, improvement proposals. **A5:** Public feedback captures potential clients' perceptions and broader market response.
