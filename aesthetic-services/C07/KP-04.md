# KERTAS PENERANGAN — S960-002-3:2020-C07/KP(4/4) | WA 4: Sales & Promotional Report | WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain feedback gathering methods for promotional activities.
2. Describe report preparation and improvement proposals.

## 1.0 Feedback Gathering
| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Questionnaires | Written/digital surveys distributed after promotions |
| Face-to-face interviews | Direct conversations with clients |
| Online chat | Social media/website chat feedback |
| Review platforms | Google, Facebook reviews |

## 2.0 Feedback Analysis
- Analyse client and public feedback.
- Identify what worked well and what needs improvement.
- Record findings systematically.

## 3.0 Report Preparation
Include: promotion details, participation numbers, sales figures, client feedback, public feedback, improvement proposals. Submit to superior.
