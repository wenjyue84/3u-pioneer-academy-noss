**Q1:** List FIVE methods for executing promotional activities. **Q2:** Name FOUR occasions suitable for seasonal promotions. **Q3 (MCQ):** Before executing a promotional campaign, the plan must be: A. Posted online B. Approved by superior C. Kept confidential D. Sent to clients **Q4 (T/F):** a) Social media is not an effective promotional channel. b) Terms and conditions must be determined before a promotion. **Q5:** What elements should be included in a promotional flyer?

## Answers: **A1:** Calls, email, social media, face-to-face, SMS/WhatsApp. **A2:** Chinese New Year, Hari Raya, Christmas, Year-End, anniversary, birthdays. **A3:** B. **A4:** a) FALSE. b) TRUE. **A5:** Promotion details, products/services, duration, terms & conditions, pricing, contact info.
