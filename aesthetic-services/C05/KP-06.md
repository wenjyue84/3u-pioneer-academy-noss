# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KP(6/6)
**CU:** C05 — Body Wrap Therapy | **WA 6:** Upkeep Work Area
**Paper Colour:** WHITE

---

## Objectives
1. Describe sterilisation of body wrap tools and equipment.
2. Explain replenishment, inventory and waste disposal.

---

## 1.0 Sterilisation
- Wash brushes, spatulas, bowls with soap; soak in disinfectant.
- Wipe treatment couch with disinfectant.
- Launder towels at 60 C.
- Dispose of used plastic sheeting.
- Clean thermal/space blanket with damp cloth and disinfectant.

## 2.0 Replenishment
- Wrap products (mud, seaweed, herbs, clay), cleansing products, exfoliants, moisturisers, plastic sheeting, gloves, towels.

## 3.0 Inventory
- Physical count, stock cards, FIFO, reorder level, expiry checks.

## 4.0 Waste Disposal
- Used plastic sheeting → general waste
- Used gloves → clinical waste (yellow)
- Expired products → chemical waste
- Packaging → recycling
