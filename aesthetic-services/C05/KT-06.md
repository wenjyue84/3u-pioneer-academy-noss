# KERTAS TUGASAN / ASSIGNMENT SHEET
**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KT(6/6) | **Ref:** KP(6/6) | **WA 6:** Upkeep | **Paper:** PINK

**Q1:** How should body wrap brushes and bowls be sterilised? **Q2 (T/F):** a) Used plastic sheeting should be recycled. b) Expired wrap products are classified as chemical waste. c) Thermal blankets should be submerged in water for cleaning. **Q3:** List FOUR items to replenish after body wrap sessions. **Q4 (MCQ):** Used disposable gloves from body wrap therapy should be disposed in: A. General waste B. Clinical waste (yellow) C. Recycling D. Chemical waste **Q5:** Describe the inventory management process in THREE steps.

## Answers
**A1:** Wash with soap, soak in disinfectant solution, rinse, air dry. **A2:** a) FALSE — Contaminated; general waste. b) TRUE. c) FALSE — Wipe with damp cloth and disinfectant. **A3:** Wrap products, cleansing products, plastic sheeting, gloves, towels, exfoliants. **A4:** B. **A5:** Physical count, update stock cards/compare records, flag reorder items/submit report.
