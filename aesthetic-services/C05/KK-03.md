## Objectives: Prepare client and perform cleansing/deep cleansing.

## Procedure
1. Usher client to change; provide attire. 2. Store belongings. 3. Sterilise feet. 4. Drape hair (shower cap). 5. Ensure modesty. 6. Don PPE. 7. Select cleansing method per skin type. 8. Cleanse body with effleurage. 9. Remove with warm mitts. 10. Apply exfoliant; circular motions; focus on rough areas. 11. Remove thoroughly. 12. Pat dry. 13. Skin ready for wrap.
