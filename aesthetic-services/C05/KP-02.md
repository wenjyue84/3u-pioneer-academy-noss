# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KP(2/6)
**CU:** C05 — Body Wrap Therapy | **WA 2:** Consultation
**Paper Colour:** WHITE

---

## Objectives
1. Explain the consultation process for body wrap therapy.
2. Identify contraindications specific to body wraps.
3. Describe home care explanation and record keeping.

---

## 1.0 Client Profile Review
- Review existing consultation card from C01.
- Check skin type, conditions, allergies (especially to seaweed, mud, herbs, essential oils).
- Determine client's goal: detoxification, skin rejuvenation, inch loss, moisturising, toning.

## 2.0 Contraindications

### Absolute
- Allergy to wrap ingredients
- Open wounds, burns, skin infections
- Fever / acute illness
- Thrombosis / DVT
- Cancer
- Claustrophobia (severe — for full body wraps)

### Relative
- Pregnancy (certain products contraindicated; heat wraps avoided)
- Sensitive skin (patch test required)
- Asthma (strong aromas may trigger)
- Varicose veins (avoid wrapping directly)
- Diabetes (reduced sensation)

## 3.0 Consultation Record
- Record wrap-specific findings on card.
- Explain body wrap home care to the client.
- Answer client queries.
- Complete consultation record with signature.
