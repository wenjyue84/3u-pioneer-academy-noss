# KERTAS TUGASAN / ASSIGNMENT SHEET
**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KT(3/6) | **Ref:** KP(3/6) | **WA 3:** Pre-Procedure | **Paper:** PINK

**Q1:** Describe the deep cleansing procedure in FOUR steps. **Q2 (MCQ):** Why is deep cleansing performed before a body wrap? A. For relaxation B. Maximum product absorption C. To diagnose skin conditions D. For fragrance **Q3 (T/F):** a) Exfoliation focuses on rough areas like elbows and knees. b) The client's feet should not be sterilised before body wraps. **Q4:** List THREE cleansing methods suitable for pre-wrap preparation. **Q5:** Why is modesty important during body wrap preparation?

## Answers
**A1:** Apply exfoliant, massage in circles, focus on rough areas, remove with warm mitts. **A2:** B. **A3:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — Feet should be sterilised. **A4:** Body cleanser, exfoliating scrub (salt/sugar/enzyme), dry brushing. **A5:** Client is exposed during product application; modesty must be maintained through proper draping and communication.
