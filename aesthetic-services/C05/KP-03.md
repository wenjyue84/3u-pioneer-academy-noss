# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KP(3/6)
**CU:** C05 — Body Wrap Therapy | **WA 3:** Pre-Procedure Preparation
**Paper Colour:** WHITE

---

## Objectives
1. Describe client preparation procedures including change, feet sterilisation, and modesty.
2. Explain cleansing and deep cleansing methods for body wrap therapy.

---

## 1.0 Client Preparation
- Usher client to change area; provide service attire.
- Store belongings safely.
- Sterilise feet with antiseptic spray.
- Drape hair (shower cap).
- Ensure modesty throughout.
- Don PPE (apron, gloves).

## 2.0 Cleansing and Deep Cleansing

### 2.1 Selecting Methods
Select based on: client's skin type, wrap type to follow, manufacturer recommendations.

### 2.2 Cleansing Procedure
1. Apply body cleanser using effleurage movements.
2. Remove with warm damp mitts.
3. Pat dry.

### 2.3 Deep Cleansing
1. Apply exfoliating scrub (salt, sugar, or enzyme).
2. Massage in circular motions.
3. Focus on rough areas (elbows, knees, heels).
4. Remove thoroughly with warm damp mitts.
5. Skin is now prepared for maximum product absorption during the wrap.
