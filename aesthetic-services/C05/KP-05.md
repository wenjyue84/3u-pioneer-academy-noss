# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KP(5/6)
**CU:** C05 — Body Wrap Therapy | **WA 5:** Post-Wrap Advice
**Paper Colour:** WHITE

---

## Objectives
1. Explain after care and home care advice for body wrap clients.
2. Describe recording and scheduling procedures.

---

## 1.0 After Care
- Drink 2+ litres water in 24 hours (detoxification continues).
- Avoid heat treatments for 24 hours.
- Avoid sun exposure for 24 hours (skin sensitised).
- Avoid caffeine/alcohol for 24 hours.
- Mild redness is normal; fades within hours.
- Do not shower for 4-6 hours to allow residual product benefits.
- Light exercise encouraged to enhance circulation.

## 2.0 Home Care
- Apply moisturiser daily.
- Continue adequate hydration.
- Use recommended body products between sessions.
- Dry body brushing to maintain circulation benefits.

## 3.0 Recording
Record: wrap type, products used, duration, client response, results, advice given, date/signature.

## 4.0 Scheduling
Based on service plan: typically weekly for a course of 6-8 treatments.
