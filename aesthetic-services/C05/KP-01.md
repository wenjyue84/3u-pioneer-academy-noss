# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KP(1/6)
**CU:** C05 — Body Wrap Therapy | **WA 1:** Work Area Preparation
**Paper Colour:** WHITE

---

## Objectives
1. Explain hygiene and safety practices for body wrap service areas.
2. Identify tools, equipment and materials for body wrap therapy.
3. Describe equipment arrangement according to service flow.

---

## 1.0 Hygiene and Safety
- All surfaces disinfected before and after each client.
- Non-slip, waterproof flooring (wraps may drip).
- Adequate ventilation (herbal wraps produce aromatic vapours).
- Shower/rinse area accessible.
- PPE: apron, disposable gloves, mask (for mud/herbal products), closed-toe shoes.

---

## 2.0 Tools, Equipment and Materials

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Treatment couch with heating blanket/thermal blanket | Client positioning; maintaining warmth during wrap |
| Trolley | Organise products and tools |
| Plastic sheeting / cling film (*filem plastik*) | Wrap body; retain heat and product |
| Thermal/space blanket (*selimut haba*) | Insulation over wrap |
| Towels (large and small) | Draping, wiping, warmth |
| Bowls / brushes / spatulas | Mix and apply wrap products |
| Timer | Treatment duration |
| Body wrap products (mud, seaweed, herbal, clay) | Active treatment ingredients |
| Cleansing products | Pre-treatment skin preparation |
| Measuring tape | Pre/post body measurements |
| PPE (apron, gloves, mask) | Therapist protection |

---

## 3.0 Arrangement per Service Flow
1. Preparation zone — cleansing products, measuring tools
2. Application zone — wrap products, brushes, plastic sheeting
3. Treatment zone — heated couch/blanket for wrapping period
4. Rinse zone — shower access for product removal
5. Post-treatment zone — moisturiser, after care products
