# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET
**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KK(1/6) | **WA 1:** Work Area Prep | **Paper:** BLUE

## Objectives: Set up body wrap work area with proper hygiene, PPE, and equipment arrangement.

## Equipment: Treatment couch with thermal blanket, trolley, plastic sheeting, bowls/brushes/spatulas, wrap products, towels, PPE, disinfectant.

## Procedure
1. Don PPE (apron, gloves, mask, closed-toe shoes). 2. Disinfect couch, trolley, all surfaces. 3. Lay fresh linen on couch. 4. Check thermal blanket functionality. 5. Prepare wrap products on trolley (bowls, brushes, spatulas). 6. Cut plastic sheeting to size. 7. Prepare space/thermal blanket and towels. 8. Arrange in service flow: prep → application → treatment → rinse → post-treatment. 9. Ensure shower/rinse area clean and accessible. 10. Check ventilation. 11. Safety check complete.
