# KERTAS TUGASAN / ASSIGNMENT SHEET
**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KT(5/6) | **Ref:** KP(5/6) | **WA 5:** Post-Wrap Advice | **Paper:** PINK

**Q1:** List FIVE after care advice points for body wrap clients. **Q2 (MCQ):** How long should the client avoid showering after a body wrap? A. 1 hour B. 4-6 hours C. 12 hours D. 24 hours **Q3 (T/F):** a) Caffeine should be avoided for 24 hours after a wrap. b) Results from a single body wrap are permanent. **Q4:** State the typical course frequency for body wrap treatments. **Q5:** List THREE items to record after a body wrap session.

## Answers
**A1:** Drink 2L water, avoid heat 24h, avoid sun 24h, avoid caffeine/alcohol 24h, don't shower 4-6h, light exercise, apply moisturiser. **A2:** B. **A3:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — A course of 6-8 treatments is typically needed. **A4:** Weekly for 6-8 treatments. **A5:** Wrap type, products used, duration, client response, results, advice given.
