1. Wash brushes/spatulas/bowls with soap; soak in disinfectant; rinse; air dry. 2. Disinfect couch and thermal blanket (wipe, do not submerge). 3. Launder towels at 60 C. 4. Dispose used plastic sheeting (general waste). 5. Replenish wrap products, cleansing products, sheeting, gloves, towels. 6. Check expiry dates; FIFO. 7. Physical count; update stock cards. 8. Waste: gloves → clinical (yellow), sheeting → general, expired products → chemical, packaging → recycling. 9. Final check: area ready.
