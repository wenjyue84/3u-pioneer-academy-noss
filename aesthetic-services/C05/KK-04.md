# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET
**Kod:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KK(4/6) | **WA 4:** Carry Out Body Wrap Therapy | **Paper:** BLUE

## Objectives: Perform body wrap using selected method; monitor client; remove products.

## Equipment: Selected wrap product, brushes/spatulas, plastic sheeting, thermal blanket, towels, timer, water.

## Safety: Monitor every 5-10 min. Watch for overheating, itching, rash, breathing difficulty. Have water available.

## Procedure
1. Select wrap method per consultation (cold/warm/herbal/mud). 2. Prepare product per manufacturer instructions (mix/warm). 3. Apply product evenly to body using brush/gloved hands: legs → torso → arms. 4. Wrap body in plastic sheeting. 5. Cover with thermal/space blanket and towels. 6. Set timer (20-45 min depending on wrap type). 7. Monitor client every 5-10 min; offer water. 8. At completion: remove blanket and towels. 9. Unwrap plastic sheeting. 10. Remove product with warm damp towels or direct to shower. 11. Pat dry. 12. Apply moisturiser/finishing product. 13. Return belongings; usher to change. 14. Record feedback and results. 15. Store used tools for sterilisation.
