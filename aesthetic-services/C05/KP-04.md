# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C05/KP(4/6)
**CU:** C05 — Body Wrap Therapy | **WA 4:** Carry Out Body Wrap Therapy
**Paper Colour:** WHITE

---

## Objectives
1. Describe the different methods of body wraps (cold, warm, herbal, mud).
2. Explain the application and monitoring procedures.
3. Describe product removal and client transition.

---

## 1.0 Types of Body Wraps

### 1.1 Cold Wrap (Balutan Sejuk)
- **Products:** Cooling gels, menthol, peppermint, aloe vera
- **Effects:** Vasoconstriction, firms skin, reduces puffiness, energising
- **Suitable for:** Fluid retention, hot weather, post-exercise, sensitive skin
- **Temperature:** Room temperature or slightly chilled
- **Duration:** 20-30 minutes

### 1.2 Warm Wrap (Balutan Hangat)
- **Products:** Warming creams, cinnamon, ginger, thermal clays
- **Effects:** Vasodilation, perspiration, detoxification, relaxation, inch loss
- **Suitable for:** Cellulite, detoxification, weight management, muscle tension
- **Temperature:** Warm (applied warm, body heat maintained with thermal blanket)
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes

### 1.3 Herbal Wrap (Balutan Herba)
- **Products:** Herbal infusions, dried herbs in muslin, herbal pastes
- **Effects:** Depends on herbs — detoxifying, relaxing, stimulating, skin healing
- **Common herbs:** Rosemary (stimulating), lavender (relaxing), chamomile (soothing), green tea (antioxidant)
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes

### 1.4 Mud Wrap (Balutan Lumpur)
- **Products:** Dead Sea mud, marine mud, clay (kaolin, bentonite)
- **Effects:** Mineral absorption, detoxification, skin purification, oil absorption
- **Suitable for:** Oily/congested skin, detoxification, skin refining
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes

---

## 2.0 Application Procedure

1. **Prepare product** — Mix/warm as per manufacturer instructions.
2. **Apply product** — Using brushes or gloved hands, apply wrap product evenly over the body. Work systematically (legs → torso → arms).
3. **Wrap** — Wrap the body in plastic sheeting/cling film to seal product.
4. **Insulate** — Cover with thermal/space blanket and towels for warmth retention.
5. **Set timer** — Duration per wrap type.
6. **Monitor** — Check client every 5-10 minutes; offer water.
7. **Watch for reactions** — Overheating, itching, rash, breathing difficulty.

---

## 3.0 Product Removal and Transition

1. Remove thermal blanket and towels.
2. Unwrap plastic sheeting.
3. Remove residual product with warm damp towels or direct client to shower.
4. Pat dry.
5. Apply moisturiser or finishing product.
6. Return belongings; usher to change.
7. Record client feedback and results.
8. Store used tools for sterilisation.
