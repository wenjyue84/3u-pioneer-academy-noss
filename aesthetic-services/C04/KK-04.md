# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KK(4/10)
**CU:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy | **WA 4:** Galvanic Service
**Paper Colour:** BLUE

---

## Objectives
1. Perform galvanic electrotherapy (iontophoresis/desincrustation). 2. Demonstrate correct polarity application. 3. Monitor and adjust parameters safely.

## Equipment: Galvanic unit, electrodes (roller/disc), conductive gel/treatment product, desincrustation solution, timer, consultation card.

## Procedure
1. Obtain card; confirm suitability. 2. Thermal/tactile tests confirmed. 3. Apply appropriate product (iontophoresis product or desincrustation solution). 4. Select correct polarity based on product/treatment. 5. Apply active electrode to treatment area. 6. Apply indifferent electrode. 7. Turn on at zero; increase gradually (max 1-5 mA body). 8. Monitor client response. 9. Maintain for prescribed duration (10-20 min). 10. Reduce to zero before moving electrodes. 11. Remove electrodes; wipe skin. 12. Client transition; record results. 13. Clean equipment; replenish materials.
