**Q1:** List FIVE (5) after care advice points for electrotherapy clients.

**Q2 (MCQ):** Post-electrotherapy redness typically: A. Indicates a serious burn B. Is normal and fades within hours C. Requires medical attention D. Lasts 1-2 weeks

**Q3:** List FOUR (4) items to record on the client card after electrotherapy.

**Q4 (T/F):** a) Heat treatments may be applied immediately after electrotherapy. b) Most electrotherapy treatments require a course of sessions. c) Sun exposure should be avoided for 24 hours after electrotherapy.

**Q5:** State the typical course frequency for faradic/EMS treatment.

---

## Answers
**A1:** Drink water, mild redness is normal, avoid heat 24h, avoid sun 24h, rest if fatigued, report prolonged redness/blistering. **A2:** B. **A3:** Modality used, treatment area, parameters (intensity/duration), client response, adverse reactions, therapist/date. **A4:** a) FALSE. b) TRUE. c) TRUE. **A5:** 2-3 times per week for 6-12 weeks.
