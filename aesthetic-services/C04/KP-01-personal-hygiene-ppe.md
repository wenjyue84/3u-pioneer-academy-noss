## Objectives
1. Explain personal hygiene and PPE requirements for electrotherapy.
2. Identify, describe and explain the arrangement of electrotherapy tools, equipment and materials.

---

## 1.0 Personal Hygiene and PPE

| PPE Item | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Apron (*apron*) | Protect uniform from conductive gels and products |
| Mask (*topeng muka*) | Protection from aerosols (high frequency) |
| Disposable gloves | Hygiene during electrode placement and gel application |
| Closed-toe shoes | Electrical safety; anti-static soles preferred |

Therapist must: wash hands before and after each client, maintain short filed nails, remove metallic jewellery (electrical conduction risk).

---

## 2.0 Electrotherapy Equipment Overview

| Equipment | Function | Key Parameters |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| **Faradic/EMS unit** (*unit faradik/EMS*) | Passive muscle contraction via interrupted direct current | Frequency, pulse width, intensity |
| **Galvanic unit** (*unit galvanik*) | Iontophoresis (product penetration) and desincrustation (deep cleansing) | Polarity (anode/cathode), intensity (mA) |
| **Vacuum suction machine** (*mesin sedutan vakum*) | Lymphatic drainage, cellulite reduction | Suction pressure, cup size |
| **Mechanical/gyratory massager** (*pengurut mekanikal*) | Mechanical massage using rotating applicators | Speed, applicator head type |
| **High frequency unit** (*unit frekuensi tinggi*) | Stimulate circulation, germicidal effect (direct/indirect methods) | Electrode type, intensity |
| **Micro-current unit** (*unit arus mikro*) | Muscle re-education, skin tightening, anti-ageing | Current (microamps), frequency, waveform |

---

## 3.0 Equipment Arrangement

Arrange electrotherapy area in treatment flow:
1. Client preparation zone — couch, trolley with preparation products
2. Treatment zone — electrotherapy equipment positioned within arm's reach
3. Product zone — conductive gels, electrode pads, covers
4. Clean-up zone — waste bins, sanitising products

Equipment should be on stable, non-conductive surfaces. Cords organized to prevent tripping.

---

## 4.0 Pre-Session Checks

1. Inspect all equipment cables for damage.
2. Check plugs and connections are secure.
3. Test each machine on low setting before client use.
4. Verify earth/ground connections are intact.
5. Check electrode pads/applicators for wear and hygiene.
6. Record equipment check in the log book.

---

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Electrotherapy equipment operates with mains electricity. Never operate near water, on damp surfaces, or with wet hands. Faulty, damaged, or improperly earthed equipment presents a risk of electric shock to both therapist and client. Any equipment showing cable damage, sparking, or unusual sounds must be taken out of service immediately and tagged for repair before reuse.
