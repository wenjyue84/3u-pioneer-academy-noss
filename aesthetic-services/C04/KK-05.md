## Objectives
1. Perform vacuum suction for lymphatic drainage. 2. Select correct ventouse and pressure. 3. Monitor client response.

## Equipment: Vacuum suction machine, ventouses (various sizes), massage oil/cream, timer, consultation card.

## Procedure
1. Obtain card; confirm suitability. 2. Thermal/tactile tests confirmed. 3. Apply massage medium to treatment area. 4. Select ventouse size for body area. 5. Set suction pressure (start at 20%). 6. Place ventouse on skin; glide towards nearest lymph node. 7. Release suction (press vent hole) before lifting cup. 8. Work systematically across treatment area. 9. Increase pressure gradually (max 40% body). 10. Monitor for discomfort; do not hold stationary. 11. Duration: 15-20 min per area. 12. Remove; wipe skin. 13. Client transition; record results. 14. Clean ventouses; replenish.
