## Objectives
1. Perform micro-current treatment on body areas. 2. Demonstrate correct probe technique. 3. Monitor client (sub-sensory treatment).

## Equipment: Micro-current unit, probes/cotton tips, micro-current gel/serum, timer, consultation card.

## Procedure
1. Obtain card; confirm suitability. 2. Thermal/tactile tests confirmed. 3. Cleanse treatment area. 4. Apply conductive gel/serum. 5. Set parameters (10-600 uA, frequency as per protocol). 6. Using probes: place both probes on skin at specific points. 7. Work along muscle groups (origin to insertion). 8. Maintain continuous probe contact. 9. Client should feel nothing or very mild tingling — if felt, reduce. 10. Duration: 30-60 min. 11. At completion: turn off unit. 12. Remove probes; wipe skin. 13. Client transition; record results. 14. Clean probes; replace cotton tips; replenish.
