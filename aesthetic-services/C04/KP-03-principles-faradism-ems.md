## Objectives
1. Explain the principles of faradism and electrical muscle stimulation (EMS).
2. Describe the procedure for body faradic/EMS treatment.
3. Explain precautions for duration and intensity.

---

## 1.0 Principles of Faradism/EMS

**Faradism** (*faradisme*) uses an interrupted direct current to cause involuntary muscle contraction. **EMS** (Electrical Muscle Stimulation / Rangsangan Otot Elektrik) is the modern equivalent.

### 1.1 How It Works
- Electrical current stimulates motor nerves.
- Motor nerves trigger muscle fibres to contract.
- Mimics voluntary exercise without physical exertion.
- Used for muscle toning, body contouring, and improving muscle tone.

### 1.2 Physiological Effects
| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Muscle contraction | Passive exercise for muscles |
| Improved muscle tone | Firmer, more defined muscles |
| Increased circulation | Blood flow to treated area |
| Lymphatic stimulation | Waste product removal |
| Body contouring | Improved shape through toning |

---

## 2.0 Equipment and Parameters

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Faradic/EMS unit | Main machine with adjustable settings |
| Electrode pads (*pad elektrod*) | Placed on motor points of target muscles |
| Conductive gel (*gel konduktif*) | Applied between pad and skin for conductivity |
| Straps/bandages | Secure electrode pads in place |
| Timer | Control treatment duration |

### 2.1 Treatment Parameters
| Parameter | Typical Range |
|-----------|--------------|
| Frequency | 30-100 Hz |
| Pulse width | 100-400 microseconds |
| Intensity | Gradually increased until visible contraction |
| Duration per area | 15-20 minutes |
| Contraction:rest ratio | 1:1 to 1:3 (e.g., 6 sec on, 6-18 sec off) |

---

## 3.0 Electrode Placement

- Electrodes placed on the **motor point** of the target muscle.
- Motor point: where the motor nerve enters the muscle belly.
- Correct placement ensures effective, comfortable contraction.
- Incorrect placement: weak contraction, discomfort, or no response.

### 3.1 Common Body Motor Points
| Muscle Group | Location |
|-------------|----------|
| Rectus abdominis | Either side of midline, above and below navel |
| External obliques | Lateral abdomen |
| Quadriceps | Anterior thigh |
| Gluteals | Centre of buttock |
| Biceps | Mid-upper arm (anterior) |
| Triceps | Mid-upper arm (posterior) |

---

## 4.0 Procedure Overview

1. Obtain consultation card; confirm suitability.
2. Apply conductive gel to motor points.
3. Place electrode pads; secure with straps.
4. Perform thermal and tactile tests.
5. Turn on machine; increase intensity GRADUALLY.
6. Ensure visible, comfortable muscle contraction.
7. Monitor client every few minutes.
8. Maintain treatment for prescribed duration.
9. At completion: reduce intensity to zero before removing pads.
10. Remove pads; wipe residual gel from skin.
11. Usher client to change; return belongings.
12. Clean and sterilise equipment.
13. Record results on consultation card.

---

## 5.0 Precautions

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Faradic/EMS current must NEVER be placed across the thorax (chest), over the carotid sinuses (neck), or across the brain (temples). Such placements risk cardiac arrhythmia, circulatory collapse, or seizure. Always start intensity at zero and increase only until a visible, comfortable contraction is achieved — pain indicates incorrect placement or excessive intensity.

- **Duration:** Do not exceed 20 minutes per muscle group per session.
- **Intensity:** Always start at zero; increase gradually; visible contraction without pain is the target endpoint.
- **Padding:** Ensure even, full contact — air gaps concentrate current and risk localised burns.
- **Skin checks:** Inspect skin before treatment, at 5-minute intervals during, and after completion.
- **Never** place electrodes over the heart, carotid sinuses (neck), or across the cranium.
- **Contraindications specific to faradic/EMS:** Muscle disease (myopathy), recent fracture, acute inflammation, thrombosis, recent surgery in treatment area.
