# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KK(7/10)
**CU:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy | **WA 7:** High Frequency Service
**Paper Colour:** BLUE

---

## Objectives
1. Perform direct and indirect high frequency methods. 2. Select correct electrode. 3. Apply safety precautions.

## Equipment: HF unit, glass electrodes (mushroom, roller), saturator electrode, talcum powder/cream, consultation card.

## Safety: Remove all metal jewellery from client. No flammable products. Direct method: place electrode on skin BEFORE switching on.

## Procedure — Direct Method
1. Remove client jewellery. 2. Apply talcum powder or appropriate product. 3. Select electrode (mushroom for large areas). 4. Place electrode on skin. 5. Switch on; increase to comfortable level. 6. Glide electrode over treatment area. 7. Duration: 5-10 min per area. 8. Reduce to zero; switch off. 9. Remove electrode from skin.

## Procedure — Indirect Method
1. Client holds saturator electrode. 2. Apply massage cream to treatment area. 3. Switch on; increase gradually. 4. Therapist performs effleurage/petrissage (current flows through client). 5. Sparking occurs when lifting fingers (normal). 6. Duration: 5-10 min per area. 7. Reduce to zero; switch off. 8. Client releases saturator.

## Post: Wipe skin; client transition; clean electrodes (alcohol + UV cabinet); record results.
