## Objectives
1. Explain the principles of mechanical/gyratory massage.
2. Describe applicator types and their uses.
3. Explain precautions for mechanical massage.

---

## 1.0 Principles

**Mechanical/gyratory massage** (*urutan mekanikal/giratori*) uses an electrically powered device with interchangeable applicator heads that rotate, vibrate, or oscillate to massage body tissues.

### 1.1 Effects
- Deep tissue relaxation and tension release
- Improved circulation
- Cellulite reduction
- Fat emulsification
- Lymphatic drainage stimulation
- Muscle tone improvement

---

## 2.0 Applicator Types

| Applicator | Description | Use |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| **Round/ball sponge** | Soft foam ball | General body massage; large, fleshy areas |
| **Curved/football** | Elongated foam | Legs, arms, curved body areas |
| **Spike/pin cushion** | Rubber with raised points | Stimulating; cellulite areas |
| **Smooth disc/plate** | Flat rubber | Relaxation; broad areas |
| **Eggbox** | Undulating surface | Kneading effect; fleshy areas |

---

## 3.0 Treatment Parameters

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Speed | Low to medium for relaxation; medium to high for stimulation |
| Pressure | Light to moderate; heavier on fleshy areas |
| Direction | Towards lymph nodes; avoid circular on one spot |
| Duration | 15-30 minutes per area |
| Medium | Talcum powder (reduces friction) or light oil |

---

## 4.0 Precautions

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Mechanical/gyratory massage must NEVER be used over bony prominences (spine, ribs, shoulder blades, knee), varicose veins, or inflamed joints. Continued application over these areas risks periosteal bruising, vascular damage, and aggravation of inflammation. The machine must be in motion across the skin before and after contact — never press a stationary rotating head onto skin.

- Avoid bony prominences, spine, ribs, breast tissue, and neck at all times.
- Do not remain on one spot with the applicator — bruising occurs rapidly.
- Reduce speed and pressure when working over fleshy areas with thinner subcutaneous tissue (e.g., inner arm, inner thigh).
- Check applicator attachment is secure and locked before switching machine on.
- Clean and disinfect all applicator heads between clients using approved disinfectant.
- **Contraindications specific to mechanical/gyratory:** Osteoporosis (*osteoporosis*), inflamed or arthritic joints, varicose veins, recent haematoma, bruising tendency, skin hypersensitivity.

---

## 5.0 Machine Specifications

| Feature | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Motor type | Electric motor with eccentric cam or gear drive |
| Speed range | Typically 1,000–3,500 RPM (variable via dial) |
| Applicator attachment | Bayonet or screw-fit; interchangeable |
| Power supply | Mains (220–240V, 50Hz) |
| Safety features | Thermal cut-off; applicator lock; variable speed control |
