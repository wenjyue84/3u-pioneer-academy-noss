**Q1:** List FIVE (5) absolute contraindications for ALL electrotherapy treatments.

**Q2 (MCQ):** What test must be performed before electrotherapy to check skin sensation? A. Patch test B. Thermal and tactile test C. Allergy test D. Blood pressure test

**Q3:** Describe the thermal test procedure and what constitutes a pass/fail.

**Q4 (T/F):** a) A client with a pacemaker may receive vacuum suction treatment. b) If a client fails the tactile test, electrotherapy should not proceed on that area. c) Vacuum suction is contraindicated for clients with bruising tendency.

**Q5:** List the steps for preparing a client for electrotherapy service (minimum 6 steps).

---

## Answers
**A1:** Any 5: Pacemaker, metal implants in area, uncontrolled epilepsy, pregnancy, cancer in area, thrombosis/DVT, skin infections/open wounds, loss of sensation. **A2:** B. **A3:** Apply hot and cold test tubes to area; client must correctly identify hot vs cold. If unable → fail → do not proceed. **A4:** a) FALSE — Pacemaker is absolute contraindication for all electrotherapy. b) TRUE. c) TRUE. **A5:** Obtain card, review findings, explain service, answer queries, store belongings/change, cleanse area, position client, perform thermal/tactile tests, record results.
