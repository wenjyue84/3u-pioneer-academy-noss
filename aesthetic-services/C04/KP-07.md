# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KP(7/10)
**CU:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy | **WA 7:** High Frequency Electrotherapy
**Paper Colour:** WHITE

---

## Objectives
1. Explain the principles of high frequency current.
2. Describe direct and indirect methods of high frequency application.
3. Explain precautions for high frequency treatment.

---

## 1.0 Principles of High Frequency

**High frequency** (*frekuensi tinggi*) uses a rapidly oscillating alternating current that produces heat, ozone, and a germicidal effect. It is used to stimulate circulation, promote healing, and improve skin condition.

---

## 2.0 Two Methods

### 2.1 Direct Method (Kaedah Langsung)
- The glass electrode is applied directly to the client's skin.
- Electrode produces a mild tingling sensation and warmth.
- Produces ozone (O3) — germicidal and healing.
- **Effects:** Stimulates, warms, increases circulation, germicidal.
- **Used for:** Sluggish skin, acne-prone areas, stimulating treatments.

### 2.2 Indirect Method (Kaedah Tidak Langsung)
- Client holds the saturator (metal bar) electrode.
- Current flows through the client's body.
- Therapist performs manual massage movements; sparking occurs when lifting fingers.
- **Effects:** Relaxing, warming, nourishing, improves product absorption.
- **Used for:** Relaxation, mature skin, improving product penetration.

---

## 3.0 Electrode Types (Body)

| Electrode | Shape | Use |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| Mushroom | Flat, round | Large body areas (back, abdomen) |
| Roller | Cylindrical | Arms, legs — gliding application |
| Saturator (indirect) | Metal bar | Client holds; indirect method only |

---

## 4.0 Treatment Parameters

| Parameter | Direct Method | Indirect Method |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Electrode type | Glass (mushroom/roller) | Saturator bar (client holds) |
| Application | Electrode on skin | Therapist uses hands on client |
| Intensity | Low to medium | Low to medium |
| Duration per area | 5–10 minutes | 5–10 minutes |
| Frequency of machine | 100,000–250,000 Hz (oscillating AC) | Same |
| Ozone production | Yes (germicidal) | Minimal |
| Client sensation | Mild warmth, tingling | Warmth, relaxation; sparking at therapist's fingertips |

---

## 5.0 Precautions

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** High frequency must NEVER be used near flammable products (hairspray, alcohol-based sprays, oxygen equipment). The electrical discharge from the glass electrode can ignite volatile substances, causing fire or burns. Always ensure all flammable products are sealed and away from the treatment area before commencing high frequency treatment.

- Remove all metal jewellery from client before treatment.
- Do not use near flammable products, aerosols, or oxygen equipment.
- For the direct method: apply electrode to skin before switching on; switch off before removing electrode.
- Do not spark excessively — excessive sparking causes discomfort, erythema, and risk of burns.
- Avoid use over excessive body hair — risk of singeing (*terbakar*).
- Duration: 5–10 minutes per area; do not exceed 10 minutes.
- Do not use on sensitive, broken, or highly vascular skin.
- **Contraindications specific to high frequency:** Excessive body/facial hair, metallic implants in area, high anxiety (sparking sound distressing), rosacea/telangiectasia.
