## Objectives
1. Perform gyratory massage with correct applicators. 2. Apply appropriate speed and pressure. 3. Monitor client response.

## Equipment: Gyratory massager, applicator heads (sponge, spike, disc, football), talcum powder or oil, consultation card.

## Procedure
1. Obtain card; confirm suitability. 2. Thermal/tactile tests confirmed. 3. Select applicator head; attach securely. 4. Apply talcum powder or light oil to area. 5. Start at low speed. 6. Apply applicator to fleshy body areas. 7. Move continuously — never hold stationary. 8. Avoid bony areas, spine, breasts, neck, kidneys. 9. Increase speed/pressure on fleshy areas as tolerated. 10. Duration: 15-30 min per area. 11. Monitor client comfort. 12. Remove; wipe residual product. 13. Client transition; record results. 14. Detach, clean and disinfect applicators; replenish.
