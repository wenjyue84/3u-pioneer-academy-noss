# PELAN MENGAJAR TEORI / THEORY LESSON PLAN

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04
**CU:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy / Elektroterapi Badan
**Paper Colour:** YELLOW | **Theory Duration:** 14 hours | **KP:** 10 | **KT:** 10

---

## Instructional Plan

### Session 1: KP(1/10)+KT — Electrotherapy Work Area, PPE and Equipment (80 min)
### Session 2: KP(2/10)+KT — Client Assessment and Contraindications (90 min)
### Session 3: KP(3/10)+KT — Faradism/EMS (100 min)
### Session 4: KP(4/10)+KT — Galvanic Current (100 min)
### Session 5: KP(5/10)+KT — Vacuum Suction (90 min)
### Session 6: KP(6/10)+KT — Mechanical/Gyratory Massage (90 min)
### Session 7: KP(7/10)+KT — High Frequency (100 min)
### Session 8: KP(8/10)+KT — Micro-Current (100 min)
### Session 9: KP(9/10)+KT — Post-Electrotherapy Advice (70 min)
### Session 10: KP(10/10)+KT — Equipment Maintenance and Storage (60 min)

Each session follows: Persediaan (10 min) → Penyampaian (35-50 min) → Penggunaan (25-30 min) → Pengesahan (10 min).
