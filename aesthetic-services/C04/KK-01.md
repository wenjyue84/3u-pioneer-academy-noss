## Objectives
1. Demonstrate correct PPE usage. 2. Identify, select and arrange electrotherapy equipment. 3. Perform pre-session equipment checks.

## Equipment: All electrotherapy units, trolley, disinfectant, PPE, electrode pads, gels, towels, equipment log.

## Procedure
1. Don PPE (apron, mask, closed-toe shoes, gloves ready). 2. Remove metallic jewellery. 3. Inspect all equipment cables for damage. 4. Check plugs and earth connections. 5. Test each machine on low setting. 6. Check electrode pads/applicators condition. 7. Record checks in equipment log. 8. Clean all equipment surfaces with disinfectant. 9. Arrange in service flow: prep zone → treatment zone → product zone → cleanup zone. 10. Ensure non-conductive surfaces, cords organised.
