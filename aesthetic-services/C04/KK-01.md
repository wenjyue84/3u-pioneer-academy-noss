# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KK(1/10)
**Competency Unit:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy / Elektroterapi Badan
**Work Activity 1:** Prepare Body Electrotherapy Work Area
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** BLUE / BIRU

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this work sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Demonstrate correct PPE usage for electrotherapy work area preparation.
2. Inspect and test all electrotherapy equipment for safe operation.
3. Clean and disinfect equipment and surfaces to professional hygiene standards.
4. Arrange the work area in correct service flow for client safety.

## Safety Precautions (Langkah Keselamatan)

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** All electrotherapy equipment operates on mains electricity. Damaged cables or faulty earth connections create shock hazards. Do NOT use any equipment with damaged insulation — remove from service immediately and report.

1. Wear PPE (apron, mask, closed-toe shoes) throughout setup.
2. Remove all metallic jewellery before handling electrical equipment.
3. Inspect every cable for cuts, fraying, or melted insulation before plugging in.
4. Do not overload electrical sockets — use one machine per socket.
5. Ensure RCD (residual current device) protection is active for all circuits.
6. Keep all electrical equipment away from water and wet surfaces.
7. Fire extinguisher (CO2) must be accessible; first aid kit must be within reach.

## Procedure (Prosedur)

### Step 1: Personal Preparation (Persediaan Peribadi)
1.1 Wash hands thoroughly with antibacterial soap.
1.2 Don PPE: apron, mask, closed-toe shoes. Gloves ready for use.
1.3 Remove all metallic jewellery (rings, bracelets, watches).
1.4 Ensure clean uniform and neat personal presentation.

### Step 2: Equipment Cable and Safety Inspection (Pemeriksaan Kabel dan Keselamatan)
2.1 Lay out each electrotherapy unit on the trolley or equipment stand.
2.2 Inspect all power cables: check for cuts, fraying, kinks, or melted insulation.
2.3 Inspect all plugs: check for cracked casings, loose pins, scorch marks.
2.4 Check earthing connections on all metal-cased equipment.
2.5 Remove any equipment with damaged cables from service; tag and report to supervisor.
2.6 Record inspection results in the equipment log book with date and signature.

### Step 3: Equipment Function Testing (Ujian Fungsi Peralatan)
3.1 **Galvanic unit:** Power on; check milliampere meter functions; verify polarity switch.
3.2 **High frequency unit:** Power on; test vacuum electrode produces violet/orange glow.
3.3 **Vacuum suction unit:** Power on; apply cup to forearm; verify suction and release.
3.4 **Faradic/EMS unit:** Power on; check intensity control from minimum; verify pulse output.
3.5 **Micro-current unit:** Power on; verify display; check probe connections.
3.6 **Gyratory massager:** Power on; check all speed settings; verify attachment security.
3.7 Record all test results in the equipment log book.

### Step 4: Clean and Disinfect Equipment (Pembersihan dan Pensterilan Peralatan)
4.1 Power OFF and unplug all equipment before cleaning.
4.2 Wipe all machine housing surfaces with disinfectant-dampened cloth — do NOT spray liquid directly onto machines.
4.3 Clean all electrode pads/applicators: wipe with 70% isopropyl alcohol solution.
4.4 Replace any cracked, torn, or discoloured electrode pads.
4.5 Apply disposable covers to reusable electrodes where available.
4.6 Wipe treatment couch and trolley surfaces with disinfectant; place fresh linen on couch.
4.7 Allow all surfaces to air dry before placing equipment back.

### Step 5: Arrange Work Area (Susun Atur Kawasan Kerja)
5.1 Set up in service flow: preparation zone → treatment zone → product/gel zone → cleanup zone.
5.2 Place each electrotherapy unit in designated position — no cords crossing treatment path.
5.3 Organise cords with cable ties; ensure no tripping hazards.
5.4 Place conductive gel and disposable covers on the trolley within therapist reach.
5.5 Position fresh towels on the treatment couch footrest and headrest.

### Step 6: Client Environment Preparation (Persediaan Persekitaran Klien)
6.1 Ensure room temperature is comfortable (24–26°C) — check thermostat.
6.2 Ensure adequate, even lighting for treatment area.
6.3 Confirm that the client changing area is clean and private.
6.4 Place clean, undamaged client gown/wrap and slippers in the changing area; inspect each item before placing.
6.5 Confirm first aid kit location is known and accessible.
6.6 Ensure a pre-treatment instruction notice has been issued to the client (via reception or verbally) requiring removal of ALL metallic accessories — jewellery, piercings, watches, hairpins, hearing aids — before entering the treatment room.

### Step 7: Pre-Client Entry Verification (Semakan Sebelum Klien Masuk)
7.1 Receive the client at the treatment room entrance; verify client identity against booking.
7.2 Visually inspect and verbally confirm the client has removed all metal jewellery, piercings, watches and metallic accessories.
7.3 Ask the client to confirm: no pacemaker, no implanted electrical device, no metal implants in the treatment area (refer to C04 contraindication list if uncertain).
7.4 Direct the client to the changing area; allow full privacy; knock before re-entering.
7.5 Assist the client onto the treatment couch in a supine position; provide blanket/towel draping for warmth and dignity.

### Step 8: Final Check (Semakan Akhir)
8.1 Survey the entire work area — confirm no tripping hazards.
8.2 Verify all equipment is plugged into separate sockets; RCD tested.
8.3 Confirm log book entries are complete with date and signature.
8.4 Confirm client is comfortable, correctly positioned and draped; area ready for treatment.
