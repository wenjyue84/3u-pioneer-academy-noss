**Q1 (MCQ):** Faradic/EMS works by: A. Heating tissue B. Stimulating motor nerves to cause muscle contraction C. Chemical reaction in skin D. Ultrasonic vibration

**Q2:** List FOUR (4) physiological effects of faradic/EMS treatment.

**Q3:** Explain the importance of placing electrodes on the motor point of a muscle.

**Q4 (T/F):** a) Intensity should start at maximum for best results. b) Electrodes must never be placed over the heart. c) A contraction:rest ratio of 1:1 means 6 seconds on, 6 seconds off.

**Q5:** Describe the complete procedure for faradic/EMS treatment in 8 key steps.

---

## Answers
**A1:** B. **A2:** Muscle contraction, improved muscle tone, increased circulation, lymphatic stimulation, body contouring. **A3:** Motor point is where the motor nerve enters the muscle belly; correct placement ensures effective, comfortable contraction. Incorrect placement causes weak/no contraction or discomfort. **A4:** a) FALSE — Always start at zero and increase gradually. b) TRUE. c) TRUE. **A5:** Obtain card → apply gel → place pads → thermal/tactile test → turn on, increase gradually → monitor → reduce to zero → remove pads/wipe → record.
