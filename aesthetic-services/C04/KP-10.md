## Objectives
1. Describe sterilisation and maintenance of electrotherapy equipment.
2. Explain product replenishment and inventory management.
3. Describe waste disposal for electrotherapy consumables.

---

## 1.0 Equipment Sterilisation and Maintenance

| Equipment | Cleaning/Sterilisation |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Electrode pads (reusable) | Wipe with antiseptic; store in sealed bags |
| Electrode pads (disposable) | Discard after each client |
| Glass electrodes (HF) | Wipe with alcohol; store in UV cabinet |
| Ventouses/cups | Wash with soap; soak in disinfectant; air dry |
| Gyratory applicators | Detach; wash with soap; disinfect; air dry |
| Micro-current probes | Wipe tips with alcohol; replace cotton tips |
| Machine housings | Wipe with damp cloth (not wet); do not submerge |
| Cables and leads | Inspect for damage; wipe clean; coil loosely for storage |

---

## 2.0 Replenishment

- Conductive gels, creams, ampoules
- Disposable electrode pads, cotton tips
- Disposable gloves, tissues
- Cleaning/disinfecting products

---

## 3.0 Inventory and Waste Disposal

Standard inventory procedures (stock cards, FIFO, physical count). Waste: used disposable pads → clinical waste (yellow bag); used cotton → clinical waste; packaging → recycling; expired gels/products → chemical waste.
