## Objectives
1. Explain how to interpret body consultation findings for electrotherapy selection.
2. Identify contraindications specific to electrotherapy treatments.
3. Describe client preparation procedures for electrotherapy.

---

## 1.0 Interpreting Consultation Findings

Review the client's body consultation card/form to determine:
- Which electrotherapy modality is most suitable
- Body areas requiring treatment
- Muscle condition (hypertonic/hypotonic)
- Cellulite grade and distribution
- Skin sensitivity
- Prior electrotherapy experience

---

## 2.0 Electrotherapy Contraindications

### 2.1 Absolute Contraindications (All electrotherapy)
| Condition | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Pacemaker (*perentak jantung*) | Electrical interference |
| Metal implants/pins in treatment area | Electrical conduction, burns |
| Epilepsy (uncontrolled) | Seizure risk |
| Pregnancy | Risk to foetus |
| Cancer in treatment area | Risk of stimulating growth |
| Thrombosis/DVT | Risk of dislodging clots |
| Skin infections, open wounds | Infection spread, burns |
| Loss of skin sensation | Cannot detect overheating or discomfort |

### 2.2 Modality-Specific Contraindications
| Modality | Additional Contraindications |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Faradic/EMS | Muscle disease, severe atrophy |
| Galvanic | Broken skin, excessive sensitivity |
| Vacuum suction | Bruising tendency, fragile skin, thin skin |
| High frequency | Excessive facial/body hair (sparking risk), metal in area |
| Micro-current | Same as general plus severe heart conditions |

---

## 3.0 Thermal and Tactile Tests

Before electrotherapy, perform:
- **Thermal test:** Apply hot and cold test tubes/cotton to the treatment area; client must distinguish between hot and cold correctly.
- **Tactile (sharp/dull) test:** Apply sharp and blunt ends of an orange stick; client must distinguish correctly.
- If client fails either test: DO NOT proceed with electrotherapy on that area.

---

## 4.0 Client Preparation

1. Obtain consultation card; review findings.
2. Explain recommended electrotherapy service to client.
3. Answer client queries.
4. Store belongings; change into service attire.
5. Cleanse treatment area on client's body.
6. Position client on couch.
7. Perform thermal and tactile tests.
8. Record test results.
