# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KP(2/10)
**CU:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy | **WA 2:** Prepare Client for Body Electrotherapy
**Paper Colour:** WHITE

---

## Objectives
1. Explain how to interpret body consultation findings for electrotherapy selection.
2. Identify contraindications specific to electrotherapy treatments.
3. Describe client preparation procedures for electrotherapy.

---

## 1.0 Interpreting Consultation Findings

Review the client's body consultation card/form to determine:
- Which electrotherapy modality is most suitable
- Body areas requiring treatment
- Muscle condition (hypertonic/hypotonic)
- Cellulite grade and distribution
- Skin sensitivity
- Prior electrotherapy experience

---

## 2.0 Electrotherapy Contraindications

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Failure to screen for contraindications before electrotherapy may result in serious harm including cardiac events, burns, nerve damage, or embolism. A complete consultation and sensitivity tests MUST be completed and documented before any electrotherapy treatment commences.

### 2.1 Absolute Contraindications (All electrotherapy)
| Condition | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Pacemaker (*perentak jantung*) | Electrical interference — risk of cardiac arrest |
| Metal implants/pins in treatment area | Electrical conduction and localised burn risk |
| Epilepsy (uncontrolled) | Risk of triggering seizure |
| Pregnancy (*mengandung*) | Risk of foetal harm from electrical current |
| Cancer (*kanser*) in or near treatment area | Risk of stimulating tumour growth or metastasis |
| Thrombosis/DVT (*trombosis*) | Risk of dislodging clot leading to embolism |
| Skin infections, open wounds (*luka terbuka*) | Infection spread; concentration of current at wound edges |
| Loss of skin sensation (*kehilangan deria sentuh*) | Client cannot report overheating or electrical discomfort |
| Acute inflammation or fever (*demam*) | Electrical stimulation may worsen inflammation |

### 2.2 Modality-Specific Contraindications
| Modality | Additional Contraindications |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Faradic/EMS | Muscle disease (myopathy), muscle atrophy, recent fracture/surgery in area |
| Galvanic | Broken skin, excessive skin sensitivity, hypersensitivity to electrical current |
| Vacuum suction | Bruising tendency, fragile or thin skin, varicose veins over treatment area |
| Mechanical/Gyratory | Osteoporosis, bony prominences, inflamed joints, varicose veins |
| High frequency | Excessive body hair (sparking/singeing risk), metallic implants in area |
| Micro-current | Severe cardiac conditions, phlebitis, recent scar tissue (< 6 months) |

---

## 3.0 Thermal and Tactile Tests

Before electrotherapy, perform:
- **Thermal test:** Apply hot and cold test tubes/cotton to the treatment area; client must distinguish between hot and cold correctly.
- **Tactile (sharp/dull) test:** Apply sharp and blunt ends of an orange stick; client must distinguish correctly.
- If client fails either test: DO NOT proceed with electrotherapy on that area.

---

## 4.0 Client Preparation

1. Obtain consultation card; review findings.
2. Explain recommended electrotherapy service to client.
3. Answer client queries.
4. Store belongings; change into service attire.
5. Cleanse treatment area on client's body.
6. Position client on couch.
7. Perform thermal and tactile tests.
8. Record test results.
