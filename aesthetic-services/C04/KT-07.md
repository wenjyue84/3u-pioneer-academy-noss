# KERTAS TUGASAN / ASSIGNMENT SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KT(7/10) | **Ref:** KP(7/10) | **WA 7:** High Frequency
**Paper Colour:** PINK

---

**Q1:** Differentiate between direct and indirect methods of high frequency application.

**Q2 (MCQ):** Ozone (O3) produced during high frequency has which effect? A. Moisturising B. Germicidal C. Exfoliating D. Bleaching

**Q3 (T/F):** a) Metal jewellery should be removed before high frequency treatment. b) High frequency electrode should be switched on before placing on skin (direct method). c) The indirect method uses a saturator electrode held by the client.

**Q4:** List THREE (3) precautions for high frequency treatment.

**Q5:** State the typical duration per area for high frequency body treatment.

---

## Answers
**A1:** Direct: electrode touches skin directly, produces ozone, stimulating. Indirect: client holds saturator, therapist massages, relaxing and nourishing. **A2:** B. **A3:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — Place electrode on skin first, then switch on. c) TRUE. **A4:** Remove jewellery, no flammable products nearby, apply before switching on, avoid excessive hair, no broken skin. **A5:** 5-10 minutes per area.
