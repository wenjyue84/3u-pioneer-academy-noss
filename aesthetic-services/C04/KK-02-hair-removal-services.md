## Objectives
1. Interpret consultation findings for electrotherapy. 2. Check contraindications. 3. Perform thermal and tactile tests. 4. Prepare client for electrotherapy.

## Equipment: Consultation card, hot/cold test tubes, orange stick, PPE, client attire, lockable locker, cleansing products.

## Procedure
1. Obtain consultation card; review findings. 2. Check contraindications (pacemaker, metal implants, epilepsy, pregnancy, cancer, DVT, skin infections, loss of sensation). 3. Explain recommended electrotherapy to client; answer queries. 4. Store belongings; change into attire. 5. Position client on couch. 6. Cleanse treatment area. 7. Perform thermal test (hot/cold tubes). 8. Perform tactile test (sharp/blunt). 9. Record test results. 10. If pass → proceed. If fail → do NOT proceed on that area.
