## Objectives
1. Describe sterilisation and maintenance of electrotherapy equipment.
2. Explain product replenishment and inventory management.
3. Describe waste disposal for electrotherapy consumables.

---

## 1.0 Equipment Sterilisation and Maintenance

| Equipment | Cleaning/Sterilisation |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Electrode pads (reusable) | Wipe with antiseptic; store in sealed bags |
| Electrode pads (disposable) | Discard after each client |
| Glass electrodes (HF) | Wipe with alcohol; store in UV cabinet |
| Ventouses/cups | Wash with soap; soak in disinfectant; air dry |
| Gyratory applicators | Detach; wash with soap; disinfect; air dry |
| Micro-current probes | Wipe tips with alcohol; replace cotton tips |
| Machine housings | Wipe with damp cloth (not wet); do not submerge |
| Cables and leads | Inspect for damage; wipe clean; coil loosely for storage |

---

## 2.0 Replenishment

- Conductive gels, creams, ampoules
- Disposable electrode pads, cotton tips
- Disposable gloves, tissues
- Cleaning/disinfecting products

---

## 3.0 Inventory and Waste Disposal

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Used disposable electrode pads, cotton tips, and single-use gloves that have been in contact with client skin are classified as **clinical waste** (*sisa klinikal*). They must be disposed of in yellow clinical waste bags — NOT general waste. Improper disposal breaches infection control standards and may violate Malaysian environmental regulations under the Environmental Quality Act 1974.

### 3.1 Waste Classification
| Waste Item | Classification | Disposal Method |
|------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Used disposable electrode pads | Clinical waste | Yellow clinical waste bag |
| Used cotton tips (micro-current) | Clinical waste | Yellow clinical waste bag |
| Disposable gloves | Clinical waste | Yellow clinical waste bag |
| Expired gels, creams, ampoules | Chemical waste | Sealed container; scheduled waste contractor |
| Packaging (boxes, blister packs) | General/recycling | Recycling bin or general waste |
| Broken glass electrodes (HF) | Sharps/clinical | Sharps container |
| Machine cleaning cloths | General waste | General waste bin |

### 3.2 Inventory Management
- Use stock cards and FIFO (First In First Out) rotation for all consumables.
- Conduct physical stock count monthly; reconcile with usage records.
- Record low stock items and initiate replenishment orders before stock depletes to less than 2 weeks' supply.
