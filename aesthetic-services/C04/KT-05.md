# KERTAS TUGASAN / ASSIGNMENT SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KT(5/10) | **Ref:** KP(5/10) | **WA 5:** Vacuum Suction
**Paper Colour:** PINK

---

**Q1:** List FOUR (4) effects of vacuum suction therapy.

**Q2 (MCQ):** Vacuum suction cups should be moved: A. In any direction B. Towards the nearest lymph node C. Away from lymph nodes D. In circular motion only

**Q3 (T/F):** a) It is safe to hold the vacuum cup stationary on one spot for 30 seconds. b) Suction should be released before lifting the cup from the skin. c) Vacuum suction is suitable for use over varicose veins.

**Q4:** State the typical suction pressure range for body vacuum treatment.

**Q5:** List THREE (3) contraindications specific to vacuum suction.

---

## Answers
**A1:** Lymphatic drainage, improved circulation, cellulite reduction, skin stimulation, detoxification. **A2:** B. **A3:** a) FALSE — Risk of bruising. b) TRUE — Press vent hole first. c) FALSE — Contraindicated over varicose veins. **A4:** 20-40%. **A5:** Bruising tendency, fragile/thin skin, varicose veins, broken skin, moles, bony prominences.
