**Q1 (MCQ):** Which PPE item is specifically important for electrotherapy to prevent electrical conduction? A. Mask B. Apron C. Anti-static closed-toe shoes D. Hair net

**Q2:** List FOUR (4) electrotherapy equipment types and state the function of each.

**Q3 (T/F):** a) Equipment cables should be inspected for damage before each session. b) Electrotherapy equipment may be placed on metal trolleys. c) The therapist should remove metallic jewellery before performing electrotherapy.

**Q4:** Describe FIVE (5) pre-session equipment checks for electrotherapy.

**Q5:** Explain the correct arrangement of an electrotherapy work area in service flow order.

---

## Answers
**A1:** C. **A2:** Any 4 with functions from KP(1/10). **A3:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — Should be on non-conductive surfaces. c) TRUE. **A4:** Inspect cables, check plugs, test on low setting, verify earth connections, check electrode pads. **A5:** Client prep zone → treatment zone → product zone → cleanup zone.
