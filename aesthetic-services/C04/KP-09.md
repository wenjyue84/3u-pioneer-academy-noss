## Objectives
1. Explain after care and home care advice for electrotherapy clients.
2. Describe the recording requirements for electrotherapy services.
3. Explain appointment scheduling for electrotherapy courses.

---

## 1.0 After Care Advice

| Advice | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| Drink plenty of water | Flush metabolic waste stimulated by treatment |
| Mild redness is normal | Expected response to electrical stimulation; fades within hours |
| Avoid heat treatments for 24 hours | Skin may be sensitised |
| Avoid sun exposure for 24 hours | Treated skin is more photosensitive |
| Rest if feeling fatigued | Muscle stimulation treatments can cause tiredness |
| Report any prolonged redness, blistering, or pain | Indicates adverse reaction |

---

## 2.0 Recording

Record on client body consultation card:
- Modality used (faradic, galvanic, vacuum, gyratory, HF, micro-current)
- Treatment area
- Parameters used (intensity, duration, frequency)
- Client response during treatment
- Any adverse reactions
- Therapist name, date, signature

---

## 3.0 Appointment Scheduling

Most electrotherapy treatments require a course:
| Modality | Typical Course |
|----------|---------------|
| Faradic/EMS | 2-3 times per week for 6-12 weeks |
| Micro-current | 1-2 times per week for 6-12 sessions |
| Vacuum suction | 1-2 times per week for 6-10 sessions |
| Galvanic | As part of combined treatment course |
| High frequency | As needed, often combined with other treatments |
