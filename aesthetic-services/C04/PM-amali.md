# PELAN MENGAJAR AMALI / PRACTICAL LESSON PLAN

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04
**CU:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy | **Practical Duration:** 34 hours | **Work Sheets:** 10
**Paper Colour:** YELLOW

---

## Instructional Plan

### Session 1: KK(1/10) — Work Area Preparation (3 hrs)
### Session 2: KK(2/10) — Client Preparation & Thermal/Tactile Tests (3 hrs)
### Session 3: KK(3/10) — Faradic/EMS Treatment (4 hrs)
### Session 4: KK(4/10) — Galvanic Treatment (4 hrs)
### Session 5: KK(5/10) — Vacuum Suction Treatment (3 hrs)
### Session 6: KK(6/10) — Mechanical/Gyratory Massage (3 hrs)
### Session 7: KK(7/10) — High Frequency Treatment (3 hrs)
### Session 8: KK(8/10) — Micro-Current Treatment (4 hrs)
### Session 9: KK(9/10) — Post-Electrotherapy Advice (3 hrs)
### Session 10: KK(10/10) — Equipment Maintenance & Upkeep (4 hrs)

Each session: Persediaan → Penyampaian (demonstration) → Penggunaan (practice) → Pengesahan (assessment).

## Resources
All electrotherapy equipment (faradic, galvanic, vacuum, gyratory, HF, micro-current), conductive gels, electrode pads, ventouses, applicator heads, PPE, towels, consultation cards, sterilisation equipment, waste bins.
