**Q1 (MCQ):** Micro-current therapy operates at: A. Milliamperes B. Amperes C. Microamperes D. Kilovolts

**Q2:** List FOUR (4) effects of micro-current therapy on the body.

**Q3 (T/F):** a) The client should feel a strong tingling during micro-current. b) Micro-current increases ATP production in cells. c) A single micro-current session provides permanent results.

**Q4:** State the typical current range for micro-current treatment.

**Q5:** Explain why micro-current probes must maintain good skin contact during treatment.

---

## Answers
**A1:** C. **A2:** ATP production increase, protein synthesis, muscle re-education/firming, skin tightening, lymphatic drainage, accelerated healing. **A3:** a) FALSE — Sub-sensory; client should feel nothing or very mild tingling. b) TRUE. c) FALSE — A course of 6-12 sessions is typically required. **A4:** 10-600 microamperes. **A5:** Lifting probes during treatment wastes current/breaks the circuit; continuous contact ensures effective delivery and optimal results.
