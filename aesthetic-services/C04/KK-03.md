# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KK(3/10)
**CU:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy | **WA 3:** Faradic/EMS Service
**Paper Colour:** BLUE

---

## Objectives
1. Apply faradic/EMS electrodes to correct motor points. 2. Operate the faradic/EMS unit safely. 3. Monitor client and adjust parameters.

## Equipment: Faradic/EMS unit, electrode pads, conductive gel, straps/bandages, timer, towels, consultation card.

## Safety: Start intensity at zero. Never place over heart/neck. Monitor continuously.

## Procedure
1. Obtain consultation card; confirm suitability. 2. Apply conductive gel to motor points of target muscles. 3. Place electrode pads on motor points; secure with straps. 4. Confirm thermal/tactile tests passed. 5. Turn on machine at zero. 6. Increase intensity GRADUALLY until visible muscle contraction. 7. Check client comfort: "Is this comfortable?" 8. Set timer (15-20 min per muscle group). 9. Monitor every 3-5 minutes. 10. At completion: reduce intensity to zero. 11. Switch off machine. 12. Remove pads carefully. 13. Wipe residual gel from client's skin. 14. Usher client to change; return belongings. 15. Sterilise/clean equipment. 16. Replenish materials. 17. Record: modality, parameters, response, results.
