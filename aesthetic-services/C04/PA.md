## BAHAGIAN B: Client Screening Checklist (*Senarai Semak Saringan Klien*)

Complete ALL items below BEFORE treatment initiation. Any FAIL = treatment CANNOT proceed.

| # | Screening Item (*Item Saringan*) | Pass | Fail | Action if Fail |
|---|----------------------------------|------|------|----------------|
| 1 | Pregnancy test / declaration (*ujian/pengisytiharan kehamilan*) | ☐ | ☐ | Decline treatment; refer to physician |
| 2 | Metal implants check — pacemaker, pins, plates (*semakan implan logam*) | ☐ | ☐ | Decline electrotherapy; offer manual alternative |
| 3 | Skin sensitivity / patch test (*ujian sensitiviti kulit*) | ☐ | ☐ | Do not proceed on affected area |
| 4 | Thermal sensation test — hot/cold (*ujian sensasi terma*) | ☐ | ☐ | Do not apply thermal modalities |
| 5 | Tactile sensation test — sharp/dull (*ujian sensasi sentuhan*) | ☐ | ☐ | Do not apply current-based modalities |
| 6 | Epilepsy / seizure history (*sejarah epilepsi/sawan*) | ☐ | ☐ | Decline high-frequency and EMS |
| 7 | Cardiovascular condition (*keadaan kardiovaskular*) | ☐ | ☐ | Obtain physician clearance before proceeding |
| 8 | Open wounds / broken skin (*luka terbuka / kulit rosak*) | ☐ | ☐ | Avoid affected area; adjust electrode placement |
| 9 | Recent surgery — within 6 months (*pembedahan baru — dalam 6 bulan*) | ☐ | ☐ | Obtain physician clearance |
| 10 | Allergies to conductive gels/electrodes (*alahan gel/elektrod*) | ☐ | ☐ | Use hypoallergenic alternative or decline |

**Screening Result:** ALL PASS ☐ → Proceed | ANY FAIL ☐ → Do NOT proceed without resolution

### D3. Troubleshooting Decision Tree (*Pokok Keputusan Penyelesaian Masalah*)

```
Equipment fault detected?
├── YES → Is there visible damage (cable, casing, electrode)?
│   ├── YES → Tag OUT OF SERVICE → Report to supervisor → Send for repair
│   └── NO → Does the unit power on?
│       ├── NO → Check fuse → Check power outlet → Replace fuse or try different outlet
│       │   └── Still NO → Tag OUT OF SERVICE → Send for repair
│       └── YES → Is output inconsistent or abnormal?
│           ├── YES → Recalibrate using manufacturer procedure
│           │   └── Still abnormal → Tag OUT OF SERVICE → Send for repair
│           └── NO → Check electrode connections → Clean contact points
│               └── Still faulty → Tag OUT OF SERVICE → Send for repair
└── NO → Proceed with treatment
```
