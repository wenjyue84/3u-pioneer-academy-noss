**Q1 (MCQ):** The cathode (negative pole) in galvanic treatment produces: A. Acidic reaction B. Alkaline reaction C. Neutral reaction D. No chemical reaction

**Q2:** Differentiate between iontophoresis and desincrustation.

**Q3 (T/F):** a) Current should be turned to zero before moving electrodes. b) Galvanic can be safely used over broken skin. c) The anode (positive) has a tightening and soothing effect on skin.

**Q4:** List THREE (3) precautions specific to galvanic treatment.

**Q5:** State the typical current range for body galvanic treatment.

---

## Answers
**A1:** B. **A2:** Iontophoresis: uses current to drive charged products into skin for treatment. Desincrustation: uses alkaline reaction at cathode to emulsify sebum for deep cleansing. **A3:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — Contraindicated over broken skin. c) TRUE. **A4:** Any 3: Test on low setting, zero before moving electrodes, no broken skin/moles, check polarity, ensure good contact, monitor for redness/burning. **A5:** 0.01-0.05 mA per cm², typically 1-5 mA total for body.
