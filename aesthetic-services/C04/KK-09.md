## Objectives
1. Deliver after care and home care advice. 2. Record service on consultation card. 3. Schedule next appointment.

## Equipment: Consultation card, home care leaflets, appointment system, pen.

## Procedure
1. Seat client. 2. Deliver after care: drink water, mild redness normal, avoid heat 24h, avoid sun 24h, rest if fatigued, report any blistering/prolonged redness. 3. Recommend home care if applicable. 4. Record on card: modality used, area treated, parameters, response, adverse reactions, date/signature. 5. Schedule next appointment per course plan. 6. Provide confirmation. 7. Return belongings; escort client.
