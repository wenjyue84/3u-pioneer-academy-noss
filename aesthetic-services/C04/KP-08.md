## 1.0 Principles of Micro-Current

**Micro-current** (*arus mikro*) uses extremely low-level electrical currents (measured in microamperes, uA) that mirror the body's own bio-electrical currents. It is sub-sensory — the client typically cannot feel the current.

### 1.1 Effects
| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| ATP production (*pengeluaran ATP*) | Increases cellular energy production by up to 500% |
| Protein synthesis | Enhances collagen and elastin production |
| Muscle re-education | Lifts and firms muscles |
| Skin tightening | Improves skin tone and elasticity |
| Lymphatic drainage | Reduces puffiness and fluid retention |
| Accelerated healing | Promotes tissue repair |

## 3.0 Procedure Overview

1. Cleanse treatment area.
2. Apply conductive gel/serum.
3. Using probes: place both probes on the skin at specific points.
4. Work along muscle groups, following muscle origin to insertion.
5. Lift, tone, and firm target areas.
6. Client should feel nothing or a very mild tingling.
7. Duration: 30-60 minutes per session.

---

## 4.0 Precautions

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Micro-current is sub-sensory — if the client can feel the current, the level is too high. Reduce immediately. Although the currents are extremely low, micro-current carries the same absolute contraindications as all electrical modalities. NEVER use on clients with pacemakers (*perentak jantung*) — even sub-sensory microampere levels can interfere with demand-type pacemakers.

- Current must be sub-sensory — not felt by the client. If felt, reduce current immediately.
- Absolute contraindications: pacemaker, metal implants in area, cancer, pregnancy, epilepsy (uncontrolled).
- Additional contraindications specific to micro-current: severe cardiac conditions, phlebitis (*flebitis*), recent surgical incisions (< 6 months), active skin infection in treatment area.
- Clean probes, gloves, or conductive pads between clients using appropriate disinfectant.
- Maintain full probe contact with skin — lifting probes mid-treatment reduces efficacy.
- A course of treatments is required for sustained results (6–12 sessions, 1–2 per week).
- Use only dedicated micro-current conductive gel — standard conductive gel is insufficient for microampere-level current transmission.
