## 1.0 Principles of Micro-Current

**Micro-current** (*arus mikro*) uses extremely low-level electrical currents (measured in microamperes, uA) that mirror the body's own bio-electrical currents. It is sub-sensory — the client typically cannot feel the current.

### 1.1 Effects
| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| ATP production (*pengeluaran ATP*) | Increases cellular energy production by up to 500% |
| Protein synthesis | Enhances collagen and elastin production |
| Muscle re-education | Lifts and firms muscles |
| Skin tightening | Improves skin tone and elasticity |
| Lymphatic drainage | Reduces puffiness and fluid retention |
| Accelerated healing | Promotes tissue repair |

## 2.0 Equipment and Parameters

| Parameter | Typical Range |
|-----------|--------------|
| Current | 10-600 microamperes (uA) |
| Frequency | 0.1-500 Hz |
| Waveform | Modified square wave or sinusoidal |
| Electrode type | Probes (cotton tip), gloves, or conductive pads |
| Conductive medium | Specialised micro-current gel or serum |
| Duration | 30-60 minutes |

---

## 3.0 Procedure Overview

1. Cleanse treatment area.
2. Apply conductive gel/serum.
3. Using probes: place both probes on the skin at specific points.
4. Work along muscle groups, following muscle origin to insertion.
5. Lift, tone, and firm target areas.
6. Client should feel nothing or a very mild tingling.
7. Duration: 30-60 minutes per session.

---

## 4.0 Precautions

- Current must not be felt by the client (sub-sensory); if felt, reduce.
- Contraindicated for: pacemakers, metal implants, cancer, pregnancy, epilepsy.
- Clean probes/gloves between clients.
- Maintain good contact — lifting probes during treatment wastes current.
- Course of treatments typically required (6-12 sessions).
