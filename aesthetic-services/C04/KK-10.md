# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KK(10/10)
**CU:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy | **WA 10:** Upkeep Work Area
**Paper Colour:** BLUE

---

## Objectives
1. Sterilise/sanitise electrotherapy equipment. 2. Replenish materials. 3. Update inventory. 4. Dispose waste correctly.

## Equipment: Disinfectant, alcohol wipes, UV cabinet, replacement consumables, stock cards, waste bins, gloves.

## Procedure
1. Dispose of used disposable electrode pads → clinical waste (yellow bag). 2. Wipe reusable electrode pads with antiseptic; store in sealed bags. 3. Wipe glass HF electrodes with alcohol; store in UV cabinet. 4. Wash ventouses with soap; soak in disinfectant; rinse; air dry. 5. Detach gyratory applicators; wash, disinfect, air dry. 6. Wipe micro-current probes; replace cotton tips. 7. Wipe machine housings with damp cloth (NOT wet). 8. Inspect and coil cables loosely. 9. Disinfect couch and trolley surfaces. 10. Replenish: gels, pads, tips, gloves, cleaning products. 11. Check expiry dates; FIFO rotation. 12. Physical inventory count; update stock cards. 13. Dispose waste: used pads/cotton → clinical, packaging → recycling, expired gels → chemical waste. 14. Submit inventory report.
