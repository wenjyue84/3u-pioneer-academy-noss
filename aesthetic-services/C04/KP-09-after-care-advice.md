## Objectives
1. Explain after care and home care advice for electrotherapy clients.
2. Describe the recording requirements for electrotherapy services.
3. Explain appointment scheduling for electrotherapy courses.

---

## 1.0 After Care Advice

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** If a client reports prolonged redness (exceeding 24 hours), blistering (*lepuh*), swelling, intense pain, or any signs of electrical burn following electrotherapy, refer immediately to a medical practitioner. Do not dismiss these signs as "normal reaction." Document the incident and suspend further electrotherapy treatment until medical clearance is received.

| Advice | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| Drink 6–8 glasses of water | Flush metabolic waste and lactic acid stimulated by treatment |
| Mild erythema (redness) is normal | Expected vascular response; fades within 1–4 hours |
| Avoid heat treatments (sauna, steam) for 24 hours | Skin is sensitised; additional heat risks burns |
| Avoid sun exposure and UV tanning for 24 hours | Treated skin is more photosensitive |
| Rest if fatigued | Muscle stimulation (faradic/EMS) causes genuine muscle exertion |
| Avoid vigorous exercise for 12–24 hours after EMS/faradic | Muscle fibres need recovery time, same as post-exercise |
| Report prolonged redness, blistering, or pain immediately | Indicates adverse reaction requiring medical attention |

### 1.1 Modality-Specific Aftercare Notes
| Modality | Additional Advice |
|----------|-------------------|
| Faradic/EMS | Client may feel mild DOMS (delayed onset muscle soreness) 24–48 hours post-treatment — normal |
| Galvanic | Avoid washing treated area with soap for 4 hours (preserves product penetration effect) |
| Vacuum suction | Mild petechiae (pin-point bruising) may appear if suction was heavy — reassure client it fades |
| High frequency | Keep treated area free of flammable products (perfume, aerosols) for 2 hours post-treatment |
| Micro-current | Results are cumulative — client may not notice effect until after 3–4 sessions |

## 2.0 Recording

Record on client body consultation card:
- Modality used (faradic, galvanic, vacuum, gyratory, HF, micro-current)
- Treatment area
- Parameters used (intensity, duration, frequency)
- Client response during treatment
- Any adverse reactions
- Therapist name, date, signature

---

## 3.0 Appointment Scheduling

Most electrotherapy treatments require a course:
| Modality | Typical Course |
|----------|---------------|
| Faradic/EMS | 2-3 times per week for 6-12 weeks |
| Micro-current | 1-2 times per week for 6-12 sessions |
| Vacuum suction | 1-2 times per week for 6-10 sessions |
| Galvanic | As part of combined treatment course |
| High frequency | As needed, often combined with other treatments |
