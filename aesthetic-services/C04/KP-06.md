## Objectives
1. Explain the principles of mechanical/gyratory massage.
2. Describe applicator types and their uses.
3. Explain precautions for mechanical massage.

---

## 1.0 Principles

**Mechanical/gyratory massage** (*urutan mekanikal/giratori*) uses an electrically powered device with interchangeable applicator heads that rotate, vibrate, or oscillate to massage body tissues.

### 1.1 Effects
- Deep tissue relaxation and tension release
- Improved circulation
- Cellulite reduction
- Fat emulsification
- Lymphatic drainage stimulation
- Muscle tone improvement

---

## 2.0 Applicator Types

| Applicator | Description | Use |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| **Round/ball sponge** | Soft foam ball | General body massage; large, fleshy areas |
| **Curved/football** | Elongated foam | Legs, arms, curved body areas |
| **Spike/pin cushion** | Rubber with raised points | Stimulating; cellulite areas |
| **Smooth disc/plate** | Flat rubber | Relaxation; broad areas |
| **Eggbox** | Undulating surface | Kneading effect; fleshy areas |

---

## 3.0 Treatment Parameters

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Speed | Low to medium for relaxation; medium to high for stimulation |
| Pressure | Light to moderate; heavier on fleshy areas |
| Direction | Towards lymph nodes; avoid circular on one spot |
| Duration | 15-30 minutes per area |
| Medium | Talcum powder (reduces friction) or light oil |

---

## 4.0 Precautions

- Avoid bony prominences, spine, breasts, neck.
- Do not remain on one spot — risk of bruising.
- Reduce speed/pressure on sensitive areas.
- Check applicator attachment is secure before use.
- Clean and disinfect applicators between clients.
