# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C04/KP(4/10)
**CU:** C04 — Body Electrotherapy | **WA 4:** Galvanic Electrotherapy
**Paper Colour:** WHITE

---

## Objectives
1. Explain the principles of galvanic current and its aesthetic applications.
2. Describe iontophoresis and desincrustation procedures.
3. Explain precautions for galvanic treatment.

---

## 1.0 Principles of Galvanic Current

**Galvanic current** (*arus galvanik*) is a constant, uninterrupted direct current (DC). It produces chemical and ionic effects in the skin.

### 1.1 Polarity and Effects

| Pole | Chemical Effect | Skin Effect |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **Cathode (-) / Negative** | Alkaline reaction (NaOH) | Softens tissue, opens pores, increases circulation, stimulates |
| **Anode (+) / Positive** | Acidic reaction (HCl) | Tightens, firms, soothes, reduces redness, germicidal |

### 1.2 Two Main Applications

**Iontophoresis** (*iontoforesis*): Uses galvanic current to drive charged treatment products deep into the skin. Active electrode carries the product; polarity matches the product's ionic charge.

**Desincrustation** (*desinkrustasi*): Uses the alkaline reaction at the cathode to saponify (emulsify) sebum and deeply cleanse congested skin. A specific desincrustation solution is applied.

---

## 2.0 Treatment Parameters

| Parameter | Typical Range |
|-----------|--------------|
| Current | 0.01 - 0.05 mA per cm² of electrode |
| Maximum total | Usually 1-5 mA (body) |
| Duration | 10-20 minutes |
| Electrode type | Roller, disc, or mask |
| Conductive medium | Treatment gel/ampoule (iontophoresis) or desincrustation fluid |

---

## 3.0 Galvanic Treatment Procedure Overview

1. Cleanse treatment area thoroughly.
2. Apply desincrustation fluid (for desincrustation) or treatment gel/ampoule (for iontophoresis).
3. Select appropriate electrode; set polarity to match treatment type.
4. Ensure complete electrode contact; secure to skin.
5. Set machine to zero; slowly increase current to prescribed mA.
6. Monitor client throughout — mild tingling is acceptable; burning is not.
7. At completion: reduce current to zero before removing electrode (prevents galvanic shock).
8. Cleanse and tone treated area; apply post-treatment product.
9. Record parameters on consultation card.

---

## 4.0 Precautions

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Galvanic current must always be reduced to zero before placing or removing electrodes. Failure to do so causes a galvanic shock — a sharp, painful electrical jolt that can cause localised burns and client distress. Check electrode contact throughout treatment; poor or uneven contact concentrates current and risks localised chemical burns.

- Always test on low setting first.
- Turn current to zero before moving or removing electrodes (prevents galvanic shock).
- Do not apply over broken skin, moles, raised lesions, or areas of hypersensitivity.
- Check polarity of treatment product before application — incorrect polarity reduces penetration effectiveness.
- Monitor client for excessive redness, tingling, or burning — reduce current immediately if reported.
- Ensure full electrode contact to prevent current concentration burns.
- **Contraindications specific to galvanic:** Broken skin, hypersensitive skin, extreme sensitivity to electrical current, metallic taste sensitivity (indicator of sensitivity to galvanic effects).
