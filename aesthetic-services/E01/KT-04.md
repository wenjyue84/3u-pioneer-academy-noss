Wire brush **Q3:** Describe the bust area cleansing procedure in FIVE steps. **Q4 (T/F):** a) The nipple area should be avoided during deep cleansing. b) Heavy pressure exfoliants are ideal for bust skin. **Q5:** What is the treatment area for bust firming? Define the boundaries.
## Answers: **A1:** Bust area skin is thin and sensitive; breast tissue is delicate. **A2:** C. **A3:** Select cleanser → apply with light effleurage → remove with warm sponges → deep cleanse gently → tone and pat dry. **A4:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE. **A5:** From the shoulder area to below the bust line.
