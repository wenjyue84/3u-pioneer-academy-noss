**Q1:** List FOUR modesty protocols for bust firming therapy. **Q2 (T/F):** a) A male therapist may perform bust firming therapy without client request. b) A call bell should be provided to the client. **Q3:** Describe the correct client positioning for bust firming therapy. **Q4 (MCQ):** Before each step, the therapist should: A. Remain silent B. Communicate clearly what they are about to do C. Ask the client to look away D. Dim all lights **Q5:** Why should only the area being actively worked be exposed?
## Answers: **A1:** Never fully expose unnecessarily, draping towels, communicate before each step, confirm consent at each stage, call bell, female therapist. **A2:** a) FALSE. b) TRUE. **A3:** Supine on couch, head slightly elevated. **A4:** B. **A5:** To maintain client modesty and dignity in this sensitive treatment.
