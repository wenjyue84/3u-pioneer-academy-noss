# KERTAS KERJA — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KK(1/9) | WA 1: Work Area Setup | BLUE
## Objectives: Set up bust firming therapy work area with privacy, equipment and PPE.
## Equipment: Treatment couch, magnifying lamp, vaporizer, trolley, bust firming products, towels, PPE.
## Procedure
1. Ensure private enclosed room. 2. Check door lockable from inside. 3. Don PPE (apron). 4. Disinfect couch, trolley, lamp, vaporizer. 5. Lay fresh linen. 6. Test magnifying lamp and vaporizer. 7. Arrange products on trolley in service flow. 8. Set room temperature (24-26 C), soft lighting. 9. Prepare draping towels. 10. Final safety check.
