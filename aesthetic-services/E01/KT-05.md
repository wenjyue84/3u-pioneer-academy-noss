# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KT(5/9) | Ref: KP(5/9) | WA 5 | PINK
**Q1:** Name the five classical massage movements and describe how each is adapted for the bust area. **Q2 (MCQ):** Massage direction on the bust area should be: A. Downward B. Upward (lifting and firming) C. Random D. Away from body **Q3 (T/F):** a) Deep pressure may be applied directly on breast tissue. b) Effleurage strokes on the bust should move toward axillary lymph nodes. **Q4:** What massage mediums are suitable for bust firming? **Q5:** List THREE monitoring points during bust firming massage.
## Answers: **A1:** Effleurage (light sweeping upward), petrissage (gentle kneading on pectorals only), tapotement (light finger tapping on pectorals), vibration (light fingertip vibrations), friction (small circles on pectoral muscles). **A2:** B. **A3:** a) FALSE. b) TRUE. **A4:** Bust firming cream or oil containing collagen, elastin, vitamin E. **A5:** Client comfort, redness, emotional distress.
