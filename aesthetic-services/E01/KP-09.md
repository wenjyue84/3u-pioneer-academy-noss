# KERTAS PENERANGAN — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KP(9/9) | WA 9: Upkeep Work Area | WHITE

## Objectives
1. Describe sterilisation of bust firming therapy tools.
2. Explain replenishment, inventory and waste disposal.

## 1.0 Sterilisation
- Wipe magnifying lamp and vaporizer with disinfectant.
- Wash bowls, spatulas with soap; soak in disinfectant.
- Disinfect couch surface.
- Launder towels at 60 C.

## 2.0 Replenishment
- Bust firming products (cleansers, massage cream, masks, serums, ampoules).
- Cotton pads, sponges, gauze.
- Towels, disposable gloves.

## 3.0 Inventory and Waste
Standard procedures: physical count, FIFO, stock cards, expiry checks. Waste: used cotton/sponges → clinical (yellow), used gloves → clinical, packaging → recycling, expired products → chemical waste.
