# KERTAS PENERANGAN — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KP(3/9) | WA 3: Client Preparation | WHITE

## Objectives
1. Describe client preparation specific to bust firming therapy.
2. Explain modesty and draping protocols for bust area treatment.

## 1.0 Client Preparation
1. Usher client to change into bust firming salon attire (front-opening gown or wrap).
2. Guide to designated treatment area.
3. Store belongings safely.
4. Sterilise feet.
5. Drape hair (headband or cap).
6. Ensure modesty throughout — this is a sensitive treatment area.
7. Don PPE (apron).
8. Position client supine on couch with head slightly elevated.

## 2.0 Modesty Protocols
- **Never** fully expose the bust area unnecessarily.
- Use draping towels; expose only the area being actively worked on.
- Communicate clearly before each step: "I am now going to..."
- Confirm client consent at each stage.
- Provide a call bell for the client.
- Only female therapists should perform bust treatments (unless client specifically requests otherwise).
