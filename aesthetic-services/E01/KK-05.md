# KERTAS KERJA — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KK(5/9) | WA 5: Bust Firming Massage | BLUE
## Objectives: Perform bust firming massage using five classical movements; monitor client.
## Equipment: Bust firming cream/oil, warm sponges, towels.
## Safety: Light to moderate pressure only. Never deep pressure on breast tissue. Monitor continuously.
## Procedure
1. Select massage medium. 2. Warm between palms. 3. Apply to bust area. 4. **Effleurage:** Light sweeping strokes from below bust line upward to shoulders; around bust; toward axillary lymph nodes. 5. **Petrissage:** Gentle kneading on pectoral muscles (above breast). 6. **Tapotement:** Light finger tapping on pectoral area only. 7. **Vibration:** Light fingertip vibrations on pectoral area. 8. **Friction:** Small circles with fingertips on pectoral muscles. 9. Monitor client throughout — check comfort, redness, distress. 10. Remove massage medium with warm damp cotton pads/sponges.
