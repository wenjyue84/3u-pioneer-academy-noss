# KERTAS KERJA — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KK(6/9) | WA 6: Bust Firming Mask | BLUE
## Objectives: Select, apply, monitor and remove bust firming mask.
## Equipment: Bust firming mask (cream or rubber), gauze/cotton pads, bowls, spatula, warm sponges.
## Procedure
1. Select mask type per client condition. 2. Mix rubber mask per instructions (if applicable). 3. Cover nipple area with gauze/cotton pads. 4. Apply mask evenly (shoulder to below bust line). 5. Set timer (15-20 min). 6. Monitor client during setting. 7. **Cream mask:** Remove with warm damp sponges. **Rubber mask:** Peel from edges, gentle steady pull. 8. Remove residue. 9. Tone area.
