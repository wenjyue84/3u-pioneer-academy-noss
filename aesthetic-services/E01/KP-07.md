# KERTAS PENERANGAN — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KP(7/9) | WA 7: Bust Firming Protective Care | WHITE

## Objectives
1. Describe bust firming protective care products.
2. Explain application procedure and client monitoring.

## 1.0 Protective Care Products

| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Bust firming cream (*krim pengencangan bust*) | Maintain firmness between sessions |
| Bust firming serum (*serum pengencangan*) | Concentrated active ingredients |
| Vials / ampoules (*vial / ampul*) | Single-dose concentrated treatment |
| Complex (*kompleks*) | Multi-ingredient protective formula |

## 2.0 Application
1. Select protective care product based on client's condition.
2. Apply to bust area (shoulder to below bust line).
3. Use light effleurage movements for even distribution.
4. Allow to absorb; do not remove.
5. Monitor client reaction.

## 3.0 Client Transition
1. Return personal belongings.
2. Usher client to change into own attire.
3. Guide to designated area for post-therapy advice.
