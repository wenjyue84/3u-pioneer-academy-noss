# KERTAS PENERANGAN — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KP(5/9) | WA 5: Bust Firming Massage | WHITE

## Objectives
1. Describe massage medium selection for bust area.
2. Explain the five classical massage movements applied to the bust area.
3. Describe monitoring and medium removal.

## 1.0 Massage Medium
- Bust firming cream or oil (containing collagen, elastin, vitamin E, essential oils).
- Select based on client's skin type and treatment goals.

## 2.0 Five Classical Movements for Bust Area

| Movement | Application to Bust Area |
|----------|-------------------------|
| **Effleurage** | Light, sweeping strokes from below bust line upward to shoulders; around bust; toward axillary lymph nodes |
| **Petrissage** | Gentle kneading of the pectoral muscles (above the breast tissue); avoid direct kneading of breast |
| **Tapotement** | Very light tapping/finger tapping on pectoral area only; never on breast tissue |
| **Vibration** | Light vibrations using fingertips on pectoral area; soothing |
| **Friction** | Small circular movements with fingertips on pectoral muscles and around bust |

### 2.1 Important Guidelines
- Work on the area from shoulder to below the bust line.
- Movements should be lifting and firming (upward direction).
- Pressure must be light to moderate — breast tissue is delicate.
- Never apply deep pressure directly on breast tissue.
- Monitor client reaction continuously.

## 3.0 Monitoring and Completion
- Check client comfort throughout.
- Watch for redness, discomfort, emotional distress.
- On completion: remove massage medium with warm damp cotton pads or sponges.
