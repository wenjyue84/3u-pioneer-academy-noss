# KERTAS PENERANGAN — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KP(8/9) | WA 8: Post-Therapy Advice | WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain home care advice for bust firming therapy clients.
2. Describe recording requirements and scheduling.

## 1.0 Home Care Advice
- Apply bust firming cream/serum daily as recommended.
- Wear supportive undergarments.
- Maintain good posture (supporting chest muscles).
- Chest exercises (push-ups, chest presses) to strengthen pectoral muscles.
- Avoid excessive sun exposure on bust area.
- Stay hydrated; balanced diet rich in collagen-supporting nutrients (vitamin C, zinc, protein).

## 2.0 Recording
Record: treatment performed (cleansing, massage, mask type, protective care), products used, client response, advice given, date/signature.

## 3.0 Scheduling
Typical course: weekly or fortnightly sessions for 8-12 treatments. Schedule next appointment.
