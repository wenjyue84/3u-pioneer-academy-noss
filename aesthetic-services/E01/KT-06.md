# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KT(6/9) | Ref: KP(6/9) | WA 6 | PINK
**Q1:** Name TWO types of bust firming masks and describe their effects. **Q2 (MCQ):** During mask application, the nipple area should be: A. Heavily covered with mask B. Covered with gauze/cotton pads C. Left exposed D. Massaged **Q3:** Describe the removal procedure for a rubber peel-off mask. **Q4 (T/F):** a) A cream mask is removed by peeling. b) Mask setting time is typically 15-20 minutes. **Q5:** Why must the client be monitored during mask setting?
## Answers: **A1:** Cream mask (moisturising, nourishing); rubber/peel-off mask (tightening, firming, contouring). **A2:** B. **A3:** Peel from edges; gentle, steady pull; remove residue; tone. **A4:** a) FALSE — Removed with warm damp sponges. b) TRUE. **A5:** To check for adverse reactions (irritation, discomfort, allergic reaction).
