# KERTAS TUGASAN — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KT(2/9) | Ref: KP(2/9) | WA 2 | PINK
**Q1:** List FOUR absolute contraindications for bust firming therapy. **Q2 (MCQ):** If a client has a breast lump, the therapist should: A. Proceed with extra care B. Refer to medical professional immediately C. Ignore it D. Apply more pressure **Q3:** What additional profile data is needed for bust firming consultation? **Q4 (T/F):** a) Breast implants are not a contraindication. b) Breastfeeding is a contraindication for bust firming therapy. **Q5:** Why must the client sign a consent form before bust firming therapy?
## Answers: **A1:** Breast cancer/tumours/lumps, implants, active skin infections, open wounds, pregnancy/breastfeeding. **A2:** B. **A3:** Bust size/shape concerns, skin condition, surgery history, breastfeeding history, hormonal conditions. **A4:** a) FALSE — Implants are generally an absolute contraindication. b) TRUE. **A5:** Treatment involves an intimate area; consent confirms client understanding, agreement, and protects both parties legally.
