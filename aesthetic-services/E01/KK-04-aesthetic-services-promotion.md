## Objectives: Cleanse and deep cleanse bust area; tone and dry.
## Equipment: Gentle cleanser, enzyme exfoliant, warm sponges/cotton pads, toner, towels.
## Procedure
1. Select cleansing medium per skin type. 2. Apply cleanser to bust area (shoulder to below bust line) using light effleurage. 3. Avoid excessive pressure on breast tissue. 4. Remove with warm damp sponges/cotton pads. 5. Apply gentle exfoliant (enzyme/light granular). 6. Light circular movements; avoid nipple area. 7. Remove thoroughly. 8. Apply toner. 9. Pat dry.
