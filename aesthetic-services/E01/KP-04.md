# KERTAS PENERANGAN — S960-002-3:2020-E01/KP(4/9) | WA 4: Cleansing Bust Area | WHITE

## Objectives
1. Explain cleansing and deep cleansing mediums for the bust area.
2. Describe the cleansing procedure and toning.

## 1.0 Cleansing Medium Selection
Select based on skin type of bust area: gentle cleanser for sensitive skin, foaming cleanser for normal/oily skin.

## 2.0 Procedure
1. Select cleansing medium. 2. Apply to bust area (from shoulder to below bust line) using light effleurage. 3. Cleanse gently; avoid excessive pressure on breast tissue. 4. Remove with warm damp sponges/cotton pads. 5. Apply deep cleansing medium (gentle exfoliant). 6. Light circular movements; avoid nipple area. 7. Remove thoroughly. 8. Apply toner to the area. 9. Pat dry.

## 3.0 Notes
- Bust area skin is thin and sensitive — always use light pressure.
- Avoid harsh exfoliants; use enzyme or gentle granular products.
- Maintain modesty throughout.
