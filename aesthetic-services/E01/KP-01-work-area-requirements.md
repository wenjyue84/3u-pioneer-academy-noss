## Objectives
1. Explain hygiene and safety practices for bust firming therapy.
2. Identify tools, equipment and materials including magnifying lamp and vaporizer.

## 1.0 Work Area Requirements
- Private, enclosed treatment room (door lockable from inside by client).
- Additional privacy measures due to sensitive treatment area.
- Comfortable room temperature (24-26 C).
- Soft lighting (adjustable).
- PPE: Apron for therapist.

## 2.0 Equipment
| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Treatment couch | Client positioning |
| Magnifying lamp (*lampu pembesar*) | Detailed skin examination of bust area |
| Vaporizer (*penyejat*) | Steam to open pores; soften skin |
| Trolley | Organise products |
| Towels (multiple) | Draping, modesty, wiping |
| Bust firming products | Cleansers, massage cream/oil, masks, serums |
| Disposable breast covers | Modesty if required during assessment |

## 3.0 Arrangement
Arrange per bust firming service flow: consultation → cleansing → massage → mask → protective care.
