## Objectives
1. Explain the bust firming therapy consultation process.
2. Identify contraindications specific to bust treatments.

## 1.0 Client Profile
Review existing consultation card. Additional assessment for bust area:
- Bust size and shape concerns (sagging, asymmetry, loss of firmness)
- Skin condition of bust area (stretch marks, dryness, loss of elasticity)
- History of breast surgery or implants
- Breastfeeding history
- Hormonal conditions affecting breast tissue

## 2.0 Contraindications
### Absolute
- Breast cancer / tumours / lumps (refer to medical professional immediately)
- Breast implants (depending on implant type; may damage or shift)
- Active skin infections in bust area
- Open wounds / recent scars on bust area
- Pregnancy / breastfeeding (tissue sensitivity)

### Relative
- Hormonal conditions (with GP advice)
- Sensitive skin (patch test required)
- Recent tattoo on bust area

## 3.0 Consultation Record
Explain therapy, home care, answer queries. Record findings; complete consultation with signature.
