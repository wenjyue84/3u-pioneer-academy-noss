## Learning Objectives (Objektif Pembelajaran)

Upon completion of this theory unit, trainees shall be able to:
1. Explain the consultation process and contraindications for body thermal therapy.
2. Describe work area setup, safety precautions and equipment testing for thermal therapy.
3. Explain client preparation procedures including PPE, towel management and draping.
4. Describe pre-procedure cleansing and deep cleansing methods.
5. Explain thermal therapy methods, monitoring and precautions.
6. Describe post-therapy advice, after care and appointment scheduling.
7. Explain sterilisation, replenishment and waste disposal for thermal therapy equipment.

---

## Instructional Plan (Kaedah Pengajaran 4 Langkah)

### Session 1: KP(1/7) + KT(1/7) — Thermal Therapy Consultation and Contraindications (90 min)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | State objectives. Link to C01 body consultation knowledge. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(1/7): Client profiling for thermal therapy, specific contraindications (heat sensitivity, circulatory disorders, pregnancy), consultation recording, informed consent. | 40 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(1/7). | 30 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review answers, clarify thermal-specific contraindications. | 10 min |

### Session 2: KP(2/7) + KT(2/7) — Work Area Setup, Safety and Equipment Testing (90 min)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Introduce thermal therapy equipment types. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(2/7): Room setup, safety precautions, equipment functionality testing, cleaning/sterilisation methods, arrangement. | 40 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(2/7). | 30 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review and confirm. | 10 min |

### Session 3: KP(3/7) + KT(3/7) — Client Preparation, PPE and Towel Management (80 min)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Link to C01 client preparation; introduce thermal-specific additions. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(3/7): PPE use, client materials, designated area, feet sanitisation, positioning, towel/body covering management, hair draping. | 35 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(3/7). | 25 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review. | 10 min |

### Session 4: KP(4/7) + KT(4/7) — Pre-Treatment Cleansing and Deep Cleansing (90 min)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Explain purpose of pre-treatment cleansing for thermal therapy. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(4/7): Pre-treatment methods, manufacturer instructions, cleansing/deep cleansing product selection, techniques, skin preparation. | 40 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(4/7). | 30 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review. | 10 min |

### Session 5: KP(5/7) + KT(5/7) — Body Thermal Therapy Methods and Monitoring (100 min)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Introduce thermal therapy principles: heat transfer, perspiration, detoxification. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(5/7): Types of thermal therapy (sauna, steam, infrared, paraffin wax, hot stones), methods, manufacturer instructions, precautions, monitoring client response, product removal, result recording. | 50 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(5/7). | 30 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review. | 10 min |

### Session 6: KP(6/7) + KT(6/7) — Post-Therapy Advice (80 min)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Recap therapy effects; introduce post-care needs. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(6/7): After care (hydration, rest, avoiding heat), home care, recording, scheduling. | 35 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(6/7). | 25 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review. | 10 min |

### Session 7: KP(7/7) + KT(7/7) — Sterilisation, Replenishment and Waste Disposal (70 min)

| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **1. Persediaan** | Link to C01 sterilisation knowledge; introduce thermal-specific equipment care. | 10 min |
| **2. Penyampaian** | Deliver KP(7/7): Thermal equipment sterilisation, product replenishment, inventory, waste disposal. | 30 min |
| **3. Penggunaan** | Trainees complete KT(7/7). | 20 min |
| **4. Pengesahan** | Review. Summarise entire CU theory. | 10 min |
