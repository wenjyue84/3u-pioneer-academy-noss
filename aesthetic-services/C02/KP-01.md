# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C02/KP(1/7)
**Competency Unit:** C02 — Body Thermal Therapy / Terapi Haba Badan
**Work Activity 1:** Carry Out Body Thermal Therapy Consultation
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Explain the process of recording client personal profiles for thermal therapy per the Data Protection Act.
2. Identify contraindications specific to body thermal therapy.
3. Describe how to determine client thermal therapy service requirements.
4. Explain the importance of informed consent for thermal therapy.

---

## 1.0 Introduction (Pengenalan)

Body thermal therapy (*terapi haba badan*) uses heat to induce perspiration (*peluh*), emulsify adipose fats and lipids, reduce fluid retention, promote detoxification (*detoksifikasi*) and improve blood circulation (*peredaran darah*). Before any thermal therapy can be performed, a thorough consultation must be conducted to ensure the client is suitable for heat-based treatments.

---

## 2.0 Client Personal Profile for Thermal Therapy

Building on the general body consultation (C01), the thermal therapy consultation must additionally record:

| Additional Data | Purpose |
|----------------|---------|
| Heat tolerance / sensitivity (*toleransi haba*) | Determine if client can withstand heat applications |
| Circulatory conditions | Assess risk of vasodilation effects |
| Blood pressure status (current reading if possible) | Hypotension/hypertension risk during heat exposure |
| Medication affecting heat response | Beta-blockers, vasodilators, anticoagulants |
| Previous thermal therapy experience | Gauge client familiarity and any past adverse reactions |
| Skin sensitivity to heat | History of heat rash, burns, photosensitivity |

All data recorded per the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

---

## 3.0 Contraindications for Body Thermal Therapy

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Thermal therapy poses significant risk to clients with cardiovascular, circulatory, or skin conditions. NEVER proceed without completing a full contraindication screening (*tapisan kontraindikasi haba*). If there is any doubt about a client's suitability, STOP and obtain written medical clearance (*pelepasan perubatan bertulis*) from a registered medical practitioner before treatment.

### 3.1 Absolute Contraindications (Treatment MUST NOT proceed)

| Contraindication | Reason |
|-----------------|--------|
| Heart conditions / cardiovascular disease (*penyakit jantung*) | Heat increases heart rate and vasodilation; risk of cardiac event |
| Thrombosis / deep vein thrombosis (*trombosis vena dalam*) | Heat may dislodge blood clots |
| Severe hypertension (*hipertensi teruk*) | Risk of stroke or cardiac emergency |
| Fever / acute infection (*demam / jangkitan akut*) | Body already under thermal stress |
| Skin infections / contagious conditions | Risk of spread; heat exacerbates infection |
| Epilepsy (uncontrolled) | Risk of seizure in heated environment |
| Cancer / malignancy | Heat may promote metastasis |
| Recent surgery (< 6 months) | Risk to healing tissues |
| Open wounds / burns | Heat aggravates damaged tissue |

### 3.2 Relative Contraindications (Proceed with caution / GP approval)

| Contraindication | Action |
|-----------------|--------|
| Pregnancy | Avoid all thermal therapy; risk of overheating the foetus |
| Diabetes | Reduced skin sensation; monitor closely for burns |
| Mild hypertension / hypotension | Monitor blood pressure; reduce treatment duration/temperature |
| Varicose veins | Avoid direct heat on affected areas |
| Metal implants / pins | Heat conduction risk; avoid area |
| Asthma / respiratory conditions | Steam treatments may trigger breathing difficulty |
| Claustrophobia (*klaustrofobia*) | Avoid enclosed thermal equipment (sauna cabinets) |

## 4.0 Determining Client Service Requirements

**Overview of Thermal Therapy Modalities** (*Gambaran Keseluruhan Modaliti Terapi Haba*):

| Modality | Bilingual Term | Heat Type | Primary Goal |
|----------|---------------|-----------|-------------|
| Sauna cabinet | Kabinet sauna | Dry heat (*haba kering*) | Perspiration, detoxification |
| Steam cabinet | Kabinet stim | Moist heat (*haba lembap*) | Deep cleansing, pore opening |
| Infrared therapy | Terapi inframerah | Radiant heat (*haba sinaran*) | Deep tissue, fat emulsification |
| Paraffin wax | Lilin parafin | Conductive heat (*haba konduksi*) | Moisturising, joint relief |
| Hot stone therapy | Terapi batu panas | Conductive heat (*haba konduksi*) | Muscle relaxation |
| Body wrap | Balutan badan | Occlusive heat (*haba oklusif*) | Detoxification, inch loss |

The aesthetician determines the appropriate thermal therapy by considering:

1. **Client's primary goal** — Weight loss, detoxification, relaxation, cellulite reduction, improved circulation.
2. **Body condition** — As assessed in body consultation (C01).
3. **Contraindication status** — Which thermal methods are safe for this client.
4. **Available equipment** — Match client needs to salon capabilities.
5. **Treatment course** — Single session vs. course of treatments.
6. **Budget** — Client's financial considerations.

---

## 5.0 Recording Consultation Findings

The thermal therapy consultation findings must be:
- Updated on the client's existing body consultation card/form.
- Recorded in a dedicated thermal therapy section.
- Include specific notes on heat tolerance, contraindications reviewed, and recommended thermal method.
- Dated and signed by the aesthetician.

---

## 6.0 Informed Consent (Persetujuan Termaklum)

### 6.1 Why Informed Consent is Required
- Thermal therapy carries specific risks (burns, dehydration, vasovagal response).
- Client must understand the procedure, risks, and expected outcomes.
- Legal protection for both client and salon.

### 6.2 Consent Form Content
The consent form must include:
- Description of the thermal therapy procedure to be performed
- Expected benefits
- Potential risks and side effects
- Client's confirmation that contraindications have been disclosed
- Client's signature and date
- Witness signature (therapist)

---

## 7.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Thermal therapy consultation builds upon the general body consultation (C01) with additional focus on heat-related contraindications, client heat tolerance, and informed consent. The consultation determines which thermal method is appropriate, ensures client safety, and provides the legal basis for proceeding with treatment.
