# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C02/KP(3/7)
**Competency Unit:** C02 — Body Thermal Therapy / Terapi Haba Badan
**Work Activity 3:** Prepare Client for Body Thermal Therapy Service
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Explain the correct use of PPE for thermal therapy services.
2. Describe client preparation procedures including feet sanitisation, positioning and draping.
3. Explain towel management techniques for body thermal therapy.

---

## 1.0 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

For body thermal therapy, the aesthetician must use:

| PPE Item | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Apron (*apron*) | Protect uniform from moisture, products, and heat |
| Disposable gloves (*sarung tangan pakai buang*) | Hygiene during product application and client handling |
| Mask (*topeng muka*) | Protection from steam and product vapours |
| Closed-toe shoes (*kasut bertutup*) | Protection from hot water/wax spills |

---

## 2.0 Client Preparation Steps

### 2.1 Tools and Materials for Client
- Service attire (disposable wrap / gown)
- Disposable slippers
- Shower cap / hair drape
- Towels (large for body covering, small for wiping)
- Glass of water for hydration

### 2.2 Designated Area and Service Attire
- Usher the client to the thermal therapy room's private change area.
- Provide service attire and instruct on wearing.
- Ensure privacy during change.

### 2.3 Personal Belongings
- Store all client belongings in a lockable locker.
- Client retains locker key.

### 2.4 Feet Sanitisation (*Sanitasi Kaki*)
Purpose: Prevent transfer of bacteria from feet to treatment surfaces.
1. Ask the client to stand on a clean towel.
2. Spray feet with antiseptic foot spray or wipe with sanitising wipe.
3. Provide clean disposable slippers.

### 2.5 Client Positioning
- For treatments requiring lying down: position client supine (face up) or prone (face down) on the treatment couch.
- For sauna/steam cabinet: assist client to seated position inside the cabinet.
- Ensure client comfort; adjust couch height/angle as needed.

### 2.6 Towel Management (Body Covering)
Towel management (*pengurusan tuala*) is critical for:
- **Client modesty** — Only expose the area being treated.
- **Warmth** — Maintain body temperature in non-treated areas.
- **Hygiene** — Fresh towels create a barrier between client and equipment.

Techniques:
- **Full body drape** — Large towel covering entire body; fold back to expose treatment area.
- **Sectional draping** — Separate towels for upper and lower body; move as treatment progresses.
- **Cabinet draping** — Towel around neck to seal sauna/steam cabinet; towel on seat.

### 2.7 Hair Draping (*Pembalutan Rambut*)
- Gather client's hair and secure with a clip or hair band.
- Place a shower cap or head towel to protect hair from steam and products.
- Ensure no loose hair interferes with the treatment.

---

## 3.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Client preparation for thermal therapy includes correct PPE usage by the therapist, proper attire for the client, feet sanitisation, comfortable positioning, appropriate towel management for modesty and hygiene, and hair draping. These steps ensure client safety, comfort and professional service delivery.
