## Soalan Dwibahasa / Bilingual Assessment Questions

**Q1 (MCQ):**
**EN:** What is the primary physiological effect of heat on blood vessels?
**BM:** Apakah kesan fisiologi utama haba ke atas saluran darah?
A. Vasoconstriction  B. Vasodilation  C. Coagulation  D. Calcification | **Answer / Jawapan:** B

**Q2 (MCQ):**
**EN:** Which condition is an absolute contraindication for thermal therapy?
**BM:** Keadaan manakah merupakan kontraindikasi mutlak bagi terapi haba?
A. Mild eczema  B. Controlled hypertension  C. Thrombosis  D. Low blood pressure | **Answer / Jawapan:** C

**Q3 (Short Answer / Jawapan Pendek):**
**EN:** List FOUR (4) physiological effects of heat application.
**BM:** Senaraikan EMPAT (4) kesan fisiologi aplikasi haba.
**Answer / Jawapan:** Any 4: Vasodilation, increased perspiration, muscle relaxation, improved lymphatic drainage, pain relief, raised metabolic rate.

**Q4 (Short Answer / Jawapan Pendek):**
**EN:** Describe TWO (2) differences between sauna and steam cabinet therapy.
**BM:** Huraikan DUA (2) perbezaan antara terapi sauna dan kabinet stim.
**Answer / Jawapan:** Sauna: dry heat, 50–70°C; Steam cabinet: moist heat, 40–50°C. (Accept any 2 valid differences.)

**Q5 (Scenario / Senario):**
**EN:** A hypertensive client on controlled medication requests steam therapy. What action would you take and why?
**BM:** Seorang pelanggan hipertensi dengan ubat terkawal memohon terapi stim. Apakah tindakan anda dan mengapa?
**Answer / Jawapan:** Relative contraindication. Proceed with caution; reduce temperature and duration; monitor client closely; ensure hydration.

## Marking Scheme

### Section A
1-B, 2-C, 3-B, 4-C, 5-B, 6-B, 7-B, 8-B, 9-C, 10-C

### Section B
**11.** FALSE — Infrared therapy penetrates deep into tissues (up to several centimetres), providing deep tissue heating.
**12.** TRUE — Claustrophobia is a relative contraindication; alternative non-enclosed methods (infrared lamp) can be used.
**13.** FALSE — Dry body brushing should be in strokes TOWARDS the heart to stimulate lymphatic drainage.
**14.** TRUE — Diabetes reduces skin sensation, increasing burn risk.
**15.** FALSE — Informed consent is required for EVERY treatment, even returning clients.

### Section C
**16.** Any 6: Vasodilation, increased perspiration, increased metabolic rate, muscle relaxation, pain relief, adipose fat emulsification, improved lymphatic drainage, detoxification.
**17.** Sauna: dry heat, 50-70 C, dehydrating. Steam: moist heat, 40-50 C, hydrating/pore opening. (Accept any 3 valid differences.)
**18.** 1. Stop treatment immediately. 2. Remove client from equipment. 3. Cool client with towels. 4. Provide water. 5. Place in recovery position if needed. 6. Monitor vital signs. 7. Call emergency services if symptoms worsen. 8. Record the incident.
**19.** 1. Select cleanser for skin type. 2. Apply with effleurage from extremities to torso. 3. Remove with warm damp sponges. 4. Apply deep cleansing exfoliant with circular movements. 5. Remove, tone, pat dry.

### Section D
**20a.** Mild hypertension (controlled): Relative contraindication. Action: Proceed with caution, monitor blood pressure, reduce treatment duration/temperature, ensure GP approval if needed.
**20b.** Infrared blanket or steam cabinet (moderate heat). Justification: lower temperature than sauna; less cardiovascular stress for hypertensive client; effective for detoxification and weight management.
**20c.** 1. Don PPE. 2. Prepare materials. 3. Usher client to change. 4. Store belongings. 5. Sanitise feet. 6. Position on couch/in cabinet. 7. Drape with towels. 8. Drape hair.
**20d.** Any 6: Drink 2L water, avoid alcohol/caffeine 24h, avoid hot baths 4-6h, rest 30min, avoid strenuous exercise 24h, eat light, monitor blood pressure, continue BP medication as prescribed, report unusual symptoms.
**20e.** Thermal method used, temperature/duration, client response, any adverse reactions, results observed, date/signature.
