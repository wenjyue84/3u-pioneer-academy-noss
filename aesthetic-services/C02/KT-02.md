## Questions (Soalan)

### Q1 — Short Answer
List FIVE (5) specific room requirements for a body thermal therapy treatment area that differ from a standard consultation room.

### Q3 — True or False (with justification)
a) The emergency exit door in a thermal therapy room should lock from inside for privacy.
b) An RCD (residual current device) is required on electrical circuits for thermal equipment.
c) A CO2 fire extinguisher is appropriate for electrical fires in the thermal therapy room.

### Q4 — Short Answer
Describe the four zones of a thermal therapy work area arranged in service flow order.

### Q5 — Multiple Choice
How often should sauna/steam cabinet interiors be deep cleaned?
A. After every client B. Daily C. Weekly D. Monthly

---

## Answer Scheme

**A1:** Any 5 of: Adequate ventilation/exhaust fan, waterproof non-slip flooring, floor drainage, dedicated electrical circuits (RCD protected), emergency outward-opening door, shower access nearby, temperature control (thermostat).

**A2:** 1-D, 2-A, 3-B, 4-C, 5-E

**A3:** a) FALSE — The door must open outward and must NOT lock clients inside; there should be no locks that trap clients. b) TRUE — RCD protection is required for high-power thermal equipment to prevent electrical shock. c) TRUE — CO2 fire extinguishers are appropriate for electrical fires.

**A4:** 1. Consultation/preparation zone — couch, trolley with pre-treatment products. 2. Thermal treatment zone — thermal equipment (sauna, steam, infrared). 3. Rinse/cool-down zone — shower, cool towels, water station. 4. Post-treatment zone — couch for rest and post-treatment application.

**A5:** C. Weekly (with wipe-down after every client)
