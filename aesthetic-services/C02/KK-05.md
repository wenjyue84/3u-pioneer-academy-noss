# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C02/KK(5/7)
**Competency Unit:** C02 — Body Thermal Therapy
**Work Activity 5:** Carry Out Body Thermal Therapy Service
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** BLUE / BIRU

---

## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Determine and perform the appropriate method of body thermal therapy.
2. Monitor client response throughout treatment.
3. Remove residual products and transition client post-treatment.
4. Record thermal therapy service results.

---

## Equipment and Materials
| Item | Qty |
|------|-----|
| Sauna cabinet OR steam cabinet OR infrared lamp/blanket | 1 |
| Timer | 1 |
| Thermometer / temperature gauge | 1 |
| Drinking water and glass | 1 |
| Warm damp towels (for wiping) | 4 |
| Fresh dry towels | 3 |
| Cool towels (emergency) | 2 |
| Client consultation card | 1 |
| Pen | 1 |

---

## Safety Precautions
1. NEVER leave client unattended during thermal therapy.
2. Check client every 5 minutes.
3. Keep emergency cool towels and water within reach.
4. Know the location of the emergency stop button on all equipment.
5. Have first aid kit accessible.

---

## Procedure

### Step 1: Determine Thermal Method
1.1 Based on consultation findings, select the appropriate thermal method.
1.2 Verify equipment is at the correct operating temperature.
1.3 Set the timer for the prescribed duration.

### Step 2: Commence Thermal Therapy
**For Sauna/Steam Cabinet:**
2.1 Assist client into the cabinet; ensure seated comfortably.
2.2 Close cabinet; seal neck opening with towel.
2.3 Start timer; record start time.

**For Infrared Lamp/Blanket:**
2.4 Position client on couch.
2.5 Set infrared device at appropriate distance/setting.
2.6 Cover client with infrared blanket or position lamp.
2.7 Start timer; record start time.

### Step 3: Monitor Client Response
3.1 Check client verbally every 5 minutes: "How are you feeling?"
3.2 Observe for: excessive redness, sweating rate, breathing, facial expression.
3.3 Offer water at regular intervals.
3.4 Watch for adverse reactions: dizziness, nausea, excessive distress, difficulty breathing.
3.5 **If adverse reaction occurs:** STOP immediately, remove client from equipment, cool with towels, provide water, place in recovery position if needed, call for help.

### Step 4: Complete Treatment
4.1 When timer sounds, turn off equipment.
4.2 Open cabinet door or remove blanket.
4.3 Allow client to adjust for 1-2 minutes.

### Step 5: Remove Residual Products
5.1 Wipe perspiration from client's body with warm, damp towels.
5.2 Pat dry with fresh towels.
5.3 Offer water.

### Step 6: Client Transition
6.1 Assist client to sit up SLOWLY (risk of postural hypotension).
6.2 Allow client to rest for 5-10 minutes.
6.3 Usher client to change into own attire.
6.4 Return all personal belongings.

### Step 7: Record Results
7.1 On the consultation card, record:
- Thermal method used
- Temperature and duration
- Client response during treatment
- Any adverse reactions
- Visible results (skin condition, perspiration level)
7.2 Date and sign.
