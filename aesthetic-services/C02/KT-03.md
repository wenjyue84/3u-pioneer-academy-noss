## Questions (Soalan)

### Q1 — List
List the FOUR (4) PPE items required for a therapist performing body thermal therapy.

### Q2 — Short Answer
Explain the purpose of feet sanitisation before thermal therapy and describe the procedure in THREE (3) steps.

### Q3 — True or False (with justification)
a) A shower cap is optional and not required during steam cabinet treatment.
b) Towel management serves the purposes of modesty, warmth and hygiene.
c) The client should remove all jewellery before thermal therapy.

### Q4 — Multiple Choice
For a sauna cabinet treatment, the correct towel draping technique is:
A. Full body drape covering the entire client B. Towel around the neck to seal the cabinet C. No towel required as the cabinet encloses the body D. Towel over the face only

### Q5 — Short Answer
Describe THREE (3) different towel management techniques and state when each is used.

---

## Answer Scheme

**A1:** Apron, disposable gloves, mask, closed-toe shoes.

**A2:** Purpose: Prevent transfer of bacteria from feet to treatment surfaces. Procedure: 1. Ask client to stand on a clean towel. 2. Spray feet with antiseptic foot spray or wipe with sanitising wipe. 3. Provide clean disposable slippers.

**A3:** a) FALSE — Hair draping (shower cap or head towel) is required to protect hair from steam and products. b) TRUE — These are the three purposes of towel management. c) TRUE — Metal jewellery can conduct heat and cause burns.

**A4:** B. Towel around the neck to seal the cabinet.

**A5:** 1. Full body drape — large towel covering entire body; fold back to expose treatment area; used for couch-based treatments (infrared, hot stone). 2. Sectional draping — separate towels for upper and lower body; move as treatment progresses; used for massage and product application. 3. Cabinet draping — towel around neck to seal cabinet, towel on seat; used for sauna/steam cabinet treatments.
