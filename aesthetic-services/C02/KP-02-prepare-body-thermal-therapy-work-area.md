## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Describe the setup requirements for a body thermal therapy room/cubicle.
2. Explain safety precautions specific to thermal therapy environments.
3. Describe equipment functionality testing procedures.
4. Explain cleaning and sterilisation methods for thermal therapy equipment.

---

## 1.0 Room/Cubicle Setup (Persediaan Bilik/Kubikel)

The thermal therapy room requires specific considerations beyond a standard consultation room:

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Ventilation (*pengudaraan*) | Adequate air circulation; exhaust fan for steam equipment |
| Temperature control | Room thermostat; ability to regulate ambient temperature |
| Waterproof flooring | Non-slip, water-resistant surface for steam/sweat spillage |
| Drainage | Floor drain for steam cabinets and shower areas |
| Electrical points | Dedicated circuits for high-power thermal equipment; RCD protected |
| Emergency access | Door must open outward; no locks that trap clients inside |
| Shower access | Nearby shower for post-treatment rinse |

---

## 2.0 Safety Precautions (Langkah Keselamatan)

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Thermal equipment operates at high temperatures and uses mains electrical supply in a wet environment. Ensure all equipment is connected to a Residual Current Device (RCD) (*Peranti Arus Baki*) circuit breaker rated ≤ 30 mA. NEVER operate thermal equipment with wet hands or allow water to contact electrical connections. Inspect power cords and heating elements before each use — frayed cables or damaged elements must be taken out of service immediately and reported to a qualified electrician (*juruteknik elektrik berkelayakan*).

### 2.1 General Safety
- All electrical thermal equipment must be PAT tested and grounded.
- Emergency stop/off switch must be accessible on all thermal equipment.
- Fire extinguisher (CO2 type for electrical fires) within 5 metres.
- First aid kit stocked with burn treatment supplies.
- Timer visible to both therapist and client.
- Temperature gauges calibrated and visible.

### 2.2 Client Safety
- Never leave a client unattended during thermal therapy.
- Provide a call bell or alert system for the client.
- Ensure water is available for hydration during treatment.
- Monitor client continuously for signs of distress (dizziness, nausea, excessive redness).

---

## 3.0 Equipment Functionality Testing (Ujian Kefungsian Peralatan)

Before each service day, test all thermal equipment:

| Equipment | Test Procedure |
|-----------|---------------|
| Sauna cabinet (*kabinet sauna*) | Power on, check heating element, verify thermostat reading, timer function |
| Steam cabinet (*kabinet stim*) | Fill water reservoir, power on, check steam output, verify temperature gauge |
| Infrared lamp/blanket (*lampu/selimut inframerah*) | Power on, check heat output at different settings, verify timer |
| Paraffin wax heater (*pemanas lilin parafin*) | Check wax temperature (52-54 C for body), thermostat accuracy |
| Hot stone heater (*pemanas batu panas*) | Fill with water, set temperature (50-55 C), verify stone heating |

Record all test results in the equipment log.

---

## 4.0 Cleaning and Sterilisation of Thermal Equipment

| Equipment | Cleaning Method |
|-----------|----------------|
| Sauna/steam cabinet interior | Wipe with antibacterial solution after each client; deep clean weekly |
| Cabinet seat/bench | Disinfectant spray and wipe after each use |
| Infrared blanket | Wipe exterior with disinfectant; use disposable liner between client and blanket |
| Paraffin wax heater | Remove used wax; clean interior with warm water; refill with fresh wax |
| Hot stones | Wash with antibacterial soap; sterilise in disinfectant solution |
| Towels and linens | Launder at 60 C minimum after each use |

---

## 5.0 Arrangement of Tools, Equipment and Materials

Arrange the thermal therapy area in service flow order:
1. **Consultation/preparation zone** — Couch, trolley with pre-treatment products
2. **Thermal treatment zone** — Thermal equipment (sauna, steam, infrared)
3. **Rinse/cool-down zone** — Shower, cool towels, water station
4. **Post-treatment zone** — Couch for rest and post-treatment application

---

## 6.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Preparing the thermal therapy work area requires attention to ventilation, waterproofing, electrical safety, equipment testing, and proper cleaning/sterilisation protocols. The area must be arranged in a logical service flow and all safety measures must be in place before any client enters the treatment room.
