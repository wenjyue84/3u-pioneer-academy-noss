## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Deliver appropriate after care and home care advice.
2. Record post-therapy advice on the client card.
3. Schedule the client's next service appointment.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Deliver After Care Advice
1.1 Seat client comfortably. 1.2 Advise: drink 2+ litres water in next 24 hours. 1.3 Avoid alcohol and caffeine for 24 hours. 1.4 Avoid hot baths for 4-6 hours. 1.5 Rest for 30 minutes. 1.6 Avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours. 1.7 Eat light meals. 1.8 Report any unusual symptoms.

### Step 2: Home Care Advice
2.1 Apply body moisturiser to rehydrate skin. 2.2 Continue adequate daily water intake. 2.3 Recommend appropriate home care products. 2.4 Provide written instructions.

### Step 3: Record
3.1 Record all advice given on consultation card. 3.2 Note client's response and questions. 3.3 Date and sign.

### Step 4: Schedule Appointment
4.1 Determine next appointment date per service plan. 4.2 Confirm with client. 4.3 Provide appointment confirmation. 4.4 Inform of cancellation policy.

### Step 5: Farewell
5.1 Ensure client has all belongings. 5.2 Thank client; escort to reception.
