# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C02/KP(6/7)
**Competency Unit:** C02 — Body Thermal Therapy / Terapi Haba Badan
**Work Activity 6:** Carry Out Post Body Thermal Therapy Advice
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Explain after care advice specific to body thermal therapy.
2. Describe home care recommendations following thermal therapy.
3. Explain the importance of recording post-therapy advice.
4. Describe the procedure for scheduling follow-up appointments.

---

## 1.0 After Care Advice (Nasihat Penjagaan Selepas)

Following thermal therapy, the client must be advised:

| Advice | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| Drink at least 2 litres of water over the next 24 hours | Replace fluids lost through perspiration |
| Avoid alcohol and caffeine for 24 hours | These promote further dehydration |
| Avoid hot baths/showers for 4-6 hours | Body needs time to regulate temperature |
| Rest for 30 minutes after treatment | Allow cardiovascular system to normalise |
| Avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours | Body is in recovery mode |
| Eat light meals; avoid heavy food | Digestive system redirects blood after heat exposure |
| Expect increased urination | Detoxification continues post-treatment |
| Report any unusual symptoms | Contact salon or seek medical advice if feeling unwell |

---

## 2.0 Home Care Advice

- Apply body moisturiser to rehydrate skin after perspiration.
- Continue adequate water intake daily.
- Maintain balanced diet to support detoxification benefits.
- Use recommended body products between sessions.
- Follow the salon service plan for treatment frequency.

---

## 3.0 Recording Post-Therapy Advice

All advice given must be documented on the client's card including:
- Specific after care advice delivered
- Home care products recommended
- Client's understanding confirmed
- Any concerns raised by the client
- Date and therapist signature

---

## 4.0 Scheduling Next Appointment

- Recommend follow-up based on the service plan (typically weekly for a course of treatments).
- Confirm date and time with the client.
- Provide appointment card or digital confirmation.
- Remind client of cancellation policy.

---

## 5.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Post-thermal therapy advice focuses on hydration, rest, temperature regulation and product use. Recording all advice ensures continuity of care and client safety between sessions.
