## Questions (Soalan)

### Q1 — Multiple Choice
Which of the following is an ABSOLUTE contraindication for body thermal therapy?
A. Mild hypertension B. Claustrophobia C. Cardiovascular disease D. Varicose veins

### Q2 — Short Answer
List FOUR (4) additional data items that must be recorded during a thermal therapy consultation beyond the standard body consultation data.

### Q3 — True or False (with justification)
a) A client with uncontrolled epilepsy may receive thermal therapy if they sign a consent form.
b) Informed consent must include a description of potential risks and side effects.
c) Pregnancy is an absolute contraindication for all body thermal therapy.
d) A client's heat tolerance should be assessed before thermal therapy.

### Q4 — Short Answer
Explain the purpose of informed consent for thermal therapy and list THREE (3) items that must be included on the consent form.

### Q5 — Multiple Choice
Which legislation governs the collection of client personal data in Malaysia?
A. OSHA 1994 B. PDPA 2010 C. Environmental Quality Act 1974 D. Employment Act 1955

---

## Answer Scheme

**A1:** C. Cardiovascular disease

**A2:** Any 4 of: Heat tolerance/sensitivity, circulatory conditions, blood pressure status, medication affecting heat response, previous thermal therapy experience, skin sensitivity to heat.

**A3:** a) FALSE — Uncontrolled epilepsy is an absolute contraindication; consent does not override it. b) TRUE — Informed consent must describe risks and side effects. c) TRUE — Pregnancy is listed as a contraindication where thermal therapy should be avoided due to risk of overheating the foetus. d) TRUE — Heat tolerance assessment is essential before selecting thermal therapy method and parameters.

**A4:** Purpose: To ensure client understands the procedure, risks, and expected outcomes; legal protection for both client and salon. Consent form must include (any 3): description of procedure, expected benefits, potential risks/side effects, confirmation contraindications disclosed, client signature and date, witness signature.

**A5:** B. PDPA 2010
