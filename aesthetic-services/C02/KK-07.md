# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C02/KK(7/7)
**Competency Unit:** C02 — Body Thermal Therapy
**Work Activity 7:** Upkeep Body Thermal Therapy Work Area
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** BLUE / BIRU

---

## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Sterilise and sanitise thermal therapy tools and equipment.
2. Replenish materials and products.
3. Update stock inventory.
4. Dispose of waste per local authority requirements.

---

## Equipment and Materials
| Item | Qty |
|------|-----|
| Disinfectant spray/solution | 1 |
| Clean cloths | 4 |
| Disposable gloves | 2 pairs |
| Replacement consumables | As needed |
| Stock cards / inventory sheets | As required |
| Waste bins (general, clinical, recycling) | 1 each |
| Equipment log book | 1 |

---

## Procedure

### Step 1: Sterilise and Sanitise
1.1 Wipe sauna/steam cabinet interior with antibacterial solution; ventilate.
1.2 Disinfect cabinet seat/bench.
1.3 Wipe infrared equipment with disinfectant; replace disposable liner.
1.4 Drain and clean paraffin wax heater if applicable; refill with fresh wax.
1.5 Wash hot stones with antibacterial soap; soak in disinfectant; rinse; air dry.
1.6 Disinfect treatment couch, trolley, all surfaces.
1.7 Send used towels/linen to laundry (60 C minimum).

### Step 2: Replenish Materials
2.1 Restock disposable liners, gloves, towels, slippers.
2.2 Check pre-treatment products levels; replenish.
2.3 Refill water station for clients.
2.4 Check product expiry dates; remove expired.
2.5 Apply FIFO rotation.

### Step 3: Inventory
3.1 Physical count all thermal therapy supplies.
3.2 Update stock cards.
3.3 Flag items at reorder level.
3.4 Complete and submit inventory report.

### Step 4: Waste Disposal
4.1 Used paraffin wax — general waste.
4.2 Used gloves, contaminated items — yellow clinical waste bag.
4.3 Empty clean containers — recycling.
4.4 Seal and label waste bags.
4.5 Place at designated collection point.
4.6 Update waste disposal log.

### Step 5: Final Check
5.1 All equipment off (unless pre-heating for next session).
5.2 Room ventilated and clean.
5.3 Work area ready for next session.
