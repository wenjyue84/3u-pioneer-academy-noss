## Procedure

### Step 1: Determine Pre-Treatment Method
1.1 Review client's skin type and condition from consultation card.
1.2 Check manufacturer instructions for selected products.
1.3 Select method: dry brushing, body scrub, or pre-treatment oil.
1.4 Check client for allergies to selected products.

### Step 2: Perform Pre-Treatment (if dry brushing)
2.1 Using natural bristle brush, brush the body in long strokes towards the heart.
2.2 Start from feet, work up legs, then arms, then torso.
2.3 Avoid face, breasts, and any irritated areas.
2.4 Duration: 5-10 minutes.

### Step 3: Cleansing
3.1 Select cleanser appropriate to client's skin type.
3.2 Apply cleanser with long effleurage movements.
3.3 Work from extremities toward the torso.
3.4 Remove with warm, damp sponges.
3.5 Blot dry.

### Step 4: Deep Cleansing
4.1 Apply deep cleansing exfoliant.
4.2 Massage with circular movements, moderate pressure.
4.3 Focus on congested areas (back, shoulders, upper arms).
4.4 Remove thoroughly with warm, damp sponges.
4.5 Tone with hydrating toner or thermal water spray.
4.6 Pat dry.

### Step 5: Confirm Readiness
5.1 Skin is clean, prepared and ready for thermal therapy.
5.2 Re-drape client with fresh towel if needed.
