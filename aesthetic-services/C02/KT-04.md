## Questions (Soalan)

### Q1 — Short Answer
State THREE (3) purposes of pre-treatment before thermal therapy.

### Q2 — Multiple Choice
Which pre-treatment method uses a natural bristle brush in long strokes towards the heart?
A. Body scrub B. Pre-treatment oil application C. Dry body brushing D. Deep cleansing

### Q3 — Short Answer
Describe the FIVE (5) steps of the body cleansing procedure before thermal therapy.

### Q4 — True or False (with justification)
a) Deep cleansing products should be applied with circular movements using moderate pressure.
b) The aesthetician may ignore manufacturer instructions if they have prior experience with similar products.
c) Pre-treatment helps open pores and improves product absorption.

### Q5 — Matching
Match the pre-treatment type with the product:
| Type | | Product |
|------|---|---------|
| 1. Dry body brushing | | A. Salt scrub / sugar scrub |
| 2. Body scrub | | B. Warming essential oil blend |
| 3. Pre-treatment oil | | C. Natural bristle brush |

---

## Answer Scheme

**A1:** Any 3 of: Remove surface impurities (dirt, oil, dead skin), open pores for perspiration, improve product absorption, assess skin condition before applying heat.

**A2:** C. Dry body brushing.

**A3:** 1. Select appropriate cleansing product for client's skin type. 2. Apply cleanser using long, sweeping effleurage movements. 3. Work systematically from extremities toward the torso. 4. Remove cleanser with warm, damp sponges or mitts. 5. Blot dry with a clean towel.

**A4:** a) TRUE. b) FALSE — Manufacturer instructions must always be followed regardless of experience. c) TRUE.

**A5:** 1-C, 2-A, 3-B.
