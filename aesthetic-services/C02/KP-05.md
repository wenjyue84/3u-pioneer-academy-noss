## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Describe the different methods of body thermal therapy.
2. Explain the physiological effects of heat on the body.
3. Explain the precautions and monitoring requirements during thermal therapy.
4. Describe proper procedures for product removal and result recording.

---

## 1.0 Physiological Effects of Heat on the Body (Kesan Fisiologi Haba)

When heat is applied to the body, the following physiological responses occur:

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Vasodilation** (*vasodilatasi*) | Blood vessels dilate, increasing blood flow to the skin surface |
| **Increased perspiration** (*peningkatan peluh*) | Sweat glands activated; toxins and excess fluid released |
| **Increased metabolic rate** (*kadar metabolisme meningkat*) | Cellular activity increases; fat emulsification begins |
| **Muscle relaxation** (*relaksasi otot*) | Heat reduces muscle tension and spasm |
| **Pain relief** (*melegakan kesakitan*) | Nerve endings desensitised; endorphin release |
| **Adipose fat emulsification** (*pengemulsian lemak adipos*) | Heat softens subcutaneous fat deposits |
| **Improved lymphatic drainage** | Fluid movement enhanced; oedema reduced |
| **Detoxification** (*detoksifikasi*) | Toxins released through perspiration |

## 2.0 Methods of Body Thermal Therapy

### 2.1 Sauna Cabinet (Kabinet Sauna)

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Type | Dry heat in enclosed cabinet (head exposed) |
| Temperature | 50-70 C |
| Duration | 15-20 minutes |
| Benefits | Perspiration, detoxification, weight loss, relaxation |
| Precautions | Monitor for dizziness; provide water; do not exceed time |

### 2.2 Steam Cabinet (Kabinet Stim)

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Type | Moist heat (steam) in enclosed cabinet (head exposed) |
| Temperature | 40-50 C |
| Duration | 15-20 minutes |
| Benefits | Deep cleansing, pore opening, perspiration, skin softening |
| Precautions | Check water level; monitor for breathing difficulty; provide water |

### 2.3 Infrared Therapy (Terapi Inframerah)

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Type | Infrared lamps or infrared body blanket |
| Temperature | Adjustable; typically 40-60 C at skin surface |
| Duration | 20-30 minutes |
| Benefits | Deep tissue heating, improved circulation, fat emulsification, pain relief |
| Precautions | Maintain safe distance (lamp); check skin regularly for overheating |

### 2.4 Paraffin Wax Treatment (Rawatan Lilin Parafin)

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Type | Warm paraffin wax applied to body areas |
| Temperature | 52-54 C (body application); test on inner wrist before application |
| Duration | 15-20 minutes (while wax cools and sets) |
| Benefits | Moisturising, softening, joint pain relief, improved circulation |
| Precautions | Always test temperature; never reheat used wax; check for wax allergy |

### 2.5 Hot Stone Therapy (Terapi Batu Panas)

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Type | Heated basalt stones placed on or used to massage body |
| Temperature | 50-55 C; test on therapist's inner wrist |
| Duration | 45-60 minutes |
| Benefits | Deep muscle relaxation, improved circulation, stress relief |
| Precautions | Always test stone temperature; never place stone directly on spine or bony prominences without towel barrier |

### 2.6 Body Wrap (Balutan Badan)

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Type | Occlusive thermal treatment — body coated in active product, wrapped in plastic film and thermal blanket |
| Temperature | Ambient body heat retained by occlusion; no external heat source required |
| Duration | 30-60 minutes |
| Benefits | Inch loss (via perspiration and temporary fluid reduction), detoxification, skin softening, improved circulation |
| Precautions | Monitor for overheating and claustrophobia; client must not be left unattended; ensure adequate ventilation |

**Body wrap procedure overview:**
1. Apply active product (seaweed gel, mud, clay, or slimming cream) to the body using effleurage movements.
2. Wrap the body tightly (but not restrictively) in plastic film (*filem plastik*) from feet to chest.
3. Cover with thermal blanket (*selimut haba*) or foil sheet to retain body heat.
4. Client rests for prescribed duration; monitor continuously.
5. Remove wrap, wipe off residual product, apply post-wrap moisturiser.

**Common body wrap products (*produk balutan badan*)**:

| Product | Active Ingredients | Primary Effect |
|---------|-------------------|---------------|
| Seaweed wrap (*balutan rumpai laut*) | Iodine, minerals, antioxidants | Detoxification, skin firming |
| Mud/clay wrap (*balutan tanah liat*) | Minerals, silicates | Detoxification, exfoliation |
| Herbal wrap (*balutan herba*) | Plant extracts, essential oils | Relaxation, circulation |
| Slimming wrap (*balutan kurus*) | Caffeine, L-carnitine, botanical extracts | Inch loss, cellulite reduction |

## 3.0 Performing Thermal Therapy Per Manufacturer Instructions

All thermal equipment must be operated per manufacturer's instructions:
- Follow specified warm-up times.
- Do not exceed maximum temperature settings.
- Use recommended cleaning agents only.
- Follow specified treatment durations.
- Observe maintenance schedules.
- Keep manufacturer manuals accessible in the treatment room.

---

## 4.0 Precautions During Thermal Therapy (Langkah Berjaga-jaga)

### 4.1 Specific Contraindications Per Modality (*Kontraindikasi Spesifik Mengikut Modaliti*)

| Modality | Additional Specific Contraindications |
|----------|--------------------------------------|
| Sauna cabinet (*kabinet sauna*) | Claustrophobia; chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD); extreme obesity (client unable to exit safely) |
| Steam cabinet (*kabinet stim*) | Asthma or breathing difficulties; claustrophobia; sinusitis (steam may aggravate) |
| Infrared therapy (*terapi inframerah*) | Tattoos over treatment area (risk of overheating pigment); photosensitive medications; implanted electronic devices (pacemakers) |
| Paraffin wax (*lilin parafin*) | Wax allergy; very sensitive or inflamed skin; open lesions or abrasions |
| Hot stone therapy (*terapi batu panas*) | Peripheral neuropathy (reduced sensation); bruising or inflammation; sunburn |
| Body wrap (*balutan badan*) | Product allergies (patch test required 24 hours prior); claustrophobia; high/low blood pressure; kidney disease |

### 4.2 General Precautions During Treatment

| Precaution | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Monitor client response | Check every 5 minutes; ask how the client feels |
| Watch for adverse reactions | Dizziness (*pening*), nausea (*loya*), excessive redness (*kemerahan berlebihan*), fainting (*pengsan*), difficulty breathing (*kesukaran bernafas*) |
| Emergency protocol | If adverse reaction occurs: stop treatment immediately, cool client, provide water, place in recovery position if needed, call emergency services if required |
| Hydration (*penghidratan*) | Offer water before, during and after treatment |
| Duration control | Use a timer; never exceed recommended treatment time |
| Temperature monitoring | Check equipment temperature readings regularly |

## 5.0 Post-Treatment Procedures

### 5.1 Removing Residual Products
- Remove paraffin wax by peeling off in sections.
- Wipe perspiration from client's body with warm, damp towels.
- Rinse off any remaining products (exfoliant, oil) if required.
- Pat the client dry with a fresh towel.

### 5.2 Client Transition
- Assist the client to sit up slowly (risk of postural hypotension after heat treatment).
- Offer a glass of water.
- Allow the client to rest for 5-10 minutes before changing.
- Usher the client to change into own attire.
- Return all personal belongings.

### 5.3 Recording Results
Record on the client's consultation card:
- Type of thermal therapy performed
- Temperature and duration used
- Client's response during treatment
- Any adverse reactions
- Results observed (skin condition, perspiration level, relaxation)
- Date and therapist signature

---

## 6.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Body thermal therapy encompasses several methods including sauna, steam, infrared, paraffin wax and hot stone treatments. Each method induces perspiration, improves circulation, emulsifies fat and promotes detoxification. The aesthetician must follow manufacturer instructions, maintain constant client monitoring, and record all results accurately.
