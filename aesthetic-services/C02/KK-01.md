## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Record client personal profiles for thermal therapy per Data Protection Act.
2. Check client contraindications specific to thermal therapy.
3. Determine client thermal therapy service requirements.
4. Obtain informed consent.

## Safety Precautions
1. Do not proceed if absolute contraindication is identified.
2. Maintain client data confidentiality.

---

## Procedure

### Step 1: Obtain and Review Client Profile
1.1 Retrieve client's body consultation card (from C01).
1.2 Review existing medical history, allergies, and conditions.
1.3 Record additional thermal-specific data: heat tolerance, circulatory conditions, blood pressure, heat-affecting medications, previous thermal therapy experience.

### Step 2: Check Contraindications
2.1 Systematically check absolute contraindications (cardiovascular disease, thrombosis, severe hypertension, fever, skin infections, uncontrolled epilepsy, cancer, recent surgery, open wounds).
2.2 Check relative contraindications (pregnancy, diabetes, mild hypertension, varicose veins, metal implants, asthma, claustrophobia).
2.3 Record all findings.
2.4 If absolute contraindication found: inform client, decline treatment, recommend GP.

### Step 3: Determine Service Requirements
3.1 Discuss client's primary goal (detoxification, weight loss, relaxation, cellulite reduction).
3.2 Consider body condition from C01 consultation.
3.3 Match client needs with available thermal therapy methods.
3.4 Recommend appropriate thermal therapy type, frequency and duration.
3.5 Discuss cost and treatment course.

### Step 4: Obtain Informed Consent
4.1 Explain the selected thermal therapy procedure to the client.
4.2 Explain expected benefits and potential risks.
4.3 Present the consent form; allow client time to read.
4.4 Answer all client questions.
4.5 Obtain client signature on the consent form.
4.6 Countersign as witness.

### Step 5: Record and Update
5.1 Update consultation card with all thermal therapy consultation findings.
5.2 File consent form with consultation card.
5.3 Date and sign entry.
