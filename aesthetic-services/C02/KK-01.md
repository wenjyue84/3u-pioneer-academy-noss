## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Record client personal profiles for thermal therapy per Data Protection Act.
2. Check client contraindications specific to thermal therapy.
3. Determine client thermal therapy service requirements.
4. Obtain informed consent.

## Safety Precautions
> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Never proceed with thermal therapy consultation without completing a full contraindication check. Thermal treatments carry serious cardiovascular and circulatory risks. Refer all absolute contraindications to a GP before any treatment.

1. Wash hands and disinfect consultation surfaces before each client.
2. Do not proceed if absolute contraindication is identified.
3. Maintain client data confidentiality (PDPA 2010).
4. Keep blood pressure monitor calibrated and in service.

---

## Procedure

### Step 1: Prepare Consultation Area and Receive Client (Persediaan Kawasan dan Penerimaan Klien)
1.1 Wash hands thoroughly with antibacterial soap.
1.2 Spray disinfectant on the consultation desk, chair, and any surfaces the client will contact; wipe dry.
1.3 Place a fresh consultation card and consent form on a clean clipboard.
1.4 Greet the client warmly; offer a seat in the consultation area.
1.5 Explain that a brief health consultation will be conducted before any treatment.

### Step 2: Obtain and Review Client Profile (Rekod dan Semak Profil Klien)
2.1 Retrieve client's body consultation card (from C01).
2.2 Review existing medical history, allergies, and conditions.
2.3 Record additional thermal-specific data: heat tolerance, circulatory conditions, blood pressure, heat-affecting medications, previous thermal therapy experience.

### Step 3: Check Contraindications (Semakan Kontraindikasi)
3.1 Systematically check absolute contraindications (cardiovascular disease, thrombosis, severe hypertension, fever, skin infections, uncontrolled epilepsy, cancer, recent surgery, open wounds).
3.2 Check relative contraindications (pregnancy, diabetes, mild hypertension, varicose veins, metal implants, asthma, claustrophobia).
3.3 Record all findings.
3.4 If absolute contraindication found: inform client, decline treatment, recommend GP.

### Step 4: Determine Service Requirements (Tentukan Keperluan Perkhidmatan)
4.1 Discuss client's primary goal (detoxification, weight loss, relaxation, cellulite reduction).
4.2 Consider body condition from C01 consultation.
4.3 Match client needs with available thermal therapy methods.
4.4 Recommend appropriate thermal therapy type, frequency and duration.
4.5 Discuss cost and treatment course.

### Step 5: Obtain Informed Consent (Perolehan Persetujuan Termaklum)
5.1 Explain the selected thermal therapy procedure to the client.
5.2 Explain expected benefits and potential risks.
5.3 Present the consent form; allow client time to read.
5.4 Answer all client questions.
5.5 Obtain client signature on the consent form.
5.6 Countersign as witness.

### Step 6: Record and Update (Rekod dan Kemaskini)
6.1 Update consultation card with all thermal therapy consultation findings.
6.2 File consent form with consultation card.
6.3 Date and sign entry.
