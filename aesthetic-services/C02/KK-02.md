# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C02/KK(2/7)
**Competency Unit:** C02 — Body Thermal Therapy
**Work Activity 2:** Prepare Body Thermal Therapy Work Area
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** BLUE / BIRU

---

## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Set up the thermal therapy room/cubicle to professional standards.
2. Apply safety precautions for thermal therapy environments.
3. Test equipment functionality and confirm readiness.
4. Clean and sterilise thermal therapy equipment.

---

## Safety Precautions
1. Check all electrical cords for damage before use.
2. Ensure RCD protection is active.
3. Verify fire extinguisher (CO2) is accessible.
4. Check emergency exit door opens outward.
5. First aid kit with burn supplies accessible.

---

## Procedure

### Step 1: Room Preparation
1.1 Check ventilation — exhaust fan operational.
1.2 Check floor — clean, dry, non-slip.
1.3 Set room thermostat to comfortable ambient temperature.
1.4 Ensure shower/rinse area is clean and available.
1.5 Check emergency exit; ensure door opens freely.

### Step 2: Equipment Functionality Testing
2.1 **Sauna cabinet:** Power on; check heating element heats up; verify thermostat reads correctly; test timer; check door/latch.
2.2 **Steam cabinet:** Fill water reservoir; power on; check steam output; verify temperature gauge; test timer.
2.3 **Infrared lamp/blanket:** Power on; check heat output at low and high settings; verify timer.
2.4 **Paraffin wax heater:** Check wax level; power on; verify temperature reaches 52-54 C.
2.5 Record all test results in the equipment log book with date and signature.

### Step 3: Clean and Sterilise Equipment
3.1 Wipe interior of sauna/steam cabinet with antibacterial solution.
3.2 Disinfect seat/bench surfaces.
3.3 Place disposable liner in cabinet seat.
3.4 Wipe infrared equipment housing with disinfectant.
3.5 Wipe treatment couch and trolley.
3.6 Place fresh linen on the treatment couch.

### Step 4: Arrange Work Area
4.1 Arrange in service flow: consultation/preparation zone, thermal treatment zone, rinse/cool-down zone, post-treatment zone.
4.2 Place pre-treatment products on trolley.
4.3 Set out towels, drinking water, timer.
4.4 Ensure call bell / alert system for client is functional.

### Step 5: Final Safety Check
5.1 Verify all equipment is at correct temperature.
5.2 Check water availability (drinking water, shower).
5.3 Confirm first aid kit location.
5.4 Work area ready for client.
