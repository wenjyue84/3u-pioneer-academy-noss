# PENILAIAN PRESTASI / PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT

## Instructions to Trainee
Perform a COMPLETE body thermal therapy service on a model/volunteer, covering all 7 work activities.

| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|
| Client profile reviewed and thermal-specific data recorded | 3 |

| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|

| Equipment cleaned/sterilised | 2 |

| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|

| Positioning and towel management | 2 |
| Hair draped | 2 |

| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|

| Cleansing performed correctly | 4 |
| Deep cleansing performed correctly | 4 |

|----------|-------|
