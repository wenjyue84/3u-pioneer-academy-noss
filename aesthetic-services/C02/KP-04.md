## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Explain the purpose and methods of pre-treatment for body thermal therapy.
2. Describe cleansing and deep cleansing methods used before thermal therapy.
3. Explain the importance of following manufacturer instructions for pre-treatment products.

---

## 1.0 Purpose of Pre-Treatment (Tujuan Pra-Rawatan)

Pre-treatment before thermal therapy serves to:
- **Remove surface impurities** — Dirt, oil, dead skin cells, and cosmetic residue that block perspiration.
- **Open pores** — Prepare skin to receive heat and release toxins.
- **Improve product absorption** — Ensure thermal therapy products penetrate effectively.
- **Assess skin condition** — Final check before applying heat.

---

## 2.0 Pre-Treatment Methods (Kaedah Pra-Rawatan)

### 2.1 Determining the Pre-Treatment Method

The method selected depends on:
- Client's skin type and condition
- Type of thermal therapy to follow
- Manufacturer's product recommendations
- Client allergies (check consultation card)

### 2.2 Types of Pre-Treatment

| Method | Product | Technique |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Dry body brushing** (*memberus badan kering*) | Natural bristle brush | Brush in long strokes towards the heart; stimulates lymphatic drainage |
| **Body scrub / exfoliation** (*pengelupasan badan*) | Salt scrub, sugar scrub, enzyme scrub | Apply in circular motions; rinse off |
| **Pre-treatment oil** | Warming oil, essential oil blend | Apply with effleurage movements to prepare skin |

---

## 3.0 Cleansing and Deep Cleansing (Pembersihan dan Pembersihan Mendalam)

### 3.1 Cleansing

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Select appropriate cleansing product for client's skin type |
| 2 | Apply cleanser to the body area using long, sweeping (effleurage) movements |
| 3 | Work systematically from the extremities toward the torso |
| 4 | Remove cleanser with warm, damp sponges or mitts |
| 5 | Blot dry with a clean towel |

### 3.2 Deep Cleansing

Deep cleansing goes beyond surface cleaning to remove embedded impurities:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Apply deep cleansing product (e.g., exfoliating scrub) |
| 2 | Massage into the skin using circular movements with moderate pressure |
| 3 | Focus on areas of congestion (back, shoulders, upper arms) |
| 4 | Remove thoroughly with warm, damp sponges or mitts |
| 5 | Tone the skin with a hydrating toner or thermal water spray |
| 6 | Pat dry with a clean towel |

### 3.3 Manufacturer Instructions

All pre-treatment products must be used per manufacturer instructions:
- Correct dilution ratios
- Specified application time
- Recommended removal method
- Temperature requirements (if applicable)
- Contraindications listed by the manufacturer

---

## 4.0 Summary (Rumusan)

The pre-procedure phase prepares the client's skin for thermal therapy by removing surface impurities, opening pores, and ensuring optimal product absorption. The aesthetician must select the appropriate cleansing method based on the client's skin type and the thermal treatment to follow, always adhering to manufacturer instructions.
