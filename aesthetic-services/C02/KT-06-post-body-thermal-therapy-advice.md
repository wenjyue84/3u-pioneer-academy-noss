## Questions (Soalan)

### Q1 — Short Answer
List FIVE (5) after care advice points specific to body thermal therapy.

### Q2 — Multiple Choice
How much water should the client drink in the 24 hours following thermal therapy?
A. 500 ml B. 1 litre C. 2 litres D. 3 litres

### Q3 — True or False (with justification)
a) The client may take a hot bath immediately after thermal therapy.
b) Strenuous exercise should be avoided for 24 hours after thermal therapy.
c) Recording post-therapy advice is optional if the client verbally confirms understanding.

### Q4 — Short Answer
Explain WHY alcohol and caffeine should be avoided for 24 hours after thermal therapy.

### Q5 — Short Answer
Describe the procedure for scheduling a follow-up thermal therapy appointment, including THREE (3) steps.

---

## Answer Scheme

**A1:** Any 5 of: Drink 2+ litres of water, avoid alcohol/caffeine for 24 hours, avoid hot baths for 4-6 hours, rest for 30 minutes, avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours, eat light meals, expect increased urination, report unusual symptoms.

**A2:** C. 2 litres.

**A3:** a) FALSE — Hot baths should be avoided for 4-6 hours to allow body temperature regulation. b) TRUE. c) FALSE — All post-therapy advice must be recorded on the client card regardless of verbal confirmation.

**A4:** Alcohol and caffeine are both diuretics, which promote further dehydration. Since thermal therapy causes significant fluid loss through perspiration, consuming these substances would compound the dehydration and may cause dizziness, headaches, and other adverse effects.

**A5:** 1. Recommend follow-up based on the service plan (typically weekly). 2. Confirm date and time with the client. 3. Provide appointment card or digital confirmation and remind of cancellation policy.
