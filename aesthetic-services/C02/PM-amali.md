## Instructional Plan (Kaedah Pengajaran 4 Langkah)

### Session 1: KK(1/7) — Thermal Therapy Consultation (3 hours)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Review KP(1/7). Set up consultation area. | 15 min |
| Penyampaian | Demonstrate: thermal-specific profiling, contraindication checking, consent form completion. | 45 min |
| Penggunaan | Trainees practise in role-play pairs. Complete KK(1/7). | 90 min |
| Pengesahan | Assess using checklist. Feedback. | 30 min |

### Session 2: KK(2/7) — Work Area Preparation (3 hours)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Review KP(2/7). Tour thermal equipment. | 15 min |
| Penyampaian | Demonstrate: room setup, equipment testing, safety checks. | 45 min |
| Penggunaan | Trainees set up thermal therapy stations. Complete KK(2/7). | 90 min |
| Pengesahan | Assess each station. | 30 min |

### Session 3: KK(3/7) — Client Preparation (3 hours)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Review KP(3/7). Prepare materials. | 15 min |
| Penyampaian | Demonstrate: PPE, feet sanitisation, positioning, towel/draping techniques. | 45 min |
| Penggunaan | Trainees practise on models. Complete KK(3/7). | 90 min |
| Pengesahan | Assess draping and preparation skills. | 30 min |

### Session 4: KK(4/7) — Pre-Procedure (3 hours)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Review KP(4/7). Display pre-treatment products. | 15 min |
| Penyampaian | Demonstrate: dry brushing, cleansing, deep cleansing techniques. | 45 min |
| Penggunaan | Trainees practise on models. Complete KK(4/7). | 90 min |
| Pengesahan | Assess technique and product usage. | 30 min |

### Session 5: KK(5/7) — Thermal Therapy Service (5 hours)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Review KP(5/7). Safety briefing. | 20 min |
| Penyampaian | Demonstrate at least 2 thermal methods (e.g., steam cabinet + infrared). Show monitoring, product removal, result recording. | 80 min |
| Penggunaan | Trainees practise thermal therapy on models (rotate through methods). Complete KK(5/7). | 150 min |
| Pengesahan | Assess technique, monitoring, safety compliance. | 50 min |

### Session 6: KK(6/7) — Post-Therapy Advice (3 hours)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Review KP(6/7). | 10 min |
| Penyampaian | Demonstrate: advice delivery, recording, scheduling. | 40 min |
| Penggunaan | Role-play post-therapy advice. Complete KK(6/7). | 90 min |
| Pengesahan | Assess communication and documentation. | 40 min |

### Session 7: KK(7/7) — Work Area Upkeep (4 hours)
| Step | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Persediaan | Review KP(7/7). | 10 min |
| Penyampaian | Demonstrate: thermal equipment cleaning, paraffin disposal, inventory, waste segregation. | 60 min |
| Penggunaan | Trainees clean and upkeep stations. Complete KK(7/7). | 120 min |
| Pengesahan | Assess cleanliness, inventory accuracy, waste handling. | 50 min |

- Sauna cabinet, steam cabinet, infrared lamp/blanket, paraffin wax heater, hot stone set
- Cleansing/deep cleansing products, body brushes, scrubs
- PPE, towels, linen, disposable liners
- Consultation cards, consent forms
- Waste bins, sterilisation equipment
