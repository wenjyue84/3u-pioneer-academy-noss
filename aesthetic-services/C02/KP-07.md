# KERTAS PENERANGAN / INFORMATION SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C02/KP(7/7)
**Competency Unit:** C02 — Body Thermal Therapy / Terapi Haba Badan
**Work Activity 7:** Upkeep Body Thermal Therapy Work Area
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** WHITE / PUTIH

---

## Objectives (Objektif)

Upon completion of this information sheet, trainees shall be able to:
1. Describe sterilisation and sanitisation methods for thermal therapy equipment.
2. Explain material and product replenishment procedures.
3. Describe stock inventory management for thermal therapy supplies.
4. Explain waste disposal requirements specific to thermal therapy.

---

## 1.0 Sterilisation and Sanitisation of Thermal Equipment

| Equipment | Method |
|-----------|--------|
| Sauna/steam cabinet interior | Wipe with approved antibacterial solution; ventilate to dry |
| Cabinet seat/bench | Disinfectant spray after each client |
| Infrared blanket/lamp | Wipe housing with disinfectant; use disposable liner for blankets |
| Paraffin wax heater | Drain used wax; clean interior; refill with fresh wax |
| Hot stones | Wash with antibacterial soap; soak in disinfectant; rinse; air dry |
| Towels and linen | Launder at 60 C minimum; store in covered linen cupboard |
| Treatment couch | Disinfect surface after each client |

---

## 2.0 Materials and Product Replenishment

After each thermal therapy session, replenish:
- Disposable liners/sheets for cabinets
- Paraffin wax supply
- Pre-treatment cleansing products
- Towels and linen
- Disposable gloves and PPE
- Water for client hydration
- Sanitising products

---

## 3.0 Stock Inventory

Follow the standard inventory procedures:
- Conduct physical count of thermal therapy supplies
- Update stock cards
- Flag items at reorder level
- Apply FIFO rotation
- Check product expiry dates
- Submit inventory report

---

## 4.0 Waste Disposal

Thermal therapy generates specific waste:
- Used paraffin wax — general waste (not recyclable due to contamination)
- Wet/used linen — laundry, not waste
- Disposable liners — general waste
- Used gloves — clinical waste (yellow bag)
- Expired products — chemical waste disposal
- Empty product containers — recyclable if clean

All waste disposal per Environmental Quality Act 1974 and local authority requirements.

---

## 5.0 Summary (Rumusan)

Upkeeping the thermal therapy work area involves thorough cleaning of heat equipment, replenishing consumables (especially wax and liners), maintaining inventory, and properly disposing of thermal therapy waste. These practices ensure hygiene compliance and readiness for the next treatment session.
