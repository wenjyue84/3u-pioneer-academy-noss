## Questions (Soalan)

### Q1 — Short Answer
List FIVE (5) physiological effects of applying heat to the body.

### Q2 — Matching
Match the thermal therapy method with the correct temperature range:
| Method | | Temperature |
|--------|---|------------|
| 1. Steam cabinet | | A. 52-54 C |
| 2. Sauna cabinet | | B. 50-55 C |
| 3. Paraffin wax (body) | | C. 40-50 C |
| 4. Hot stone heater | | D. 50-70 C |

### Q3 — Multiple Choice
How frequently should the therapist check the client during thermal therapy?
A. Every 2 minutes B. Every 5 minutes C. Every 10 minutes D. Every 15 minutes

### Q4 — Short Answer
A client becomes dizzy during a steam cabinet treatment. Describe the FOUR (4) immediate steps the aesthetician must take.

### Q5 — True or False (with justification)
a) Used paraffin wax may be reheated and reused on another client to reduce waste.
b) After thermal therapy, the client should sit up slowly to avoid postural hypotension.
c) The duration for a sauna cabinet treatment is typically 45-60 minutes.
d) Hot stones should be tested on the therapist's inner wrist before applying to the client.

### Q6 — Short Answer
List FOUR (4) items that must be recorded on the client's consultation card after a thermal therapy session.

---

## Answer Scheme

**A1:** Any 5 of: Vasodilation, increased perspiration, increased metabolic rate, muscle relaxation, pain relief, adipose fat emulsification, improved lymphatic drainage, detoxification.

**A2:** 1-C, 2-D, 3-A, 4-B.

**A3:** B. Every 5 minutes.

**A4:** 1. Stop treatment immediately — open/remove from cabinet. 2. Cool the client — apply cool towels, move to ventilated area. 3. Provide water for hydration. 4. Place in recovery position if needed; call emergency services if symptoms worsen.

**A5:** a) FALSE — Used paraffin wax must never be reheated or reused on another client due to cross-contamination risk. b) TRUE — Postural hypotension is common after heat treatment due to vasodilation. c) FALSE — Sauna cabinet duration is typically 15-20 minutes, not 45-60 minutes. d) TRUE — Temperature must be tested on the therapist's inner wrist first for safety.

**A6:** Any 4 of: Type of thermal therapy performed, temperature and duration used, client's response during treatment, any adverse reactions, results observed, date and therapist signature.
