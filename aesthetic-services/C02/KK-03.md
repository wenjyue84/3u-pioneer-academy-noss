# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod / Code:** S960-002-3:2020-C02/KK(3/7)
**Competency Unit:** C02 — Body Thermal Therapy
**Work Activity 3:** Prepare Client for Body Thermal Therapy Service
**Paper Colour / Warna Kertas:** BLUE / BIRU

---

## Objectives (Objektif)
1. Demonstrate correct PPE usage for thermal therapy.
2. Prepare client materials and usher client to change.
3. Sanitise client feet and position client correctly.
4. Perform towel management and hair draping.

---

| Item | Qty |
|------|-----|
| PPE: apron, gloves, mask, closed-toe shoes | 1 set |
| Client gown / disposable wrap | 1 |
| Disposable slippers | 1 pair |
| Shower cap / hair drape | 1 |
| Antiseptic foot spray / wipes | 1 |
| Towels (large x 3, small x 2) | As listed |
| Glass of water | 1 |
| Lockable locker with key | 1 |

---

## Procedure

### Step 1: Don PPE
1.1 Put on clean apron. 1.2 Put on mask. 1.3 Ensure closed-toe shoes. 1.4 Prepare disposable gloves (put on when handling client).

### Step 2: Prepare Client Materials
2.1 Lay out client gown/wrap, slippers, shower cap.
2.2 Prepare glass of water.
2.3 Prepare towels for draping.

### Step 3: Usher Client
3.1 Guide client to designated thermal therapy area.
3.2 Show change area; provide attire with wearing instructions.
3.3 Store client belongings in lockable locker; provide key.
3.4 Wait for client to signal readiness.

### Step 4: Sanitise Feet
4.1 Place clean towel on floor. 4.2 Spray client's feet with antiseptic spray. 4.3 Provide clean slippers.

### Step 5: Position Client
5.1 For couch-based treatment: assist client onto couch; ensure comfort.
5.2 For cabinet-based treatment: assist client into cabinet; seat comfortably.

### Step 6: Towel Management
6.1 For couch: full body drape with large towel; expose only treatment area.
6.2 For cabinet: place towel on seat; wrap towel around neck to seal opening.
6.3 Ensure modesty throughout.

### Step 7: Hair Draping
7.1 Gather client's hair. 7.2 Secure with clip/band. 7.3 Place shower cap or head towel.
