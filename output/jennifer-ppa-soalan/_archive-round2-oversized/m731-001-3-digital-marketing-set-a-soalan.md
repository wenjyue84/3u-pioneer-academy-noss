**AROMA LESTARI SDN. BHD.** *(rekaan)* is a homegrown Malaysian home-fragrance and natural home-care brand producing reed diffusers, soy candles and **refill pouches**. The company sells through its own website, one e-commerce marketplace storefront and its social media channels.

The Marketing Manager, **Puan Nurul Hidayah binti Zainal** *(rekaan)*, has approved a **14-day campaign** with a total allocated budget of **RM 1,500.00**, split per channel (see the per-channel allocation table in Appendix 3 — search engine marketing carries **RM 1,200.00** of that total), to launch the new *Kopi Tenom* soy candle and to push the refill line, which supports the company's low-waste and reduced-packaging positioning.

You are required to execute the digital marketing activities for this campaign across all six channels, using only the company, product, campaign, audience, order and performance data supplied in Appendix 1 to Appendix 7, and to record your work in Appendix 8 to Appendix 21.

## E. TUGASAN TERPERINCI

### TUGASAN 1 — C01 Execute social media marketing activities. (35 minit)

Rujuk Appendix 1, 2, 3, 4 (Part A) dan 5. Lengkapkan Appendix 8, 9 dan 10.

1. **Create the social media business account** on the sandbox platform provided. Obtain and record the business account credentials for all mandatory fields (username, password reference, business profile, billing information). Complete the account information — business category, business name, contact number, email, business description, banner and profile picture — using Appendix 1 and Appendix 2. Set the user role and the automated response. Publish or schedule the publication of the account and record the scheduled date and time. *(Appendix 8, Part A)*
2. **Carry out the social media marketing campaign.** Transfer the campaign objective, type of campaign, **the social-media channel allocation** from the Appendix 3 allocation table, campaign duration and expected campaign result from Appendix 3 into the campaign setup block. State the content requirements, the billing method, the campaign structure and the conversion tracking method. *(Appendix 8, Part B)*
3. **Produce one (1) publishable social media post.** Identify three (3) hashtags — one brand-specific, one content, one trending. Select the placement and state the selection criterion applied. Prepare the copywriting caption in adherence to the social media content policy. Prepare the creative media brief (format, source, copyright status). Publish or schedule the post and evidence the scheduled date/time with a screenshot. *(Appendix 9, Part A)*
4. **Moderate the social media posts.** Reply to **all five (5)** audience comments listed in Appendix 5. Identify the type of assistance required for each and write the reply. *(Appendix 9, Part B)*
5. **Monitor social media marketing performance.** Using Appendix 4 Part A, compute the pageview, follower growth, post reach, post engagement rate and average responsive time. Carry out and state **two (2)** optimisation methods. Compile the performance report in the required format and submit it to Puan Nurul Hidayah. *(Appendix 10)*

### TUGASAN 2 — C02 Execute search engine optimisation activities. (28 minit)

Rujuk Appendix 1, 2, 3 dan 4 (Part B). Lengkapkan Appendix 11 dan 12.

1. **Build on-page search engine optimisation** for the refill-line landing page. Perform target keyword research using the keyword tool provided and record **five (5)** keywords with their search volume and difficulty. Identify the on-page SEO essentials. Produce an on-page SEO article introduction of **150–200 words** relevant to the target keyword. Insert the SEO essentials — website structure, title tag, meta description, permalink/URL, header tags (H1, H2, H3) and image alt text — completing all mandatory fields. Publish or schedule the page on the sandbox CMS provided by the centre (Section G item 12) and record the scheduled date/time. *(Appendix 11)*
2. **Build off-page search engine optimisation.** Perform off-page components research and list **three (3)** link-building targets with the reason for each. Prepare one (1) guest-blogging outline. Produce the off-page copy snippet, identify and insert the off-page components, and schedule the publication. *(Appendix 12, Part A)*
3. **Monitor search engine optimisation ranking performance.** Using Appendix 4 Part B, compute the number of visits, click-through rate, keyword ranking movement and the competitor comparison. Propose the optimisation methods to be carried out. Compile the report using the report metrics required (volume of traffic per day / week / month and SEO ranking) and submit it. *(Appendix 12, Part B)*

### TUGASAN 3 — C03 Execute search engine marketing activities. (28 minit)

Rujuk Appendix 1, 2, 3 dan 4 (Part C). Lengkapkan Appendix 13, 14 dan 15.

1. **Create the search engine marketing account** on the sandbox tool. Obtain the business account credentials for all mandatory fields, complete the account information including **email and destination URL**, acknowledge the platform policy, and publish or schedule the account. *(Appendix 13, Part A)*
2. **Carry out the search engine marketing campaign.** Identify the campaign requirements from Appendix 3 — objectives, type of campaign, **the search-engine-marketing channel allocation** from the Appendix 3 allocation table, duration and expected results — and identify the content requirements. Set up the campaign: billing, campaign structure and conversion tracking. *(Appendix 13, Part B)*
3. **Produce the search engine marketing content.** Identify the target keywords using the keyword tool. Specify the placement (**Search network** or **Display network**) and state the selection criterion applied. Prepare **three (3) headlines and two (2) descriptions**. Prepare the creative media brief. **Link the call to action to the target destination URL.** Publish or schedule the ad and record the scheduled date/time. *(Appendix 14)*
4. **Monitor search engine marketing performance.** Using Appendix 4 Part C, compute the click-through rate, conversion rate, impressions, average cost per click, cost per mille, total number of clicks and cost per acquisition. Carry out and state the optimisation strategies applied. Compile and submit the performance report. *(Appendix 15)*

### TUGASAN 4 — C04 Execute e-commerce marketing activities. (35 minit)

Rujuk Appendix 1, 2, 3, 4 (Part D) dan 6. Lengkapkan Appendix 16, 17, 18 dan 19.

1. **Create the e-commerce seller account.** Obtain the credentials for all mandatory fields, including **billing and shipping information**. Complete the account information — business category, business name, contact number, email, business description, banner, profile picture — set the user role and the automated response, and publish or schedule the account. *(Appendix 16, Part A)*
2. **Carry out the e-commerce marketing campaign.** Identify the campaign requirements from Appendix 3 and the content requirements. Set up the campaign: billing, **shipping**, campaign structure and conversion tracking. *(Appendix 16, Part B)*
3. **Produce the e-commerce marketing content.** Key in the full product information for **all three (3) SKUs** — category, description, shipping and product price. Prepare the product content (images, copywriting, video plan). Prepare the creative media brief. Publish or schedule the product listing. *(Appendix 17)*
4. **Moderate the e-commerce store management.** Process **all five (5)** orders in Appendix 6 through the correct e-commerce fulfilment state. Update the store information. Update the stock inventory table (stock keeping unit, stock level, safety stock, stock forecast). Compile and submit the store management report using the required report content. *(Appendix 18)*
5. **Monitor e-commerce marketing performance.** Using Appendix 4 Part D, compute the pageview, total orders, conversion rate, number of visits, shop rating and total sales. Carry out and state the optimisation strategies. Compile and submit the performance report. *(Appendix 19)*

### TUGASAN 5 — C05 Execute mobile marketing activities. (12 minit)

Rujuk Appendix 1, 2, 3 dan 4 (Part E). Lengkapkan Appendix 20 (Parts A–D).

1. **Create the mobile marketing account** on the sandbox broadcast tool — username/phone number, password reference, business profile, billing information, business category, business name, contact number, email, business description; set the user role and the automated response; publish or schedule the account. *(Part A)*
2. **Carry out the mobile marketing campaign.** Identify the campaign requirements including the **type of mobile channel preference** (WhatsApp blaster / SMS / in-app), **the mobile channel allocation** from the Appendix 3 allocation table and the campaign duration. Identify the content requirements. Set up the campaign: billing, structure and conversion tracking. *(Part B)*
3. **Produce the mobile marketing content.** Prepare one (1) broadcast message of **not more than 160 characters** (SMS-safe) together with the creative media brief. Schedule the send and record the scheduled date/time. *(Part C)*
4. **Monitor mobile marketing performance.** Using Appendix 4 Part E, compute the reach, open rate, response rate and conversion rate. State **two (2)** optimisation methods. Compile and submit the report. *(Part D)*

### TUGASAN 6 — C06 Execute email marketing activities. (12 minit)

Rujuk Appendix 1, 2, 3, 4 (Part F) dan 7. Lengkapkan Appendix 21 (Parts A–D).

1. **Create the email marketing account.** Obtain the credentials for all mandatory fields, complete the account setup and acknowledge the email marketing platform policy. Publish or schedule the account. *(Part A)*
2. **Carry out the email marketing campaign.** Identify the campaign requirements from Appendix 3. **Select the audience email list** from the three (3) segments supplied in Appendix 7 and justify the selection. Identify the content requirements and set up the campaign (billing and campaign structure). *(Part B)*
3. **Produce the email marketing content.** Set up the email template on the sandbox tool. Prepare the subject line, preheader, body copy and the call-to-action link. Prepare the creative media brief. Schedule the send and record the scheduled date/time. *(Part C)*
4. **Monitor email marketing performance.** Using Appendix 4 Part F, compute the total number of emails sent, open rate, click-through rate, bounce rate and unsubscribe rate. Recommend the optimisation method. Compile and submit the report. *(Part D)*

**1) Perkara Kritikal**

The candidate uses the company, product, campaign, order and performance data provided in Appendix 1 to Appendix 7 **without falsifying or materially altering** the source information, and performs all account, listing and publishing actions **only on the sandbox/training accounts provided** — never on live commercial accounts and never incurring real advertising spend.

**2) Proses Kerja**

Assessed across all six competency units according to NOSS Competency Weightage: C01 25, C02 20, C03 20, C04 25, C05 5, C06 5 marks.

**3) Hasil Kerja**

a) Social media post, five moderation replies and performance report are complete and accurate (C01).
b) On-page SEO page with all essentials inserted, off-page plan and ranking report are complete (C02).
c) Complete ad set with the call to action linked to the destination, and the performance report (C03).
d) Published three-SKU product listing, five processed orders, store and performance reports (C04).
e) Scheduled broadcast message and performance summary (C05).
f) Scheduled email with template and call to action, and performance summary (C06).

**4) Sikap / Keselamatan dan Alam Sekitar**

a) Candidate attends and starts the assessment at the specified time.
b) Work plan is carried out according to the specified instructions.
c) Business data confidentiality is maintained (credentials, customer data, email list).
d) Regulatory and platform requirements are complied with.
e) Tasks are completed with minimum supervision.
f) Information is analysed logically and work issues are addressed rationally.
g) Creative and alternative thinking is applied in content tasks.
h) Workplace health and safety requirements are complied with.
i) Good ergonomics are practised at the workstation.
j) Clean and green workplace practices are applied; files, equipment and work area are left organised.

**5) Soal Jawab**

Twenty (20) oral questions, two (2) marks each, distributed proportionally to CU weightage: C01 5, C02 4, C03 4, C04 5, C05 1, C06 1. The oral questions are **supplementary** to the practical tasks and never a substitute for them.
