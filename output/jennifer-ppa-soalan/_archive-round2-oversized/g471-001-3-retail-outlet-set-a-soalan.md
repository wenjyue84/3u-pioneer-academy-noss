**Task:**

a) **Carry out stock receiving.** Physically check the **quantity and quality** of every line item on both Delivery Orders and the matching Invoice against the goods delivered. Record the checked quantity and the quality condition of each line item in **Appendix 3A**.

h) **Produce back end reports.** Update all back end documents and complete the **Back End Report (Appendix 3H)** summarising receiving, stock take, ordering, return and disposal for 12 October 2026. Present the completed report to the assessor.

## H. KRITERIA PENILAIAN

Your performance will be assessed based on the following criteria:

**1) Perkara Kritikal**

a) Calon **tidak boleh mengubah, memalsukan atau menambah** data stok, invois, transaksi POS atau maklumat pelanggan yang dibekalkan. Semua dokumen mesti diisi berdasarkan data sumber dalam Appendix 2.
b) Pemisahan **produk Halal dan bukan Halal** semasa penerimaan dan penyimpanan stok mesti dilaksanakan.

> **PERINGATAN:**
> Sekiranya perkara kritikal ini tidak dilaksanakan maka calon dianggap tidak terampil bagi keseluruhan penilaian.

**2) Proses Kerja (60%)**

The work process is assessed from stock receiving, back end stock take, stock ordering, product return and disposal; through product merchandising, price display and change, selling floor stock count; front end inspection, customer handling, front end customer services (redemption and free gifts, utilities billing, loyalty programme, special-needs customer), cashiering and reconciliation; staff scheduling, briefing, coaching and performance review; and equipment, utilities, hygiene, housekeeping, security and safety monitoring.

**3) Hasil Kerja (10%)**

a) Back End Report is complete, accurate and usable.
b) Sales Floor Operations Report is complete, accurate and usable.
c) Front End Services Report and transaction reconciliation are complete, accurate and usable.
d) Staff Work Schedule and Staff Coaching and Performance Report are complete, accurate and usable.
e) Hygiene checklist, Housekeeping Verification Record and Loss and Prevention Report are complete, accurate and usable.

**4) Sikap / Keselamatan dan Alam Sekitar (10%)**

a) Calon hadir pada masa yang ditetapkan.
b) Maklumat stok, jualan dan pelanggan dikendalikan dengan jujur tanpa mengubah data sumber.
c) Peralatan kedai, POS dan alat pengendalian stok digunakan dengan betul dan selamat.
d) Amalan kebersihan diri dan keselamatan kerja dipatuhi sepanjang penilaian.
e) Dokumen, stok, peralatan dan kawasan kerja dikemas dan disusun semula selepas tamat.

**5) Soal Jawab (20%)**

Dua belas (12) soalan lisan meliputi kelima-lima Unit Kompetensi. Jawapan direkodkan oleh Pemeriksa dalam **ASSESSMENT ANSWER SHEET — BAHAGIAN D (Rekod Jawapan Soal Jawab Lisan)**.
