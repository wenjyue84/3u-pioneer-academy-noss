## C. TUGASAN

> **SYARIKAT REKAAN — FICTIONAL COMPANY.** Semua nama syarikat, individu, produk, URL dan angka
> dalam kertas ini adalah rekaan semata-mata untuk tujuan penilaian.

**SENARIO:** **AROMA LESTARI SDN. BHD.** *(rekaan)* is a Malaysian home-fragrance brand selling
reed diffusers, soy candles and refill pouches through its own online channels. The company is
launching the new *Kopi Tenom* soy candle in a **14-day campaign (1–14 September 2026)** with a
total budget of **RM1,500.00**, allocated per channel in Appendix 2. You are the **Digital
Marketing Assistant**, reporting to the Marketing Manager, **Puan Nurul Hidayah binti Zainal**
*(rekaan)*. All performance reports are submitted to her.

1. Calon dikehendaki melaksanakan aktiviti pemasaran digital bagi AROMA LESTARI SDN. BHD.
   berdasarkan maklumat dan data yang disediakan dalam Appendix 1 hingga Appendix 4, dan
   melengkapkan **Borang A hingga Borang E**. (Jawapan bertulis tangan atau bentuk digital
   diterima.)

2. Tugasan (masa dicadangkan):

   **TUGASAN 1 (C01) — Social Media Marketing — Borang A (32 minit)**

   I. Identify **three (3)** hashtags for the campaign — one brand-specific, one content and one
      trending hashtag.
   II. Select the social media placement and state the selection criterion applied.
   III. Prepare **one (1)** post caption for the *Kopi Tenom* launch and complete the creative
      media brief using the assets listed in Appendix 1.
   IV. Moderate the **three (3)** audience comments in Appendix 4 — state the type of assistance
      for each comment and write your reply.
   V. Using Appendix 3 Part A, compute the social media performance indicators, state **two (2)**
      optimisation methods and complete the report block.

   **TUGASAN 2 (C02) — Search Engine Optimisation — Borang B (24 minit)**

   I. Perform target keyword research — state **three (3)** target keywords for the campaign.
   II. Prepare the on-page search engine optimisation essentials — title tag, meta description,
      permalink/URL, header tags (H1, H2) and image optimisation.
   III. Produce one on-page SEO copy of **100–150 words** relevant to the target keywords.
   IV. Using Appendix 3 Part B, compute the SEO performance indicators and complete the report
      metrics.

   **TUGASAN 3 (C03) — Search Engine Marketing — Borang C (24 minit)**

   I. Transfer the campaign objective, allocated SEM budget and campaign duration from Appendix 2.
   II. Specify the search engine placement and state the selection criterion applied.
   III. Prepare **two (2)** ad headlines and **one (1)** description, and link the call to action
      to the destination URL given in Appendix 2.
   IV. Using Appendix 3 Part C, compute the SEM performance indicators and state **one (1)**
      optimisation method.

   **TUGASAN 4 (C04) — E-Commerce Marketing — Borang D (32 minit)**

   I. Key in the product information for the **two (2)** SKUs in Appendix 1 — category,
      description, shipping and product price.
   II. Process the **three (3)** customer orders in Appendix 4 — state the correct fulfilment
      state for each order.
   III. Monitor the stock inventory in Appendix 4 — state the stock level, safety stock and stock
      availability status for each SKU.
   IV. List **five (5)** contents of a store management report.
   V. Using Appendix 3 Part D, compute the e-commerce performance indicators.

   **TUGASAN 5 (C05) — Mobile Marketing — Borang E Bahagian 1 (12 minit)**

   I. Prepare **one (1)** broadcast message of not more than 160 characters and state the mobile
      channel preference selected.
   II. Using Appendix 3 Part E, compute the mobile marketing performance indicators and state
      **one (1)** optimisation method.

   **TUGASAN 6 (C06) — Email Marketing — Borang E Bahagian 2 (12 minit)**

   I. Select **one (1)** audience email segment from Appendix 2 and state the reason.
   II. Prepare the subject line, email body and call-to-action for **one (1)** marketing email.
   III. Using Appendix 3 Part F, compute the email marketing performance indicators and state
      **one (1)** optimisation method.

All tasks shall be carried out systematically and accurately using the documents, equipment and
digital tools provided.
