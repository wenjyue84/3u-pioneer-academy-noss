## F. KRITERIA PENILAIAN

Your performance will be assessed based on the following criteria:

**1) Perkara Kritikal**

The candidate uses the market, product and sales information provided without falsifying or materially altering the source information or data.

**2) Proses Kerja**

The work process is assessed from survey planning and data analysis through online promotional planning, preparation of online sales collateral, KPI review, sales activity effectiveness evaluation, identification of sales best practices and recommended improvement actions.

**3) Hasil Kerja**

a) Market Survey Analysis and Summary are complete and accurate.
b) Online Sales Collateral and FAQ meet the required sales information.
c) Self Sales Performance Assessment Report is complete, accurate and usable.

**4) Sikap / Keselamatan dan Alam Sekitar**

a) Candidate attends the assessment at the specified time.
b) Information is collected, processed and applied according to task requirements.
c) Computer and digital equipment are handled correctly and safely.
d) Company, customer and sales information is handled responsibly and systematically.
e) Digital files, documents, equipment and work area are properly organised upon completion.
