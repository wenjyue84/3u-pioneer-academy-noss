**B. STORE SOP / GUIDELINE (EXTRACT)**

2. Expired or damaged items must be removed from sale immediately, separated and stored in the designated return area. The supplier collects return items every Thursday. A Product Return Note must be completed and signed for every return.
3. Item exchange is allowed within three (3) days of purchase with the original receipt, item unused and packaging intact. Refund is allowed for defective or spoiled products with the original receipt; cash purchases are refunded in cash.
4. All stock variances, cash shortages and safety findings must be recorded and reported to the Store Manager.
5. Emergency exits must be kept clear at all times; a warning sign must be placed immediately on any wet floor; waste at the back-lane waste area must be disposed of daily.
6. Staff appraisal: attendance and punctuality are verified against the attendance record; appearance, knowledge and skill are evaluated based on supervisor observation; the result and recommendation are recorded in the appraisal record.
