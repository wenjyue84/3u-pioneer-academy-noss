## Arahan kepada Calon / Instructions to Candidates

1. Penilaian prestasi ini menilai kemahiran praktikal anda dalam semua EMPAT (4) Aktiviti Kerja CU-C01. / This performance assessment evaluates your practical skills in all FOUR (4) Work Activities of CU-C01.
2. Masa penilaian: **4 jam / Assessment time: 4 hours** (termasuk rehat 30 minit / including 30-minute break).
3. Anda akan diberikan satu taklimat projek multimedia oleh penilai. Semua tugasan mestilah berdasarkan taklimat tersebut. / You will be given a multimedia project brief by the assessor. All tasks must be based on that brief.
4. Semua hasil kerja hendaklah diserahkan kepada penilai dalam format yang ditetapkan pada akhir sesi. / All work outputs must be submitted to the assessor in the specified format at the end of the session.
5. Penilai akan memerhati dan menilai proses kerja anda sepanjang sesi. / The assessor will observe and evaluate your work process throughout the session.
6. Calon **TIDAK** dibenarkan meminta bantuan daripada calon lain atau menggunakan bahan rujukan yang tidak diluluskan. / Candidates are **NOT** permitted to seek assistance from other candidates or use unapproved reference materials.

## Tugasan Penilaian / Assessment Tasks

### TUGASAN 1: Analisis Taklimat Projek / Project Brief Analysis
*(60 minit / 60 minutes)*

Calon dikehendaki menganalisis taklimat projek yang diberikan menggunakan kaedah 5W1H dan MoSCoW, serta menghasilkan dokumen keperluan projek yang lengkap. / Candidates are required to analyse the given project brief using the 5W1H and MoSCoW method, and produce a complete project requirements document.

**Hasil Kerja Dikehendaki / Required Outputs:**
- Borang analisis 5W1H yang dilengkapi / Completed 5W1H analysis form
- Senarai keperluan MoSCoW / MoSCoW requirements list
- Dokumen keperluan projek (minimum 1 halaman) / Project requirements document (minimum 1 page)

### TUGASAN 2: Pembangunan Konsep dan Skrip / Concept and Script Development
*(60 minit / 60 minutes)*

Calon dikehendaki membangunkan konsep kreatif (mood board ringkas) dan skrip multimedia berdasarkan analisis taklimat. / Candidates are required to develop a creative concept (brief mood board) and multimedia script based on the brief analysis.

**Hasil Kerja Dikehendaki / Required Outputs:**
- Peta minda konsep kreatif / Creative concept mind map
- Mood board digital (minimum 6 elemen) / Digital mood board (minimum 6 elements)
- Skrip multimedia lengkap / Complete multimedia script

### TUGASAN 3: Penghasilan Papan Cerita / Storyboard Production
*(60 minit / 60 minutes)*

Calon dikehendaki menghasilkan papan cerita multimedia berdasarkan skrip yang telah ditulis. / Candidates are required to produce a multimedia storyboard based on the script written.

**Hasil Kerja Dikehendaki / Required Outputs:**
- Papan cerita digital yang lengkap (minimum 8 panel) / Complete digital storyboard (minimum 8 panels)
- Semua panel dilengkapi anotasi penuh / All panels completed with full annotations

### TUGASAN 4: Penyediaan Sumber Pengeluaran / Production Resource Preparation
*(30 minit / 30 minutes — dilakukan serentak dengan Tugasan 1-3 / performed concurrently with Tasks 1-3)*

Calon dikehendaki menyediakan persekitaran kerja, struktur folder projek dan mengumpul minimum 5 aset berlesenkan. / Candidates are required to set up the work environment, project folder structure and collect a minimum of 5 licensed assets.

**Hasil Kerja Dikehendaki / Required Outputs:**
- Bukti struktur folder projek (screenshot) / Project folder structure evidence (screenshot)
- Log aset digital (minimum 5 aset dengan maklumat lesen) / Digital asset log (minimum 5 assets with licence information)
- Senarai semak sumber projek / Project resource checklist
