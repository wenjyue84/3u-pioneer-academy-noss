# Standard Practice

## National Occupational Skills Standard (NOSS) for Computer System Administration Level 4

**Code:** IT-020-4:2013

---

## 1. Introduction

The ever-changing world fuelled largely by the continuing advancement in computer technology requires organizations to be well-equipped to remain current and relevant. Businesses from multi-national corporations to home-based enterprises have to adopt current technologies to be competitive. Government agencies and institutions too, need to stay at the top of the game in order to provide fast, reliable and accurate services to the people. This climate creates job opportunities in computer system administration job area personnel to support the industry growth.

The computer system administration job area personnel normally known as **IT Executive** by most of the organisations. This IT Executive is the middle management level who supervises and assigns job order to computer technician. As part of a technical team, and under the direction of the computer system and IT manager, the IT Executive operates and maintains desktop and laptop computers with special focus on customer and their business needs.

Under the direction of IT manager, they need to perform various system administration activities such as:

- Operating system administration
- Evaluating, testing and installation of applications
- Performing or assisting with routine system maintenance
- Producing and maintaining current system configurations to maintain efficient day-to-day operation

In addition, the IT Executive creates and maintains records and inventories.

Job prospect for this job area personnel is expected to grow in this decade. Through Economic Transformation Program (ETP) initiated by the government and rapid development of information technology in the workplace will continue to drive demand for more IT Executive in this country. To remain competitive, firms will continue to install sophisticated computer system with latest technology, making the outlook for IT Executive remains strong.

The IT Executive must possess strong interpersonal, communication, and leadership skills because they are required to interact not only with staff members, but also with other people inside and outside their organizations. They must possess teamwork skills to work on group projects and other collaborative efforts. The IT Executive may advance to progressively higher positions, IT Manager in the information technology department provided that they prepare themselves in managerial skills, leadership skill and business functions skills.

### Pre-requisite

The candidate must complete any of the following as a pre-requisite to pursue this course:

- Computer System Operation Level 3 certificate, or
- Computer Network Services Level 3 certificate with additional two (2) competency units (Computer Set-up and Computer System Maintenance), or
- IT-080-3 Information Systems Administrative Supervisor, or
- IT-020-3 Computer System Technician, or
- IT-030-3 Computer Network Technician

## 3. Description of Competency Level

The NOSS is developed for various occupational areas. Candidates for certification must be assessed and trained at certain levels to substantiate competencies. Below is a guideline of each NOSS Level as defined by the Department of Skills Development, Ministry of Human Resources, Malaysia.

| Level | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** | SKM (Operation/Production) | Routine and predictable work activities. |
| **L2** | SKM (Operation/Production) | Varied work in multiple contexts; some non-routine, requiring individual responsibility. |
| **L3** | SKM (Supervisory) | Complex, non-routine work; considerable autonomy; control/guidance of others often required. |
| **L4** | DKM (Executive) | Complex technical/professional work; substantial personal responsibility; resource allocation and supervision of others. |
| **L5** | DLKM (Managerial) | Fundamental principles and complex techniques across unpredictable contexts; significant responsibility for others and substantial resources; accountable for analysis, diagnosis, planning, execution, and evaluation. |

## 4. Malaysian Skill Certification

Candidates who have attended and successfully completed each competency shall be awarded Certificate of Attendance on that competency. Those who have attended and successfully completed all the core competencies and fulfilled Malaysian Skill certification requirements shall receive **Diploma Kemahiran Malaysia (Malaysia Skills Diploma)** in Computer System Administration at Level 4.

## 5. Job Competencies

IT Executive is competent in performing the following **core competencies**:

1. Server Configuration
2. Computer System Security Control
3. Computer System & Network Procurement
4. Network Cabling Management
5. Computer Network Installation Management
6. Computer System Maintenance Management

---

## 6. Working Condition

IT Executive generally works in offices, computer rooms and at their clients' workplaces. The job requires them to constantly move around, either within the office or to other business premises. They have to supervise computer system technician who act as a support staff.

In bigger organisations, IT Executive is expected to work in shifts or be on call and may have to work extra hours to finish their job. The IT Executive generally works 40 hours a week at normal office hours, but depending on the nature of the business, they may have to work beyond the normal hours including on weekends.

They need to keep up to date with the ever-changing specific software and operating systems and hardware related technology. Further, they must be able to understand new terminologies and grasp new technologies fast especially mobile devices and mobile technology. In addition, the IT Executive must also work well with people and should be able to communicate effectively.

## 7. Employment Prospect

IT Executives are employed by a range of organisations including:

- Government departments and agencies
- Institutions of higher learning
- Private corporations
- Banks
- Private companies that provide computer, database and network services to clients
- Telecommunication companies
- Many other private organisations

A growing number of computer system administration diploma holders are self-employed, working independently as contractors or consultants.

## 8. Training, Industrial/Professional Recognition, Other Qualifications and Advancement

### 8.1 Industrial Recognition / Professional Qualification

Computer system administration diploma holders have strong foundation in both areas, computer system and network. There are a few professional certifications that recognise IT personnel such as:

- The Computing Technology Industry Association (CompTIA)
- Cisco Networking Academy
- Linux Professional Institute
- SANS Institute
- EC-Council

### 8.2 Other Prominent Qualifications Recognised (Malaysia or International)

Technologies change rapidly and it is an essential part of the IT Executive to remain up to date with any development in hardware and software, as well as being aware of previous versions that may still be in operation. Among professional technical training that can enhance the IT Executive skill in performing their daily tasks are listed below. These training may prepare them to be an IT manager.

| Certification | Description |
|---|---|
| **MCTS** (Microsoft Certified Technology Specialist) | Proficiency with specific Microsoft technologies. |
| **VCP** (VMware Certified Professional) | VMware virtual environment implementation and maintenance. |
| **CSSA** (Certified SonicWALL Security Administrator) | Security administration. |
| **CISSP** (Certified Information Systems Security Professional) | Information security. |
| **ACSP** (Apple Certified Support Professional) | Apple platform support. |
| **PMP** (Project Management Professional) | Project planning, budgeting, and delivery. |

### 8.3 Types of Occupation for Career Advancement

Career advancement for computer system administration level 4 personnel locally and internationally is enormous. Among them are:

- IT Manager
- Computer Specialist
- Computer Technologies
- IT Sales Professional
- Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

### 8.4 Related Industries

Computer system personnel are employed in every sector of the economy, private and public sector.
