## Tugasan / Tasks

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (10 marks)

*Circle the letter of the best answer.*

**A1.** What is the PRIMARY purpose of a computer repair job order?

- (a) To record the technician's overtime hours
- (b) To formally document the fault reported, scope of repair, and authorisation to proceed
- (c) To list the prices of spare parts
- (d) To schedule the user's training programme

**A2.** Before accepting a repair job order, a technician MUST first:

- (a) Disassemble the computer immediately
- (b) Verify the completeness and accuracy of information in the job order
- (c) Order all replacement parts from the supplier
- (d) Transfer the unit to the workshop without reading the job order

**A3.** A change request differs from an initial repair job order because it:

- (a) Does not require a technician's signature
- (b) Documents a modification to the previously agreed scope of repair
- (c) Only applies to software faults
- (d) Is issued by the supplier, not the user

**A4.** Which of the following information is ESSENTIAL in a repair job order?

- (a) Technician's personal vehicle registration number
- (b) Asset tag, reported fault description, and user contact details
- (c) The brand of the technician's toolkit
- (d) The user's lunch preference

**A5.** When a repair job order contains incomplete fault information, the technician should:

- (a) Proceed with repair based on assumption
- (b) Discard the job order and wait for a new one
- (c) Clarify missing details with the requestor before proceeding
- (d) Replace all components as a precaution

**B1.** List FIVE (5) mandatory fields that must be present in a computer repair job order. *(5 marks)*

**B2.** Explain THREE (3) criteria a technician uses when assessing whether a repair job order is valid and ready to be accepted. *(6 marks)*

**B3.** Describe TWO (2) situations in which a change request would be raised during a repair job, and state the correct action to take for each. *(4 marks)*

### Bahagian C: Situasi / Situational (15 marks)

**C1.** You are a technician at the ICT Helpdesk. You receive the following repair job order:

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Asset Tag | PC-ACC-047 |
| User | Puan Rosnah bt. Kamarudin, Accounts Department |
| Reported Fault | "The computer is very slow and keeps freezing, especially when opening Excel files with large datasets." |
| Additional Notes | Unit is still under 3-year warranty (expires 31 December 2026) |
| Urgency | High — monthly financial report due in 2 days |
| Authorised By | En. Hafizuddin (Head of Department) |

Based on the job order above:

(i) State whether this job order is complete and valid. Justify your answer with reference to at least THREE (3) fields. *(5 marks)*

(ii) Describe the steps you would take to assess and classify the reported fault before scheduling the repair. *(5 marks)*

(iii) The user calls you after submitting the job order and requests that you also upgrade the RAM from 8 GB to 16 GB. Explain how you would handle this additional request according to proper change request procedures. *(5 marks)*
