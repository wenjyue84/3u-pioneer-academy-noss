## Objektif / Objectives

Upon completion of this activity, the trainee will be able to:
1. Receive and interpret a computer repair job order or change request.
2. Identify the reported fault symptoms and affected hardware or software components.
3. Verify customer information, warranty status, and service-level requirements.
4. Determine the scope of repair and escalation path where applicable.
5. Document the assessment findings on a job order form in accordance with organisational procedures.

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Handle all customer documents and records with confidentiality; do not share customer data with unauthorised personnel.
- Verify identity before accessing any customer system or account record.
- Do not modify any live system or customer data at the assessment stage.
- Record all findings accurately — incomplete or falsified job orders can lead to incorrect repairs and safety hazards.

---

## Prosedur / Procedure

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Receive the simulated job order form from the instructor. Read all fields thoroughly: customer name, contact, asset tag, reported symptoms, and requested action. |
| 2 | Confirm the customer details against the simulated asset register. Verify that the asset tag and model number on the job order match the register entry. |
| 3 | Check the warranty status reference sheet for the unit. Record whether the unit is (a) under active manufacturer warranty, (b) under extended service contract, or (c) out of warranty. Note any warranty voiding conditions (e.g. tampered seals). |
| 4 | Analyse the reported fault symptoms. Using the fault symptom reference chart, classify each symptom into one of the following categories: (a) hardware fault, (b) software / OS fault, (c) peripheral / connectivity fault, or (d) user-operation issue. |
| 5 | Identify the likely affected component(s) based on the symptom classification. For each affected component, note whether specialist tools or spare parts will be required. |
| 6 | Determine the repair scope: (a) can the fault be resolved in-house, or (b) does it require escalation to the manufacturer or a specialist centre? Document the justification for your decision. |
| 7 | Assess the urgency and service-level requirement stated on the job order (e.g. standard 3-day turnaround vs. priority next-day). Note any customer-stated constraints (e.g. data must be preserved, unit must not be wiped). |
| 8 | Complete the job order assessment form with all findings: confirmed asset details, warranty status, fault classification, affected components, repair scope, escalation decision, estimated repair time, and any special handling notes. |
| 9 | Sign and date the completed assessment form. Submit to the instructor for review and approval before proceeding to any repair action. |

---

## Hasil Dijangka / Expected Outcome

- A fully completed job order assessment form containing:
  - Verified customer and asset details
  - Warranty status clearly stated
  - Fault symptoms classified by category
  - Affected components identified
  - Repair scope and escalation decision documented
  - Service-level and special-handling requirements noted
  - Trainee signature and date
