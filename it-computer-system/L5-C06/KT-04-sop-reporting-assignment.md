## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 4 marks each)

**A1.** Which of the following SOP programme report types is produced ONCE, at the conclusion of the entire programme, and serves as the permanent record?

- (a) Progress Report (Laporan Kemajuan)
- (b) Training Completion Report (Laporan Latihan)
- (c) Completion / Closure Report (Laporan Penutupan)
- (d) Monthly Compliance Monitoring Report

**A2.** An Executive Summary in a management report should be:

- (a) A full reproduction of the findings section with all supporting data
- (b) No longer than one page, written in plain language, summarising key findings and recommendations
- (c) Written only for the internal audit team
- (d) Produced only if the programme achieved all its targets

**A3.** A retired SOP document (one that has been withdrawn from operational use) must be:

- (a) Permanently deleted from all systems immediately
- (b) Marked OBSOLETE and archived according to the records retention schedule — not deleted
- (c) Kept in circulation as a reference document
- (d) Transferred to the National Library

**A4.** The minimum records retention period for approved SOP documents after retirement, as stated in the course material, is:

- (a) 1 year
- (b) 3 years
- (c) 5 years
- (d) 7 years

**A5.** In a Lessons Learned Register, the "Recommendation" field should:

- (a) Assign blame to the staff member responsible for the failure
- (b) State a specific, actionable improvement for future SOP programmes, with an owner and target
- (c) Repeat the observation in different words
- (d) Be left blank if the lesson learned was a positive one

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (50 marks)

**C1.** You are the IT Systems Manager at a statutory body. You have just completed a 6-month SOP development and implementation programme covering 10 computer system and network SOPs. The programme data is summarised below:

**Programme Data:**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total SOPs developed and approved | 10 |
| SOPs fully implemented (go-live achieved) | 9 |
| SOPs deferred (not yet implemented) | 1 (ICT-NET-SOP-007: Network Capacity Planning — awaiting tool procurement) |
| Staff required to complete SOP training | 45 |
| Staff who completed training before go-live | 42 |
| Staff who completed training after go-live (within 2 weeks) | 2 |
| Staff who have not yet completed training | 1 (on extended medical leave) |
| Total audited activities in monitoring period | 200 |
| Compliant activities | 186 |
| Non-conformances raised | 14 |
| NCs closed within 30 days | 11 |
| NCs closed after 30 days | 2 |
| NCs still open | 1 (Major NC — pending system fix by vendor) |
| SOPs reviewed within scheduled review date | 9 of 10 (ICT-SEC-SOP-002 overdue by 3 weeks) |

**Lessons Learned Inputs from Programme Team:**

| Input | Area |
|---|---|
| Stakeholder interviews took longer than planned because some process owners were unresponsive | Requirements Analysis |
| The SOP document template was revised three times during drafting because the initial template did not meet ISO 27001 requirements | Document Preparation |
| Training for shift workers required three separate sessions rather than the planned two, increasing trainer hours by 50% | Training |
| The ITSM tool did not generate the compliance metrics automatically — data had to be collected manually | Compliance Monitoring |

(a) Calculate the four KPIs below. Show your working, compare each to its target, and classify each as On Target, Below Target, or Above Target. (10 marks)

| KPI | Target | Your Calculation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOP compliance rate | ≥ 95% | | |
| Training completion rate (before or within 2 weeks of go-live) | 100% | | |
| NC closure rate within 30 days | ≥ 90% | | |
| SOP document currency rate | 100% | | |

(b) Using the four lessons learned inputs provided, complete a Lessons Learned Register. For each input, provide: Reference, Area, Observation (expanded from the input), quantified Impact (where possible), and a specific, actionable Recommendation for future programmes. (20 marks)

(c) Write a complete Executive Summary for the Completion Report that you would present to the CIO and Board of Directors. Your Executive Summary must include: (i) programme outcome statement, (ii) key achievements (3–5 bullet points), (iii) headline KPI results, (iv) top issues and their resolution, and (v) 3–5 prioritised recommendations. Length: one page maximum. (20 marks)
