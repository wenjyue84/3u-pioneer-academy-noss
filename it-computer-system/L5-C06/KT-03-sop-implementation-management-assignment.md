## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 4 marks each)

**A1.** In the ADKAR change management model, the "K" element (Knowledge) is addressed in SOP implementation through:

- (a) Sending an email announcement to all staff on go-live day
- (b) Delivering training on the new procedure before the go-live date
- (c) Publishing the SOP on the intranet
- (d) Conducting an audit three months after go-live

**A2.** Which SOP implementation approach is most appropriate for a large government agency with 500 staff across eight sites when introducing a high-risk, complex network security SOP?

- (a) Simultaneous rollout to all sites on the same day
- (b) E-learning only — no instructor-led training required at this scale
- (c) Pilot rollout at one site first, then phased rollout to remaining sites incorporating lessons from the pilot
- (d) Informal rollout with no communication plan

**A3.** A non-conformance where a Systems Administrator bypassed the mandatory two-person authorisation step for a firewall rule change, causing a network outage, would be classified as:

- (a) Minor non-conformance
- (b) Observation
- (c) Major non-conformance
- (d) Opportunity for improvement only

**A4.** The SOP compliance rate KPI is calculated as:

- (a) (Total number of staff trained ÷ Total staff) × 100%
- (b) (Number of audited activities compliant with SOP ÷ Total audited activities) × 100%
- (c) (Number of SOPs reviewed ÷ Total SOPs) × 100%
- (d) (Number of non-conformances closed ÷ Total non-conformances) × 100%

**A5.** When a technology change (e.g. migration from on-premises Active Directory to Azure AD) requires revision of a user account management SOP, the correct first step is:

- (a) Immediately update the SOP and distribute to all staff
- (b) Retire the existing SOP and operate without a procedure until the new one is written
- (c) Submit a formal SOP Change Request and route it through the change control workflow
- (d) Ask staff to follow the old SOP until the next scheduled annual review

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (50 marks)

**C1.** You are the IT Systems Manager at a university with 800 staff (academic and administrative) and 8,000 students. You have just received management approval to implement a new set of five computer network SOPs, effective in six weeks. The SOPs cover: (1) Network equipment configuration change, (2) Wireless network access management, (3) Network incident response, (4) VPN access provisioning, and (5) Network capacity monitoring and reporting.

The affected staff include: 12 network engineers (two shifts), 6 IT security officers, 4 helpdesk supervisors, and 35 helpdesk agents. Three of the network engineers are stationed at a remote campus 200 km away.

(a) Design a Communication Plan for the six-week pre-implementation period. For each communication event, specify: the audience, channel, timing (weeks before go-live), content summary, and responsible person (by job title). You must include at minimum five distinct communication events. Present your plan in a table. (15 marks)

(b) Design a Training Plan for all affected staff. For each staff category, specify: the training method, the content covered, the duration, the trainer, the training delivery format (classroom / lab / e-learning / blended), and how you will address the constraint of the remote campus engineers. Justify your choice of training method for each category. (20 marks)

(c) Three months after go-live, an internal spot check reveals the following observations. For each observation, state whether it is compliant, a minor non-conformance, or a major non-conformance. For each non-conformance, state the root cause category and the corrective action you would take. (15 marks)

| Obs | Observation |
|---|---|
| 1 | A network engineer made a VLAN configuration change without completing the mandatory Change Request Form (SOP step 2.1). The engineer stated he did not know the form existed. |
| 2 | A helpdesk agent provisioned VPN access for a contractor within 4 hours of receiving an approved request — the SOP requires provisioning within 24 hours. The agent completed all required documentation. |
| 3 | The Network Capacity Monitoring Report for the previous month was submitted 5 days late. The SOP requires submission by the 5th working day of the following month. The delay was due to the reporting system being unavailable for 3 days due to a vendor outage. |
| 4 | Two VPN accounts for staff who resigned six weeks ago remain active. The SOP requires deactivation within 24 hours of HR notification. HR confirmed they sent the notification on the correct date. |
