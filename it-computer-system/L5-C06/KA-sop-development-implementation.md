## BAHAGIAN A: SOALAN PELBAGAI PILIHAN / SECTION A: MULTIPLE CHOICE (30 marks — 2 marks each)

Answer all 15 questions. Circle the correct answer.

**1.** The Bahasa Malaysia equivalent of Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is:
- (a) Prosedur Operasi Standard
- (b) Prosedur Operasi Piawai (POP)
- (c) Polisi Operasi Pusat
- (d) Panduan Operasi Piawai

**2.** Which of the following describes a "Missing SOP" gap type?
- (a) A procedure exists but has not been formally approved
- (b) A process area has no documented procedure whatsoever
- (c) A procedure exists but references superseded technology
- (d) A procedure exists but omits exception handling steps

**3.** A gap with Priority Score 9 (Compliance Risk 4 + Operational Risk 5) is classified as:
- (a) Low priority
- (b) Medium priority
- (c) High priority
- (d) Deferred

**4.** The document that lists every controlled SOP with its version, effective date, review date, and owner is called:
- (a) Requirements Register
- (b) Gap Analysis Worksheet
- (c) Document Register
- (d) Non-Conformance Register

**5.** In a RACI matrix, the role marked "A" (Accountable) is:
- (a) The person who physically performs the task
- (b) The person who is ultimately answerable for the correct completion of the activity — there must be exactly one per activity
- (c) The person whose expertise is sought before the task proceeds
- (d) The person kept informed of outcomes only

**6.** Which ISO/IEC standard is the primary reference for information security SOPs including network management and access control procedures?
- (a) MS ISO 9001:2015
- (b) MS ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- (c) MS ISO 14001:2015
- (d) NIST SP 800-12

**7.** An SOP distribution copy marked "SALINAN TERKAWAL / CONTROLLED COPY" means:
- (a) The copy is for reference only and may be out of date
- (b) The copy is an official, version-tracked copy authorised for operational use
- (c) The copy must be shredded immediately after reading
- (d) The copy has been rejected during review

**8.** Which flowchart symbol is used to represent a decision point in an SOP process flow diagram?
- (a) Rectangle
- (b) Rounded rectangle
- (c) Parallelogram
- (d) Diamond

**9.** In the ADKAR change management model, "Reinforcement" (the R element) in SOP implementation is achieved through:
- (a) Publishing the SOP on the staff intranet
- (b) Monitoring compliance, recognising good practice, and addressing non-compliance constructively after go-live
- (c) Delivering training before go-live
- (d) Sending an executive briefing memo

**10.** A non-conformance where a network engineer bypassed mandatory two-person authorisation for a firewall change that caused a production outage is classified as:
- (a) Observation
- (b) Minor non-conformance
- (c) Major non-conformance
- (d) Opportunity for improvement

**11.** The SOP compliance rate KPI is calculated as:
- (a) (Staff trained ÷ Total staff) × 100%
- (b) (Compliant audited activities ÷ Total audited activities) × 100%
- (c) (SOPs reviewed ÷ Total SOPs) × 100%
- (d) (NCs closed ÷ Total NCs) × 100%

**12.** When an SOP is revised due to a technology change, what must happen to all previously distributed controlled copies?
- (a) Nothing — they remain valid until the next annual review
- (b) They must be recalled or marked as superseded; new controlled copies of the revised SOP must be distributed
- (c) They must be submitted to MAMPU for archiving
- (d) They may continue to be used until stock is exhausted

**13.** Which of the following is NOT a required section of a standard SOP document?
- (a) Roles and Responsibilities (RACI)
- (b) Exception Handling
- (c) Trainee Assessment Results
- (d) Records and Evidence

**14.** The Completion Report for an SOP programme must be retained for a minimum of:
- (a) 1 year
- (b) 3 years
- (c) 5 years
- (d) 7 years

**15.** An "Emergency SOP Revision" is required when:
- (a) A scheduled annual review is due
- (b) A staff member suggests an improvement
- (c) A critical situation requires an immediate SOP change (e.g. active security incident or major system failure)
- (d) A new employee joins who has not yet been trained

## BAHAGIAN C: SOALAN ESEI / SECTION C: ESSAY (40 marks)

Answer ALL 2 questions.

**21.** You are the IT Systems Manager at a government ministry. Following an internal audit, the following non-conformances were raised against the ministry's IT SOP programme:

- NC-001 (Major): A server was patched without an approved Request for Change (RFC). The systems administrator stated the SOP was "too complex" and the approval process "took too long" during a critical vulnerability window.
- NC-002 (Minor): The Network Capacity Monitoring Report was submitted 8 days late for two consecutive months. The responsible officer stated that the reporting template in the SOP was unclear about which metrics to include.
- NC-003 (Major): Three user accounts for resigned staff remained active for more than 30 days after the HR termination notices were received by IT.

(a) For each non-conformance, identify the most likely root cause category (choose from: unclear SOP / inadequate training / resource constraint / process design failure / wilful non-compliance). Justify your choice. (9 marks)

(b) For each non-conformance, describe the corrective action you would implement, specifying what change (if any) is needed to the SOP, the training programme, or the monitoring process. (9 marks)

(c) Describe how you would present these three non-conformances in a Compliance Monitoring Report to the CIO. Your answer must include: how you would structure the NC summary, what KPI data you would include, and what executive-level recommendations you would make. (7 marks)

(d) NC-001 suggests that the patch management SOP approval process may be impractical during zero-day vulnerability windows. Describe how you would revise the SOP to include an emergency fast-track approval path, while maintaining compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 control A.8.8. Outline the change control steps you would follow to implement this revision. (15 marks)

**22.** An SOP development programme for a large polytechnic has just concluded. The programme produced 8 SOPs covering computer system and network management. Key programme data:

- Total staff trained: 60 of 60 (100%)
- Compliance rate at 3 months: 91% (190 compliant of 210 audited activities)
- NC closure rate within 30 days: 85% (17 of 20 NCs)
- SOP document currency: 100%
- One SOP (network segmentation) was deferred due to pending infrastructure upgrade
- Lessons learned: stakeholder engagement was stronger when process owners were formally assigned as SOP co-authors; e-learning alone was insufficient for complex procedural SOPs

(a) Calculate all four KPIs. Compare each to its target and state whether it is On Target or Below Target. (8 marks)

(b) Write the Executive Summary for the Completion Report (one page maximum). It must include: programme outcome statement, key achievements, headline KPI results, top issues and resolutions, and three prioritised recommendations. (15 marks)

(c) Using the two lessons learned provided, write complete Lessons Learned Register entries (Reference, Area, Observation, Impact, Recommendation). Then state how each recommendation should be built into the planning phase of the polytechnic's next SOP programme. (17 marks)
