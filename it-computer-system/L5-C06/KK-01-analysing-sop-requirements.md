## Objektif / Objective

Conduct a structured requirements analysis for a computer system and network SOP development programme, including stakeholder identification, gap analysis, and production of a Requirements Register.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

4 hours (240 minutes)

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Scenario brief (provided by instructor) | 1 per trainee |
| 2 | Existing SOP inventory list (simulated — provided by instructor) | 1 per trainee |
| 3 | Gap Analysis Worksheet (Form ICT-SOP-GA-001) | 1 per trainee |
| 4 | Requirements Register Template (Form ICT-SOP-RR-001) | 1 per trainee |
| 5 | Stakeholder Map Template | 1 per trainee |
| 6 | Access to MS ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO 9001:2015 clause summaries (reference handout) | 1 per trainee |
| 7 | Computer with word processing and spreadsheet software | 1 per trainee |
| 8 | Pen and notepad | 1 per trainee |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Handle all scenario documents as SULIT (Confidential) — do not share outside the training room
- Do not save scenario data on personal devices or cloud storage
- Return all printed materials to the instructor at the end of the session

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Receive the scenario brief from the instructor. Read it in full before taking any action. The scenario describes a government agency whose IT department has been directed to develop a set of SOPs for computer system and network management as part of an upcoming ISO 27001 certification audit. |
| 2 | Identify all stakeholders involved in the SOP development programme from the scenario. Complete the Stakeholder Map Template: list each stakeholder by job title, categorise them (Executive / Process Owner / End User / Compliance / External), and state their role in the SOP programme. |
| 3 | Review the Existing SOP Inventory List provided. For each process area listed, determine whether a current SOP exists and, if so, whether it is: (a) current and compliant, (b) outdated, (c) incomplete, or (d) non-compliant with ISO 27001:2022. Record your findings on the Gap Analysis Worksheet. |
| 4 | For each identified gap on the Gap Analysis Worksheet, assign a Compliance Risk score (1–5) and an Operational Risk score (1–5). Calculate the Priority Score (sum of both). Classify each gap as High (≥8), Medium (5–7), or Low (≤4) priority. |
| 5 | Map each identified gap to the applicable ISO 27001:2022 Annex A control(s) and/or ISO 9001:2015 clause(s) using the reference handout. Record the mapping in the Gap Analysis Worksheet. |
| 6 | Transfer all identified requirements to the Requirements Register Template. For each requirement, complete all fields: Ref, Process Area, Gap Type, Requirement Statement, Priority, Owner (job title), and Target Date. |
| 7 | Draft a one-page Executive Summary for the Requirements Report. The summary must state: the purpose of the SOP programme, the number of gaps identified by priority category, the top three high-priority requirements, and a recommended sequencing for development. |
| 8 | Compile all outputs (Stakeholder Map, completed Gap Analysis Worksheet, Requirements Register, Executive Summary) into a structured Requirements Report. Submit to the instructor for review. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

Completed Requirements Report comprising:
- Stakeholder Map with all identified stakeholders categorised and their roles stated
- Gap Analysis Worksheet with all process areas assessed, risk scores assigned, and standards mapping completed
- Requirements Register with all requirements documented, prioritised, and assigned
- One-page Executive Summary presenting findings and sequencing recommendation
