## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 4 marks each)

**A1.** In the context of Malaysian ICT governance, the Bahasa Malaysia term for Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is:

- (a) Prosedur Operasi Standard
- (b) Prosedur Operasi Piawai (POP)
- (c) Panduan Operasi Pusat
- (d) Polisi Operasi Piawai

**A2.** Which of the following gap types describes a situation where staff follow an informal, undocumented procedure that has never been formally reviewed and approved?

- (a) Missing SOP
- (b) Non-compliant SOP
- (c) Outdated SOP
- (d) Unapproved SOP

**A3.** A gap with a Compliance Risk score of 4 and an Operational Risk score of 5 would be classified as:

- (a) Low priority (score ≤ 4)
- (b) Medium priority (score 5–7)
- (c) High priority (score ≥ 8)
- (d) Critical priority (score ≥ 10)

**A4.** The primary purpose of a Scope Statement in an SOP Requirements Report is to:

- (a) List all staff who will be trained on the SOP
- (b) Define what processes, systems, and personnel the SOP programme covers and explicitly exclude what is not covered
- (c) Describe the budget allocated for SOP development
- (d) Assign document numbers to each SOP

**A5.** Which ISO standard requires documented procedures for information security controls, including operational procedures for managing IT systems and networks?

- (a) MS ISO 9001:2015
- (b) MS ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- (c) MS ISO 14001:2015
- (d) MS ISO 45001:2018

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (50 marks)

**C1.** You are an IT Systems Manager at a government agency. Your CIO has instructed you to lead an SOP development programme to prepare the organisation for an MS ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification audit. The agency has 150 IT staff across three sites (headquarters and two branch offices). A review of existing documentation reveals the following situation:

| Process Area | Existing Documentation Status |
|---|---|
| Firewall rule change | No documented procedure — engineers follow tribal knowledge |
| Server patch management | SOP last updated in 2019; references superseded systems |
| User account provisioning | SOP exists but has no offboarding or dormant account review steps |
| Incident response | Informal checklist only; never formally approved or version-controlled |
| Network capacity planning | No documentation |
| Backup and recovery | SOP exists and was reviewed 8 months ago; currently compliant |

(a) Conduct a gap analysis on the six process areas. For each area, classify the gap type and assign a Compliance Risk score (1–5) and Operational Risk score (1–5) with justification. Calculate the Priority Score and classify each as High, Medium, or Low priority. Present your analysis in a structured table. (20 marks)

(b) Based on your gap analysis, draft the Requirements Register entries for the THREE (3) highest-priority requirements. Each entry must include all required fields. (15 marks)

(c) Write a one-page Executive Summary for the SOP Requirements Report that you would present to the CIO and IT Steering Committee. The summary must cover: programme purpose, number of gaps by priority, top three requirements, and your recommended development sequence with justification. (15 marks)
