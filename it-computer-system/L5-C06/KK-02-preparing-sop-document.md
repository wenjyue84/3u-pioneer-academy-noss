## Objektif / Objective

Draft a complete, standards-compliant SOP document for a computer system or network management process, including document control header, all mandatory body sections, a swimlane process flow diagram, at least one supporting form, and a completed review comment register.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

5 hours (300 minutes)

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | SOP Assignment Brief (provided by instructor — specifies the process to be documented) | 1 per trainee |
| 2 | SOP Document Template (Form ICT-SOP-TMPL-001) | 1 per trainee |
| 3 | Supporting Form Template (Form ICT-SOP-FORM-001) | 1 per trainee |
| 4 | Review Comment Register Template (Form ICT-SOP-RC-001) | 1 per trainee |
| 5 | Document Register entry form | 1 per trainee |
| 6 | Computer with word processing software (MS Word or equivalent) | 1 per trainee |
| 7 | Swimlane diagram tool (MS Visio, draw.io, or equivalent) | 1 per trainee |
| 8 | Flowchart symbol reference card | 1 per trainee |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Save work frequently — data loss during drafting is the author's responsibility
- Do not use real staff names or actual system credentials in the SOP document; use role titles and placeholder values
- All draft SOP documents must be clearly watermarked "DRAF / DRAFT — NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE" until formally approved

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Receive the SOP Assignment Brief from the instructor. Read the brief carefully. It specifies the process to be documented, the applicable standards, the roles involved, and any specific requirements (e.g. mandatory two-person authorisation, maximum response time). |
| 2 | Complete the Document Control Header in the SOP Document Template. Assign a document number following the convention [DEPT]-[CATEGORY]-SOP-[NNN] (e.g. ICT-NET-SOP-001). Set the version to Draft v0.1, leave the effective date blank (to be assigned after approval), set the review period to 12 months, and enter the document owner and approver job titles. |
| 3 | Draft Section 1 (Purpose) of the SOP. The Purpose statement must answer: what process the SOP governs, why it exists (the business or compliance rationale), and what outcome compliance with the SOP produces. It must not exceed 150 words. |
| 4 | Draft Section 2 (Scope) of the SOP. Clearly state the functional scope (which process/sub-processes), organisational scope (which departments or sites), technical scope (which systems or infrastructure), and any explicit exclusions with rationale. |
| 5 | Draft Section 3 (Definitions). List all key terms, abbreviations, and acronyms used in the SOP. Each definition must be precise and free of circular references. |
| 6 | Draft Section 4 (Roles and Responsibilities) using a RACI matrix. Identify all roles involved in the process. For each major activity, assign: Responsible (R), Accountable (A), Consulted (C), and Informed (I). |
| 7 | Draft Section 5 (Prerequisites). List all conditions, tools, access rights, and approvals that must be in place before the procedure can begin. |
| 8 | Draft Section 6 (Procedure Steps) using the step table format: Step number | Responsible Role | Action | Input | Output/Evidence. Write each step in active voice, imperative mood. Include decision points where the procedure branches. Ensure exception handling steps are included. |
| 9 | Draft Section 7 (Exception Handling). Describe what the operator must do if a step cannot be completed as written — including escalation path, emergency contact, and temporary workaround (if any). |
| 10 | Draft Section 8 (Records and Evidence). Specify what must be recorded, in which form or system, who is responsible for the record, and how long it must be retained. |
| 11 | Draft Sections 9 and 10 (Related Documents and References). List all related SOPs, policies, forms, and external standards. |
| 12 | Create a swimlane flowchart diagram of the complete procedure. Use the correct flowchart symbols. Each swimlane must represent one role. Insert the diagram into the SOP document as a figure with a caption. |
| 13 | Design the supporting form referenced in the procedure (e.g. a request form, authorisation record, or log template). Apply the form template: include the parent SOP document number, a unique form number, version, date fields, and signature fields. |
| 14 | Conduct a self-review of the complete draft SOP against the review checklist (provided on the Review Comment Register Template). Record any issues found and the corrective action taken. |
| 15 | Submit the completed draft SOP (including diagram and supporting form) and the Review Comment Register to the instructor, who will act as the technical reviewer. Incorporate all review comments and update the Review Comment Register to show each comment's status (Resolved / Rejected with rationale). |
| 16 | Update the Document Register entry form with the SOP details. Submit all outputs to the instructor. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

Completed SOP document package comprising:
- SOP document (all 10 sections complete, Draft v0.1 watermark)
- Swimlane flowchart diagram embedded in the document
- Supporting form with document control header
- Review Comment Register showing all review comments and their resolution status
- Document Register entry form completed
