## Objektif / Objective

Assemble and install computer hardware components correctly following manufacturer specifications and safety procedures.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

90 minutes

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Computer case (ATX or micro-ATX) | 1 |
| 2 | Motherboard (compatible with case and CPU) | 1 |
| 3 | CPU with heatsink and fan | 1 |
| 4 | Thermal paste | 1 tube (shared) |
| 5 | RAM modules (DDR5) | 2 |
| 6 | Storage drive (NVMe SSD or SATA SSD) | 1 |
| 7 | Power supply unit (PSU) | 1 |
| 8 | Expansion cards (GPU, if applicable) | As needed |
| 9 | Screwdriver set (Phillips, Torx) | 1 set |
| 10 | Anti-static wrist strap | 1 |
| 11 | Anti-static mat | 1 |
| 12 | Cable ties or velcro straps | As needed |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- **MANDATORY:** Wear anti-static wrist strap connected to a grounded point at all times during assembly
- Handle all components by their edges -- never touch pins, contacts, or circuit traces
- Ensure the PSU power switch is OFF and the power cable is disconnected during assembly
- Apply thermal paste carefully -- use only a pea-sized amount; excess can damage components
- Do not force any component into its socket or slot; if it does not fit, verify compatibility
- Tighten screws firmly but do not overtighten (risk of cracking the motherboard)

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Install the I/O shield.** Press the I/O shield into the case opening from the inside. Ensure all tabs are properly seated and the shield is flush with the case. |
| 2 | **Install standoffs.** Verify the motherboard form factor and install the correct standoffs in the case. Align standoff positions with the mounting holes on the motherboard. |
| 3 | **Install the motherboard.** Lower the motherboard into the case, aligning the I/O ports with the I/O shield and the mounting holes with the standoffs. Secure with screws -- tighten in a cross pattern to distribute pressure evenly. |
| 4 | **Install the CPU.** Open the CPU socket latch. Align the CPU using the triangle marker or notch. Lower the CPU gently into the socket without force. Close the latch to secure. |
| 5 | **Apply thermal paste.** Apply a pea-sized amount of thermal paste to the centre of the CPU. Do not spread manually -- the heatsink pressure will distribute it. |
| 6 | **Install the heatsink/fan.** Place the heatsink on the CPU, ensuring even contact. Secure using the mounting mechanism (push-pins, screws, or bracket). Connect the CPU fan cable to the CPU_FAN header on the motherboard. |
| 7 | **Install RAM modules.** Identify the correct slots for dual-channel configuration (refer to motherboard manual). Open the retention clips. Align the notch on each module with the slot key. Press firmly and evenly until the clips snap into place. |
| 8 | **Install storage.** For NVMe: insert into the M.2 slot at an angle, press down, and secure with the standoff screw. For SATA SSD: mount in the drive bay and connect the SATA data cable and SATA power cable. |
| 9 | **Install the PSU.** Mount the PSU in the case (typically at the bottom). Secure with screws. |
| 10 | **Connect power cables.** Connect the 24-pin ATX power connector to the motherboard. Connect the 8-pin (or 4+4 pin) CPU power connector. Connect PCIe power to the GPU if applicable. Connect SATA power to storage drives. |
| 11 | **Connect front-panel cables.** Connect the case front-panel wires (power button, reset, HDD LED, power LED) to the motherboard front-panel header according to the motherboard manual. |
| 12 | **Organize cables.** Route cables behind the motherboard tray where possible. Bundle excess cable length with cable ties. Ensure no cable obstructs fans or airflow paths. |
| 13 | **Final visual inspection.** Verify all components are properly seated and secured. Check that no loose screws, tools, or packaging remain inside the case. Confirm all power and data connections are firm. |
| 14 | **Do not power on yet.** The system is ready for software installation (KK-04). Close the side panel loosely (do not screw closed until after initial boot verification). |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

Fully assembled computer with:
- Motherboard correctly mounted with I/O shield
- CPU installed with thermal paste and cooler securely attached
- RAM installed in correct slots for dual-channel operation
- Storage drive(s) installed and connected
- PSU mounted with all power connections made
- Cables organized and not obstructing airflow
- No loose components, screws, or foreign objects inside the case
