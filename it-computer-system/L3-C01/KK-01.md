## Tajuk / Title: Analysing and Planning a Computer Build

---

## Objektif / Objective

Analyse a job request and plan the computer set-up sequence.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

45 minutes

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Job request template (simulated) | 1 |
| 2 | Customer specifications sheet | 1 |
| 3 | Hardware checklist form | 1 |
| 4 | Parts inventory list | 1 |
| 5 | Pen and notepad | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- No specific hardware safety required for this planning exercise
- Handle all documents carefully and return to instructor after completion

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Receive the simulated job request from the instructor. Read the entire document carefully. The job request specifies a CPU model, RAM capacity, storage type, and software requirements. |
| 2 | Extract and verify all customer requirements from the job request. Record each requirement on the hardware checklist form. |
| 3 | Cross-check the specified hardware against the parts inventory list. Identify which parts are available in stock and which need to be ordered. |
| 4 | Create a logical set-up sequence: list the order in which you will install hardware components and software. Justify the order. |
| 5 | Identify any missing parts, incompatibilities, or unclear specifications. Document each issue and the action you would take (e.g. contact requestor, substitute part). |
| 6 | Prepare a bill of materials listing every part, tool, and software item needed for the build. |
| 7 | Document the complete analysis in a structured format: requirements table, compatibility check, set-up sequence, bill of materials, and open issues. Submit to the instructor for review. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

Completed analysis document showing:
- All identified requirements from the job request
- Compatibility verification results
- Logical set-up sequence with justification
- Bill of materials
- Open issues or clarifications needed
