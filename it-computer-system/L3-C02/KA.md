### Q1 — Types of Maintenance
**Type:** MCQ | **Difficulty:** L1 | **Marks:** 2 | **LO Ref:** WA1.K1

Preventive maintenance differs from corrective maintenance in that it is:

(a) Unscheduled and triggered by a fault report  (b) Scheduled and performed before failure
(c) Performed only after warranty expiry  (d) Requested by the user after breakdown

**Answer:** (b)  **Rubric:** 3=Correct+clear explanation | 2=Correct | 1=Partially correct | 0=Wrong

Which tool is used to clean thermal paste from the CPU surface before reapplication?

(a) Compressed air  (b) Soft brush  (c) Isopropyl alcohol with lint-free cloth  (d) Vacuum cleaner

**Answer:** (c)  **Rubric:** 3=Correct+reason | 2=Correct | 1=Partially correct | 0=Wrong

### Q3 — Thermal Paste Replacement
**Type:** Short Answer | **Difficulty:** L2 | **Marks:** 4 | **LO Ref:** WA2.K3

State THREE steps for safely replacing thermal paste on a CPU.

**Model Answer:** (1) Power off and unplug; remove heatsink. (2) Clean old paste from CPU and heatsink
with IPA and lint-free cloth; allow to dry. (3) Apply pea-sized amount centrally; reseat heatsink evenly;
verify temperatures after boot.

**Rubric:** 3=All 3 steps correct and complete | 2=2 steps correct | 1=1 step correct | 0=Incorrect/blank

---

### Q4 — Software Maintenance Tasks
**Type:** Short Answer | **Difficulty:** L2 | **Marks:** 4 | **LO Ref:** WA2.K4

List FOUR software maintenance tasks a technician performs during scheduled preventive maintenance.

**Model Answer:** (1) Apply OS security/cumulative updates. (2) Update antivirus definitions and run a scan.
(3) Review Windows Event Viewer for warnings or errors. (4) Check disk SMART data for early failure signs.

**Rubric:** 3=4 tasks listed correctly | 2=3 tasks correct | 1=1–2 tasks correct | 0=Incorrect/blank
