## Objektif / Objective

By the end of this activity, trainees will be able to:
- Identify the type and scope of maintenance required for a given computer system
- Differentiate between preventive, corrective, and predictive maintenance requirements
- Examine a computer system to determine its maintenance needs
- Complete a Maintenance Request Form (Borang Permintaan Penyelenggaraan) accurately

---

## Tempoh / Duration

3 hours

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Wear the anti-static wrist strap before opening any computer casing to prevent electrostatic discharge (ESD) damage to components
- Ensure the computer is powered off and the power cable is disconnected before any physical inspection
- Do not force-open any panel or connector — inspect visually first
- Handle all documentation and forms carefully; return to instructor upon completion
- Report any unusual odour (burning smell), visible damage, or exposed wiring to the instructor immediately before proceeding

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Receive the simulated Maintenance Request Form from the instructor. Read the entire form carefully. Note the computer asset number, reported symptoms, date of last maintenance, and user's description of the problem. |
| 2 | Based on the form, determine the category of maintenance needed: **Preventive** (scheduled, before failure), **Corrective** (in response to a fault), or **Predictive** (based on performance trend data). Record your determination with justification. |
| 3 | Power off the computer. Disconnect the power cable. Wear your anti-static wrist strap. Open the computer casing using the appropriate screwdriver. |
| 4 | Perform a visual inspection of internal components: check for dust accumulation, loose cables, swollen capacitors, burnt components, and cable management condition. Record all observations on the Maintenance Checklist template. |
| 5 | Reconnect the power cable. Power on the computer and observe the boot sequence. Note any POST beep codes, error messages, or abnormal startup behaviour. Record findings. |
| 6 | Review the Fault History Log (simulated) for the machine. Identify recurring faults, previously replaced components, and patterns that may indicate systemic issues. Record relevant history on the checklist. |
| 7 | Based on all gathered information (form, visual inspection, boot observation, fault history), compile a list of maintenance tasks required. Categorise each task as: Physical Cleaning, Hardware Check/Replacement, Software Update, or Backup/Restore. |
| 8 | Estimate the priority level of each identified task: **Critical** (immediate action required), **High** (within 24 hours), **Medium** (within one week), or **Low** (next scheduled cycle). |
| 9 | Complete the Maintenance Request Form — fill in the Assessment section with your findings, required tasks, estimated time, and priority classification. Sign and date the form. |
| 10 | Submit the completed form and checklist to the instructor for review and discussion. |

---

## Hasil Dijangka / Expected Outcome

Upon completion, the trainee will have produced:
- A fully completed Maintenance Request Form identifying the type of maintenance required
- A Maintenance Checklist documenting all physical and software observations
- A prioritised task list categorised by urgency and type
- A clear written justification for the maintenance category assigned (preventive / corrective / predictive)
