## Arahan kepada Pelatih / Instructions to Trainee

1. This is a PRACTICAL examination. You will be assessed on your ability to manage a network cabling scenario end-to-end: plan, install, document, coordinate, and process a change.
2. You will be given a scenario package including a floor plan, cable schedule, contractor submission, and a pending change request.
3. You will be assessed on: **Process** (how you do it), **Output** (the result), **Attitude** (professionalism and precision), **Safety** (compliance with safety procedures), and **Environmental** compliance.
4. All work must be documented on the provided forms.
5. Total time allowed: 4 hours.

---

## Arahan kepada Penilai / Instructions to Assessor

- Provide each trainee with the complete scenario package (see below).
- Observe each trainee continuously. Do NOT assist unless there is a safety hazard.
- Mark each criterion on the assessment rubric as the trainee works.
- Verify all output documents at the end of the assessment.

---

## Tugasan / Task Description

**Scenario:** You are the Level 4 Network Cabling Administrator for Syarikat Teknologi Maju Sdn. Bhd. You have been given the following package:

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Floor plan | Building C, Floor 2 — showing TR-C2-01, 12 existing outlets (WA-C2-201-A to WA-C2-206-B), and one empty room (C2-207) |
| Existing cable schedule | 12 cables (HC-C2-01-001 to HC-C2-01-012); all Active; lengths 22–68 m; all certified |
| Contractor submission | Draft test report for 2 new cables — one has a FAIL on NEXT; materials delivery docket includes Cat6 cable (SOW specified Cat6A) |
| Pending change request | CCR-PA-001: Add 2 new Cat6A outlets in Room C2-207 (WA-C2-207-A and WA-C2-207-B); cables HC-C2-01-013 and HC-C2-01-014 already installed by contractor (lengths 31 m and 34 m) |

**You must complete the following tasks:**

1. Review the contractor's material delivery and test report; identify non-conformances (20 minutes)
2. Produce a Non-Conformance Report (NCR) for identified issues (20 minutes)
3. Terminate and label two pre-pulled Cat6A cable ends at the patch panel in the training rack (60 minutes)
4. Run a certification test on both cables; record results (30 minutes)
5. Update the cable schedule and port assignment record for CCR-PA-001 (30 minutes)
6. Update the as-built floor plan drawing to Rev B for the two new outlets (30 minutes)
7. Complete the Change Completion Report for CCR-PA-001 (20 minutes)
8. Produce a pre-installation meeting agenda for a hypothetical expansion of Floor 3 (20 minutes)

**Total time: 4 hours**
