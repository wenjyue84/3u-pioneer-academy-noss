## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (10 marks)

**A1.** The document that records every cable in an installation with its origin, destination, length, and test result is called:

- (a) As-built drawing
- (b) Cable schedule
- (c) Port assignment record
- (d) Change log

**A2.** When a new cable is added to an existing installation, the as-built drawing revision should be incremented:

- (a) Only at the end of the year
- (b) Immediately; within 5 working days of the physical change
- (c) Only when the client requests it
- (d) After the next audit

**A3.** The minimum record retention period for as-built drawings is:

- (a) 1 year
- (b) 3 years
- (c) Life of installation plus 7 years after decommission
- (d) 10 years

**A4.** A cable audit is best described as:

- (a) A financial review of cabling costs
- (b) A physical verification that cable records match the actual installed infrastructure
- (c) A test of cable performance using a certification tester
- (d) A review of the cable specification documents

**A5.** Which of the following is NOT a required field in a fibre optic cable record?

- (a) Insertion loss (dB)
- (b) OTDR trace file reference
- (c) Cable jacket colour
- (d) Connector type

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (20 marks)

**B1.** List FIVE (5) fields that must be included in a cable schedule record for each horizontal cable. (5 marks)

**B2.** Describe the THREE (3) types of as-built drawings required for a multi-floor office building cabling installation. State the content of each. (9 marks)

**B3.** Describe the steps of a cable record audit for a floor with 60 outlets. Include: scope, physical tracing method, comparison with records, and what to do if discrepancies are found. (6 marks)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (20 marks)

**C1.** A company's network has grown over three years without any formal cable record maintenance. The original installation had 80 outlets. Since then, approximately 30 outlets have been added in unrecorded moves and additions. The IT administrator has been asked to bring the records up to date.

Describe the process the IT administrator should follow to: (a) establish the current accurate state of the cabling infrastructure through a full audit; (b) update the cable schedule and as-built drawings; (c) implement a system to prevent this situation from recurring. (20 marks)
