## BAHAGIAN A: SOALAN PELBAGAI PILIHAN / SECTION A: MULTIPLE CHOICE (30 marks)

Answer all 15 questions. Each question carries 2 marks. Circle the correct answer.

**1.** The maximum horizontal cabling permanent link length under ANSI/TIA-568 is:

- (a) 55 m
- (b) 70 m
- (c) 90 m
- (d) 100 m

**2.** Cat6A UTP supports 10 Gbps at a full horizontal distance of:

- (a) 55 m
- (b) 75 m
- (c) 90 m
- (d) 100 m

**3.** The wiring standard most commonly used for commercial cabling installations in Malaysia is:

- (a) T568A
- (b) T568B
- (c) T568C
- (d) USOC

**4.** TIA-606-B labels must be placed within __________ of each termination point:

- (a) 50 mm
- (b) 100 mm
- (c) 150 mm
- (d) 300 mm

**5.** Which cable type is rated for outdoor underground installation between buildings?

- (a) CMR (riser) rated cable
- (b) CMP (plenum) rated cable
- (c) Direct burial outdoor-rated cable
- (d) Cat5e UTP patch cord

**6.** The maximum recommended conduit fill ratio for telecommunications cabling is:

- (a) 20%
- (b) 40%
- (c) 60%
- (d) 80%

**7.** A Non-Conformance Report (NCR) is issued by the IT administrator when:

- (a) The contractor requests additional payment
- (b) The contractor's materials or work do not meet the specified requirements
- (c) The project is completed ahead of schedule
- (d) A design change is requested by the client

**8.** The minimum pair untwist rule for Cat6A cable termination is most critical because excessive untwist:

- (a) Makes the cable too long
- (b) Increases near-end crosstalk (NEXT), which may cause the certification test to fail
- (c) Prevents the cable from fitting into the conduit
- (d) Reduces cable length available for future moves

**9.** In the TIA-606-B label convention, the outlet identifier "WA-B2-F203-A" refers to:

- (a) Work Area, Building B, Floor 2, Room 203, Outlet A
- (b) Work Area, Building 2, Floor B, Room 203, Outlet A
- (c) Wireless Access point, Building B, Floor 2, Room 203
- (d) Wide Area network, Building B, Floor 2, Port 203A

**10.** The as-built drawing must be updated within __________ of a physical cabling change:

- (a) 1 working day
- (b) 5 working days
- (c) 30 days
- (d) At the next scheduled audit

**11.** Which document formally initiates a cabling change before any physical work begins?

- (a) Change Completion Report
- (b) As-built drawing revision
- (c) Cabling Change Request Form
- (d) Certification test report

**12.** A Permit-to-Work (PTW) is required for cabling work in all of the following EXCEPT:

- (a) Working above a suspended ceiling in an occupied building
- (b) Working in an electrical room near live panels
- (c) Updating cable records in the IT office
- (d) Drilling through a fire-rated wall

**13.** The star topology for horizontal cabling means:

- (a) Each outlet daisy-chains to the next outlet before returning to the TR
- (b) Each outlet has a dedicated cable running directly from the TR — no branching or sharing
- (c) Outlets are connected in a ring and the TR is at the centre
- (d) Two cables share one conduit between adjacent workstations

**14.** For Cat6A channel certification testing, the test should be performed with:

- (a) A basic continuity tester
- (b) A Cat5e certified tester set to Cat6A mode
- (c) A Category-rated tester verified and calibrated for Cat6A (e.g. Fluke DSX-600 or equivalent)
- (d) A multimeter set to resistance mode

**15.** The minimum recommended system warranty period for a Cat6A structured cabling installation (manufacturer channel warranty) is:

- (a) 5 years
- (b) 10 years
- (c) 15 years
- (d) 20 years

## BAHAGIAN B: SOALAN JAWAPAN PENDEK / SECTION B: SHORT ANSWER (30 marks)

Answer all 6 questions.

**16.** List the SIX (6) subsystems of a structured cabling system and give a one-sentence description of each. (6 marks)

**17.** State the T568B colour assignment for all 8 pins of an RJ-45 connector. (4 marks)

**18.** A new office floor requires 30 workstations (2 outlets each), 1 boardroom (6 outlets), and 1 IT closet (4 outlets). Calculate: (a) total outlets before spare capacity; (b) total outlets after adding 15% spare capacity (round up to nearest 12); (c) total Cat6A cable required if the average run length is 52 m and 6 m of slack is added per cable. Show all working. (6 marks)

**19.** List FOUR (4) items that must be included in a Scope of Work (SOW) document prepared for a cabling contractor. (4 marks)

**20.** Describe the content of a Change Completion Report. List ALL required sections. (5 marks)

**21.** State FIVE (5) consequences of not maintaining accurate cable records in a large office building. (5 marks)

## BAHAGIAN C: SOALAN ESEI / SECTION C: ESSAY (40 marks)

Answer all 2 questions.

**22.** You are the network cabling administrator for a company relocating to a new five-floor building. Each floor has 50 workstations. You have been appointed to manage the entire cabling infrastructure project from planning through handover.

(a) Describe how you would plan the cabling layout for the building, including the site survey process, TR placement decisions, backbone design, and cable category selection. (10 marks)

(b) Describe how you would manage the cabling contractor during installation, including the scope of work preparation, pre-installation coordination, staged inspections, and final acceptance process. (10 marks)

**23.** After two years of operation, the company's network has grown through uncontrolled moves, adds, and changes. The original cable records are partially outdated. A new IT administrator has been appointed and tasked with restoring full documentation accuracy.

(a) Describe the full cable records audit process the new IT administrator should follow, including how to conduct a physical trace, compare with existing records, and resolve discrepancies. (10 marks)

(b) Describe the change management system the IT administrator should implement to prevent documentation degradation in future, including the change request process, record update procedure, drawing revision control, and periodic audit schedule. (10 marks)
