## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (10 marks)

**A1.** A Scope of Work (SOW) document for a cabling project must include:

- (a) The contractor's staff salary details
- (b) Materials specification, testing requirements, documentation deliverables, and warranty terms
- (c) The building owner's personal information
- (d) The network equipment brand only

**A2.** A Permit-to-Work (PTW) is required for cabling work when:

- (a) The project involves more than 10 cables
- (b) Work is performed in occupied buildings, electrical rooms, above ceilings, or at height
- (c) The contractor is from outside the state
- (d) The work is done during office hours

**A3.** A Non-Conformance Report (NCR) is issued when:

- (a) The contractor completes the work ahead of schedule
- (b) The contractor's work or materials do not meet the SOW or applicable standard
- (c) The IT administrator is absent during inspection
- (d) The final payment is due

**A4.** All cabling variations (changes to the original SOW) must be:

- (a) Agreed verbally between IT administrator and contractor
- (b) Documented in a formal variation order signed by both parties before work proceeds
- (c) Decided by the facilities manager alone
- (d) Recorded only in the meeting minutes

**A5.** Which legislation governs occupational safety requirements for on-site cabling work in Malaysia?

- (a) Communications and Multimedia Act 1998
- (b) Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 (OSHA 1994)
- (c) Factories and Machinery Act 1967
- (d) Building By-Laws 1984

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (20 marks)

**B1.** List FIVE (5) items that must be included on a pre-installation meeting agenda for a cabling project. (5 marks)

**B2.** Describe the staged inspection process for a cabling installation. List FOUR (4) stages and state TWO (2) specific items checked at each stage. (8 marks)

**B3.** A contractor proposes to substitute the specified Cat6A cable with Cat6 cable because Cat6A is temporarily out of stock. Explain: (a) whether this substitution should be accepted; (b) what the IT administrator should do; (c) what document should be issued. (7 marks)

---

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (20 marks)

**C1.** You are the IT administrator responsible for a cabling project in a new six-storey building (approximately 200 outlets per floor, 1,200 outlets total). A cabling contractor has been appointed. Describe the complete coordination process from project start to handover, including: (a) pre-qualification of the contractor; (b) scope of work preparation; (c) pre-installation meeting; (d) staged inspections during installation; (e) final acceptance and handover requirements. (20 marks)
