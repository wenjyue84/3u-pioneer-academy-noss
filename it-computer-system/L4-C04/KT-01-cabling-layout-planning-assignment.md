## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (10 marks)

**A1.** The maximum permanent link length for horizontal copper cabling under ANSI/TIA-568 is:

- (a) 100 m
- (b) 90 m
- (c) 70 m
- (d) 55 m

**A2.** Cat6A UTP cable is rated for 10 Gbps at a distance of:

- (a) 55 m
- (b) 70 m
- (c) 100 m
- (d) 150 m

**A3.** The standard that defines labelling conventions for telecommunications infrastructure is:

- (a) ANSI/TIA-568.2-D
- (b) ANSI/TIA-569-D
- (c) ANSI/TIA-606-B
- (d) ISO/IEC 11801

**A4.** In a structured cabling system, the Telecommunications Room (TR) connects to the work area via:

- (a) Backbone cabling
- (b) Horizontal cabling
- (c) Entrance facility cable
- (d) Equipment room cable

**A5.** Which cable type is most appropriate for a 400 m inter-building backbone link requiring 10 Gbps?

- (a) Cat6A UTP
- (b) Cat5e UTP
- (c) OM3 multimode fibre
- (d) OS2 single-mode fibre

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (20 marks)

**B1.** Name and briefly describe FOUR (4) of the six subsystems of a structured cabling system. (8 marks)

**B2.** A new office floor has 25 workstations (2 outlets each), 1 meeting room (6 outlets), and 1 server room (4 outlets). Calculate: (a) the minimum number of outlets; (b) the number of outlets after adding 15% spare capacity (round up to nearest 12). Show your working. (6 marks)

**B3.** State THREE (3) factors that must be considered when selecting cable category for a new installation. (6 marks)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (20 marks)

**C1.** You are designing the cabling layout for a new four-storey office building. Each floor has approximately 40 workstations. Describe the complete planning process you would follow, including: (a) site survey steps; (b) TR location decisions; (c) topology selection; (d) backbone design (between floors); (e) cable category selection. (20 marks)
