## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (10 marks)

**A1.** The T568B wiring standard assigns which colour to Pin 1?

- (a) White/Green
- (b) White/Orange
- (c) Orange
- (d) Blue

**A2.** The maximum pair untwist allowed at a Cat6 keystone jack termination is:

- (a) 25 mm
- (b) 13 mm
- (c) 6 mm
- (d) 50 mm

**A3.** Which label placement is required by TIA-606-B for horizontal cables?

- (a) One label at the midpoint of the cable
- (b) One label at the patch panel end only
- (c) One label within 150 mm of each termination point (both ends)
- (d) Label on the cable tray only

**A4.** What is the recommended maximum conduit fill ratio for cable installations?

- (a) 80%
- (b) 60%
- (c) 40%
- (d) 20%

**A5.** When terminating fibre optic connectors, the end-face should be inspected using:

- (a) The naked eye
- (b) A magnifying glass
- (c) A fibre microscope at 200–400× magnification
- (d) An OTDR only

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (20 marks)

**B1.** List the T568B pin-colour assignments for all 8 pins. (8 marks)

**B2.** Describe FOUR (4) quality checkpoints that must be performed during a cable installation, and state what is checked at each stage. (8 marks)

**B3.** State TWO (2) differences between the requirements for pulling copper Cat6A cable and pulling fibre optic cable. (4 marks)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (20 marks)

**C1.** During a Cat6A installation, an inspector finds the following issues: (i) several cables have been pulled through a conduit without labels; (ii) the pair untwist at three keystone jacks is estimated at 25 mm; (iii) cables in the cable tray are secured with plastic tie wraps that have been pulled very tight. For each issue, explain: (a) the standard or rule that has been violated; (b) the likely consequence if left uncorrected; (c) the corrective action required. (20 marks)
