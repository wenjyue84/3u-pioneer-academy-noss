## Objektif / Objective

Simulate the contractor coordination process: prepare a scope of work document, conduct a simulated pre-installation meeting, perform staged inspections using checklists, and issue a non-conformance report for identified deficiencies.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

3 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Scope of Work (SOW) template | 1 |
| 2 | Pre-installation meeting agenda template | 1 |
| 3 | Staged inspection checklist (material delivery, post-pull, post-termination, final) | 1 set |
| 4 | Non-Conformance Report (NCR) template | 1 |
| 5 | Simulated "contractor submission" package (material delivery docket + sample test report with deliberate errors) | 1 |
| 6 | Pen and notepad | 1 |
| 7 | Reference: ANSI/TIA-568.2-D extract (distance limits, wiring standards) | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- This is a simulation exercise using documents and role-play — no physical cabling tools are used
- Treat all simulated documents as if they were real project documents
- Record all decisions and findings in writing — verbal agreements are not accepted in professional practice

---

## Senario / Scenario

Your organisation is expanding to a new floor (Floor 3, Building B). You have been appointed as the IT administrator responsible for the network cabling project. The cabling contractor is **NetCable Sdn. Bhd.** The project scope: 30 Cat6A outlets (star topology), one telecommunications room (TR-B3-01), fibre backbone from TR-B3-01 to the equipment room (ER) in the basement.

The instructor will role-play as the contractor representative during the pre-installation meeting segment.

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Prepare the Scope of Work (SOW):** Using the SOW template, complete the following sections: (a) project description (30 Cat6A outlets, Building B Floor 3); (b) materials specification (Cat6A UTP, brand: approved manufacturer, OM4 fibre for backbone); (c) testing requirements (100% Cat6A channel certification; OTDR traces for all backbone fibres); (d) documentation deliverables (as-built drawings in 30 days; cable schedule; test reports); (e) warranty requirement (minimum 20-year system warranty). Submit the SOW to the instructor for review before proceeding. |
| 2 | **Prepare the Pre-Installation Meeting Agenda:** Using the meeting agenda template, list the agenda items for the pre-installation meeting. Minimum items: approve drawings, confirm material delivery schedule, identify access restrictions, agree working hours, review PTW procedure, establish escalation contacts, agree inspection milestones. |
| 3 | **Conduct the Pre-Installation Meeting (Role-Play):** The instructor plays the contractor representative. You (the IT administrator) chair the meeting using your agenda. Ask the contractor to confirm: (a) when materials will be delivered; (b) who the site supervisor will be; (c) how PTW will be obtained; (d) what their test equipment model is. Record all responses as meeting minutes. |
| 4 | **Material Delivery Inspection:** The instructor provides a simulated material delivery docket showing the materials the contractor has brought to site. Check the delivery docket against your SOW specification. Identify any non-conforming items (the instructor has inserted one deliberate substitution: Cat6 cable instead of Cat6A for 10 of the 30 runs). Record your finding. |
| 5 | **Issue a Non-Conformance Report (NCR):** Using the NCR template, prepare NCR-001 for the non-conforming Cat6 cable. State: (a) the specific non-conformance; (b) the SOW clause violated; (c) the required corrective action (replace all Cat6 with specified Cat6A); (d) the deadline for correction (2 working days); (e) your name and date. |
| 6 | **Post-Pull Inspection:** The instructor provides a simulated post-pull inspection scenario description (8 cables pulled; 2 cables do not have labels at the outlet end; 1 cable has a visible kink). Complete the post-pull inspection checklist, recording each finding. State what action you would require the contractor to take for each finding before termination proceeds. |
| 7 | **Post-Termination Inspection:** The instructor provides a simulated termination inspection scenario (one cable terminated with T568A at the patch panel but T568B at the outlet — a split standard error). Identify the fault. Describe the consequence (link will fail near-end crosstalk test). State the corrective action required. |
| 8 | **Final Acceptance Review:** Review the simulated final documentation package provided by the instructor. Check: (a) all test reports show PASS; (b) as-built drawings are present and correctly revised; (c) cable schedule is complete; (d) labels are listed as applied. Identify any missing items (the instructor has omitted the OTDR traces for the backbone fibre). State what action is required before final sign-off. |
| 9 | **Complete Meeting Minutes:** Write up the meeting minutes from the pre-installation meeting (Step 3). Include: date, attendees, agenda items, decisions made, action items with responsible parties and due dates. |
| 10 | Submit: completed SOW, meeting agenda, meeting minutes, NCR-001, post-pull and post-termination inspection checklists, and final acceptance review notes to the instructor. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

- Completed SOW document covering all required sections
- Pre-installation meeting agenda with minimum 7 items
- Meeting minutes recording all decisions and action items
- NCR-001 correctly identifying the Cat6 substitution and requiring corrective action
- Post-pull inspection checklist with findings documented and corrective actions stated
- Post-termination fault identified (split wiring standard) with corrective action
- Final acceptance review identifying missing OTDR traces and action required
