## Objektif / Objective

Pull, terminate, label, and certify a Cat6A horizontal cable run in the training lab, following TIA-568 termination (T568B) and TIA-606-B labelling standards.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

4 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Cat6A UTP cable (pre-cut to training run length, approx. 10–15 m) | 2 per trainee |
| 2 | Cat6A keystone jack (T568B colour-coded) | 4 |
| 3 | 24-port patch panel (Cat6A) | 1 (shared, 2 ports per trainee) |
| 4 | Single-gang wall outlet faceplate | 2 |
| 5 | 110-type punch-down tool with blade | 1 |
| 6 | Cable stripper (adjustable, non-contact) | 1 |
| 7 | Label printer (Brady or equivalent) with Cat6A cable labels | 1 (shared) |
| 8 | Cable certification tester (e.g. Fluke DSX-600 or equivalent) | 1 (shared) |
| 9 | Cable tie wraps (velcro) | 10 |
| 10 | Horizontal cable manager (1U, training rack) | 1 (shared) |
| 11 | TIA-606-B label convention reference card | 1 |
| 12 | Safety glasses | 1 pair |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Wear safety glasses during punch-down operations — small wire offcuts can spring into eyes
- Keep punch-down tool perpendicular to the jack; do not force at an angle
- Do not kink or bend Cat6A cable beyond its minimum bend radius (8× cable diameter)
- Ensure the training rack is stable before routing cables through the cable manager
- Dispose of all wire offcuts in a waste container — do not leave on the floor

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Receive two pre-cut Cat6A UTP cables from the instructor. Inspect each cable for jacket damage. Record the cable IDs assigned by the instructor (e.g. HC-LAB-01-001 and HC-LAB-01-002). |
| 2 | Print two labels per cable (four labels total) using the label printer. Label format: one label per cable end. Labels must show the Cable ID. Apply labels at both ends of each cable — within 150 mm of where the jacket will be stripped. |
| 3 | Route Cable 1 from the simulated outlet position (wall bracket) to the patch panel in the training rack, through the horizontal cable manager. Maintain the cable's natural twist; do not overbend at corners. Leave 1 m slack at the outlet end and 1.5 m slack at the patch panel end. |
| 4 | At the outlet end: strip 25 mm of jacket from Cable 1. Untwist each pair only as far as needed (maximum 13 mm). Terminate into a Cat6A keystone jack following T568B colour coding. Punch down using the 110-type tool; remove offcuts. Snap the jack into the faceplate. |
| 5 | At the patch panel end: strip 25 mm of jacket from Cable 1. Terminate into the assigned patch panel port following T568B colour coding. Punch down. Label the patch panel port with the cable ID. |
| 6 | Repeat steps 3–5 for Cable 2. |
| 7 | Dress the cables through the horizontal cable manager. Use velcro tie wraps to secure cables at 300 mm intervals along the cable tray. Ties must be firm but not crush the cable jacket. |
| 8 | Connect the cable certification tester (main unit at patch panel; remote at outlet). Set the tester to Cat6A channel test. Run the autotest for Cable 1. Record: PASS or FAIL; worst-case NEXT margin (dB); worst-case insertion loss margin (dB); length (m). |
| 9 | Run the autotest for Cable 2. Record results on the test results form. If any cable FAILS, inspect the termination for split pairs, excessive untwist, or incorrect colour assignment. Re-terminate and re-test. |
| 10 | Verify all labels are present and legible: both ends of each cable, both patch panel ports, both faceplates. Submit the completed test result form and checklist to the instructor. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

- Two Cat6A horizontal cables correctly pulled, terminated, and dressed in the training rack
- All terminations comply with T568B wiring standard
- Both cables pass Cat6A channel certification test with positive margin
- All cable ends, patch panel ports, and faceplates correctly labelled per TIA-606-B
- Patch panel ports dressed neatly through horizontal cable manager
