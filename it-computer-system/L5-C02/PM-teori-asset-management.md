## Kaedah Pengajaran / Teaching Method (4-Step)

### 1. PERSEDIAAN (Preparation)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity | Masa / Duration |
|----------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| 1.1 | Welcome trainees and take attendance | 5 min |
| 1.2 | State the learning objectives for the session and link to the relevant KP | 5 min |
| 1.3 | Contextualise the topic with a real-world organisational scenario or news item (e.g. government asset audit findings, software licence non-compliance penalty) | 10 min |
| 1.4 | Provide overview of the KP content structure and key terms for the session | 5 min |

### 2. PENYAMPAIAN (Presentation)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 2.1 | Deliver theory content from the relevant KP using slides, diagrams, worked examples, and case studies |
| 2.2 | Explain key terms and definitions; use bilingual labelling (English / Bahasa Malaysia) throughout |
| 2.3 | Show organisational examples: sample asset registers, depreciation schedules, ITAM system screenshots, disposal forms, and tag format specifications |
| 2.4 | Demonstrate common errors and their consequences through case study discussion |
| 2.5 | Highlight relevant legislation: MFRS 116, PDPA 2010, Environmental Quality Act 1974, Pekeliling Perbendaharaan |
| 2.6 | Encourage questions; correct misconceptions in real time |

### 3. PENGGUNAAN (Application)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 3.1 | Distribute the corresponding KT (Assignment Sheet) for the session |
| 3.2 | Trainees complete the assignment individually; MCQ and short answer in class, structured question as take-home or extended session |
| 3.3 | Instructor circulates to support and identify common errors |
| 3.4 | Review answers with the class; discuss common mistakes and correct reasoning |

### 4. PENGESAHAN (Confirmation)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 4.1 | Review the three to five key learning points from the session |
| 4.2 | Conduct brief oral questioning (two to three questions per trainee) to verify conceptual understanding |
| 4.3 | Collect completed KT for marking and feedback |
| 4.4 | Preview the next session topic; advise trainees of any pre-reading required |
| 4.5 | Record attendance and session completion in the training record |

| Session | Key Theory Topics | Legislation / Standard to Highlight |
|---------|------------------|--------------------------------------|
| KP(1/7) — Inventory | ITAM definition; asset categories; asset register fields; physical vs logical inventory; ghost/phantom assets; CMDB vs inventory | MFRS 116; ISO/IEC 19770-1 |

## Penilaian Teori / Theory Assessment

Upon completion of all 7 KP/KT sessions, trainees will sit for the Knowledge Assessment (KA):

- **Code:** IT-020-5:2013-C02/KA
- **Duration:** 1 hour 30 minutes
- **Format:** Written examination — Section A: 15 MCQ (30 marks); Section B: 6 short answer (30 marks); Section C: 2 essay questions (40 marks)
- **Pass mark:** 60 / 100 (60%)
- **Venue:** Examination hall (closed book)
