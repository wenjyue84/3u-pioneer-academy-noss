## Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks)

**A1.** Which of the following BEST defines IT Asset Management (ITAM)?

- (a) The process of purchasing new computer equipment
- (b) A discipline combining financial, contractual, and inventory functions to manage IT assets throughout their lifecycle
- (c) The installation and configuration of software applications
- (d) The daily monitoring of network performance

**A2.** A "ghost asset" in the context of an IT inventory audit refers to:

- (a) An asset recorded in the system but not physically found
- (b) A physical asset found but not recorded in the asset register
- (c) An asset that has been disposed of correctly
- (d) A software licence that has expired

**A3.** Which tool is MOST appropriate for performing an agentless network discovery scan to locate all connected IT assets?

- (a) Microsoft Word
- (b) Advanced IP Scanner or PRTG
- (c) Windows Defender
- (d) Adobe Acrobat

**A4.** The CMDB differs from the asset inventory primarily because the CMDB:

- (a) Only tracks hardware assets
- (b) Is maintained by the Finance department
- (c) Stores configuration items and their service relationships, not just financial asset data
- (d) Is updated only during annual audits

**A5.** Under which Malaysian financial reporting standard are tangible IT assets (property, plant and equipment) governed?

- (a) MFRS 9
- (b) MFRS 116
- (c) MFRS 15
- (d) MFRS 138

## Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (30 marks)

**B1.** List FIVE (5) mandatory fields that must be present in every IT asset record. (5 marks)

**B2.** Describe the difference between a physical inventory and a logical (automated) inventory. State ONE advantage and ONE limitation of each method. (8 marks)

**B3.** Explain what is meant by "reconciliation" in the context of an IT asset inventory audit. Describe the THREE types of discrepancy that must be resolved during reconciliation and the recommended action for each. (9 marks)

**B4.** State FOUR (4) categories of IT assets that must be included in a comprehensive enterprise asset inventory. Give one example of an asset in each category. (8 marks)

## Bahagian C: Soalan Berstruktur / Structured Question (50 marks)

**C1.** A large government agency in Malaysia has 850 IT assets distributed across three buildings. The IT department has not conducted a formal inventory audit for three years. The current asset register is maintained in an Excel spreadsheet, last updated 18 months ago.

(a) Identify FOUR (4) risks the agency faces as a result of the outdated asset register. For each risk, explain the potential consequence. (12 marks)

(b) As the newly appointed IT Asset Manager, describe the step-by-step plan you would follow to conduct a comprehensive asset inventory. Your plan must address: team and zone organisation, physical survey methodology, automated discovery, reconciliation process, and register update. (20 marks)

(c) After completing the inventory, you discover 47 assets in the physical survey that are not in the register (ghost assets) and 31 assets in the register that cannot be found (phantom assets). Explain how you would handle each category. What escalation is required for the phantom assets? (10 marks)

(d) Recommend THREE (3) process improvements to prevent the same situation from recurring. Justify each recommendation. (8 marks)
