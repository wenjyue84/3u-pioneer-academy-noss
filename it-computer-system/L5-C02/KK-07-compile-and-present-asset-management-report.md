## Objektif / Objective

Compile a comprehensive annual asset management report from a simulated dataset, calculate key ITAM KPIs, produce data visualisations, and present findings with strategic recommendations to a simulated management audience.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

5 hours (4 hours preparation + 1 hour presentation)

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Laptop or workstation with Microsoft Excel/LibreOffice Calc and PowerPoint/Impress | 1 |
| 2 | Simulated annual asset dataset (100 asset records in CSV) — provided by instructor | 1 |
| 3 | Simulated software licence data (10 products) — provided | 1 |
| 4 | Simulated financial data: purchase costs, depreciation rates, disposal proceeds — provided | 1 |
| 5 | Simulated repair records for the year — provided | 1 |
| 6 | Annual Asset Management Report template (provided) | 1 |
| 7 | KPI Calculation Worksheet (provided) | 1 |
| 8 | Projector or large monitor for presentation | 1 (shared) |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Save all work frequently; use version-labelled filenames (e.g. AnnualReport_v1_YourName.xlsx)
- Treat all asset and financial data as confidential
- Verify all calculations before including figures in the report; errors in management reports have governance consequences

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Import the 100-asset dataset into Excel/Calc. Create a pivot table showing the distribution of assets by: (a) category; (b) operational status; (c) department; (d) age group (0–2 years, 3–4 years, 5+ years). These pivot tables will form the basis of the Asset Portfolio Overview section. |
| 2 | Calculate the following KPIs using the KPI Calculation Worksheet formulas from KP-07 Section 5: Asset Register Accuracy, Tag Compliance Rate, Active Asset Utilisation, Idle Asset Rate, Mean Time to Repair (using repair records provided), Disposal Cycle Time, and Licence Compliance Rate. Show all working in the worksheet. |
| 3 | Using the financial data provided, prepare the annual depreciation summary: total depreciation charge for the year by asset category; total gross asset value; total accumulated depreciation; total net book value. Identify assets that are fully depreciated but still in use. |
| 4 | Prepare the software licence compliance table (KP-07 Section 7.1): for each of the 10 software products, record licences purchased, licences deployed (from discovery data provided), variance, and compliance status. Flag any non-compliant products. |
| 5 | Produce the following charts in Excel/Calc: (a) Pie chart — assets by operational status; (b) Stacked bar chart — assets by category and age group; (c) Line chart — monthly repair count trend (12 months); (d) Table with conditional formatting — licence compliance (red for non-compliant). |
| 6 | Compile the Annual Asset Management Report using the 11-section structure from KP-07 Section 4.1. Write the Executive Summary last, after all other sections are complete. Each finding in the Executive Summary must reference its supporting section. |
| 7 | Prepare a 10-minute management presentation (8–10 slides) covering: portfolio overview, key KPIs vs targets, top 3 findings, and 3 strategic recommendations. Use the charts from Step 5 in the slides. Apply the executive summary writing guidelines from KP-07 Section 6.2 — each finding must include impact and recommendation. |
| 8 | Deliver the presentation to the class (instructor and peers act as the management committee). Allow 5 minutes for questions. Respond to questions using evidence from the report data. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

1. **Pivot tables** — asset distribution by category, status, department, and age group
2. **KPI Calculation Worksheet** — all 9 KPIs calculated with working shown; comparison against targets
3. **Depreciation summary** — annual charge, gross value, accumulated depreciation, net book value by category
4. **Licence compliance table** — all 10 products; variance and compliance status; non-compliant items flagged
5. **Four charts** — pie, stacked bar, line, and conditional-format table; correctly labelled
6. **Annual Asset Management Report** — all 11 sections complete; executive summary self-contained and action-oriented
7. **Management presentation** — 8–10 slides; delivered within 10 minutes; questions answered with data
