## Objektif / Objective

Apply the standard operational status classification criteria to a sample asset population, update the asset register with classified statuses, and produce a status assessment report with management recommendations.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

4 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Laptop or workstation with ITAM system access | 1 |
| 2 | Simulated asset register with 30 asset records (provided by instructor) | 1 |
| 3 | Simulated technical assessment reports for selected assets | 1 set |
| 4 | Simulated repair quotations for assets in question | 1 set |
| 5 | Status Classification Criteria reference card (from KP-02) | 1 |
| 6 | Status Assessment Worksheet (provided) | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Handle all simulated asset records as confidential documents
- Do not alter records without applying the correct classification criteria
- Document all classification decisions with supporting evidence

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Receive the simulated asset register (30 records) from the instructor. Review all records. Note assets that already have an assigned status and those with missing or outdated status fields. |
| 2 | For each asset, review the available evidence: purchase date, last known location, last network scan date, repair history, and any attached technical reports or repair quotations. Record this evidence in the Status Assessment Worksheet. |
| 3 | Apply the classification criteria from KP-02 to assign a status to each asset. For each classification, record the primary criterion used (e.g. "Age ≥ 5 years" or "Repair cost > 50% replacement value"). If the evidence is insufficient to classify, flag the asset as "Requires Further Investigation" and note what additional information is needed. |
| 4 | Identify all assets classified as Idle. For each idle asset, assess whether it can be redeployed to meet a known organisational need. Document your redeployment recommendation (or the reason redeployment is not feasible). |
| 5 | Identify all assets classified as End of Life or Uneconomical to Repair. For each, confirm that at least two criteria are met (as per KP-02 Section 3.4). Prepare a disposal trigger memo for each confirmed End-of-Life asset. |
| 6 | Update the ITAM system (or the simulated register spreadsheet) with the new status for each asset. Record the date of assessment and your name in the "Last Assessed By" and "Last Assessed Date" fields. |
| 7 | Produce the Status Assessment Report using the structure in the Expected Outcome section. Include a summary table showing the before and after status distribution across the 30 assets. |
| 8 | Present findings to the instructor. Be prepared to justify each classification for the five assets the instructor selects for oral questioning. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A completed Status Assessment Report containing:

1. **Before/After Status Distribution Table** — count of assets per status before and after the assessment
2. **Classification Decision Log** — for each of the 30 assets: previous status, new status, primary criterion applied, evidence reference
3. **Idle Asset Redeployment Analysis** — for each idle asset: recommended action (redeploy to [department] / hold / dispose)
4. **End-of-Life Asset List** — with criteria satisfied, disposal trigger memo reference
5. **Assets Requiring Further Investigation** — with specific information gaps identified
6. **Recommendations** — process improvements for ongoing status management
