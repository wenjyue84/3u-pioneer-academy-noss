## Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks)

**A1.** Which of the following is NOT a component of Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)?

- (a) Annual electricity cost
- (b) Disposal and data destruction cost
- (c) The colour of the asset
- (d) Training cost for users

**A2.** A server was purchased for RM 25,000 with a residual value of RM 1,250 and a useful life of 5 years. Using straight-line depreciation, what is the annual depreciation charge?

- (a) RM 5,000
- (b) RM 4,750
- (c) RM 6,250
- (d) RM 5,750

**A3.** The reducing balance method of depreciation is most suitable for assets that:

- (a) Have a constant rate of value decline over their useful life
- (b) Lose value rapidly in early years (e.g. mobile devices)
- (c) Are non-depreciable (land)
- (d) Are fully funded by operating expenditure

**A4.** A standard 42U server rack has equipment installed requiring 28U. Applying a 20% spare capacity rule, what is the minimum rack capacity required?

- (a) 33.6U — one 42U rack is sufficient
- (b) 35U — one 42U rack is sufficient
- (c) 28U — exactly one 42U rack is sufficient with no spare
- (d) 56U — two 42U racks are required

**A5.** The payback period of an IT investment is calculated as:

- (a) TCO divided by the number of assets
- (b) TCO divided by the annual net benefit
- (c) Annual net benefit divided by TCO
- (d) Total benefits minus TCO

## Bahagian B: Soalan Pengiraan / Calculation Questions (40 marks)

**B1.** A government ministry is purchasing 50 desktop PCs. The following data applies:

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Unit price (per PC) | RM 2,800 |
| Delivery and setup per unit | RM 80 |
| Annual electricity cost per unit | RM 150 |
| Annual maintenance contract per unit | RM 100 |
| Useful life | 5 years |
| Residual value | RM 140 per unit |
| Disposal cost per unit | RM 30 |

(a) Calculate the TCO per unit over 5 years. (5 marks)

(b) Calculate the total TCO for all 50 units. (2 marks)

(c) Prepare the straight-line depreciation schedule (per unit) for all 5 years, showing opening book value, annual depreciation, accumulated depreciation, and closing book value. (10 marks)

**B2.** A technology company's laptop was purchased for RM 5,500. The company uses a reducing balance depreciation rate of 35% per annum. 

(a) Calculate the book value at the end of Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3. Show all working. (6 marks)

(b) In Year 3, the laptop requires a repair costing RM 1,800. Should the company repair the laptop or classify it as uneconomical to repair? Use the 50% threshold rule. Show your working and justify your recommendation. (5 marks)

**B3.** A data centre expansion project requires the following equipment in one server rack:

| Equipment | Units | Rack Height per Unit |
|-----------|-------|---------------------|
| 2U rack server | 8 | 2U |
| 1U network switch | 2 | 1U |
| 1U patch panel | 3 | 1U |
| 1U KVM switch | 1 | 1U |
| 2U cable management | 2 | 2U |

(a) Calculate the total rack units required for all equipment. (3 marks)

(b) Applying a 20% spare capacity rule, determine the minimum total rack capacity required and whether one or two standard 42U racks are needed. (4 marks)

(c) Each server draws 350W. The two switches draw 80W each. Calculate the total power load for the rack. If the UPS must have 25% headroom, what is the minimum UPS capacity in VA? (Assume power factor = 0.9) (5 marks)

## Bahagian C: Soalan Berstruktur / Structured Question (40 marks)

**C1.** Your organisation plans to replace 30 ageing laptops used by field engineers. The current laptops (5 years old, fully depreciated) have an estimated residual value of RM 200 each if sold. Two vendors have submitted quotations:

**Vendor A:** RM 4,500 per unit; 2-year warranty; delivery included; annual support contract RM 180/unit  
**Vendor B:** RM 3,900 per unit; 1-year warranty; delivery RM 50/unit; no support contract available

Additional information:
- Labour rate for setup: RM 60/hour; estimated 1.5 hours per unit
- Annual electricity cost: RM 120/unit
- Useful life: 4 years; residual value: 5% of purchase price
- Disposal cost: RM 40/unit
- The 30 current laptops will be sold at residual value (RM 200 each) before the new ones arrive

(a) Calculate the full 4-year TCO per unit for both vendors. Include all relevant cost components. (16 marks)

(b) Identify TWO factors beyond TCO that should influence the vendor selection decision. Explain how each factor affects the decision. (8 marks)

(c) The Finance department asks for a simple Cost-Benefit Analysis. The new laptops are expected to save each engineer 1 hour per day due to improved performance. There are 30 engineers; average hourly cost (salary + overhead) is RM 45/hour; working days per year = 240.

Calculate: (i) Total annual productivity benefit; (ii) Net benefit over 4 years (benefits minus TCO, total for 30 units); (iii) Payback period in months; (iv) ROI. (16 marks)
