## Objektif / Objective

Calculate the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) and depreciation schedule for a set of IT assets, estimate the physical space requirements for a proposed IT expansion, and prepare a cost-benefit analysis to justify a procurement recommendation.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

5 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Laptop or workstation with spreadsheet software (Microsoft Excel or LibreOffice Calc) | 1 |
| 2 | Simulated procurement scenario brief (provided by instructor) | 1 |
| 3 | Vendor quotations (simulated, 3 vendors) | 1 set |
| 4 | Current asset register extract (simulated) | 1 |
| 5 | Floor plan of the target area (simulated, to scale) | 1 |
| 6 | TCO and CBA calculation template (provided) | 1 |
| 7 | Scientific calculator | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Verify all calculations before submitting; arithmetic errors in procurement documents have financial consequences
- Treat all cost and pricing information as commercially confidential
- Save work frequently; label all spreadsheet files with your name and the date

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Read the procurement scenario brief provided by the instructor. The scenario specifies: the number and type of assets to be procured, the intended users, the current assets being replaced, and the proposed useful life. Identify all cost components that must be included in the TCO calculation. |
| 2 | Review the three vendor quotations. For each vendor, extract: unit price, warranty terms, delivery cost, and any included support services. Create a vendor comparison table in the spreadsheet. |
| 3 | Calculate the full TCO for the recommended vendor option using the formula from KP-03. Include: acquisition cost, deployment and configuration cost (estimate 2 hours labour per unit at the provided labour rate), annual electricity cost (use the wattage specification and local tariff rate provided), annual maintenance cost, and estimated disposal cost. |
| 4 | Prepare the straight-line depreciation schedule for the proposed assets over their full useful life. Create a table showing: opening book value, annual depreciation charge, accumulated depreciation, and closing book value for each year. |
| 5 | Using the floor plan provided, estimate the physical space required to deploy all new assets. Apply the space allowances from KP-03 (Section 3.1). Verify that the total space required does not exceed the available area. If it does, propose a layout adjustment or a phased deployment plan. |
| 6 | If any of the new assets are servers, calculate the rack space required (in U). Determine the number of racks needed, including 20% spare capacity. Estimate the power density (kW/rack) and verify it is within the room's power capacity (provided in the scenario brief). |
| 7 | Prepare a Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA) for the procurement. Quantify at least three benefits (e.g. productivity gain, reduced downtime, energy savings). Calculate the net benefit, payback period, and ROI using the formulas in KP-03 Section 4.2. |
| 8 | Compile the complete Cost and Space Estimate Report. Present the recommendation to the instructor, including the preferred vendor, the TCO, the CBA result, and the space plan. Be prepared to defend your calculations. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A completed Cost and Space Estimate Report containing:

1. **Vendor Comparison Table** — price, warranty, support, delivery for all three vendors
2. **TCO Calculation** — itemised for the recommended vendor; 5-year total
3. **Depreciation Schedule** — year-by-year table for all new assets
4. **Space Plan** — annotated floor plan showing placement of new assets; available vs required space
5. **Rack Plan** (if applicable) — rack layout diagram; U-count; power density
6. **Cost-Benefit Analysis** — benefits quantified; net benefit; payback period; ROI
7. **Procurement Recommendation** — recommended vendor with justification
