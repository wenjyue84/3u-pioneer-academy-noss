## BAHAGIAN A: SOALAN PELBAGAI PILIHAN / SECTION A: MULTIPLE CHOICE (30 marks)

Answer all 15 questions. Each question carries 2 marks. Circle the correct answer.

**1.** IT Asset Management (ITAM) combines which three functions?

- (a) Hardware, software, and networking
- (b) Financial, contractual, and inventory functions
- (c) Procurement, installation, and maintenance
- (d) Human resources, finance, and operations

**2.** An asset found during a physical inventory that does not exist in the asset register is called a:

- (a) Phantom asset
- (b) Ghost asset
- (c) Idle asset
- (d) Reserved asset

**3.** Which Malaysian financial reporting standard governs the depreciation of tangible IT assets (property, plant and equipment)?

- (a) MFRS 9
- (b) MFRS 15
- (c) MFRS 116
- (d) MFRS 138

**4.** A desktop PC was purchased for RM 3,600. Its residual value is RM 180 and useful life is 4 years. Using straight-line depreciation, the annual depreciation charge is:

- (a) RM 900
- (b) RM 855
- (c) RM 720
- (d) RM 1,044

**5.** The CMDB (Configuration Management Database) differs from the ITAM asset inventory primarily because the CMDB:

- (a) Only stores financial data about assets
- (b) Stores configuration items and their service dependency relationships
- (c) Is maintained by the Finance department
- (d) Is only updated during annual audits

**6.** Which operational status is assigned to an asset that is functional but currently unassigned and held in the IT storeroom?

- (a) Active
- (b) In Repair
- (c) Idle / Unassigned
- (d) Pending Disposal

**7.** Before an IT asset can be classified as "End of Life / Obsolete", the classification criteria require:

- (a) Only one criterion to be met
- (b) At least two criteria to be met
- (c) All available criteria to be met
- (d) Management approval only — no criteria needed

**8.** Which NIST publication provides the standard guidelines for media sanitisation, defining Clear, Purge, and Destroy methods?

- (a) NIST SP 800-53
- (b) NIST SP 800-88 Rev.1
- (c) NIST SP 800-171
- (d) NIST SP 800-30

**9.** ATA Secure Erase is the recommended sanitisation method for SSDs because:

- (a) It physically shreds the SSD platters
- (b) It uses the drive's own firmware to erase all storage cells, including areas inaccessible to software overwrite tools
- (c) It applies a strong magnetic field to destroy stored data
- (d) It requires no special tools and can be performed in Windows

**10.** Which of the following is an open-source ITAM platform that supports asset tracking, licence management, and a REST API?

- (a) ServiceNow ITAM
- (b) ManageEngine AssetExplorer
- (c) Snipe-IT
- (d) IBM Maximo

**11.** Under Malaysian law, which Act creates liability for an organisation that disposes of IT assets containing personal data without secure data destruction?

- (a) Companies Act 2016
- (b) Environmental Quality Act 1974
- (c) Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)
- (d) Communications and Multimedia Act 1998

**12.** The standard asset tag placement for a laptop is:

- (a) Inside the battery compartment
- (b) On the screen bezel
- (c) On the bottom panel, lower right corner
- (d) On the power adapter

**13.** An organisation has 480 non-disposed assets. Of these, 408 are Active, 48 are Idle, 16 are In Repair, and 8 are Pending Disposal. What is the Active Asset Utilisation rate?

- (a) 85.0%
- (b) 87.5%
- (c) 89.2%
- (d) 90.0%

**14.** The licence compliance report shows AutoCAD LT has 30 licences purchased and 36 deployed. This means:

- (a) The organisation is under-utilising its licences
- (b) The organisation is over-deployed by 6 licences and is non-compliant
- (c) The organisation has 6 spare licences to allocate
- (d) No action is required as the variance is within 20%

**15.** Which section of the Annual Asset Management Report must be self-contained, action-oriented, and suitable for reading without reference to the full document?

- (a) Section 7 — Asset Disposal Report
- (b) Section 1 — Executive Summary
- (c) Section 9 — Physical Audit Results
- (d) Section 11 — Appendices

## BAHAGIAN B: SOALAN JAWAPAN PENDEK / SECTION B: SHORT ANSWER (30 marks)

Answer all 6 questions.

**16.** Define "Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)" and list FOUR (4) cost components that must be included beyond the initial purchase price. (5 marks)

**17.** An organisation uses the reducing balance method at 40% per annum for a tablet purchased at RM 2,400. Calculate the book value at the end of Year 1 and Year 2. Show your working. (4 marks)

**18.** List FIVE (5) standard operational status categories used in IT asset management. For each, write a one-sentence definition. (5 marks)

**19.** Describe the difference between agent-based and agentless asset discovery. State ONE advantage of each. (4 marks)

**20.** Explain the purpose of a Data Destruction Certificate. State FOUR (4) mandatory fields it must contain. (6 marks)

## BAHAGIAN C: SOALAN ESEI / SECTION C: ESSAY (40 marks)

Answer all 2 questions.

**22.** A state government department in Malaysia has 1,500 IT assets. The IT Asset Manager has been tasked with conducting the annual review of the asset portfolio and preparing the Annual Asset Management Report for the State Director.

(a) Describe the full process the IT Asset Manager must follow to conduct a physical and logical asset inventory. Include: team preparation, zone assignment, physical survey, automated discovery, and reconciliation. (10 marks)

(b) After the inventory, the following data is compiled:
- 980 Active, 210 Idle, 85 In Repair, 110 End of Life (awaiting disposal), 115 Disposed during the year
- 42 ghost assets discovered; 28 phantom assets discovered
- Software: Oracle Database has 15 licences purchased but 22 deployed
- Mean Time to Repair (MTTR): 9.2 days (target ≤ 5 days)

For each of the four issues highlighted (ghost assets, phantom assets, Oracle licence non-compliance, MTTR above target), write a Finding-Impact-Recommendation statement as it would appear in the Executive Summary. (16 marks)

(c) The 110 End-of-Life assets include 40 servers that contain confidential government data and 70 desktop PCs. Describe the complete disposal process for each category, including: disposal trigger evidence, data destruction method and standard, disposal method, authorisation required, and documentation produced. (14 marks)

**23.** You are the IT Asset Manager at a private hospital with 600 IT assets. The hospital is implementing a new ITAM software platform to replace its current Excel-based register.

(a) Define the functional requirements for the ITAM system. List FIVE (5) key requirements and explain why each is important in a healthcare environment. (10 marks)

(b) You have shortlisted Snipe-IT (open-source) and ManageEngine AssetExplorer (commercial). Describe the evaluation process you would follow. Include: requirements weighting, Proof of Concept (PoC) methodology, scoring, and how the final recommendation would be presented to management. (16 marks)

(c) The hospital's Finance Director is concerned that the ITAM report shows different asset values from the financial fixed asset register. Explain THREE (3) reasons why these figures may differ and describe the reconciliation process that should be established between the ITAM function and the Finance department. (14 marks)
