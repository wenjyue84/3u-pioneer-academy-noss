## Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks)

**A1.** Under Malaysian law, which Act classifies CRT monitors and lithium-ion batteries as scheduled waste that must be disposed of through a licensed contractor?

- (a) Personal Data Protection Act 2010
- (b) Communications and Multimedia Act 1998
- (c) Environmental Quality Act 1974 and Scheduled Wastes Regulations 2005
- (d) Companies Act 2016

**A2.** According to NIST SP 800-88, which data destruction method is appropriate for a hard disk drive that stored "Confidential" data and is being donated to a school?

- (a) Clear (single overwrite)
- (b) Purge (multi-pass overwrite or degaussing)
- (c) Simply deleting files and emptying the recycle bin
- (d) Reformatting the drive using the operating system's format command

**A3.** ATA Secure Erase is the preferred method for sanitising SSDs because:

- (a) It physically destroys the SSD
- (b) It uses the drive's own firmware to erase all storage cells, including areas inaccessible to overwrite tools
- (c) It is faster than any other method and works on all storage types
- (d) It does not require any software or tools

**A4.** Before an asset can be formally listed on the Disposal Approval Form for a Malaysian public sector organisation, which body must provide approval?

- (a) The IT help desk
- (b) The Lembaga Pelupusan Aset (Asset Disposal Board) or equivalent authorising committee
- (c) The asset's original vendor
- (d) The Department of Environment (DOE)

**A5.** A laptop scheduled for donation to an NGO contains HR records and financial data classified as "Confidential". Which sequence of actions is correct?

- (a) Donate first; advise the NGO to format the drive
- (b) Obtain disposal approval → Purge the drive (multi-pass overwrite or degaussing) → Obtain Data Destruction Certificate → Donate
- (c) Remove the hard drive and keep it; donate the rest of the laptop
- (d) Delete all files → Uninstall all software → Donate

**B1.** List FIVE (5) triggers that cause an asset to enter the disposal workflow. For each trigger, state the evidence that must be documented. (10 marks)

**B2.** Describe FOUR (4) approved methods of IT asset disposal. For each method, state the type of asset it is most suitable for and one key requirement or limitation. (12 marks)

**B3.** Explain the purpose of a Data Destruction Certificate. List SIX (6) fields that must appear in this document. (8 marks)

## Bahagian C: Soalan Berstruktur / Structured Question (50 marks)

**C1.** A private hospital in Malaysia is disposing of 25 desktop PCs (5 years old), 10 CRT monitors (8 years old), and 5 servers (7 years old). The servers contain patient health records. You are the IT Asset Manager responsible for managing this disposal.

(a) For each asset category (desktop PCs, CRT monitors, servers), recommend the most appropriate disposal method. Justify your recommendation with reference to the asset type, data sensitivity, and applicable Malaysian regulations. (15 marks)

(b) Describe in detail the data destruction process you would apply to the servers. Include: data sensitivity classification, destruction method, standard applied, tools used, documentation required, and who must witness and certify the destruction. Explain why the standard overwrite method used for HDDs may not be sufficient for SSDs. (20 marks)

(c) After the disposal is completed, you discover that one of the 25 desktop PCs was disposed of without going through the formal approval process. The IT technician claims he "threw it away because it was broken". Describe: (i) the compliance failures that occurred; (ii) the risk exposure this creates for the organisation; (iii) the corrective and preventive actions the organisation must take. (15 marks)
