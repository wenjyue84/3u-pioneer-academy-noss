## Kaedah Pengajaran / Teaching Method (4-Step)

### 1. PERSEDIAAN (Preparation)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity | Masa / Duration |
|----------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| 1.1 | Welcome trainees; take attendance; verify workstation readiness | 5 min |
| 1.2 | State the practical learning objectives and link to the corresponding KK | 5 min |
| 1.3 | Review safety and compliance rules for the session (data handling, software tools, label printer operation) | 5 min |
| 1.4 | Distribute the KK (Work Sheet) and explain the task structure, expected outputs, and assessment criteria | 5 min |
| 1.5 | Verify all required equipment, software, and simulated documents are available at each workstation | 5 min |

### 2. PENYAMPAIAN (Demonstration)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 2.1 | Demonstrate the full practical task step-by-step as per the KK procedures (instructor or video demonstration) |
| 2.2 | Highlight critical steps: verifying drive serial number before data destruction; checking tag placement guide before affixing; validating CSV data before import |
| 2.3 | Show common errors and their consequences: wrong drive selected for wipe; tag placed over ventilation; depreciation formula applied incorrectly |
| 2.4 | Demonstrate the ITAM system workflows (Snipe-IT or GLPI): creating assets, importing CSV, generating reports, recording status changes |
| 2.5 | Answer trainee questions before independent practice begins |

### 3. PENGGUNAAN (Practice)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 3.1 | Trainees perform the practical task independently at their workstations following the KK procedures |
| 3.2 | Instructor circulates continuously to observe technique, identify errors, and provide guidance without completing tasks for the trainee |
| 3.3 | Conduct brief oral questioning during practice to verify conceptual understanding of decisions being made |
| 3.4 | Trainees complete the KK assessment checklist as each step is completed |

### 4. PENGESAHAN (Confirmation)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 4.1 | Instructor reviews each trainee's completed outputs against the KK assessment checklist |
| 4.2 | Verify key outputs: ITAM records created correctly; labels scannable and correctly placed; destruction certificates complete; reports structured correctly |
| 4.3 | Provide feedback on process, output quality, compliance, and efficiency |
| 4.4 | Trainees save and organise all digital outputs; clean workstation; return physical materials |
| 4.5 | Record session completion, trainee performance observations, and any issues in the training record |

## Keselamatan dan Pematuhan / Safety and Compliance Requirements

- **Data destruction:** Always verify drive serial number against the job card before initiating any wipe or secure erase. A wipe on the wrong drive causes irreversible data loss.
- **PDPA compliance:** All simulated data must be treated as real personal data for practice purposes. Do not share or print outside the session.
- **E-waste:** Disposal of actual IT equipment (even training units at end-of-life) must be through a DOE-licensed contractor. Do not place IT equipment in general waste.
- **Label printer:** Do not adjust mechanical settings without instructor approval. Clean the printer head after each session.
- **ITAM system:** Log out after every session. Do not delete records created by other trainees. Report unexpected system errors to the instructor.

## Penilaian Amali / Practical Assessment

Upon completion of all 7 KK sessions, trainees will sit for the Performance Assessment (PA):

- **Code:** IT-020-5:2013-C02/PA
- **Duration:** 4 hours
- **Format:** Integrated practical examination — six tasks covering inventory reconciliation, status classification, TCO/CBA, asset tagging, data destruction, and management reporting
- **Pass mark:** 60 / 100 (60%)
- **Assessment criteria:** Process (30%), Output (30%), Attitude (15%), Safety and Compliance (15%), Environmental (10%)
