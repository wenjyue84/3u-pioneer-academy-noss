## Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 4 marks each)

**A1.** A Goods Received Note (GRN) should be signed:

- (a) Before the delivery truck arrives
- (b) After physical counting and condition checking of delivered items
- (c) After the vendor issues an invoice
- (d) After the Acceptance Certificate is signed

**A2.** During delivery inspection, you discover that 3 units out of 20 have damaged packaging. The correct action is to:

- (a) Sign the GRN and note the damage later
- (b) Reject all 20 units
- (c) Photograph the damage, note it on the GRN, and issue a Discrepancy Note for the 3 damaged units
- (d) Accept all units and claim warranty later

**A3.** The Acceptance Certificate is significant because it:

- (a) Replaces the Purchase Order
- (b) Triggers payment processing and marks the start of the warranty period
- (c) Cancels the vendor's registration
- (d) Is used to record the disposal of old assets

**A4.** A purchase contract states an LD rate of 0.5% per week. If the contract value is RM 100,000 and delivery is 3 weeks late, the LD amount is:

- (a) RM 500
- (b) RM 1,500
- (c) RM 5,000
- (d) RM 15,000

**A5.** For government agencies, ICT assets above a defined value threshold must be registered in:

- (a) ePerolehan portal
- (b) Sistem Pengurusan Aset dan Stor (SPAS)
- (c) MySPIKE system
- (d) MAMPU asset ledger

## Bahagian C: Soalan Berstruktur / Structured Question (50 marks)

**C1.** Read the following scenario and answer all parts.

> **Scenario:** You are the IT administrator at a state government department. A Purchase Order was issued to Vendor ABC Sdn Bhd for 20 desktop PCs at RM 4,500 each (total RM 90,000). The required delivery date was 15 June 2026. The vendor delivered on 22 June 2026 — 7 days (1 week) late. Upon inspection:
> - 18 units match the specification exactly
> - 2 units have scratched casings but are otherwise functional
> - 1 unit powers on but the Device Manager shows 2 unknown devices
> - All 20 units have serial number labels attached
> - The vendor has not yet provided warranty registration confirmation
>
> The contract includes an LD clause of 0.5% per week of delay, maximum 10%.

**(a)** Calculate the Liquidated Damages (LD) amount applicable to this delivery. Show your working clearly. (8 marks)

**(b)** Explain how you would handle the 2 units with scratched casings. State what documents you would complete and what decision you would make regarding acceptance. (10 marks)

**(c)** For the 1 unit with unknown devices in Device Manager, describe the Functional Acceptance Test steps you would perform to diagnose the issue and determine whether to accept or reject the unit. (12 marks)

**(d)** The vendor has not provided warranty registration confirmation. Explain the risk this poses and describe TWO (2) actions you would take to resolve this before closing the procurement. (10 marks)

**(e)** Upon final acceptance, describe what entries you would make in the ICT Asset Register for the 20 accepted units. List all fields required. (10 marks)
