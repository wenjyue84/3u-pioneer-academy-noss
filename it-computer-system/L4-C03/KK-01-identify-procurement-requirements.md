**Tujuan / Objective:** Conduct a structured ICT procurement needs assessment for a given organisational scenario and produce a complete Needs Assessment Report.

**Arahan:** Pelatih dikehendaki melaksanakan tugasan/kerja mengikut prosedur yang ditetapkan dalam kertas ini. Rujuk pensyarah sekiranya perlu penjelasan lanjut.

---

## Objektif / Objective

Complete a procurement needs assessment for the scenario provided, determine the appropriate procurement method, and produce a Needs Assessment Report for instructor review.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

3 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Organisational scenario brief (provided by instructor) | 1 |
| 2 | ICT asset inventory list (simulated) | 1 |
| 3 | Needs Assessment Report template | 1 |
| 4 | Current Arahan Perbendaharaan procurement threshold reference card | 1 |
| 5 | Computer with word processing software | 1 |
| 6 | Pen and notepad | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Handle all scenario documents and templates as confidential organisational records
- Do not share your assessment with other trainees during the exercise
- Save your work regularly; back up to a USB drive or shared drive as directed by instructor

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Read the organisational scenario brief provided by the instructor. Identify the department requesting the procurement, the business justification, the number of users affected, and the required operational timeline. |
| 2 | Review the simulated ICT asset inventory list. Identify which existing assets can be retained, which require upgrading, and which must be fully replaced. Record your findings in the Needs Assessment Report template under "Current State." |
| 3 | Translate the business needs into functional requirements. Categorise requirements into: hardware, software, network, and services. Record a minimum of 3 items per category. |
| 4 | For each hardware item, define at least 3 measurable technical parameters (e.g. processor generation, RAM capacity, storage speed). Avoid brand names — use performance specifications. |
| 5 | Estimate the total procurement value based on the instructor-provided price reference list. Determine the appropriate procurement method (direct purchase / quotation / limited tender / open tender) based on the estimated value and current AP thresholds. |
| 6 | Identify any policy compliance requirements applicable to this procurement (e.g. MOF registration, Bumiputera participation, Energy Star, PDPA). Document each requirement and its source. |
| 7 | Complete the Needs Assessment Report template in full, including: business justification, current state, functional requirements, non-functional requirements, estimated budget, recommended procurement method, and your name as the preparer. |
| 8 | Submit the completed Needs Assessment Report to the instructor for review and assessment checklist sign-off. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A completed Needs Assessment Report containing:
- Clear business justification tied to organisational need
- Current state summary with asset retention / replacement decisions
- Minimum 12 functional requirements across 4 categories
- Measurable, technology-neutral technical parameters
- Correct procurement method selected with AP threshold justification
- Policy compliance requirements identified
- Document properly formatted, signed, and dated
