## Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 4 marks each)

**A1.** In a structured procurement evaluation, technical evaluation should be completed:

- (a) After price evaluation
- (b) Before price evaluation
- (c) At the same time as price evaluation
- (d) By a different committee than price evaluation

**A2.** A vendor submits a quotation that offers a Core i5 processor when the specification requires Core i7. This submission is classified as:

- (a) Technically compliant
- (b) Technically non-compliant on a mandatory item
- (c) Commercially non-compliant
- (d) Administratively disqualified

**A3.** The price scoring formula "Lowest Price ÷ Vendor Price × Maximum Price Marks" will always award the maximum price score to:

- (a) The most expensive vendor
- (b) The vendor with the best technical score
- (c) The vendor with the lowest price
- (d) The vendor with the most experience

**A4.** Under the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission Act 2009, an evaluation committee member who accepts a gift from a vendor during the evaluation period may be liable for:

- (a) A civil fine only
- (b) Criminal prosecution for corruption
- (c) A warning letter only
- (d) Disqualification from future committees

**A5.** Which of the following is a method of verifying a vendor's MOF registration status in Malaysia?

- (a) Calling the vendor's sales representative
- (b) Checking the ePerolehan portal (Kementerian Kewangan)
- (c) Reviewing the vendor's company website
- (d) Asking the vendor to provide a self-declaration letter

## Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (30 marks)

**B1.** List FIVE (5) pre-evaluation administrative checks that must be performed on each quotation submission before technical evaluation begins. (10 marks)

**B2.** Explain the purpose of a Conflict of Interest Declaration in a procurement evaluation. State TWO (2) actions a committee member must take if a conflict of interest is identified. (8 marks)

**B3.** A tender evaluation uses the following weightages: Technical 60%, Price 30%, Due Diligence 10%. Vendor X scores 50/60 technical, RM 120,000 price (lowest is RM 110,000), and 8/10 due diligence. Calculate Vendor X's combined score. Show your working. (12 marks)

## Bahagian C: Soalan Berstruktur / Structured Question (50 marks)

**C1.** Study the following quotation data and answer all parts.

> **Procurement:** 15 desktop PCs  
> **Specification requirements:** Core i7 Gen 13, 16 GB DDR5 RAM, 512 GB NVMe Gen 4 SSD, 3-year on-site warranty, Energy Star 8.0 certified  
> **Evaluation weightage:** Technical 60%, Price 30%, Vendor Experience 10%

| Item | Vendor Alpha | Vendor Beta | Vendor Gamma |
|------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| Processor | Core i7-1370P | Core i5-1340P | Core i7-13700 |
| RAM | 16 GB DDR5 | 16 GB DDR4 | 16 GB DDR5 |
| Storage | 512 GB NVMe Gen 4 | 512 GB NVMe Gen 3 | 256 GB NVMe Gen 4 |
| Warranty | 3 yr on-site | 3 yr carry-in | 3 yr on-site |
| Energy Star | 8.0 certified | Not certified | 8.0 certified |
| Unit price | RM 4,800 | RM 4,200 | RM 4,600 |
| References provided | 3 references | 1 reference | 2 references |

**(a)** Complete a Compliance Matrix for the five mandatory specification items. For each vendor, mark each item as Compliant (✓) or Non-compliant (✗). (15 marks)

**(b)** Based on the compliance matrix in (a), which vendor(s) — if any — should be considered for disqualification? Justify your answer with reference to the specific non-compliant items. (10 marks)

**(c)** Calculate the total price for each vendor (15 units). Apply the price scoring formula (maximum 30 marks) for technically compliant vendors only. Show your working. (10 marks)

**(d)** Write a recommendation paragraph for the Evaluation Report. Recommend which vendor should be awarded the contract, referencing their technical compliance, price score, and vendor experience. (15 marks)
