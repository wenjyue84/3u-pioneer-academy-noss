## Objektif Pembelajaran / Learning Objectives

Upon completion of this Information Sheet, trainees shall be able to:

1. Apply a structured technical and commercial evaluation methodology to received quotations or tenders
2. Conduct vendor due diligence including financial capacity, technical capability, and compliance status
3. Prepare a comparative evaluation report and recommendation for award
4. Identify and manage conflicts of interest and ethical obligations during evaluation

---

## 1.0 Overview of the Evaluation Process

The evaluation of quotations and vendor proposals is a critical governance step. Incorrect or biased evaluation can result in:
- Procurement of non-compliant equipment
- Financial loss from overpriced or poor-quality goods
- Legal liability and audit findings
- Reputational damage to the organisation

The evaluation process must be:
- **Objective** — based on pre-defined criteria, not personal preference
- **Documented** — all decisions and scores recorded with justification
- **Transparent** — evaluation committee composition disclosed; no undisclosed conflicts of interest
- **Consistent** — same criteria applied to all quotations received

---

## 2.0 Pre-Evaluation Checks

Before technical evaluation begins, perform the following administrative checks on each submission:

| Check | Pass Condition |
|-------|---------------|
| Submission received before closing date and time | Yes |
| Submission is complete (all required documents included) | Yes |
| Vendor is registered with MOF (for government procurement) | Yes |
| No conflict of interest declared | Confirmed by committee members |
| Quotation is on vendor's official letterhead with authorised signature | Yes |

Submissions that fail mandatory administrative checks are typically disqualified (non-responsive) before technical evaluation proceeds.

---

## 3.0 Technical Evaluation

Technical evaluation assesses whether each vendor's proposed solution meets the minimum technical requirements stated in the specifications.

### 3.1 Compliance Matrix

A compliance matrix (matriks pematuhan) lists every specification item and records whether each vendor's submission is compliant, partially compliant, or non-compliant:

| Spec Item | Required | Vendor A | Vendor B | Vendor C |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Processor ≥ Core i7 Gen 13 | Core i7-1370P | ✓ Compliant | ✗ Non-compliant (i5 offered) | ✓ Compliant |
| RAM ≥ 16 GB DDR5 | 16 GB DDR5 | ✓ Compliant | ✓ Compliant | ✓ Compliant |
| Storage ≥ 512 GB NVMe | 512 GB NVMe Gen 4 | ✓ Compliant | ✓ Compliant | ✗ Non-compliant (SATA SSD offered) |
| Warranty ≥ 3 years on-site | 3 yr on-site | ✓ Compliant | ✓ Compliant | ✓ Compliant |
| Energy Star 8.0 | Energy Star 8.0 cert | ✓ Compliant | ✗ Not certified | ✓ Compliant |

Vendors with any **mandatory** non-compliances may be disqualified. Evaluators must document the disqualification rationale.

### 3.2 Technical Scoring (for weighted evaluation)

Where a scoring matrix is used, technical items are assigned scores:

| Criterion | Max Score | Vendor A | Vendor B | Vendor C |
|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Technical compliance (mandatory items) | 40 | 40 | 30* | 35* |
| Technical superiority (exceeds spec) | 10 | 8 | 5 | 6 |
| Warranty / support terms | 10 | 9 | 7 | 8 |
| **Technical subtotal** | **60** | **57** | **42** | **49** |

*Vendors with mandatory non-compliances may receive reduced scores or be disqualified.

---

## 4.0 Commercial / Price Evaluation

Price evaluation must only be performed on technically compliant submissions (or those meeting the minimum technical pass threshold).

### 4.1 Price Comparison Table

| Item | Qty | Vendor A Unit Price | Vendor A Total | Vendor B Unit Price | Vendor B Total | Vendor C Unit Price | Vendor C Total |
|------|-----|---------------------|----------------|---------------------|----------------|---------------------|----------------|
| Desktop PC | 20 | RM 4,500 | RM 90,000 | RM 4,200 | RM 84,000 | RM 4,800 | RM 96,000 |
| 24" Monitor | 20 | RM 750 | RM 15,000 | RM 700 | RM 14,000 | RM 800 | RM 16,000 |
| Delivery & installation | — | RM 2,000 | RM 2,000 | RM 1,500 | RM 1,500 | RM 2,500 | RM 2,500 |
| **Grand Total** | | | **RM 107,000** | | **RM 99,500** | | **RM 114,500** |

### 4.2 Price Scoring

Using a ratio formula (lowest price receives maximum score):

Price Score = (Lowest Price / Vendor Price) × Maximum Price Marks

| Vendor | Grand Total | Price Score (max 30) |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| Vendor A | RM 107,000 | (99,500/107,000) × 30 = 27.9 |
| Vendor B | RM 99,500 | 30.0 |
| Vendor C | RM 114,500 | (99,500/114,500) × 30 = 26.1 |

---

## 5.0 Vendor Due Diligence

Beyond the quotation documents, evaluate the vendor's organisational capability:

| Criterion | Method of Verification |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Financial stability | Request audited accounts or bank reference letter |
| Technical support capability | Verify number of certified engineers, workshop location, response SLA evidence |
| Track record | Request customer references; verify past contracts of similar scale |
| MOF registration status | Check MOF online portal (ePerolehan) for active registration and code |
| Authorised dealer status | For branded equipment, confirm vendor is an authorised reseller |
| Warranty support network | Confirm service centre locations relative to delivery site |

---

## 6.0 Combined Score and Recommendation

After technical and price scores are combined, a final ranking is produced:

| Vendor | Technical Score (60%) | Price Score (30%) | Due Diligence (10%) | Total |
|--------|----------------------|------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Vendor A | 57 × 0.6 = 34.2 | 27.9 × 0.3 = 8.4 | 9 × 0.1 = 0.9 | **43.5** |
| Vendor B | 42 × 0.6 = 25.2 | 30 × 0.3 = 9.0 | 6 × 0.1 = 0.6 | **34.8** |
| Vendor C | 49 × 0.6 = 29.4 | 26.1 × 0.3 = 7.8 | 8 × 0.1 = 0.8 | **38.0** |

**Recommendation: Vendor A** — highest combined score; technically compliant; best support capability despite not being lowest price. Justification must be documented.

---

## 7.0 Conflict of Interest and Ethics

All evaluation committee members must:
- Sign a **Conflict of Interest Declaration** before participating in evaluation
- Disclose any personal, family, or financial relationship with any vendor
- Recuse themselves from evaluation of any vendor where a conflict exists
- Not accept gifts, entertainment, or any benefit from any vendor during or after the evaluation period

Failure to manage conflicts of interest is a serious governance breach and may constitute corruption under the **Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission Act 2009 (MACC Act)**.

---

## 8.0 Common Errors in Quotation and Vendor Evaluation

| Error | Consequence | Prevention |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| Evaluating price before technical compliance | Awarding to non-compliant vendor | Always complete technical evaluation first |
| No documented justification for recommendation | Audit finding; perception of bias | Document scores and reasoning for every decision |
| Accepting verbal quotations | Unverifiable; invalid for procurement | Require all quotations in writing |
| Ignoring vendor financial stability | Vendor defaults mid-contract | Conduct financial due diligence for contracts above RM100,000 |
| Undisclosed conflict of interest | Criminal liability under MACC Act | Mandatory COI declaration by all committee members |
