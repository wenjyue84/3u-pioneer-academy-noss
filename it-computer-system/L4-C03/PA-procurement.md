## Arahan kepada Pelatih / Instructions to Trainee

1. This is a PRACTICAL assessment. You will be assessed on your ability to conduct a complete ICT procurement exercise from needs assessment to procurement closure.
2. You will work with the scenario, documents, and simulated quotations provided by the assessor.
3. You will be assessed on: **Process** (how you conduct each step), **Output** (quality of documents produced), **Attitude** (professionalism and diligence), **Safety** (document handling, confidentiality, ethics), and **Environmental** (workspace management and records organisation).
4. All documents produced are treated as official procurement records — handle them accordingly.
5. You must sign a Conflict of Interest Declaration before commencing the evaluation phase.

---

## Arahan kepada Penilai / Instructions to Assessor

- Provide each trainee with: the procurement scenario brief, simulated asset inventory, three simulated vendor quotations, all required form templates, and access to a computer with word processing and spreadsheet software.
- Observe the trainee continuously. Do NOT provide guidance on procurement decisions unless there is a procedural error that would invalidate the assessment.
- Assess documents as they are produced at each stage; do not wait until the end.
- Verify the final procurement file for completeness and accuracy.

---

## Senario Tugasan / Task Scenario

**Scenario:** You are the IT Administrator (Pentadbir Sistem) at Jabatan Perkhidmatan Awam Negeri Selangor. Your department has been allocated RM 115,000 to upgrade the ICT infrastructure at the District Office, which currently runs 10 desktop PCs that are 6 years old and a 10/100 Mbps unmanaged switch. The District Officer has submitted a formal request to upgrade to 10 new workstations and a managed Gigabit switch to support a new district management system rollout scheduled in 3 months.

You must complete the following tasks within the allocated assessment time:

| Task | Description | Time Allowed |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Identify procurement requirements and produce a Needs Assessment Report | 45 minutes |
| 2 | Prepare the RFQ document set (specifications, compliance checklist, terms) | 60 minutes |
| 3 | Evaluate three simulated vendor quotations and produce an Evaluation Report | 60 minutes |
| 4 | Issue a Purchase Order, inspect simulated delivery, conduct FAT, complete GRN and Acceptance Certificate | 45 minutes |
| 5 | Compile the procurement file and produce a Procurement Closure Report | 30 minutes |

**Total time allowed: 4 hours**
