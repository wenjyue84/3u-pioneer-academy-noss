## BAHAGIAN A: SOALAN PELBAGAI PILIHAN / SECTION A: MULTIPLE CHOICE (30 marks — 2 marks each)

Answer all 15 questions. Circle the correct answer.

**1.** The formal document that records the business justification, functional requirements, and recommended procurement method before a procurement exercise begins is called:

- (a) Purchase Order
- (b) Needs Assessment Report
- (c) Goods Received Note
- (d) Acceptance Certificate

**2.** Under Arahan Perbendaharaan (AP), a Malaysian government ICT procurement with an estimated value of RM 350,000 must use which method?

- (a) Direct purchase (Pembelian Terus)
- (b) Quotation (Sebut Harga)
- (c) Limited Tender (Tender Terhad)
- (d) Open Tender (Tender Terbuka)

**3.** A technical specification that states "Processor: HP EliteBook i7" is non-compliant with best practice because:

- (a) It specifies too high a processor speed
- (b) It is brand-specific and restricts competition
- (c) The i7 processor is not suitable for office use
- (d) It does not state the warranty period

**4.** The minimum number of written quotations required for a Malaysian government Sebut Harga (quotation) exercise is:

- (a) 1
- (b) 2
- (c) 3
- (d) 5

**5.** In a weighted tender evaluation, if technical compliance is 60% and a vendor scores 48 out of 60 technical marks, the vendor's weighted technical score is:

- (a) 28.8
- (b) 48.0
- (c) 60.0
- (d) 80.0

**6.** A pre-evaluation administrative check that fails will typically result in:

- (a) A reduced technical score for the vendor
- (b) The submission being disqualified before technical evaluation
- (c) A request for the vendor to resubmit
- (d) No action — price evaluation proceeds

**7.** The Conflict of Interest Declaration in a procurement evaluation committee is required to:

- (a) Record the committee members' personal financial goals
- (b) Ensure evaluation is objective and free from personal bias or undisclosed relationships
- (c) Determine the budget allocation for the procurement
- (d) Set the evaluation weightages

**8.** Liquidated Damages (LD) in a procurement contract are charged when:

- (a) The vendor delivers early
- (b) The vendor's unit price increases after award
- (c) The vendor delivers late beyond the contractual delivery date
- (d) The buyer fails to provide the delivery address

**9.** Which action should be taken FIRST when a delivery is received?

- (a) Sign the Acceptance Certificate
- (b) Process the vendor's payment invoice
- (c) Count items and check physical condition against the Purchase Order
- (d) Register the assets in SPAS

**10.** The Functional Acceptance Test (FAT) is conducted to verify that:

- (a) The vendor's invoice total is correct
- (b) Delivered equipment meets the technical specification and functions correctly
- (c) The procurement file is complete
- (d) The vendor's MOF registration is valid

**11.** The Acceptance Certificate is significant because it:

- (a) Cancels the original Purchase Order
- (b) Confirms physical delivery only, not technical compliance
- (c) Formally accepts the goods and triggers payment and the warranty start date
- (d) Replaces the Goods Received Note

**12.** ICT assets above a defined value threshold in Malaysian government agencies must be registered in:

- (a) ePerolehan portal
- (b) Sistem Pengurusan Aset dan Stor (SPAS)
- (c) MySPIKE
- (d) MAMPU database

**13.** The minimum retention period for tender evaluation documents in a Malaysian government agency is:

- (a) 3 years from contract completion
- (b) 5 years from contract completion
- (c) 7 years from contract completion
- (d) 10 years from contract completion

**14.** The document that records lessons learned and compares procurement objectives against outcomes is called:

- (a) Compliance Matrix
- (b) Procurement Closure Report
- (c) Quotation Opening Record
- (d) Needs Assessment Report

**15.** Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), an organisation procuring a system that stores personal data must ensure the system:

- (a) Is the cheapest available option
- (b) Complies with PDPA data security principles
- (c) Is procured only from Bumiputera vendors
- (d) Has the highest processor speed available

Answer all 5 questions.

**17.** Describe THREE (3) characteristics of a well-written technical specification. Give one example of a poorly written parameter and its corrected, technology-neutral version. (6 marks)

**18.** Explain the difference between a Goods Received Note (GRN) and an Acceptance Certificate. State when each document is signed. (6 marks)

## BAHAGIAN C: SOALAN ESEI / SECTION C: ESSAY (40 marks)

Answer all 2 questions.

**21.** Your organisation has been allocated RM 480,000 to procure ICT equipment for a new data centre. The equipment includes 4 servers, 2 managed core switches, a firewall, and associated software licences.

(a) Determine the appropriate procurement method under current AP thresholds. Justify your answer with reference to the value and the regulatory framework. (8 marks)

(b) Describe the complete end-to-end procurement process for this exercise, from Needs Assessment to Procurement Closure. Cover all five Work Activities of CoCu 3 in your answer. (20 marks)

(c) Three vendors respond to the tender. Vendor X is technically fully compliant and scores 55/60 on technical, RM 465,000 on price (lowest). Vendor Y scores 48/60 technical, RM 440,000 on price. Vendor Z is non-compliant on two mandatory items. Using a 60/30/10 weightage (technical/price/due diligence) and assuming due diligence scores of 9, 7, and 0 respectively, calculate the combined scores for all three vendors and recommend an award. Show your working. (12 marks)

**22.** You are reviewing a completed procurement exercise and discover the following:

- The Needs Assessment Report is missing from the procurement file
- The Quotation Opening Record is unsigned
- The Acceptance Certificate was signed 2 days before the Functional Acceptance Test was conducted
- The ICT Asset Register has not been updated for the 15 newly delivered laptops

(a) For each of the four issues above, explain the audit risk it creates and the corrective action that should be taken. (20 marks)

(b) Explain the document retention obligations for the procurement file under Malaysian law, and describe how an Electronic Document Management System (EDMS) can support compliance. (10 marks) (c) Describe THREE (3) entries you would make in the "Lessons Learned" section of the Procurement Closure Report based on the issues identified above. (10 marks)
