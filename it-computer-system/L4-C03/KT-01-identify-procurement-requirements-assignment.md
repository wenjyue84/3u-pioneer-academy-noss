## Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 4 marks each)

**A1.** Which document formally records the business justification and functional requirements before a procurement exercise begins?

- (a) Purchase Order
- (b) Goods Received Note
- (c) Needs Assessment Report
- (d) Acceptance Certificate

**A2.** Under Malaysian government procurement regulations, which method applies to a purchase with an estimated value of RM180,000?

- (a) Direct purchase (Pembelian Terus)
- (b) Quotation (Sebut Harga)
- (c) Limited Tender (Tender Terhad)
- (d) Open Tender (Tender Terbuka)

**A3.** A system administrator is identifying procurement requirements for a new branch office. Which step should be performed FIRST?

- (a) Issue a request for quotation to vendors
- (b) Review the existing ICT asset inventory
- (c) Register a vendor on the MOF portal
- (d) Sign the acceptance certificate

**A4.** Which of the following is a NON-FUNCTIONAL requirement?

- (a) The server must have 32 GB RAM
- (b) The system must support 50 concurrent users
- (c) The switch must have 24 ports
- (d) The OS must be Windows 11 Pro

**A5.** Procurement of ICT equipment handling personal data in Malaysia must comply with:

- (a) Multimedia and Communications Act 1998
- (b) Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)
- (c) Companies Act 2016
- (d) National Land Code

**B2.** Describe the SIX (6) steps of the procurement needs assessment process in the correct sequence. (12 marks)

## Bahagian C: Soalan Berstruktur / Structured Question (50 marks)

**C1.** Read the following scenario and answer all parts.

> **Scenario:** Jabatan Hal Ehwal Pelajar at a public university is opening a new Student Services Centre. The centre will serve 200 students per day and require 15 staff workstations, a network printer, a managed network switch, and a server for student record management. The current ICT assets are 5-year-old desktop PCs that are beyond their refresh cycle. Budget allocated: RM 220,000. The procurement must be completed within 90 days.

**(a)** Identify the procurement trigger for this scenario and classify it from the table of procurement triggers covered in KP(1/5). (4 marks)

**(b)** Conduct a preliminary needs assessment. List the functional requirements in a table with four columns: Category (Hardware / Software / Network / Services), Item Description, Estimated Quantity, and Justification. Include at least 10 requirements. (20 marks)

**(c)** Estimate the total procurement value and determine the appropriate procurement method under current Arahan Perbendaharaan thresholds. Justify your answer. (8 marks)

**(d)** Identify THREE (3) non-functional requirements applicable to this scenario (e.g. scalability, security, performance). For each, write a clear, measurable requirement statement. (9 marks)

**(e)** List TWO (2) policy compliance requirements applicable to this procurement and explain why each is relevant to this scenario. (9 marks)
