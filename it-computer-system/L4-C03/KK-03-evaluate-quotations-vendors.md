**Tujuan / Objective:** Evaluate three simulated vendor quotations using a structured compliance matrix and scoring methodology, and produce a documented Evaluation Report with a justified recommendation for award.

**Arahan:** Pelatih dikehendaki melaksanakan tugasan/kerja mengikut prosedur yang ditetapkan dalam kertas ini. Rujuk pensyarah sekiranya perlu penjelasan lanjut.

---

## Objektif / Objective

Apply a structured technical and commercial evaluation process to three simulated quotations and produce a formal Evaluation Report recommending a vendor for award.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

4 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Three simulated vendor quotations (provided by instructor) | 1 set |
| 2 | Approved RFQ document and technical specification (from KK-02) | 1 |
| 3 | Compliance Matrix template | 1 |
| 4 | Price Comparison spreadsheet template | 1 |
| 5 | Evaluation Report template | 1 |
| 6 | Conflict of Interest Declaration form | 1 per trainee |
| 7 | Computer with spreadsheet software | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Sign the Conflict of Interest Declaration before reviewing any quotation
- Do not discuss quotation prices or scores with other trainees during the exercise (simulate confidential evaluation)
- All evaluation documents are treated as confidential; do not photograph or copy outside the session

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Sign and date the Conflict of Interest Declaration form. Declare whether you have any personal or financial relationship with any of the three simulated vendors. Submit the signed form to the instructor before proceeding. |
| 2 | Perform pre-evaluation administrative checks on all three quotations. Verify: submission received before closing date, complete documents included, vendor details present, and authorised signature. Record pass/fail for each check in the Evaluation Report. |
| 3 | Open the Compliance Matrix template. List every mandatory specification item from the RFQ in the first column. For each vendor, mark each item as: Compliant (✓), Non-compliant (✗), or Partially Compliant (~). Add a brief note explaining non-compliances. |
| 4 | Apply technical scoring. For each technically compliant item, assign a score as per the scoring guide in the Evaluation Report template. Vendors with mandatory non-compliances receive a reduced score or are flagged for potential disqualification. Calculate the technical subtotal for each vendor. |
| 5 | Perform price evaluation. Open the Price Comparison spreadsheet. Enter each vendor's unit prices and quantities. Calculate the grand total for each vendor. Apply the price scoring formula: (Lowest Price ÷ Vendor Price) × Maximum Price Marks. Record the price score for each vendor. |
| 6 | Conduct vendor due diligence assessment using the information in the simulated quotation documents. Score each vendor on: track record (references provided), warranty support capability (service centre details), and MOF registration status. |
| 7 | Calculate the combined score for each vendor: Technical (60%) + Price (30%) + Due Diligence (10%). Rank the vendors from highest to lowest combined score. |
| 8 | Write the Evaluation Report. Include: administrative check results, compliance matrix summary, technical scores, price comparison, due diligence scores, combined ranking, and a written recommendation paragraph justifying the recommended vendor. Sign and date the report. Submit to instructor. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A complete Evaluation Report comprising:
- Signed Conflict of Interest Declaration
- Pre-evaluation administrative check results for all 3 vendors
- Completed Compliance Matrix with pass/fail for every specification item
- Technical scores with justification for any non-compliance deductions
- Price comparison table with price scoring applied
- Due diligence scores with evidence
- Combined score ranking
- Written recommendation with justification
