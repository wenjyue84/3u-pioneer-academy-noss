## Objektif / Objective

Conduct a systematic analysis of a simulated network installation specification document, identify gaps and risks, and produce a Site Survey Checklist and Gap Register.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

3 hours (including site walk-through simulation)

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Simulated network installation specification (provided by instructor) | 1 set |
| 2 | Building floor plan (A3 print) | 1 |
| 3 | Site Survey Checklist template | 1 |
| 4 | Gap and Risk Register template | 1 |
| 5 | Cable measurement worksheet | 1 |
| 6 | Measuring tape (30 m) | 1 |
| 7 | Pen, pencil, highlighter | 1 set |
| 8 | Calculator | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Wear appropriate footwear and personal protective equipment (PPE) when conducting the site walk-through
- Do not enter restricted electrical rooms or confined spaces without authorisation
- Report any safety hazard observed during the site walk-through to the instructor immediately
- Handle all specification documents with care; return all materials to the instructor upon completion

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Receive the simulated network installation specification from the instructor. Read the entire document from cover to cover without making any annotations on the first pass. |
| 2 | On the second pass, use a highlighter to mark: (a) items that are clearly specified — highlight in green; (b) items that are ambiguous or incomplete — highlight in yellow; (c) items that appear to be missing entirely — note in the margin in red pen. |
| 3 | Complete the Specification Review Checklist (Table A below) by ticking each item as Present / Incomplete / Missing. |
| 4 | Using the provided floor plan, conduct a simulated site walk-through of the training lab. Measure the following cable runs and record them on the Cable Measurement Worksheet: (a) from the designated MDF location to each of the 4 simulated IDF locations; (b) from IDF-1 to each of the 5 simulated network outlet locations on Floor 1. Add 20% to each measured run for routing allowance. |
| 5 | Complete the Site Survey Checklist (Table B below) based on your observations of the training lab environment. |
| 6 | Using the information gathered in Steps 2–5, complete the Gap and Risk Register (Table C below). Identify at least FIVE (5) gaps or risks from the specification. |
| 7 | Prepare a one-page written summary addressed to the "Project Manager" explaining: which gaps require client clarification before planning can proceed, and which items you have made an assumption on. List your assumptions clearly. |
| 8 | Present your findings verbally to the instructor (5 minutes). Instructor will ask two questions about your gap register. |
