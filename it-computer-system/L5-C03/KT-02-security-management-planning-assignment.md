## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks)

**A1.** In a risk assessment using the formula Risk = Likelihood × Impact, a threat with Likelihood 4 and Impact 4 produces a risk score of:

- (a) 8
- (b) 12
- (c) 16
- (d) 20

**A2.** The risk treatment option "Transfer" means:

- (a) Eliminating the risk by stopping the activity that creates it
- (b) Accepting the risk without action
- (c) Sharing the financial consequence of the risk through insurance or outsourcing
- (d) Implementing controls to reduce the likelihood or impact

**A3.** A Statement of Applicability (SoA) is required by:

- (a) ISO/IEC 27005:2022
- (b) ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.1.3(d)
- (c) NIST SP 800-30
- (d) The Personal Data Protection Act 2010

**A4.** Which of the following is at the TOP of the security policy hierarchy?

- (a) A technical configuration standard (e.g. server hardening baseline)
- (b) A topic-specific policy (e.g. acceptable use policy)
- (c) The Information Security Policy (ISP) — the top-level management statement
- (d) A step-by-step work instruction

**A5.** "Separation of Duties" as an IAM governance principle means:

- (a) Different staff members use different computers
- (b) Critical business functions are divided so no single person can complete a high-risk action alone
- (c) IT and security staff work in separate offices
- (d) Duties are separated by department budget

**A6.** A SMART security objective must be:

- (a) Simple, Manageable, Achievable, Reasonable, Timely
- (b) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound
- (c) Strategic, Methodical, Authorised, Reliable, Testable
- (d) Scalable, Monitored, Agile, Risk-based, Tracked

**A7.** The role of the Information Security Steering Committee is to:

- (a) Configure and monitor the SIEM
- (b) Make strategic security decisions, approve policies and risk acceptance, and oversee the ISMS at executive level
- (c) Respond to security incidents as the first-response team
- (d) Conduct penetration testing

**A8.** In a phased implementation roadmap, which risks should be addressed in Phase 1 (0–3 months)?

- (a) Low risks that are easy to fix
- (b) Medium risks that have been formally accepted
- (c) Critical risks and mandatory regulatory compliance requirements
- (d) Risks that the insurance policy covers

**A9.** The "Joiner-Mover-Leaver" (JML) process in IAM governance ensures that:

- (a) IT equipment is purchased when new staff join
- (b) Access is provisioned on joining, updated on role change, and revoked promptly on departure
- (c) All staff use the same login credentials
- (d) New joiners complete a skills test before receiving access

**A10.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.2 requires the organisation to establish:

- (a) A vendor management register
- (b) Measurable information security objectives consistent with the information security policy
- (c) A list of approved hardware suppliers
- (d) A training timetable for all IT staff

**B1.** Describe the FOUR (4) risk treatment options available under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and state when each option is most appropriate. (8 marks)

**B2.** Explain the purpose of a Statement of Applicability (SoA) and list FOUR (4) pieces of information that must be recorded for each Annex A control in the SoA. (8 marks)

**B3.** Describe the THREE (3) levels of an information security policy hierarchy. Give an example of a document at each level. (6 marks)

**B4.** List the FIVE (5) sections that must appear in a Security Management Plan and briefly describe the purpose of each section. (8 marks)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (50 marks)

**C1.** You are the Information Security Manager at a regional bank. A recent external audit identified several gaps in the bank's information security controls. The Board has approved a budget of RM 500,000 to address the most critical risks.

(a) Explain how you would conduct a formal risk assessment for the bank. Describe the risk methodology you would use, the asset categories you would inventory, and how you would prioritise the identified risks. (15 marks)

(b) Three of the highest-rated risks identified are:
  - Risk 1: Customer online banking credentials stolen via phishing (Likelihood 4, Impact 5)
  - Risk 2: Ransomware attack on core banking servers (Likelihood 3, Impact 5)
  - Risk 3: Insider data theft by privileged staff (Likelihood 3, Impact 4)

  For each risk, select and justify an appropriate risk treatment option, identify the relevant ISO/IEC 27002:2022 Annex A control(s), and propose the control owner (by role). (15 marks)

(c) Draft THREE (3) SMART information security objectives for the bank, derived from the risks identified above. For each objective, state the KPI, the current baseline, the target, and the deadline. (10 marks)

(d) Explain why security governance structures — including the policy hierarchy, defined roles, and security committee — are essential to effective security management, and how they support ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 5 (Leadership) requirements. (10 marks)
