## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks)

**A1.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 9.2 requires:

- (a) The organisation to conduct penetration testing annually
- (b) The organisation to conduct internal audits at planned intervals to determine ISMS conformity
- (c) The CISO to present a report to the Board every week
- (d) The organisation to obtain ISO 27001 certification within 12 months

**A2.** A "Major Nonconformity" in an ISMS audit is defined as:

- (a) A minor administrative error in a policy document
- (b) A small deviation from procedure that does not affect security outcomes
- (c) The absence of a required control, a complete breakdown of a process, or a failure to meet an ISMS objective
- (d) An observation that the organisation could improve

**A3.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 9.3 (Management Review) must be conducted by:

- (a) The external certification auditor
- (b) Top management
- (c) The SOC team
- (d) The internal audit team only

**A4.** Which of the following is a REQUIRED INPUT to the Management Review (Clause 9.3.2)?

- (a) The organisation's marketing plan
- (b) The results of risk assessments and the status of the risk treatment plan
- (c) The IT procurement budget for the next year
- (d) The organisation chart of the IT department

**A5.** When writing a security report for the Board of Directors, the security manager should:

- (a) Include full technical details of all vulnerability scan results
- (b) Use CVSS scores and CVE identifiers to describe each risk
- (c) Lead with business risk and impact in clear language; reserve technical detail for annexes
- (d) Write the report in the same format as the SOC operational daily log

**A6.** The "5-Why" technique is used in root cause analysis to:

- (a) List five reasons why an audit finding should be dismissed
- (b) Repeatedly ask "Why?" to trace a problem back to its underlying root cause
- (c) Identify five corrective actions for each nonconformity
- (d) Count the number of times a control was tested

**A7.** The "Patch Compliance Rate" metric measures:

- (a) The percentage of IT staff who have completed patch management training
- (b) The percentage of systems that have been patched within the defined SLA for each vulnerability severity
- (c) The cost of patching as a percentage of the total IT budget
- (d) The number of patches released by vendors in a given period

**A8.** A Management Review Output (Clause 9.3.3) must include:

- (a) A complete re-assessment of all 93 Annex A controls
- (b) Decisions on continual improvement opportunities and any changes needed to the ISMS
- (c) A new risk assessment methodology
- (d) The resignation of any underperforming security staff

**A9.** An executive security dashboard should primarily communicate:

- (a) Raw log data from the SIEM
- (b) Detailed firewall configuration settings
- (c) A visual, concise summary of the organisation's security posture, key risks, and performance against targets
- (d) The names and salaries of all SOC staff

**A10.** The purpose of an Improvement Action Register is to:

- (a) Record complaints from customers about security incidents
- (b) Track all improvement actions from management reviews, audits, and incidents — with owners, deadlines, and completion status
- (c) Store backup copies of all security policies
- (d) Log all network traffic for forensic purposes

**B2.** Describe the THREE (3) classifications of audit findings in an ISMS internal audit, explain what each means, and state the required management response for each. (9 marks)

**B3.** List SIX (6) required inputs to a Management Review under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 9.3.2. (6 marks)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (50 marks)

**C1.** You are the CISO of a telecommunications company. The annual ISMS internal audit has just been completed. The audit identified the following findings:

- **Major NC:** No formal incident response plan exists; the organisation has been responding to incidents on an ad hoc basis
- **Minor NC:** Training completion rate for mandatory annual security training is 71% (target: 95%); records show two departments have not submitted completion evidence
- **Observation:** The risk register has not been reviewed or updated in 14 months; the last review was noted as "overdue" in the previous management review

(a) For the Major Nonconformity (no incident response plan), conduct a root cause analysis using the 5-Why method. Propose a corrective action plan including the action, the owner (by role), and the deadline. (15 marks)

(b) Describe how you would prepare and present the Management Review Report for this organisation. What are the Clause 9.3.2 inputs you must address? What Clause 9.3.3 outputs do you expect from the review based on the audit findings? (15 marks)

(c) Draft the key sections of an Executive Security Dashboard for the Board — listing each section, its format (e.g. RAG indicator, chart type), and an example of what it would show based on the scenario. Write the Overall Security Posture narrative (3–4 sentences) in business language suitable for Board consumption. (10 marks)

(d) Explain the principle of continual improvement in the context of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 10. How should the three audit findings above be processed through the nonconformity and corrective action process? What makes a corrective action "effective" and how is this verified? (10 marks)
