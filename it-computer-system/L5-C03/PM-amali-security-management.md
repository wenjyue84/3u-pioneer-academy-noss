## Kaedah Pengajaran / Teaching Method (4-Step)

### 1. PERSEDIAAN (Preparation)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity | Masa / Duration |
|----------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| 1.1 | Welcome trainees; take attendance | 5 min |
| 1.2 | State the practical learning objectives for the session | 5 min |
| 1.3 | Emphasise the management orientation: trainees are practising as Security Managers, not as technical operators — all decisions must be documented and justified | 5 min |
| 1.4 | Distribute the KK (Work Sheet) and scenario briefing pack; explain the exercise structure and deliverables | 10 min |
| 1.5 | Verify all workstations have required software and templates loaded | 5 min |

### 2. PENYAMPAIAN (Demonstration)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 2.1 | Demonstrate the practical task step-by-step: show the completed output format for the key deliverable of this session |
| 2.2 | Walk through a model answer for the first 2–3 steps of the KK procedure to establish standards |
| 2.3 | Highlight common errors and poor decision-making patterns — show examples of weak vs. strong management outputs |
| 2.4 | Clarify the scenario facts and answer questions before trainees begin independent work |

### 3. PENGGUNAAN (Practice)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 3.1 | Trainees work through the KK procedures independently at their workstations |
| 3.2 | Instructor circulates: observe decision-making quality, challenge reasoning ("Why did you rate this risk as High?"), and redirect errors early |
| 3.3 | For KK(3/4) incident management: instructor may introduce a mid-exercise update (e.g. "The affected employee has returned from leave") to test adaptive decision-making |
| 3.4 | Trainees complete the KK assessment checklist as they finalise each deliverable |

### 4. PENGESAHAN (Confirmation)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 4.1 | Instructor reviews each trainee's completed document set against the assessment checklist |
| 4.2 | Conduct a brief class debrief: share common strengths and errors; discuss 2–3 key decision points |
| 4.3 | Provide individual feedback on document quality, reasoning, and professional language |
| 4.4 | Trainees save and submit completed documents; instructor records completion |
| 4.5 | Preview the deliverables for the next KK session |

## Penyampaian Tugasan Terperinci / Detailed KK Delivery Notes

### KK(1/4) — Security Requirements Analysis (48.3 hours)

- Key challenge: trainees often confuse *technical* gaps (e.g. "no firewall") with *management-level* gaps (e.g. "no formal access control policy"; "no risk assessment process")
- Correct this early: the gap analysis at Level 5 evaluates management processes and governance structures against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements — not just technology controls
- Model the ISMS scope statement on the board before trainees draft their own
- For the executive summary task, mark strictly on language appropriateness — technical jargon in an executive summary is a common error at this level

### KK(2/4) — Security Management Planning (42.0 hours)

- Ensure trainees apply the risk formula correctly — common error: adding Likelihood + Impact instead of multiplying
- Challenge trainees on treatment decisions: "Why are you treating this risk and not that one?" — they must reference the organisation's stated risk appetite
- SoA task is time-intensive — allocate sufficient time and guide trainees to prioritise depth of justification for the controls selected in their Risk Treatment Plan
- Budget exercise: trainees must provide at least rough cost estimates — this grounds the roadmap in business reality

### KK(3/4) — Security Operations Management (105.0 hours)

- This KK carries the highest hour allocation as it covers the most complex and broad content
- Incident management task: trainees must demonstrate *management* decision-making (approvals, regulatory notifications, escalations) — not technical forensics
- PDPA notification task: trainees must reason through the law, not just state "notify the PDPC" — require them to identify the trigger condition and the applicable provision
- SOC KPI task: require RAG ratings with written justification — do not accept a dashboard with no commentary
- Phishing remediation task: trainees should identify the root cause (not just "more training") — probe deeper causes such as training content relevance or management culture

### KK(4/4) — Audit and Management Review (14.7 hours)

- Audit findings must be evidence-referenced — do not accept findings stated without specific evidence
- The 5-Why exercise for the Major NC is assessed closely — superficial "Why" answers that loop back to the symptom are a common error
- Management Review Report: check that trainees address all Clause 9.3.2 inputs, not just those that are obvious from the scenario data
- Executive Dashboard task: assess clarity and visual logic — a dashboard that requires explanation to understand has failed its purpose

## Keselamatan / Safety Requirements

- No physical hardware safety requirements for this practical unit — it is entirely document-based management simulation
- Information security awareness during the session: trainees must not share scenario data or their work with other groups (simulates confidentiality obligations in a real workplace)
- Protect access to any shared network drives or template repositories used during the session

## Penilaian Amali / Practical Assessment

Upon completion of all 4 KK sessions, trainees will sit for the Performance Assessment (PA):

- **Code:** IT-020-5:2013-C03/PA
- **Duration:** 4 hours 15 minutes (including 15-minute oral debrief)
- **Format:** Management simulation — complete security management document set from scenario briefing
- **Assessment criteria:** Process, Output, Attitude, Compliance (see PA rubric)
- **Pass mark:** 60 marks out of 100
- **Note:** PA is semi-open-book — ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause reference and regulatory extracts permitted; KP documents are not permitted
