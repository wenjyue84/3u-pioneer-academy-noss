## BAHAGIAN A: SOALAN PELBAGAI PILIHAN / SECTION A: MULTIPLE CHOICE (30 marks)

*Answer all 15 questions. Each question carries 2 marks. Circle the correct answer.*

**1.** The primary purpose of an Information Security Management System (ISMS) is to:

- (a) Install antivirus software on all computers
- (b) Establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the management of information security risks within a defined scope
- (c) Manage the IT department's annual budget
- (d) Provide technical training for IT staff

**2.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 4.2 requires the organisation to identify:

- (a) The make and model of all servers
- (b) All software licences owned by the organisation
- (c) Interested parties and their requirements relevant to information security
- (d) The number of security incidents in the previous year

**3.** In a risk assessment, a vulnerability is defined as:

- (a) An attacker who targets the organisation
- (b) A weakness in an asset or control that could be exploited by a threat
- (c) The financial loss resulting from a security incident
- (d) A software patch that has not been applied

**4.** If a risk has Likelihood 3 and Impact 5, what is the risk score and category (using a 1–5 scale)?

- (a) Score 8 — Medium
- (b) Score 15 — High
- (c) Score 15 — Critical
- (d) Score 8 — Low

**5.** The risk treatment option "Avoid" means:

- (a) Purchasing cyber insurance to cover financial losses
- (b) Implementing a firewall to reduce the likelihood of an attack
- (c) Accepting the risk without taking any action
- (d) Ceasing the activity that creates the risk entirely

**6.** A Statement of Applicability (SoA) must justify:

- (a) Only the controls that are included in the ISMS
- (b) Both included and excluded Annex A controls, with reasons
- (c) The organisation's annual security budget
- (d) The list of security vendors contracted by the organisation

**7.** Which ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause covers security objectives?

- (a) Clause 4 — Context of the Organisation
- (b) Clause 5 — Leadership
- (c) Clause 6.2 — Information Security Objectives
- (d) Clause 9 — Performance Evaluation

**8.** The "Separation of Duties" principle requires that:

- (a) Different staff members use separate computer workstations
- (b) Critical functions are divided so no single person can complete a high-risk transaction alone
- (c) Security and IT departments occupy separate floors in the office building
- (d) Audit and operations staff are employed by different companies

**9.** Just-in-Time (JIT) access in Privileged Access Management (PAM) means:

- (a) All administrator accounts are active continuously
- (b) Privileged access is granted on-demand for a specific, time-limited window, then automatically revoked
- (c) New staff receive administrator access on their first day
- (d) Access rights are reviewed once a year

**10.** Mean Time to Respond (MTTR) in a SOC measures:

- (a) The average time from an incident occurring to the SOC detecting it
- (b) The average time for a security patch to be deployed
- (c) The average time from detection of an incident to the completion of containment actions
- (d) The average time to complete a management review

**11.** A P1 (Critical) security incident requires management involvement within:

- (a) 72 hours
- (b) 24 hours
- (c) 8 hours
- (d) 1 hour (immediate)

**12.** Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, an organisation that experiences a personal data breach must notify:

- (a) The Royal Malaysia Police (PDRM)
- (b) The Personal Data Protection Commissioner (PDPC) as soon as practicable
- (c) The Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC) within 72 hours
- (d) No notification is required — PDPA 2010 has no mandatory breach notification clause

**13.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 9.2 requires internal audits to determine whether the ISMS:

- (a) Conforms to the organisation's own requirements and the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- (b) Has achieved a return on investment for the security budget
- (c) Employs sufficient security staff
- (d) Has been certified by an external certification body

**14.** A "Major Nonconformity" in an ISMS audit requires:

- (a) No action — it is noted for reference only
- (b) Corrective action within 90 days
- (c) Immediate root cause analysis and corrective action, typically within 30 days, with a re-audit required
- (d) Dismissal of the security manager

**15.** A key characteristic of an executive security report for the Board is that it must:

- (a) Include full CVSS scores and CVE references for all vulnerabilities
- (b) Be written in business language, leading with risk and business impact rather than technical detail
- (c) Be identical in format to the SOC daily operational report
- (d) Be at least 50 pages in length to demonstrate thoroughness

## BAHAGIAN C: SOALAN ESEI / SECTION C: ESSAY (40 marks)

*Answer all 2 questions.*

**21.** You are appointed as Information Security Manager of a medium-sized logistics company with 800 employees, warehouses in three states, and an online customer portal. The company has no formal ISMS and has recently experienced two security incidents: a ransomware attack on the warehouse management system and a phishing attack that compromised an accounts payable employee's credentials.

(a) Describe the process you would follow to analyse the company's information security management requirements. Include: defining the ISMS scope, identifying interested parties and their requirements, and conducting a gap analysis. (8 marks)

(b) Conduct a risk assessment for the two incidents that have already occurred. For each incident, identify the assets affected, the threat, the vulnerability exploited, assign a Likelihood and Impact score (1–5), calculate the risk score, and select the appropriate risk treatment option with justification. (8 marks)

(c) Describe the governance framework you would establish for the ISMS: define the policy hierarchy (at least 3 levels), propose the security roles and responsibilities, and describe the committee structure. (8 marks)

(d) Explain how you would measure and report the effectiveness of the ISMS to management. Describe the KPIs you would track, the reporting format for different audiences, and the role of the annual internal audit and management review in continual improvement. (8 marks) *(Note: C21 sub-questions a–d total 32 marks.)*

**22.** A critical infrastructure organisation's SOC reports the following monthly metrics: MTTD = 8.5 hours (target ≤ 4 hours); MTTR = 5.2 hours (target ≤ 2 hours); phishing click rate = 28% (target < 5%); patch compliance for critical vulnerabilities = 82% (target ≥ 98%).

(a) For each metric, assign a RAG status, identify the likely root cause of the underperformance, and propose one specific corrective action with a measurable target. (8 marks)
