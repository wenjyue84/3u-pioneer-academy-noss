## Arahan kepada Pelatih / Instructions to Trainee

1. This is a PRACTICAL (management simulation) assessment. You will be assessed on your ability to perform the complete security management cycle for a given organisation scenario.
2. You will receive a scenario briefing pack. You must produce a set of professional management documents within the allocated time.
3. You will be assessed on: **Process** (how you approach each task), **Output** (the quality of your documents), **Attitude** (professionalism and decision-making), **Compliance** (alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and applicable regulations).
4. You may use reference materials as specified by the assessor (open-book as agreed).
5. All outputs must be submitted in the format specified in the scenario.

---

## Arahan kepada Penilai / Instructions to Assessor

- Provide each trainee with the scenario briefing pack (see Scenario section below).
- Observe each trainee and assess their systematic approach to each task.
- Do NOT provide guidance on decisions — only clarify the scenario facts if genuinely ambiguous.
- Mark each criterion on the rubric as the trainee works and reviews submitted outputs.
- Conduct a 15-minute oral debrief at the end to verify understanding and decision-making rationale.

## Tugasan / Task Description

### Scenario: Nexus Digital Sdn Bhd

You are the Information Security Manager at **Nexus Digital Sdn Bhd**, a fintech company providing digital payment processing services to 50 retail clients. The company has 180 employees, offices in Kuala Lumpur and Penang, and processes approximately 2 million transactions per month. Customer payment card data is stored and transmitted in accordance with PCI DSS requirements.

The CEO has provided you with the following briefing:

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Regulatory obligations | PDPA 2010 (personal data); Bank Negara Malaysia RMiT (payment systems); PCI DSS v4.0 |
| Recent incident | 3 weeks ago: phishing attack → 2 employee credentials compromised → 4,200 customer records accessed by an unauthorised party |
| IAM issue | Post-incident review found 19 former employee accounts still active |
| SOC metrics (last month) | MTTD: 7.1 hrs; MTTR: 4.3 hrs; false positive rate: 38%; phishing click rate: 19% |
| Vulnerability status | 5 critical vulnerabilities open for 18 days; IT team cites change management backlog |
| Current ISMS status | No formal ISMS; security policies last updated 3 years ago; no risk register; no formal audit conducted |
| Management priority | Board wants: (1) full regulatory compliance within 6 months, (2) ISO/IEC 27001 certification within 18 months |

**You must complete the following tasks in order:**

| Task | Description | Time |
|------|-------------|------|
| Task 1 | Produce a Security Requirements Analysis document: ISMS scope statement, interested parties register (minimum 4 parties), internal and external context tables, gap analysis summary (minimum 5 gaps with risk rating) | 60 min |
| Task 2 | Produce a Security Management Plan: risk register (minimum 8 risks, risk-rated), risk treatment plan for all Critical/High risks, 3 SMART security objectives, phased implementation roadmap (Phase 1 within 90 days) | 60 min |
| Task 3 | Address the current operational security issues: (a) classify and document the phishing incident lifecycle (all 6 phases); (b) assess the regulatory notification obligation under PDPA 2010 and BNM RMiT; (c) produce an IAM remediation action list; (d) write a vulnerability escalation directive to the IT Manager | 45 min |
| Task 4 | Produce a Management Report: SOC KPI dashboard with RAG ratings, incident summary (one paragraph), executive security dashboard outline (list sections and content), and a 1-page Board brief on the current security posture and recommended immediate actions | 45 min |
| Oral Debrief | Assessor asks 3–5 questions on your decisions and document outputs | 15 min |

**Total time allowed: 4 hours 15 minutes (including oral debrief)**
