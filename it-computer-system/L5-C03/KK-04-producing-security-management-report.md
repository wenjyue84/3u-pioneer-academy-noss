## Objektif / Objective

Conduct a simulated ISMS internal audit, document findings, produce a formal Internal Audit Report, and prepare a Management Review Report meeting ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 9.3 requirements, including an executive security dashboard.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

4 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | ISMS audit evidence pack (simulated — provided by instructor) | 1 set |
| 2 | Internal Audit Checklist — ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses 4–10 | 1 |
| 3 | Internal Audit Report template | 1 |
| 4 | Management Review Report template | 1 |
| 5 | Executive Dashboard template | 1 |
| 6 | Corrective Action Register template | 1 |
| 7 | Computer with word processing and spreadsheet software | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Audit evidence documents are simulated training materials — treat with the same confidentiality as real audit records
- Audit findings must be evidence-based — do not record findings without reference to specific evidence

---

## Senario / Scenario

Twelve months have passed since ISMS implementation at Prisma Teknologi Sdn Bhd. You are conducting the **first annual ISMS internal audit** and preparing the **annual Management Review**.

The instructor will provide an evidence pack containing:
- ISMS scope statement and policy documents (some not updated since initial drafting)
- Risk register and Risk Treatment Plan (updated 6 months ago)
- SOC reports for the past 12 months
- Training completion records (showing 78% completion — target 95%)
- Access review records (showing one quarterly review was skipped)
- Vulnerability scan reports (showing 2 high-severity vulnerabilities open for 45 days)
- One incident report from the data exfiltration event (KK-03 scenario)
- Statement of Applicability (showing 14 controls not yet implemented)

**Annual performance data:**
- Total incidents: 24 (P1: 1, P2: 4, P3: 12, P4: 7)
- Average MTTD: 5.1 hours (target: ≤ 4 hours)
- Average MTTR: 2.3 hours (target: ≤ 2 hours)
- Patch compliance (critical): 91% (target: ≥ 98%)
- Training completion: 78% (target: ≥ 95%)
- Phishing click rate (year-end): 14% (target: < 5%)
- Controls implemented: 79 of 93 applicable (85%)

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Review all documents in the evidence pack. Using the Internal Audit Checklist, assess each ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause (4 through 10) against the evidence. Record your findings (Conformity / Minor NC / Major NC / Observation) for each clause. |
| 2 | Identify and document at least 3 specific findings — at least 1 Major Nonconformity, 1 Minor Nonconformity, and 1 Observation. For each finding, record: the clause reference, the requirement, the evidence reviewed, the gap identified, and the classification. |
| 3 | Write the complete Internal Audit Report using the template. Ensure all required sections are completed: Audit Scope and Objectives, Methodology, Findings Summary, Detailed Findings (minimum 3), Conclusions, Recommendations, and Distribution list. |
| 4 | For each nonconformity, complete the Corrective Action Register: state the root cause (using 5-Why analysis for the Major NC), propose the corrective action, name the action owner (by role), and set a deadline. |
| 5 | Calculate all annual KPIs from the performance data provided. Compare each KPI to its target and assign a RAG status. Record in the Management Review KPI table. |
| 6 | Build the Executive Security Dashboard using the template: populate the Overall Security Posture indicator (RAG), Risk Distribution chart (use data from the risk register), Incident Trend (monthly breakdown for the year), Control Implementation progress bar, Top 3 Risks, Regulatory Compliance status, Training Completion, and Open Audit Findings count. |
| 7 | Complete the Management Review Report using the template. Address all required Clause 9.3.2 inputs: status of previous actions, changes in context, ISMS performance data, interested party feedback, risk assessment results, and improvement opportunities. |
| 8 | Draft the Clause 9.3.3 Outputs section: list all management decisions made at the review, including: improvement decisions (minimum 3), any changes to the ISMS, resource decisions, revised security objectives for the coming year. |
| 9 | Prepare a 10-minute Management Review presentation outline (slide headings only, with one key point per slide) as if you were presenting to the Board. |
| 10 | Compile all outputs and submit to the instructor. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

- Completed Internal Audit Checklist
- Internal Audit Report (all required sections, minimum 3 documented findings)
- Corrective Action Register (with 5-Why root cause analysis for Major NC)
- Annual KPI table with RAG ratings
- Executive Security Dashboard
- Management Review Report (Clause 9.3 compliant, all inputs and outputs documented)
- Management Review Presentation Outline
