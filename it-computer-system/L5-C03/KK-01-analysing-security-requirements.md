## Objektif / Objective

Conduct a structured analysis of an organisation's information security management requirements, identify interested parties and their security expectations, and produce a gap analysis report against ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

3 hours

---

## Senario / Scenario

You are the newly appointed Information Security Manager at **Prisma Teknologi Sdn Bhd**, a mid-sized IT services company with 250 employees operating in Kuala Lumpur and Johor Bahru. The company provides managed IT services, cloud hosting, and software development to clients in the financial services and healthcare sectors.

The CEO has tasked you with initiating an ISMS implementation project targeting ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification within 18 months. Your first task is to analyse the organisation's security management requirements.

**Key facts about the organisation:**
- Holds personal data of approximately 15,000 end-customers (PDPA obligations apply)
- Two financial sector clients have contractually required ISO/IEC 27001 certification by end of next year
- The company currently has no formal ISMS; security is managed on an ad hoc basis
- A recent internal review identified that patch management is inconsistent and no formal incident response process exists
- The organisation uses a mix of on-premises servers and AWS cloud infrastructure

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Read the full case study profile provided. Note the organisation's business context, key processes, IT infrastructure, and existing security measures. |
| 2 | Define the proposed ISMS scope. State which systems, locations, business units, and processes will be included. Justify any exclusions. Document your scope statement (1 paragraph). |
| 3 | Identify all internal context factors relevant to security management (organisational structure, existing controls, risk culture). Record in the Internal Context table of the gap analysis template. |
| 4 | Identify all external context factors (regulatory, contractual, threat landscape, technology environment). Record in the External Context table. |
| 5 | Complete the Interested Parties Register. For each stakeholder group (Board, customers, employees, regulators, suppliers), state their security requirements and expectations. |
| 6 | Review the 93 Annex A controls from ISO/IEC 27001:2022. For each of the 8 control themes listed in the gap analysis template, assess the organisation's current state as: Not Implemented / Partially Implemented / Fully Implemented. Provide a brief justification for your assessment. |
| 7 | Identify the TOP 5 security gaps — areas where the current state is furthest from requirements, and where the risk exposure is highest. For each gap, state: the requirement source (standard clause or regulation), the current state, the gap description, and your preliminary risk rating (Critical / High / Medium / Low). |
| 8 | Draft a brief Executive Summary (maximum 1 page) of your findings, suitable for presentation to the CEO. Use business language — avoid technical jargon. State the overall security posture, the most significant gaps, and a recommended next step. |
| 9 | Review your completed gap analysis document. Check that all sections are complete, justified, and consistent. Submit to the instructor. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A completed Security Requirements Analysis document comprising:
- ISMS Scope Statement (1 paragraph)
- Internal Context Table (minimum 5 factors)
- External Context Table (minimum 5 factors)
- Interested Parties Register (minimum 5 stakeholder groups with requirements)
- Gap Analysis Table (all 8 control themes assessed with justification)
- Top 5 Gaps Register (with risk rating and justification)
- Executive Summary (maximum 1 page, business language)
