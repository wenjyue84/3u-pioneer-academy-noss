## Objektif / Objective

Conduct a formal information security risk assessment for a given organisation, select appropriate risk treatment options, produce a Statement of Applicability (SoA), and compile a structured Security Management Plan.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

4 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Case study output from KK(1/4) — completed gap analysis | 1 set |
| 2 | Risk register template (spreadsheet) | 1 |
| 3 | Risk treatment plan template | 1 |
| 4 | Statement of Applicability (SoA) template — 93 controls pre-listed | 1 |
| 5 | Security Management Plan template | 1 |
| 6 | Computer with spreadsheet and word processing software | 1 |
| 7 | ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 27002:2022 reference copies | 1 set |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- All planning documents are confidential training materials — treat as internal documents
- Risk register contains sensitive information about security vulnerabilities — do not share beyond the training environment

---

## Senario / Scenario

Continuing from KK(1/4), you have presented your Security Requirements Analysis Report to the CEO of Prisma Teknologi Sdn Bhd. The CEO has approved the ISMS scope and requested that you proceed with a full risk assessment and produce a Security Management Plan for management review.

The following additional information has been provided:
- The organisation's risk appetite: risks rated **High (12–17)** or **Critical (18–25)** must be treated; **Medium (6–11)** risks require management acceptance or treatment; **Low (1–5)** risks may be accepted
- Budget allocation for security improvements: RM 250,000 for Year 1
- The organisation has no cyber insurance currently
- Three incidents in the past 12 months: one phishing attack (employee credentials stolen), one accidental data exposure (misconfigured cloud storage), one failed ransomware attempt (blocked by existing antivirus)

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Using the gap analysis from KK(1/4), identify at least 10 information security risks. For each risk, complete the Risk Register template: Asset, Threat, Vulnerability, Likelihood (1–5), Impact (1–5), Risk Score (L×I), Risk Category (Low/Medium/High/Critical). |
| 2 | Review the three past incidents described in the scenario. Add them to the Risk Register as separate risk entries, reflecting the vulnerabilities they exposed. |
| 3 | Prioritise the Risk Register by Risk Score (highest first). Highlight all Critical and High risks. |
| 4 | For each Critical and High risk, complete the Risk Treatment Plan: select the treatment option (Modify / Accept / Avoid / Transfer), identify the relevant ISO/IEC 27002:2022 Annex A control(s), name the control owner (by role), and set a target implementation date. |
| 5 | For Medium risks, document the management decision — either accept the risk (with formal acknowledgement) or treat it. For Low risks, formally accept. |
| 6 | Complete the Statement of Applicability (SoA) for the 93 Annex A controls. For each control: mark Applicable (Y/N); provide a brief justification; indicate current implementation status (Implemented / Partial / Not Yet). Focus detail on the controls selected in your Risk Treatment Plan. |
| 7 | Draft the Security Governance Framework section of the Security Management Plan: (a) define the policy hierarchy (ISP + 3 topic-specific policies relevant to this organisation), (b) define the security roles and responsibilities table, (c) propose the committee structure. |
| 8 | Formulate 3 SMART security objectives derived from the highest-priority risks. For each objective, state: the objective, the KPI, the current baseline, the target, and the deadline. |
| 9 | Produce an Implementation Roadmap with three phases (0–3 months, 3–12 months, 1–3 years). Assign each Risk Treatment Plan action to a phase. Estimate the Year 1 cost and verify it is within the RM 250,000 budget. |
| 10 | Compile all outputs into the Security Management Plan template. Write a 1-page Executive Summary for management review. Submit to instructor. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A completed Security Management Plan comprising:
- Risk Register (minimum 12 risks, risk-rated and prioritised)
- Risk Treatment Plan (all Critical and High risks treated)
- Statement of Applicability (all 93 controls assessed)
- Security Governance Framework (policy hierarchy, roles, committee structure)
- 3 SMART Security Objectives with KPIs
- Phased Implementation Roadmap (within budget)
- Executive Summary (1 page)
