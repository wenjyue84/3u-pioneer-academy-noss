## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks)

**A1.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 4 requires the organisation to determine:

- (a) The number of servers in the data centre
- (b) The internal and external issues that are relevant to its purpose and that affect its ability to achieve the intended outcomes of the ISMS
- (c) The firewall rules to be applied
- (d) The budget for security software procurement

**A2.** Which of the following is an EXTERNAL context factor relevant to ISMS planning?

- (a) The number of IT staff employed
- (b) The organisation's existing patch management process
- (c) Applicable regulatory requirements such as the Personal Data Protection Act 2010
- (d) The current version of the antivirus software deployed

**A3.** A "Statement of Applicability" (SoA) in ISO/IEC 27001:2022 must include:

- (a) The salary of the CISO
- (b) All 93 Annex A controls with a statement of whether each is applicable and the justification
- (c) A list of all software installed on company computers
- (d) The organisation's financial statements

**A4.** In a security gap analysis, a control assessed as "Partially Implemented" means:

- (a) The control is not needed for this organisation
- (b) The control has been fully deployed and is operating effectively
- (c) Some elements of the control are in place but it does not fully meet the requirement
- (d) The control is planned but has not yet been started

**A5.** Which of the following BEST describes the difference between a Level 4 and Level 5 security role?

- (a) Level 5 configures firewalls; Level 4 only monitors them
- (b) Level 5 is responsible for security governance, risk oversight, and management reporting; Level 4 performs hands-on security operations
- (c) Level 5 works in the data centre; Level 4 works in the office
- (d) Level 5 has a lower salary than Level 4

**A6.** The ISMS scope (ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 4.3) defines:

- (a) The list of approved software vendors
- (b) The boundaries and applicability of the ISMS, including which systems, locations, and processes are included
- (c) The organisation's profit and loss statement
- (d) The technical specifications of the firewall

**A7.** Which regulatory body in Malaysia must be notified in the event of a data breach affecting personal data?

- (a) Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia (SSM)
- (b) Jabatan Pembangunan Kemahiran (JPK)
- (c) Personal Data Protection Commissioner (PDPC) under the PDPA 2010
- (d) The Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC)

**A8.** An interested party analysis in ISMS planning identifies:

- (a) The hardware models used in the organisation
- (b) Stakeholders who have requirements relevant to information security, and what those requirements are
- (c) The number of security incidents in the past year
- (d) The organisation's annual revenue

**A9.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls are grouped into how many themes?

- (a) 2 (Technical and Administrative)
- (b) 3 (People, Process, Technology)
- (c) 4 (Organisational, People, Physical, Technological)
- (d) 5 (Governance, Risk, Compliance, Operations, Technology)

**A10.** Which of the following is a PRIMARY output of Work Activity 1 (Security Requirements Analysis)?

- (a) A configured SIEM system
- (b) A completed Security Requirements Analysis Report including gap analysis, interested parties register, and preliminary recommendations
- (c) A signed employment contract for a new security staff member
- (d) A list of approved hardware vendors

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (30 marks)

**B1.** List FIVE (5) categories of sources from which information security management requirements may be derived. Give one example for each category. (10 marks)

**B2.** Explain the purpose of an ISMS gap analysis and describe THREE (3) steps involved in conducting one. (6 marks)

**B3.** State FOUR (4) internal context factors and FOUR (4) external context factors that are relevant to an organisation's ISMS planning. (8 marks)

**B4.** Distinguish between the roles of the CISO/Security Manager and the IT Manager/System Administrator in an ISMS governance framework. (6 marks)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (50 marks)

**C1.** A manufacturing company with 500 employees is preparing to implement ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The organisation processes customer personal data, holds trade secrets related to its production processes, and has recently experienced a ransomware attack that disrupted production for two days.

(a) Describe the process you would follow to define the ISMS scope for this organisation. What factors would influence the scope boundary decisions? (10 marks)

(b) Identify and justify the THREE (3) most likely interested parties for this organisation's ISMS. For each, state their specific security requirements. (15 marks)

(c) Explain how you would conduct a gap analysis for this organisation. Describe the steps, the outputs, and how the results would be used to inform security management planning. (15 marks)

(d) Describe the key differences between a strategic security management role (Level 5) and a technical security operations role (Level 4) in the context of this organisation. (10 marks)
