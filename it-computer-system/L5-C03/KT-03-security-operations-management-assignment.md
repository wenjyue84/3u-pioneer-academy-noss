## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks)

**A1.** Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) measures:

- (a) The average time taken to patch a vulnerability
- (b) The average time from an incident occurring to the SOC detecting it
- (c) The average time for a user to complete security awareness training
- (d) The average time to complete an ISMS audit

**A2.** A SIEM system in security operations is used to:

- (a) Block all incoming network traffic
- (b) Aggregate log data from multiple sources and correlate events to detect potential security incidents
- (c) Encrypt all data stored on servers
- (d) Manage user account provisioning and deprovisioning

**A3.** Under the principle of "Least Privilege" in IAM governance, users should receive:

- (a) The highest access level to avoid disrupting their work
- (b) The same access level as their manager
- (c) Only the minimum access required to perform their job role
- (d) Access to all systems for the first 30 days to learn the environment

**A4.** A Critical (P1) security incident requires:

- (a) Logging and review at the next monthly meeting
- (b) Immediate management involvement and response within 1 hour
- (c) A written report to be submitted within 30 days
- (d) Referral to the vendor's technical support team

**A5.** The "Containment" phase of incident management aims to:

- (a) Identify the root cause of the incident
- (b) Restore affected systems to normal operation
- (c) Isolate affected systems to prevent the incident from spreading further
- (d) Notify regulatory bodies of the incident

**A6.** Which of the following is a Privileged Access Management (PAM) control?

- (a) Allowing administrators to use their personal email for privileged tasks
- (b) Recording all privileged sessions for audit purposes
- (c) Granting administrator access to all new employees by default
- (d) Removing the requirement for MFA for administrator accounts to improve convenience

**A7.** A phishing simulation returning a 22% click rate indicates:

- (a) The security awareness programme is effective — this rate is within target
- (b) The security awareness programme requires immediate improvement — the click rate is significantly above the 5% target
- (c) The simulated emails were too easy to identify as phishing
- (d) Only 22% of staff received the simulated phishing email

**A8.** Under Malaysia's PDPA 2010, what must an organisation do when a breach of personal data occurs?

- (a) Nothing — notification is not required unless the data was sold
- (b) Notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner as soon as practicable
- (c) Wait 90 days before deciding whether to notify
- (d) Only notify if more than 10,000 records were affected

**A9.** A critical CVSS 9.2 vulnerability should be patched within:

- (a) 90 days (standard change process)
- (b) 30 days (medium SLA)
- (c) 7 days (high SLA)
- (d) 24 hours (emergency change process)

**A10.** The Post-Incident Review (also called "lessons learned") is conducted to:

- (a) Assign blame to the individuals responsible for the incident
- (b) Determine the root cause, capture lessons, and implement improvements to prevent recurrence
- (c) Prepare the invoice for incident response consulting services
- (d) Close the incident record in the ticketing system

**B1.** Describe the SIX (6) phases of the information security incident management lifecycle. For each phase, state ONE key management action. (12 marks)

**B2.** Explain FOUR (4) Privileged Access Management (PAM) controls and state how each reduces the risk associated with privileged accounts. (8 marks)

**B3.** State FOUR (4) KPIs that a Security Operations Centre (SOC) should report to management and explain what each KPI measures. (8 marks)

**B4.** List THREE (3) components of a security awareness programme and explain how the effectiveness of each component can be measured. (6 marks) *(Note: marks adjusted; total remains 30.)*

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (50 marks)

**C1.** You are the Information Security Manager of a hospital. At 03:00 on a Tuesday morning, the SOC detects that the hospital's Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system is being accessed from an unfamiliar IP address overseas. Investigation reveals that an administrator account belonging to a doctor who recently resigned is still active and was used to access and download patient records for approximately 4,000 patients.

(a) Classify this incident by severity level. Justify your classification. (5 marks)

(b) Describe the Containment and Eradication actions you would direct for this incident. Identify which decisions require management approval. (10 marks)

(c) This incident involves personal health data. Describe the legal obligations the hospital must fulfil under Malaysia's PDPA 2010 and any other applicable regulatory framework for healthcare data. (10 marks)

(d) Conduct a root cause analysis of this incident using the 5-Why method. Based on your analysis, propose THREE (3) corrective actions with control owners and deadlines. (15 marks)

(e) The hospital's IAM review finds that 23 former employee accounts are still active. Describe the governance process you would implement to prevent this from recurring, referencing the Joiner-Mover-Leaver (JML) framework. (10 marks)
