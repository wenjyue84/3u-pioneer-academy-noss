## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 4 marks each)

**A1.** An IT Manager presents the following monthly availability result to senior management: "The ERP system achieved 99.3% availability against an SLA target of 99.5%." The appropriate RAG (Red/Amber/Green) status for this result is:

- (a) Green — the system was available 99.3% of the time, which is high
- (b) Amber — the system is below target but not catastrophically so
- (c) Red — the SLA target was not met; the system is in breach
- (d) Blue — this category is not applicable to availability reporting

**A2.** An IT Manager calculates a Year-to-Date (YTD) budget variance of +RM 15,000 on a YTD budget of RM 100,000. This means:

- (a) The IT department has saved RM 15,000 against budget
- (b) The IT department has spent RM 15,000 more than the YTD budget (adverse variance)
- (c) The IT department has an underspend of 15%
- (d) The budget line is exactly on track

**A3.** In the IT Risk Assessment Matrix, a risk rated as "Likely" (4) in likelihood and "Major" (4) in impact would be classified as:

- (a) Low risk — no action required
- (b) Medium risk — monitor only
- (c) High risk — active management required
- (d) Extreme risk — immediate escalation and treatment required

**A4.** The "Accept" risk treatment strategy is most appropriate when:

- (a) The risk is Extreme and elimination is not feasible
- (b) The risk is Low or Medium and the cost of treatment exceeds the expected loss
- (c) The organisation can transfer the risk to an insurance provider
- (d) The activity that causes the risk can be discontinued

**A5.** An IT management report Executive Summary should:

- (a) Reproduce all the detailed data tables from the report body
- (b) Be written only for an IT-specialist audience
- (c) Be readable in 2 minutes; highlight key achievements, concerns, and items requiring management decision
- (d) Include full technical root cause analysis for each incident

**B2.** A risk has been identified with the following details:
- Description: Primary data centre power supply has no redundancy — a single utility power failure would cause complete data centre outage
- Likelihood: Possible (3)
- Impact: Critical (5)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Situasi / Situational Question (50 marks)

**C1.** You are the IT Manager preparing the Q3 management report for your organisation's EXCO (Executive Committee). The following information is available:

**Service Availability (Q3):**

| Service | SLA Target | July | August | September |
|---------|-----------|------|--------|-----------|
| ERP System | 99.5% | 99.7% | 98.9% | 99.1% |
| Email | 99.9% | 99.95% | 99.90% | 99.88% |
| Internet connectivity | 99.0% | 97.5% | 99.2% | 99.4% |

**Budget (YTD — 9 months):**

| Line | Annual Budget (RM) | YTD Budget (RM) | YTD Actual (RM) |
|------|--------------------|----------------|----------------|
| Hardware | 300,000 | 225,000 | 261,000 |
| Cloud | 120,000 | 90,000 | 108,000 |
| Maintenance | 80,000 | 60,000 | 59,500 |
| Staff & training | 200,000 | 150,000 | 112,000 |
| Contingency | 30,000 | 0 | 0 |

**Risk Register (3 active risks):**

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Treatment | Status |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|--------|
| ERP software approaching EOL (12 months) | 5 | 4 | Upgrade project initiated | On track |
| No DR site for primary data centre | 3 | 5 | Cloud DR being evaluated | Delayed — 2 months behind plan |
| Key network engineer resignation | 2 | 4 | Cross-training in progress | In progress |

(a) Write the complete IT Operations Status Report for Q3. Use the structure specified in KP(4/4): Executive Summary (max 5 bullets); Service Availability table with RAG status; a brief Incident Summary narrative (state that Q3 total incidents were 98, SLA breach rate 4.8%); Issues and Escalations; Actions and Next Steps. Apply management writing principles throughout. (20 marks)

(b) Prepare the Budget Variance Report for Q3 YTD. Calculate all variances (RM and %). Write a commentary for each line with variance > ±10%. Provide a full-year forecast outturn. State whether any budget re-allocation or supplementary approval is needed. (15 marks)

(c) Write the Risk Register section of the Q3 management report. For each risk: state the risk rating score; assign the risk level; confirm or revise the treatment strategy. Identify the risk that requires the most urgent management attention and explain why. Write this section in language suitable for a non-technical EXCO audience. (15 marks)
