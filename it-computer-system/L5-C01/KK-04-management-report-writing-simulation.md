## Objektif / Objective

Produce a complete quarterly IT management report package — covering operations status, budget variance, and risk register — and present it in a simulated management briefing.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

3 hours (individual; desk-based report writing + 10-minute oral presentation)

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Quarterly IT data package (simulated — provided by instructor) | 1 |
| 2 | IT management report template (provided by instructor) | 1 |
| 3 | Budget actuals spreadsheet (simulated — provided as handout) | 1 |
| 4 | Risk register (partially completed — provided as handout) | 1 |
| 5 | Word processing and spreadsheet application | 1 workstation per trainee |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- This is a desk-based professional writing exercise. Standard office safety practices apply.
- All simulated financial and risk data is for training purposes only; treat as confidential.
- Save work regularly.

---

## Senario / Scenario

**Context:** You are the IT Manager of Prisma Tech Sdn. Bhd. It is the end of Q2 FY2026 (April–June). You must prepare the quarterly IT management report package for presentation to the Management Committee (EXCO) at the monthly management meeting next week.

**Simulated Q2 data provided (as instructor handout):**
- Service availability log (3 core services × 3 months)
- Incident log summary (total incidents, by priority, by category, MTTR, SLA breach count)
- Change log summary (changes implemented, success/fail, emergency changes)
- Budget actuals by line item (YTD: 6 months)
- Risk register with 6 risks (partially completed — likelihood and impact scores provided; treatment status blank)
- User satisfaction survey results (Q2 average CSAT: 3.7/5.0 — below the 4.0 target)

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Data review and planning (20 min):** Review all Q2 data provided. Before writing, prepare a one-page outline listing: (a) The 3 most significant positive achievements in Q2; (b) The 3 most significant concerns or issues (Red/Amber status items); (c) The items requiring management decision or approval. This outline is the basis for your Executive Summary. |
| 2 | **IT Operations Status Report (40 min):** Using the management report template, write the IT Operations Status Report for Q2. Include all required sections: Executive Summary (max 5 bullet points); Service Availability table with RAG status; Incident Summary (totals, top categories, SLA compliance rate); Change Summary; Capacity Status; Achievements; Issues and Escalations; Actions and Next Steps. Apply management writing principles: non-technical language, data before narrative, recommendations for every Red/Amber item. |
| 3 | **Budget Variance Report (30 min):** Using the budget actuals spreadsheet: (a) Calculate YTD variance for each budget line (RM and %); (b) Identify any line with variance > ±10%; (c) Write a variance commentary for each significant variance (2–3 sentences explaining root cause and corrective action); (d) Calculate the total YTD variance and project the full-year outturn based on current trend; (e) Determine whether a budget re-allocation or supplementary approval is required. Produce the completed budget variance report table with commentary. |
| 4 | **Risk Register Review and Report (30 min):** Using the partially completed risk register: (a) Complete the treatment strategy column for each risk (Avoid / Reduce / Transfer / Accept) with justification; (b) Assign an owner and due date for each risk requiring active treatment; (c) Calculate the risk rating (Likelihood × Impact) for each risk; (d) Identify the top 2 risks by rating; (e) Write a Risk Register Report section suitable for the EXCO report — 2 paragraphs covering: overall risk landscape summary + the top 2 risks with treatment status. |
| 5 | **CSAT Response Plan (20 min):** The Q2 CSAT score of 3.7 is below the 4.0 SLA target. As IT Manager: (a) Identify 3 probable root causes of the low CSAT based on the incident data; (b) Propose a 3-action Service Improvement Plan (SIP) to raise CSAT to 4.0 by Q3; (c) Include the SIP as an appendix to the management report. |
| 6 | **Report assembly and final review (20 min):** Assemble all sections into the complete management report package. Review for: consistent RAG coding; correct figures (cross-check between sections); professional language throughout; every Red/Amber item has a recommended action; the report does not exceed 8 pages (excl. appendices). |
| 7 | **Management briefing presentation (10 min per trainee):** Present the report to the instructor (in the role of EXCO member). Structure: 2 minutes on Operations Status; 2 minutes on Budget; 2 minutes on Risk; 2 minutes on CSAT and SIP; 2 minutes for questions. Demonstrate ability to respond to management questions without referring to technical detail. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

Complete quarterly IT management report package containing:
- IT Operations Status Report (with Executive Summary, all required sections, RAG coding)
- Budget Variance Report (table + commentary; variance flag for lines > ±10%)
- Risk Register Report section (risk ratings, treatment strategies, top 2 risks highlighted)
- Service Improvement Plan (CSAT response — 3 actions with owner and timeline)
- Professional language throughout; suitable for senior management audience
