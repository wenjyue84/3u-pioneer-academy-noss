## Objektif / Objective

Demonstrate competency in coordinating IT operations across internal teams, user communities, and external vendors through a structured multi-party simulation.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

3 hours (group simulation; groups of 4–6 trainees)

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Role assignment cards (IT Manager, System Admin, Service Desk Officer, User Representative, Vendor Representative) | 1 set per group |
| 2 | Incident scenario briefing sheets (provided by instructor) | 1 per participant |
| 3 | Sample SLA document (provided as handout) | 1 per group |
| 4 | ITSM ticket simulation form | 1 per trainee |
| 5 | Communication log template | 1 per group |
| 6 | Vendor performance scorecard template | 1 per group |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- This is a desk-based role-play simulation. Standard office safety practices apply.
- Maintain professional conduct throughout the simulation.
- Treat role-play scenarios as realistic management situations — decisions made must be justifiable.

---

## Senario / Scenario

**Situation:** It is a Monday morning at Prisma Tech Sdn. Bhd. The following events have occurred over the weekend and since 8:00 AM:

1. The ERP system (hosted on on-premises servers) has been intermittently unavailable since 7:45 AM. Approximately 60 users in Finance and Operations cannot complete end-of-month closing. The SLA target for the ERP service is 99.5% availability.
2. The hardware maintenance vendor has sent a contract renewal notice. The current AMC expires in 30 days. The vendor is proposing a 20% price increase. Three servers are currently in warranty under this AMC.
3. A department head from the Marketing department has submitted an urgent service request for 5 new laptops and a video conferencing upgrade for a client presentation on Wednesday.
4. The security monitoring system has generated 47 failed login alerts from a single IP address targeting the ERP server — indicating a possible brute-force attempt.

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Role assignment and briefing (15 min):** Instructor assigns roles. Each participant reads their role card and scenario brief. The IT Manager role coordinates all subsequent steps. |
| 2 | **Incident Management — ERP outage (45 min):** The IT Manager declares a Priority 1 incident. Role-play the incident management process: (a) Service Desk logs and categorises the incident; (b) IT Manager escalates to the System Admin; (c) System Admin investigates and provides updates every 30 minutes (simulated); (d) IT Manager issues user communication to affected departments; (e) Document all actions on the ITSM ticket simulation form. At the end of this step, the group must produce a written Incident Update Communication addressed to Finance and Operations department heads. |
| 3 | **Security response — brute-force alert (20 min):** IT Manager directs the Security Officer (played by System Admin) to investigate the 47 failed login alerts. Role-play the decision: (a) Should the source IP be blocked immediately? What is the risk of a false positive? (b) Who needs to be notified (Internal? Police? NACSA?)? (c) What log evidence should be preserved? Document the decisions and rationale. |
| 4 | **Vendor negotiation — AMC renewal (30 min):** IT Manager and Vendor Representative conduct a simulated contract negotiation. (a) Review the current vendor performance scorecard. (b) The vendor is proposing a 20% increase; the IT Manager's mandate is to limit increase to 10% or less. (c) Role-play the negotiation; use vendor performance data as leverage. (d) Draft a counter-proposal letter. Produce: completed vendor performance scorecard + draft negotiation position document. |
| 5 | **Service request fulfilment — Marketing laptops (20 min):** IT Manager reviews the service request. (a) Assess: Is this request within the standard Service Catalogue? (b) What approval is required (budget authority)? (c) Can it be fulfilled by Wednesday? (d) Draft a written response to the Marketing department head confirming the commitment or requesting additional time. |
| 6 | **OLA and SLA review (20 min):** After the ERP incident is resolved (instructor signals resolution at this point), IT Manager reviews the SLA compliance impact. (a) Calculate how many minutes of downtime occurred and the resulting availability %. (b) Determine whether an SLA breach has occurred. (c) If breached: draft a written SLA Breach Notification to the service owner. (d) Review OLA contributions: which team's response time affected the overall MTTR? |
| 7 | **Debrief and documentation (30 min):** Complete the Communication Log template recording all communications made during the simulation. Prepare a 1-page Incident Post-Mortem summary covering: timeline, root cause (simulated), impact, lessons learned, and preventive actions. Group presents debrief to class. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

Completed simulation outputs:
- ITSM incident ticket (complete, accurately timestamped)
- User communication — Incident Update (professional, non-technical language)
- Vendor performance scorecard + negotiation position document
- Service request response to Marketing
- SLA breach notification (if applicable) or SLA compliance confirmation
- Communication log
- Incident post-mortem summary (1 page)
