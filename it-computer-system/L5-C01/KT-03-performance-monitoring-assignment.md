## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 4 marks each)

**A1.** A server experiences 3 failures in one month. Total downtime across all 3 failures is 9 hours. The server operated for 720 hours in that month. The MTTR for this server is:

- (a) 240 hours
- (b) 3 hours
- (c) 9 hours
- (d) 80 hours

**A2.** An IT service has a monthly SLA target of 99.5% availability. The service window is 24×7 (730 hours per month). What is the maximum allowable downtime per month?

- (a) 7.3 hours
- (b) 3.65 hours
- (c) 14.6 hours
- (d) 1.83 hours

**A3.** "Alert fatigue" in IT monitoring refers to:

- (a) Monitoring tools that are too expensive to operate
- (b) A situation where too many low-priority alerts are generated, causing engineers to ignore alerts — including critical ones
- (c) A server that generates too many log entries
- (d) A monitoring dashboard that is too visually complex for management

**A4.** In IT performance monitoring, the purpose of "synthetic monitoring" is to:

- (a) Monitor only the physical server hardware
- (b) Test IT services from the end-user perspective by simulating user transactions — detecting service degradation before users report it
- (c) Monitor software licence compliance
- (d) Replace the need for a monitoring dashboard

**A5.** A KPI is described as "SMART." In this context, "Time-bound" means:

- (a) The KPI is measured using a stopwatch
- (b) The KPI has a defined measurement period (e.g., monthly average; quarterly trend)
- (c) The KPI must be calculated within 24 hours of data collection
- (d) The KPI applies only to real-time monitoring data

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (30 marks)

**B1.** An email service experienced the following downtime events in one month (service window: 730 hours):

| Incident | Duration |
|----------|----------|
| Incident 1 | 45 minutes |
| Incident 2 | 1 hour 20 minutes |
| Incident 3 | 30 minutes |

(a) Calculate the total downtime in hours. (2 marks)

(b) Calculate the monthly availability percentage. (3 marks)

(c) The SLA target is 99.5%. Has the SLA been breached? Justify your answer. (3 marks)

**B2.** A production server experienced 4 failures over a 3-month period (2,160 operating hours). Total downtime from all 4 failures was 12 hours.

(a) Calculate the MTBF. (3 marks)

(b) Calculate the MTTR. (3 marks)

(c) The following month, the server experienced 2 failures with total downtime of 9 hours. Calculate the new MTTR and state whether the trend is improving or deteriorating. What management action should be taken? (6 marks)

**B3.** Explain the difference between monitoring at the "Component" layer and monitoring at the "Service" layer, using one example for each. Why is it insufficient to monitor only at the component layer? (10 marks)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Situasi / Situational Question (50 marks)

**C1.** You are the IT Manager reviewing the following 4-month incident data for your organisation:

| Month | Total Incidents | P1 | P2 | P3/P4 | SLA Breaches | MTTR (hrs) |
|-------|----------------|----|----|-------|-------------|-----------|
| March | 87 | 2 | 8 | 77 | 6 | 2.1 |
| April | 94 | 3 | 11 | 80 | 9 | 2.8 |
| May | 108 | 4 | 14 | 90 | 14 | 3.5 |
| June | 115 | 5 | 17 | 93 | 19 | 4.2 |

The top 3 incident categories over the 4 months are:
1. Network connectivity (38%)
2. Application errors (31%)
3. User account/access issues (18%)

The same network switch has generated 22 of the 95 P3/P4 network incidents over the 4 months.

(a) Analyse the incident volume trend. Is the situation improving or deteriorating? Support your answer with specific data from the table. What is the most significant indicator of concern, and why? (10 marks)

(b) Calculate the SLA breach rate for each month and the 4-month average SLA breach rate. If the SLA requires a breach rate of ≤ 5%, in which months has the target been met? What is the trend? (10 marks)

(c) As IT Manager, what management actions would you take in response to the data above? Address each of the following: (i) the overall incident volume trend; (ii) the MTTR trend; (iii) the specific network switch generating 22 repeat incidents; (iv) the SLA breach rate. Provide specific, actionable recommendations — not generic statements. (20 marks)

(d) Design the structure of a KPI dashboard (describe each element — you do not need to draw it) that would present this data to senior management in a single-page format. Explain why each element is included. (10 marks)
