## Arahan kepada Pelatih / Instructions to Trainee

1. This is a PRACTICAL management assessment. You will be assessed on your ability to perform all four Work Activities of CoCu 1 in response to a simulated management scenario.
2. You will be given a complete organisation data package (asset register, utilisation data, budget actuals, incident log, risk register) and must produce a full management deliverable set.
3. You will be assessed on: **Process** (how you approach and structure your work), **Output** (quality and completeness of deliverables), **Attitude** (professionalism, management judgment), **Communication** (quality of written and oral reporting), and **Governance** (application of frameworks and best practices).
4. All work must be submitted as a complete, assembled management report package.
5. You must present your findings in a 15-minute management briefing to the assessor (playing the role of the organisation's CEO).

---

## Arahan kepada Penilai / Instructions to Assessor

- Provide each trainee with the assessment data package (organisation scenario, asset register, utilisation data, budget actuals, incident log, and partial risk register).
- Observe the trainee during the briefing session; assess both written outputs and oral presentation.
- Do NOT assist the trainee with analysis or recommendations.
- Mark each criterion on the assessment rubric.
- Two assessors must sign off on the result. An Internal Verifier must countersign within 5 working days.

---

## Senario Penilaian / Assessment Scenario

**Organisation:** Nexus Systems Sdn. Bhd. — a financial services company with 280 staff and a 24×7 IT operations requirement.

**You are the newly appointed IT Manager.** The previous IT Manager left without completing the quarterly review. You have been given the Q3 data package and must prepare and present the full management deliverable set within the allocated time.

**Assessment data package provided by assessor (all data is simulated):**

| Document | Content |
|----------|---------|
| Asset register (snapshot) | 18 servers, 280 workstations, 6 network switches, 3 storage arrays — with age, purchase date, warranty/support expiry |
| Utilisation data (3 months) | Monthly CPU, RAM, storage, bandwidth utilisation for all critical infrastructure |
| Budget actuals (YTD 9 months) | 6 budget lines — hardware, software, cloud, maintenance, staff/training, contingency |
| Incident log (Q3) | 3-month incident log: date, priority, category, time-to-resolve, status |
| Service availability log (Q3) | Downtime events by service (3 services) with timestamps |
| Risk register (partial) | 5 risks with likelihood and impact scores; treatment column blank |

**Total time allowed: 5 hours (4 hours production + 1 hour briefing preparation + 15-minute presentation)**

## Tugasan / Tasks

Produce the following deliverables:

| Task | Deliverable | Work Activity |
|------|------------|--------------|
| 1 | Capacity assessment and 12-month resource plan (capacity gap table + lifecycle schedule + budget proposal) | WA1 |
| 2 | Operations coordination review: SLA compliance analysis + OLA gap identification + vendor scorecard completion | WA2 |
| 3 | Performance monitoring report: availability calculations + MTBF/MTTR + incident trend analysis + KPI dashboard | WA3 |
| 4 | Quarterly management report package: Operations Status Report + Budget Variance Report + Risk Register Report | WA4 |
| 5 | 15-minute management briefing to CEO (assessor) | All WAs |
