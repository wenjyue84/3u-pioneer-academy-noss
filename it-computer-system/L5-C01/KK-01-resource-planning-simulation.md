## Objektif / Objective

Produce an integrated IT Resource Plan for a simulated organisation covering capacity assessment, annual budget proposal, and a 3-year asset lifecycle schedule.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

3 hours (management simulation; individual or pair)

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Organisation scenario brief (provided by instructor) | 1 |
| 2 | Asset register snapshot (simulated — provided as handout) | 1 |
| 3 | 6-month utilisation data extract (simulated — provided as handout) | 1 |
| 4 | Vendor quotation sheets (simulated — provided as handout) | 1 set |
| 5 | IT Resource Plan template (blank — provided by instructor) | 1 |
| 6 | Spreadsheet application (Microsoft Excel or equivalent) | 1 workstation per trainee |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- This is a desk-based management simulation. Standard office safety practices apply.
- Treat all scenario documents as confidential; do not share outside the training room.
- Save work regularly to prevent data loss.

---

## Senario / Scenario

**Organisation:** Prisma Tech Sdn. Bhd. — a mid-sized IT services company with 150 staff across three floors.

**Current IT estate:**
- 12 physical servers (average age: 4.2 years; oldest: 6.5 years)
- 150 desktop/laptop workstations (average age: 3.8 years)
- 6 core network switches (average age: 5.5 years)
- 2 × 50 TB NAS storage arrays (current utilisation: 78%)
- Internet connectivity: 500 Mbps leased line (average utilisation: 73%)
- Cloud workloads: Microsoft 365, Azure-hosted ERP (monthly cost: RM 18,500)

**Business context:** The organisation plans to hire 25 additional staff in the next financial year and is launching a new client-facing web portal that will require an additional 4 virtual machines and 10 TB of storage.

**Budget context:** Last year's IT budget was RM 650,000. Finance has requested that this year's submission not exceed a 10% increase without a detailed justification.

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Read the organisation scenario brief in full. Identify the key capacity, budget, and lifecycle challenges facing the IT Manager. List at least 5 issues or decisions that the IT Manager must address in the resource plan. |
| 2 | **Capacity Assessment:** Using the utilisation data provided, identify which resources are approaching or have exceeded the 80% utilisation threshold. Calculate the projected utilisation for each resource after the planned 25-staff expansion and new portal workloads. Document findings in a capacity gap table with columns: Resource, Current Utilisation %, Projected Utilisation %, Gap, Recommended Action. |
| 3 | **Asset Lifecycle Schedule:** Using the asset register snapshot, identify all assets with age ≥ 5 years or with warranty/support expiry within 18 months. Produce a 3-year lifecycle schedule (Year 1, Year 2, Year 3) showing: Asset type, Quantity, Recommended action (refresh/retire/extend), and estimated cost. Prioritise assets by risk level. |
| 4 | **Budget Proposal:** Develop a line-item IT budget proposal for the next financial year (FY2027). Include CAPEX lines (hardware refresh, new infrastructure) and OPEX lines (cloud, maintenance contracts, training, contingency). Justify each line with reference to the capacity assessment and lifecycle schedule. Calculate the total and compare against the Finance guideline. If the total exceeds 10% growth, prepare a written justification. |
| 5 | **TCO Analysis:** Select ONE major refresh decision from your lifecycle schedule (e.g., on-premises server refresh vs. cloud migration). Conduct a 3-year TCO analysis comparing the two options. Include acquisition cost, annual operating cost, and disposal cost. Present your recommendation. |
| 6 | **Integrated IT Resource Plan:** Compile all outputs (capacity assessment, lifecycle schedule, budget proposal, TCO analysis) into a structured IT Resource Plan document using the provided template. Include an Executive Summary of no more than one page. |
| 7 | Present the IT Resource Plan to the instructor (or class, as directed) in a 5-minute management briefing. Be prepared to justify your recommendations and respond to questions. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

Completed IT Resource Plan containing:
- Capacity gap table with projected utilisation and recommended actions
- 3-year asset lifecycle schedule with prioritised refresh plan
- Line-item IT budget proposal (CAPEX + OPEX) with variance justification if applicable
- TCO analysis for one major refresh decision with recommendation
- Executive Summary (one page)
