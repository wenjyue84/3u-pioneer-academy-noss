## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 4 marks each)

**A1.** The ITIL process that restores normal service operation as quickly as possible after an unplanned interruption is:

- (a) Problem Management
- (b) Change Management
- (c) Incident Management
- (d) Event Management

**A2.** An Operational Level Agreement (OLA) differs from a Service Level Agreement (SLA) in that an OLA:

- (a) Is signed between the IT department and an external vendor
- (b) Is an internal agreement between IT teams that supports the delivery of the SLA
- (c) Defines the financial penalty for service failures
- (d) Replaces the SLA when the IT service is outsourced

**A3.** A Standard Change in the ITIL Change Management process is defined as:

- (a) Any change that requires Emergency CAB approval
- (b) A change that has never been performed before
- (c) A pre-approved, low-risk, frequently repeated change that follows a defined procedure
- (d) A change that must be approved by senior management before implementation

**A4.** When an incident of the same root cause recurs three or more times within 30 days, the correct ITIL response is to:

- (a) Resolve it again as a new incident and close the ticket
- (b) Raise a Problem Record to investigate and permanently eliminate the root cause
- (c) Escalate to the vendor immediately
- (d) Decommission the affected system

**A5.** The purpose of the ITIL Service Catalogue is to:

- (a) List all open incident tickets
- (b) Record all IT assets in the organisation
- (c) Publish the IT services available, their scope, eligibility, and service levels — so users know what they can request
- (d) Document the IT department's budget

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (30 marks)

**B1.** An SLA requires that Priority 1 (P1) incidents must be resolved within 4 hours. The IT Service Desk must acknowledge the incident within 15 minutes. The Network team must respond to a Service Desk escalation within 1 hour. The Server team must restore a failed server within 3 hours of receiving the escalation.

(a) Identify which of these commitments are part of the SLA and which are OLAs. (4 marks)

(b) Explain why the OLA timelines must be stricter than the SLA resolution target, using the figures above. (4 marks)

**B2.** Define "Problem Management" and explain how it differs from "Incident Management." Use an example relevant to IT systems management. (6 marks)

**B3.** List and briefly explain FOUR (4) items that should appear in a Vendor Performance Scorecard used for quarterly vendor review meetings. (8 marks)

**B4.** Describe the purpose of an IT Change Advisory Board (CAB) and state THREE (3) types of information that a change request must include for CAB review. (8 marks)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Situasi / Situational Question (50 marks)

**C1.** You are the IT Manager of a manufacturing company. At 9:15 AM on a Monday, the production planning system (a business-critical application) becomes unavailable. The system is used by 80 staff in the Production, Procurement, and Logistics departments. The SLA for this system specifies 99.5% availability and a P1 resolution target of 4 hours.

By 9:30 AM, your Service Desk has confirmed the application server is unreachable on the network. The server vendor's AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) provides on-site engineer response within 3 hours of a call being raised. However, the AMC was renewed last month and you have not yet received the updated contact list from the vendor.

(a) Describe the immediate actions you would take as IT Manager in the first 30 minutes, including: who you would contact, what communications you would send to users, and what diagnostic steps would run in parallel. (15 marks)

(b) By 11:30 AM (2 hours into the incident), the server vendor's engineer has not yet arrived on-site. The SLA resolution deadline is in 2 hours. Describe: (i) how you would escalate to the vendor; (ii) what interim measures you could implement to minimise user impact; (iii) whether an SLA breach is inevitable at this point, and why. (15 marks)

(c) After the incident is resolved at 1:45 PM, describe the post-incident actions you would take as IT Manager over the following 5 business days, including: post-mortem process, SLA breach notification, vendor governance action, and preventive measures. (20 marks)
