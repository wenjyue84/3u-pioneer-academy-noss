## Kaedah Pengajaran / Teaching Method (4-Step)

### 1. PERSEDIAAN (Preparation)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity | Masa / Duration |
|----------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| 1.1 | Welcome trainees; take attendance | 5 min |
| 1.2 | State the practical learning objectives for the session — link to the corresponding KP and Work Activity | 5 min |
| 1.3 | Review the management context: briefly describe the simulated organisation, the situation, and what the IT Manager must deliver by the end of the session | 10 min |
| 1.4 | Distribute the KK (Work Sheet) and the session data package. Explain the exercise structure, deliverables, and assessment criteria | 10 min |
| 1.5 | Confirm all workstations are functioning; confirm that data package files are accessible | 5 min |

### 2. PENYAMPAIAN (Demonstration)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 2.1 | Demonstrate the first 1–2 steps of the KK procedure as a worked example — show the expected output format and management writing standard |
| 2.2 | For quantitative tasks (capacity gap calculation, availability calculation, budget variance): work through one complete example on the projector before trainees begin |
| 2.3 | For role-play simulations (KK2): demonstrate correct role-play conduct; show examples of professional management communication vs. unprofessional responses |
| 2.4 | Show a sample completed deliverable (from a previous cohort or instructor-prepared example) so trainees understand the target quality standard |
| 2.5 | Answer clarification questions before trainees begin; emphasise that the instructor will not provide answers during the exercise itself |

### 3. PENGGUNAAN (Practice)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 3.1 | Trainees execute the KK procedures at their individual workstations (or in assigned groups for KK2 role-play) |
| 3.2 | Instructor circulates every 20–30 minutes to observe progress, check that trainees are following the correct analytical approach, and identify trainees who are struggling with methodology (not answers) |
| 3.3 | For KK3 and KK4: conduct spot-check oral questioning during the session ("Why did you assign RED to this service?" / "What is the root cause of this budget variance?") to verify that trainees understand their own outputs |
| 3.4 | Trainees complete the Assessment Checklist items as they complete each procedure step |
| 3.5 | For KK2 role-play: instructor plays the role of an EXCO member or vendor representative in the final briefing; observe how trainees handle pressure and unexpected questions |

### 4. PENGESAHAN (Confirmation)

| Langkah / Step | Aktiviti / Activity |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 4.1 | Instructor reviews each trainee's completed deliverables against the Assessment Checklist |
| 4.2 | Conduct a group debrief: ask 3–4 trainees to share one key decision they made and justify it; facilitate peer discussion |
| 4.3 | Provide structured feedback to each trainee: strengths, specific improvements needed, and any critical errors |
| 4.4 | Trainees revise significant errors before submission (instructor discretion — formative KK only; for summative PA no revision is permitted after submission) |
| 4.5 | Record session completion, attendance, and any issues in the training log |

## Panduan Amali Mengikut KK / Session-Specific Practical Guidance

### KK(1/4) — Resource Planning Simulation (35 hours)

| Skill Focus | Expected Demonstrable Competency |
|------------|----------------------------------|
| Capacity baseline analysis | Trainee correctly identifies all resources exceeding 80% utilisation; calculates projected utilisation after demand growth |
| Lifecycle schedule production | All assets approaching EOL (≥ 5 years or support expiry ≤ 18 months) identified; prioritised by risk; 3-year schedule produced |
| Budget proposal development | Line-item CAPEX and OPEX proposal produced; justified against capacity and lifecycle data; TCO analysis applied |
| Executive summary writing | One-page summary suitable for management audience; no unexplained technical jargon; recommendations clearly stated |

**Instructor note:** Allow trainees to use a provided Excel budget template. The emphasis is on the quality of analysis and justification, not on spreadsheet formatting skills.

### KK(2/4) — Operations Coordination Role-Play (35 hours)

| Skill Focus | Expected Demonstrable Competency |
|------------|----------------------------------|
| Incident management coordination | IT Manager role correctly applies P1 incident process; communicates to users within 30 minutes; coordinates L1/L2/L3 response |
| SLA availability impact calculation | Trainee correctly calculates downtime and availability %; determines whether SLA breach occurred |
| Vendor negotiation | Uses performance scorecard data as objective evidence; produces a documented negotiation position |
| Management communication | Written communications are professional, concise, non-technical, and audience-appropriate |

**Instructor note:** Rotate the IT Manager role between trainees across multiple KK2 sessions so all trainees practice the coordination role. Record each trainee's performance separately.

### KK(3/4) — Performance Monitoring Simulation (35 hours)

| Skill Focus | Expected Demonstrable Competency |
|------------|----------------------------------|
| Availability and reliability calculations | All calculations mathematically correct; SLA compliance correctly determined; MTTR trend correctly interpreted |
| Incident trend analysis | Volume trend identified with direction; category breakdown correct; repeat incidents identified; 200-word commentary recommends specific management actions |
| Dashboard design | One-page dashboard is RAG-coded, visually clear, and appropriate for management audience; includes all required elements |
| Monitoring gap identification | Gaps identified are genuine (not already covered by data set); recommended tools are appropriate and realistic |

**Instructor note:** Provide at least two versions of the data set with different trend patterns (one improving, one deteriorating) so trainees cannot rely on memorising a single answer.

### KK(4/4) — Management Report Writing Simulation (35 hours)

| Skill Focus | Expected Demonstrable Competency |
|------------|----------------------------------|
| Operations status report | All sections present; RAG coding accurate; every Red/Amber item has a recommended action; language is management-appropriate |
| Budget variance report | All variances correctly calculated; commentary provided for lines > ±10%; full-year outturn projected |
| Risk register report | All risk ratings correct; treatment strategies justified; top risks highlighted; written for non-technical management audience |
| Oral management briefing | Structured presentation; within time limit; responds to assessor questions without reverting to technical jargon |

**Instructor note:** The 10-minute oral briefing component of KK4 should be treated as a rehearsal for the PA oral component. Provide specific verbal feedback on management communication style immediately after each briefing.

## Keselamatan / Safety Requirements

- All practical sessions are desk-based management simulations. Standard office safety practices apply.
- Simulated financial and organisational data must be treated as confidential and must not be shared outside the training room.
- Trainees must not use AI-generated text in any submitted deliverable. All analysis and writing must demonstrate the trainee's own management judgment.
- For KK2 role-play: trainees must maintain professional conduct throughout all simulations. Role-play scenarios may involve conflict situations (e.g., vendor negotiations, management escalations) — professionalism is assessed.

## Penilaian Amali / Practical Assessment

Upon completion of all 4 KK sessions, trainees will sit for the Performance Assessment (PA):

- **Code:** IT-020-5:2013-C01/PA
- **Duration:** 5 hours (4 hours production + 15-minute management briefing)
- **Format:** Management simulation — full deliverable set from organisational data package
- **Pass mark:** 60 / 100; all individual sections ≥ 50%
- **Assessment criteria:** Process, Output, Attitude and Management Judgment, Communication, Governance
