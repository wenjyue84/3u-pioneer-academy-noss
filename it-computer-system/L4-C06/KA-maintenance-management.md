## BAHAGIAN A: SOALAN PELBAGAI PILIHAN / SECTION A: MULTIPLE CHOICE (30 marks)

Answer all 15 questions. Each question carries 2 marks. Circle the correct answer.

**1.** The process of systematically identifying, classifying, and prioritising all maintenance needs for a computer system environment is called:

- (a) System Inventory
- (b) Maintenance Requirements Analysis (MRA)
- (c) Failure Mode and Effect Analysis (FMEA)
- (d) Incident Management

**2.** A server whose failure would directly halt core business operations is classified as:

- (a) Tier 3 — Standard
- (b) Tier 2 — Business-Critical
- (c) Tier 1 — Mission-Critical
- (d) Tier 0 — Non-Managed

**3.** System Availability is calculated as:

- (a) MTTR ÷ (MTBF + MTTR) × 100%
- (b) MTBF ÷ (MTBF + MTTR) × 100%
- (c) MTBF − MTTR × 100%
- (d) (MTBF × MTTR) ÷ 100%

**4.** A maintenance window is:

- (a) A software portal for submitting maintenance requests
- (b) A pre-approved time period during which disruptive maintenance activities may be performed
- (c) The maximum repair time allowed under the SLA
- (d) A physical inspection hatch on a server rack

**5.** Which section of a maintenance plan specifies the staffing, tools, spare parts, and budget required?

- (a) Scope
- (b) Objectives
- (c) Resource Plan
- (d) KPI and Reporting

**6.** The correct sequence of the work order lifecycle is:

- (a) Assigned → Raised → In Progress → Completed → Verified → Closed
- (b) Raised → Assigned → In Progress → Completed → Verified → Closed
- (c) Raised → In Progress → Assigned → Verified → Completed → Closed
- (d) Completed → Raised → Assigned → Verified → In Progress → Closed

**7.** Before signing off a contractor's completed work, the maintenance manager must:

- (a) Pay the contractor's invoice immediately
- (b) Verify the work against the scope of work, confirm system functionality, and obtain the contractor's service report
- (c) Allow the contractor to leave without documentation
- (d) Re-do all the contractor's work to verify quality

**8.** In ITIL, a "Problem" is defined as:

- (a) Any unplanned interruption of a service
- (b) The underlying cause of one or more incidents
- (c) A scheduled maintenance task that exceeded its time window
- (d) A formal complaint received from a user

**9.** Which incident priority requires a response within 15 minutes and resolution within 2 hours?

- (a) P4 — Routine
- (b) P3 — Medium
- (c) P2 — High
- (d) P1 — Critical

**10.** The "5 Whys" technique is used for:

- (a) Listing the top 5 preventive maintenance tasks
- (b) Scoring risk in the risk assessment matrix
- (c) Root Cause Analysis — repeatedly asking "why" to reach the fundamental cause
- (d) Calculating PM compliance rate

**11.** A Known Error Database (KEDB) entry must contain:

- (a) Root cause, workaround, and permanent fix status
- (b) Only the incident log number
- (c) The vendor's contact details
- (d) The technician's training records

**12.** The RAG status "Amber" on a KPI dashboard indicates:

- (a) The KPI is fully meeting its target
- (b) The KPI data is unavailable
- (c) The KPI is within 5% of the target threshold — action required to prevent it becoming Red
- (d) The KPI has failed critically and immediate escalation is needed

**13.** PM Compliance Rate is calculated as:

- (a) PM tasks completed ÷ total assets × 100%
- (b) PM tasks completed on schedule ÷ PM tasks scheduled × 100%
- (c) PM tasks scheduled ÷ total work orders × 100%
- (d) PM cost ÷ total maintenance budget × 100%

**14.** The Repeat Incident Rate KPI target of ≤ 10% means:

- (a) No more than 10% of maintenance tasks should be preventive
- (b) No more than 10% of incidents should be a recurrence of a previously resolved fault
- (c) No more than 10 incidents per month are acceptable
- (d) At least 10% of incidents must be P1 priority

**15.** The Annual Maintenance Management Report is presented to:

- (a) All end users of the IT system
- (b) The vendor and contractor partners only
- (c) The IT steering committee or senior management
- (d) The maintenance technicians only

## BAHAGIAN C: SOALAN ESEI / SECTION C: ESSAY (40 marks)

Answer ALL 2 questions.

**20.** You are the IT Maintenance Manager for a state government agency. The agency's IT environment includes 4 servers (finance system, HR system, email, domain controller), 80 desktop workstations, 3 network switches, and 1 UPS. The SLA requires 99.5% uptime for the finance and HR servers.

(a) Explain how you would develop the annual maintenance plan for this environment. Cover: determining maintenance strategy per asset class, scheduling (with maintenance window considerations), and resource planning. (14 marks)

(b) Six months into the plan, the finance server fails on a Tuesday afternoon. The failure is caused by a RAID 5 drive failure. The second drive in the RAID set also shows a warning status. Describe how you would manage this incident from the moment it is reported, including: priority classification, escalation, technician assignment, workaround, and post-incident RCA. (12 marks)

(c) Prepare a KPI dashboard for the month in which the above incident occurred, using the following data:
- Finance server downtime: 4.2 hours (month total: 720 hours)
- PM tasks scheduled: 18; completed on schedule: 15
- Total incidents: 8; repeat incidents: 1; P1 incidents: 1 (resolved in 4.2 hours — SLA target: 2 hours)
- Budget: RM 4,000; actual spend: RM 3,750

Assign a RAG status to each KPI and explain each Amber or Red result. (14 marks)

**21.** Describe the structure and content of a complete Monthly Maintenance Management Report. Explain the purpose of each section and how it supports management decision-making. In your answer, explain how the report is prepared, what data sources are used, and how findings are communicated to management. (40 marks)
