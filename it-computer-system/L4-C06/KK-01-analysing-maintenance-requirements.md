## Objektif / Objective

Conduct a structured maintenance requirements analysis for a simulated computer system environment and produce a documented MRA output.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

3 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Simulated asset inventory list (provided by instructor) | 1 |
| 2 | Simulated SLA document | 1 |
| 3 | Simulated helpdesk incident history (last 12 months) | 1 |
| 4 | MRA template (blank) | 1 |
| 5 | Risk assessment matrix template | 1 |
| 6 | Computer with word processing software | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- Handle all provided documents as confidential — do not share with other groups
- Ensure the computer workstation is used for this exercise only; do not access external systems

---

## Senario / Scenario

You are the IT Maintenance Manager for **Universiti Harapan Malaysia**, a mid-sized higher education institution. The IT environment includes:

- 3 application servers (student information system, email, LMS)
- 1 domain controller
- 50 desktop workstations in computer labs
- 20 staff laptops
- 2 managed network switches and 1 router
- 1 UPS system (server room)

The institution's SLA requires:
- Servers: 99.5% uptime; P1 resolution within 2 hours
- Workstations: best-effort; P3 resolution within 1 business day

The helpdesk history shows:
- Server incidents: 4 in the last 12 months (avg. 3.5 hours downtime each)
- Workstation incidents: 47 in the last 12 months (mainly OS crashes and hardware faults)
- The UPS battery has never been replaced (system is 4 years old)
- Network devices: last firmware update was 18 months ago

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Review the provided asset inventory. Classify each asset into Tier 1 (Mission-Critical), Tier 2 (Business-Critical), or Tier 3 (Standard). Justify each classification in a table. |
| 2 | Calculate the MTBF and MTTR for each asset class using the incident history data provided. |
| 3 | Review the SLA document. List all SLA obligations and translate each into a specific maintenance requirement (e.g. "99.5% uptime" → "Server PM must be completed within the approved maintenance window; real-time monitoring must be active"). |
| 4 | Complete the risk assessment matrix for the top 5 maintenance risks identified. Score each risk for Likelihood (1–5) and Impact (1–5). Calculate the Risk Score. |
| 5 | Conduct a maintenance gap analysis: list the current maintenance activities (if any are implied by the scenario), identify missing activities, and recommend new or adjusted activities. |
| 6 | Compile a prioritised list of required maintenance activities, ranked by risk score. |
| 7 | Produce a short MRA summary report (1–2 pages) containing: asset classification table, MTBF/MTTR summary, SLA-derived requirements, risk matrix, gap analysis, and prioritised activity list. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A completed MRA document containing:
- Asset inventory with criticality classification and justification
- MTBF/MTTR calculations per asset class
- SLA-to-maintenance requirement mapping table
- Risk assessment matrix (top 5 risks scored)
- Maintenance gap analysis table
- Prioritised maintenance activity list
