## Objektif / Objective

Develop a complete, structured Computer System Maintenance Plan for a simulated organisation, including a 12-month maintenance calendar, resource plan, and budget estimate.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

4 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | MRA output from KK-01 (or instructor-provided equivalent) | 1 |
| 2 | Maintenance plan template (blank) | 1 |
| 3 | SOP template (blank) | 1 set |
| 4 | 12-month calendar grid template | 1 |
| 5 | Budget estimation worksheet | 1 |
| 6 | Computer with word processing and spreadsheet software | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- All plan documents are internal organisational documents — treat as confidential
- Save work regularly to avoid data loss during the exercise

---

## Senario / Scenario

Continuing from KK-01, you have completed the Maintenance Requirements Analysis for **Universiti Harapan Malaysia**. You are now required to develop the full Computer System Maintenance Plan for the coming 12-month period. The IT Manager has approved a maintenance budget of RM 45,000 for the year. The organisation's approved maintenance window is every Saturday, 01:00–05:00.

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Using the MRA output, determine the maintenance strategy for each asset class (PM, PdM, or CBM). Document the rationale for each choice. |
| 2 | Determine the PM frequency for each asset class. Reference KP-02 frequency guidelines and adjust based on asset criticality and failure history from KK-01. |
| 3 | Develop a 12-month maintenance calendar. Plot all planned PM activities on the calendar grid, ensuring activities are scheduled within the approved maintenance window. Flag months with high activity (avoid scheduling multiple Tier 1 PM activities in the same window). |
| 4 | Write a brief SOP (minimum 8 steps) for ONE of the following maintenance activities: (a) Quarterly server physical inspection and cleaning, OR (b) Workstation semi-annual health check. Include: purpose, prerequisites, safety precautions, step-by-step procedure, acceptance criteria, and documentation requirements. |
| 5 | Prepare a resource plan: list the staffing requirements (roles, hours per month), tools and equipment needed, and minimum spare parts inventory. |
| 6 | Prepare a budget estimate for the full year. Itemise: labour cost, spare parts, contractor services, and software/licences. Total must not exceed RM 45,000. Show calculation for each line item. |
| 7 | Compile the full maintenance plan document with all sections from KP-02 (Scope, Objectives, Strategy, Schedule, SOP reference, Resource Plan, Budget, KPI, Review clause). |
| 8 | Prepare a one-page plan summary for presentation to the IT Manager. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A complete maintenance plan document containing:
- Asset-class maintenance strategy with rationale
- PM frequency table
- 12-month maintenance calendar (conflict-free, within approved window)
- One SOP (minimum 8 steps) for the selected maintenance activity
- Resource plan (staffing, tools, spare parts)
- Itemised budget estimate within RM 45,000 limit
- One-page plan summary
