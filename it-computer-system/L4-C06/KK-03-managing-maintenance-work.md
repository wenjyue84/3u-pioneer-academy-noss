## Objektif / Objective

Simulate the full work order management cycle for a scheduled maintenance activity, demonstrating coordination, documentation, deviation handling, and quality assurance.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

4 hours (including role-play exercise)

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Work order template (blank) | 5 sets |
| 2 | Maintenance plan from KK-02 (or instructor-provided schedule extract) | 1 |
| 3 | SOP for quarterly server PM (from KK-02 or instructor-provided) | 1 |
| 4 | Role-play scenario cards (instructor-prepared: deviation scenarios) | 1 set |
| 5 | Deviation/deviation log template | 1 |
| 6 | Contractor supervision checklist | 1 |
| 7 | Computer with word processing software | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- During role-play exercises, trainees playing the role of technicians must still follow simulated safety steps as stated in the SOP
- Do not access live production systems during this exercise

---

## Senario / Scenario

It is Saturday, 01:00. You are the IT Maintenance Manager on duty. Tonight's planned maintenance activities (from the approved maintenance plan) are:

- **WO-001:** Quarterly PM for Application Server 1 (Student Information System) — Assigned to Technician A
- **WO-002:** Quarterly PM for Application Server 2 (Email Server) — Assigned to Technician B
- **WO-003:** Firmware update for managed switch SW-01 — Assigned to Technician A (after Server 1 PM)
- **WO-004:** UPS battery inspection and load test — Assigned to external contractor (ACME Power Services)

During the session, the instructor will introduce the following deviations (via scenario cards):
- **Deviation 1:** Technician A discovers that Application Server 1 has a failed cooling fan (not in scope of the PM). The server is still running.
- **Deviation 2:** The contractor arrives 45 minutes late and does not have the UPS load test equipment on site.
- **Deviation 3:** After the firmware update on SW-01, two VLANs are unreachable. Network connectivity for three staff is lost.

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Raise the four work orders (WO-001 to WO-004) using the work order template. Complete all fields including asset ID, maintenance type, description, priority, assigned technician, scheduled time, SOP reference, and parts required. |
| 2 | Prepare a pre-session briefing checklist for each technician. Brief the technicians (role-played by peers or instructor) on their work orders — objectives, SOP, safety, communication protocol, and escalation trigger. |
| 3 | Simulate monitoring during the maintenance window. At 30-minute intervals, record the status of each work order (as reported by technicians) in a monitoring log. |
| 4 | Receive Deviation 1 (failed cooling fan discovered). Decide and document your management action: raise a new work order, assess priority, decide whether to fix now or schedule. Justify your decision. |
| 5 | Receive Deviation 2 (contractor late and unprepared). Complete the contractor deviation log. Determine and document your action: proceed with available items, reschedule the load test, and record the contractor's non-compliance. |
| 6 | Receive Deviation 3 (VLAN unreachable after firmware update). Classify this as an induced incident. Raise an emergency work order. Direct Technician A to troubleshoot. Document the communication to the IT Manager. |
| 7 | At the close of the maintenance window, verify the completed work for WO-001, WO-002, and WO-003 against the SOP acceptance criteria. Sign off completed work orders. Close the work orders in the log. |
| 8 | Prepare a maintenance session summary report (1 page) covering: work orders completed, deviations encountered, actions taken, outstanding items, and recommendations. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

- Four completed work orders (raised, filled, and closed or flagged)
- Pre-session briefing checklist for each technician
- Monitoring log with 30-minute status entries
- Deviation log with documented actions for all three deviations
- Emergency work order for the VLAN incident
- Maintenance session summary report (1 page)
