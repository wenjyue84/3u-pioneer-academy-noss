## Objektif / Objective

Compile a complete monthly maintenance management report from simulated operational data, incorporating KPI calculations, a RAG status dashboard, and management recommendations.

---

## Tempoh / Duration

4 hours

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

| No. | Item | Quantity |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | Simulated monthly data pack (provided by instructor) | 1 |
| 2 | Monthly maintenance management report template | 1 |
| 3 | KPI calculation worksheet | 1 |
| 4 | RAG dashboard template | 1 |
| 5 | Computer with word processing and spreadsheet software | 1 |

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

- All data in the simulated data pack is fictional and confidential to this exercise
- Do not use real personal or organisational data in your report

---

## Senario / Scenario

You are preparing the May 2026 Monthly Maintenance Management Report for **Universiti Harapan Malaysia**. The instructor will provide a simulated data pack containing:

- Work order log for May 2026 (22 work orders: PM and corrective)
- Incident log for May 2026 (9 incidents: 1 P1, 2 P2, 6 P3)
- Server uptime log for May 2026 (App Server 1 was down for 3.5 hours due to KK-04 incident)
- Spare parts consumption log
- Budget expenditure summary for May 2026 (YTD spend: RM 18,200 of RM 45,000 budget, 5 months elapsed)
- Contractor performance record (1 contractor non-compliance event from KK-03)

KPI targets (from the maintenance plan):
- System Availability (Tier 1): ≥ 99.5%
- PM Compliance Rate: ≥ 95%
- MTTR (P1 Resolution): ≤ 2 hours
- Repeat Incident Rate: ≤ 10%
- First-Time Fix Rate: ≥ 85%
- YTD Budget Utilisation: ≤ proportional budget (< 41.7% at 5 months)

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Review all data in the simulated data pack. Identify any data quality issues (missing fields, inconsistencies) and note them in the report. |
| 2 | Calculate each of the six KPIs using the provided data. Show all calculations clearly (formula, numerator, denominator, result). |
| 3 | Assign a RAG status to each KPI based on comparison against the target. Explain the Amber and Red ratings with a one-sentence root cause. |
| 4 | Complete the Maintenance Activity Summary: PM tasks completed vs scheduled for the month; corrective maintenance count; contractor activities performed. |
| 5 | Complete the Incident Report section: incidents by priority; trend vs April (assume April had 5 incidents: 0 P1, 1 P2, 4 P3); write a summary paragraph for the P1 incident (from KK-04 scenario). |
| 6 | Complete the System Availability Report for App Server 1: calculate May availability (%). Identify the downtime event and cause. Determine if the SLA threshold was breached. |
| 7 | Complete the Budget Utilisation section: calculate YTD budget utilisation (%). Determine if spend is within the proportional target. Explain any variance. |
| 8 | Complete the Open Items section: list outstanding work orders, unresolved known errors, and deferred maintenance items. |
| 9 | Write the Recommendations section: prepare at least THREE specific, management-actionable recommendations based on the report data. Each recommendation must state the problem, the recommendation, and the expected benefit. |
| 10 | Write the Executive Summary (maximum 1 page): highlight the key message for management — what is going well, what requires attention, and what decisions are needed. |
| 11 | Assemble the complete report with all sections, table of contents, and page numbers. |
| 12 | Prepare a 5-minute verbal presentation of the report for the instructor (acting as IT Manager). Focus on the executive summary, Red/Amber KPIs, and recommendations. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A complete Monthly Maintenance Management Report containing:
- KPI calculations with full working shown
- RAG dashboard with status and root cause for Amber/Red items
- Maintenance activity summary
- Incident report with trend comparison
- System availability calculation and SLA compliance determination
- Budget utilisation with variance explanation
- Open items list
- Three or more specific management recommendations
- Executive summary (max 1 page)
- 5-minute verbal presentation delivered to the instructor
