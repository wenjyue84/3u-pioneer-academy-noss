## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks)

**A1.** Which type of maintenance is performed at fixed time intervals, regardless of the system's current condition?

- (a) Corrective maintenance
- (b) Preventive maintenance
- (c) Predictive maintenance
- (d) Condition-based maintenance

**A2.** MTBF stands for:

- (a) Mean Time Before Failure
- (b) Maximum Time Between Failures
- (c) Mean Time Between Failures
- (d) Minimum Threshold Before Fault

**A3.** A server that hosts the organisation's core ERP system would be classified as:

- (a) Tier 3 — Standard
- (b) Tier 2 — Business-Critical
- (c) Tier 1 — Mission-Critical
- (d) Tier 4 — Non-Essential

**A4.** The primary purpose of reviewing failure history during MRA is to:

- (a) Identify which technician made errors
- (b) Calculate the purchase cost of replacement parts
- (c) Identify recurring failure patterns and adjust maintenance frequency accordingly
- (d) Prepare the annual budget submission

**A5.** A Service Level Agreement (SLA) typically specifies:

- (a) The technician's salary and work hours
- (b) Uptime targets, response times, and resolution time obligations
- (c) The brand of spare parts to be used
- (d) The number of users in each department

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (30 marks)

**B1.** List and briefly explain FOUR (4) types of maintenance relevant to computer system management. (8 marks)

**B2.** Describe the FIVE (5) steps of the Maintenance Requirements Assessment process as outlined in KP(1/5). (10 marks)

**B3.** A company has the following data for its file server over the past 12 months:
- Total operating time: 8,568 hours
- Number of failures: 3
- Total repair time: 9 hours

Calculate: (a) MTBF, (b) MTTR, (c) System Availability (%). Show all working. (6 marks)

**B4.** Explain why stakeholder consultation is important in maintenance requirements analysis. Name THREE (3) groups of stakeholders and the type of information each provides. (6 marks)

---

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (30 marks)

**C1.** You are appointed as the new IT Maintenance Manager for a logistics company with the following IT environment:
- 2 web servers (public e-commerce platform — company revenue-critical)
- 1 database server
- 30 warehouse workstations (barcode scanning and inventory system)
- 2 network switches and 1 firewall
- The company's SLA requires 99.9% uptime for the e-commerce platform (P1 resolution: 1 hour)

The previous maintenance manager left no records. There is no maintenance plan.

Describe how you would conduct a Maintenance Requirements Analysis for this company. Your answer should cover: asset classification, failure history approach, SLA-derived maintenance requirements, risk assessment, and the structure of the MRA output document. (30 marks)
