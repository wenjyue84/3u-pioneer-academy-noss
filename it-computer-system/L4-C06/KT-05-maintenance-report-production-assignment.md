## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks)

**A1.** The primary purpose of the monthly maintenance management report is:

- (a) To justify the maintenance manager's salary
- (b) To demonstrate accountability, measure performance against KPI targets, and support management decision-making
- (c) To list every technical fault in detail for the users
- (d) To replace the maintenance plan at the end of each month

**A2.** In a RAG dashboard, an Amber status for a KPI means:

- (a) The KPI is meeting its target — no action needed
- (b) The KPI has failed completely — immediate escalation required
- (c) The KPI is within 5% of the target threshold — action is required to prevent it becoming Red
- (d) The KPI data is unavailable for the period

**A3.** The Repeat Incident Rate KPI measures:

- (a) The number of times a technician repeats the same task
- (b) The percentage of incidents that are a recurrence of a previously resolved fault
- (c) How many times the same report is submitted
- (d) The number of incidents per month divided by the number of assets

**A4.** Which formula correctly calculates System Availability?

- (a) MTTR ÷ (MTBF + MTTR) × 100%
- (b) MTBF ÷ (MTBF + MTTR) × 100%
- (c) (MTBF − MTTR) ÷ MTBF × 100%
- (d) MTTR × MTBF × 100%

**A5.** The Executive Summary of the maintenance report should be:

- (a) A copy of the full technical KPI section
- (b) A maximum of one page covering key highlights, SLA compliance status, and management actions required
- (c) A list of all work orders completed in the month
- (d) Written by the IT Manager, not the maintenance manager

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (30 marks)

**B1.** List the TEN (10) sections of a complete monthly maintenance management report as described in KP(5/5). (10 marks)

**B2.** State FIVE (5) data sources a maintenance manager uses when compiling the monthly report. Briefly describe what information each source provides. (10 marks)

**B3.** The following data relates to June 2026 for a government agency's IT environment:
- Tier 1 server total operating time: 720 hours
- Total downtime (Tier 1 servers): 2.4 hours
- PM tasks scheduled: 20; PM tasks completed on time: 17
- P1 incidents: 1 (resolved in 3.5 hours)
- Total incidents: 12; incidents that were repeat faults: 2

Calculate: (a) System Availability %, (b) PM Compliance Rate %, (c) Repeat Incident Rate %. State for each whether the target is met (use: Availability ≥ 99.5%; PM Compliance ≥ 95%; Repeat Incident ≤ 10%). (10 marks)

---

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (30 marks)

**C1.** You are preparing the annual maintenance management report for the IT steering committee of a government ministry. During the year:
- System availability averaged 99.3% (target: 99.5%)
- There were 3 P1 incidents (one was caused by a delayed spare part procurement)
- The maintenance budget was underspent by 8%
- Four assets (two servers, two switches) are now 7 years old and approaching end-of-life
- PM compliance averaged 92% (target: 95%)

(a) Write the Executive Summary for this annual report (maximum 300 words). Cover: overall performance, areas of concern, and the key recommendation for the steering committee. (12 marks)

(b) Construct a KPI table for the year showing: KPI name, target, actual result, RAG status, and a one-sentence root cause for each Amber or Red item. Include at least five KPIs. (10 marks)

(c) Write THREE (3) specific management recommendations for the following year, each covering: the problem, the recommendation, and the expected benefit. (8 marks)
