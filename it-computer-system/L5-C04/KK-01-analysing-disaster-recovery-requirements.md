## Objektif / Objective

Conduct a complete disaster recovery requirements analysis for a simulated organisation, producing a BIA results table, risk register, and system priority classification.

## Senario / Scenario

**Syarikat Logistik Sejahtera Sdn. Bhd.** is a mid-sized logistics company operating 3 distribution warehouses across Peninsular Malaysia (Klang Valley, Johor Bahru, Pulau Pinang). The company operates 24 hours a day, 6 days a week.

Key IT systems:
- **WMS (Warehouse Management System):** Real-time inventory tracking; integrated with all 3 warehouses
- **TMS (Transport Management System):** Route planning and driver dispatch; used by 80 drivers
- **ERP (Accounting & Finance):** Payroll, accounts payable/receivable, financial reporting
- **Email server:** Internal communication; customer booking confirmations
- **HR portal:** Leave management, staff records
- **CCTV management server:** Security monitoring at all 3 warehouses

The company's revenue is approximately RM 2.5 million per month. Current IT infrastructure: primary data centre at Klang Valley HQ; no DR site; weekly tape backup stored on-site.

---

## Prosedur / Procedures

| Langkah | Arahan / Instruction |
|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Read the organisation scenario brief carefully. Identify all business functions and their supporting IT systems. List them in the BIA worksheet. |
| 2 | For each IT system, determine the Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD) based on the business impact. Justify your MTD values using financial, operational, and regulatory impact considerations. |
| 3 | Define the RTO and RPO for each system. Ensure RTO ≤ MTD. Document your reasoning for each value. |
| 4 | Assess each system's priority tier (Tier 1–4). Record the tier and justification in the BIA worksheet. |
| 5 | Complete the Risk Register. Identify at least FIVE (5) threats relevant to this organisation. For each threat, assess the likelihood (1–5) and impact (1–5), calculate the risk score, and classify as HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW. |
| 6 | Identify at least THREE (3) gaps in the current DR posture (e.g. no offsite backup, single point of failure, no DR plan). Document each gap and its potential consequence. |
| 7 | Compile your findings into a structured DR Requirements Report using the provided template. The report must include: BIA results table, risk register, gap analysis, and recommended system priority classification. |
| 8 | Present your DR Requirements Report to the instructor for review. Be prepared to justify your RTO/RPO values and risk ratings. |

---

## Hasil Jangkaan / Expected Outcome

A completed DR Requirements Report containing:
- BIA results table (all 6 systems; MTD, RTO, RPO, and priority tier for each)
- Risk register with minimum 5 threats assessed
- Gap analysis identifying minimum 3 gaps with consequences
- System priority classification (Tier 1–4)
- Executive summary paragraph (100–150 words)
