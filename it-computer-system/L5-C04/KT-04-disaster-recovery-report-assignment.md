## Soalan / Questions

### Bahagian A: Soalan Pelbagai Pilihan / Multiple Choice (20 marks — 2 marks each)

**A1.** A Post-Incident Report (PIR) should be submitted to management within how many business days of recovery completion (general best practice)?

- (a) 1 business day
- (b) 10 business days
- (c) 30 business days
- (d) Only when management requests it

**A2.** The PRIMARY purpose of a root cause analysis (RCA) in a DR Post-Incident Report is to:

- (a) Assign blame to the staff member who caused the incident
- (b) Identify the underlying cause(s) so that recurrence can be prevented
- (c) Calculate the exact financial loss from the incident
- (d) Satisfy the legal requirement to file a police report

**A3.** In the 5 Whys RCA method, asking "Why?" five times is intended to:

- (a) Identify five different people responsible for the incident
- (b) Drill down from the visible symptom to the true root cause
- (c) Produce five corrective actions automatically
- (d) Comply with ISO 22301 Clause 5 requirements

**A4.** Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) is calculated as:

- (a) Time from recovery start to recovery completion
- (b) Time from incident occurrence to detection/alert generation
- (c) Time from detection to DR plan activation
- (d) Total downtime divided by number of incidents in a year

**A5.** A Corrective Action Plan (CAP) item can be considered CLOSED when:

- (a) The assigned owner verbally confirms the action has been taken
- (b) The due date has passed
- (c) The remediation is verified with evidence and documented
- (d) The DR Coordinator approves the closure via email

**A6.** Under Bank Negara Malaysia's Risk Management in Technology (RMiT) policy, a significant IT incident must be reported to BNM:

- (a) Only in the annual audit report
- (b) Within 1 hour of detection; detailed report within 5 business days
- (c) Within 30 days of the incident
- (d) Only if the incident results in data loss of more than 1 TB

**A7.** Which section of the PIR is targeted at CEO-level readership and should use non-technical language?

- (a) Section 3 — Timeline
- (b) Section 10 — Recovery Procedures
- (c) Section 1 — Executive Summary
- (d) Section 8 — Technical Root Cause

**A8.** An RTO variance of 35% over the target is classified as:

- (a) Within acceptable limits — no action needed
- (b) Minor variance — document only
- (c) Major variance — requires formal corrective action plan and possible strategy change
- (d) Catastrophic — the organisation must purchase new hardware immediately

**A9.** The DR Annual Review Report should be presented to which governance body?

- (a) All company employees at the annual town hall
- (b) The Board Risk Committee or equivalent senior governance body
- (c) Only the IT helpdesk team
- (d) The software vendors supplying DR tools

**A10.** Which of the following is NOT typically a mandatory section of a Post-Incident Report?

- (a) Root Cause Analysis
- (b) Corrective Action Plan
- (c) Marketing strategy for customer recovery
- (d) Financial impact assessment

### Bahagian B: Soalan Jawapan Pendek / Short Answer (30 marks)

**B1.** You are reviewing a DR Test Report and notice the following:
- Target RTO: 4 hours
- Actual RTO achieved: 6 hours 30 minutes
- Target RPO: 1 hour
- Actual RPO achieved: 55 minutes

(a) Calculate the RTO variance (in hours and as a percentage). (2 marks)

(b) Classify the RTO variance and the RPO result. State the required management response for each. (4 marks)

(c) Identify THREE (3) plausible reasons why the RTO target was missed by such a large margin. For each reason, propose ONE corrective action. (6 marks)

**B2.** Explain the difference between a DR Test Report and a Post-Incident Report (PIR). In your answer, address: trigger event, primary audience, urgency of submission, and at least TWO sections present in the PIR that are NOT in a DR Test Report. (8 marks)

**B3.** Describe the purpose and content of the DR Annual Review Report. Why is it important that this report is presented to the Board Risk Committee rather than only to the IT Director? (5 marks)

**B4.** Define Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) and Mean Time to Respond (MTTR). Explain how high values of MTTD and MTTR individually affect an organisation's ability to meet its RTO target. (5 marks)

### Bahagian C: Soalan Esei / Essay (40 marks)

**C1.** The following data relates to a real disaster event at **Syarikat E-Dagang Gemilang Sdn. Bhd.**, an e-commerce company:

**Incident data:**
- Incident: Ransomware attack encrypting all file servers and the order management database
- Discovery time: Tuesday 11:30 (discovered by a staff member who could not open files)
- Estimated infection start: Tuesday 08:00 (based on log analysis)
- DR Coordinator notified: Tuesday 11:45
- DR plan activated: Tuesday 12:30
- Database restored from last clean backup (Monday 22:00): Tuesday 16:15
- Order management system confirmed operational: Tuesday 17:45
- Recovery declared: Tuesday 18:00
- Target RTO: 6 hours from activation
- Target RPO: 4 hours
- Estimated lost revenue: RM 320,000 (Tuesday 08:00 to Tuesday 18:00)
- Root cause (preliminary): Phishing email opened by accounts staff at 07:50; no endpoint detection and response (EDR) tool installed

(a) Reconstruct the incident timeline as a table with minimum 10 entries. Calculate the actual RTO, actual RPO, MTTD, and MTTR. Classify each metric as PASS or FAIL against the stated targets. (12 marks)

(b) Apply the 5 Whys method to the root cause (ransomware infection via phishing). Document all 5 Why levels and state the verified root cause. Identify TWO (2) contributing factors that worsened the impact. (10 marks)

(c) Write the Corrective Action Plan (CAP) for this incident. Identify FIVE (5) corrective actions. For each action, complete all fields: Item ID, Issue, Root Cause, Corrective Action, Owner (role), Target Date (weeks from incident), Priority (High/Medium/Low). Justify why the top 2 priority items are most urgent. (12 marks)

(d) Write the Executive Summary of the Post-Incident Report for this incident. Your summary must be 200–250 words, written for CEO-level readership (non-technical language), and cover: what happened, systems affected, recovery performance, financial impact, and the two most important actions being taken to prevent recurrence. (6 marks)
