## WA1 — Carry out preschool parental-community activities (P851-002-4:2025-C05-W01)

**WA Weightage:** 40%
**Jam Pengetahuan:** `⟪TBD: NOSS tidak menyatakan jam per-WA | rujuk 00-noss-extract.md §5⟫`
**Hasil Pembelajaran (dari CoCU):** Pelatih dapat mengenal pasti program tahunan ibu bapa-komuniti, mencadangkan proposal (objektif, lokasi, tarikh, bajet) untuk kelulusan pengurusan, dan mengendalikan program penglibatan komuniti mengikut objektif program.

Bab (ikut Related Knowledge, tertib CoCU):
1. Parent and community involvement theory and thoughts from scholars
2. Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bond (SMART) goals settings
3. Basic project proposal
4. Risk Assessment
5. Programme management
