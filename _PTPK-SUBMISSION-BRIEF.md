## Trainee eligibility — what recruitment must screen for (Garis Panduan 10.1)

Malaysian citizen · **18–55 years old** · not bankrupt · trained at a PL holding pentauliahan ·
not receiving other government funding during training · one TBT programme at a time ·
**once in a lifetime (SEKALI SAHAJA)** · **anyone who has previously received another government
training grant is disqualified (10.1 ix)**.

**10.2 Kumpulan Sasar Khusus names only two groups** — orang asli of the interior, and
minoriti/pribumi/bumiputera of Sabah & Sarawak. OKU and ex-offenders are **not** on that list. They
may enrol if they pass 10.1, but cannot be claimed as a special target group — and 10.1(ix) will
disqualify many ex-offenders who took government-funded prison skills training.

## What the AI field actually requires (IKLAN Lampiran 3, section iii)

The entire AI-specific criteria, in full — two items:
1. **Komitmen Penempatan** — vacancies, work location, salary range, recruitment timeline, aligned with the LoI/MoU/LoA
2. **Peta Kerjaya** — progression pathway after the course (e.g. basic → intermediate → advanced)

By comparison the EV field runs five pages and cybersecurity lists twelve domains. AI is the
lightest-specified of the seven priority fields. Both items already exist in the current proposals.

## Rules for whoever writes these

- Section numbering and headings follow Lampiran 2 exactly. Keep the Malay headings; explanatory
  body text may stay bilingual.
- **Never fabricate** an SSM number, address, phone, certificate number, capacity figure or date.
  Unknown → `⟪TBD: what | who supplies it | needed-by⟫`.
- Every RM figure must add up. The four cost lines must sum to the stated package price.
- Both packages stay at or under the **RM4,000** PTPK ceiling.
- Programme duration must sit between **7 days and 6 months** (Garis Panduan 9.3).
- Do not claim SKM, COPTPA or HRDC anywhere. The programme issues the provider's own
  Sijil Tamat Latihan; SKM would require pentauliahan not yet held.
