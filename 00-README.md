## Purpose

Convert NOSS (National Occupational Skills Standard) documents into WIM (Written Instructional Materials / Bahan Pengajaran Bertulis) for JPK (Jabatan Pembangunan Kemahiran) accreditation. WIM is the mandatory teaching/learning material package required by all Pusat Bertauliah (accredited training centres) under Malaysia's TVET system.
