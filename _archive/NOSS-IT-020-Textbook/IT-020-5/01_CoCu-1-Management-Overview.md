## Key terms (management — L5)

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Planning | Capacity planning (hardware, software, and space to meet future demand), budget planning (CAPEX/OPEX, licence and support costs), and lifecycle planning (refresh and replacement cycles). Plans must align with organisational strategy and policy and be reviewed periodically. Poor planning leads to shortages, overspend, or obsolete systems. |
| Coordination | Coordination between IT, users, and vendors: schedules for changes and maintenance windows, handover of new systems or services, and escalation paths. Clear communication and documented handover reduce errors and delays. |
| Monitoring | KPIs (e.g. availability, incident count, resolution time), system availability, and incident trends. Data is collected, reported, and escalated per procedure. Monitoring supports continuous improvement and early intervention. |
| Reporting | Status reports (projects, operations, incidents), cost reports (budget vs actual), and risk reports (issues and mitigations). Format and frequency are set by management. Accurate and timely reporting supports decision-making. |
