## Key terms (procurement — L4)

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Specifications | Technical and functional requirements for hardware, software, warranty, and support. Must align with organisational standards and budget. The specification document is used to invite quotations and to evaluate compliance; unclear specs lead to wrong or incompatible purchases. |
| Tender / quotation | Invitation to quote (ITQ) or request for quotation (RFQ) sent to vendors; evaluation criteria typically include price, technical compliance, warranty, delivery, and support. The process must follow organisational procurement policy and approval limits. |
| Vendor evaluation | Assessment of past performance, warranty terms, delivery capability, and support quality. Evaluation is documented and approved per policy. Poor vendor selection can result in delayed delivery, inadequate support, or compliance issues. |
| Procurement records | Purchase order (PO), delivery note, acceptance test record, and asset tag. Records are kept for audit, warranty claims, and asset management. Incomplete records complicate troubleshooting and audit. |
