# NOSS (National Occupational Skills Standards) Information

## Overview

**NOSS** stands for **National Occupational Skills Standards** (Standard Kemahiran Pekerjaan Kebangsaan). It is a comprehensive skills development framework established by the **Department of Skills Development (DSD)** under the **Ministry of Human Resources, Malaysia**.

## Purpose and Objectives

NOSS serves as a national framework for:
- Defining competency standards for various occupational areas
- Ensuring quality and consistency in skills training and assessment
- Aligning Malaysian workforce skills with industry requirements
- Supporting the development of a skilled workforce for national economic growth

## NOSS Framework Structure

### Competency Levels

NOSS defines five competency levels:

| Level | Certificate Type | Description |
|-------|------------------|-------------|
| **Level 1** | Malaysia Skills Certificate | Operation and Production Level - Competent in performing routine and predictable work activities |
| **Level 2** | Malaysia Skills Certificate | Operation and Production Level - Competent in varied work activities with some non-routine tasks requiring individual responsibility |
| **Level 3** | Malaysia Skills Certificate | Supervisory Level - Competent in complex and non-routine work activities with responsibility for others |
| **Level 4** | Malaysia Skills Diploma | Executive Level - Competent in complex technical/professional work with substantial personal responsibility |
| **Level 5** | Malaysia Skills Advanced Diploma | Managerial Level - Competent in applying fundamental principles across unpredictable contexts with significant responsibility |

## NOSS Development Process

### Development Committee Structure
Each NOSS is developed by expert panels consisting of:
- Industry practitioners
- Academic professionals
- Government representatives
- Training providers

### Development Phases
1. **Occupational Analysis** - Identifying job roles and requirements
2. **Competency Mapping** - Defining required skills and knowledge
3. **Standard Development** - Creating detailed competency standards
4. **Validation** - Industry and stakeholder review
5. **Approval** - Endorsement by National Skills Development Board (NSDB)

## NOSS Documentation Structure

### Standard Practice (SP)
Contains:
- Introduction and occupational overview
- Occupational structure and career paths
- Competency level descriptions
- Malaysian skill certification requirements
- Job competencies and working conditions
- Employment prospects
- Training and professional recognition
- Sources of additional information

### Standard Content (SC)
Contains:
- Competency profile charts
- Detailed competency profiles
- Curriculum of competency units (CoCU)
- Assessment criteria
- Tools, equipment, and materials requirements
- References and resources

### Curriculum of Competency Units (CoCU)
Each CoCU includes:
- Competency unit descriptor
- Work activities
- Related knowledge requirements
- Applied skills
- Attitude/safety/environmental considerations
- Training hours and delivery modes
- Assessment criteria
- Employability skills

## Certification Process

### Assessment Requirements
- Competency-based assessment
- Practical demonstration of skills
- Knowledge verification
- Workplace evidence collection
- Continuous assessment throughout training

### Certification Levels
- **Certificate of Attendance** - For individual competency units
- **Full Diploma/Advanced Diploma** - For completion of all core competencies
- **Elective Competencies** - Optional additional skills
