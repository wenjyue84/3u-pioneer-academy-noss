Businesses from multi-national corporations to home-based enterprises have to adopt current technologies to be competitive. Government agencies and institutions too, need to stay at the top of the game in order to provide fast, reliable and accurate services to the people. This climate creates job opportunities in computer system administration job area personnel to support the industry growth. The computer system administration job area personnel normally known as IT Executive by most of the organisations. This IT Executive is the middle management level who supervises and assigns job order to computer technician. As part of a technical team, and under the direction of the computer system and IT manager, the IT Executive operates and maintains desktop and laptop computers with special focus on customer and their business needs. Under the direction of IT manager, they need to perform various system administration activities such as operating system administration, evaluating, testing and installation of applications, performing or assisting with routine system maintenance and producing & maintaining current system configurations to maintain efficient day-to-day operation. In addition, the IT Executive creates and maintains records and inventories. Job prospect for this job area personnel is expected to grow in this decade. Through Economic Transformation Program (ETP) initiated by the government and rapid development of information technology in the workplace will continue to drive demand for more IT Executive in this country. To remain competitive, firms will continue to install sophisticated computer system with latest technology, making the outlook for IT Executive remains strong The IT Executive must possess strong interpersonal, communication, and leadership skills because they are required to interact not only with staff members, but also with other people inside and outside their organizations. They must possess teamwork skills to work on group projects and other collaborative efforts. Proposed Occupational Area Structure, NOSS Development Expert Panels (2012) DESCRIPTION OF COMPETENCY LEVEL The NOSS is developed for various occupational areas. Candidates for certification must be assessed and trained at certain levels to substantiate competencies. Below is a guideline of each NOSS Level as defined by the Department of Skills Development, Ministry of Human Resources, Malaysia. Malaysia Skills Certificate Level 1: (Operation and Production Level)Competent in performing a range of varied work activities, most of which are routine and predictable Malaysia Skills Certificate Level 2: (Operation and Production Level)Competent in performing a significant range of varied work activities, performed in a variety of contexts. Some of the activities are non-routine and required individual responsibility and autonomy. Malaysia Skills Certificate Level 3: (Supervisory Level)Competent in performing a broad range of varied work activities, performed in a variety of contexts, most of which are complex and non-routine. There is considerable responsibility and autonomy, and control or guidance of others in often required. Malaysia Skills Diploma Level 4: (Executive Level)Competent in performing a broad range of complex technical or professional work activities, performed in a variety of contexts, and with substantial degree of personal responsibility and autonomy. Responsibility for the work of others and allocation of resources is often present. Malaysia Skills Advanced Diploma Level 5: (Managerial Level)Competent in applying a significant range of fundamental principles and complex techniques across a wide and often unpredictable variety of contexts. Very substantial personal autonomy and often significant responsibility for the work of others and for the allocation of substantial resources features strongly, as do personal accountabilities for analysis, diagnosis, planning, execution and evaluation. MALAYSIAN SKILL CERTIFICATION Candidates who have attended and successfully completed each competency shall be awarded Certificate of Attendance on that competency. Those who have attended and successfully completed all the core competencies and fulfilled Malaysian Skill certification requirements shall receive Diploma Kemahiran Malaysia (Malaysia Skills Diploma) in Computer System Administration at Level 4. JOB COMPETENCIES IT Executive is competent in performing the following core competencies: Server Configuration Computer Network Installation Management Computer System Security Control Computer System Maintenance Management Computer System and Network Procurement Optionally, the IT Executive is competent in performing Computer Network Cabling Management as an elective competency. WORKING CONDITION IT Executive generally works in offices, computer rooms and at their clients workplaces. The job requires them to constantly move around, either within the office or to other business premises. They have to supervise computer system technician who act as a support staff. In bigger organisation, IT Executive is expected to work in shifts or be on call and may have to work extra hours to finish their job. The IT Executive generally work 40 hours a week at normal office hours, but depending on the nature of the business, they may have to work beyond the normal hours including on weekends. They need to keep up to date with the ever-changing specific software and operating systems and hardware related technology. Further, they must be able to understand new terminologies and grasp new technologies fast especially mobile devices and mobile technology. In addition, the IT Executive must also work well with people and should be able to communicate effectively. EMPLOYMENT PROSPECT IT Executives are employed by a range of organisations including government departments and agencies, institutions of higher learning, private corporations, banks, private companies that provide computer, database and network services to clients, telecommunication companies and many other private organisations. A growing number of computer system administration diploma holders are self-employed, working independently as contractors or consultants. TRAINING, INDUSTRIAL/PROFESSIONAL RECOGNITION, OTHER QUALIFICATIONS AND ADVANCEMENT 8.1 Industrial Recognition / professional qualification Computer system administration diploma holders have strong foundation in both areas, computer system and network. There are a few professional certifications that recognise IT personnel such as TheComputing Technology Industry Association (CompTIA), Cisco Networking Academy, Linux Professional Institute, SANS Institute and EC-Council. 8.2 Other prominent qualification recognised (in Malaysia or international) Technologies change rapidly and it is an essential part of the IT Executive to remain up to date with any development in hardware and software, as well as being aware of previous versions that may still be in operation. Among professional technical training that can enhance the IT Executive skill in performing their daily tasks are listed below. These training may prepare them to be an IT manager. Microsoft Certified Technology Specialist (MCTS) certification provides candidates with an opportunity to demonstrate proficiency with specific technologies that organizations worldwide struggle to effectively design, implement, and maintain every day. VMware Certified Professional (VCP) certification give employers (both current and future) confidence they can implement and maintain VMware-powered virtual environments Certified SonicWALL Security Administrator (CSSA) certification Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) Apple Certified Support Professional (ACSP) Project Management Professional (PMP) certification. The PMP certifies candidates ability to plan, budget, and complete projects efficiently, on time, and without cost overruns. Those are skills most every medium and large business needs within its IS department and such ability isnt going to be replaced by an app or third-party developer in our lifetimes. 8.3 Types of occupation for career advancement Career advancement for computer system administration level 4 personnel locally and internationally is enormous.
