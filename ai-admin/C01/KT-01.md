## Tugasan

### Bahagian A — Soalan Struktur Pendek (40 markah)

*Jawab semua soalan. Setiap soalan bernilai 8 markah.*

**Soalan A1 (8 markah)**

An office manager at a Malaysian trading company says: "We are already very busy — we don't have time to map our workflows before implementing AI. Let's just buy the tool and figure it out later."

(a) Identify **two specific risks** of implementing AI automation without first conducting workflow analysis. *(4 markah)*

(b) Explain what the office manager should do instead, and why this approach will save more time in the long run. *(4 markah)*

---

**Soalan A2 (8 markah)**

Study the following partial flowchart narrative for a purchase requisition process at a Malaysian SME:

> An admin clerk fills in a purchase requisition form. She submits it to her supervisor. The supervisor checks whether the amount is above RM3,000. If yes, the form goes to the director for approval. If no, the supervisor approves it directly. After approval, the clerk raises a purchase order and sends it to the supplier.

(a) Identify **all the correct flowchart symbols** that should be used in this process, and state what each represents. *(4 markah)*

(b) Identify the **swim lanes** (roles) that should appear in this flowchart. *(2 markah)*

(c) Which step in this process is the most likely **automation candidate**, and what is the automation approach? *(2 markah)*

---

**Soalan A3 (8 markah)**

An HR officer (gross salary RM3,000/month) at a 30-person company in Petaling Jaya spends 45 minutes every working day (22 days/month) manually updating the staff attendance register in an Excel spreadsheet.

(a) Calculate the **effective hourly rate** of the HR officer. Show your working. *(2 markah)*

(b) Calculate the **monthly time cost** (in hours) of this task. Show your working. *(2 markah)*

(c) Calculate the **monthly labour cost** of this task. Show your working. *(2 markah)*

(d) Calculate the **annual labour cost** of this task. *(2 markah)*

---

**Soalan A4 (8 markah)**

A company is conducting an AI Readiness Gap Assessment across five dimensions (Data, Technology, People, Process, Governance).

For each of the following situations, state which dimension is at risk and suggest one remediation action:

(a) The company stores all customer order information in physical notebooks — no digital records exist. *(2 markah)*

(b) The company's accounting software is a standalone desktop system purchased in 2009 with no internet connectivity and no API. *(2 markah)*

(c) The HR manager plans to adopt a new AI HR platform, but has not informed any of the 40 staff members about the change. *(2 markah)*

(d) The company has no documented policy on who is allowed to access customer data or what AI tools staff may use. *(2 markah)*

---

**Soalan A5 (8 markah)**

Using the Automation Opportunity Matrix (2×2 grid: low/high effort on one axis, low/high impact on the other):

(a) Define what "Quick Wins" means in this matrix. *(2 markah)*

(b) A Malaysian clinic manually generates 200 patient reminder SMS per week by copying and pasting from an Excel list into WhatsApp. An AI automation tool can do this automatically at a cost of RM80/month. Where on the matrix does this task fall? Justify your answer. *(3 markah)*

(c) The same clinic wants to automate its patient diagnosis workflow using AI — a complex project requiring clinical data integration and medical device connectivity. Where does this fall on the matrix? *(3 markah)*

### Bahagian B — Soalan Esei Gunaan (60 markah)

*Jawab KEDUA-DUA soalan. Setiap soalan bernilai 30 markah.*

**Soalan B1 (30 markah)**

You are the new admin assistant at **Syarikat Maju Bersama Sdn Bhd**, a Malaysian SME with 25 employees that provides cleaning and facility management services. Currently, all customer invoices are generated manually:
- The admin clerk types each invoice from scratch in Word
- The invoice is printed, signed, and physically handed or couriered to the customer
- Payment details are recorded in a paper ledger
- Monthly, the admin clerk manually tallies all invoices and payments in Excel for the accounts report
- Average volume: 60 invoices per month
- Time per invoice (typing + printing + recording): 20 minutes
- Admin clerk salary: RM2,200/month

(a) Draw or describe (in written form) the complete flowchart for this invoice process. Include all symbols used, identify decision points, and label each swim lane. *(10 markah)*

(b) Conduct a partial AI Readiness Gap Assessment for this scenario. Assess **three of the five dimensions** (your choice), identify the gap present in each, rate its severity (High/Medium/Low), and propose a remediation action. *(12 markah)*

(c) Calculate the total annual labour cost of the manual invoice process for this company. Then identify **one** AI tool from KP-02 that could partially or fully automate this process, state its approximate cost, and explain how it integrates with this company's existing workflow. *(8 markah)*

---

**Soalan B2 (30 markah)**

Review the following scenario:

> **MNK Trading** is a bumiputera-owned wholesale distributor in Kota Bharu, Kelantan. The company has 18 staff. All HR functions — leave applications, attendance tracking, and monthly HR summary reports — are handled manually by one HR clerk (salary RM2,500/month). Leave applications are submitted on paper forms, stored in a physical folder. The clerk manually cross-checks leave records against the attendance register each month before producing the HR summary, a process that takes approximately 4 hours every month-end. The company uses no HR software.

(a) Identify **five characteristics** of the leave application and attendance tracking process that make it a strong candidate for AI automation. For each, give one specific example from the MNK Trading scenario. *(10 markah)*

(b) The HR clerk, Puan Rohaida, is worried that the new HR automation system will eliminate her job. Using Kotter's 8-Step Change Model (as referenced in KP-03), identify **three steps** that the company director should take to address Puan Rohaida's concerns and build her support for the change. *(9 markah)*

(c) MNK Trading's director asks: "How do we know if the automation is worth it?" Design a simple **ROI tracking table** that the company could use for the first three months after implementing the new HR automation system. The table must include at least four metrics that can be measured and reported monthly. *(11 markah)*

## Skema Pemarkahan (Panduan Pensyarah)

### Bahagian A

**A1:** Risk 1 — automating inefficiency (executing redundant steps faster); Risk 2 — integration gaps discovered post-purchase / missed bottlenecks. (2 marks each) | Should map workflow first, identify bottleneck and root cause, select tool to address specific problem — saves rework cost and avoids purchasing wrong tool. (4 marks)

**A2:** Symbols: oval (start/end), rectangle (process steps — fill form, raise PO, send to supplier), diamond (RM3,000 decision), arrow (flow direction). (1 mark each, max 4) | Swim lanes: Admin Clerk, Supervisor, Director, Supplier. (0.5 each, max 2) | Decision step "check amount vs RM3,000" — automate with rule-based trigger in Power Automate (if amount ≥ RM3,000 → route to director approval email). (2 marks)

**A3:** (a) RM3,000 ÷ 208 = RM14.42/hour. (b) 45 min ÷ 60 = 0.75 hours/day × 22 days = 16.5 hours/month. (c) 16.5 × RM14.42 = RM237.93/month. (d) RM237.93 × 12 = RM2,855.16/year.

**A4:** (a) Data — remediate by digitising order records to Google Sheets or AutoCount. (b) Technology — remediate by upgrading accounting software or using a cloud-based alternative with API. (c) People — remediate by conducting staff briefings and identifying an AI Champion. (d) Governance — remediate by drafting an Acceptable Use Policy and data access control policy.

**A5:** (a) Quick Wins = low effort, high impact — automate first because they deliver visible results quickly with minimal risk. (b) Low effort (standard automation via WhatsApp Business API, RM80/month), High impact (200 reminders/week saves significant staff time, reduces missed appointments) → Quick Win quadrant. (c) High effort (complex clinical data integration, medical device connectivity, regulatory compliance), potentially high impact but too complex for immediate action → Major Project quadrant.

### Bahagian B

**B1(a):** Flowchart description must include: Start oval ("New invoice required") → Process rectangle ("Admin clerk types invoice in Word") → Process rectangle ("Print and sign") → Decision diamond ("Courier or hand-deliver?") with branches → Process rectangle ("Record in paper ledger") → Process rectangle ("Monthly tallying in Excel") → End oval. Swim lanes: Admin Clerk, Customer, Accounts. (10 marks: 4 for correct symbols, 3 for correct swim lanes, 3 for logical sequence)

**B1(b):** Any 3 of 5 dimensions assessed, each with gap + severity rating + remediation. Full marks require: accurate gap identified for the scenario (e.g., no digital invoice system = Data gap, High severity), severity justified, remediation specific (e.g., "adopt SQL Accounting or Xero for invoice generation"). (4 marks per dimension × 3 = 12 marks)

**B1(c):** Annual cost = (20 min ÷ 60) × 60 invoices = 20 hours/month × RM10.58/hour (RM2,200 ÷ 208) = RM211.54/month × 12 = RM2,538.46/year. Tool: any reasonable option from KP-02 (e.g., Xero or SQL Accounting for invoice generation; Zapier to trigger email delivery). Cost stated approximately. Integration explanation logical. (3+2+3 marks)

**B2(a):** Five characteristics from KP-01 criteria: Repetitive, Rule-based, High volume, Structured data, Measurable output. Each paired with a specific MNK Trading example. (2 marks each)

**B2(b):** Any 3 steps from Kotter's 8 steps, applied specifically to Puan Rohaida's situation. Best answers: Step 1 (create urgency — show Puan Rohaida the value of the time saved, which she can redirect to higher-value work), Step 4 (enlist volunteers — ask Puan Rohaida to be the AI Champion since she knows the HR process best), Step 6 (generate short-term wins — celebrate first successful automated leave approval with Puan Rohaida credited as the implementation champion). (3 marks each)

**B2(c):** Table must include at minimum: (1) Time spent on leave processing per month, (2) Number of leave application errors/corrections, (3) Time spent on monthly HR summary, (4) Staff satisfaction rating with leave process. Additional valid metrics accepted. Table format must include: metric name, baseline (Month 0), Month 1 actual, Month 2 actual, Month 3 actual, trend direction. (11 marks: 4 for metrics relevance, 4 for table structure, 3 for logical baseline values)
