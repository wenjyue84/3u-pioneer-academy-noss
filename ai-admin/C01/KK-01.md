## Work Activity Title

Map an Office Workflow and Identify Three Automation Opportunities for a Malaysian SME

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this work activity, trainees will have:
- Documented one existing office process using a standard flowchart with correct symbols
- Identified a minimum of three repetitive, rule-based tasks within the process suitable for AI automation
- Calculated the monthly time cost and labour cost for each identified automation opportunity
- Produced a written Automation Opportunity Report (Laporan Peluang Automasi) suitable for management review

### Step 3: Document the Workflow (30 minutes)

Open your chosen flowchart tool (Visio, Lucidchart, or draw.io) and draw the existing workflow.

**Requirements for the flowchart:**
- [ ] Use correct flowchart symbols (oval for start/end, rectangle for process steps, diamond for decisions, wavy rectangle for documents)
- [ ] Include at least 6 process steps
- [ ] Include at least 1 decision point (diamond) with Yes and No branches
- [ ] Use swim lanes to show which role (person/department) performs each step
- [ ] Add timing to each process step (e.g., "10 minutes")
- [ ] Total up all step times at the bottom (Total workflow time: ___ minutes per occurrence)

**Swim Lane Template (minimum roles to include):**

### Step 7: Write the Automation Opportunity Report (30 minutes)

Produce a written **Automation Opportunity Report (Laporan Peluang Automasi)** using Microsoft Word or Google Docs.

**Required sections (maximum 2 pages):**

**1. Executive Summary** (1 paragraph)
State the company scenario, the workflow analysed, and the top recommendation.

**2. Workflow Description**
Brief narrative description of the existing process (2–3 sentences). Attach the flowchart as Appendix A.

**3. Automation Opportunity Summary**
Insert the completed Section A table from Step 4.

**4. Time-Cost Analysis**
Insert the completed Section B table from Step 5. Note the total annual labour cost of the three identified tasks.

**5. Priority Recommendation**
State which opportunity should be addressed first (reference the priority matrix from Section C), the recommended AI tool, and the estimated cost of that tool (refer to KP-02 pricing data).

**6. Next Steps**
Three bullet points describing what the organisation should do next to begin implementation (e.g., "Obtain a trial account for [Tool X]", "Conduct a PDPA data handling review before connecting the tool to customer records").

Filename: `Report_AutomasiPeluang_[YourName]_C01KK01.docx`

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. Which step in the workflow analysis process did you find most difficult? What would help you do it better next time?
2. Were any of the automation opportunities you identified "Quick Wins"? What makes them quick wins rather than major projects?
3. If you were the manager of this SME, what would be the single most important factor in deciding whether to proceed with the top automation recommendation?
4. Did your time-cost calculation surprise you? Is the annual labour cost of these three tasks higher or lower than you expected, and why?
