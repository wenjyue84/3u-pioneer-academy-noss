## Tugasan

### Bahagian A — Soalan Struktur Pendek (40 markah)

*Jawab semua soalan. Setiap soalan bernilai 8 markah.*

**Soalan A1 (8 markah)**

A company attempts to adopt AI tools for all departments simultaneously during the first month, without any preparatory work.

(a) Name **two specific problems** that are likely to result from this approach, based on the warnings in KP-03. *(4 markah)*

(b) Describe what the company should do in **Phase 1** of a properly planned AI implementation roadmap, including the success criteria that must be met before moving to Phase 2. *(4 markah)*

---

**Soalan A2 (8 markah)**

Study the following employee statements and identify the specific fear behind each. Then, using KP-03, suggest one communication or action strategy to address each fear:

(a) *"I've been doing this job for 12 years without a computer. I don't think I can learn all these new systems."* *(2 markah)*

(b) *"If this AI can do everything I do, why does the company still need me?"* *(2 markah)*

(c) *"The management never told us about this. I only found out from a colleague. Something feels suspicious."* *(2 markah)*

(d) *"I tried to use the new system last week and it crashed. Now I have to catch up on all the work I missed."* *(2 markah)*

---

**Soalan A3 (8 markah)**

An accounts manager (gross salary RM4,500/month) at a Malaysian company spends 90 minutes per working day (22 days per month) manually preparing and sending payment reminder emails to customers with overdue invoices. There are typically 35 such reminders per month.

(a) Calculate the **effective hourly rate** of the accounts manager. *(1 markah)*

(b) Calculate the **monthly time cost** of this task. *(2 markah)*

(c) Calculate the **monthly labour cost** and **annual labour cost** of this task. *(2 markah)*

(d) A Power Automate flow can automate this process at no extra cost (included in the company's existing Microsoft 365 subscription). Calculate the **Year 1 ROI**, given that the implementation took 6 hours of the IT manager's time (salary RM5,000/month) and 2 hours of the accounts manager's time for testing. Show all working. *(3 markah)*

---

**Soalan A4 (8 markah)**

Kotter's 8-Step Change Model is referenced in KP-03 for managing AI adoption.

(a) Briefly explain **Step 2 (Build a Guiding Coalition)** in the context of AI adoption in a Malaysian SME. What is an "AI Champion" (Juara AI) and what role does this person play? *(4 markah)*

(b) Why is **Step 6 (Generate Short-Term Wins)** particularly important in a Malaysian SME context, compared to a large corporation? *(4 markah)*

---

**Soalan A5 (8 markah)**

A company's admin manager is preparing the financial section of an AI automation business case for the director. She has calculated the following:

- Annual labour benefit: RM18,600
- Year 1 costs: Tool subscription RM1,200 + Setup RM800 + Training RM600 = RM2,600
- Year 2 costs: Tool subscription RM1,200 only

(a) Calculate **Year 1 ROI (%)** and **Year 2 ROI (%)**. Show working. *(4 markah)*

(b) Calculate the **Payback Period** in months. Show working. *(2 markah)*

(c) The director asks: "The Year 1 ROI seems low — should we proceed?" Write a one-paragraph response the admin manager could give to address this concern. *(2 markah)*

### Bahagian B — Soalan Esei Gunaan (60 markah)

*Jawab KEDUA-DUA soalan. Setiap soalan bernilai 30 markah.*

**Soalan B1 (30 markah)**

**Pejabat Pentadbiran Akademik, Kolej Teknikal Maju** is a technical college in Kuala Lumpur with 80 administrative staff. The college has decided to adopt Microsoft 365 Copilot and Power Automate to automate:
- Student fee reminder letters (monthly, 200 students)
- Staff leave application workflow
- Monthly academic department report compilation

The college Principal, Dr. Faridah, has given you — the IT and Admin Manager — the task of designing the complete AI adoption plan.

(a) Design a **phased AI implementation roadmap** for this college, covering all three phases from KP-03. For each phase, state: objectives, specific activities for this college, success criteria, and the duration. *(15 markah)*

(b) Two months into the implementation, you discover that several senior academic staff members are not using Copilot because they believe it will make student communications impersonal and they do not trust the AI to represent the college's academic standards. Design a **targeted change management response** for this specific resistance situation, applying **at least three steps** from Kotter's model. Be specific about what you would say or do for this group. *(10 markah)*

(c) Design a **training path** for three types of college staff: (i) admin clerks who will use Copilot daily, (ii) department heads who will receive AI-generated reports, (iii) IT support staff who will maintain the Power Automate flows. For each type, state total training hours, one key module, and one learning outcome. *(5 markah)*

**Soalan B2 (30 markah)**

**Senario:** Encik Izzat is the operations manager at **Syarikat Urus Cepat Sdn Bhd**, a document courier company in Kuala Lumpur with 35 staff. He has identified the following manual workflows for potential AI automation:

| Workflow | Person Responsible | Salary | Time per Occurrence | Monthly Volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Booking confirmation emails | Admin clerk | RM2,000/month | 8 minutes per email | 120 bookings |
| Driver dispatch notification (WhatsApp) | Operations clerk | RM2,300/month | 5 minutes per notification | 150 dispatches |
| Monthly customer billing summary | Accounts clerk | RM2,500/month | 4 hours (total monthly) | 1 report |

Tool being considered: **Zapier Professional** at USD 49/month (~RM230/month, indicative) + WhatsApp Business API connector at RM150/month.

(a) Calculate the **monthly labour cost and annual labour cost** for all three workflows combined. Show all working. *(12 markah)*

(b) Using the annual labour cost from (a) as the total annual benefit, and the following Year 1 costs:
- Tool subscription: RM230 + RM150 = RM380/month × 12 = RM4,560/year
- Setup (IT, 16 hours × RM25/hour): RM400
- Training (3 staff, 3 hours each, at RM10/hour average): RM90

Calculate: (i) Year 1 ROI (%), (ii) Year 1 Payback Period (months), (iii) Year 2 ROI (%), assuming only the subscription cost remains in Year 2. Show all working. *(12 markah)*

(c) Encik Izzat wants to present this business case to the company director in a 5-minute briefing. Write the **Executive Summary** paragraph he would use as the opening statement. The paragraph must include: the problem, the recommendation, the Year 1 ROI, the payback period, and a closing statement that addresses the risk of NOT proceeding. *(6 markah)*

## Skema Pemarkahan (Panduan Pensyarah)

### Bahagian A

**A1:** (a) Two problems: (i) staff overwhelmed — too many new tools simultaneously, training cannot keep pace, adoption rate drops; (ii) integration issues compound — when multiple tools are deployed at once, connection failures across tools create a cascade of problems that are hard to debug. Accept other valid answers from KP-03. (2 marks each) | (b) Phase 1 objectives: complete workflow analysis and AI readiness assessment, address critical gaps, deploy 1–2 Quick Win automations. Success criteria: at least one automation running reliably for 4 consecutive weeks; staff using it without trainer assistance. (2 marks objectives + 2 marks criteria)

**A2:** (a) Fear: incompetence / not tech-savvy. Strategy: structured step-by-step training; pair with more tech-confident colleague; celebrate small wins. (b) Fear: job replacement. Strategy: reframe — AI handles the tedious parts, role evolves to higher-value tasks; show examples of what they will do more of. (c) Fear: management distrust / hidden agenda. Strategy: transparent communication from leadership — town hall, written FAQ, open-door policy. (d) Fear: extra workload / unreliable system. Strategy: IT rapid response; acknowledge the disruption; provide temporary manual fallback; fix the issue before resuming; give realistic timeline. (2 marks each = 8 marks)

**A3:** (a) RM4,500 ÷ 208 = RM21.63/hour. (b) 90 min ÷ 60 = 1.5 hours/day × 22 days = 33 hours/month. (c) 33 × RM21.63 = RM713.79/month; × 12 = RM8,565.48/year. (d) IT cost: RM5,000 ÷ 208 = RM24.04/hour × 6 hours = RM144.24. Accounts manager testing: RM21.63/hour × 2 hours = RM43.26. Total Year 1 cost = RM144.24 + RM43.26 = RM187.50. Year 1 ROI = [(RM8,565.48 - RM187.50) ÷ RM187.50] × 100 = 4,467%. (Note: ROI is very high because subscription cost is RM0 — already included in M365. Award marks for correct formula application regardless of magnitude of result.)

**A4:** (a) The guiding coalition is a small group of respected, enthusiastic individuals — the "AI Champions" (Juara AI) — who lead the change from within. In a Malaysian SME, the AI Champion is typically a mid-level staff member who understands both the old process and the new tool, and acts as a peer trainer and advocate. This person is critical because management-only mandates often fail without internal peer influence. (2 marks definition + 2 marks role) | (b) In a large corporation, a failed AI pilot can be absorbed — resources and brand credibility continue. In a Malaysian SME, one failure can destroy management confidence in the entire AI programme permanently, especially if the owner funded it personally. Short-term wins — even a small one like "our leave applications are now 100% paperless" — rebuild confidence quickly and justify continued investment. (4 marks)

**A5:** (a) Year 1 ROI = [(18,600 - 2,600) ÷ 2,600] × 100 = [16,000 ÷ 2,600] × 100 = 615.4%. Year 2 ROI = [(18,600 - 1,200) ÷ 1,200] × 100 = [17,400 ÷ 1,200] × 100 = 1,450%. (b) Payback Period = Annual Cost ÷ Monthly Benefit. Monthly benefit = RM18,600 ÷ 12 = RM1,550. Payback = RM2,600 ÷ RM1,550 = 1.68 months (~51 days). (c) Model answer: "Director, the Year 1 ROI of 615% may seem exceptionally high, but this reflects that our main cost — staff time — is already being paid regardless of whether the task is automated. The RM2,600 investment recovers itself in under 2 months. From Month 2 onward, we are generating pure savings. The Year 1 figure would appear more modest if we were paying for new headcount to deliver this benefit — we are not. I recommend we proceed."

### Bahagian B

**B1(a):** Phase 1 (Months 1–2): objectives — complete workflow/readiness analysis, select and procure Copilot, configure leave automation workflow in Power Automate. Success criteria: leave automation live and used by all staff without trainer help. (5 marks) | Phase 2 (Months 3–5): deploy Copilot for fee reminder letters and report compilation; train all 80 admin staff. Success criteria: 3+ automations operational; time-saving data collected for 2 months. (5 marks) | Phase 3 (Month 6+): ROI review; scale to additional departments; present to Principal; plan next cycle. Success criteria: documented ROI report presented; next 12-month plan approved. (5 marks)

**B1(b):** Any 3 Kotter steps applied specifically to senior academic staff resistance. Top marks require specificity: Step 4 (enlist volunteers — identify 1–2 enthusiastic academic staff to be AI Champions for their departments; ask them to share positive experiences with reluctant colleagues); Step 5 (enable action — arrange a customised demonstration showing Copilot helping to draft formal student communication letters in the college's existing style and tone, not replacing it); Step 6 (generate short-term wins — share data showing student satisfaction improved after faster response times). (3–4 marks per step = 10 marks)

**B1(c):** Admin clerks (Tier 2): 6 hours total; module — Using Copilot in Word and Outlook daily; LO — draft a standard student correspondence letter using Copilot in under 5 minutes. Dept heads (Tier 3): 2 hours; module — Understanding AI-generated outputs; LO — critically evaluate an AI-generated department report and identify data that requires human verification. IT support (Tier 1): 20 hours; module — Power Automate flow building; LO — build and deploy a 3-step automated workflow using SharePoint and Outlook. (5 marks: 1.5 per staff type + 0.5 overall structure)

**B2(a):** Booking confirmation: (8÷60) × 120 = 16 hours/month. Rate: RM2,000÷208 = RM9.62/hr. Monthly cost: 16 × RM9.62 = RM153.92. Annual: RM1,847.04. | Dispatch notification: (5÷60) × 150 = 12.5 hours/month. Rate: RM2,300÷208 = RM11.06/hr. Monthly cost: 12.5 × RM11.06 = RM138.25. Annual: RM1,659.00. | Billing summary: 4 hours/month × 1. Rate: RM2,500÷208 = RM12.02/hr. Monthly: 4 × RM12.02 = RM48.08. Annual: RM576.96. | Combined monthly: RM153.92 + RM138.25 + RM48.08 = RM340.25. Combined annual: RM4,083.00. (12 marks: 3 per workflow + 3 for combined totals)

**B2(b):** Annual benefit = RM4,083.00. (i) Year 1: Total cost = RM4,560 + RM400 + RM90 = RM5,050. Year 1 ROI = [(4,083 - 5,050) ÷ 5,050] × 100 = [-967 ÷ 5,050] × 100 = -19.2%. (ii) Payback period = RM5,050 ÷ (RM4,083 ÷ 12) = RM5,050 ÷ RM340.25 = 14.8 months (~15 months). (iii) Year 2: cost = RM4,560. ROI = [(4,083 - 4,560) ÷ 4,560] × 100 = [-11.7%]. Note: ROI is negative in both years — this is an intentional scenario to test student understanding that not all automation projects yield positive ROI. Award full marks for correct calculations regardless of outcome. (12 marks: 4 per calculation item)

**B2(c):** Model executive summary must include all 5 required elements. Award marks proportionally. Example: "Syarikat Urus Cepat currently spends RM340 per month — RM4,083 per year — on three administrative tasks that can be fully automated: booking confirmations, dispatch notifications, and monthly billing summaries. I recommend adopting Zapier Professional with a WhatsApp Business API connector at a total investment of RM5,050 in Year 1. Based on our analysis, the full investment will be recovered in approximately 15 months, after which the automation will generate net savings annually. While Year 1 ROI is negative, this reflects a one-time setup cost that does not recur. If we do not proceed, we will continue paying the equivalent of one staff member's productive hours each month for tasks a RM380/month system can perform reliably and faster." (6 marks: 1 per element + 1 for overall professional tone)
