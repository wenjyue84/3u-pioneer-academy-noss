## Work Activity Title

Build a 90-Day AI Implementation Roadmap and Present to a Simulated Management Panel

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this work activity, trainees will have:
- Produced a complete 90-day AI implementation roadmap document for a Malaysian SME scenario
- Selected at least two AI tools with written justification based on the CITED framework (KP-02)
- Estimated subscription and setup costs in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR)
- Completed a simple ROI projection table for Year 1 and Year 2
- Presented key findings in a structured 5-minute management briefing (verbal or slide-based)

---

## Safety Precautions

- Use only the fictional SME scenario from KK-01 — do not use real company data
- Do not present AI tool pricing as confirmed figures — label all costs as "indicative" or "anggaran"
- Do not fabricate statistics or government data — use only what is provided in the KP reference materials
- Save a backup copy of your work before the management briefing session

### Step 7: Prepare and Deliver the 5-Minute Management Briefing (30 minutes preparation + 5 minutes delivery)

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tool selection justification** | CITED all 5 criteria completed for both tools; clear recommendation with rationale | 4 criteria completed; recommendation stated | 3 criteria; recommendation without rationale | Incomplete table; no recommendation |
| **Roadmap completeness** | All 13 weeks covered; owners assigned; deliverables specific | 10+ weeks; most owners assigned | 8+ weeks; minimal owner detail | Fewer than 8 weeks; no owners |

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. During the CITED framework comparison, which criterion did you find hardest to evaluate, and why?
2. If the ROI calculation showed a negative return in Year 1 and Year 2, would you still recommend proceeding? Under what circumstances might a negative ROI still be justified?
3. What would you include in the 5-minute management briefing that you did not have time to present, and how would you make it available to the management panel afterwards?
4. If you were implementing this roadmap in a real company, which week in the 90-day plan do you think carries the highest risk of delay? What would you do to prevent that delay?
