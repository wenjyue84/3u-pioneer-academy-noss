## Bahagian A — Pilihan Berganda / Multiple Choice (20 markah)

*Setiap soalan bernilai 2 markah. Bulatkan jawapan yang PALING TEPAT.*

**1.** Which of the following is the FIRST step that should be taken before implementing any AI automation tool in an office?

A. Purchase the AI tool subscription  
B. Train all staff on the new AI tool  
C. Document and analyse existing workflows  
D. Calculate the expected ROI of the new tool  

**2.** In a flowchart, which symbol is used to represent a decision point (e.g., "Is the invoice amount above RM5,000?")?

A. Rectangle  
B. Oval  
C. Diamond  
D. Parallelogram  

**3.** A task is described as: "performed 200 times per month, uses a standard form, and the outcome is always either approved or rejected based on clear rules." Which characteristic does this task MOST demonstrate that makes it a good automation candidate?

A. It requires creative judgment  
B. It is repetitive and rule-based  
C. It involves unstructured data input  
D. It requires physical presence  

**4.** Microsoft Power Automate is BEST suited for organisations that:

A. Need to automate actions in legacy software with no internet connectivity  
B. Already use Microsoft 365 and want to connect Outlook, SharePoint, and Teams  
C. Want an open-source, self-hosted solution to reduce data residency risks  
D. Need to automate tasks on a budget below RM50/month  

**5.** What does "self-hosted" mean in the context of n8n (as explained in KP-02)?

A. The tool is installed on the user's mobile phone  
B. The tool runs on the company's own server, keeping data within their infrastructure  
C. The tool is provided for free by the Malaysian government  
D. The tool does not require an internet connection to operate  

**6.** An admin clerk (RM2,400/month gross) spends 30 minutes per day (22 working days/month) on a manual task. What is the CORRECT monthly labour cost of this task?

A. RM69.23

A. The AI tools are too expensive  
B. The internet connection is too slow  
C. Employee resistance and poor change management

A. Drafting the official government policy for AI use  
B. Identifying a group of respected internal champions to lead the change  
C. Hiring external consultants to manage the AI project  
D. Building the technical infrastructure before announcing the change  

**9.** The ROI formula used in KP-03 is:

A. (Annual Cost − Annual Benefit) ÷ Annual Benefit × 100  
B. (Annual Benefit − Annual Cost) ÷ Annual Benefit × 100

A. Efficiency

## Bahagian B — Soalan Berstruktur / Structured Questions (50 markah)

*Jawab EMPAT (4) daripada lima soalan berikut. Setiap soalan bernilai 12–13 markah.*

**B1 (12 markah)**

An HR officer (RM3,200/month) at a 50-person Malaysian manufacturing company manually prepares the monthly overtime summary report. This takes 5 hours every month. The HR manager wants to automate this using Power Automate.

(a) Calculate the **monthly labour cost** of the manual overtime report. Show all working. *(3 markah)*

(b) The Power Automate setup is estimated to take 8 hours of IT support time (IT support salary: RM3,500/month). The Power Automate licence is included in the existing Microsoft 365 subscription. Calculate **Year 1 total cost** and **Year 1 ROI**. Show working. *(5 markah)*

(c) What is the **payback period** in months? *(2 markah)*

(d) The HR officer expresses concern that the automated report might contain errors she would not catch. Suggest **two safeguards** to address this concern, drawing from KP-03. *(2 markah)*

---

**B2 (12 markah)**

Study the table below and answer all parts:

| Characteristic | Task X | Task Y |
|---|---|---|
| Repetitive | Yes — 300 times/month | No — one-off per quarter |
| Rule-based | Yes — standard approval rules | No — requires management judgement |
| Volume | High | Low |
| Data type | Structured form | Verbal discussion notes |
| Creativity required | None | High |

(a) Which task is the better automation candidate? Justify using **all five characteristics** in the table. *(5 markah)*

(b) Using the Automation Opportunity Matrix from KP-01, classify Task X (assume it takes only 3 minutes to set up automation but saves 50 hours/month). Which quadrant does it fall in, and what action should be taken? *(3 markah)*

(c) Name **two types of tasks** that are explicitly identified in KP-01 as NOT suitable for AI automation. For each, explain why. *(4 markah)*

---

**B3 (13 markah)**

A Malaysian property development company is evaluating two tools for automating its client communication workflow:

- **Tool A:** Zapier Professional — USD 49/month (~RM230/month), 2,000 tasks/month, 400+ integrations
- **Tool B:** Microsoft Power Automate Premium — USD 15/user/month (~RM70/user/month), unlimited flows, deep Microsoft integration, no usage cap

The company has 8 staff members and currently uses Microsoft 365 Business Standard.

(a) Using the CITED framework, evaluate **both tools** against all five criteria for this company. *(10 markah — 1 mark per criterion per tool)*

(b) State your final tool recommendation and give **one decisive reason** in the context of this company's existing Microsoft 365 environment. *(3 markah)*

---

**B4 (12 markah)**

A Malaysian SME director has received your AI automation proposal and says: "The Year 1 ROI looks good, but I'm still not convinced. What if the tool company closes down? What if our staff refuse to use it? What if it causes data leaks?"

(a) Each of the director's three concerns represents a risk. Name the risk category and provide one mitigation for each. *(6 markah)*

(b) In a well-structured AI automation business case (KP-03 structure), which of the **five sections** directly addresses the director's concerns? State the section name and explain what it should contain to address these specific concerns. *(4 markah)*

(c) The KP-03 roadmap recommends a 90-day phased approach. Which phase would most effectively demonstrate to a sceptical director that the project is working? Explain. *(2 markah)*

---

**B5 (12 markah)**

Describe the three-phase AI implementation roadmap from KP-03 for the following scenario:

**Scenario:** Syarikat Pakar Cukai Sdn Bhd, a tax consultancy firm in Penang with 15 staff, wants to automate three processes: (1) client document request emails (currently 40 per month, 10 min each), (2) monthly tax deadline reminder notifications (50 clients, 5 min each), and (3) generating standard tax computation worksheets from client-supplied Excel data.

For each phase, state:
- Phase name and timeframe
- Which of the three processes (if any) would be addressed in this phase, and why
- One specific activity
- One success criterion

*(12 markah — 4 per phase)*

Discuss this statement with reference to: (a) the purpose of workflow mapping and what information it captures; (b) the role of the AI Readiness Gap Analysis in identifying organisation-specific barriers; (c) one worked example showing how skipping workflow analysis could result in a costly or ineffective automation outcome for a Malaysian SME.

| 2 | **C** | Diamond = decision point (KP-01, Section 2.1) |
| 3 | **B** | Repetitive + rule-based = automation candidate (KP-01, Section 3.1) |
| 4 | **B** | Power Automate best for Microsoft 365 environments (KP-02, Section 4.2) |
| 5 | **B** | Self-hosted = runs on company's own server (KP-02, Section 4.3) |
| 6 | **A** | Rate = RM2,400÷208 = RM11.54/hr; 0.5hr×22=11hr/month; 11×RM11.54=RM69.23 (KP-01, Section 5.2) |
| 7 | **C** | "most common reason…is not technology — it is people" (KP-03, Section 2.1) |
| 8 | **B** | Guiding coalition = respected internal champions (KP-03, Section 2.2) |

### Bahagian B — Panduan Pemarkahan

**B1:** (a) Rate = RM3,200÷208 = RM15.38/hr. Monthly cost = 5 × RM15.38 = RM76.92. (b) IT cost = RM3,500÷208×8 = RM134.62. Year 1 total cost = RM134.62. Annual benefit = RM76.92×12 = RM923.04. ROI = [(923.04−134.62)÷134.62]×100 = 585.7%. (c) Payback = RM134.62 ÷ (RM923.04÷12) = RM134.62÷RM76.92 = 1.75 months. (d) Build in email notification when report is generated; HR officer reviews and approves before distribution; maintain manual override option.

**B2:** (a) Task X is better: (1) Repetitive — 300 times/month, high frequency means high time savings; (2) Rule-based — standard rules can be programmed into automation; (3) High volume — greater savings per automation unit; (4) Structured data — forms are easily processed by automation tools; (5) No creativity — no judgment required, automation can execute reliably. Task Y fails all five criteria. (b) Task X: Low effort (3-minute setup), High impact (50 hours/month saved) → Quick Win quadrant → automate immediately. (c) Complex negotiations — require nuanced human relationship management; one-off tasks — setup cost exceeds benefit (1 mark each reason).

**B3:** CITED table — both tools scored across all 5 criteria. Key points: Cost: Zapier RM230/month flat; Power Automate RM70×8=RM560/month for 8 users (or included in M365 Standard for basic flows). Integration: Power Automate far superior for Microsoft 365. Training: similar. Environment: both cloud-based; Power Automate has Microsoft's enterprise DPA. Dependability: both established. Recommendation: Power Automate — basic flows already included in M365 Business Standard subscription at no additional cost; deep native Microsoft integration removes need for external connectors. (10 marks table + 3 recommendation)

**B4:** Three risks: (1) Tool vendor closure — risk: vendor dependency; mitigation: export automation logic documentation; evaluate vendor stability before committing. (2) Staff refusal — risk: change resistance; mitigation: apply Kotter steps 2–6 (AI Champion, training, short-term wins). (3) Data leak — risk: data privacy breach; mitigation: PDPA-compliant data handling policy; restrict what data types flow through the automation; review vendor DPA. (2 marks each risk+mitigation) | Section 5 (Implementation Plan and Risk Register) — must include risk table with likelihood, impact, and mitigation for each identified risk. | Phase 1 / the first 4 weeks — by demonstrating one Quick Win automation running reliably, the director can see tangible evidence before the full budget is committed.

**B5:** Phase 1 (Months 1–2): Address process 1 (client document request emails — Quick Win). Activity: deploy Power Automate to trigger standard email when document checklist item is marked complete in SharePoint. Success criterion: 40 emails automated with zero manual intervention for 4 consecutive weeks. Phase 2 (Months 3–5): Address process 2 (deadline reminders). Activity: configure Zapier or Power Automate to send reminder emails/WhatsApp 30 days, 14 days, and 7 days before each client's tax deadline using a Dates spreadsheet. Success criterion: all 50 clients receive correctly timed reminders for one full deadline cycle. Phase 3 (Month 6+): Address process 3 (tax worksheet generation — more complex, requires data validation). Activity: configure Excel Copilot or a Python-based automation to generate worksheet from client-provided CSV. Success criterion: worksheet generated in under 5 minutes with accuracy validated against manual check on 10 sample clients.

### Bahagian C — Panduan Pemarkahan

**C1 (15 marks):** Award marks for: (a) workflow mapping captures sequence, roles, timing, decision points, handoffs — forms the evidence base for automation design (5 marks); (b) AI Readiness Gap Analysis identifies organisation-specific barriers across Data/Technology/People/Process/Governance — prevents generic solutions applied to specific problems (5 marks); (c) worked example must show a logical cause-effect: e.g., automating a flawed approval process that has a missing data step — without mapping, the automation replicates the gap; company discovers the problem only after 3 months and RM5,000 of subscription fees (5 marks).

**C2 (15 marks):** Award marks for: specific fears named with Malaysian workplace examples (3 marks); Kotter model steps applied with specificity — not just naming the steps but explaining what the admin manager would do in a 30-person SME context (8 marks); AI Champion concept applied to SME where there is no dedicated HR or IT department — the Champion is a mid-level staff person trusted by peers (2 marks); short-term wins and sustained communication described with concrete examples, not generic statements (2 marks).

**C3 (15 marks):** (a) ROI formula + effective hourly rate formula explained with correct variables (4 marks); (b) Three non-financial benefits: error reduction, faster processing enabling better customer service, staff redeployment to higher-value tasks — brief explanation of each (6 marks); (c) Worked example with Malaysian salary data: must include a real occupation, a realistic salary, a common workflow (e.g., leave application, invoice processing, report compilation), correct Year 1 ROI, Year 2 ROI, and Payback Period calculations with working shown (5 marks — deduct 1 mark for each missing calculation element).
