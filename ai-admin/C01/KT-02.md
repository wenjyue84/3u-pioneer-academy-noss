## Tugasan

### Bahagian A — Soalan Struktur Pendek (40 markah)

*Jawab semua soalan. Setiap soalan bernilai 8 markah.*

**Soalan A1 (8 markah)**

KP-02 classifies AI office automation tools into three categories.

(a) Name the three categories and give **one example tool** for each. *(6 markah)*

(b) A Malaysian admin manager wants to automatically draft a response to every customer complaint email received in Outlook, without sending it — just drafting for human review. Which **category** of tool is most appropriate, and which specific tool would you recommend? *(2 markah)*

---

**Soalan A2 (8 markah)**

Compare Microsoft 365 Copilot and Google Workspace Gemini across **four criteria** most relevant to a Malaysian SME that currently uses Microsoft Office desktop applications and AutoCount accounting software.

Present your answer in a comparison table. Conclude with a one-sentence recommendation. *(8 markah)*

---

**Soalan A3 (8 markah)**

Zapier, Microsoft Power Automate, and n8n are all workflow automation platforms, but each has a distinct advantage.

(a) Explain the primary advantage of **Microsoft Power Automate** over Zapier for a company already on Microsoft 365. *(2 markah)*

(b) Explain the primary advantage of **n8n (self-hosted)** over Zapier from a **PDPA 2010 compliance** perspective. *(3 markah)*

(c) A startup in Cyberjaya has a monthly automation budget of RM150 and no dedicated IT staff. Rank the three platforms (Zapier, Power Automate, n8n) in order of suitability and justify your ranking. *(3 markah)*

---

**Soalan A4 (8 markah)**

An insurance company in Malaysia uses a legacy claims management system from 2008. The system has no API, no web interface, and no export function — data can only be accessed by logging into the system's desktop application and reading information from the screen.

(a) Which category of automation tool is required to automate tasks in this system? Explain why. *(3 markah)*

(b) Name **one specific tool** from KP-02 that performs this type of automation, and explain how it would interact with this legacy system. *(3 markah)*

(c) What is the main maintenance risk of using this type of automation, and how can it be mitigated? *(2 markah)*

---

**Soalan A5 (8 markah)**

Apply the CITED framework to evaluate the following tool for a given scenario:

**Tool:** Zapier Professional (USD 49/month)
**Scenario:** A 10-person architecture firm in Kuala Lumpur that uses Google Workspace (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets) and wants to automate sending client project status updates via WhatsApp whenever a Google Sheet is updated.

Evaluate Zapier Professional against **all five CITED criteria**. Conclude with a recommendation (proceed / do not proceed / conditional proceed with conditions stated). *(8 markah)*

### Bahagian B — Soalan Esei Gunaan (60 markah)

*Jawab KEDUA-DUA soalan. Setiap soalan bernilai 30 markah.*

**Soalan B1 (30 markah)**

**Syarikat Bakat Gemilang Sdn Bhd** is a Malaysian training provider in Kuala Lumpur with 30 staff. The company currently uses:
- Microsoft 365 Business Standard (Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint)
- A legacy student management system (desktop application, no API)
- Paper-based student registration forms

The company is considering adopting AI tools for three use cases:
1. Drafting student progress reports in Word automatically
2. Sending automated email notifications to students when their class schedule changes (currently done manually in Outlook)
3. Entering student attendance data from a paper register into the legacy student management system

(a) For each of the three use cases, identify the most appropriate **category** of AI automation tool and recommend a **specific tool** from KP-02. Justify each recommendation. *(15 markah — 5 marks per use case)*

(b) The company's IT budget for AI tools is RM800/month across all tools. Based on your recommendations in (a), construct a budget allocation table showing the estimated monthly MYR cost for each tool and the total. State whether the total fits within the RM800/month budget, and if not, propose one adjustment. *(10 markah)*

(c) The company's director is concerned about PDPA 2010 compliance, specifically because student records contain personal data (name, IC number, contact number, academic history). For the tools recommended in (a), identify the most significant PDPA risk for **one** of the three tools and propose a control measure. *(5 markah)*

---

**Soalan B2 (30 markah)**

Read the following case study:

> **Agensi Hartanah Wawasan** is a real estate agency in Ipoh, Perak with 12 staff. The agency currently operates entirely on WhatsApp and Excel spreadsheets — there is no formal software. Each day, the admin assistant (salary RM2,000/month) spends approximately 3 hours manually: (1) copying new property enquiries from WhatsApp into an Excel tracking sheet, (2) sending a standard acknowledgement message to each enquirer, and (3) updating the property status column in the Excel sheet when a deal closes. Average daily enquiries: 15.

(a) The agency owner wants to use **Zapier** to automate the three tasks described above. However, direct WhatsApp integration via Zapier requires a third-party WhatsApp Business API connector (estimated at RM150/month). Evaluate whether this is the right tool choice using the CITED framework. Conclude with a clear recommendation. *(12 markah)*

(b) Explain what a **Trigger** and an **Action** are in Zapier/Power Automate. Then design a specific Zap (3 steps) that would automate Task 1 above (copying WhatsApp enquiries into Excel). State the Trigger, Action 1, and Action 2. *(8 markah)*

(c) The admin assistant, Encik Farid, manages to implement the Zapier automation successfully. Three months later, the agency switches from Excel to Google Sheets. Explain what happens to the Zapier Zap, what Encik Farid must do to fix it, and what this scenario illustrates about ongoing automation maintenance. *(10 markah)*

## Skema Pemarkahan (Panduan Pensyarah)

### Bahagian A

**A1:** Categories: (1) AI Writing & Productivity Assistants — e.g., Microsoft 365 Copilot / ChatGPT; (2) Workflow Automation Platforms — e.g., Zapier / Power Automate / n8n; (3) Robotic Process Automation — e.g., UiPath / Power Automate Desktop. (2 marks per category) | Category 1; Microsoft 365 Copilot (Outlook draft feature). (2 marks)

**A2:** Comparison table: criteria must include at least 4 of: desktop application, offline capability, integration with AutoCount/Excel, AI feature depth, cost, Malaysian corporate adoption. Recommendation clearly states Microsoft 365 Copilot is preferred given existing Office + AutoCount usage. (7 marks table + 1 recommendation)

**A3:** (a) Power Automate is included in most Microsoft 365 plans — no additional subscription cost; deep native integration with SharePoint, Outlook, Teams, Excel. (2 marks) | (b) Self-hosted n8n means company data never leaves its own server — no third-party cloud provider processes the data, reducing PDPA data processor risk and removing need for a Data Processing Agreement with Zapier. (3 marks) | (c) Ranking: Power Automate (1st — free with M365 if they have it; but no M365 stated → Zapier Starter); Zapier Starter (2nd — low cost RM90/month, no IT needed); n8n (3rd — free but requires IT/server knowledge). Accept logical ranking with justification. (3 marks)

**A4:** (a) RPA (Robotic Process Automation) — because the system has no API and no web interface; RPA bots interact with the UI exactly as a human would (mouse + keyboard simulation). (3 marks) | (b) Microsoft Power Automate Desktop — records mouse clicks and keyboard inputs when a user operates the legacy claims desktop application; replays these recorded actions on the desktop. (3 marks) | (c) Main risk: RPA breaks if the UI changes (e.g., software update changes screen layout). Mitigation: monitor release notes; test automation after every software update; document the recorded flow so it can be re-recorded quickly. (2 marks)

**A5:** CITED: Cost — USD 49/month (~RM230/month; label as indicative); reasonable for 10-person firm. Integration — Zapier connects to Google Workspace (good) and WhatsApp via 360dialog or Twilio connector (possible, requires additional connector cost). Training — drag-and-drop, no-code; suitable for non-technical admin. Environment — Zapier is US-based cloud; PDPA risk if client data passes through Zapier; should review Zapier DPA. Dependability — established platform, 99.9% uptime SLA. Recommendation: Conditional proceed — confirm WhatsApp connector cost and review Zapier Data Processing Agreement for PDPA compliance before committing. (1.5 marks per criterion + 0.5 recommendation = 8 marks)

### Bahagian B

**B1(a):** Use case 1 — Category 1 (AI Writing Assistant); Microsoft 365 Copilot in Word — generates report draft from structured data. (5 marks) | Use case 2 — Category 2 (Workflow Automation); Power Automate — trigger: calendar event updated; action: send email to students via Outlook. No extra subscription as Power Automate included in M365. (5 marks) | Use case 3 — Category 3 (RPA); Power Automate Desktop — records keystrokes to enter attendance data from spreadsheet into legacy system desktop UI. (5 marks)

**B1(b):** Budget table: Copilot (RM150–175/user; only for key staff, e.g., 3 users = ~RM525/month); Power Automate (included in M365); Power Automate Desktop (free on Windows). Total: ~RM525/month — within RM800 budget. If over budget: reduce Copilot to 2 users. (10 marks: 3 cost accuracy, 3 table structure, 2 total calculation, 2 budget fit assessment)

**B1(c):** Most significant PDPA risk: for Copilot — student personal data (IC, academic history) is processed via Microsoft 365 Copilot using Microsoft Graph; must confirm Microsoft's DPA covers this data category and that staff do not paste raw IC numbers into Copilot prompts. Control: internal policy prohibiting input of specific PII into Copilot prompts; use pseudonymised student IDs instead. (5 marks)

**B2(a):** CITED applied to Zapier + WhatsApp connector: Cost: Zapier Professional USD 49/month (~RM230) + WhatsApp connector RM150 = ~RM380/month. For a 12-person agency this is significant; flag as moderately high. Integration: good with WhatsApp Business API + Google Sheets. Training: low — drag and drop. Environment: PDPA concern — customer phone numbers (personal data) pass through Zapier cloud. Dependability: established. Recommendation: Conditional proceed — implement PDPA-compliant data handling; consider n8n self-hosted as alternative if PDPA is a priority. (12 marks: 2 per criterion + 2 recommendation)

**B2(b):** Trigger = the event that starts an automation. Action = the step(s) that run automatically after the trigger fires. (3 marks) | Zap design: Trigger — new message received on WhatsApp Business (via 360dialog connector) containing keyword "enquiry"; Action 1 — create new row in Google Sheets with: name (extracted), phone number, property interest, timestamp; Action 2 — send acknowledgement WhatsApp message to the enquirer (standard template). (5 marks)

**B2(c):** The Zap uses the Google Sheets connector; when the agency switches from Excel to Google Sheets, the Zap action that was configured for Excel (Microsoft Excel connector) no longer points to the right data destination — the Zap will fail or write to the wrong place. Encik Farid must: (1) edit the Zap, (2) replace the Excel action step with a Google Sheets action step, (3) re-map the data fields to the new sheet structure. This illustrates that automations are not set-and-forget — they require maintenance whenever connected systems change, and changes must be tested before going live. (10 marks: 3 what happens, 4 what to do, 3 lesson learned)
