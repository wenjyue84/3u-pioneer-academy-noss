## Bahagian A — Soalan Objektif / Objective Questions (20 markah)

*Pilih jawapan yang paling tepat. Setiap soalan bernilai 2 markah.*

**1.** According to KP-03, the standard number of working hours per month used to calculate the hourly rate for Malaysian office staff (based on Employment Act 1955 — 8 hours/day, 22 working days) is:

- A. 160 hours
- B. 176 hours
- C. 200 hours
- D. 144 hours

**2.** An employee earns RM 4,400 per month and works standard office hours. Using the formula in KP-03, their hourly rate is approximately:

- A. RM 20.00 per hour
- B. RM 27.50 per hour
- C. RM 25.00 per hour
- D. RM 22.50 per hour

**3.** A meeting with 5 attendees, each earning RM 20/hour, runs for 2 hours. What is the cost of this meeting?

- A. RM 100
- B. RM 200
- C. RM 40
- D. RM 400

**4.** According to KP-03's five-question ROI framework, a meeting that is used only to **broadcast one-way information** (no discussion, no decisions made) would score "Concern" on which question?

- A. Does every attendee contribute?
- B. Is the meeting consistently ending on time?
- C. Do action items have named owners?
- D. Could the outcome be achieved by email or async update?

**5.** **Time blocking (pengehadan masa)** is best described as:

- A. Setting a maximum number of meetings per week
- B. Dividing the workday into dedicated blocks assigned to specific types of work
- C. Blocking external booking links during peak hours
- D. Limiting the duration of each calendar event to 30 minutes

**6.** According to KP-03, which of the following is classified as **Deep Work (Kerja Mendalam)**?

- A. Forwarding emails to relevant colleagues
- B. Attending a daily stand-up meeting
- C. Drafting a complex financial reconciliation report requiring sustained concentration
- D. Filing standard forms into the designated folder

**7.** The "Monk mode scheduling" strategy described in KP-03 involves:

- A. Attending no meetings for an entire week
- B. Blocking the first 90 minutes of every working day for deep work before checking email or messages
- C. Scheduling all meetings before 9 AM to leave the rest of the day free
- D. Working from home on Mondays to avoid office interruptions

**8.** According to KP-03, which AI tool transcribes a meeting in real time within Microsoft Teams and generates a post-meeting summary with action items?

- A. Otter.ai
- B. Fireflies.ai
- C. Microsoft Copilot for Teams
- D. Google Meet AI Notes

**9.** An action item extracted from a meeting's AI summary should be transferred to the project management tool with which three fields as a minimum?

- A. Meeting title, attendee list, recording link
- B. Task description, named owner, due date
- C. Meeting cost, ROI verdict, risk score
- D. Agenda number, speaker name, word count

**10.** According to KP-03, what should an admin do with AI-generated meeting minutes within the first **30 minutes** after the meeting ends?

- A. Send them immediately to all attendees without review
- B. Archive them in the project folder without reading
- C. Review the AI output for accuracy before distributing or entering action items into the system
- D. Delete them and write the minutes manually
