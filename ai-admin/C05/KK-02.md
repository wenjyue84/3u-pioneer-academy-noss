## Work Activity Title

Build a 4-Week AI Tool Rollout Project Plan with Task Tracking, Risk Register, and Meeting Cost ROI Analysis

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this practical activity, trainees will have:

- Created a 4-week project plan in Notion AI or Microsoft Project covering a simulated AI tool rollout in a Malaysian SME
- Defined at least 12 tasks with named owners, due dates, statuses, and at least 3 task dependencies
- Produced a Gantt chart (timeline view) showing the full 4-week project schedule
- Completed a risk register with minimum 2 risks, including likelihood, impact, mitigation, and owner
- Calculated the monthly and annual cost of the project's weekly progress meeting
- Produced a 1-page project brief (Ringkasan Projek) suitable for presenting to management

## Safety Precautions

- Use only the simulated company name and simulated team member names provided in the scenario. Do not enter real employee names, IC numbers, or salary details.

## Scenario

**Company:** Prisma Logistics Sdn Bhd *(fictional — used for training purposes only)*
**Project:** Rollout of **Reclaim.ai** as the company-wide AI scheduling tool for all 15 office staff
**Project Start Date:** 1 September 2026
**Project End Date:** 28 September 2026 (4 weeks)
**Your Role:** Admin Project Coordinator

**Simulated Team Members (fictional):**
- **Hafizuddin** — IT Administrator (manages software setup and access)
- **Suraya** — HR Manager (coordinates staff training schedule)
- **Boon Kheng** — Finance Executive (handles subscription payment and cost tracking)
- **You** — Admin Project Coordinator (project documentation, communications, meeting minutes)

**Project Scope:**
The company has decided to implement Reclaim.ai to reduce scheduling conflicts and protect staff focus time. Your job is to plan, track, and report on the 4-week rollout. The project ends when all 15 staff members have active Reclaim.ai accounts connected to their company email and have attended the 2-hour onboarding training.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Open Notion and Create Your Project Database (20 minutes)

1. Log in to **notion.so** and create a new page titled: `C05 KK-02 — AI Tool Rollout Project`
2. Inside the page, create a new **Database** (Table view).
3. Set up the following columns (properties):
   - **Task Name** (Title)
   - **Owner** (Text or Select — use simulated names above)
   - **Start Date** (Date)
   - **Due Date** (Date)
   - **Status** (Select: Not Started / In Progress / Done / Blocked)
   - **Priority** (Select: High / Medium / Low)
   - **Depends On** (Text — name the task that must be done first)
   - **Notes** (Text)

Record the column names you created: _______________________________________________

**Screenshot Label:** Save as `Step1_DatabaseSetup.png`

### Step 2: Use Notion AI to Generate Your Initial Task List (15 minutes)

Click the **Ask AI** button in your Notion page and enter the following prompt:

```
I am planning a 4-week rollout of Reclaim.ai as a company scheduling tool for 15 staff members at a Malaysian SME. The project runs from 1 September to 28 September 2026.

Generate a task list for this project covering:
- Week 1: Planning and setup
- Week 2: IT configuration and testing
- Week 3: Staff training
- Week 4: Go-live and review

Include at least 12 tasks with suggested owners (choose from: Admin Coordinator, IT Administrator, HR Manager, Finance Executive), suggested duration (days), and key dependencies.
```

Review the AI output. Adjust any tasks that do not make sense for the Malaysian SME context. Add at least 2 tasks of your own based on your knowledge of the project scenario.

Enter all tasks into your Notion database. Assign realistic start and due dates within the 4-week window (1–28 September 2026).

**Minimum requirement:** 12 tasks, all with owner, start date, due date, and status set to "Not Started".

**Screenshot Label:** Save as `Step2_TaskList.png` (show the full list of 12+ tasks)

### Step 7: Produce the 1-Page Project Brief (Ringkasan Projek) (15 minutes)

In Microsoft Word or Google Docs, produce a 1-page project brief for management covering the following sections. The brief should be professional enough to present to a company director.

**Required sections:**

1. **Project Name and Objective** — State what the project is and what it aims to achieve
2. **Timeline** — Start date, end date, 4 key milestones (one per week)
3. **Team Roles** — List the 4 team members and their project roles (use simulated names)
4. **Key Risks** — Summarise the 2 risks from your Risk Register in 1 sentence each
5. **Meeting Cost Summary** — State the total cost of the 4 weekly progress meetings and your ROI verdict
6. **Success Criteria** — Define how you will know the project is complete (e.g., "All 15 staff accounts active and onboarding training attended")

**Word count:** Approximately 350–500 words. Use headings and bullet points for clarity.

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. After calculating the total cost of the 4 weekly progress meetings, do you think this meeting series is cost-justified for a 4-week project? What format change (if any) would you recommend, and why?
2. Which of the 12 tasks you created would you classify as **deep work** tasks and which as **shallow work** tasks? Give two examples of each.
3. If Suraya (HR Manager) falls ill during Week 3 — the staff training week — which tasks in your project plan would be immediately affected? How does your Risk Register help you respond?
4. You notice that the Notion AI generated a task called "Get management approval" but placed it in Week 3. Explain why this is a poor sequencing decision and where it should be placed instead.
