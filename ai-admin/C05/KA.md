## Bahagian A — Soalan Aneka Pilihan / Multiple Choice Questions (30 markah)

*Pilih satu jawapan yang paling tepat bagi setiap soalan. Setiap soalan bernilai 2 markah.*

**1.** Which AI scheduling tool allows an organisation to **self-host** the booking system on its own server, keeping all booking data within the organisation's control?

- A. Reclaim.ai
- B. Calendly
- C. Cal.com
- D. Google Calendar Smart Scheduling

**2.** A company based in **Alor Setar, Kedah** wants to configure an AI scheduling tool for its executives. What weekend setting should the admin configure?

- A. Saturday–Sunday
- B. Friday–Saturday
- C. Thursday–Friday
- D. Sunday–Monday

**3.** Malaysia Standard Time (MST / Waktu Malaysia Standard) is:

- A. UTC+7
- B. UTC+8
- C. UTC+9
- D. UTC+6

**4.** An employee earns RM 3,520 per month and works 22 days × 8 hours per month. Their hourly rate (to two decimal places) is:

- A. RM 18.50
- B. RM 20.00
- C. RM 16.00
- D. RM 22.00

**5.** A project manager says: "Task C cannot start until Task B is finished." This describes which type of task dependency?

- A. Start-to-Start (SS)
- B. Finish-to-Finish (FF)
- C. Finish-to-Start (FS)
- D. Start-to-Finish (SF)

**6.** The **critical path** of a project is:

- A. The list of the highest-priority tasks regardless of sequence
- B. The longest sequence of dependent tasks that determines the minimum time to complete the project
- C. The path of tasks assigned to the project manager only
- D. The most expensive set of tasks in the project budget

**7.** According to KP-03, the standard monthly working hours figure for a Malaysian employee on an 8-hour, 5-day work week (Employment Act 1955 standard) is:

- A. 160 hours
- B. 190 hours
- C. 176 hours
- D. 168 hours

**8.** A risk with **Medium Likelihood** and **High Impact** is classified in the risk score matrix as:

- A. Low
- B. Medium
- C. High
- D. Critical

**9.** The "Habits" feature in Reclaim.ai is designed to:

- A. Track the number of meetings attended per week
- B. Protect recurring personal and work commitments from being overridden by new meeting bookings
- C. Automatically schedule tasks in the most cost-efficient order
- D. Generate a summary report of daily calendar events

**10.** Which of the following best describes **deep work (kerja mendalam)** as defined in KP-03?

- A. Work that involves communicating with many people simultaneously
- B. Repetitive, well-defined tasks that can be done while distracted
- C. Focused, cognitively demanding work that produces high-quality output in an undistracted state
- D. Work that is performed after standard working hours

**11.** Asana's **Workload view** primarily helps an administrator to:

- A. Generate a Gantt chart from meeting notes
- B. Identify over-allocated team members and rebalance task assignments
- C. Automatically send invoices after project completion
- D. Configure public holiday blocks for the project timeline

**12.** A meeting that is used only for one-way information broadcasting (no discussion, no decisions) would score "Concern" on which question in KP-03's five-question ROI framework?

- A. Does every attendee contribute?
- B. Does the meeting produce a decision or action?
- C. Both A and B
- D. Neither A nor B — the meeting scores "Positive" on all questions

**13.** According to KP-01, which of the following integration methods connects Calendly to Zoho CRM when there is no native direct integration?

- A. Microsoft Power BI
- B. Zapier or Zoho Flow
- C. Notion AI
- D. Fireflies.ai

**14.** Which project management tool is described in KP-02 as best suited for **cross-departmental task management** where tasks are owned by different people in HR, Finance, and Operations simultaneously?

- A. Notion AI
- B. Microsoft Project Copilot
- C. Asana
- D. Cal.com

**15.** According to KP-03, what is the recommended time window for an admin to review and correct AI-generated meeting minutes?

- A. Within 24 hours of the meeting
- B. At the next scheduled meeting
- C. Within 30 minutes of the meeting ending
- D. Within 1 week of the meeting
