## Work Activity Title

Configure an AI-Managed Weekly Calendar for a Simulated Executive, Incorporating Malaysian Scheduling Context and Automated External Booking

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this practical activity, trainees will have:

- Created a Reclaim.ai (or equivalent) account and connected it to a Google Calendar or Outlook calendar
- Configured working hours, public holiday blackout dates, and a Friday–Saturday weekend variation for a state-based executive
- Created and protected at least two recurring focus blocks using the AI scheduling tool's habit feature
- Set up a Calendly booking link with three distinct meeting types and tested it successfully
- Scheduled one multi-timezone team meeting and verified that the calendar displays the correct local time for all parties
- Produced an annotated calendar screenshot and a Panduan Persediaan Sistem Penjadualan (Scheduling System Setup Guide)

## Safety Precautions

- Do not enter real personal data (real names, IC numbers, real phone numbers, or real client data) into any AI scheduling tool during this training exercise. Use only simulated names provided in the scenario below.
- Use a training Google or Microsoft account, not your primary personal or work account, where possible.
- Do not grant third-party tools more calendar permissions than the minimum required to complete the task (read/write to calendar only).
- Save screenshots of each completed step as you progress — do not wait until the end.
- Verify all public holiday dates against the official source (federalgazette.agc.gov.my) before blocking them in a real-world system. For this exercise, use the indicative dates provided in KP-01.

## Scenario

You are the personal assistant to **Encik Zulkarnain** (fictional), the General Manager of a medium-sized trading company. Encik Zulkarnain is based in **Kota Bharu, Kelantan** and observes a **Friday–Saturday weekend**. He works standard hours: **8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Sunday through Thursday**.

His current week has the following commitments:
- A standing Monday 10:00 AM, 1-hour board briefing call (recurring)
- He has asked you to protect two 90-minute deep work blocks per week — one on Sunday morning and one on Wednesday morning (before 11 AM)
- Three external stakeholders need to book time with him: one for a 30-minute supplier intro call, one for a 60-minute client review, and one for a 15-minute check-in
- His team includes a colleague in **Jakarta, Indonesia (UTC+7)** and one in **Singapore (UTC+8)**. Schedule a 30-minute team call at a time that works for all three locations.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Set Up Your AI Scheduling Account (15 minutes)

1. Go to **reclaim.ai** and sign up for a free account using your training Google account.
2. Connect Reclaim to your Google Calendar when prompted.
3. Navigate to **Settings → Working Hours**.
4. Set your working hours to reflect Encik Zulkarnain's schedule:
   - Working days: **Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday**
   - Non-working days: **Friday, Saturday**
   - Working hours: **8:00 AM – 5:00 PM**

### Step 5: Set Up Calendly Booking Link with Three Meeting Types (25 minutes)

Go to **calendly.com** and sign up with your training account.

Create three Event Types:

| Event Type | Duration | Buffer After | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supplier Intro Call** | 30 minutes | 10 minutes | Allow booking on Tuesdays and Thursdays only, 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM |
| **Client Review Meeting** | 60 minutes | 15 minutes | Allow booking on Sundays only, 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM |
| **Quick Check-In** | 15 minutes | 5 minutes | Allow booking any working day (Sun–Thu), 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM |

For each Event Type, configure:
- Confirmation email text (write a professional Malay-English bilingual confirmation message)
- One reminder email 24 hours before
- The "What's this meeting about?" intake question (required field)

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. When configuring Calendly for Encik Zulkarnain's Kelantan schedule, what is the main scheduling risk if a new admin forgets to change the default weekend setting from Saturday–Sunday to Friday–Saturday?
2. You discover that the AI scheduling tool has placed an external meeting during Encik Zulkarnain's protected Deep Work block. What steps would you take to prevent this from happening again?
3. The Jakarta colleague says the proposed Wednesday meeting time falls during their lunch break. How would you use World Time Buddy to find an alternative slot? What constraints exist?
4. If Encik Zulkarnain's company were to switch from Reclaim.ai to Cal.com (self-hosted), what is the main additional IT requirement, and what is the benefit in terms of data privacy?
