## Work Activity Title

Run an AI-Assisted Recruitment Cycle for a Simulated Job Opening

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:

- Written a professional job advertisement using an AI writing tool
- Applied a structured scoring rubric to screen 5 sample resumes
- Produced a shortlist memo with justified candidate rankings
- Generated a structured interview question bank using AI
- Completed a recruitment documentation folder ready for HR filing

---

## Tools & Materials Required

| Item | Purpose | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Computer / laptop with internet connection | Primary workspace | Lab / personal device |
| ChatGPT (free account) or Claude | Job advertisement and interview question generation | chat.openai.com / claude.ai |
| Microsoft Word / Google Docs | Document production | Lab or personal device |
| Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets | Resume scoring log | Lab or personal device |
| 5 sample resume files (provided by trainer) | Screening exercise | Printed or digital — provided |
| Worksheet Answer Sheet (provided) | Record all responses | Printed / digital |

---

## Safety Precautions

- Do not enter real personal data (real names, real IC numbers, real phone numbers) of actual individuals into any AI tool.
- The 5 sample resumes provided are fictional training materials — treat them as confidential for the purposes of this exercise.
- Verify all AI-generated content before including it in your deliverables — AI may produce legally problematic clauses or inaccurate job market information.
- Save your work every 15 minutes and back up to cloud storage before the session ends.

## Scenario Briefing

You are the HR & Admin Executive of **Prisma Sdn Bhd**, a growing technology services company based in Petaling Jaya, Selangor. The Operations Manager has requested that you fill the following vacancy:

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Position** | Admin Coordinator |
| **Department** | Operations |
| **Location** | Petaling Jaya, Selangor |
| **Employment Type** | Full-time, permanent |
| **Salary Range** | RM2,800 – RM3,500 per month |
| **Working Hours** | Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM |
| **Language Requirement** | Proficient in Bahasa Malaysia and English; Mandarin is an advantage |
| **Key Duties** | Filing and document management, scheduling meetings, coordinating office supplies, HR administration support, updating attendance records |
| **Minimum Qualification** | Diploma in Business Administration or equivalent; minimum 1 year relevant experience |
| **Preferred Skills** | Proficient in Microsoft Office; experience with any HR platform is an advantage |

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Write the Job Advertisement (25 minutes)

Using ChatGPT or Claude, generate a professional job advertisement for the Admin Coordinator position described in the Scenario Briefing above.

Your prompt must include:
- Job title, department, location, employment type
- Key responsibilities (use the scenario details)
- Minimum qualifications and preferred skills
- Salary range
- Language requirements
- How to apply (instruct AI to add "Send resume to hr@prismasb.com.my")
- Tone: professional but approachable; length: 250–300 words

Record your **exact prompt** in Section 1 of the Answer Sheet.

After receiving the AI output, review and edit it:
- [ ] Remove or correct any salary claim the AI added that contradicts the scenario
- [ ] Verify that no discriminatory criteria (gender, ethnicity, religion) appear in the requirements
- [ ] Confirm that qualifications stated match the scenario
- [ ] Ensure the closing date and application method are clear

Paste the **final, edited job advertisement** in Section 1 of the Answer Sheet.

---

### Step 2: Review Sample Resumes and Prepare Scoring Rubric (15 minutes)

Your trainer will provide you with **5 sample resumes** (Resume A through Resume E) for the Admin Coordinator position.

Before screening, prepare your scoring rubric in Google Sheets or Excel using the criteria below:

| Criterion | Maximum Marks | Scoring Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum education (Diploma or equivalent) | 20 | 20 = Diploma or higher in relevant field; 10 = Diploma in unrelated field or SPM with relevant experience; 0 = Below Diploma, no relevant experience |
| Work experience (years in admin/HR role) | 25 | 25 = 2+ years; 15 = 1–2 years; 5 = less than 1 year; 0 = no relevant experience |
| Required software skills (Microsoft Office) | 20 | 20 = Microsoft Office confirmed + HR platform mentioned; 10 = Microsoft Office only; 0 = neither mentioned |
| Language proficiency (BM + English) | 15 | 15 = both confirmed in resume; 8 = one language clearly stated; 0 = neither clear |
| Industry or company type relevance | 10 | 10 = same industry (tech / services); 5 = adjacent industry; 0 = unrelated |
| Resume presentation quality | 10 | 10 = professional, well-structured, no errors; 5 = adequate but with minor issues; 0 = poor layout or contains significant errors |
| **TOTAL** | **100** | |

Paste your **scoring rubric (screenshot or table)** in Section 2 of the Answer Sheet.

---

### Step 3: AI-Assisted Resume Screening (30 minutes)

For each of the 5 resumes, do the following:

1. **AI Extraction:** Type or paste the resume text into ChatGPT with this prompt:

```
Read the following resume and extract this information in a structured table:
- Highest education level and field of study
- Total years of relevant work experience
- Software tools or platforms mentioned
- Languages stated or inferred from education
- Industry or sector of past employers
- Resume presentation assessment: Professional / Adequate / Poor

Resume:
[paste resume text here]
```

2. **Score:** Apply your rubric to the AI-extracted information. Enter the scores for each criterion.

3. **Total:** Sum the scores for each resume.

Record all AI extractions and scores in **Section 3 of the Answer Sheet** (one table per resume).

> **IMPORTANT:** If the AI extraction appears to misread or omit information that is clearly visible in the resume, use your own judgment to correct the score. Note any discrepancy in the "Remarks" column.

---

### Step 4: Produce the Shortlist Memo (20 minutes)

Based on your screening scores:

- **Shortlist (score 70 and above):** Include in the interview invitation list
- **Reserve list (score 50–69):** Document but do not invite unless shortlist pool is insufficient
- **Not shortlisted (score below 50):** Document with reason

Using Microsoft Word or Google Docs, write a **Shortlist Memo** addressed to the Operations Manager with the following structure:

```
TO:       [Operations Manager name — use "Operations Manager, Prisma Sdn Bhd"]
FROM:     [Your name]
DATE:     [Today's date]
RE:       Admin Coordinator Recruitment — Resume Screening Results

SUMMARY
[2 sentences describing the screening process and total applications reviewed]

SHORTLISTED CANDIDATES
[Table: Candidate code | Score | Key Strengths | Recommended Interview Slot]

RESERVE LIST
[Candidate code | Score | Notes]

NOT SHORTLISTED
[Candidate code | Score | Primary Reason]

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP
[1 paragraph]

Signed: ____________________
```

Paste the **completed Shortlist Memo** in Section 4 of the Answer Sheet.

---

### Step 5: Generate Interview Question Bank (20 minutes)

Using ChatGPT or Claude, generate a structured interview question bank for the Admin Coordinator position. Your prompt must request:

- 3 behavioural questions (STAR method) covering: handling multiple priorities, dealing with a difficult stakeholder, and maintaining confidentiality
- 2 situational questions: one on detecting an error in an attendance record, one on handling a last-minute meeting schedule change
- 2 technical knowledge questions: one on Employment Act 1955 leave entitlement, one on HR platform data entry procedures

Record your **exact prompt** in Section 5 of the Answer Sheet.

Review the AI-generated questions and apply the following checklist:
- [ ] No question asks about age, marital status, religion, ethnicity, pregnancy, or disability
- [ ] All questions are clearly relevant to the Admin Coordinator role
- [ ] Questions are clear, unambiguous, and professionally worded
- [ ] Remove any questions that are duplicates or off-topic

Paste the **final interview question bank (7 questions minimum)** in Section 5 of the Answer Sheet.

---

### Step 6: Compile Recruitment Folder (10 minutes)

Organise the following documents into a named folder (digital or physical):

| Document | File / Section |
|---|---|
| Final Job Advertisement | Recruitment_C03_JobAd.docx |
| Resume Scoring Log (all 5 resumes) | Recruitment_C03_ScoringLog.xlsx |
| Shortlist Memo | Recruitment_C03_ShortlistMemo.docx |
| Interview Question Bank | Recruitment_C03_InterviewQuestions.docx |

Record the folder structure in **Section 6 of the Answer Sheet** and confirm all four documents are complete.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Job Advertisement Quality** | Professional, all scenario details included, no discriminatory content, AI errors corrected | Clear and professional; most details included; minor AI errors corrected | Functional; key details present; some uncorrected AI errors | Incomplete or contains discriminatory / legally problematic content |
| **Scoring Rubric Application** | All 5 resumes scored consistently and accurately; discrepancies noted and explained | 4–5 resumes scored correctly; minor inconsistencies | 3–4 resumes scored; some misapplication of rubric | Fewer than 3 resumes scored or rubric misapplied throughout |
| **Shortlist Memo** | Professional format; all sections complete; decisions clearly justified with scores | Most sections complete; decisions with scores | Core decisions present; format incomplete | Incomplete or decisions unjustified |
| **Interview Question Bank** | 7+ questions; all types represented; checklist applied; no problematic questions | 6–7 questions; most types covered; checklist mostly applied | 5 questions; some types missing | Fewer than 5 questions or checklist not applied |

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. At which step did AI assistance save you the most time, and why?
2. Were there any instances where the AI-generated content was inaccurate or needed significant correction? Describe one example.
3. How would you explain the bias risks of AI resume screening to a colleague who has never used an AI tool?
4. If this were a real recruitment exercise and you needed to reject unsuccessful candidates, what would you do with their resume files under PDPA 2010?
