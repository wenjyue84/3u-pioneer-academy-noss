## 1. Overview of Statutory Payroll Contributions in Malaysia

### 1.1 The Three Mandatory Schemes

Every employer in Malaysia who employs employees is subject to three mandatory statutory payroll contribution schemes:

| Scheme | Full Name (EN) | Full Name (BM) | Administering Body | Legislation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EPF** | Employees Provident Fund | Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja (KWSP) | KWSP | Employees Provident Fund Act 1991 |
| **SOCSO** | Social Security Organisation | Pertubuhan Keselamatan Sosial (PERKESO) | PERKESO | Employees' Social Security Act 1969 |
| **EIS** | Employment Insurance System | Sistem Insurans Pekerjaan (SIP) | PERKESO | Employment Insurance System Act 2017 |

Each scheme serves a distinct social protection purpose:
- **EPF** provides retirement savings for employees.
- **SOCSO** provides injury and invalidity benefits in the event of work-related accidents or illness.
- **EIS** provides temporary financial assistance to employees who lose their jobs involuntarily.

### 1.2 Coverage — Who is Covered?

| Scheme | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **EPF** | All Malaysian citizens and permanent residents employed by registered employers; foreign employees may also contribute voluntarily or compulsorily depending on their employment pass category |
| **SOCSO** | Malaysian employees earning below the wage ceiling established in the Act (refer to PERKESO portal for current ceiling); domestic servants, self-employed, and federal/state government employees have separate arrangements |
| **EIS** | All employees aged 18–60 covered under the Employment Act 1955, regardless of wage level; excludes domestic servants, self-employed, civil servants, and employees aged 57 or above who have never contributed |

## 2. EPF (KWSP) — Employees Provident Fund (Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja)

### 2.1 Purpose and Structure

The EPF is a compulsory savings scheme established under the **Employees Provident Fund Act 1991**. Employee and employer contributions are deposited into individual member accounts, which are divided into:

- **Account 1 (Akaun Persaraan):** Retirement savings; can only be withdrawn upon reaching the statutory withdrawal age or under specific approved reasons.
- **Account 2 (Akaun Sejahtera):** Can be used for specific purposes including housing, education, medical, and pre-retirement withdrawals.
- **Account 3 (Akaun Fleksibel):** Flexible withdrawal; accessible at any time (introduced in 2024 reforms).

> **Contribution Rate:** EPF contribution rates are set by KWSP regulations and are reviewed periodically. Trainees must verify the current employer contribution rate and the current employee contribution rate from the official KWSP portal at **https://www.kwsp.gov.my** before processing any payroll. The rate may differ for employees aged 60 and above.

### 2.2 Monthly Submission — i-Akaun Majikan

Employers must submit EPF contributions monthly through the **i-Akaun Majikan** portal:

| Step | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Log in to i-Akaun Majikan | N/A |
| 2 | Upload or enter employee contribution data | By the **15th of the following month** |
| 3 | Generate payment order | Before submission deadline |
| 4 | Make payment (online banking / payment gateway) | By the **15th of the following month** |
| 5 | Download payment receipt for records | After successful payment |

> **Late payment penalty:** A dividend rate penalty applies for late EPF contributions. Employers who consistently delay contributions risk prosecution under the EPF Act 1991.

### 2.3 New Employee EPF Registration

When an employee does not yet have an EPF membership number:
1. Employer submits **KWSP Form 3** (or equivalent online registration) to KWSP.
2. KWSP assigns the employee a membership number.
3. Contributions can then be made under the new membership number.

For employees who already have an EPF membership number, the employer simply links their existing member number to the company's employer account.

## 3. SOCSO (PERKESO) — Social Security (Keselamatan Sosial)

### 3.1 Purpose and Two Schemes

SOCSO operates two separate schemes under the **Employees' Social Security Act 1969**:

| Scheme | Coverage | Who Contributes |
|---|---|---|
| **Employment Injury Scheme (EIS — under SOCSO)** | Work-related accidents, occupational diseases, commuting accidents | Employer only |
| **Invalidity Scheme** | Invalidity due to non-work-related causes; death | Both employer and employee |

> **Note on terminology:** The abbreviation "EIS" in the context of SOCSO refers to the Employment Injury Scheme. This is different from the Employment Insurance System (EIS under SIP), which is a separate scheme under the Employment Insurance System Act 2017. Do not confuse these two.

### 3.2 SOCSO Contribution Rates

SOCSO contribution rates are set by the **Second Schedule of the Employees' Social Security Act 1969** and are based on a wage ceiling and contribution table. The rates are structured by wage band.

> **Trainees must consult the current PERKESO Contribution Table at https://www.perkeso.gov.my to determine the precise rate applicable to each employee's wage band before processing payroll. The contribution table is updated periodically.**

### 3.3 SOCSO Registration for New Employees

New employees must be registered with SOCSO via the **ASSIST (Auto Submission System)** portal:
1. Employer logs in to ASSIST portal (https://assist.perkeso.gov.my).
2. Register new employee with their IC number, date of birth, and employment start date.
3. SOCSO assigns a SOCSO registration number.
4. Monthly contributions begin from the first month of employment.

### 3.4 Monthly Submission — ASSIST Portal

- Submit monthly contribution file by the **last day of the following month**.
- Employers with 50+ employees must submit contribution data in the prescribed electronic format.
- Employers with fewer than 50 employees may submit manually or electronically.

### 3.5 Benefits Under SOCSO

| Benefit | Description |
|---|---|
| Medical benefit | Medical treatment expenses for work injuries |
| Temporary disability benefit | Income replacement during recovery from work injury |
| Permanent disability benefit | Lump sum or periodic payments for permanent disability |
| Death benefit | Payment to dependants if employee dies from work injury or invalidity |
| Invalidity pension | Monthly pension for employees rendered invalid by non-work-related causes |
| Funeral benefit | Fixed payment to cover funeral expenses |

## 4. EIS (SIP) — Employment Insurance System (Sistem Insurans Pekerjaan)

### 4.1 Purpose

The **Employment Insurance System (EIS / SIP)** was established under the **Employment Insurance System Act 2017** and is administered by PERKESO. It provides short-term financial assistance to employees who involuntarily lose their jobs (retrenchment, company closure, constructive dismissal).

### 4.2 EIS Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|---|---|
| Job Search Allowance (JSA) | Monthly allowance while unemployed (duration based on contribution history) |
| Early Re-Employment Allowance | Incentive to accept employment before JSA period ends |
| Reduced Income Allowance | For employees who return to work at a lower salary |
| Training Fee Assistance | Subsidised retraining through approved training providers |
| Career Guidance | One-to-one career counselling services |

### 4.3 EIS Contribution Rates

EIS contribution rates are set by the **Employment Insurance System Act 2017** and are expressed as a percentage of the employee's monthly wages, subject to a wage ceiling. Both employer and employee contribute.

> **Trainees must verify the current EIS contribution rate from the official PERKESO EIS portal at https://www.perkeso.gov.my/eis before processing payroll. The rate has been subject to change since the scheme's launch in 2018.**

### 4.4 EIS Submission

EIS contributions are submitted together with SOCSO contributions through the **ASSIST portal** in a single monthly submission. The system automatically separates EIS and SOCSO amounts.

## 5. Payroll Processing Workflow (Aliran Kerja Pemprosesan Gaji)

### 5.1 Monthly Payroll Steps

A standard monthly payroll processing workflow for a Malaysian SME:

| Step | Activity | Tool / Method |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect attendance data | Kakitangan.com attendance report |
| 2 | Calculate gross salary | Base salary + overtime + allowances |
| 3 | Calculate deductions | EPF (employee share) + SOCSO (employee share, if applicable) + EIS + PCB / MTD |
| 4 | Calculate net pay | Gross salary − total deductions |
| 5 | Generate payslips | Kakitangan.com / AI-assisted template |
| 6 | Prepare statutory contribution files | EPF i-Akaun + SOCSO/EIS ASSIST format |
| 7 | Submit and pay statutory contributions | Online banking / portal payment |
| 8 | File and archive payroll records | Secure cloud storage, 7-year retention |

### 5.2 PCB / Monthly Tax Deduction (MTD)

The **Monthly Tax Deduction (MTD / Potongan Cukai Bulanan — PCB)** is a system whereby the employer deducts estimated income tax from the employee's salary each month and remits it to LHDN.

- Employers must use the **PCB calculator** on the LHDN e-PCB system (https://edaftarpcb.hasil.gov.my) to determine the correct monthly deduction for each employee.
- The PCB amount depends on the employee's salary level, marital status, number of dependants, and other tax relief factors.
- PCB must be remitted to LHDN by the **15th of the following month**.

> **Trainees must use LHDN's official PCB calculator to determine deduction amounts. Do not estimate PCB manually. Incorrect deductions create tax liability for the employee.**

## 6. HR Reporting — Auto-Generating Monthly Summary Reports (Laporan Ringkasan HR Bulanan)

### 6.1 What a Monthly HR Summary Report Contains

A monthly HR summary report (Laporan Ringkasan HR Bulanan) provides management with a snapshot of workforce status. Standard sections:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Headcount Summary | Total employees by department; new joiners; resignations; transfers |
| Attendance Summary | Average attendance rate; total late arrivals; total absent days |
| Leave Summary | Total leave taken by type (annual, sick, maternity, unpaid) |
| Overtime Summary | Total overtime hours and cost |
| Payroll Summary | Total gross payroll; total statutory contributions (EPF, SOCSO, EIS) by employer; total PCB |
| Recruitment Summary | Vacancies filled; pending vacancies; applications received |
| Compliance Calendar | Due dates for next EPF/SOCSO submission; any pending LHDN submissions |

### 6.2 Using AI to Generate HR Summary Reports

**Workflow:**
1. Export data from Kakitangan.com (attendance report, leave report, payroll summary).
2. Upload the exported CSV to ChatGPT or use Excel Copilot.
3. Use the following prompt:

```
I have uploaded a CSV file containing HR data for [month/year] for a company with [n] employees.
Please generate a concise management-ready HR summary report with the following sections:
1. Headcount summary (total staff, new joiners, leavers)
2. Attendance highlights (attendance rate, top absence reasons if available)
3. Leave utilisation (total days taken by leave type)
4. Payroll summary (total gross payroll, total statutory contributions)
5. Any anomalies or trends worth flagging to management

Format as a structured report with section headers. Use professional English.
Do not invent or estimate any figures not present in the data.
```

4. Review the AI-generated report against the source data before submitting to management.

### 6.3 Payroll Data Security and PDPA 2010 Obligations

Payroll data contains **sensitive personal data** including salary amounts, bank details, tax deduction amounts, and statutory contribution records. Under PDPA 2010:

| Obligation | Specific Action Required |
|---|---|
| **Access control** | Payroll files must be password-protected; access limited to HR and authorised finance staff |
| **Do not upload to public AI tools** | Payslip files with real employee data must NOT be uploaded to public AI tools (ChatGPT free tier, etc.) |
| **Anonymise before analysis** | If using AI for data analysis, replace employee names and IC numbers with employee ID codes first |
| **Secure disposal** | Printed payslips that are no longer needed must be shredded, not discarded in general waste |
| **Retention** | Payroll records must be retained for 7 years per Income Tax Act 1967 requirements |

> **CRITICAL WARNING:** Uploading a payslip or payroll file containing real employee names, IC numbers, salary, and bank details to a public AI chatbot is a **potential breach of PDPA 2010**. Always anonymise data before AI analysis, or use a private/enterprise AI deployment with a data processing agreement.
