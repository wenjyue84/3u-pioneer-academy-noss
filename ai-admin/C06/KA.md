### Bahagian B Marking Guide

**B1a:** 128,000 + 64,000 + 17,000 + 24,500 = **233,500 ≠ 247,000** — discrepancy! The row sums to 233,500 but the Admin category is missing from the table. *Trainer note: The table above intentionally omits the "Admin" category (RM 13,500/month). Expected answer: trainees should notice the four categories sum to 233,500, not 247,000. Award 3 marks for: (i) correctly summing the four categories to 233,500 (1 mark), (ii) identifying the discrepancy (1 mark), (iii) noting a fifth category must exist accounting for 247,000 − 233,500 = RM 13,500 (1 mark).*

**B1b:** 128,000 ÷ 247,000 × 100 = **51.8%** (award marks for correct formula and working; accept 51.8% or 51.9% depending on rounding)

**B1c:** Problem: typographical error — letter "O" used instead of digit "0". Technique: Standardise Formats (Technique 3). Fix: Use Find & Replace (Ctrl + H) to find "5,5OO" and replace with "5,500", then verify the cell is stored as a number (right-aligned).

**B1d:** Sample answer: "Q2 logistics spend was consistent at RM 20,000–22,000 per month, totalling RM 64,000 for the quarter. Logistics represented [64,000 ÷ 247,000 × 100 = 25.9%] of Q2 total expenses, which is within the expected range based on prior months' data." Award marks for use of correct figures and specific data support.

**B2a:** 1,480,500 ÷ 97,000 = **15.3×** — exceeds the 10× threshold; status GREEN.

**B2b:** 142,500 ÷ 8 = **RM 17,813 per person** — does NOT meet RM 18,000 target; status YELLOW.

**B2c:** (35.6 + 36.8 + 38.7) ÷ 3 = 111.1 ÷ 3 = **37.03% ≈ 37.0%** — correct. Award full marks.

**B2d:** Accept any narrative that: uses only figures from KP-01/02/03 (RM 1,480,500; 12.2% growth; 32.5% annual margin; etc.); states what happened; gives a data-supported recommendation.

### Bahagian C Answer Key

**C1a Verification:**
- Revenue: 220,000 + 235,000 + 250,000 = **705,000** ✓
- Expenses: 165,000 + 172,000 + 178,000 = **515,000** ✓
- Gross Profit: 55,000 + 63,000 + 72,000 = **190,000** ✓
- Cross-check: 705,000 − 515,000 = **190,000** ✓

**C1b KPI calculations:**
- Revenue Growth (vs Q3 RM 650,000): (705,000 − 650,000) ÷ 650,000 × 100 = 55,000 ÷ 650,000 × 100 = **8.5%**
- Q4 GP Margin: 190,000 ÷ 705,000 × 100 = **26.9%**
- Employee Productivity: 705,000 ÷ 31 = **RM 22,742 per person-month**
- Marketing Efficiency: 705,000 ÷ 46,500 = **15.2×**

**C1c Scorecard:**
- Revenue Growth 8.5% vs. target ≥5%: **GREEN**
- GP Margin 26.9% vs. target ≥30%: **RED**
- Employee Productivity RM 22,742 vs. target ≥RM 20,000: **GREEN**
- Marketing Efficiency 15.2× vs. target ≥12×: **GREEN**

**C1d Executive Summary (sample):** Accept any summary that: includes Q4 revenue of RM 705,000 (8.5% growth); states GP Margin of 26.9%; identifies Employee Productivity and Marketing Efficiency as GREEN; flags GP Margin as RED; and gives one specific recommendation tied to the data (e.g., review expense structure, as expenses of RM 515,000 on revenue of RM 705,000 left only 26.9% margin).
