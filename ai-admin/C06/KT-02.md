**Expected answers for B1a (verification):**
- Staff Costs: 524,000 ÷ 999,000 × 100 = **52.5%** ✓
- Logistics: 254,000 ÷ 999,000 × 100 = **25.4%** ✓
- Utilities: 70,700 ÷ 999,000 × 100 = **7.1%** ✓
- Marketing: 97,000 ÷ 999,000 × 100 = **9.7%** ✓
- Admin: 53,300 ÷ 999,000 × 100 = **5.3%** ✓
- Sum: 52.5 + 25.4 + 7.1 + 9.7 + 5.3 = **100.0%** ✓

**Expected answer for B1c:** ChatGPT's figure of 54.2% is **incorrect**. The correct figure is 52.5% (524,000 ÷ 999,000 × 100). This figure must NOT be included in the management report without correction.

**Expected answers for B2a:**
- July: 34,000 ÷ 122,000 × 100 = **27.87% ≈ 27.9%** ✓
- August: 29,000 ÷ 115,000 × 100 = **25.22% ≈ 25.2%** ✓

**Expected answer for B2b:** The statement is correct. January (26.5%) and August (25.2%) are the two lowest. *However*, July (27.9%) is the third lowest. The Copilot statement asks about the "two lowest-margin months" — January and August are the correct answer, not July and August. Award 7 marks for identifying January (26.5%) and August (25.2%) as the two lowest.
