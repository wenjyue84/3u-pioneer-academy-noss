## Work Activity Title

Generate a Quarterly Business Performance Report Using AI Tools and Analyse Operational KPIs with AI to Produce Improvement Recommendations

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:

- Used ChatGPT Advanced Data Analysis or Excel Copilot to generate a structured quarterly performance report
- Calculated all five core KPIs for a specified quarter using the provided dataset
- Written a KPI Scorecard with RAG (Red/Amber/Green) status coding
- Identified two specific, data-supported improvement recommendations
- Included a data source declaration and AI-usage disclosure in the final report

## Provided Dataset (Q3 Focus: July, August, September 2025)

For this activity, trainees will produce a **Q3 2025 Quarterly Report** using data for the months of July, August, and September only.

**Syarikat Mentari Raya Sdn Bhd — Q3 2025 Data Extract:**

| Month | Revenue (RM) | Expenses (RM) | Gross_Profit (RM) | Staff_Costs (RM) | Logistics (RM) | Utilities (RM) | Marketing (RM) | Admin (RM) | Sales_Staff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jul-25 | 122,000 | 88,000 | 34,000 | 46,000 | 22,000 | 6,500 | 9,000 | 4,500 | 7 |
| Aug-25 | 115,000 | 86,000 | 29,000 | 46,000 | 21,000 | 6,000 | 8,500 | 4,500 | 7 |
| Sep-25 | 128,000 | 84,000 | 44,000 | 44,000 | 21,000 | 6,000 | 8,500 | 4,500 | 7 |
| **Q3 TOTAL** | **365,000** | **258,000** | **107,000** | **136,000** | **64,000** | **18,500** | **26,000** | **13,500** | — |

**Verification of Q3 totals:**
- Revenue: 122,000 + 115,000 + 128,000 = **365,000** ✓
- Expenses: 88,000 + 86,000 + 84,000 = **258,000** ✓
- Gross Profit: 34,000 + 29,000 + 44,000 = **107,000** ✓
- Staff_Costs: 46,000 + 46,000 + 44,000 = **136,000** ✓
- Logistics: 22,000 + 21,000 + 21,000 = **64,000** ✓

**Quarterly KPI Reference Values (for Step 3):**

| KPI | Q2 2025 (Apr+May+Jun) | Q3 2025 (Jul+Aug+Sep) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue (RM) | 373,000 | 365,000 | −8,000 (−2.1%) |
| Total Expenses (RM) | 247,000 | 258,000 | +11,000 (+4.5%) |
| Total Gross Profit (RM) | 126,000 | 107,000 | −19,000 (−15.1%) |
| Gross Profit Margin % | 33.8% | 29.3% | −4.5 percentage points |
| Revenue per Sales Staff (avg) | 17,810 | 17,381 | −429 |
| Marketing Spend (RM) | 24,500 | 26,000 | +1,500 |

**Verification of Q2 reference values:**
- Q2 Revenue: 118,000 + 125,000 + 130,000 = **373,000** ✓
- Q2 Gross Profit: 38,000 + 43,000 + 45,000 = **126,000** ✓
- Q2 GP Margin: 126,000 ÷ 373,000 × 100 = **33.8%** ✓
- Q3 GP Margin: 107,000 ÷ 365,000 × 100 = **29.3%** ✓
- Q2 to Q3 Revenue change: (365,000 − 373,000) ÷ 373,000 × 100 = **−2.1%** ✓

**Q3 KPI Targets (set by fictional management):**

| KPI | Management Target |
|---|---|

| Q3 Gross Profit Margin | ≥ 32% |
| Staff Costs as % of Expenses | ≤ 50% |
| Marketing Spend Efficiency (Revenue ÷ Marketing) | ≥ 12× |
| AR Days | ≤ 45 days |

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Data Preparation and Manual KPI Pre-Calculation (25 minutes)

Before using AI, manually calculate the five KPIs for Q3 2025 using the data above. This gives you the correct answers to compare against AI output.

**1a. Quarterly Revenue Growth Rate (Q3 vs. Q2):**

Formula: (Q3 Revenue − Q2 Revenue) ÷ Q2 Revenue × 100

Your calculation: _______________________________________________

Expected answer: (365,000 − 373,000) ÷ 373,000 × 100 = **−2.1%**

**1b. Q3 Gross Profit Margin:**

Formula: Q3 Gross Profit ÷ Q3 Revenue × 100

Your calculation: _______________________________________________

Expected answer: 107,000 ÷ 365,000 × 100 = **29.3%**

**1c. Q3 Employee Productivity Index (Revenue per Staff):**

Formula: Q3 Total Revenue ÷ (Average monthly staff count × 3 months)

Note: All 3 months in Q3 had 7 staff, so average = 7. Total person-months = 7 × 3 = 21.

Your calculation: 365,000 ÷ 21 = _______________________________________________

Expected answer: 365,000 ÷ 21 = **RM 17,381 per person-month**

**1d. Q3 Marketing Spend Efficiency:**

Formula: Q3 Revenue ÷ Q3 Marketing Spend

Your calculation: 365,000 ÷ 26,000 = _______________________________________________

Expected answer: 365,000 ÷ 26,000 = **14.0×**

**1e. Staff Costs as % of Q3 Total Expenses:**

Your calculation: 136,000 ÷ 258,000 × 100 = _______________________________________________

Expected answer: 136,000 ÷ 258,000 × 100 = **52.7%**

Record all five results in your KPI pre-calculation table before proceeding.

---

### Step 2: Upload Dataset to ChatGPT and Generate AI Analysis (30 minutes)

**2a. Prepare the CSV for Upload:**

Open `mentari_raya_2025.csv` in Excel. Filter to show only Jul-25, Aug-25, and Sep-25. Save as a new file named `mentari_raya_Q3_2025.csv`.

**2b. Upload to ChatGPT Advanced Data Analysis:**

In ChatGPT, click the paperclip icon → upload `mentari_raya_Q3_2025.csv` → then enter the following prompt:

```
I am uploading Q3 2025 (July, August, September) financial data for a fictional Malaysian 
electrical goods wholesaler called Syarikat Mentari Raya Sdn Bhd.

Act as a senior business analyst. Using only the data in this file:

1. Calculate the total Q3 Revenue, Expenses, and Gross Profit
2. Calculate the Q3 Gross Profit Margin %
3. Calculate the average monthly Revenue per Sales Staff for Q3
4. Calculate the Q3 Marketing Spend Efficiency (Total Revenue ÷ Total Marketing Spend)
5. Calculate Staff Costs as a percentage of Total Q3 Expenses
6. Identify the single weakest month in Q3 and explain why based on the data
7. Suggest two specific improvement actions for Q4 based solely on Q3 data patterns

Format your response as:
- Section 1: Q3 KPI Summary Table (KPI | Value | Status vs. Target)
- Section 2: Weakest Month Analysis (paragraph)
- Section 3: Two Q4 Recommendations (bullet points)

Targets for comparison: Revenue Growth ≥ +5%; GP Margin ≥ 32%; Staff Costs ≤ 50% of Expenses; Marketing Efficiency ≥ 12×

Note: All data is fictional training material. Do not add information not present in the uploaded file.
```

**2c. Record the ChatGPT output in your Process Log.**

> **VALIDATION STEP:** Compare ChatGPT's Q3 KPI values to your manual calculations from Step 1. For any value that does not match, re-check your own calculation first. If ChatGPT's figure is wrong, note the discrepancy in your Answer Sheet.

Using your verified figures (from Step 1 or corrected ChatGPT output), complete the KPI Scorecard:

| KPI | Q3 Target | Q3 Actual | Status (GREEN/YELLOW/RED) | Gap (if not GREEN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue Growth vs. Q2 | ≥ +5% | | | |
| Gross Profit Margin | ≥ 32% | | | |
| Staff Costs % of Expenses | ≤ 50% | | | |
| Marketing Spend Efficiency | ≥ 12× | | | |
| AR Days | ≤ 45 days | 45.6 days | | |

**Status coding rules:**
- **GREEN:** Meets or exceeds target
- **YELLOW:** Within 5% of target (e.g., target is 32%; actual is 30.5%–32%)
- **RED:** More than 5% below target
