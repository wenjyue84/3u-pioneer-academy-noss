**Expected answers (for marker reference):**

B1a: Q1 total RM 315,000 vs. Q2 RM 373,000. Growth = (373,000 − 315,000) ÷ 315,000 × 100 = 58,000 ÷ 315,000 × 100 = **18.4%** (Q1 to Q2, not Q2 to Q1 — accept either direction if stated clearly; award marks for correct formula and working regardless of which direction is used)

B1b: Q1 GP Margin = 90,000 ÷ 315,000 × 100 = **28.6%**

B1c: Total person-months = (5 × 1) + (5 × 1) + (6 × 1) = 16. Q1 Productivity = 315,000 ÷ 16 = **RM 19,688 per person-month**

B1d: Q1 Marketing Efficiency = 315,000 ÷ 22,500 = **14.0×**

B1e KPI Scorecard:
- Revenue Growth vs. Q2: Q1 came before Q2, so this comparison cannot be made as stated. Accept: "Q1 total RM 315,000; comparison to prior quarter not applicable" or mark GREEN if trainee calculates vs. a prior stated baseline. *(Note to trainer: re-examine this target wording — it makes more sense to compare Q1 to Q2 as percentage growth. Mark GREEN if ≥5%; YELLOW if 0–5%; RED if negative.)*
- Q1 GP Margin: 28.6% — **YELLOW** (28.6% is between 25% and 32%; within 5 percentage points below 30% target — alternatively RED if below 30%)
- Q1 Productivity: RM 19,688 — **GREEN** (≥ RM 18,000)
- Q1 Marketing Efficiency: 14.0× — **GREEN** (≥ 10×)
- AR Days: 45.6 — **YELLOW** (above target of ≤45, but within 5% of target)

B2 errors:
1. "RM 1,560,000" should be RM 1,480,500 (KP-01 total)
2. "15% growth" — KP-03 states 12.2% annual growth rate vs. RM 1,320,000 baseline
3. "Staff costs 55% of total expenses" — KP-02 states 52.5%
4. "No AI disclosure required" — KP-03 Section 4.3 states all reports must include AI and data source disclosure regardless of whether the tool is internal

C1a: 126,000 ÷ 373,000 × 100 = **33.8%**

C1b: Total person-months = 19. Productivity = 373,000 ÷ 19 = **RM 19,632 per person-month**
