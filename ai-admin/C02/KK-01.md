## Work Activity Title

Draft, Review, and Submit a Set of Three Business Correspondence Documents Using AI Assistance

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:

- Used a structured CRAFT prompt to generate a formal BM surat rasmi to a government body
- Produced a formal English quotation letter with an accurate SST calculation
- Drafted a professional complaint-response memo in English
- Applied the 4-point AI quality check to each document before finalisation
- Compiled a prompt log documenting all AI instructions used

---

---

## Safety Precautions

- Do **not** enter real client names, IC numbers, personal phone numbers, or company SSM registration numbers into any AI tool. Use the simulated scenario details provided in this worksheet only.
- Do **not** use statistics or legal provision numbers unless you can verify them from KP-01 or a cited government source.
- Save your work documents every 10 minutes.
- Submit your Prompt Log together with the final documents — incomplete logs will result in deduction of marks.

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. Which of the three documents was hardest to get right using AI, and why? What did you have to edit most?
2. When you applied the quality check to the quotation letter, did the AI calculate the SST correctly? What does this tell you about the reliability of AI for financial calculations?
3. How would you use a Prompt Log in your future workplace? Who else in the organisation should have access to it?
4. If you had to draft all three documents without AI, how much longer would it have taken? What was the single biggest time saving from using AI?
