## Delivery Notes

- **Environment:** Computer lab — one computer per trainee with internet access; shared printer
- **Class size:** Maximum 25 trainees
- **Trainer role in practical sessions:** Observer and coach — trainees work independently; trainer circulates, answers questions, does not do the work for trainees
- **KK link:** Practical sessions P1–P6 prepare trainees for KK-01 and KK-02 assessed tasks
- **PDPA reminder:** At the start of every practical session, trainer must remind trainees not to enter real personal data into AI tools

### SESSION P1 — WA1, Part 1 of 4

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Session Code** | P1 |
| **Work Activity** | WA1: DRAFT BUSINESS CORRESPONDENCE IN MULTIPLE LANGUAGES USING AI WRITING TOOLS |
| **Duration** | 2 hours |
| **Prerequisite** | Completion of Theory Session T1 and T2 |

**Session Objectives:**

By the end of this session, trainees will have:
- Independently written and tested 3 CRAFT prompts for different correspondence types
- Generated a BM memo using ChatGPT with a correctly structured prompt
- Identified at least 2 errors in an AI-generated document during quality check

**Activity Plan:**

| Time | Trainee Activity | Trainer Action | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Open ChatGPT or Claude; log in; review KP-01 CRAFT framework reference card | Brief setup reminder; confirm all computers have internet | Computer; CRAFT reference card |
| 0:10–0:40 | **Task P1-A:** Write a CRAFT prompt and generate a BM internal memo announcing new office hours (specific details given by trainer). Apply quality check. Record in Prompt Log. | Circulate; check prompt quality; prompt trainees on weak CRAFT elements | Task P1-A brief handout; KP-01; Prompt Log template |
| 0:40–1:10 | **Task P1-B:** Write a CRAFT prompt and generate a short English business email (not a letter) from a training company admin to a corporate client confirming a meeting. Apply quality check. | Observe; note common errors for debrief | Task P1-B brief handout |
| 1:10–1:40 | **Task P1-C:** Use Microsoft Word Copilot (if available) OR copy the P1-B email into Word and manually format it as a business letter. Compare Copilot output (if used) vs. ChatGPT output. | Assist trainees with Word Copilot access if needed | Microsoft Word / Microsoft 365 |
| 1:40–2:00 | Debrief: 3 trainees share their Prompt Logs; class identifies the most and least effective prompts; trainer summarises key CRAFT improvement points | Facilitate discussion | Projector (trainee screen sharing) |

**Observable Learning Outcomes:**
- Trainee can write a CRAFT prompt without referring to the template
- Trainee applies quality check without prompting
- Prompt Log is completed with at least 2 of 3 columns filled

**Session Objectives:**
