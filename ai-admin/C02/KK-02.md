## Work Activity Title

Design, Configure, and Document a Cloud-Based Document Management System with PDPA 2010 Compliance Features

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:

- Designed a folder structure for a simulated Malaysian SME appropriate to its business functions
- Applied a standardised file naming convention to at least 6 sample documents
- Configured access permissions in Google Drive or SharePoint aligned with the Principle of Least Privilege
- Created a PDPA-compliant retention schedule covering at least 6 document types
- Produced a one-page DMS Policy document (Polisi Pengurusan Dokumen)

## Safety Precautions

- Use only the simulated company scenario provided — do not use real employee names, real IC numbers, or real company SSM registration numbers.
- Do not upload actual personal data or confidential business information to your personal Google Drive or SharePoint.
- When configuring sharing settings in Google Drive, ensure the shared folders are set to **"Restricted"** (not accessible to anyone with the link) at the end of the activity.
- Save screenshots of your folder structure and permission settings as evidence.

## SME Scenario

You are the **Admin Manager** at **Bintang Cemerlang Trading Sdn Bhd**, a trading company in Shah Alam, Selangor. The company has **15 staff** in 4 departments:

| Department | Staff Count | Types of Documents They Handle |
|---|---|---|
| **Administration** | 3 | Correspondence, contracts, licences, meeting minutes |
| **Human Resources** | 2 | Employee records, payroll records, leave records, EPF/SOCSO files |
| **Finance** | 3 | Invoices, receipts, bank statements, tax returns |
| **Sales & Operations** | 7 | Quotations, purchase orders, delivery orders, client correspondence |

The company currently stores all documents in a single WhatsApp group and individual staff members' personal email — no shared system exists. You have been tasked with setting up a proper cloud-based DMS.

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. When you set the HR folder to "restricted" in Google Drive, what happened to any shared links that previously existed for that folder? What does this tell you about the risk of using shared links for sensitive HR documents?
2. Looking at the retention schedule, which document type has the **longest** retention period? Why does Malaysian law require such a long period for this type of document?
3. When you drafted the DMS Policy using AI, did the AI include specific references to PDPA 2010 correctly? Did you need to edit or verify those references? What does this tell you about using AI for compliance documentation?
4. How would this DMS design change if the company grew from 15 to 80 staff across 3 locations? Name two specific structural changes you would make.
