## Arahan Penilai / Assessor Instructions

- This Performance Assessment is conducted by the **trainer / assessor** through **direct observation** of the trainee performing real tasks.
- The assessor must observe the trainee completing Tasks 1–4 below, which correspond directly to the four Work Activities of C02.
- Mark each criterion on the 4-point rubric scale: **4 = Excellent**, **3 = Good**, **2 = Adequate**, **1 = Needs Work**.
- Evidence (printed documents, screenshots, DMS screenshots) must be collected and attached to this assessment sheet.
- **Minimum passing total: 60 / 100 (60%)**
- The assessor must NOT assist the trainee during the assessment tasks. Observe and mark only.

The trainee must also describe the correct secure disposal method for one specific document type.
