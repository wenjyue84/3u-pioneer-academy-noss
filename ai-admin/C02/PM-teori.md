## Delivery Notes

- **Medium of instruction:** English (bilingual explanations in Malay where necessary for regulatory and format-specific content)
- **Class size:** Maximum 25 trainees
- **Room requirements:** Computer lab or lecture hall with projector; trainees require access to a computer with internet for live demonstrations
- **Pre-reading:** Trainees should have completed KP-01, KP-02, and KP-03 reference sheets before attending theory sessions
- **Assessment links:** KT-01 due at end of KP-01 block; KT-02 due at end of KP-02 block; KT-03 due at end of KP-03 block

## Session Plans

### SESSION T1 — KP-01 Block, Part 1 of 2

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Session Code** | T1 |
| **Topic** | AI Writing Tools Overview and CRAFT Prompt Engineering |
| **KP Reference** | KP-01 Sections 1–2 |
| **Duration** | 2 hours |
| **Work Activities Covered** | WA1: Draft Business Correspondence in Multiple Languages Using AI Writing Tools |

**Learning Objectives:**

By the end of this session, trainees will be able to:
1. Name five AI writing tools and describe their key features and access methods.
2. Explain the two main limitations of AI writing tools that require human review.
3. Apply the CRAFT framework to construct a structured prompt for a business correspondence task.

**Session Outline:**

| Time | Activity | Method | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Welcome and unit overview — what C02 covers, assessment structure | Lecture | Slide deck: C02 Overview |
| 0:15–0:45 | AI writing tools comparison: ChatGPT, Claude, Word Copilot, Google Docs AI, Notion AI — live demonstration of each | Demonstration (live, trainer's screen) | Internet access; ChatGPT and Claude free accounts |
| 0:45–1:00 | AI limitations: hallucination, outdated language, cultural inappropriateness — case study of BM letter with wrong honorific | Discussion | Printed case study handout |
| 1:00–1:30 | CRAFT framework introduction — each element explained with examples; trainees practice writing one CRAFT prompt for an assigned scenario | Guided practice (pairs) | Whiteboard; KP-01 handout |
| 1:30–1:45 | Pairs share prompts; trainer demonstrates entering prompts into ChatGPT and reviewing output | Demonstration + Q&A | Projector |
| 1:45–2:00 | Recap and check: each trainee writes a CRAFT prompt for a new scenario independently (exit ticket) | Individual writing | Exit ticket form |

**Resources Required:**

- KP-01 printed reference sheets (25 copies)
- Computer with internet + projector (trainer)
- ChatGPT free account (trainer demo)
- Claude free account (trainer demo)
- CRAFT framework reference card (laminated, one per pair)
- Case study handout: BM letter with honorific error (25 copies)

### SESSION T2 — KP-01 Block, Part 2 of 2

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Session Code** | T2 |
| **Topic** | Malaysian Correspondence Formats and AI Quality-Check Protocol |
| **KP Reference** | KP-01 Sections 2–5 |
| **Duration** | 2 hours |
| **Work Activities Covered** | WA1: Draft Business Correspondence in Multiple Languages Using AI Writing Tools |

**Learning Objectives:**

By the end of this session, trainees will be able to:
1. Identify all required structural elements of a BM Surat Rasmi, English business letter, and internal memo.
2. Produce a correctly formatted quotation letter including SST calculation.
3. Apply the 4-point quality check to an AI-generated document and identify errors.
4. Explain the purpose and required content of a Prompt Log.

**Session Outline:**

| Time | Activity | Method | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | BM Surat Rasmi format — annotated example projected; trainer walks through each element; trainees spot errors in a deliberately incorrect sample | Lecture + Error-spotting exercise | Projector; printed sample letters (correct and incorrect versions) |
| 0:30–0:50 | English business letter — "Yours sincerely" vs. "Yours faithfully" drill; quotation letter structure; SST calculation worked example (RM 3,000 × 1.08 = RM 3,240) | Lecture + Whiteboard calculation | Whiteboard; calculator available |
| 0:50–1:10 | Internal memo format vs. formal letter — comparison table; trainees complete a blank memo template from a given scenario | Guided practice | Printed blank memo template |
| 1:10–1:35 | 4-point quality check — each point explained; trainees apply the checklist to a pre-generated AI letter (with deliberate errors seeded) | Small group activity | Printed AI letter with errors; quality check checklist handout |
| 1:35–1:50 | Prompt Log — what it is, why it matters for PDPA compliance; demonstrate completing a Prompt Log entry | Demonstration | Prompt Log template handout |
| 1:50–2:00 | Q&A; preview of KT-01 assignment | Q&A | KT-01 assignment brief |

**Resources Required:**

- KP-01 printed reference sheets
- Annotated BM Surat Rasmi sample (correctly formatted)
- Deliberately incorrect BM Surat Rasmi (for error-spotting exercise) — 25 copies
- Blank memo template (25 copies)
- AI-generated letter with seeded errors + quality check checklist (25 copies)
- Prompt Log template (25 copies)
- KT-01 assignment brief (25 copies)

### SESSION T3 — KP-02 Block, Part 1 of 2

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Session Code** | T3 |
| **Topic** | AI Presentation Design and Chart Type Selection |
| **KP Reference** | KP-02 Sections 1–2 |
| **Duration** | 2 hours |
| **Work Activities Covered** | WA2: Create Reports and Presentations with AI-Assisted Data Visualisation |

**Learning Objectives:**

By the end of this session, trainees will be able to:
1. Use PowerPoint Copilot (step-by-step) to generate a presentation deck from a brief.
2. Select the appropriate chart type for a given business data scenario.
3. Identify the critical post-generation editing steps required before submitting AI-generated presentations.
4. Explain the risk of AI hallucination in statistics within presentation content.

**Session Outline:**

| Time | Activity | Method | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Recap of T2; introduction to KP-02 — the 3 skills covered (presentations, charts, reports) | Lecture | Slide deck: C02-T3 |
| 0:20–0:55 | Live demo: PowerPoint Copilot — trainer generates a training proposal deck from a brief; walks through 5-step process; highlights what Copilot does well and where it fails | Live demonstration | Microsoft 365 with Copilot (trainer computer) |
| 0:55–1:15 | Chart type selection — trainer shows 5 chart types; for each: when to use, when NOT to use; pie chart limitation (>6 segments) demonstrated with a bad example | Lecture + visual examples | Slide with chart examples |
| 1:15–1:40 | Chart selection exercise: trainees receive 4 dataset descriptions and must choose the correct chart type and explain why (same format as KT-02 Q2) | Individual exercise | Exercise handout |
| 1:40–1:55 | Debrief exercise; discuss AI hallucination in statistics — example: "47% growth" scenario from KA | Discussion | Projector |
| 1:55–2:00 | Summary; assign KT-02 Soalan 1 as pre-work | Instruction | KT-02 partial handout |

**Resources Required:**

- Computer with Microsoft 365 Copilot + projector (trainer)
- Slide deck with chart type examples (good and bad)
- Chart selection exercise handout (25 copies)
- KT-02 partial handout (Soalan 1 section only, 25 copies)

### SESSION T4 — KP-02 Block, Part 2 of 2

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Session Code** | T4 |
| **Topic** | Executive Report Structure, Data Verification, and Notion AI |
| **KP Reference** | KP-02 Sections 3–4 |
| **Duration** | 2 hours |
| **Work Activities Covered** | WA2: Create Reports and Presentations with AI-Assisted Data Visualisation |

**Learning Objectives:**

By the end of this session, trainees will be able to:
1. Name the 10 sections of a Malaysian management report in the correct sequence with BM equivalents.
2. Apply the AI-assisted report drafting workflow (4 phases) to a given reporting scenario.
3. Describe the data verification procedure and explain why it must be performed manually.
4. Use Notion AI to generate meeting minutes action items from a transcript.

**Session Outline:**

| Time | Activity | Method | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Management report structure — trainer walks through all 10 sections; trainees fill in a blank template as trainer presents | Lecture + fill-in activity | Blank 10-section report template (25 copies) |
| 0:25–0:50 | AI report drafting workflow — 4 phases demonstrated: outline → section draft → human data insertion → final formatting; trainer demos Phase 1 and 2 live with ChatGPT | Live demonstration | ChatGPT (trainer); projector |
| 0:50–1:10 | Data verification protocol — "never trust AI figures without checking" rule; trainer shows an example where ChatGPT swapped data series in a chart; trainees practise the 5-step verification procedure with a provided dataset | Demonstration + practise | Sample dataset with AI output (25 copies); calculator |
| 1:10–1:35 | Notion AI — demo: paste a meeting transcript, use Summarise, then "Extract action items"; trainees see the difference between raw AI output and edited minutes | Live demonstration | Notion account (trainer); projector |
| 1:35–1:50 | Meeting minutes structure — trainer explains the BM format from KP-02; comparison with memo format | Lecture | KP-02 reference sheet |
| 1:50–2:00 | Recap; preview KT-02; Q&A | Q&A | KT-02 brief |

**Resources Required:**

- Blank management report template (25 copies)
- Sample dataset with intentional AI chart error (25 copies)
- Notion account (trainer demo)
- Meeting transcript for Notion AI demo (prepared by trainer)
- KT-02 assignment brief (25 copies)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **KP Reference** | KP-03 Sections 1–2 |

**Learning Objectives:**

By the end of this session, trainees will be able to:
1. Name all 8 core components of a DMS and explain each one's function.
2. Design a 3-level folder structure for a simulated Malaysian SME.

**Session Outline:**

| Time | Activity | Method | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|

| 0:45–1:10 | Folder structure design — recommended 4-department SME structure shown; trainees draw their own structure for a 3-department scenario (school, clinic, or trading company) on paper | Guided design activity | Blank folder structure worksheet (25 copies) |
| 1:10–1:35 | File naming convention — rule explained; 6 "bad filename" examples projected; trainees rename each one correctly; discuss version numbering rule (no "final") | Pair exercise | Renaming worksheet (25 copies) |
| 1:35–1:55 | Google Drive vs. SharePoint comparison — key features; pricing (2026 rates); which is better for Malaysian SMEs at different scales | Lecture + discussion | Comparison table slide |
| 1:55–2:00 | Summary; preview T6 | Instruction | |

**Resources Required:**

- DMS component card sets (one set per pair — 13 pairs × 2 sets of cards each)
- Blank folder structure worksheet (25 copies)
- Renaming worksheet (25 copies)
- KP-03 reference sheets (25 copies)
- Google Drive and SharePoint pricing comparison slide (updated 2026 rates)

### SESSION T6 — KP-03 Block, Part 2 of 2

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Session Code** | T6 |
| **Topic** | Electronic Signatures, PDPA 2010 Compliance, and DMS Policy |
| **KP Reference** | KP-03 Sections 4–6 |
| **Duration** | 2 hours |
| **Work Activities Covered** | WA3: Build an AI-Powered Document Management and Retrieval System; WA4: Apply PDPA 2010 Compliance Standards to Document Handling Procedures |

**Learning Objectives:**

By the end of this session, trainees will be able to:
1. State the Malaysian legislation governing electronic signatures and identify which document types are excluded.
2. Apply PDPA 2010's seven principles to document management scenarios.
3. Produce a document retention schedule with correct legal retention periods and disposal methods.
4. Describe all 10 required sections of a DMS Policy document.

**Session Outline:**

| Time | Activity | Method | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Electronic Commerce Act 2006 — overview; Section 7, 9, 10 explained; three excluded document types emphasised; DocuSign and SignNow demonstrated briefly | Lecture + mini demo | Slide deck; DocuSign account (trainer) |
| 0:25–0:55 | PDPA 2010 — 7 principles applied to documents; "client requests data deletion" scenario worked through as a class; retention obligations vs. deletion obligation balanced | Case study discussion | Printed scenario handout (25 copies) |
| 0:55–1:20 | Retention schedule — class completes the retention table together (6 document types); legal Acts cited for each; disposal methods discussed | Guided completion | Blank retention schedule (25 copies) |
| 1:20–1:45 | DMS Policy structure — all 10 sections listed; trainer walks through each section; trainees draft a 2-sentence description for Sections 1, 8, and 9 individually | Lecture + individual writing | KP-03 reference |
| 1:45–1:55 | PDPA penalty emphasis — RM 1,000,000 / 3 years (as amended by Act A1727, in force 1 Apr 2025); real-world Malaysian enforcement context | Lecture | Slide |
