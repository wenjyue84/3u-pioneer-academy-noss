## BAHAGIAN A — Soalan Aneka Pilihan (Multiple Choice) (30 Markah)

*Setiap soalan bernilai 1.5 markah. Pilih satu jawapan yang PALING TEPAT.*

**1.** Which element of the CRAFT prompt engineering framework specifies the length and layout of the AI output?

- A. Context
- B. Role
- C. Format
- D. Tone

**2.** A Malaysian government-standard Surat Rasmi requires the second paragraph to begin with which number?

- A. "1."
- B. "2."
- C. "a."
- D. The second paragraph has no number.

**3.** An admin officer addresses a formal English letter to "Dear Sir/Madam." The correct closing should be:

- A. Yours sincerely
- B. With regards
- C. Yours faithfully
- D. Best wishes

**4.** Which of the following is **NOT** a listed capability of Microsoft PowerPoint Copilot?

- A. Generating a complete deck from a written brief
- B. Rewriting individual slides to improve conciseness
- C. Automatically calculating financial figures in a slide
- D. Adjusting the tone of a slide's content

**5.** According to KP-02, which chart type becomes unreadable when used with more than 5–6 data segments?

- A. Bar chart
- B. Line chart
- C. Pie chart
- D. Scatter plot

**6.** The standard Malaysian management report section that provides a 1-page summary of key findings and recommendations is called the:

- A. Introduction
- B. Methodology
- C. Executive Summary
- D. Conclusion

**7.** In the recommended DMS file naming convention, the correct format for a date is:

- A. DD-MM-YYYY
- B. YYYYMMDD
- C. MM/DD/YYYY
- D. DD MMM YYYY

**8.** Which Malaysian Act provides the legal basis for the validity of electronic documents and signatures?

- A. Personal Data Protection Act 2010
- B. Electronic Commerce Act 2006
- C. Communications and Multimedia Act 1998
- D. Companies Act 2016

**9.** Under the Electronic Commerce Act 2006, which of the following documents **cannot** be executed with an electronic signature?

- A. Employment contract
- B. Non-disclosure agreement
- C. Service agreement
- D. Transfer of land ownership

**10.** Under the Income Tax Act 1967, business financial records must be retained for a minimum of:

- A. 3 years
- B. 5 years
- C. 7 years
- D. 10 years

**11.** The PDPA 2010 principle that prohibits a company from sharing client contact details with a third-party marketing firm without consent is the:

- A. Security Principle
- B. Retention Principle
- C. Disclosure Principle
- D. Access Principle

**12.** A Prompt Log records which of the following? *(Select the BEST answer)*

- A. The monthly cost of using AI tools
- B. The AI instructions used to generate a document, changes made, and the reviewer's name
- C. The word count and grammar score of the AI-generated document
- D. The IT administrator's approval for AI tool usage

**13.** Notion AI's "Action items" feature is used to:

- A. Assign tasks to meeting attendees via email
- B. Extract numbered action items with owners and deadlines from meeting notes
- C. Calculate the cost of actions identified in a meeting
- D. Generate a Gantt chart from meeting discussions

**14.** The Principle of Least Privilege (PLP) in access control means:

- A. All staff should have equal access to all company documents
- B. Senior management should have no access restrictions
- C. Each staff member can only access the documents needed for their specific job function
- D. Passwords must be changed every 30 days

**15.** Under PDPA 2010 as amended by Act A1727 (in force 1 April 2025), the correct maximum penalty for a data breach due to improper document disposal is:

- A. RM 100,000 fine / 1 year imprisonment
- B. RM 300,000 fine / 2 years imprisonment *(original 2010 Act figure, now superseded)*
- C. RM 500,000 fine / 3 years imprisonment
- D. RM 1,000,000 fine / 3 years imprisonment

**16.** Which feature distinguishes Microsoft SharePoint from Google Drive for enterprise document management?

- A. SharePoint is free while Google Drive requires a subscription
- B. SharePoint supports rich metadata columns and retention labels
- C. SharePoint cannot be used for co-authoring documents
- D. Google Drive does not support access permissions

**17.** In a BM Surat Rasmi, the closing motto used when writing to a government body is:

- A. "BERSAMA MEMBANGUN NEGARA"
- B. "BERKHIDMAT UNTUK NEGARA"
- C. "RAKYAT DIDAHULUKAN"
- D. "MALAYSIA BOLEH"

**18.** When using ChatGPT for data analysis, the verification step requires that:

- A. ChatGPT re-runs the analysis twice to confirm accuracy
- B. The user manually checks at least 3 data points in the AI chart against the source data
- C. The IT department reviews the CSV file before upload
- D. The AI output is compared to a previous year's report

**19.** A DMS Retention Schedule for employee personal data (non-payroll) after resignation is governed by which legislation?

- A. Companies Act 2016
- B. Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and Employment Act 1955
- C. Income Tax Act 1967
- D. Electronic Commerce Act 2006

**20.** The recommended maximum folder hierarchy depth in a DMS is:

- A. 2 levels
- B. 3–4 levels
- C. 5–6 levels
- D. There is no recommended limit
