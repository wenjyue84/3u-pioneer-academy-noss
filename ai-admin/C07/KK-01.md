## Work Activity Title

Draft an AI Usage Policy (Polisi Penggunaan AI) and Deliver a 5-Minute Staff Briefing for a Simulated Malaysian SME

**Primary Work Activities Covered:** Work Activities 1 and 3

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:

- Produced a 2-page AI Usage Policy document covering all required components
- Correctly applied PDPA 2010 principles to data handling rules within the policy
- Referenced MCMC AI usage guidance in the policy
- Prepared and delivered a 5-minute briefing explaining key policy points to "staff"
- Responded to at least two challenge questions from the trainer/assessor

## Business Scenario

You are the newly appointed administrative executive at **Syarikat Cemerlang Sdn Bhd**, a fictional Malaysian SME operating in the accounting and bookkeeping services sector, with 12 employees. The Managing Director has asked you to:

1. Draft a formal **AI Usage Policy (Polisi Penggunaan AI)** for the company
2. Present the key points of the policy to staff in a 5-minute briefing

The company currently uses these AI tools (check current approved/unapproved status as part of your task):
- Microsoft 365 with Copilot (licensed for business)
- ChatGPT Free (used informally by several staff — not officially approved)
- WhatsApp for client communication

### Step 2: Draft the AI Usage Policy (45 minutes)

Using Microsoft Word or Google Docs, produce a **2-page AI Usage Policy** for Syarikat Cemerlang Sdn Bhd. The policy MUST contain ALL eight sections listed below. Write the full content of each section — do not write headers only.

#### Section 1 — Policy Purpose and Scope

State: (a) why the policy exists, (b) who it applies to, and (c) the effective date.

#### Section 2 — Approved AI Tools

Provide a table of AI tools approved for business use. For each approved tool, state: tool name, approved business purpose, and any conditions of use (e.g., "Enterprise license required; no client PII to be entered without DPA").

#### Section 3 — Prohibited Uses

List a minimum of **six (6)** specific prohibited behaviours. Each item must be stated clearly enough that an employee could not misinterpret it. At least two items must be directly linked to PDPA 2010 obligations (state the PDPA principle by name).

#### Section 4 — Data Handling Rules

Write data handling rules for AI tool use covering:
- What categories of data (refer to your classification from KP-03) may and may not be entered into AI tools
- The requirement to obtain consent before processing client personal data through an AI tool
- The requirement to verify all AI-generated outputs before using them in official documents

#### Section 5 — Verification and Human Oversight

State: (a) the verification protocol that must be followed before any AI-generated content is used officially, and (b) which categories of documents require sign-off by a named human reviewer.

#### Section 6 — Intellectual Property and Confidentiality

State: (a) rules on publishing AI-generated content externally, and (b) prohibition on uploading confidential or restricted data to any AI tool.

#### Section 7 — Consequences of Breach

State the disciplinary action that will apply to employees who violate this policy. Include at least two graduated consequence levels (e.g., warning → dismissal).

#### Section 8 — Staff Acknowledgement Block

Provide a signature block for employees to sign to confirm they have read and understood the policy. Include: employee name, designation, date, signature line.

### Step 4: Deliver the Briefing and Respond to Questions (10 minutes)

Deliver your 5-minute briefing to the trainer/assessor (who will role-play as a staff member). After the briefing, the trainer will ask **two challenge questions** from the list below. Answer them without referring to your notes.

Possible challenge questions (trainer will select two):
1. "I use ChatGPT to help me write client emails — is that allowed?"
2. "What's the difference between Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT Free in terms of our client data?"
3. "If I accidentally send a client's personal details to the wrong person by email, what do I do?"
4. "Why can't we just use any AI tool we want? They all do the same thing, don't they?"
5. "Does PDPA apply to data about our employees as well, or just clients?"

## Reflection Questions (Soalan Refleksi)

1. Why is it important for an AI Usage Policy to be a written document rather than just a verbal agreement among staff?
2. A staff member argues that the prohibition on ChatGPT Free is too strict — "I only use it to summarise meeting notes, not client data." How would you respond?
3. How often should an AI Usage Policy be reviewed and updated? What events might trigger an out-of-cycle review?
