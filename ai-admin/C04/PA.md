## Arahan Penilai / Assessor Instructions

1. This performance assessment is conducted during supervised practical sessions (KK-01 and KK-02).
2. Observe each trainee individually and record observations in real time.
3. Each Work Activity is assessed separately. Score only what is directly observed — do not award marks for tasks the trainee describes but does not demonstrate.
4. A trainee must achieve a minimum of 50% in the overall PA to be deemed competent.
5. Where a task cannot be completed (e.g., tool unavailable), record the reason in the Notes column and assess the trainee on equivalent simulation or written substitution as authorised by the trainer.
