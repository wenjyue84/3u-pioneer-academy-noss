## Objektif Pembelajaran / Learning Objectives

Upon completion of this Information Sheet, trainees will be able to:

1. Explain the structure of Malaysia's Sales and Service Tax (SST) and identify which businesses must register.
2. Describe the SST-02 return filing process and the admin staff role in supporting it.
3. Explain the LHDN MyInvois e-Invoice system — its purpose, mandatory rollout, and the admin workflow for e-Invoice submission.
4. Identify the key SSM administrative obligations relevant to a Malaysian SME's admin staff.
5. Apply financial document retention requirements aligned with Malaysian legislation.

## 1. Sales and Service Tax (SST) — Cukai Jualan dan Perkhidmatan

### 1.1 Background

Malaysia re-introduced the **Sales and Service Tax (SST / Cukai Jualan dan Perkhidmatan)** on 1 September 2018, replacing the Goods and Services Tax (GST) which was zero-rated in June 2018. SST is administered by the **Royal Malaysian Customs Department (RMCD / Jabatan Kastam Diraja Malaysia)**.

SST has two components:
- **Sales Tax (Cukai Jualan):** A single-stage tax imposed on manufacturers and importers of taxable goods
- **Service Tax (Cukai Perkhidmatan):** A tax imposed on providers of taxable services

> **NOTA:** The rate and scope of SST are subject to changes by the Malaysian government. As of the writing of this WIM, the current rates and thresholds are available at the RMCD portal (https://mysst.customs.gov.my). Admin staff must always verify the current applicable rate with the RMCD portal or the company's accountant — do not rely on this document for rate confirmation.

### 1.2 Sales Tax (Cukai Jualan)

Sales Tax is levied at the **manufacturer or importer level** — it is a single-stage tax, meaning it is charged once in the supply chain (when goods leave the manufacturer or are imported), not at every transaction level.

**Key characteristics:**

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Who pays | Manufacturers and importers of taxable goods |
| Tax base | Sale value of taxable goods |
| Rate | Refer to current RMCD schedule (varies by goods category) |
| Threshold | Registration is required when annual sales of taxable goods exceed the registration threshold; check current threshold with RMCD |
| Exemptions | Certain goods are Sales Tax exempt (raw materials for manufacturers, specific essential goods); see RMCD exemption schedule |

**Admin staff responsibility:** Admin staff in a manufacturing or importing business must ensure that Sales Tax-inclusive invoices correctly state the Sales Tax amount and that the accounting software applies the correct Sales Tax code.

### 1.3 Service Tax (Cukai Perkhidmatan)

Service Tax is levied on providers of **prescribed taxable services** as listed in the Service Tax Regulations 2018 (and amendments).

**Common taxable service categories:**

| Service Category | Example Businesses |
|---|---|
| Professional services | Accountants, lawyers, consultants, IT service providers |
| Food and beverage (above threshold) | Restaurants, cafes (above registration threshold) |
| Accommodation | Hotels, serviced apartments |
| Telecommunications | Telcos (Maxis, Celcom, Digi, U Mobile) |
| Insurance | General and life insurance providers |
| Courier and logistics | Nationwide courier companies |

> **NOTA PENTING / IMPORTANT NOTE:** Whether a specific service is taxable depends on the current Service Tax Regulations. Service Tax exemptions and scope have been amended since 2018. Admin staff must verify with the company's accountant or the RMCD portal whether their employer's services are taxable under the current regulations.

### 1.4 SST Registration

A business must register for SST when its annual sales of taxable goods or provision of taxable services exceed the registration threshold prescribed by RMCD. The registration threshold has been updated periodically — consult the current RMCD MySST portal for the applicable threshold.

**Registration process (MySST portal):**

1. Access the MySST portal at https://mysst.customs.gov.my
2. Submit online SST registration application with supporting documents
3. Upon approval, receive SST Registration Number (No. Pendaftaran SST)
4. Display SST Registration Number on all tax invoices issued
5. Begin charging SST from the effective registration date

### 1.5 Tax Invoice Requirements

Once SST-registered, the business must issue **Tax Invoices (Invois Cukai)** that include the following mandatory fields:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Words "Tax Invoice" / "Invois Cukai" | Must appear prominently |
| Tax Invoice number | Unique sequential number |
| Date of issue | Date the invoice is issued |
| Supplier's SST Registration Number | Issued by RMCD |
| Supplier's name and address | Registered business particulars |
| Customer's name and address | Recipient details |
| Description of goods/services | Clear description |
| Quantity and unit price | For goods |
| Net amount (before SST) | Subtotal |
| SST amount | Amount of SST charged |
| Total amount (including SST) | Grand total |

### 1.6 SST-02 Return Filing

SST-registered businesses must file a **SST-02 Return (Penyata SST-02)** bi-monthly (every 2 months). The return reports total taxable sales and the SST collected, and any input credits (for service tax only, under specific conditions).

**Filing process:**

1. Gather all Tax Invoices issued during the taxable period
2. Reconcile with accounting software SST transaction report
3. Complete SST-02 form on the MySST portal
4. Pay any SST due by the filing deadline (check current deadline with RMCD)
5. Keep all supporting documents for at least 7 years

**Admin staff role in SST-02 filing:**
- Export SST transaction report from AutoCount/SQL Accounting
- Verify all tax invoices are recorded in the system
- Provide data to accountant or SST agent for return preparation
- File the return via MySST portal (if authorised by employer)
- Save confirmation of filing and payment receipt

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Late filing or non-filing of SST-02 attracts penalties under the Service Tax Act 2018 and Sales Tax Act 2018. Admin staff must track the bimonthly filing calendar and alert management in advance of each deadline. The current penalty rate — consult the relevant Acts or the RMCD portal; do not rely on figures in training materials that may be outdated.

## 2. LHDN e-Invoicing — MyInvois System

### 2.1 What is e-Invoicing?

**e-Invoicing (e-Invois)** is the electronic creation, transmission, and validation of invoices through a government-approved system. LHDN (Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri / Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia) has mandated a nationwide e-Invoice system called **MyInvois** as part of Malaysia's digital tax administration modernisation.

**Purpose of e-Invoicing:**
- Improve tax compliance and reduce tax leakage
- Reduce paperwork and automate invoice verification
- Provide LHDN with real-time transactional data
- Streamline audit processes

### 2.2 MyInvois System — Key Concepts

| Concept | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **MyInvois Portal** | Web-based portal at https://myinvois.hasil.gov.my for manual e-Invoice submission |
| **API Integration** | Businesses with accounting software can integrate directly with LHDN via API (Application Programming Interface) |
| **e-Invoice Validation** | Every e-Invoice is submitted to LHDN for real-time validation; LHDN returns a unique validation code |
| **Unique Identification Number (UIN)** | The LHDN-assigned validation code embedded in every valid e-Invoice |
| **XML / JSON Format** | e-Invoices must be in LHDN-specified XML or JSON format |
| **Self-Billed e-Invoice** | Issued by buyer on behalf of seller in specific circumstances (e.g., freelancer payments) |

### 2.3 Mandatory Rollout — Timeline

> **NOTA PENTING / IMPORTANT NOTE:** The LHDN e-Invoice mandatory rollout has been implemented in phases based on business annual turnover thresholds. These thresholds and timelines have been updated multiple times since the programme was announced. **Admin staff must consult the current LHDN MyInvois guidelines at https://www.hasil.gov.my for the applicable phase and threshold for their employer.** Do not act on threshold figures from any source other than the current official LHDN guideline, as they are subject to government revision.

The phased implementation approach means that larger businesses (higher annual turnover) were mandated first, with smaller SMEs following in later phases. The intent is that eventually all businesses above the minimum threshold will be required to issue e-Invoices through MyInvois.

**Key principle for admin staff:** Know which phase your employer falls under, and ensure the accounting software is configured for MyInvois API integration (or that staff know how to use the MyInvois portal for manual submission) before the mandatory date.

### 2.4 e-Invoice Workflow for Admin Staff

**Scenario A: Manual submission via MyInvois Portal (for businesses not yet API-integrated)**

```
Step 1: Create invoice in accounting software as usual
Step 2: Log in to MyInvois portal (myinvois.hasil.gov.my) using company credentials
Step 3: Enter invoice details into MyInvois portal (or upload XML file)
Step 4: Submit invoice — LHDN validates in real time
Step 5: Receive Unique Identification Number (UIN) upon successful validation
Step 6: Add UIN to the printed/digital invoice issued to customer
Step 7: Keep validation confirmation in records for audit
```

**Scenario B: API-integrated submission via accounting software**

```
Step 1: Create invoice in AutoCount / SQL Accounting as usual
Step 2: Accounting software automatically formats invoice in LHDN XML/JSON standard
Step 3: Software submits to LHDN API in real time
Step 4: LHDN validates and returns UIN to software
Step 5: UIN auto-populates on the invoice in the software
Step 6: Issue invoice to customer (UIN visible on document)
Step 7: Invoice stored in software with UIN for audit trail
```

### 2.5 e-Invoice Mandatory Fields

An LHDN e-Invoice must contain the following mandatory data fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Supplier's Tax Identification Number (TIN) | LHDN-issued TIN |
| Supplier's SST Registration Number | If SST-registered |
| Supplier's business registration number | SSM registration number |
| Buyer's TIN | If buyer has a TIN |
| Buyer's name and address | Recipient details |
| Invoice date and time | Timestamp of invoice creation |
| Invoice number | Unique sequential number |
| Description of goods/services | Line items |
| Unit price, quantity, subtotal | For each line item |
| Tax type and amount | SST or other applicable tax |
| Total amount | Grand total including tax |

### 2.6 Rejected e-Invoices

If an e-Invoice submission is rejected by the MyInvois system, the admin staff must:

1. Review the rejection error code from LHDN
2. Correct the identified field(s) in the accounting software
3. Resubmit the corrected e-Invoice
4. Record the correction in the invoice correction log

> **NOTA:** Do not issue a paper invoice as a substitute for a rejected e-Invoice if your business is already under the mandatory e-Invoice phase. The e-Invoice requirement is not satisfied by a paper document alone once the business is in the mandatory phase.

### 2.7 e-Invoice Cancellation and Credit Notes

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| Invoice issued with error, same day | Cancel via MyInvois within the allowed window (check LHDN guideline for current cancellation timeframe) |
| Invoice issued with error, after cancellation window | Issue e-Credit Note / e-Debit Note via MyInvois |
| Customer returns goods | Issue e-Credit Note via MyInvois |
| Price adjustment | Issue e-Debit Note or e-Credit Note as appropriate |

## 3. SSM Administrative Obligations

### 3.1 What is SSM?

**SSM (Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia / Companies Commission of Malaysia)** is the statutory body responsible for business registration, administration, and enforcement in Malaysia under the **Companies Act 2016** (for Sdn Bhd companies) and the **Registration of Businesses Act 1956** (for sole proprietorships and partnerships).

### 3.2 Types of Business Entities and Their SSM Requirements

| Entity Type | Registration | Annual Obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Sole Proprietorship (Milikan Tunggal) | Register business name under ROB | Renew business registration annually (check current fee with SSM) |
| Partnership (Perkongsian) | Register under ROB | Renew annually; update partner details on changes |
| Private Limited Company (Sdn Bhd) | Incorporate under Companies Act 2016 | File Annual Return; file audited accounts (for certain companies) |

### 3.3 Annual Return (Penyata Tahunan) for Sdn Bhd

A **Private Limited Company (Sdn Bhd)** must file an **Annual Return** with SSM within one month of the company's anniversary of incorporation.

**Annual Return contains:**
- Details of the company's directors and shareholders
- Registered office address
- Share capital structure
- Financial year end

**Admin staff role in Annual Return:**
- Maintain accurate and current records of directors, shareholders, and registered address
- Alert management when Annual Return filing deadline is approaching
- Coordinate with company secretary (Setiausaha Syarikat) who prepares and files the return
- Ensure any changes to directors or shareholders are reported to SSM within the statutory timeframe

> **NOTA:** The Annual Return for Sdn Bhd is filed through the **MyCOID (My Company Online Incorporation) portal** at https://mycoid.ssm.com.my or through a licensed Company Secretary. Admin staff typically do not file directly but must supply accurate information to the Company Secretary.

### 3.4 Business Particulars Filing

Any changes to the following must be notified to SSM within the statutory period:

| Change | Timeframe | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Change of registered business address | Check current SSM requirements | SSM counter or online |
| Change of business partners / directors | Check current SSM requirements | Through Company Secretary |
| Change of business name | Check current SSM requirements | SSM application |
| Change of business activities | Check current SSM requirements | SSM notification |
| Change of shareholders | Check current SSM requirements | Through Company Secretary |

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Failure to update SSM records when changes occur is an offence under the Companies Act 2016 and Registration of Businesses Act 1956. Penalties may apply. Admin staff must have a system to track and action SSM notification obligations whenever business particulars change.

## 4. Financial Document Retention Requirements

### 4.1 Legal Retention Periods

Malaysian legislation requires financial documents to be retained for specified minimum periods:

| Document Type | Minimum Retention Period | Legal Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Income Tax records (receipts, invoices, accounts) | 7 years from the relevant year of assessment | Income Tax Act 1967, Section 82A |
| SST records (tax invoices, SST returns) | 7 years | Sales Tax Act 2018 / Service Tax Act 2018 |
| Company documents (Sdn Bhd) | As long as the company exists, plus 7 years after dissolution | Companies Act 2016 |
| Employment records (payslips, contracts) | 6 years from the end of employment | Employment Act 1955 |

> **NOTA:** The 7-year rule is the most common benchmark. However, specific records may have different periods depending on the applicable legislation. Consult your company's accountant or legal advisor for a comprehensive retention schedule specific to the company's industry and entity type.

### 4.2 Storage and Disposal of Financial Documents

**Physical documents:**
- Store in locked, fireproof cabinets
- Label clearly with document type, year, and disposal date
- At end of retention period, shred physically — do not place in general waste

**Electronic documents:**
- Store in access-controlled folders on company server or approved cloud storage
- Maintain backup copies (at least one offsite or cloud backup)
- At end of retention period, securely delete — use certified deletion software
- Do not store financial documents on personal devices or personal cloud accounts

**PDPA 2010 interaction:** When the retention period expires, financial documents containing personal data (names, IC numbers, bank accounts) must be disposed of in a way that prevents reconstruction of the data. Secure shredding (physical) or certified deletion (electronic) satisfies this requirement.

## 5. Integration — SST, e-Invoice, and Accounting Software

### 5.1 How SST and e-Invoice Integrate in AutoCount / SQL Accounting

Modern versions of AutoCount and SQL Accounting are being updated to support MyInvois e-Invoice integration. The integration flow is:

1. Invoice created in accounting software with correct SST codes applied
2. Software formats invoice in LHDN-required XML/JSON
3. Invoice submitted to LHDN MyInvois API
4. UIN received and stored
5. Invoice sent to customer (digitally or printed with UIN visible)
6. At bi-monthly SST period close, SST report extracted from accounting software for SST-02 preparation

### 5.2 Admin Staff Compliance Checklist

| Task | Frequency | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| Verify all invoices issued carry correct SST code | Per invoice | AutoCount / SQL |
| Check SST registration number on all tax invoices | Per invoice | AutoCount / SQL |
| Submit e-Invoices to MyInvois | Per invoice (or batch, per schedule) | MyInvois portal or API |
| Extract SST transaction report | Bi-monthly | AutoCount / SQL |
| Support preparation of SST-02 return | Bi-monthly | MySST portal + accountant |
| File Annual Return (Sdn Bhd) | Annually | Company Secretary |
| Update SSM on business particulars changes | As changes occur | SSM / Company Secretary |
| Archive financial documents | Ongoing | Physical + electronic storage |
| Review retention schedule and dispose expired documents | Annually | Retention schedule register |
