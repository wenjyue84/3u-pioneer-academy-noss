## Work Activity Title

Set Up an AI-Assisted Expense Tracking and Invoice Processing System for a Simulated Malaysian SME

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, trainees will have:

- Processed 10 simulated supplier invoices using an AI/OCR-assisted workflow
- Categorised expenses correctly using a standard Chart of Accounts
- Reconciled processed expenses against a simulated bank statement extract
- Identified and flagged SST-chargeable items correctly
- Produced a categorised expense report and a discrepancy resolution log
- Applied PDPA-aligned data handling practices throughout the workflow

---

---

## Safety Precautions

- Do not enter real personal data (names, IC numbers, bank account numbers) of real individuals into any AI or OCR tool during this exercise
- All invoices used in this exercise are simulated — do not treat them as real financial records
- Do not share the simulated invoice set or bank statement outside the training room
- Save your work in the training folder provided; do not store training files on personal cloud accounts
- If using a free trial of an OCR tool, do not enter your personal credit card details — use the trainer-provided trial account credentials

---

## Simulated Business Scenario

**Business:** Cahaya Riang Trading Sdn Bhd (simulated)
**Business type:** General trading — office supplies and stationery wholesaler
**SST status:** SST-registered (Service Tax, for delivery services provided)
**Accounting period:** March 2026
**Your role:** Finance Admin Executive

You have received 10 supplier invoices for the month of March 2026. Your task is to process, categorise, and reconcile them using an AI-assisted workflow.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Receive and Review the Invoice Set (15 minutes)

Collect the **Simulated Invoice Set** from your trainer. This set contains 10 supplier invoices for Cahaya Riang Trading Sdn Bhd — March 2026.

Before processing, perform a **pre-processing check** on each invoice:

| Pre-Processing Check | For Each Invoice |
|---|---|
| Is the invoice addressed to Cahaya Riang Trading Sdn Bhd? | Y / N |
| Is the invoice dated within March 2026? | Y / N |
| Does the invoice have an invoice number? | Y / N |
| Is the total amount clearly stated? | Y / N |
| Does the invoice state whether SST is charged? | Y / N |

Record your pre-processing findings in **Answer Sheet — Section 1**.

Flag any invoice that fails a check and note the reason. Do not process flagged invoices until the issue is resolved (consult trainer).

### Step 3: Expense Categorisation in Spreadsheet (25 minutes)

| J | SST Chargeable? (Y/N) |

**Sub-step 3a:** For each of the 10 invoices, enter the data into the spreadsheet. Use the following expense category guide (based on Cahaya Riang Trading's Chart of Accounts):

| Supplier Type | Account Code | Category |
|---|---|---|
| Office rental / landlord | 6300 | Rental |
| TNB / electricity bill | 6400 | Utilities — Electricity |

**Sub-step 3b:** For each invoice, mark Column J (SST Chargeable?) as **Y** if the invoice shows an SST amount charged by the supplier. Mark **N** if no SST is charged.

**Sub-step 3c:** Add a SUM formula to calculate:
- Total Net Amount (Column G)
- Total SST Amount (Column H)
- Total Amount (Column I)

Record your categorisation results in **Answer Sheet — Section 3**.
