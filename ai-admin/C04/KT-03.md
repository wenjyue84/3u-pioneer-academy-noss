## Skema Jawapan / Marking Scheme

*(For trainer use only — not to be distributed to trainees)*

**Q1(a):** Sales Tax — single-stage tax on manufacturers and importers of taxable goods; example: a plastic goods manufacturer or an electronics importer. Service Tax — tax on providers of prescribed taxable services; example: an accounting firm, IT consultant, or restaurant above threshold. (3 marks each component: 1 for correct definition, 1 for correctly identifying the single-stage vs service-based nature, 1 for correct example type)

**Q1(b):** Any five of the eleven mandatory fields from KP-03: words "Tax Invoice"; unique tax invoice number; date of issue; supplier's SST Registration Number; supplier's name and address; customer's name and address; description of goods/services; quantity and unit price (for goods); net amount before SST; SST amount; total amount including SST. (1 mark each)

**Q1(c):** Admin executive should consult the current MySST portal (https://mysst.customs.gov.my) or seek confirmation from the company's accountant or SST agent. This is important because: SST thresholds have been amended since 2018; acting on an outdated threshold could mean registering late (penalty risk) or failing to register when required (compliance risk). (4 marks)

**Q2(a):** Bi-monthly (every 2 months). (2 marks)

**Q2(b):** Five steps from KP-03: (1) Gather all Tax Invoices issued during the taxable period; (2) Reconcile with accounting software SST transaction report; (3) Complete SST-02 form on MySST portal; (4) Pay any SST due by the filing deadline; (5) Keep all supporting documents for at least 7 years. (2 marks each)

**Q2(c):** She should check the current filing deadline on the MySST portal (https://mysst.customs.gov.my) or contact RMCD directly or confirm with the company's SST agent — not guess or rely on the training material, as deadlines may change. (3 marks)

**Q3(a):** Purpose: mandatory electronic creation, transmission, and validation of invoices through the LHDN-approved MyInvois system to modernise tax administration. Two reasons from KP-03: improve tax compliance and reduce tax leakage; reduce paperwork and automate invoice verification; provide LHDN with real-time transactional data; streamline audit processes. (2 marks for purpose; 1.5 marks each reason)

**Q3(b):** Six steps for manual portal submission from KP-03: (1) Create invoice in accounting software; (2) Log in to MyInvois portal; (3) Enter invoice details or upload XML; (4) Submit — LHDN validates; (5) Receive UIN upon successful validation; (6) Add UIN to invoice issued to customer / keep confirmation in records. (1 mark each)

**Q3(c):** Four steps from KP-03: (1) Review the rejection error code from LHDN; (2) Correct the identified field(s) in accounting software; (3) Resubmit the corrected e-Invoice; (4) Record the correction in the invoice correction log. (1 mark each)

**Q4(a):** e-Credit Note — adjusts downward the amount owed (reduces liability). Scenario: customer returns goods or overcharge occurred after cancellation window. e-Debit Note — adjusts upward the amount owed (increases liability). Scenario: undercharge discovered after invoice period, or additional charges to be added. (3 marks each: 1 definition, 1 correct direction, 1 valid scenario)

**Q4(b)(i):** Company Secretary (Setiausaha Syarikat) — a licensed professional who prepares and files on behalf of the company; admin provides information but does not file directly. (2 marks)

**Q4(b)(ii):** Any three of: details of directors; details of shareholders; registered office address; share capital structure; financial year end. (1 mark each)

**Q4(c):** Company must notify SSM of the change within the statutory timeframe prescribed in the Companies Act 2016 (trainee must state: SSM, through Company Secretary, within the prescribed statutory period — not guess a day count as it may have changed). Failure to notify is an offence under Companies Act 2016. (4 marks: 2 for correct body/method, 2 for consequence)

**Q5(a):** Two options: (1) Manual MyInvois portal — log in, enter or upload XML manually, receive UIN; suitable for lower invoice volumes; (2) API integration via accounting software — software auto-formats, submits, and receives UIN; suitable for high invoice volumes. Determining factor: invoice volume and whether the accounting software supports MyInvois API. High volume → invest in API; low volume → manual portal is sufficient. (8 marks: 3 marks per option description, 2 marks for factor identification)

**Q5(b):** Eight fields from KP-03 mandatory e-Invoice fields list: supplier's TIN; supplier's SST Registration Number; supplier's SSM registration number; buyer's TIN (if applicable); buyer's name and address; invoice date and time; invoice number; description of goods/services; unit price/quantity/subtotal; tax type and amount; total amount. (Accept any 8 from the list, 1 mark each)

**Q5(c):** Ikhwan should: (1) immediately assess whether the 5 invoices are within the e-Invoice cancellation window — if so, resubmit as e-Invoices; (2) if outside cancellation window, consult LHDN on the correct remediation procedure for missed e-Invoice submissions; (3) not issue paper invoices as a substitute; (4) implement a tracking checklist to prevent recurrence. (4 marks)

**Q6(a):** Income tax records — 7 years, Income Tax Act 1967 Section 82A; SST records — 7 years, Sales Tax Act 2018 / Service Tax Act 2018; Employment records — 6 years from end of employment, Employment Act 1955; Company documents (Sdn Bhd) — as long as company exists plus 7 years after dissolution, Companies Act 2016; Bank statements — 7 years (apply income tax rule as conservative benchmark). (2 marks per row: 1 for period, 1 for correct Act)

**Q6(b):** Correct procedure: shred physical documents using cross-cut shredder; use a certified document destruction service for bulk volumes; certify destruction in a Destruction Certificate (Sijil Pemusnahan). Standard paper waste disposal is insufficient because: financial documents contain personal data (names, amounts, bank details); documents placed in general waste can be retrieved and misused — this constitutes a breach of the Security principle under PDPA 2010. (5 marks)

**Q6(c):** Not straightforwardly advisable without caveats. Under PDPA 2010 Retention principle (Pengekalan), personal data should not be retained longer than necessary for its stated purpose. If records contain personal data (employee payroll, customer invoices with individual names), retaining beyond the statutory retention period without a documented business reason could constitute a PDPA violation. The correct approach: retain for the statutory minimum period; if extended retention is needed (e.g., ongoing litigation), document the specific reason; anonymise personal data where feasible before extended retention; review and dispose of records that have no further business or legal purpose. (5 marks)
