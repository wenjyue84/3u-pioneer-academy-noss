## SESI P-01 / SESSION P-01 — Pengenalan Alatan dan Persediaan OCR

**Aktiviti:** Tool Introduction and OCR Setup
**Masa:** 2 jam (120 minit)
**Kertas Kerja:** KK-01 (preparation)
**Aktiviti Kerja:** WA1 (Automate Expense Categorisation), WA3 (AI Invoice Processing)

### Objektif Sesi
- Trainees set up and familiarise themselves with the OCR tool (Dext trial or equivalent)
- Trainees understand the workflow from invoice capture to posting
- Trainer confirms all trainees have working access to required tools

### Rancangan Sesi

| Masa / Time | Aktiviti / Activity | Kaedah / Method |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | **Briefing and tool account setup** — Trainer distributes trainer-provided Dext trial credentials (or equivalent); trainees log in and verify access; trainer confirms Excel / Google Sheets available on all PCs | Individual setup |
| 0:20–0:50 | **Trainer demonstration — Dext full workflow** — Trainer uploads one sample invoice, demonstrates OCR extraction, shows validation panel, corrects a deliberate OCR error, and shows the export/push function | Live demonstration, projector |
| 0:50–1:20 | **Guided practice — trainees process 2 invoices** — Trainer distributes Simulated Invoice Set; trainees process Invoice 1 and Invoice 2 under trainer guidance; trainer circulates and corrects errors | Guided individual practice |
| 1:20–1:50 | **Expense categorisation setup** — Trainer distributes the Expense Categorisation Spreadsheet template; trainees open in Excel/Sheets; trainer walks through the column structure; trainees enter Invoice 1 and Invoice 2 data as practice | Guided individual practice |
| 1:50–2:00 | **Q&A and preparation** — Trainer answers tool setup questions; confirms all trainees are ready for P-02; sets expectation: P-02 is independent work with trainer support available | Q&A, briefing |

**Total: 2 hours**

## SESI P-02 / SESSION P-02 — Pemprosesan Invois, Pengkategorian, dan Penyesuaian Bank

**Aktiviti:** Invoice Processing, Categorisation, and Bank Reconciliation
**Masa:** 6 jam (360 minit)
**Kertas Kerja:** KK-01 Steps 1–4
**Aktiviti Kerja:** WA1, WA3, WA4

### Objektif Sesi
- Trainees independently process all 10 simulated invoices through OCR
- Trainees categorise all expenses in the spreadsheet
- Trainees reconcile against the bank statement extract

### Rancangan Sesi

| Masa / Time | Aktiviti / Activity | Kaedah / Method |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | **KK-01 Briefing** — Trainer distributes KK-01 worksheet; reads through the steps; confirms scenario (Cahaya Riang Trading); reminds trainees of PDPA precautions | Briefing |

| 1:15–2:45 | **Step 2 — OCR capture and verification** — Trainees upload all 10 invoices to Dext; verify extracted fields against physical invoices; correct errors; record findings in Answer Sheet Section 2 | Individual work; trainer circulates |
| 2:45–3:00 | **Break** | |
| 3:00–4:00 | **Step 3 — Expense categorisation** — Trainees enter all 10 invoices into the Expense Categorisation Spreadsheet; apply COA categories; mark SST-chargeable items; add SUM formulas | Individual work |
| 4:00–5:30 | **Step 4 — Bank statement reconciliation** — Trainer distributes Simulated Bank Statement Extract; trainees match bank transactions to invoices; identify unmatched items; determine probable reasons | Individual work |

## Catatan Penyampaian / Delivery Notes

1. **Simulated materials must be prepared by trainer in advance.** The Simulated Invoice Set (10 invoices), Simulated Bank Statement Extract, and Simulated Financial Dataset must be fabricated by the trainer using fictional company names, fictional amounts, and fictional invoice numbers. No real company data, no real individual names, no real registration numbers.
2. **OCR tool access.** If Dext trial access is unavailable, equivalent alternatives (Zoho Expense, Hubdoc free trial, or a trainer-built mock OCR worksheet) may be substituted. Document the substitution in the lesson plan record.
3. **Excel Copilot dependency.** If Microsoft 365 Copilot is not available on lab machines, ChatGPT with data analysis is the primary alternative. Both are covered in KK-02. Confirm tool availability before the practical sessions begin.
4. **PA scheduling.** If the class has more than 15 trainees, the PA Q&A in P-09 may need to be split across two sub-sessions. Ensure all trainees are assessed individually — group PA is not acceptable.
5. **30/70 split compliance.** Theory = 12 hours, Practical = 28 hours. Total = 40 hours. This satisfies the JPK 30% : 70% requirement for C04. Do not adjust the hour allocation.
