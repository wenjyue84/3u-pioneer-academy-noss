**B1(a):** Five steps from KP-01 Dext workflow: photograph/email invoice to Dext; OCR extracts key fields; review in Dext interface (confidence score); approve and push to AutoCount; original image stored in Dext. (1 mark each)

**B1(b):** Confidence score = AI's certainty that the extracted value is correct; below 90% = low confidence; admin staff should manually verify the field against the physical invoice and correct if needed before posting. (3 marks)

**B1(c):** Security (Keselamatan) principle; example: require password login for Dext; restrict access to authorised personnel only; or encrypt exported files. (1 mark principle, 1 mark example)

**B2(a):** Net Profit = RM 95,000 − RM 38,000 − RM 41,000 − RM 2,500 = RM 13,500; NPM = (13,500 ÷ 95,000) × 100 = 14.21% (accept 14.2%). (2 marks NP, 2 marks NPM)

**B2(b):** Pie chart — shows proportions of a whole; each expense category as a % of total operating expenses is best displayed as a pie chart for non-accountant management audience; easy comparison of relative size. (1 mark chart, 2 marks reason)

**B2(c):** What happened — net profit margin is 14.21% for February. Why — salaries represent the largest expense category at 60.9% of total operating expenses; if this is above budget, it may reflect overtime or new hires. Action — management should review the payroll budget and compare against February targets. Accept any valid three-part observation using the February data. (1 mark each part)

**B3(a):** Scenario A: Manual — log in to MyInvois portal, enter or upload XML, receive UIN manually; advantage: no API development cost, suitable for low invoice volume. Scenario B: API — accounting software auto-submits to LHDN, UIN auto-populates; advantage: fast and automated, reduces manual errors, suitable for high volume. (3 marks each: 2 for description, 1 for advantage)

**B3(b):** Any four from KP-03 mandatory fields list: supplier's TIN; supplier's SST Registration Number; supplier's SSM registration number; buyer's TIN; buyer's name and address; invoice date and time; invoice number; description; tax type and amount; total amount. (1 mark each)

**B4(a):** Three tasks from KP-03: export SST transaction report from accounting software; verify all tax invoices are recorded; provide data to accountant or SST agent; file SST-02 via MySST portal; save confirmation of filing. Plus explanation of the bi-monthly filing cycle and the general admin coordination role. (2 marks each task)

**B4(b):** SSM — through the Company Secretary. (1 mark each)

**B4(c):** Confirm current director details, shareholder details, registered office address, and share capital structure are correct and up to date. (2 marks for any two accurate items)

**C1(a):** Q1 Revenue = 120,000 + 105,000 + 135,000 = RM 360,000; Q1 Expenses = 88,000 + 92,000 + 79,500 = RM 259,500; Q1 Net Profit = RM 100,500; NPM = (100,500 ÷ 360,000) × 100 = 27.92% (accept 27.9%). (6 marks: 1 revenue, 1 expenses, 2 net profit, 2 NPM)

**C1(b):** January: 88,000 ÷ 120,000 = 73.3%; February: 92,000 ÷ 105,000 = 87.6%; March: 79,500 ÷ 135,000 = 58.9%. Highest: February. Implication: in February, the company spent RM 0.876 for every RM 1 of revenue earned — the tightest margin; may indicate a revenue dip combined with fixed cost burden; management should investigate whether February has seasonally lower demand or higher costs. (6 marks: 1 each ratio correct, 1 highest month, 3 implication)

**C1(c):** Any four from KP-02 data cleaning checklist: standardise date formats; remove blank rows; ensure amounts are numbers not text; remove PII before upload; check for duplicates; ensure no merged cells; separate SST from net amounts; confirm consistent currency. With why it matters for each. (1 mark each pair, 4 total)

**C1(d):** Steps: check whether invoices are within MyInvois cancellation window — if so, submit immediately; if outside window, consult LHDN for remediation procedure; implement a tracking checklist. Must NOT: issue paper invoices as a substitute for e-Invoices once in mandatory phase. (4 marks)

**C2(a):** Award 2 marks per row (1 per task): Accounting software — individual user login; monthly bank reconciliation; etc. SST — verify tax invoice fields per invoice; track SST-02 filing calendar (with note that deadline must be confirmed with RMCD). e-Invoice — submit every invoice to MyInvois; check UIN received for each. Document retention — maintain retention schedule; secure disposal at end of period. Reward any technically correct task that is consistent with KP-01, KP-02, or KP-03. Deduct if trainee states a specific SST rate or threshold without the required "verify with portal" caveat. (12 marks total)

**C2(b):** Full marks (8) require: identification of SST-02 module capability as key consideration (2); acknowledgement that AutoCount has a stronger native SST module (2); cloud trade-off addressed — Xero is cloud-first but SST module less comprehensive (2); clear recommendation for AutoCount with the condition that if international cloud access is critical, to choose Xero with a qualified Xero Adviser for SST workaround (2). Deduct marks if recommendation contradicts KP-01 content or if specific rates are stated without verification caveat.
