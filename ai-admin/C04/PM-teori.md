## SESI T-01 / SESSION T-01

**Tajuk:** AI dalam Perisian Perakaunan — AutoCount, SQL Accounting, dan Alatan Awan (KP-01)
**Masa:** 4 jam (240 minit)
**Kertas Penerangan:** KP-01

### Objektif Sesi

Pada akhir sesi ini, pelatih akan dapat:
1. Describe the features of AutoCount Accounting and SQL Accounting relevant to Malaysian SME compliance
2. Explain the OCR invoice capture workflow using Dext or Hubdoc
3. Apply expense categorisation rules using a Chart of Accounts
4. Explain bank reconciliation steps with AI-assisted matching
5. Identify the correct audit trail document exports
6. Apply PDPA 2010 Security and Retention principles to financial data handling

## Catatan Penyampaian / Delivery Notes

1. **No specific SST rate or MyInvois threshold should be stated as fact in class.** The trainer must consistently direct trainees to verify current figures with RMCD (mysst.customs.gov.my) and LHDN (myinvois.hasil.gov.my). Rates and thresholds change — stating a figure that has since been revised creates a compliance risk for trainees.
2. **PDPA 2010 must be integrated throughout** — not treated as a separate topic. Reference PDPA principles naturally when discussing financial data handling in T-01, AI tool selection in T-02, and document disposal in T-03.
3. **Differentiate admin staff role clearly** — trainees are not accountants. Their role is to process, categorise, record, and report accurately. The accountant, auditor, or company secretary handles the formal tax and legal submissions. Trainees should be clear about where their responsibility ends.
4. **Use Malaysian context throughout** — examples should reference Malaysian businesses, Malaysian banks (Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank), Malaysian SME scenarios. Avoid using global examples that have no local relevance.
5. **Assessment alignment** — all theory content tested in KA and KT must trace back to KP-01, KP-02, or KP-03 sections covered in these sessions. Do not introduce new concepts during KA/KT administration.
