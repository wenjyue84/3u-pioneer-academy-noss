## Occupational Prerequisites

- Minimum age 18 years old (Employment Act 1955 - Act 265)
- Medically fit (medical check-up by licensed Medical Officer / Occupational Health Doctor)
- Pass colour blind test (verified by licensed Medical Officer)
- Certified BEV Technician (SKM Level 3) or equivalent

## Training Prerequisites

One of the following certificates:
1. G452-002-2:2018 Light Vehicle - Repair Service
2. G452-001-2:2017 Commercial Vehicles - Repair Service
3. TP-300-2:2013 Automotive After Sales Servicing
4. P-115-2 Mekanik Kenderaan Motor
5. P-119-2 Mekanik Kenderaan Motor

Plus: Able to read and write (Bahasa Malaysia and/or English language)
