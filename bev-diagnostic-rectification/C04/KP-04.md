## Work Activity 4: Carry Out BEV Motorised Transaxle Servicing

## Objektif Pembelajaran
1. Explain motorised transaxle function and components
2. Describe transaxle diagnostic procedures
3. Explain the transaxle servicing procedure including lubricant management
4. Describe the procedure for faulty transaxle unit replacement

---

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** The motorised transaxle (e-axle) integrates the HV traction motor. Full HV de-energisation is required before any transaxle service. Heavy component — e-axle assembly weight is 50–120 kg. Use mechanical lifting aids and two-person handling at all times to prevent injury. Refer to Manufacturer's Service Manual for lifting attachment points and torque specifications.

## 1. BEV Motorised Transaxle

### 1.1 Function
The motorised transaxle (or e-axle) integrates the traction motor, single-speed reduction gear, and differential into a single compact unit. It transmits motor torque to the drive wheels. Most BEVs use a single-speed fixed-ratio reduction (no multi-speed gearbox needed because electric motors provide full torque from 0 RPM).

### 1.2 Components
| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| Reduction gear | Steps down motor RPM and increases torque (typical ratio 8:1 to 12:1) |
| Differential | Distributes torque equally between left and right drive wheels while allowing speed difference in turns |
| Output shafts / CV joints | Transmit torque to wheels |
| Bearings | Support gear shafts and differential |
| Housing/casing | Contains all components; holds lubricant |
| Lubricant | Gear oil (specific specification for each manufacturer) |
| Seals | Prevent lubricant leakage |

### 1.3 Transaxle Specifications (Typical)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Gear ratio | 8:1 to 12:1 (single speed) |
| Lubricant type | Synthetic gear oil (e.g., 75W-80, or manufacturer-specific EV fluid) |
| Lubricant volume | 0.8-2.0 litres |
| Service interval | 60,000-150,000 km (or per manufacturer) |
| Weight | 50-120 kg (complete e-axle with motor) |

## 2. Transaxle Diagnostic Procedures

### 2.1 Inspection
1. Diagnostic scan tool: read transaxle-related fault codes
2. Listen for abnormal noise: whining (gear wear), grinding (bearing failure), clicking (CV joint wear)
3. Check for lubricant leaks at seals, drain plug, housing
4. Check lubricant level and condition (if fill/check plug accessible)
5. Inspect CV joint boots for splits or damage

### 2.2 Lubricant Condition Assessment
- Colour: should be clean, manufacturer-specified colour
- Smell: burnt smell indicates overheating
- Metal particles: indicate gear or bearing wear (use magnetic drain plug inspection)
- Volume: should be within specification

---

## 3. Transaxle Lubricant Service

### 3.1 Lubricant Drainage
1. Raise vehicle on lift
2. Place collection container under transaxle drain plug
3. Remove drain plug; allow complete drainage
4. Inspect drain plug magnet for metal particles
5. Clean drain plug and replace seal washer if needed
6. Reinstall drain plug and torque to specification

### 3.2 Lubricant Refill
1. Remove fill/level plug (typically on the side of the transaxle housing)
2. Fill with manufacturer-specified lubricant type
3. Fill until lubricant reaches the bottom of the fill plug hole (level full)
4. Reinstall fill plug and torque to specification
5. Check for leaks

### 3.3 Waste Management
- Used transaxle lubricant is SW305 scheduled waste
- Collect in sealed containers; dispose via licensed contractor

---

## 4. Transaxle Replacement Procedure

1. Complete HV de-energisation (motor is integrated)
2. Drain transaxle lubricant
3. Drain motor coolant if integrated
4. Disconnect HV motor cables
5. Disconnect LV connectors (resolver, sensors)
6. Remove drive shafts from transaxle output
7. Support transaxle with transmission jack or hydraulic crane
8. Remove transaxle mounting bolts
9. Lower and remove the transaxle assembly
10. Install new transaxle in reverse order
11. Refill lubricant and coolant
12. Re-energise HV; verify with diagnostic tool
13. Road test — verify smooth drive, no noise, no vibration

---

## 5. Transaxle Failure Modes (Mod Kegagalan Transaksel)

| Component | Failure Mode | Symptom | Diagnosis |
|-----------|-------------|---------|-----------|
| Reduction gear | Tooth wear / pitting | Whining noise proportional to speed | Inspect drain plug metal particles; noise test |
| Differential | Bearing failure | Growling noise on turns | Road test — noise changes on left/right turns |
| Output shaft seal | Oil leak | Lubricant puddle at wheel; low level | Visual inspection; UV dye test |
| CV joint | Boot split / wear | Clicking on full-lock turns | Inspect boots; axle shaft end-play test |
| Lubricant | Contamination (water ingress) | Milky appearance; premature wear | Drain and inspect; check seals and breather |
