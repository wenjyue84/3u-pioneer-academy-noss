# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod WIM / WIM Code:** G452-010-3:2023-C04/KK(4/5)
**Kertas Warna / Paper Color:** BIRU / BLUE

## Work Activity 4: Carry Out BEV Motorised Transaxle Servicing

## Objektif Prestasi
1. Perform transaxle diagnostic inspection
2. Execute transaxle lubricant drainage and refill
3. Demonstrate faulty transaxle replacement procedure (if required)

## Equipment
Insulated tools, common tools, diagnostic scan tool, torque wrench, transmission jack or hydraulic crane, collection container, manufacturer-specified transaxle lubricant, all safety equipment and PPE, training BEV

## Langkah Keselamatan
> **AMARAN:** Transaxle may be integrated with HV motor (e-axle). HV de-energisation required before service. Transaxle assembly weighs 50-120 kg — use mechanical lifting aids.

## Prosedur Kerja

### Step 1: Transaxle Diagnosis
1.1 Connect diagnostic scan tool; read transaxle fault codes
1.2 Listen for abnormal noise (whining, grinding, clicking)
1.3 Check for lubricant leaks under vehicle
1.4 Inspect CV joint boots for damage

### Step 2: HV De-Energisation (if e-axle integrated — per C02/KK(2/6))

### Step 3: Lubricant Drainage
3.1 Raise vehicle on lift
3.2 Place collection container under transaxle drain plug
3.3 Remove drain plug; allow complete drainage
3.4 Inspect drain plug magnet for metal particles
3.5 Clean drain plug; replace seal washer
3.6 Reinstall drain plug and torque to specification
3.7 Label and contain spent lubricant (SW305)

### Step 4: Lubricant Refill
4.1 Remove fill/level plug (side of housing)
4.2 Fill with manufacturer-specified lubricant
4.3 Fill until lubricant reaches bottom of fill plug hole
4.4 Reinstall fill plug and torque to specification
4.5 Check for leaks

### Step 5: Transaxle Replacement (if required)
5.1 Drain lubricant and coolant (if integrated motor)
5.2 Disconnect HV motor cables
5.3 Disconnect LV connectors
5.4 Remove drive shafts
5.5 Support transaxle with transmission jack
5.6 Remove mounting bolts
5.7 Lower and remove transaxle
5.8 Install new transaxle (reverse)
5.9 Refill lubricant and coolant

### Step 6: Functionality Test
6.1 Re-energise HV system
6.2 Verify no fault codes
6.3 Road test: smooth drive, no abnormal noise, no vibration
