## Work Activity 3: Carry Out BEV Traction Motor Replacement

## Objektif Prestasi
1. Perform traction motor diagnostic inspection
2. Execute motor removal using lifting equipment
3. Demonstrate motor installation, coolant refill, and functionality test

## Equipment
Insulated tools, diagnostic scan tool, CAT III multimeter, hydraulic crane lifter (1 tonne), coolant refiller, replacement motor (training), manufacturer-specified coolant, all safety equipment and PPE, training BEV

## Langkah Keselamatan
> **AMARAN:** Traction motor weighs 30-80 kg. Use lifting equipment. Motor contains HV connections. Full HV de-energisation required.

## Prosedur Kerja
### Step 1: Motor Diagnosis
1.1 Read motor fault codes
1.2 Check resolver signals, temperature readings
1.3 Listen for abnormal noise (bearing wear)
1.4 Confirm motor requires replacement

### Step 2: HV De-Energisation (per C02/KK(2/6))

### Step 3: Coolant Drainage from motor cooling circuit

### Step 4: Motor Removal
4.1 Disconnect 3-phase HV cables
4.2 Disconnect resolver/encoder connector
4.3 Disconnect temperature sensor connectors
4.4 Disconnect coolant hoses
4.5 Support motor with hydraulic crane
4.6 Remove motor mounting bolts
4.7 Carefully remove motor from vehicle/transaxle

### Step 5: Motor Installation (reverse of removal)
### Step 6: Coolant Refill and Bleed
### Step 7: HV Re-Energisation and Functionality Test
7.1 No fault codes, smooth acceleration, no vibration, regen braking functional, temperature normal
