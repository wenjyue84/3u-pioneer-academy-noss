## TASK 1: Workplace Preparation (15 marks) — 25 min
1. Prepare drive train rectification workplace (5 marks)
2. Inspect lifting equipment and special service tools (5 marks)
3. Complete JHA with heavy-component and HV hazards (5 marks)

## TASK 2: Inverter or Motor Replacement (40 marks) — 80 min
*Assessor assigns ONE:*

**Option A — Inverter:**
1. Diagnostic inspection (8 marks)
2. HV de-energisation and coolant drainage (8 marks)
3. Inverter removal and installation (12 marks)
4. Coolant refill and functionality test (8 marks)
5. Documentation (4 marks)

**Option B — Traction Motor:**
Same mark distribution as Option A.

## TASK 3: Transaxle Servicing (20 marks) — 35 min
1. Transaxle diagnostic inspection (5 marks)
2. Lubricant drainage and drain plug inspection (5 marks)
3. Lubricant refill to correct level (5 marks)
4. Waste management and documentation (5 marks)

## TASK 4: Brake Control System (25 marks) — 40 min
1. Brake system diagnostic inspection (7 marks)
2. Component replacement demonstration (8 marks)
3. Brake system bleeding (5 marks)
4. System calibration and functionality test (5 marks)
