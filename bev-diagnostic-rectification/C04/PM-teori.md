## Competency Unit

| CU Code | G452-010-3:2023-C04 |
| CU Title | Perform BEV Electric Drive Train System Rectification |
| CU Weight | 20% |

## Learning Outcomes
1. Explain electric drive train workplace preparation, safety, and hazard identification
2. Describe inverter unit function, diagnosis, and replacement procedures
3. Explain traction motor function, types, diagnosis, and replacement
4. Describe motorised transaxle function, diagnosis, and servicing
5. Explain integrated brake control system function, diagnosis, and replacement

## Reference Documents

## 4-Step Method

### Langkah 1: PERSEDIAAN (15 min)
- Introduce CU: the drive train delivers power from battery to wheels
- Overview of 5 Work Activities

### Langkah 2: PENYAMPAIAN (200 min)
| 30 min | KP(1/5) | Workplace preparation |
| 45 min | KP(2/5) | Inverter unit: function, components, diagnosis, LV/HV circuits, coolant, replacement |
| 45 min | KP(3/5) | Traction motor: types (PSM/ASM), function, diagnosis, coolant, replacement |
| 40 min | KP(4/5) | Motorised transaxle: function, diagnosis, lubricant management, servicing |
| 40 min | KP(5/5) | Integrated brake control: regenerative braking, ABS/ESC integration, diagnosis |
