**A2.** PSM stands for: A. Power System Module B. Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor C. Pre-charge Safety Mechanism D. Parallel Serial Motor
**A3.** Typical BEV transaxle gear ratio: A. 1:1 B. 3:1 C. 8:1 to 12:1 D. 20:1
**A4.** Regenerative braking extends range by: A. 1-5% B. 15-30% C. 50-70% D. 80-100%
**A5.** The iBooster operates on: A. Engine vacuum B. 12V electrical C. HV electrical D. Compressed air
**A6.** IGBT stands for: A. Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistor B. Integrated Ground Bus Terminal C. Internal Gear Box Transmission D. Isolated Grid Battery Terminal
**A7.** BEV traction motor maximum RPM: A. 3,000 B. 6,000 C. 12,000 D. 20,000

## SECTION B: Short Answer (40 marks)
**B1.** (10 marks) Compare PSM, ASM, and SRM motor types: construction, advantages, common usage.
**B2.** (10 marks) Describe the inverter replacement procedure in sequential steps.
**B3.** (10 marks) Explain the integrated brake control system and how regenerative and friction braking are blended.
**B4.** (10 marks) Describe the transaxle lubricant service procedure including drainage, inspection, and refill.

## SECTION C: Structured (40 marks)
**C1.** (20 marks) A BEV has an inverter over-temperature fault. a) List possible causes (5m). b) Describe diagnostic procedure (8m). c) If inverter replacement is needed, describe the procedure (7m).
**C2.** (20 marks) A BEV has a complaint of unusual noise during driving. a) List possible drive train causes (5m). b) Describe systematic diagnosis (10m). c) If transaxle gear wear is confirmed, describe the servicing procedure (5m).

## MARKING SCHEME
**A:** A1:B A2:B A3:C A4:B A5:B A6:A A7:D A8:B A9:B A10:B
**B1:** PSM — permanent magnets, highest efficiency, most common. ASM — no magnets, robust, lower cost. SRM — simplest, no magnets, high torque ripple, emerging.
**B2:** HV de-energise, drain coolant, disconnect HV DC and AC cables, disconnect LV, disconnect coolant hoses, remove bolts, remove unit, install new, reconnect all, refill coolant, re-energise, test, road test.
**B3:** Regen: motor as generator, kinetic to electrical. Friction: hydraulic disc brakes. Blended by integrated brake control unit that distributes braking force between regen and friction based on driver demand, SOC, temperature, and speed.
**B4:** Raise vehicle, drain plug removal, catch lubricant, inspect magnetic plug, clean and replace seal washer, reinstall plug, fill through fill plug hole to level, torque fill plug, check for leaks, dispose spent lubricant as SW305.
