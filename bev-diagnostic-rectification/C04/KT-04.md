## Work Activity 4: BEV Motorised Transaxle Servicing | **Reference:** C04/KP(4/5)

### S1 — Short Answer: a) What is an e-axle and what three components does it integrate? b) Why do most BEVs use a single-speed fixed-ratio reduction?
### S2 — MCQ: What is the typical gear reduction ratio in a BEV transaxle? A. 1:1 B. 3:1 to 5:1 C. 8:1 to 12:1 D. 20:1 to 30:1
### S3 — Short Answer: Describe the transaxle lubricant condition assessment. What indicators suggest the lubricant needs replacement?
### S4 — T/F: a) BEV transaxle lubricant volume is typically 0.8-2.0 litres. b) The transaxle drain plug always has a magnetic insert. c) Multi-speed gearboxes are standard in BEVs.
### S5 — Short Answer: Describe the transaxle lubricant refill procedure. How do you know when the correct level is reached?
### S6 — MCQ: What noise indicates possible gear wear in a transaxle? A. Clicking B. Whining C. Popping D. Hissing

## Skema Jawapan
**S1:** a) E-axle integrates traction motor, single-speed reduction gear, and differential. b) Electric motors provide full torque from 0 RPM and operate efficiently across a wide RPM range, eliminating the need for multiple gears.
**S2:** C
**S3:** Check colour (should be clean), smell (burnt = overheating), metal particles on drain plug magnet (gear/bearing wear), volume within spec. Replace if: dark/burnt, metallic particles present, overdue per service interval.
**S4:** a) TRUE b) Not always, but commonly — mark as TRUE for learning purposes c) FALSE
**S5:** Remove fill/level plug. Fill until lubricant reaches bottom of fill plug hole. Reinstall fill plug and torque. Check for leaks.
**S6:** B
