## Work Activity 1: Carry Out Electric Drive Train System Rectification Workplace Preparation

## Objektif Prestasi
1. Demonstrate drive train rectification workplace preparation
2. Verify special service tools and lifting equipment
3. Complete JHA for drive train work including heavy-component handling hazards

## Equipment
All standard BEV safety equipment and PPE, plus: hydraulic crane lifter (1 tonne), oil seal installer, special service tools per manufacturer

## Prosedur Kerja
### Step 1: PPE and Safety Setup (same structure as C02/KK(1/6))
### Step 2: Heavy Equipment Verification
2.1 Inspect hydraulic crane lifter: capacity rating, hydraulic fluid level, boom condition
2.2 Inspect sling belts/chains: rated capacity, no damage
2.3 Verify oil seal installer availability and correct sizing
2.4 Verify transmission jack availability (if transaxle work planned)
### Step 3: Tool Inspection (insulated and common tools, special service tools)
### Step 4: JHA — include heavy-component handling, HV hazards, lubricant management
### Step 5: Toolbox Talk
