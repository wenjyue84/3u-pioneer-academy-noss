## Competency Unit: Perform BEV Electric Drive Train System Rectification
## Work Activity 1: Carry Out Electric Drive Train System Rectification Workplace Preparation

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** Electric drive train components (inverter, traction motor) operate at HV (up to 800V DC on some platforms). The inverter contains large DC bus capacitors that retain lethal charge up to 10 minutes after HV isolation. Full HV de-energisation with zero-energy verification is mandatory before work. Refer to Manufacturer's Service Manual for vehicle-specific isolation procedure.

## Objektif Pembelajaran
1. State regulations and certification requirements for drive train rectification
2. Explain drive train rectification safety, health, and environment requirements
3. List tools and equipment including special service tools for drive train work
4. Describe hazard identification and waste management procedures

---

## 1. BEV Rules and Regulations
All regulatory requirements from C01 and C02 apply. Drive train rectification involves HV components (inverter / unit kawalan motor, motor traksi / traction motor) and heavy mechanical components (transaxle / transaksel), requiring both electrical and mechanical safety competence.

## 2. Safety, Health, and Environment
- Anti-static flooring, full HV PPE requirements apply
- Drive train components are heavy — motor assemblies 30-80 kg, transaxle assemblies 50-120 kg
- Lifting equipment (hydraulic crane, transmission jack) required
- Transaxle lubricant is scheduled waste; coolant is SW305

## 3. Tools and Equipment

| Category | Items |
|----------|-------|
| Insulated tools | Class 0 (1000V) — full set |
| Common tools | Socket set, spanner set, torque wrench |
| Special service tools | Oil seal installer, bearing puller, alignment tools (per manufacturer) |
| Equipment | Digital multimeter (CAT III), Mega Ohm meter (CAT III), hydraulic crane lifter (1 tonne), oil seal installer, coolant refiller, vehicle lift |
| Safety | All standard BEV safety equipment |

## 4. Hazard Identification

| Hazard | Source | Control |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Electrical | Inverter, motor (HV) | Full HV de-energisation |
| Mechanical | Heavy components, rotating parts | Lifting equipment, lockout |
| Chemical | Coolant, transaxle lubricant | PPE, waste management |
| Ergonomic | Heavy lifting, awkward positions | Mechanical aids, two-person handling |

## 5. Waste Management
- Spent coolant: SW305
- Used transaxle lubricant: SW305
- Contaminated materials: SW410

## 6. Workplace Preparation
Equipment list, tools list, toolbox talk, assembly point/emergency escape route. Same structure as C02/KP(1/6) adapted for drive train work.
