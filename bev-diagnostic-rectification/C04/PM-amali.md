# PELAN MENGAJAR AMALI / PRACTICAL LESSON PLAN

**Kod WIM / WIM Code:** G452-010-3:2023-C04/PM(Amali)
**Kertas Warna / Paper Color:** KUNING / YELLOW

## Competency Unit: Perform BEV Electric Drive Train System Rectification (20%)

## Performance Outcomes
1. Demonstrate drive train rectification workplace preparation
2. Perform inverter and traction motor diagnostic inspection and replacement
3. Execute motorised transaxle servicing including lubricant management
4. Perform integrated brake control system diagnosis and replacement

## 4-Step Method

### Langkah 1: PERSEDIAAN (20 min)
### Langkah 2: PENYAMPAIAN (150 min)
| 20 min | KK(1/5) | Workplace preparation |
| 35 min | KK(2/5) | Inverter: diagnosis, HV disconnect, coolant drain, removal/install, test |
| 35 min | KK(3/5) | Motor: diagnosis, coolant drain, removal/install, test |
| 30 min | KK(4/5) | Transaxle: diagnosis, lubricant drain/refill, servicing |
| 30 min | KK(5/5) | Brake control: diagnosis, replacement, bleeding, calibration |

### Langkah 3: PENGGUNAAN (300 min)
| 30 min | KK(1/5) | Workplace preparation practice |
| 70 min | KK(2/5) | Inverter replacement practice |
| 70 min | KK(3/5) | Motor replacement practice |
| 60 min | KK(4/5) | Transaxle servicing practice |
| 70 min | KK(5/5) | Brake system practice |

### Langkah 4: PENGESAHAN (30 min)

## Total: approximately 500 minutes (8 hours 20 minutes)
