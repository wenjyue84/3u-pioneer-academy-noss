## Work Activity 3: BEV Traction Motor Replacement | **Reference:** C04/KP(3/5)

### S1 — Short Answer: Compare PSM and ASM motor types. List TWO advantages of each.
### S2 — MCQ: What is the typical maximum RPM of a BEV traction motor? A. 3,000 B. 6,000 C. 12,000 D. 20,000
### S3 — Short Answer: List FIVE components of a BEV traction motor and describe their function.
### S4 — T/F: a) A resolver measures rotor position. b) PSM motors use rare-earth permanent magnets. c) BEV traction motors are air-cooled.
### S5 — Short Answer: List FOUR common traction motor fault codes and their possible causes.
### S6 — MCQ: What is the typical weight range of a BEV traction motor? A. 5-10 kg B. 30-80 kg C. 200-400 kg D. 500-700 kg

## Skema Jawapan
**S1:** PSM: high efficiency, high power density. ASM: no permanent magnets (lower cost), more robust/tolerant of field weakening.
**S2:** D
**S3:** Stator (creates rotating magnetic field), rotor (rotates to produce torque), bearings (support shaft), resolver (position sensing), cooling jacket (temperature management). 
**S4:** a) TRUE b) TRUE c) FALSE — liquid-cooled
**S5:** Over-temperature (winding overheat), resolver fault (sensor failure), insulation fault (winding degradation), phase imbalance (unequal current), vibration (bearing wear).
**S6:** B
