## Work Activity 2: Carry Out BEV Inverter Unit Replacement

## Objektif Prestasi
1. Perform inverter diagnostic inspection and fault identification
2. Execute HV de-energisation, coolant drainage, and inverter removal
3. Demonstrate inverter installation, coolant refill, and functionality test

## Equipment
Insulated tools, diagnostic scan tool, CAT III multimeter, Mega Ohm meter, coolant refiller/suction tool, collection container, replacement inverter (training), manufacturer-specified coolant, all safety equipment and PPE, training BEV

## Langkah Keselamatan
> **AMARAN:** Inverter contains DC bus capacitors retaining lethal charge. MUST wait prescribed discharge time after de-energisation. NEVER open inverter housing.

## Prosedur Kerja
### Step 1: Inverter Diagnosis
1.1 Connect scan tool; read inverter fault codes
1.2 Check temperature readings, phase current balance
1.3 Confirm inverter requires replacement

### Step 2: HV De-Energisation (per C02/KK(2/6))

### Step 3: Coolant Drainage
3.1 Drain inverter/powertrain cooling circuit
3.2 Contain and label coolant (SW305)

### Step 4: Inverter Removal
4.1 Disconnect HV DC input cables
4.2 Disconnect 3-phase AC motor cables
4.3 Disconnect LV connectors
4.4 Disconnect coolant hoses
4.5 Remove mounting bolts
4.6 Remove inverter (10-25 kg — use proper technique)

### Step 5: Inverter Installation (reverse of removal)
### Step 6: Coolant Refill and Bleed
### Step 7: HV Re-Energisation and Functionality Test
7.1 No fault codes, phase current balanced, temperature normal, vehicle drives normally, regen braking functional
