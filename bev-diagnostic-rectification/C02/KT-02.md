## Skema Jawapan / Answer Scheme

**S1:** C — Orange
**S2:** a) V = I x R; V=Voltage(Volts), I=Current(Amps), R=Resistance(Ohms). I = V/R = 400/2 = 200A.
b) The 12V system powers the HV contactor coils. Disconnecting 12V first disables the contactor drive circuits, ensuring the HV contactors cannot close and re-energise the HV bus.
**S3:** Correct sequence (write number 1–6):
1. Turn vehicle to "READY" OFF mode
2. Store vehicle key in safety box
3. Disconnect 12V battery negative terminal
4. Disconnect interlock/service plug and store in safety box
5. Wait for capacitor discharge (5–10 minutes)
6. Verify zero-energy state with CAT III multimeter
**S4:** a) FALSE — never splice/repair HV cables; always replace complete assembly. b) TRUE c) FALSE — must wait 5-10 minutes for capacitor discharge. d) TRUE
**S5:** Set Mega Ohm meter to 500V DC test voltage. Connect one lead to cable conductor, other to cable shield/chassis. Initiate test, allow reading to stabilise. Read insulation resistance value. Pass criteria: >1 MOhm (or per manufacturer specification). Record reading.
**S6:** Diagram should show battery pack connected through main contactors and pre-charge circuit to HV bus, with inverter/motor, OBC, and DC-DC converter branching from the HV bus. Interlock plug shown at the battery pack.
