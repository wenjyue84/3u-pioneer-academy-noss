## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** OBC contains HV DC even when not charging. Full HV de-energisation required before any physical contact. Coolant is classified as scheduled waste (SW305) — follow waste management procedures strictly.

1. Full PPE must be worn at all times during this activity
2. HV work permit must be signed and posted before commencing
3. Minimum TWO qualified persons must be present throughout
4. Complete HV de-energisation and zero-energy verification (per KK(2/6) Steps 2-3) before touching any OBC connector
5. Do NOT open OBC coolant circuit until zero-energy state is confirmed
6. Use only manufacturer-specified dielectric coolant — standard automotive coolant is NOT acceptable
7. Collect all drained coolant in a sealed, labelled container (SW305) — do NOT pour down drain

## Prosedur Kerja / Work Procedure

### Step 1: OBC Diagnostic Inspection
1.1 Connect diagnostic scan tool; read OBC-specific fault codes
1.2 Record fault codes, freeze frame data, and fault descriptions
1.3 Verify fault confirms OBC as the failed component
1.4 Check AC charging inlet connector condition visually

### Step 2: HV De-Energisation
2.1 Perform complete HV de-energisation per KK(2/6) Steps 2-3
2.2 Verify zero-energy state with CAT III multimeter

### Step 3: Coolant Drainage
3.1 Place collection container under OBC coolant connections
3.2 Identify OBC coolant inlet and outlet hoses
3.3 Loosen hose clamps using insulated tools
3.4 Allow coolant to drain completely
3.5 Cap vehicle-side hoses to prevent contamination
3.6 Transfer coolant to sealed, labeled container (SW305)
3.7 Record volume of coolant drained

### Step 4: OBC Removal
4.1 Disconnect HV DC output cables from OBC
4.2 Disconnect AC input connection
4.3 Disconnect LV control/communication connectors
4.4 Disconnect coolant hoses (already drained)
4.5 Remove OBC mounting bolts
4.6 Support OBC weight and remove unit carefully (5-15 kg)
4.7 Place removed OBC on clean surface; label as defective

### Step 5: OBC Installation
5.1 Verify new OBC part number matches specification
5.2 Position new OBC and install mounting bolts — torque to specification
5.3 Reconnect coolant hoses and secure with clamps
5.4 Reconnect LV control/communication connectors
5.5 Reconnect AC input connection
5.6 Reconnect HV DC output cables — torque to specification

### Step 6: Coolant Refill
6.1 Prepare manufacturer-specified coolant (correct type and volume)
6.2 Fill through designated fill point
6.3 Bleed air from the cooling circuit per manufacturer procedure
6.4 Run cooling pump via diagnostic tool active test
6.5 Recheck level after circulation; top up if needed
6.6 Verify no coolant leaks at all connection points

### Step 7: HV Re-Energisation and Functionality Test
7.1 Perform HV re-energisation per KK(2/6) Step 8
7.2 Connect diagnostic scan tool — verify no OBC fault codes
7.3 Verify OBC communicates with BMS and VCU
7.4 Connect AC charging cable to vehicle
7.5 Verify charging initiates and operates at expected power
7.6 Monitor OBC temperature during charging
7.7 Disconnect charging cable; verify clean shutdown
7.8 Record all test results
