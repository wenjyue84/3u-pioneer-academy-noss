## Soalan / Questions

### Soalan 1 — Multiple Choice
What additional equipment is required for BMS rectification work compared to scheduled maintenance?
A. Standard automotive jack
B. Insulated mat (Class 0, 1000V) and Mega Ohm meter
C. Air compressor
D. Engine hoist

### Soalan 2 — True/False
a) An AED (Automated External Defibrillator) should be available within 30 seconds reach during BMS rectification work.
b) BMS rectification work can be performed by a single technician working alone.
c) Damaged lithium-ion battery modules can be stored in standard plastic bins.

### Soalan 3 — Short Answer
a) List FOUR (4) specific hazards unique to BMS rectification work that are not typically present in ICE vehicle maintenance.
b) Explain why a HV work permit must be signed before commencing BMS rectification work.

### Soalan 4 — Multiple Choice
What is the correct scheduled waste code for spent HV battery coolant?
A. SW110
B. SW305
C. SW409
D. SW410

### Soalan 5 — Short Answer
List the complete pre-work checklist items (minimum 8) that must be verified before commencing BMS rectification work.

### Soalan 6 — Matching (Padanan)

Match each PPE item in Column A with its correct specification or primary protection purpose in Column B.

## Skema Jawapan / Answer Scheme

### S1
**Answer: B** — Insulated mat (Class 0, 1000V) and Mega Ohm meter are additional requirements for BMS rectification work.

### S2
a) **TRUE** — An AED should be accessible within 30 seconds to respond to cardiac arrest from electrocution.

b) **FALSE** — BMS rectification work requires a minimum of two qualified persons present at all times. A lone technician cannot safely work on HV systems — the second person acts as safety observer and first responder.

c) **FALSE** — Damaged lithium-ion battery modules must be stored in fireproof, temperature-monitored containers. Standard plastic bins do not provide fire containment if thermal runaway occurs.

### S3
a) Any four from: electrocution risk (200–800V DC lethal voltage), arc flash (explosive energy release), chemical burns from battery electrolyte (LiPF₆), thermal runaway (cell temperatures >600°C), crush injury (battery pack 200–700 kg), toxic gas exposure (HF, CO), high-pressure gas venting.

b) The HV work permit ensures: supervisor authorisation is obtained; hazard assessment is documented; all control measures are confirmed as in place; and an auditable record is created for OSHA 1994 compliance. It also sets a clear "stop work" reference point if conditions change.

### S4
**Answer: B** — SW305 is the scheduled waste code for spent HV coolant/process water from maintenance activities.

### S5
Minimum 8 pre-work checklist items (any 8 from):
1. HV work permit signed and displayed
2. Two qualified BEV technicians present
3. PPE inspected, serviceable, and donned (gloves, glasses, FR coverall, boots)
4. Insulated tools inspected for damage and calibration validity
5. Insulated mat (Class 0) placed in work area
6. Barriers and HV warning signage deployed around work zone
7. Safety box positioned to store service plug/interlock
8. Class D fire extinguisher within reach
9. Electric rescue hook accessible
10. AED location confirmed and path clear
11. Spill kit available for electrolyte/coolant
12. JHA completed and signed
13. Toolbox talk conducted with all persons present

### S6
**Answer: 1-b, 2-a, 3-d, 4-c, 5-e**
