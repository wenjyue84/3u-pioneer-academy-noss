## Skema Jawapan / Answer Scheme

**S1:** B
**S2:** a) TRUE b) FALSE — minimum two qualified persons required c) FALSE — must be stored in fireproof, temperature-monitored containers
**S3:** a) Electrocution (200-800V DC), arc flash, chemical burns (battery electrolyte LiPF6), thermal runaway (>600 deg C), crush injury (battery 200-700 kg), toxic gas exposure (HF, CO). Any four.
b) The HV work permit ensures: supervisor authorisation, hazard assessment is documented, control measures are confirmed, and there is an auditable record of HV work activities for safety compliance.
**S4:** B
**S5:** HV work permit signed; two qualified persons present; PPE inspected and donned; insulated tools inspected; insulated mat placed; barriers and HV signage deployed; safety box positioned; fire extinguisher within reach; electric rescue hook accessible; AED location confirmed; spill kit available; JHA completed; toolbox talk conducted.
