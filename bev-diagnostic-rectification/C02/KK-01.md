## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** BMS rectification involves direct work on HV systems (200-800V DC). Fatal electrocution risk. Strict adherence to all HV safety procedures is mandatory.

1. Full PPE mandatory at all times
2. Minimum TWO qualified persons present
3. HV work permit signed before commencing
4. Insulated gloves MUST pass air inflation test
5. Remove ALL metallic jewellery

---

## Prosedur Kerja / Work Procedure

### Step 1: HV Work Permit
1.1 Obtain HV work permit form from supervisor
1.2 Complete all sections: task description, personnel, risk assessment
1.3 Obtain supervisor signature authorising HV work
1.4 Post the permit at the work bay entry point

### Step 2: PPE Inspection and Donning
2.1 Lay out all required PPE items on a clean surface
2.2 Perform air inflation test on insulated gloves: roll cuff towards fingers and check for air leaks; inspect for cuts, cracks, or discolouration
2.3 Inspect safety glasses for cracks, scratches, or damaged frames
2.4 Inspect dielectric footwear for damage to sole or upper
2.5 Don PPE in sequence: footwear, safety glasses, helmet, insulated gloves with leather over-gloves

### Step 3: HV Isolation Zone Setup
3.1 Deploy retractable barrier stanchions around the entire work bay perimeter
3.2 Post HV warning signage at ALL entry points (minimum 3 signs)
3.3 Place insulated mat (Class 0) at all work positions around the vehicle
3.4 Verify HV warning signage on vehicle is visible and legible
3.5 Post "HV WORK IN PROGRESS — AUTHORISED PERSONNEL ONLY" notice

### Step 4: Equipment Verification
4.1 Fire extinguisher (Class D): service date, pressure, pin
4.2 Electric rescue hook: mounted, accessible, condition verified
4.3 Safety box: positioned near work area for key and interlock storage
4.4 AED: confirm location within 30 seconds reach
4.5 Spill kit: verify contents — absorbent pads, neutralising agent, containment supplies

### Step 5: HV Tool Inspection
5.1 Inspect insulated tools: check IEC 60900 markings, insulation integrity
5.2 Inspect digital multimeter: verify CAT III-1000V rating, leads in good condition
5.3 Inspect Mega Ohm meter: verify CAT III-1000V rating, calibration date current
5.4 Inspect lifting equipment (if battery pack work planned): capacity rating, condition
5.5 Complete tool inventory form — list all tools verified as serviceable

### Step 6: JHA for BMS Rectification
6.1 Complete JHA with specific attention to: electrocution, arc flash, chemical exposure, crush injury, thermal runaway
6.2 Include emergency response plan for battery thermal event
6.3 Include spill containment plan
6.4 Brief all team members on JHA findings

### Step 7: Toolbox Talk
7.1 Conduct toolbox talk with emphasis on HV-specific hazards
7.2 Review emergency procedures: electrocution response (rescue hook, call 999, CPR/AED), thermal runaway response (evacuate, Class D extinguisher, call BOMBA)
7.3 Record attendance
