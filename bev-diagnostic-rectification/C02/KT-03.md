### Soalan 6 — True/False

## Skema Jawapan / Answer Scheme

**S1:** B
**S2:** a) 3.3 kW (single-phase slow), 7.4 kW (single-phase), 11 kW (three-phase), 22 kW (three-phase fast).
b) OBC dissipates 5-10% of charging power as heat. Without cooling, internal temperatures exceed safe limits, causing thermal shutdown, reduced efficiency, or permanent damage to power electronics.
**S3:** C
**S4:** 1-d, 2-c, 3-b, 4-a
**S5:** Place collection container under OBC coolant connections. Identify inlet and outlet hoses. Loosen hose clamps with insulated tools. Allow complete drainage. Cap vehicle-side hoses. Transfer coolant to sealed, labeled containers. Store in secondary containment area. Dispose via licensed waste contractor. Classified as SW305.
**S6:** a) FALSE — coolant must be drained before removal. b) TRUE c) TRUE
