## Skema Jawapan / Answer Scheme

**S1:** C
**S2:** a) Battery modules, battery cells, BMS master controller, Cell Management Units (CMUs), HV contactors, current sensor, cooling plates/channels, housing/enclosure, service plug/interlock, HV junction box. Any five.
b) NMC — high energy density; LFP — excellent thermal stability and long cycle life; NCA — high specific energy. (Other valid advantages accepted.)
**S3:** a) TRUE b) TRUE c) FALSE — should be +/- 0.05V d) TRUE e) FALSE — should be stored at <30% SOC
**S4:** 1. Complete HV disconnection and zero-energy verification. 2. Drain coolant. 3. Remove underbody shields. 4. Disconnect HV cables. 5. Disconnect LV control cables. 6. Disconnect coolant hoses. 7. Position lifting table under vehicle. 8. Raise lifting table to contact battery pack. 9. Remove mounting bolts. 10. Slowly lower lifting table. 11. Roll out from under vehicle. 12. Secure on transport hanger.
**S5:** B
**S6:** Use manufacturer-specified coolant type and volume. Fill through designated fill point. Bleed air per manufacturer procedure. Run cooling pump via diagnostic tool. Recheck level and top up. Verify no leaks. Air bleeding is important because air pockets in the cooling circuit reduce heat transfer efficiency, can cause localised hot spots, pump cavitation, and may lead to component overheating.
