## Competency Unit: Perform Battery Management System (BMS) Rectification

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Duration | 4 hours |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Passing Mark | 60% |
| Work Activities Covered | WA1-WA6 |

---

## Instructions to Candidates

1. This assessment consists of THREE (3) practical tasks
2. Follow ALL HV safety procedures — any critical safety violation results in automatic failure
3. Minimum TWO qualified persons present at all times during HV work

---

## TASK 1: BMS Workplace Preparation and HV De-Energisation (25 marks)
**Time: 45 minutes**

1. Prepare BMS rectification workplace with HV isolation zone (8 marks)
2. Complete JHA with HV-specific hazards (4 marks)
3. Perform complete HV de-energisation procedure in correct sequence (8 marks)
4. Perform zero-energy verification with 3 multimeter measurements (5 marks)

## TASK 2: HV Component Replacement (45 marks)
**Time: 120 minutes**

Choose ONE of the following (assessor assigns):

**Option A — HV Cable Replacement:**
1. Cable inspection and insulation resistance test (10 marks)
2. Cable replacement with correct routing (15 marks)
3. Interlock continuity verification (5 marks)
4. HV re-energisation and diagnostic verification (10 marks)
5. Documentation (5 marks)

**Option B — OBC/DC-DC Converter Replacement:**
1. Diagnostic inspection and fault identification (10 marks)
2. Coolant drainage and component removal (15 marks)
3. Component installation and coolant refill (10 marks)
4. Functionality test (5 marks)
5. Documentation (5 marks)

## TASK 3: Battery Pack Operations (30 marks)
**Time: 75 minutes**

1. Battery diagnostic inspection (SOC, SOH, cell voltages, insulation) (8 marks)
2. Battery module identification and voltage measurement (on training pack) (8 marks)
3. Demonstrate faulty module removal/installation procedure (on training pack) (8 marks)
4. Coolant management (drainage procedure and refill procedure) (6 marks)

---

## AUTOMATIC FAILURE CRITERIA
- Contact with energised HV component
- Failure to perform zero-energy verification before HV work
- Failure to wear insulated gloves during HV work
- Skipping the capacitor discharge waiting period
- Working alone on HV components (two-person rule violation)
