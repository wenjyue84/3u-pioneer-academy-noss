## Competency Unit: Perform Battery Management System (BMS) Rectification
## Work Activity 5: Carry Out BEV Traction Battery Pack Replacement

---

## Objektif Prestasi / Performance Objectives

1. Perform traction battery pack diagnostic inspection
2. Execute coolant drainage and battery pack removal using lifting equipment
3. Demonstrate battery module inspection and faulty module replacement
4. Perform battery pack reinstallation, coolant refill, and functionality test

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

- Insulated tool kit, diagnostic scan tool, CAT III multimeter, Mega Ohm meter
- HV battery lifting table (1 tonne), balancing bar (>700 kg), sling belts (>200 kg)
- Battery assembly transport hanger, hydraulic crane lifter (1 tonne)
- HV battery module balancer, insulation sheet
- Coolant refiller/suction tool, collection containers
- Replacement battery module (training), manufacturer-specified coolant
- All safety equipment and PPE as per KK(1/6)
- Training BEV — 1 unit

> **MINIMUM PERSONNEL: THREE (3) qualified persons required for battery pack removal.**

---

## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** The traction battery pack weighs 200-700 kg and contains lethal HV. Improper handling can cause crushing injuries, electrocution, or thermal runaway. This is the highest-risk activity in BEV servicing.

1. ALL standard HV PPE mandatory
2. Three qualified persons minimum
3. Lifting equipment must be rated for the battery pack weight
4. Never position any body part under the battery pack during lifting operations
5. Have Class D fire extinguisher and spill kit within immediate reach

---

## Prosedur Kerja / Work Procedure

### Step 1: Battery Pack Diagnostic Inspection
1.1 Connect diagnostic scan tool
1.2 Read SOC, SOH, cell voltages, temperatures
1.3 Identify faulty cells/modules from voltage deviations
1.4 Record insulation resistance value
1.5 Record all diagnostic data for comparison after replacement

### Step 2: HV De-Energisation
2.1 Perform complete HV de-energisation per KK(2/6)
2.2 Verify zero-energy state

### Step 3: Coolant Drainage
3.1 Locate battery pack coolant drain points (inlet/outlet)
3.2 Place collection containers at drain points
3.3 Open drain valves or disconnect lowest-point hoses
3.4 Allow complete drainage (10-20 minutes)
3.5 Cap all open connections
3.6 Properly contain and label drained coolant (SW305)
3.7 Record volume drained

### Step 4: Disconnect and Prepare for Removal
4.1 Raise vehicle on lift to working height
4.2 Remove underbody shields/covers
4.3 Disconnect ALL HV cables from battery pack (label each for reassembly)
4.4 Disconnect ALL LV control cables (BMS, CMU, sensors — label each)
4.5 Disconnect coolant hoses (already drained)
4.6 Identify all mounting bolt locations (typically 8-16 bolts)

### Step 5: Battery Pack Removal
5.1 Position HV battery lifting table directly under the battery pack
5.2 Raise lifting table until it contacts and supports the battery pack
5.3 Attach sling belts through designated lift points on the battery pack
5.4 Connect balancing bar to sling belts
5.5 Person 1: operates lifting table controls
5.6 Person 2 and 3: guide and monitor from each side
5.7 Remove ALL mounting bolts
5.8 Slowly lower the lifting table — battery pack separates from vehicle
5.9 **CAUTION:** Move slowly, watch for snagged cables or hoses
5.10 Lower completely; roll the lifting table out from under the vehicle
5.11 Transfer battery pack to transport hanger
5.12 Secure with straps

### Step 6: Battery Module Inspection (on removed pack)
6.1 Place battery pack on a stable work surface (transport hanger)
6.2 Remove battery pack housing cover bolts
6.3 Carefully remove the cover
6.4 Visual inspection: check all modules for swelling, discolouration, electrolyte leakage
6.5 Check module connectors and bus-bar connections
6.6 Measure individual module voltages with insulated multimeter
6.7 Compare readings to specification — identify faulty module(s)
6.8 Record all findings with photos

### Step 7: Faulty Module Replacement
7.1 Disconnect bus-bar connections from faulty module
7.2 Disconnect CMU connector from faulty module
7.3 Remove module mounting brackets
7.4 Carefully remove the faulty module (10-40 kg — use proper lifting technique)
7.5 Place faulty module in fireproof storage container
7.6 Install new module — verify correct orientation
7.7 Reconnect bus-bar connections — torque to specification
7.8 Reconnect CMU connector
7.9 Measure new module voltage — must match others within +/- 0.05V
7.10 Use HV battery module balancer if equalisation is needed
7.11 Close battery pack housing cover — torque all bolts

### Step 8: Battery Pack Reinstallation
8.1 Position battery pack on lifting table under vehicle
8.2 Person 1: operates lifting table; Persons 2-3: guide from sides
8.3 Raise carefully, aligning pack with mounting points
8.4 Install mounting bolts — torque to specification in manufacturer-prescribed sequence
8.5 Reconnect coolant hoses
8.6 Reconnect LV control cables (match labels from Step 4)
8.7 Reconnect HV cables (match labels from Step 4)
8.8 Reinstall underbody shields

### Step 9: Coolant Refill
9.1 Use manufacturer-specified coolant type
9.2 Fill to specified volume through designated fill point
9.3 Bleed air from system per manufacturer procedure
9.4 Run cooling pump via diagnostic tool
9.5 Recheck level; top up if needed
9.6 Verify no leaks at all connections

### Step 10: Functionality Test
10.1 Reconnect interlock/service plug
10.2 Reconnect 12V battery
10.3 Turn vehicle to "READY" mode
10.4 Diagnostic verification: cell voltages, insulation resistance, BMS communication, pre-charge sequence, cooling system, no fault codes
10.5 Record all results
10.6 Road test if applicable
