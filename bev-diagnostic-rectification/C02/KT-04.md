## Soalan / Questions

## Skema Jawapan / Answer Scheme

**S1:** a) The pre-charge system prevents inrush current surge into inverter capacitors. Without pre-charge, closing the main contactor would cause: welding of contactor contacts, damage to inverter capacitors, arc flash, BMS fault.
b) Pre-charge relay, pre-charge resistor, main positive contactor, main negative contactor, BMS control circuit.
**S2:** 4, 5, 7, 3, 1, 6, 2 → VCU request, negative contactor, pre-charge relay, capacitors charge, BMS monitors voltage, main positive contactor, pre-charge relay opens.
Correct: 5, 7, 3, 1, 4, 2, 6
**S3:** B
**S4:** a) FALSE — pre-charge only operates during initial energisation sequence, then the relay opens. b) TRUE c) FALSE — pre-charge resistors can be hot after recent operation.
**S5:** Connect scan tool, read pre-charge fault codes. After HV de-energisation: measure pre-charge resistor value (compare to spec, typically 50-200 ohm). Measure pre-charge relay coil resistance (compare to spec). Check wiring continuity between BMS and pre-charge components. Inspect connector condition.
