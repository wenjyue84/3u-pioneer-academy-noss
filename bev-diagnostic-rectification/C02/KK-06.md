## Competency Unit: Perform Battery Management System (BMS) Rectification
## Work Activity 6: Carry Out BEV DC-DC Converter Replacement

---

## Objektif Prestasi / Performance Objectives

1. Perform DC-DC converter diagnostic inspection and fault identification
2. Execute coolant drainage and DC-DC converter removal
3. Demonstrate DC-DC converter installation and coolant refill
4. Perform DC-DC converter functionality test

---

## Peralatan dan Bahan / Equipment and Materials

- Insulated tool kit, diagnostic scan tool, CAT III multimeter
- Coolant refiller/suction tool, collection container
- Replacement DC-DC converter (training part), manufacturer-specified coolant
- All safety equipment and PPE
- Training BEV — 1 unit

---

## Prosedur Kerja / Work Procedure

### Step 1: DC-DC Converter Diagnosis
1.1 Connect diagnostic scan tool; read DC-DC converter fault codes
1.2 Check DC-DC output voltage with vehicle in "READY" mode (should be 13.5-14.4V)
1.3 Monitor output stability; check 12V battery charging status
1.4 Confirm fault requires converter replacement

### Step 2: HV De-Energisation
2.1 Perform complete HV de-energisation per KK(2/6)
2.2 Verify zero-energy state

### Step 3: Coolant Drainage
3.1 Place collection container under DC-DC converter
3.2 Disconnect coolant hoses
3.3 Allow complete drainage
3.4 Cap open connections; contain and label coolant (SW305)

### Step 4: DC-DC Converter Removal
4.1 Disconnect LV output cables
4.2 Disconnect HV input cables (verify zero energy confirmed)
4.3 Disconnect LV control/communication connectors
4.4 Remove coolant hoses (already drained)
4.5 Remove mounting bolts
4.6 Remove the DC-DC converter unit (3-8 kg)

### Step 5: DC-DC Converter Installation
5.1 Verify new unit part number
5.2 Position new converter; install mounting bolts to specification
5.3 Reconnect coolant hoses with clamps
5.4 Reconnect LV control/communication connectors
5.5 Reconnect HV input cables to specification
5.6 Reconnect LV output cables

### Step 6: Coolant Refill
6.1 Fill with manufacturer-specified coolant to specified volume
6.2 Bleed air from circuit
6.3 Run cooling pump via diagnostic tool
6.4 Recheck level; verify no leaks

### Step 7: Functionality Test
7.1 Re-energise HV system
7.2 Turn vehicle to "READY" mode
7.3 Verify DC-DC output: 13.5-14.4V DC
7.4 Verify no fault codes; BMS/VCU communication established
7.5 Test LV systems under load (headlights, HVAC blower)
7.6 Verify 12V auxiliary battery is charging
7.7 Record all results
