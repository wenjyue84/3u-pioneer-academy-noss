## Competency Unit

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| CU Code | G452-010-3:2023-C02 |
| CU Title | Perform Battery Management System (BMS) Rectification |
| CU Weight | 30% |
| Theory Allocation | 30% of CU hours |

## Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this theory module, trainees will be able to:
1. Explain BMS rectification workplace preparation, including HV safety, tools, and hazard identification
2. Describe BEV low voltage and high voltage circuit fundamentals, including Ohm's Law, wiring standards, and disconnection procedures
3. Explain the function, inspection, diagnosis, and replacement procedures for HV cables, OBC, pre-charge control system, traction battery pack, and DC-DC converter
4. Describe coolant management procedures for HV components
5. Explain diagnostic fault code interpretation for BMS components

## Kaedah Pengajaran 4 Langkah / 4-Step Instructional Method

### Langkah 1: PERSEDIAAN (Preparation) — 20 minutes
- Introduce CU: BMS Rectification — the critical importance of battery system maintenance for BEV safety and performance
- Connect to prior knowledge from C01 (scheduled maintenance concepts)
- State learning outcomes; overview of the 6 Work Activities
- Emphasise: this CU involves HV work — highest-risk activities in BEV servicing

### Langkah 2: PENYAMPAIAN (Presentation) — 240 minutes

| Time | KP | Topic | Method |
|------|-----|-------|--------|
| 30 min | KP(1/6) | BMS workplace preparation: regulations, HV PPE, insulated tools, hazard identification, waste management | Lecture, group discussion |
| 45 min | KP(2/6) | HV cable system: LV/HV fundamentals, Ohm's Law, wiring colours, HV disconnection procedure, cable inspection, interlock system | Lecture with wiring diagrams, video demonstration |
| 45 min | KP(3/6) | OBC: definition, function, cooling system, inspection, LV/HV disconnection, coolant drainage, OBC diagnosis and replacement | Lecture with OBC cutaway model |
| 30 min | KP(4/6) | Pre-charge control system: function, components, inspection, diagnosis, relay pack replacement | Lecture with circuit diagrams |
| 50 min | KP(5/6) | Traction battery pack: safety, tools/lifting equipment, inspection, removal, module system, replacement, coolant management | Lecture with battery pack model, video |
| 40 min | KP(6/6) | DC-DC converter: function, inspection, diagnosis, coolant drainage, replacement | Lecture with converter display model |

### Langkah 3: PENGGUNAAN (Application) — 90 minutes

| Time | KT | Activity |
|------|-----|----------|
| 15 min | KT(1/6) | Workplace preparation questions |
| 15 min | KT(2/6) | HV cable system questions |
| 15 min | KT(3/6) | OBC questions |
| 15 min | KT(4/6) | Pre-charge control system questions |
| 15 min | KT(5/6) | Traction battery pack questions |
| 15 min | KT(6/6) | DC-DC converter questions |

### Langkah 4: PENGESAHAN (Confirmation) — 20 minutes
- Review answers, discuss common errors
- Q&A session
- Key summary: HV disconnection procedure is the foundation of ALL BMS rectification work
- Preview practical sessions

## Total Theory Session Duration: approximately 370 minutes (6 hours 10 minutes)
