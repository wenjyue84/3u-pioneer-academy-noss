# PELAN MENGAJAR AMALI / PRACTICAL LESSON PLAN

**Kod WIM / WIM Code:** G452-010-3:2023-C02/PM(Amali)
**Kertas Warna / Paper Color:** KUNING / YELLOW

## Competency Unit

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| CU Code | G452-010-3:2023-C02 |
| CU Title | Perform Battery Management System (BMS) Rectification |
| CU Weight | 30% |
| Practical Allocation | 70% of CU hours |

## Performance Outcomes

Upon completion, trainees will be able to:
1. Demonstrate BMS rectification workplace preparation with HV safety protocols
2. Perform HV circuit de-energisation and re-energisation procedures
3. Execute HV cable inspection, insulation resistance testing, and replacement
4. Perform OBC, pre-charge system, traction battery pack, and DC-DC converter servicing/replacement
5. Manage HV coolant drainage and refill procedures

## 4-Step Method

### Langkah 1: PERSEDIAAN (20 min)
- PPE inspection (emphasis on insulated gloves air test), safety briefing, HV work permit verification
- Emphasise: this is the highest-risk CU — strict adherence to HV procedures

### Langkah 2: PENYAMPAIAN (180 min)
| Time | KK | Demonstration |
|------|-----|---------------|
| 30 min | KK(1/6) | Workplace preparation for BMS rectification |
| 30 min | KK(2/6) | HV de-energisation, cable inspection, insulation resistance test, cable replacement |
| 30 min | KK(3/6) | OBC inspection, coolant drainage, OBC removal/installation |
| 20 min | KK(4/6) | Pre-charge system access, relay inspection, replacement |
| 40 min | KK(5/6) | Battery pack: coolant drain, lifting table operation, pack removal, module inspection |
| 30 min | KK(6/6) | DC-DC converter: inspection, removal, installation, coolant refill |

### Langkah 3: PENGGUNAAN (360 min)
| Time | KK | Activity |
|------|-----|----------|
| 40 min | KK(1/6) | Trainees prepare BMS workplace |
| 60 min | KK(2/6) | Trainees perform HV disconnection, cable inspection, insulation test |
| 60 min | KK(3/6) | Trainees perform OBC diagnosis, coolant drain, removal/install |
| 40 min | KK(4/6) | Trainees service pre-charge system |
| 90 min | KK(5/6) | Trainees perform battery pack removal, module inspection, reinstallation |
| 70 min | KK(6/6) | Trainees perform DC-DC converter replacement |

### Langkah 4: PENGESAHAN (40 min)
- Evaluate using KK assessment checklists
- Review critical safety points — HV disconnection sequence, zero-energy verification
- Clean-up and tool storage

## Total Practical Session Duration: approximately 600 minutes (10 hours)
