## Langkah Keselamatan / Safety Precautions

> **AMARAN / WARNING:** BEV high voltage systems operate between 200V-800V DC. Contact with energised HV components can cause fatal electrocution.

1. **Always wear full PPE** before entering the BEV work bay
2. Inspect all insulated gloves using the air inflation test before use
3. Do NOT proceed if any PPE item is damaged or missing
4. Ensure the electric rescue hook is accessible and all personnel know its location
5. Verify fire extinguisher (Class D) is within reach and within service date
6. Remove all metallic jewellery (rings, watches, bracelets)
7. Ensure at least TWO qualified persons are present for HV-related activities

## Prosedur Kerja / Work Procedure

### Step 1: PPE Inspection and Donning
1.1 Lay out all required PPE on a clean surface
1.2 Inspect insulated gloves: perform air inflation test by rolling the cuff towards fingers and checking for air leaks. Check for cuts, cracks, or discolouration
1.3 Inspect safety glasses for cracks, scratches, or damaged frames
1.4 Inspect dielectric footwear for damage to sole or upper
1.5 Don PPE in sequence: footwear, safety glasses, helmet, insulated gloves with leather over-gloves

### Step 2: Work Bay Preparation
2.1 Verify anti-static flooring is clean and free from water, oil, or debris
2.2 Check work bay lighting is adequate (minimum 500 lux)
2.3 Place retractable barrier stanchions around the designated work area
2.4 Position HV warning signage at all entry points to the work zone
2.5 Lay insulated mat (if available) at the primary work position beside the vehicle
2.6 Verify emergency stop button is functional (press test if applicable)

### Step 3: Equipment Verification
3.1 Check fire extinguisher (Class D): service date current, pressure gauge in green zone, safety pin in place
3.2 Locate electric rescue hook and verify it is mounted within arm's reach of the work area
3.3 Place safety box at the designated location near the work bay (for vehicle key and interlock plug storage)
3.4 Verify EV service bay lift is operational: test lift up/down with no vehicle

### Step 4: Tool Inspection and Layout
4.1 Lay out insulated tools on the tool cart/bench
4.2 Inspect each insulated tool: check for damage, verify IEC 60900 marking and 1000V rating is legible
4.3 Any tool with visible damage to insulation — remove from service and tag as defective
4.4 Lay out common tools separately from insulated tools
4.5 Power on diagnostic scan tool and verify battery/charge level is sufficient
4.6 Complete the tool inventory form — list all tools verified as serviceable

### Step 5: Job Hazard Analysis (JHA)
5.1 Obtain a blank JHA checklist form
5.2 Identify the task: "BEV Scheduled Maintenance"
5.3 Break the task into major steps (workplace prep, health check, maintenance)
5.4 For each step, identify potential hazards (electrical, chemical, mechanical, thermal, ergonomic)
5.5 Assess risk level for each hazard (Likelihood x Severity)
5.6 Record control measures for each identified hazard
5.7 Sign and date the completed JHA form
5.8 Brief all team members on the JHA findings

### Step 6: Waste Management Area Setup
6.1 Identify the designated temporary waste storage area
6.2 Verify waste containers are available: sealed drum (liquid waste), segregated bin (solid waste), fireproof container (battery waste)
6.3 Ensure containers are properly labeled with waste type and hazard symbols
6.4 Check secondary containment (bunding) is in place for liquid waste containers
6.5 Place waste management log near the waste area

### Step 7: Emergency Preparedness Verification
7.1 Identify the emergency assembly point and confirm all team members know its location
7.2 Walk the primary and secondary escape routes from the work bay
7.3 Verify first aid kit is stocked and accessible
7.4 Confirm emergency contact numbers are posted visibly (BOMBA: 999, DOSH hotline)

### Step 8: Toolbox Talk
8.1 Gather all team members at the work bay
8.2 Conduct the toolbox talk covering: task overview, identified hazards (from JHA), PPE requirements, emergency procedures, roles and responsibilities
8.3 Record attendance on the toolbox talk form
8.4 Invite questions and clarify any concerns

## Soalan Lisan / Oral Questions

The instructor may ask the following questions during or after the practical session:

1. Why is anti-static flooring important in a BEV work bay?
2. What would you do if your insulated glove fails the air inflation test?
3. Where is the emergency assembly point located?
4. Name three types of hazards you identified in the JHA.
5. What class of fire extinguisher is required for lithium battery fires?
