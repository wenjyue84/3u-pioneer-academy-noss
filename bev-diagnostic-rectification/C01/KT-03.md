## Skema Jawapan / Answer Scheme

### Soalan 1
**Answer: C** — 200V DC to 800V DC is the typical operating voltage range for BEV high voltage systems.

### Soalan 2
**Answer: B** — Engine oil change is not applicable to BEVs as they do not have an internal combustion engine.

### Soalan 3
a) **TRUE** — Regenerative braking reduces mechanical brake usage, resulting in brake pads lasting 80,000-100,000 km.

b) **FALSE** — The VIN consists of 17 characters (not 15).

c) **TRUE** — Negative terminal first prevents accidental short circuit through the vehicle chassis.

d) **TRUE** — The HV battery pack adds significant weight compared to a fuel tank and engine.

e) **FALSE** — Only manufacturer-specified coolant must be used. Some BEV coolants are non-conductive formulations critical for maintaining HV insulation. Using the wrong coolant can damage seals, reduce insulation resistance, and potentially create HV leakage paths.

### Soalan 4
a) Four VIN locations:
1. Dashboard (visible through windscreen, driver side)
2. Door jamb sticker (driver side B-pillar)
3. Vehicle registration card (VOC / Geran)
4. Diagnostic scan tool readout

b) BEV-specific coolant formulations are critical because:
- They are formulated to be non-conductive (low electrical conductivity) to maintain HV insulation
- They are compatible with the specific seal and gasket materials in HV components
- Using the wrong coolant type can damage seals, reduce insulation resistance between HV circuits and chassis, and create potentially fatal HV leakage paths
- Mixing coolant types can cause chemical reactions, corrosion, or gelation that blocks cooling passages

c) 12V auxiliary battery replacement procedure:
1. Locate the 12V battery (varies by model: front trunk, rear trunk, under rear seat)
2. Ensure vehicle is powered off and key removed from vehicle
3. Disconnect negative terminal first
4. Disconnect positive terminal
5. Remove battery hold-down clamp
6. Remove old battery and install new battery of same type and specification
7. Connect positive terminal first
8. Connect negative terminal
9. Verify 12V system function (dashboard lights, central locking)
10. Perform system reset / re-calibration via diagnostic scan tool if required

### Soalan 5
**Answer: B** — Incorrect lift point placement may damage the underbody battery housing. BEV battery packs are mounted on the vehicle underbody, and non-designated lift points can crush or puncture the battery casing.

### Soalan 6
a) Inspect (Periksa), Top-up (Tambah), Replace (Ganti)
b) 80,000 to 100,000 km
c) CAT III-1000V
d) reset
e) 3,000 kg

### Soalan 7
**ICE items absent in BEV (any four):**
1. Engine oil and oil filter change
2. Spark plug replacement
3. Timing belt/chain service
4. Transmission fluid change
5. Exhaust system inspection
6. Fuel filter replacement
7. Radiator coolant (ICE-type)

**BEV-unique items (any four):**
1. HV battery coolant service (non-conductive formulation)
2. HV system insulation resistance check
3. Battery SOH (State of Health) assessment
4. Software/firmware update check
5. Desiccant replacement in battery pack
6. Electric motor inspection (no oil/filter, but bearing and insulation checks)
7. HV cable routing and condition inspection
