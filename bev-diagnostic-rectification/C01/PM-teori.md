# PELAN MENGAJAR TEORI / THEORY LESSON PLAN

**Kertas Warna / Paper Color:** KUNING / YELLOW

## Competency Unit

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| CU Code | G452-010-3:2023-C01 |
| CU Title | Perform BEV Scheduled Maintenance |
| CU Weight | 15% |
| Theory Allocation | 30% of CU hours |

## Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this theory module, trainees will be able to:
1. Explain BEV rules, regulations, and certification requirements related to scheduled maintenance
2. Describe BEV workplace safety, health, and environment guidelines
3. List tools and equipment required for BEV scheduled maintenance
4. Explain BEV health check procedures, diagnostic fault codes, and reporting
5. Describe BEV maintenance activities including vehicle identification, checklists, and inspection procedures

## Kaedah Pengajaran 4 Langkah / 4-Step Instructional Method

### Langkah 1: PERSEDIAAN (Preparation) — 15 minutes

| Activity | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Sedia tempat latihan | Ensure classroom/training bay is set up with projector, whiteboard, and BEV components display |
| Pengenalan topik | Introduce the CU: "Perform BEV Scheduled Maintenance" — importance of scheduled maintenance in extending BEV lifespan and ensuring safety |
| Kaitkan pengalaman | Ask trainees about their experience with conventional vehicle maintenance; bridge to BEV differences (no engine oil, no spark plugs, but HV battery, electric motor) |
| Nyatakan objektif | State the 5 learning outcomes listed above |
| Semak pengetahuan sedia ada | Quick verbal check: What do trainees know about electric vehicles? Any prior BEV exposure? |

### Langkah 2: PENYAMPAIAN (Presentation) — 120 minutes

| Time | KP Reference | Topic | Method |
|------|-------------|-------|--------|
| 40 min | KP(1/3) | BEV rules & regulations, safety & health requirements, tools & equipment, hazard identification, waste management, workplace preparation procedures | Lecture with slides, group discussion on OSHA 1994 requirements |
| 40 min | KP(2/3) | BEV health check safety, tools & equipment, component active test procedures, diagnostic fault codes, health check reporting | Lecture with demonstration of diagnostic scan tool interface, case study of common fault codes |
| 40 min | KP(3/3) | BEV maintenance safety for HV systems, vehicle types & specifications (VIN), scheduled maintenance checklists, maintenance tools, inspection/top-up/replace activities | Lecture with real BEV maintenance checklist walkthrough, video of BEV scheduled maintenance |

### Langkah 3: PENGGUNAAN (Application) — 45 minutes

| Time | KT Reference | Activity | Method |
|------|-------------|----------|--------|
| 15 min | KT(1/3) | Trainees answer questions on regulations, safety, hazard identification, and waste management | Individual written exercise |
| 15 min | KT(2/3) | Trainees answer questions on health check procedures, diagnostic tools, and fault code interpretation | Individual written exercise |
| 15 min | KT(3/3) | Trainees answer questions on BEV identification, maintenance checklists, and inspection procedures | Individual written exercise |

### Langkah 4: PENGESAHAN (Confirmation) — 15 minutes

| Activity | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Semak jawapan | Review assignment answers with class; discuss common errors |
| Sesi soal jawab | Q&A session — clarify misconceptions |
| Rumusan | Summarise key points: (1) BEV maintenance differs from ICE — HV safety is paramount, (2) Proper workplace preparation prevents accidents, (3) Systematic health checks and checklists ensure thoroughness |
| Penilaian formatif | Verbal spot-check: randomly ask 3-5 trainees to explain one key concept |
| Penutup | Preview next session: Practical (PM-amali) — hands-on workplace preparation and BEV health check |
