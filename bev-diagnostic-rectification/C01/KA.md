**B2** (8 marks):
Procedure (4 marks): Roll cuff towards fingers to trap air (1m). Squeeze gently to check for leaks (1m). Visually inspect entire surface (1m). Check markings are legible (1m).
Replacement conditions (4 marks — 1 each): puncture, crack/cut, discolouration, exposure of inner insulation layer.

**B3** (8 marks):
JHA explanation (3 marks): systematic process to identify hazards, assess risks, and determine controls for each work step.
Five steps (5 marks — 1 each): (1) Identify task and break into steps, (2) Identify hazards at each step, (3) Assess risk level (likelihood x severity), (4) Determine control measures, (5) Document and communicate.

**B4** (8 marks — 2 each):
a) BMS ECU: Monitors battery SOC, SOH, cell voltages, temperatures; manages charge/discharge.
b) MCU: Controls motor performance, inverter operation, regenerative braking.
c) VCU: Coordinates overall vehicle systems; central controller.
d) Thermal Management ECU: Controls cooling system — pumps, valves, fan operation.

**B5** (8 marks):
ICE items absent in BEV (4 marks — 1 each): engine oil/filter, spark plugs, timing belt, transmission fluid.
BEV-unique items (4 marks — 1 each): HV battery coolant, HV insulation resistance check, battery SOH assessment, software update check.

### Section C

**C1** (12 marks):
a) Five hazards (5 marks — 1 each): electrical, chemical, mechanical, thermal, ergonomic.
b) Preparation steps (7 marks — 1 each, max 7): PPE inspection and donning, work bay cleaning, barrier placement, equipment verification (fire extinguisher, rescue hook), tool inspection, JHA completion, toolbox talk.

**C2** (14 marks):
a) Seven steps (7 marks — 1 each): receive vehicle/verify info, exterior walk-around, interior check, connect diagnostic tool, fault code reading, BEV system checks (battery/powertrain/thermal/charging), prepare report.
b) Interpretation (7 marks):
- SOH 94%: GOOD — within acceptable range for service age (1m)
- Cell #47 deviation: DEFECTIVE — 0.13V deviation exceeds +/- 0.05V tolerance. Indicates potential cell degradation. Recommend further investigation / battery module assessment (2m)
- Insulation resistance 850 ohm/V: GOOD — exceeds minimum 500 ohm/V threshold (1m)
- P0A1F fault code: Stored fault related to battery energy control. Cross-reference with TSB. May be related to cell imbalance. Recommend clearing and monitoring for recurrence (3m)

**C3** (14 marks):
a) Cabin air filter (4 marks): Open glove box, release stops, access filter housing, remove old filter noting airflow direction, install new filter with correct orientation, close housing, refit glove box.
b) Fluid checks (5 marks): Brake fluid — locate reservoir, check MIN/MAX, test moisture content, top up with correct spec if low (investigate leaks). HV coolant — visual check ONLY, do NOT open cap, report if below MIN.
c) Underbody inspection (5 marks): Battery pack housing (dents/cracks/leaks), HV cable routing, suspension components, brake pads/discs, tyre inner sidewalls. Critical: lift pads must be at manufacturer-specified points to avoid battery pack damage.
