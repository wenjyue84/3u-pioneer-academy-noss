# PENILAIAN PRESTASI / PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT

## Competency Unit: Perform BEV Scheduled Maintenance (G452-010-3:2023-C01)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Duration | 2 hours 30 minutes |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Passing Mark | 60% (60 marks) |
| Work Activities Covered | WA1, WA2, WA3 |

---

## Instructions to Candidates

1. This performance assessment consists of THREE (3) practical tasks
2. Complete ALL tasks within the allocated time
3. Follow all safety procedures — failure to comply with critical safety requirements results in automatic failure
4. You will be assessed on PROCESS, OUTPUT, ATTITUDE, SAFETY, and ENVIRONMENT
5. A training BEV and fully equipped work bay will be provided

---

## TASK 1: BEV Scheduled Maintenance Workplace Preparation (25 marks)
**Time Allocation: 30 minutes**
**Reference: WA1 — KK(1/3)**

Prepare a BEV maintenance work bay for a scheduled maintenance service. You must:

1. Inspect and don all required PPE correctly (5 marks)
2. Set up the work bay with barrier stanchions and HV signage (4 marks)
3. Verify all safety equipment: fire extinguisher (Class D), electric rescue hook, safety box (4 marks)
4. Inspect insulated tools and common tools; remove any defective items (4 marks)
5. Complete a Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) form for BEV scheduled maintenance (5 marks)
6. Conduct a toolbox talk covering task overview, hazards, PPE, and emergency procedures (3 marks)

---

## TASK 2: BEV Health Check Activities (40 marks)
**Time Allocation: 60 minutes**
**Reference: WA2 — KK(2/3)**

Perform a complete BEV health check on the provided training vehicle. You must:

1. Verify vehicle information: registration, VIN, mileage (4 marks)
2. Perform a systematic exterior walk-around inspection (6 marks)
3. Perform an interior systems check (4 marks)
4. Connect the diagnostic scan tool and perform a full system scan (5 marks)
5. Read and record all fault codes with freeze frame data (5 marks)
6. Perform BEV-specific system checks: battery SOC/SOH, cell voltages, insulation resistance, thermal management, charging status (8 marks)
7. Perform component active tests as supported by the diagnostic tool (3 marks)
8. Prepare and submit a complete BEV health check report with condition ratings and recommendations (5 marks)

---

## TASK 3: BEV Maintenance Activities (35 marks)
**Time Allocation: 60 minutes**
**Reference: WA3 — KK(3/3)**

Perform scheduled maintenance activities on the provided training vehicle. You must:

1. Identify the vehicle type and specification using VIN and documentation (4 marks)
2. Select and review the correct scheduled maintenance checklist for the vehicle (3 marks)
3. Replace the cabin air filter with correct airflow orientation (5 marks)
4. Check and top up fluid levels: windscreen washer, brake fluid (5 marks)
5. Perform a visual check of HV coolant level (without opening the reservoir) (3 marks)
6. Raise the vehicle on the lift using correct lift points and perform underbody inspection (6 marks)
7. Check the 12V auxiliary battery voltage and condition (3 marks)
8. Perform a post-maintenance diagnostic check and reset service interval (3 marks)
9. Complete the scheduled maintenance documentation (3 marks)
