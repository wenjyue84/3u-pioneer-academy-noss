## Skema Jawapan / Answer Scheme

### Soalan 1
**Answer: C** — Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 (Act 514)

### Soalan 2
**Answer: B** — Class 0 (1000V AC / 1500V DC)

### Soalan 3
a) **FALSE** — Anti-static flooring is mandatory in BEV workshops. Static discharge near HV battery components can cause electrical arcing, fire, or damage to ECUs.

b) **TRUE** — Class D fire extinguishers are designed for metal fires and are appropriate for lithium battery fires.

c) **FALSE** — PPE is the last line of defence. The hierarchy of controls places elimination first, followed by substitution, engineering controls, administrative controls, and PPE last.

d) **TRUE** — A JHA must be completed before commencing any BEV maintenance task using the company JHA checklist form.

### Soalan 4
a) Five types of hazards:
1. Electrical (HV shock, arc flash, electrocution)
2. Chemical (battery electrolyte, coolant, refrigerant)
3. Mechanical (crushing from battery weight, pinch points)
4. Thermal (battery thermal runaway, hot coolant)
5. Ergonomic (heavy lifting, repetitive motion)
(Also acceptable: Environmental — toxic gas release, noise)

b) Toolbox talk purpose: A briefing conducted before each shift or new task to communicate hazards and safety requirements.
Three topics: (any three of)
- Task overview and scope
- Identified hazards and control measures
- PPE requirements
- Emergency procedures
- Roles and responsibilities

c) Four regulatory authorities:
1. JPJ — Road Transport Act 1987 (Act 333)
2. DOSH — OSHA 1994 (Act 514)
3. Energy Commission — Electricity Supply Act 1990 (Act 447)
4. DOE — Environmental Quality Act 1974 (Act 127)

### Soalan 5
1-c, 2-a, 3-b, 4-e, 5-d

### Soalan 6
Inspection procedure: Perform an air inflation test by rolling the glove cuff towards the fingers to trap air, then squeeze gently to check for air leaks. Visually inspect for punctures, cracks, cuts, discolouration, or swelling. Conditions requiring immediate replacement: any puncture, crack, cut, discolouration, swelling, or exposure of the inner insulation layer.

### Soalan 7
Acceptable labeling (any reasonable arrangement that places items logically):
- A: Electric rescue hook (near vehicle, accessible)
- B: Safety box (for key/interlock storage)
- C: Fire extinguisher (Class D) (within 5m of work area)
- D: Barrier stanchion (to isolate work zone)
- E: Insulated mat (on work floor near vehicle)
