# PELAN MENGAJAR AMALI / PRACTICAL LESSON PLAN

**Kod WIM / WIM Code:** G452-010-3:2023-C05/PM(Amali)
**Kertas Warna / Paper Color:** KUNING / YELLOW

## Competency Unit: Perform BEV Workshop Supervisory Function (15%)

## Performance Outcomes
1. Demonstrate conducting a daily briefing session
2. Perform subordinate performance evaluation using standard forms
3. Execute training needs analysis and training coordination
4. Conduct workshop SHE inspection and prepare finding reports

## 4-Step Method

### Langkah 1: PERSEDIAAN (10 min)
### Langkah 2: PENYAMPAIAN (60 min)
| 15 min | KK(1/4) | Daily briefing demonstration |
| 15 min | KK(2/4) | Performance evaluation form completion |
| 15 min | KK(3/4) | Skills matrix and TNA form completion |
| 15 min | KK(4/4) | SHE inspection and finding report |

### Langkah 3: PENGGUNAAN (120 min)
| 30 min | KK(1/4) | Role-play: conduct daily briefing |
| 30 min | KK(2/4) | Complete performance evaluation forms |
| 30 min | KK(3/4) | Complete skills matrix and training plan |
| 30 min | KK(4/4) | Conduct SHE walk-through and prepare report |

### Langkah 4: PENGESAHAN (20 min)

## Total: approximately 210 minutes (3 hours 30 minutes)
