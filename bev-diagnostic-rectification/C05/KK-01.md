## Work Activity 1: Conduct BEV Daily Briefing

## Objektif Prestasi
1. Prepare daily briefing information and assignments
2. Execute a daily briefing session
3. Record daily briefing feedback and update the job control board

## Materials
- Briefing agenda template, attendance list form, job control board (whiteboard), sample work orders/job cards, marker pens

## Prosedur Kerja

### Step 1: Prepare Briefing
1.1 Review previous day's job status on job control board
1.2 Identify new vehicles received / scheduled for today
1.3 Allocate tasks to technicians based on skill level and workload
1.4 Prepare safety topic of the day
1.5 Gather any service information updates (TSBs, recalls)
1.6 Write briefing agenda

### Step 2: Execute Briefing
2.1 Gather all technicians at briefing area
2.2 Circulate attendance list — all sign
2.3 Deliver briefing following prepared agenda:
   - Safety reminder (HV safety topic)
   - Work progress update from yesterday
   - New assignments — distribute job cards
   - Service information updates
   - Housekeeping / 5S compliance
2.4 Invite questions and feedback
2.5 Address concerns raised
2.6 Close briefing — team disperses to assignments

### Step 3: Post-Briefing Actions
3.1 Update job control board with today's assignments
3.2 File attendance list and briefing notes
3.3 Document any safety concerns raised — initiate follow-up action
3.4 Monitor work progress during the day
