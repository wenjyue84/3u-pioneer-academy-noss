## Work Activity 3: Coordinate BEV Subordinate's Training

## Objektif Prestasi
1. Identify training needs using a skills chart
2. Perform Training Needs Analysis and complete TNA form
3. Arrange subordinate training including logistics coordination

## Materials
- Skills chart template, TNA form, training plan template, sample training provider catalogue, training notification template

## Prosedur Kerja

### Step 1: Training Needs Identification (TNI)
1.1 Review the skills chart for all subordinates
1.2 Identify skill gaps — competencies marked as "Not Competent"
1.3 Check for any new BEV service information requiring training
1.4 Review recent performance evaluations for training recommendations
1.5 List all identified training needs

### Step 2: Training Needs Analysis (TNA)
2.1 For each identified need, complete the TNA form:
   - Current competency level
   - Required competency level
   - Gap severity (critical / important / desirable)
   - Recommended training intervention type
2.2 Create a skills matrix chart showing all technicians vs. competencies
2.3 Prioritise training needs based on: safety impact, business impact, urgency

### Step 3: Arrange Training
3.1 Research available training programs (from sample catalogue):
   - Training provider and accreditation
   - Training location (in-house, external, online)
   - Duration and schedule
   - Cost
3.2 Prepare annual training plan:
   - Map each training need to a specific training program
   - Schedule by quarter
   - Assign budget
3.3 Prepare training notification for selected technician(s)
3.4 Arrange work coverage for training period

### Step 4: Post-Training Documentation and Effectiveness Evaluation
4.1 Collect training certificate/completion record and file in personnel record
4.2 Update skills chart — mark the newly trained competency as "Competent (Trained)"
4.3 Evaluate training effectiveness using Kirkpatrick levels:
   - **Level 1 (Reaction):** Collect trainee's completed post-training feedback form
   - **Level 2 (Learning):** Review post-training assessment results (test or practical)
   - **Level 3 (Behaviour):** Observe technician performing the trained skill on the job after 30 days; document observation result
4.4 Share key learnings from training during the next daily briefing
