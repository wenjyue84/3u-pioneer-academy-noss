# PELAN MENGAJAR TEORI / THEORY LESSON PLAN

**Kod WIM / WIM Code:** G452-010-3:2023-C05/PM(Teori)
**Kertas Warna / Paper Color:** KUNING / YELLOW

## Competency Unit

| CU Code | G452-010-3:2023-C05 |
| CU Title | Perform BEV Workshop Supervisory Function |
| CU Weight | 15% |

## Learning Outcomes
1. Explain BEV daily briefing communication techniques, content, and feedback reporting
2. Describe subordinate performance appraisal methods, evaluation criteria, and improvement techniques
3. Explain Training Needs Identification (TNI) and Training Needs Analysis (TNA) for BEV skills
4. Describe BEV workshop safety, health, and environment compliance monitoring and reporting

## Reference Documents
| KP 1-4 | C05/KP(1/4) through C05/KP(4/4) |
| KT 1-4 | C05/KT(1/4) through C05/KT(4/4) |

## 4-Step Method

### Langkah 1: PERSEDIAAN (15 min)
- Introduce CU: supervisory function ensures conducive work environment, disciplined workforce, quality and productivity
- This CU focuses on management, administration, and leadership skills

### Langkah 2: PENYAMPAIAN (120 min)
| 30 min | KP(1/4) | Daily briefing: communication, work activities, feedback |
| 30 min | KP(2/4) | Performance management: appraisal, evaluation, improvement, reporting |
| 30 min | KP(3/4) | Training coordination: TNI, TNA, training arrangement |
| 30 min | KP(4/4) | SHE compliance: checklists, measures, reporting |

### Langkah 3: PENGGUNAAN (60 min)
| 15 min each | KT(1/4) through KT(4/4) |

### Langkah 4: PENGESAHAN (15 min)

## Total: approximately 210 minutes (3 hours 30 minutes)
