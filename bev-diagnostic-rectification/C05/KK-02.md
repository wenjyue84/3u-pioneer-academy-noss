## Work Activity 2: Monitor BEV Subordinate's Performance

## Objektif Prestasi
1. Identify standard performance guidelines
2. Evaluate subordinate performance using company evaluation form
3. Identify improvement actions and complete a performance report

## Materials
- Performance evaluation form (company standard), counselling form, training nomination form, sample employee performance data

## Prosedur Kerja

### Step 1: Identify Performance Standards
1.1 Review the company performance evaluation criteria
1.2 Identify the evaluation dimensions: attitude, skills, knowledge, productivity
1.3 Determine the BEV-specific competency indicators relevant to each subordinate

### Step 2: Evaluate Performance
2.1 Using the provided sample employee data, complete the evaluation form:
   - Rate each dimension on the company scale
   - Provide specific comments and examples for each rating
   - Identify strengths and areas for improvement
   - Calculate overall performance score

### Step 3: Identify Improvement Actions
3.1 For areas rated below standard:
   - Determine appropriate improvement technique (OJT, refresher, counselling, mentoring)
   - If training needed: complete training nomination form
   - If counselling needed: prepare counselling session agenda
3.2 For top-performing areas: note recognition recommendations

### Step 4: Prepare Performance Report
4.1 Compile evaluation results for all subordinates (sample data)
4.2 Highlight top performers and those requiring intervention
4.3 List recommendations with supporting evidence
4.4 Prepare the report for submission to superior
