## Work Activity 4: Monitor BEV Workshop Safety, Health & Environment | **Reference:** C05/KP(4/4)

### S1 — Short Answer: List FOUR (4) employer responsibilities and FOUR (4) employee responsibilities under OSHA 1994 relevant to a BEV workshop.
### S2 — MCQ: Which emergency response is specific to BEV workshops? A. Fire drill B. Earthquake drill C. Electric shock response using rescue hook and AED D. Flood evacuation
### S3 — Short Answer: List FIVE (5) "Do's" and FIVE (5) "Don'ts" for BEV workshop safety.
### S4 — Short Answer: Describe the format and contents of a SHE finding report. Include at least SIX (6) sections.
### S5 — T/F: a) Fire extinguisher checks are done annually only. b) Near-miss reports are optional. c) The supervisor should conduct daily hazard walk-throughs.
### S6 — Short Answer: Explain the risk assessment process for a SHE finding. Include severity, likelihood, and risk rating.

### S7 — Case Study: During a routine SHE walk-through, Supervisor Lim finds: (i) A Class D fire extinguisher at Bay 3 has an expired service tag (last serviced 14 months ago). (ii) Two technicians are working on an HV battery pack without an insulated mat. (iii) The scheduled waste storage area has an unlabelled container. For EACH finding, state: (a) the risk level (Low / Medium / High / Extreme) and (b) the corrective action and urgency.

## Skema Jawapan
**S1:** Employer: provide safe workplace, train/certify staff, maintain safety equipment, provide PPE. Employee: follow safety procedures, wear PPE, report hazards, participate in training.
**S2:** C
**S3:** Do's: de-energise HV, verify zero energy, wear PPE, use insulated tools, maintain two-person rule. Don'ts: assume HV disconnected, wear jewellery, skip discharge waiting, use damaged PPE, work alone on HV.
**S4:** Date/time, location, finding description, photo evidence, risk assessment, recommendation, responsible person, target date, follow-up.
**S5:** a) FALSE — monthly or per company schedule b) FALSE — mandatory to report c) TRUE
**S6:** Severity (minor to critical), likelihood (unlikely to almost certain), risk rating (low to extreme) = severity x likelihood. Photo, date, recommendation included.
**S7:** (i) Expired extinguisher — High risk; Immediate: remove from service, arrange emergency re-servicing or replacement, do not start any HV work at Bay 3 until resolved. (ii) No insulated mat — Extreme risk; Immediate: STOP WORK immediately, deploy insulated mat before resuming, counsel technicians on procedure, document the near-miss. (iii) Unlabelled waste container — Medium risk; Same day: identify and label the container per waste classification, review waste handling procedure with staff, update waste inventory log.
