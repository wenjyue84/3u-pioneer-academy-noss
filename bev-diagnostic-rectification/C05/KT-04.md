## Skema Jawapan
**S1:** Employer: provide safe workplace, train/certify staff, maintain safety equipment, provide PPE. Employee: follow safety procedures, wear PPE, report hazards, participate in training.
**S2:** C
**S3:** Do's: de-energise HV, verify zero energy, wear PPE, use insulated tools, maintain two-person rule. Don'ts: assume HV disconnected, wear jewellery, skip discharge waiting, use damaged PPE, work alone on HV.
**S4:** Date/time, location, finding description, photo evidence, risk assessment, recommendation, responsible person, target date, follow-up.
**S5:** a) FALSE — monthly or per company schedule b) FALSE — mandatory to report c) TRUE
**S6:** Severity (minor to critical), likelihood (unlikely to almost certain), risk rating (low to extreme) = severity x likelihood. Photo, date, recommendation included.
