### S3 — Matching: 1. Evaluation form 2. Counselling form 3. Training nomination form (a. Recommend training for skill gaps b. Document counselling sessions c. Formal performance rating)
### S4 — Short Answer: Describe TWO (2) BEV-specific competency indicators a supervisor should assess.
### S5 — T/F: a) Performance appraisal is only done annually. b) Counselling is used for persistent performance problems. c) Performance reports are confidential documents.
### S6 — Short Answer: What FOUR (4) improvement techniques can a supervisor use for underperforming technicians?

### S7 — Case Study: Technician Siti consistently scores "Excellent" on HV de-energisation and diagnostics but is frequently late and has low attendance. Using the SMART framework, write ONE performance improvement target for Siti's punctuality issue. Include all five SMART elements.

## Skema Jawapan
**S1:** Attitude, skills, knowledge, productivity and efficiency.
**S2:** B
**S3:** 1-c, 2-b, 3-a
**S4:** Can technician independently perform HV de-energisation? Do they consistently follow safety procedures? Can they interpret BEV diagnostic fault codes? Any two.
**S5:** a) FALSE — formal annually/semi-annually, informal monthly b) TRUE c) TRUE
**S6:** On-the-Job Training (OJT), refresher course, counselling, mentoring.
**S7:** Example: S — Arrive at workshop by 7:45 AM (before 8:00 AM briefing start); M — Zero late arrivals in 30 consecutive working days; A — Achievable with a change in personal routine; R — Punctuality directly impacts team briefing effectiveness and customer vehicle readiness; T — Reviewed at one-month follow-up counselling session. (Accept any answer that correctly addresses all five SMART elements with a logical connection to punctuality/attendance.)
