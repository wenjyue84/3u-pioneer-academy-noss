## Work Activity 3: Coordinate BEV Subordinate's Training

## Objektif Pembelajaran
1. Explain Training Needs Identification (TNI) for BEV technicians
2. Describe Training Needs Analysis (TNA) methodology
3. Explain the process for arranging subordinate training

---

## 1. BEV Training Needs Identification (TNI) — Pengenalpastian Keperluan Latihan

### 1.1 BEV-Related Training Programs
The rapid evolution of BEV technology requires continuous training. Key training areas:
- HV safety certification and recertification
- New BEV model familiarisation
- Diagnostic tool software updates and new features
- Battery technology advances (solid-state, LFP, NMC variants)
- ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems) integration with BEV
- Environmental regulations updates

### 1.2 Skills Chart (Carta Kemahiran)
Maintain a skills chart for each technician mapping:
- Current certifications held (NOSS levels, manufacturer certifications)
- Skills demonstrated (HV work, diagnostics, specific component replacement)
- Skills gaps identified
- Training completed and planned

### 1.3 BEV Service Information Updates
Manufacturers regularly release:
- Technical Service Bulletins (TSBs) requiring new knowledge
- Safety recalls requiring specific training
- New model introduction training
- Updated service procedures

---

## 2. BEV Training Needs Analysis (TNA) — Analisis Keperluan Latihan

### 2.1 Skills Matrix Chart
A skills matrix maps each technician against required competencies:

| Competency | Tech A | Tech B | Tech C |
|-----------|--------|--------|--------|
| HV de-energisation | Y | Y | N |
| Battery pack removal | Y | N | N |
| OBC replacement | Y | Y | N |
| Inverter replacement | N | N | N |
| HVAC system service | Y | Y | Y |
| Brake system service | Y | Y | N |

Y = Competent, N = Training needed

### 2.2 TNA Form
The TNA form documents:
- Current competency level per technician
- Required competency level for their role
- Gap between current and required
- Priority ranking (critical vs. desirable)
- Recommended training intervention

### 2.3 Training Program Availability
Survey available training:
- JPK-accredited training centres (Pusat Bertauliah)
- Manufacturer training academies (e.g., Tesla, BYD, Mercedes training centres)
- Industry associations (MAA, PEKEMA)
- Online/e-learning platforms
- In-house training by senior staff

---

## 3. Arrange Subordinate's Training (Pengurusan Latihan)

### 3.1 Training Plan (TP)
Develop an annual training plan:
- List all required training for each technician
- Schedule by quarter to minimise operational disruption
- Budget allocation per training
- Approval process (supervisor, management)

### 3.2 Training Logistics
| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| Training location | Confirm venue (in-house, external centre, online) |
| Training provider | Confirm accredited provider, instructor qualifications |
| Training notification | Issue notification to trainee minimum 2 weeks in advance |
| Work coverage | Arrange work coverage during trainee's absence |
| Materials and facilities | Confirm training materials, tools, equipment availability |

### 3.3 Post-Training
- Collect training certificate/completion record
- Update skills chart and personnel file
- Evaluate training effectiveness (can the technician now perform the skill?)
- Share key learnings with team during daily briefing

### 3.4 Training Effectiveness Evaluation — Kirkpatrick Model
Evaluate training at four levels:

| Level | What to Evaluate | How |
|-------|-----------------|-----|
| **1 — Reaction (Reaksi)** | Did the trainee find the training relevant and satisfactory? | Post-training feedback form |
| **2 — Learning (Pembelajaran)** | Did the trainee acquire the knowledge/skill? | Post-training test or practical assessment |
| **3 — Behaviour (Tingkah Laku)** | Is the trainee applying new skills on the job? | Supervisor observation after 30/60 days |
| **4 — Results (Keputusan)** | Did training improve workshop performance? | Error rate, safety incidents, job completion time |

A supervisor should at minimum conduct Level 1 (feedback form) and Level 3 (observation) evaluations.
