# KERTAS KERJA / WORK SHEET

**Kod WIM / WIM Code:** G452-010-3:2023-C05/KK(4/4)
**Kertas Warna / Paper Color:** BIRU / BLUE

## Work Activity 4: Monitor BEV Workshop Safety, Health & Environment

## Objektif Prestasi
1. Interpret and complete a workshop SHE checklist
2. Conduct a workshop SHE walk-through inspection
3. Prepare a SHE finding report with risk assessment and recommendations

## Materials
- SHE checklist (company standard), SHE finding report template, risk assessment matrix, camera (for photo documentation), clipboard and pen

## Prosedur Kerja

### Step 1: Interpret SHE Checklist
1.1 Obtain the company SHE checklist
1.2 Review all checklist items — understand what each item requires
1.3 Identify BEV-specific items: HV signage, insulated mats, rescue hooks, fire extinguishers (Class D), PPE condition, waste management
1.4 Check the Chemical Register and verify Safety Data Sheets (SDS) are accessible at each point of chemical use (coolants, refrigerants, solvents)
1.5 Verify the emergency contact list is displayed and all numbers are current

### Step 2: Conduct SHE Walk-Through
2.1 Walk through the workshop systematically (bay by bay)
2.2 For each area, check every item on the SHE checklist:

**Safety items to check:**
- HV warning signage in place at all BEV bays
- Barrier stanchions available and in good condition
- Insulated mats present at HV work positions
- Electric rescue hooks accessible at each bay
- Fire extinguishers (Class D): in place, serviced, pressure OK
- First aid kits stocked
- Emergency exits clear and marked
- PPE storage area organised, all PPE serviceable
- Insulated tools stored properly, inspected recently

**Health items:**
- Ventilation adequate
- Lighting sufficient (500 lux at work surface)
- Ergonomic concerns (lifting equipment available)
- Noise levels acceptable
- AED accessible

**Environment items:**
- Waste containers properly labeled and segregated
- Secondary containment in place for liquid waste
- Waste inventory log up to date
- No spills or contamination on floor
- Scheduled waste storage area compliant

2.3 Take photos of any findings (positive or negative)
2.4 Record findings on the SHE checklist

### Step 3: Prepare SHE Finding Report
3.1 For each non-compliant finding:
   - Describe the finding
   - Attach photo evidence
   - Assess risk (severity x likelihood = risk rating)
   - Provide recommendation for corrective action
   - Assign responsible person
   - Set target completion date

3.2 For positive findings:
   - Note good practices observed
   - Recognise individuals or teams

### Step 4: Report Submission
4.1 Compile all findings into the SHE report
4.2 Prioritise: critical/high-risk findings first
4.3 Submit report to superior
4.4 Follow up on corrective actions by target dates
