## Work Activity 4: Monitor BEV Workshop Safety, Health & Environment

## Objektif Pembelajaran
1. Explain BEV workshop safety, health, and environment responsibilities
2. Describe BEV-specific safety, health, and environment awareness requirements
3. Explain the SHE finding report format and reporting process

---

## 1. BEV Safety, Health, and Environment (Keselamatan, Kesihatan dan Alam Sekitar)

### 1.1 Employer and Employee Responsibilities

**Employer responsibilities (under OSHA 1994):**
- Provide a safe workplace with appropriate HV safety equipment
- Ensure all BEV technicians are properly trained and certified
- Maintain safety equipment in serviceable condition
- Provide PPE at no cost to employees
- Establish and enforce safety procedures
- Report workplace accidents and dangerous occurrences

**Employee responsibilities:**
- Follow established safety procedures
- Wear prescribed PPE at all times
- Report hazards, incidents, and near-misses immediately
- Not tamper with or disable safety equipment
- Participate in safety training and briefings

### 1.2 Workplace Safety and Health
The supervisor must monitor:
- HV safety procedures are followed for every BEV job
- PPE is worn correctly and consistently
- Tools and equipment are in serviceable condition
- Work permits are obtained for HV work
- Two-person rule is observed for HV work
- Emergency equipment is accessible and maintained

### 1.3 Waste Management Monitoring
The supervisor ensures:
- Scheduled waste is properly classified, contained, and labeled
- Waste disposal records are maintained
- Licensed waste contractors are used
- DOE reporting requirements are met
- Waste storage areas are compliant

---

## 2. BEV Safety, Health, and Environment Awareness

### 2.1 Basics of Safety, Health, and Environment

**Proper BEV PPE:**
- Monitor that all technicians have complete, inspected PPE
- Verify insulated gloves are tested before each use
- Replace damaged PPE immediately

**Workplace Hazard Awareness:**
- Conduct regular hazard inspections (daily walk-through)
- Verify HV warning signage is in place
- Check that barriers are used during HV work
- Ensure fire extinguishers are accessible and current

**Accident Prevention:**
- Review and update SOPs regularly
- Investigate all incidents and near-misses
- Implement corrective actions from investigations
- Share lessons learned during daily briefings

### 2.2 Waste Disposal Procedure Monitoring
- Verify proper storage, segregation, tagging, and signage of waste
- Monitor waste inventory logs
- Ensure timely pickup by licensed contractors

### 2.3 Workshop Best Practice
**Do's:**
- Always de-energise HV before physical work
- Always verify zero-energy state with multimeter
- Always wear full PPE in BEV work bay
- Always use insulated tools for HV-adjacent work
- Always maintain two-person rule for HV work

**Don'ts:**
- Never assume HV is disconnected — always verify
- Never wear metallic jewellery during HV work
- Never skip the capacitor discharge waiting period
- Never use damaged PPE or tools
- Never work alone on HV systems

### 2.4 Emergency Response
The supervisor ensures all personnel are trained in:
- **First aid:** Basic first aid, CPR, AED operation
- **Firefighting:** Class D fire extinguisher operation for lithium battery fires
- **Electric shock response:** Use of rescue hook, disconnection procedures, CPR/AED
- **Thermal runaway response:** Evacuation procedure, Class D extinguisher, BOMBA notification

## 3. SHE Finding Report

### 3.1 Report Format and Contents

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Date and time | When the finding was observed |
| Location | Specific area within the workshop |
| Finding description | What was observed (hazard, non-compliance, positive observation) |
| Photo evidence | Photos of the finding |
| Risk assessment | Severity and likelihood rating |
| Recommendation | Corrective action proposed |
| Responsible person | Who should action the recommendation |
| Target date | When the corrective action should be completed |
| Follow-up | Verification that action was taken |

### 3.2 Risk Assessment Report
Each SHE finding should include a risk assessment:
- **Severity:** Minor / Moderate / Major / Critical
- **Likelihood:** Unlikely / Possible / Likely / Almost certain
- **Risk rating:** Low / Medium / High / Extreme
- **Photo documentation** of the finding
- **Date, time, and location** recorded
- **Recommendation** for corrective action

### 3.3 Reporting to Superior
- Compile SHE findings into a periodic report (weekly or monthly)
- Highlight critical and high-risk findings requiring immediate attention
- Track corrective action completion status
- Submit report to superior per company procedure

---

## 4. Additional SHE Supervisory Requirements

### 4.1 Chemical Health Risk Assessment (CHRA)
BEV workshops handle scheduled chemicals (coolants, refrigerants, cleaning solvents). The supervisor must:
- Maintain an updated Chemical Register (Daftar Bahan Kimia) listing all chemicals in use
- Ensure Safety Data Sheets (SDS/MSDS) are accessible at point of use
- Monitor compliance with CHRA requirements under Occupational Safety and Health (Use and Standards of Exposure of Chemicals Hazardous to Health) Regulations 2000
- Review CHRA report and implement recommended controls

### 4.2 Emergency Contact List
Display and maintain a current emergency contact list in the workshop including:
- BOMBA (Fire Department): 994
- Police: 999
- Ambulance: 999
- Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB) electrical emergency: 15454
- Company safety officer (with direct number)
- Nearest hospital (with address)

Review and update the emergency contact list quarterly.
