## Work Activity 2: Monitor BEV Subordinate's Performance

## Objektif Pembelajaran
1. Explain subordinate performance appraisal methods and formats
2. Describe performance evaluation criteria for BEV technicians
3. Explain performance improvement techniques
4. Describe the performance reporting process

---

## 1. Subordinate Performance Appraisal Method (Kaedah Penilaian Prestasi)

### 1.1 Appraisal Format
- **Standard evaluation form:** Company-specific form with rating scales (e.g., 1-5 or Excellent/Good/Satisfactory/Needs Improvement)
- **Contents:** Technical competency, work quality, productivity, safety compliance, teamwork, communication, initiative
- **Communication channels:** Hardcopy (filed in personnel records) and softcopy (digital HR system)

### 1.2 Appraisal Frequency
- Formal appraisal: Annually or semi-annually
- Informal check-ins: Monthly or as needed
- Probation review: At 3 months and 6 months for new employees

---

## 2. Performance Evaluation Criteria (Kriteria Penilaian Prestasi)

### 2.1 Evaluation Dimensions

| Dimension | What to Assess | BEV-Specific Examples |
|-----------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Attitude** | Work ethic, initiative, responsibility | Compliance with HV safety protocols without prompting |
| **Skills** | Technical competency, problem-solving | Ability to perform HV de-energisation correctly, diagnostic proficiency |
| **Knowledge** | Theoretical understanding | Understanding of BEV systems, fault code interpretation |
| **Productivity & Efficiency** | Job completion rate, time management | Number of vehicles serviced per week, time per standard job |

### 2.2 BEV-Specific Competency Assessment
- Can the technician independently perform HV de-energisation?
- Can they interpret BEV diagnostic fault codes?
- Do they consistently follow safety procedures?
- Can they handle BEV-specific tools and equipment properly?

---

## 3. Performance Improvement Techniques (Teknik Peningkatan Prestasi)

| Technique | Application | When to Use |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **On-the-Job Training (OJT)** | Supervised hands-on practice with experienced technician | Skill gaps in specific procedures |
| **Refresher Course** | Formal classroom/practical refresher on BEV systems | Knowledge decay, new technology introduction |
| **Counselling** | One-on-one discussion about performance issues | Attitude, behaviour, or persistent performance problems |
| **Mentoring** | Pairing with senior technician for ongoing guidance | New employees, career development |

### 3.2 SMART Performance Improvement Targets
When setting improvement goals with a subordinate, use the SMART framework:

| Element | Meaning | Example for BEV Technician |
|---------|---------|---------------------------|
| **S** — Specific | Clearly define the skill or behaviour to improve | "Complete HV de-energisation procedure without prompting" |
| **M** — Measurable | State a measurable standard | "Zero PPE non-compliance incidents in 30 days" |
| **A** — Achievable | Target must be realistic given resources | Achievable with 2 OJT sessions per week |
| **R** — Relevant | Linked to job role and company needs | Directly relevant to safe BEV workshop operations |
| **T** — Time-bound | Set a review date | Review at 6-week follow-up appraisal |

Document the SMART target in the counselling form and review at the agreed date.

---

## 4. Subordinate's Performance Report

### 4.1 Report Forms
| Form | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Evaluation form | Formal performance rating with scores and comments |
| Counselling form | Documentation of counselling sessions and agreed actions |
| Training nomination form | Recommendation for specific training to address skill gaps |

### 4.2 Reporting to Superior
- Compile performance data for all subordinates
- Highlight top performers and those requiring intervention
- Present recommendations: promotions, training, counselling, disciplinary action
- Report submitted to superior per company policy timeline
