## Work Activity 1: Conduct BEV Daily Briefing

## Objektif Pembelajaran
1. Explain small group communication techniques for BEV workshop briefings
2. Describe work activities briefing content and procedures
3. Explain the daily briefing feedback/response reporting process

---

## 1. BEV Small Group Communication (Komunikasi Kumpulan Kecil)

### 1.1 Communication Techniques
Effective daily briefing requires:
- **Clarity:** Use simple, direct language. Technical terms should be explained.
- **Brevity:** Keep briefings concise (15-20 minutes maximum).
- **Structure:** Follow a consistent format so team members know what to expect.
- **Engagement:** Ask questions, invite input, observe body language for understanding.
- **Visual aids:** Use the job control board, vehicle service boards, or whiteboards to display information.

### 1.2 Briefing Content Structure
A standard daily briefing should cover:
1. **Safety reminder** — safety topic of the day, recent incidents/near misses
2. **Work progress update** — status of vehicles currently in workshop
3. **New assignments** — vehicles arriving today, job allocation
4. **Service information updates** — manufacturer technical bulletins, new procedures
5. **Housekeeping** — 5S compliance, workshop cleanliness
6. **Questions and feedback** — open floor for team input

### 1.3 Procedure
1. Schedule briefing at start of shift (consistent time daily)
2. Gather all technicians at designated briefing area
3. Take attendance using attendance list
4. Deliver briefing using prepared notes/agenda
5. Invite questions and address concerns
6. Distribute job cards/work orders

### 1.4 Attendance List
- Maintain a signed attendance list for every briefing
- Records demonstrate compliance with safety communication requirements
- Attendance records are auditable documents for quality management systems

---

## 2. Work Activities Briefing (Taklimat Aktiviti Kerja)

### 2.1 Key Content Areas

**Safety Requirements:**
- HV safety reminders specific to today's jobs
- PPE requirements for each task
- Any special safety precautions (e.g., battery pack removal today — 3-person minimum)
- Review of any recent safety incidents or near-miss reports

**Environment Requirements:**
- Waste management reminders
- Coolant and refrigerant handling procedures
- Environmental compliance requirements

**Work Progress:**
- Status of vehicles in progress from previous shift/day
- Expected completion times
- Any technical challenges or parts on order

**New Assignments:**
- New vehicles received or scheduled today
- Task allocation to technicians (matching skill level to job complexity)
- Expected time for each job

**Service Information Updates:**
- New manufacturer Technical Service Bulletins (TSBs)
- Updated procedures or safety notices
- Recall information affecting vehicles in workshop

---

## 3. Daily Briefing Feedback/Response Report

### 3.1 Update Job Control Board
After the briefing:
- Update the workshop job control board (papan kawalan kerja) with:
  - Vehicle registration numbers and job status
  - Technician assignments
  - Expected completion times
  - Parts status (ordered, received, pending)

### 3.2 Follow Up Work Status
- During the day, the supervisor monitors progress against the briefing plan
- Identify delays early and reallocate resources if needed
- End-of-day: update job control board with actual status
- Prepare notes for the next day's briefing based on the day's outcomes

### 3.3 Report Documentation
- Briefing notes should be filed (hardcopy and/or softcopy)
- Any safety concerns raised during briefing must be documented and followed up
- Feedback from team members should be recorded and actioned

---

## 4. Leadership in the BEV Workshop (Kepimpinan di Bengkel)

### 4.1 Leadership Styles (Gaya Kepimpinan)

A workshop supervisor must adapt their leadership style to team maturity and task complexity:

| Style | Approach | When to Use |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Autocratic (Autokratik)** | Supervisor directs, team follows. Clear orders, little discussion. | Emergency situations, new/untrained technicians, strict safety compliance required |
| **Democratic (Demokratik)** | Team is consulted before decisions. Collaborative problem-solving. | Experienced team, complex diagnostic challenges, team buy-in needed |
| **Coaching (Bimbingan)** | Supervisor guides and develops skills through feedback and demonstration. | Technicians with potential, skill development focus, post-training reinforcement |
| **Situational (Situasional)** | Style varies based on the individual's skill and motivation for each specific task. | Most practical approach for a mixed-ability BEV workshop team |

### 4.2 Effective Supervisor Communication Model
Effective communication follows the Sender-Message-Channel-Receiver-Feedback model:
1. **Sender (Pengirim):** Supervisor prepares clear, accurate briefing content
2. **Message (Mesej):** Structured agenda covering safety, jobs, updates
3. **Channel (Saluran):** Face-to-face briefing, job board, written notices
4. **Receiver (Penerima):** Technicians — verify understanding through questions
5. **Feedback (Maklum Balas):** Technicians confirm, clarify, or raise concerns

Active listening by the supervisor is equally important — observe non-verbal cues and respond to hesitation.
