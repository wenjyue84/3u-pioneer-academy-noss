## Competency Unit: Perform BEV Workshop Supervisory Function

| Duration | 2 hours | Total Marks | 100 | Passing | 60% |

---

## TASK 1: Conduct BEV Daily Briefing (25 marks) — 25 min
1. Prepare a briefing agenda from provided scenario data (5 marks)
2. Conduct a daily briefing (role-play with classmates as technicians):
   - Deliver all required content items (8 marks)
   - Communication quality: clear, concise, structured (5 marks)
   - Handle questions from "technicians" appropriately (4 marks)
3. Update the job control board after briefing (3 marks)

## TASK 2: Monitor Subordinate Performance (25 marks) — 30 min
1. Complete a performance evaluation form for a sample technician using provided data (10 marks)
2. Identify performance gaps and recommend improvement actions (8 marks)
3. Complete a counselling form or training nomination form as appropriate (7 marks)

## TASK 3: Coordinate Subordinate Training (25 marks) — 30 min
1. Create a skills matrix chart for provided technician data (8 marks)
2. Complete a TNA form with gap analysis and prioritisation (9 marks)
3. Prepare a training plan with schedule, provider, and logistics (8 marks)

## TASK 4: Monitor Workshop SHE (25 marks) — 35 min
1. Conduct a SHE walk-through inspection of the workshop (8 marks)
2. Complete the SHE checklist identifying BEV-specific items (5 marks)
3. Prepare a SHE finding report with:
   - Photo documentation (or description) (3 marks)
   - Risk assessment for each finding (4 marks)
   - Recommendations and target dates (5 marks)
