## SECTION 2: SCENARIO-BASED ASSESSMENT

> Read each scenario carefully. Respond as a BEV workshop supervisor. All answers must reference CoCU C05 work activities.

### Part A: Briefing & Toolbox Talks — 30 marks (Scenarios 1–6, 5 marks each)

**Scenario 1** — Morning Briefing Preparation (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA1.1

You supervise five BEV technicians. Today's jobs: Tesla Model 3 (BMS fault BMS_F001), BYD Atto 3 (40,000km service), Ioniq 5 (HV battery visual inspection), Proton e.MAS 7 (coolant top-up). Technician Azlan is on emergency leave. Remaining four have varied competency levels. A near-miss occurred last week from skipping the HV pre-work isolation checklist. Describe how you prepare and conduct today's morning briefing.

*Sample Answer:* Review job cards; match jobs to technician competency (most experienced → BMS fault). Prepare attendance list. Brief in sequence: attendance → reference last week's near-miss and reinforce mandatory HV isolation checklist → assign jobs with technician names → progress updates → Q&A. Update job control board before work begins. File briefing notes per CoCU C05 WA1.3. Per WA1.1, briefing must be organised per job briefing policy and procedures. = 5 marks

**Scenario 2** — Toolbox Talk: HV Isolation Procedure Update (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA1.2

The OEM distributor issued a Service Information update changing the HV de-energisation sequence for BYD Atto 3. The updated procedure adds a mandatory 10-minute wait after manual service disconnect before probing. Your team is unaware and three Atto 3 units are booked next week. Conduct a toolbox talk to communicate this change and ensure all technicians understand the revised requirement.

*Sample Answer:* Gather team; distribute printed SI update. Demonstrate old vs new sequence side by side. Emphasise: 10-minute wait is mandatory per updated OEM procedure — no exceptions. Verify understanding via Q&A; require each technician to repeat back key step. Record attendance and SI reference number. Update job cards for next week's Atto 3 bookings. Per CoCU C05 WA1.2, briefing must highlight safety requirements and service information updates. = 5 marks

**Scenario 3** — Managing Conflict During Briefing (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA1.2

During the morning briefing, senior technician Rajan disputes his job assignment in front of the whole team, raising his voice and claiming a colleague should handle the BMS rectification job instead. The delay is disrupting the briefing schedule and affecting team morale. How do you manage this situation professionally while maintaining the briefing and team discipline?

*Sample Answer:* Acknowledge Rajan's concern calmly; defer detailed discussion to after briefing to maintain schedule. Continue briefing professionally. Post-team dispersal, speak to Rajan privately: explain assignment rationale using CoCU C05 WA2.2 competency evaluation as basis. If a legitimate skills gap exists, arrange a mentored OJT pairing. Document the incident on the evaluation form and report to superior per WA2.4 as required. = 5 marks

**Scenario 4** — Job Control Board Accuracy (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA1.3

After the morning briefing, three vehicles are active: Ioniq 5 in Bay 2 (HV isolation phase), BYD Atto 3 in Bay 4 (wheel-off), Proton e.MAS 7 in Bay 1 (coolant refill in progress). Two vehicles are queued and one from yesterday is awaiting parts. You must update the physical job control board to reflect current status accurately. Describe what the board must show.

*Sample Answer:* Each bay: vehicle registration, job description, responsible technician, current status (IN PROGRESS / AWAITING PARTS / QUEUED), expected completion time. Flag Bay 2 with HV warning symbol during isolation phase. Note parts order reference for the outstanding vehicle. Record update timestamp. Per CoCU C05 WA1.3, board must support follow-up of work status and form part of the daily briefing feedback/response report documentation. = 5 marks

**Scenario 5** — Emergency Toolbox Talk After Near-Miss (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA1.2

A near-miss occurred: Technician Hafiz connected a charger to an Atto 3 without completing the HV pre-work checklist — the vehicle was still in READY mode. No injury occurred but an arc flash was possible. You must conduct an unscheduled emergency toolbox talk within 30 minutes. Describe your approach and the key content required.

*Sample Answer:* Stop all HV-related work immediately across all bays. Gather team in designated safety zone away from vehicles. Describe the near-miss factually without publicly blaming the individual. Review mandatory HV pre-work checklist step by step; verify READY mode warning lights off before any connection. Record attendance and incident summary. Submit near-miss report per OSHA 1994 (Act 514) to superior. Per CoCU C05 WA1.2, briefing must highlight safety requirements. = 5 marks

**Scenario 6** — Bilingual Briefing for Mixed-Language Team (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA1.1

Your workshop recently onboarded two technicians more comfortable in Bahasa Malaysia, while two experienced technicians prefer English. One technician has only basic Malay. Critical safety content must reach all team members without confusion. How do you structure a bilingual briefing to ensure safety information is effectively communicated to a linguistically diverse team?

*Sample Answer:* Use bilingual slides and handouts (EN + BM). Deliver all safety-critical points in both languages: "Amaran / Warning — verify HV isolation before probing" with visual diagram. Assign a buddy interpreter for the technician with limited language ability. Use colour-coded visual aids for HV warning signs. Confirm comprehension from each technician verbally. Per CoCU C05 WA1.1, briefing content and procedure must be organised to ensure all team members receive complete assignment and safety information. = 5 marks

### Part B: Training Delivery — 25 marks (Scenarios 7–11, 5 marks each)

**Scenario 7** — TNA for New Technician Intake (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA3.1

Three new technicians hold G452-002-2 (Light Vehicle Repair) but have no BEV-specific training. Your workshop handles C01–C05 competency units. Before assigning live BEV work, you must identify their training needs systematically. Describe the complete TNA process including tools and forms used, aligned to CoCU C05 WA3.

*Sample Answer:* Create skills matrix: rows = technicians, columns = C01–C05 work activities. Mark current level (Not Yet Competent / Trained / Competent). Identify all NYC entries as training gaps. Complete TNA form: current competency vs required vs gap. Prioritise safety-critical units first (C01 HV safety). Select training type (internal OJT or external provider). Per CoCU C05 WA3.1, training needs must be listed in accordance with organisation requirements. = 5 marks

**Scenario 8** — Skills Matrix Gap Analysis (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA3.2

Your skills matrix reveals two technicians are Not Yet Competent in BMS rectification (C02) and one is NYC in thermal cooling (C03). Two of these technicians are assigned to customer jobs next month involving these systems. Describe how you address these competency gaps urgently while maintaining workshop operations and customer commitments.

*Sample Answer:* Immediately reassign next-month C02/C03 jobs to competent technicians. Pair NYC technicians as supervised assistants for OJT under competent senior techs. Contact approved training provider for earliest available C02/C03 course. Submit training nominations per CoCU C05 WA3.3 with minimum 2-week notification. Set 60-day competency closure target with bi-weekly progress checks. Document plan in TNA form and notify manager per WA3.2. = 5 marks

**Scenario 9** — Arranging External OBC Training (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA3.3

BYD Malaysia has released a certified OBC replacement course at its Training Centre KL — 3 days residential. Two of your technicians are eligible. Describe the complete training arrangement process per CoCU C05 WA3.3, from nomination to post-training documentation.

*Sample Answer:* Confirm training schedule and technician availability. Submit training nomination forms for both. Arrange transport and residential accommodation. Issue formal training notification minimum 2 weeks in advance. Prepare workshop coverage plan for their 3-day absence (reassign jobs or defer bookings). After training: collect OEM certificates, update skills matrix to Competent, file records per company SOP. Per CoCU C05 WA3.3, coaching materials, facilities, and training modules must be arranged per training needs. = 5 marks

**Scenario 10** — On-the-Job Coaching Delivery (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA3.3

Junior technician Farid completed classroom training for HV battery pack replacement (C02-W05) but has never performed it on a live vehicle. You have scheduled an OJT session on a workshop demonstration vehicle. Describe how you structure and conduct the session to maximise Farid's learning while maintaining HV safety throughout.

*Sample Answer:* Brief Farid fully on HV isolation procedure before any vehicle contact. Demonstrate each step once completely at full speed, then again with explanations at critical points (battery lift sequence, torque specifications, reconnection order). Have Farid perform under direct supervision. Use C02/KK-05 work sheet as assessment guide. Post-session: assess using observation checklist; record OJT completion in TNA form. Per CoCU C05 WA3.3, training module and materials must be arranged per training needs. = 5 marks

**Scenario 11** — Training Nomination After Performance Gap (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA2.3

Technician Syafiq consistently takes 40% longer than standard time on battery module balancing. His knowledge assessment score is adequate (75%), indicating the gap is skill-based. What improvement action do you recommend and how do you document and process the training nomination aligned to CoCU C05?

*Sample Answer:* Identify gap type: psychomotor (skill) deficiency, not cognitive. Recommend hands-on OJT refresher specifically for battery module balancing procedure. Complete performance evaluation form noting: "job time efficiency below standard — skill gap identified." Complete training nomination form for internal OJT. Set 30-day improvement target with bi-weekly check-ins. Report improvement plan to superior per CoCU C05 WA2.4 performance reporting requirements. = 5 marks

### Part C: SHE Monitoring — 20 marks (Scenarios 12–15, 5 marks each)

**Scenario 12** — PPE Non-Compliance During Walk-Through (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA4.2

During your Monday SHE walk-through, you observe technician Amin in Bay 3 working on an Ioniq 5 with HV battery cover removed, wearing only standard work gloves instead of mandatory Class "0" insulated gloves. The HV system has been isolated but not yet verified with a multimeter. What actions do you take immediately and what documentation is required per CoCU C05?

*Sample Answer:* Immediately instruct Amin to stop work and step away from the vehicle. Issue Class "0" insulated gloves; verify HV isolation using CAT-III multimeter before resuming. Conduct on-the-spot safety briefing. Record non-compliance on SHE checklist: date, bay, description, risk level (HIGH — direct HV contact risk). Submit SHE finding report to superior per CoCU C05 WA4.3. Per OSHA 1994 (Act 514), employer must ensure correct PPE is used by all workers. = 5 marks

**Scenario 13** — Reporting Unlabelled Chemical Waste (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA4.3

During SHE inspection, you find three unlabelled plastic containers near the coolant handling area. Two appear to contain spent dielectric coolant (cecair penyejuk dielektrik terpakai) and one may contain battery electrolyte residue. No waste manifest or hazard label is present. Describe your assessment and reporting actions per CoCU C05 and relevant Malaysian regulations.

*Sample Answer:* Immediately isolate and restrict access to the containers. Identify waste type using SDS. Label each container: waste type, date generated, hazardous classification. Record on SHE checklist: risk level HIGH (EQA 1974 Act 127 violation — unlabelled scheduled waste). Complete SHE finding report with photo, date, description, recommendation. Submit to superior and workshop manager per CoCU C05 WA4.3. Arrange disposal via licensed scheduled waste contractor. = 5 marks

**Scenario 14** — BEV-Specific Hazard: Missing HV Signage (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA4.1

Bay 5 had its HV warning signage removed during a recent repaint and has not been replaced. The bay is currently used for general service but may be assigned BEV HV work next week. How do you address this finding in your SHE checklist and what escalation actions are required per CoCU C05 WA4?

*Sample Answer:* Record on SHE checklist: "Bay 5 — HV warning signage absent." Mark bay restriction: DO NOT assign BEV HV work until signage reinstated and verified. Risk level: HIGH per CoCU C05 WA4.1 (SHE compliance requirements and EQA/OSHA obligations). Request immediate replacement from workshop admin; set same-day target date. Document in formal SHE finding report. Notify superior per WA4.3. Verify signage installed before assigning any HV work to Bay 5. = 5 marks

**Scenario 15** — Emergency Response: Arc Flash Incident (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA4.2

While supervising, you hear a loud bang from Bay 2. Technician Zulkifli has experienced an arc flash from an HV cable that was not fully isolated. He is conscious but shows burn marks on his right hand. The vehicle remains energised. Describe your immediate emergency response as BEV workshop supervisor per CoCU C05 SHE monitoring responsibilities.

*Sample Answer:* Do NOT touch Zulkifli or the energised vehicle. If still in contact with source, use insulated electric rescue hook to separate. Call for first aid; dial 999 for ambulance. Only when safe: isolate vehicle HV using insulated tools and full PPE (Class "0" gloves). Secure bay with barrier stanchions. Apply first aid for burns. Notify DOSH within 24 hours per OSHA 1994 (Act 514). Complete incident report per CoCU C05 WA4.3 and report to superior immediately. = 5 marks

### Part D: Team Coordination — 15 marks (Scenarios 16–18, 5 marks each)

**Scenario 16** — Job Distribution by Competency Level (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA2.2

Your five technicians: Ahmad (C01–C04 competent), Rashid (C01–C02 competent), Siti (C01 only), Farid (C01, OJT for C02), Mona (C01–C03 competent). Today's jobs: BMS rectification (C02), thermal cooling job (C03), scheduled maintenance (C01), and two traction motor replacements (C04). How do you allocate these five jobs per CoCU C05 WA2.2?

*Sample Answer:* Ahmad → C04 traction motor replacement #1 (fully competent, highest risk job). Rashid → BMS rectification C02 (within verified competency). Mona → thermal cooling C03 (within verified competency). Siti → scheduled maintenance C01 (within verified competency). Farid → assist Ahmad on C04 #2 as supervised OJT (extends CoCU C05 WA3.3 training plan). Update job control board with all assignments. Per WA2.2, subordinate competency must be evaluated before assignment. = 5 marks

**Scenario 17** — Addressing Repeated Performance Issue (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA2.3

Technician Boey has been late three times in two weeks. Two informal verbal reminders have already been given. Company policy: verbal warning → written warning → suspension. Today he arrived 25 minutes late with no explanation. Describe what action you take as supervisor and how you document it in accordance with CoCU C05 WA2 requirements.

*Sample Answer:* Conduct formal counselling session: present dated attendance records; state policy consequences clearly. Issue written warning (informal verbal warnings already given). Complete counselling form: date, attendance record, discussion summary, agreed improvement actions, target timeline, technician signature. Forward to HR per CoCU C05 WA2.4 reporting requirements. Set 2-week monitoring period with documented check-in dates. Note: next offence escalates to suspension per company disciplinary policy. = 5 marks

**Scenario 18** — Team Response to Customer Complaint (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA2.1

A formal customer complaint was submitted: an Ioniq 5 was returned with a greasy handprint on the white interior seat because the technician did not use seat protectors. The responsible technician has been identified. Describe how you address this as supervisor to prevent recurrence while maintaining team morale and protecting the workshop's service reputation.

*Sample Answer:* Address at next morning briefing without naming the individual — use incident as a team teaching moment. Demonstrate correct use of seat, steering wheel, and floor protectors. Separately, counsel the responsible technician privately: complete counselling form per CoCU C05 WA2.3. Implement mandatory vehicle protection checklist as a job card requirement for all jobs. Report service quality incident to superior per WA2.4. Follow up to confirm customer apology and satisfaction response. = 5 marks

### Part E: Documentation — 10 marks (Scenarios 19–20, 5 marks each)

**Scenario 19** — Daily Briefing Feedback Report (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA1.3

At end of day you must complete the daily briefing feedback/response report per CoCU C05 WA1.3. Key events today: one technician asked about BMS fault code interpretation; two jobs completed ahead of schedule; one vehicle requires parts ordering; all technicians acknowledged the toolbox talk on coolant handling. What must this report include and how is it filed?

*Sample Answer:* Report fields per CoCU C05 WA1.3: date, briefing topics delivered, attendance (names + signatures), technician questions raised and supervisor responses, job completion status (ahead/on time/delayed with vehicle registration), parts orders raised (part number, supplier, expected delivery), toolbox talk acknowledgement with technician signatures, outstanding follow-up actions with responsible person and target date. File in daily briefing folder per company SOP within 24 hours. = 5 marks

**Scenario 20** — SHE Finding Report with Risk Assessment (5 marks) — CoCU C05 WA4.3

Complete a formal SHE finding report for three this-week findings: (1) expired fire extinguisher in Bay 3, (2) cracked Class "0" insulated glove in PPE cabinet, (3) blocked emergency exit near coolant storage. The report must include risk assessment per CoCU C05 WA4.3. Provide the report framework including risk levels and recommended actions.

*Sample Answer:* Report format per CoCU C05 WA4.3 — columns: No. | Finding | Location | Date | Photo Ref | Risk Level | Recommendation | Target Date | Status. Finding 1: Expired extinguisher — HIGH risk (safety equipment failure during fire event); replace immediately; schedule quarterly inspection. Finding 2: Cracked insulated glove — HIGH risk (HV contact risk if used); remove from service immediately; replenish PPE stock. Finding 3: Blocked emergency exit — HIGH risk (OSHA 1994 Act 514 evacuation violation); clear obstruction same day. Submit report to superior same day. = 5 marks
