## Work Activity 3: Coordinate BEV Subordinate's Training | **Reference:** C05/KP(3/4)

### S1 — Short Answer: a) What is TNI (Training Needs Identification)? b) What is TNA (Training Needs Analysis)? Explain how they differ.
### S2 — Short Answer: Create a simple skills matrix for 3 technicians and 4 BEV competencies. Identify which technician needs what training.
### S3 — MCQ: Which is NOT a source for BEV training? A. JPK-accredited centres B. Manufacturer training academies C. Social media influencers D. Industry associations
### S4 — T/F: a) A skills chart maps each technician against required competencies. b) Training notification should be given on the day of training. c) Post-training evaluation includes checking if the technician can now perform the skill.
### S5 — Short Answer: List FIVE (5) training logistics items that must be arranged when coordinating subordinate training.

### S6 — Case Study: After sending Technician Kumar to a 3-day HV battery diagnostic course, his supervisor notices that Kumar still asks colleagues for help when using the new diagnostic tool two months later. (a) At which Kirkpatrick evaluation level did the training fail? (b) State TWO corrective actions the supervisor should take.

## Skema Jawapan
**S1:** a) TNI: identifying what training is needed based on skill gaps, business needs, new technology. b) TNA: analysing the gap between current and required competency levels, then prioritising and selecting appropriate training interventions. TNI identifies needs; TNA analyses and prioritises them.
**S2:** Example matrix with Y/N entries showing skill gaps; training recommended where N. (Accept any reasonable matrix.)
**S3:** C
**S4:** a) TRUE b) FALSE — minimum 2 weeks advance notice c) TRUE
**S5:** Training location, training provider, training notification (2 weeks), work coverage, materials and facilities.
**S6:** a) Level 3 — Behaviour (the technician learned in training but is not applying the skills on the job). b) Any two: arrange supervised OJT sessions with the diagnostic tool; pair Kumar with a senior technician as mentor; check if Kumar needs additional practice time with the equipment; conduct a Level 2 re-assessment to verify actual skill level; set a SMART target for independent tool use within 30 days.
