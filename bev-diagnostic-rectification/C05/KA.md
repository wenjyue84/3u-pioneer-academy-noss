## SECTION C: Structured (40 marks)

**C1.** (20 marks) You are a BEV workshop supervisor with 5 technicians. One technician has been consistently slow to complete jobs and has been observed skipping the insulated glove air test. a) How would you evaluate this technician's performance? (5 marks) b) What improvement actions would you take? (8 marks) c) How would you document and report this? (7 marks)

**C2.** (20 marks) During your weekly SHE walk-through, you find: (i) a fire extinguisher past its service date, (ii) HV warning signage missing from one bay, (iii) spent coolant container without a label. a) Assess the risk level of each finding. (6 marks) b) Write a SHE finding report for these three items. (8 marks) c) What corrective actions would you recommend? (6 marks)

## MARKING SCHEME

**Section A:** A1:B A2:D A3:B A4:B A5:C A6:C A7:A A8:B A9:B A10:B

**B1:** Prepare agenda, gather team, take attendance, deliver briefing (safety, progress, assignments, updates, housekeeping, Q&A), update job control board, file notes, document concerns.

**B2:** Attitude — follows HV safety without prompting. Skills — performs HV de-energisation correctly. Knowledge — interprets fault codes accurately. Productivity — completes standard jobs within time targets.

**B3:** Create skills matrix (technicians vs competencies), identify gaps (N entries), complete TNA form (current vs required vs gap), prioritise (safety-critical first), select training program, schedule.

**B4:** Do's: de-energise HV, verify zero energy, wear PPE, use insulated tools, two-person rule. Don'ts: assume HV off, wear jewellery, skip discharge wait, use damaged PPE, work alone on HV.

**C1:** a) Complete evaluation form rating attitude (poor — skipping safety) and productivity (slow). b) Immediate counselling on safety violation. OJT on correct glove test procedure. Set performance improvement targets with timeline. Monitor closely. c) Complete counselling form documenting the conversation and agreed actions. Update evaluation form. If no improvement, escalate via performance report to superior.

**C2:** a) Fire extinguisher: HIGH (critical safety equipment non-functional). HV signage missing: HIGH (risk of unauthorised HV area entry). Unlabeled coolant: MEDIUM (environmental non-compliance). b) Report with: date, location, description, photo, risk rating, recommendation for each. c) Fire extinguisher: immediate replacement/service. Signage: install replacement immediately. Coolant: label container immediately and retrain staff on waste procedures.
