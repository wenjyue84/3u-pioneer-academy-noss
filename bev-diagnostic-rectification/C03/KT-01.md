b) R1234yf refrigerant is mildly flammable (A2L classification).

a) List FIVE (5) specific hazards in thermal cooling system rectification work.
b) Describe the waste management requirements for refrigerant and coolant.

### S4 — Short Answer

What is the waste classification code for spent HV battery coolant?

---

## Skema Jawapan

**S1:** B **S2:** a) FALSE — chemical-resistant gloves required b) TRUE c) FALSE — classified as SW305 scheduled waste **S3:** a) Electrical (HV pumps/compressor), chemical (coolant/refrigerant), pressure (pressurised circuits), thermal (hot coolant), environmental (refrigerant GWP). b) Coolant: SW305, sealed containers, licensed disposal. Refrigerant: must be recovered using recycling machine, certified facility disposal. **S4:** Manpower arrangement, equipment/tool verification, toolbox talk, emergency route confirmation, waste management area setup. **S5:** B
