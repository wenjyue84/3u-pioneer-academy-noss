## Soalan / Questions

### S1 — Short Answer
a) Compare cabin heating in ICE vehicles versus BEVs. Why is heating a significant challenge for BEVs?
b) Explain the function of a PTC heater. What does "Positive Temperature Coefficient" mean?

### S2 — Multiple Choice
What classification is R1234yf refrigerant?
A. Non-flammable (A1) B. Mildly flammable (A2L) C. Highly flammable (A3) D. Toxic (B1)

### S3 — Matching
Match each HVAC component with its function:

| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| 1. Electric compressor | a. Directs coolant flow between circuits |
| 2. PTC heater | b. Absorbs heat from cabin air |
| 3. 3-way valve | c. Compresses refrigerant for cooling |
| 4. Evaporator | d. Provides cabin heating via electrical resistance |
| 5. Condenser | e. Rejects heat to ambient air |

### S4 — True/False
a) R1234yf and R134a refrigerants can be mixed.
b) The HVAC system must be evacuated (vacuum) before recharging with refrigerant.
c) Cabin vent temperature during maximum cooling should reach 5-10 deg C within 5 minutes.
d) The electric compressor operates at 12V.

### S5 — Short Answer
Describe the complete HVAC system functionality test procedure. Include tests for both cooling and heating modes.

### S6 — Short Answer
What precautions must be taken when replacing HVAC refrigerant line components? List at least FOUR (4).

---

## Skema Jawapan

**S1:** a) ICE: uses engine waste heat (free). BEV: no engine heat; must use PTC heater or heat pump, consuming battery energy (3-8 kW), significantly reducing driving range in cold weather. b) PTC = resistance increases as temperature rises, automatically limiting current and preventing overheating. Self-regulating electric heater.
**S2:** B
**S3:** 1-c, 2-d, 3-a, 4-b, 5-e
**S4:** a) FALSE — never mix b) TRUE c) TRUE d) FALSE — HV-powered
**S5:** Start vehicle READY. Maximum cooling: verify compressor engages, measure vent temp (5-10 deg C), check refrigerant pressures. Maximum heating: verify PTC heater activates, measure vent temp (>40 deg C), verify water pump and 3-way valve. Check for leaks and fault codes. Record all results.
**S6:** Replace O-rings and seals at every reconnection. Apply refrigerant-compatible oil to new O-rings. Evacuate system to min 29 inHg vacuum for 30+ minutes. Never mix R1234yf and R134a. Verify correct refrigerant type and charge volume before recharging.
