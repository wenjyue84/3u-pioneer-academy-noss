## Soalan / Questions

### S1 — Short Answer
a) Name FOUR (4) separate cooling circuits typically found in a BEV and state what each cools.
b) Explain the advantages of electric water pumps over mechanical (belt-driven) water pumps.

### S2 — Multiple Choice
What is the typical pressure test value for a BEV cooling circuit leak test?
A. 0.1-0.3 bar B. 1.0-1.5 bar C. 5.0-7.0 bar D. 15-20 bar

### S3 — True/False
a) BEV battery cooling maintains battery temperature between 15-35 deg C.
b) Coolant that appears brown with particulate matter is in good condition.
c) Cavitation noise from a water pump indicates possible clogging in the cooling circuit.

### S4 — Short Answer
Describe the THREE (3) types of cooling system inspection: leakage, corrosion, and clogging. Explain the method and indicators for each.

### S5 — Short Answer
Describe the general procedure for replacing a faulty cooling system component. Include coolant management steps.

### S6 — Multiple Choice
Which diagnostic tool function is used to verify water pump operation without driving the vehicle?
A. Fault code reading B. Freeze frame data C. Active test D. VIN decode

---

## Skema Jawapan

**S1:** a) Battery cooling (15-35 deg C), powertrain cooling (motor/inverter, up to 65 deg C), cabin cooling/heating, charger cooling (OBC during charging). b) Independent of motor speed, operate when vehicle is stationary (during charging), variable flow rate controlled by ECU.
**S2:** B
**S3:** a) TRUE b) FALSE — indicates degraded coolant c) TRUE
**S4:** Leakage: visual inspection + pressure tester (1.0-1.5 bar, observe pressure decay). Corrosion: check coolant colour/pH, inspect metal components for deposits. Clogging: high temperatures, reduced flow rate, pump cavitation noise.
**S5:** De-energise HV if component is HV-connected. Drain coolant. Disconnect electrical/hose connections. Remove component. Install new component. Reconnect. Refill coolant and bleed air. Test functionality.
**S6:** C
