## MARKING SCHEME

**Section A:** A1:C A2:B A3:B A4:B A5:B A6:C A7:C A8:B A9:B A10:B

**B1:** ICE compressor belt-driven vs BEV electric (HV); ICE uses engine waste heat vs BEV uses PTC/heat pump; ICE cooling depends on RPM vs BEV independent; BEV heating consumes 3-8 kW from battery reducing range.

**B2:** Leakage: visual + pressure test (1.0-1.5 bar, 10 min). Corrosion: coolant colour/pH check, metal component inspection. Clogging: temperature analysis, flow rate check, pump cavitation noise.

**B3:** Recover refrigerant with recycling machine. De-energise HV if needed. Remove component. Replace O-rings, apply oil. Install new component. Evacuate 29 inHg for 30 min. Recharge correct type/volume. Test.

**B4:** a) Circulates coolant independently of motor speed b) Electric resistance heater, self-regulating c) Directs coolant between circuits d) Compresses refrigerant (HV-powered) e) Absorbs heat from cabin air

**C1:** Diagnostic: read fault codes, check refrigerant pressures, active test compressor, measure vent temp, leak test. Possible causes: low refrigerant (leak), compressor fault, blocked condenser, faulty expansion valve, blower fault. Repair per cause. Recharge if needed. Functionality test: max cooling, 5-10 deg C vent temp, no leaks, no codes.

**C2:** a) Causes: water pump failure, clogged coolant passages, coolant leak (low level), thermostat valve fault, degraded coolant, blocked heat exchanger, fan failure. b) Diagnostic scan, active test pumps, pressure test, visual inspection. Replace faulty component. Refill coolant, bleed, test temperatures under load.
