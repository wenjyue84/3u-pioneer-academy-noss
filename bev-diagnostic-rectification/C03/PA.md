# PENILAIAN PRESTASI / PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT

**Kod WIM / WIM Code:** G452-010-3:2023-C03/PA
**Kertas Warna / Paper Color:** BIRU MUDA / LIGHT BLUE

---

## Competency Unit: Perform BEV Thermal Cooling System Rectification

| Duration | 2 hours 30 minutes | Total Marks | 100 | Passing | 60% |

---

## TASK 1: Workplace Preparation (15 marks) — 20 min
1. Prepare thermal cooling rectification workplace (5 marks)
2. Set up coolant handling area with spill containment (5 marks)
3. Verify refrigerant recycling machine readiness (5 marks)

## TASK 2: Liquid Cooling System Servicing (40 marks) — 60 min
1. Diagnostic inspection: fault codes, pump active test (8 marks)
2. Visual and pressure leak test (8 marks)
3. Coolant condition check (colour, pH, contamination) (4 marks)
4. Component replacement demonstration (10 marks)
5. Coolant refill, bleed, and functionality test (10 marks)

## TASK 3: HVAC System Servicing (45 marks) — 70 min
1. HVAC diagnostic inspection (8 marks)
2. Refrigerant recovery using recycling machine (7 marks)
3. Component replacement with O-ring and oil application (10 marks)
4. System evacuation (29 inHg, 30 min) and recharge (10 marks)
5. Functionality test: cooling (<10 deg C) and heating (>40 deg C) (10 marks)

---

## AUTOMATIC FAILURE: Venting refrigerant to atmosphere. Contact with energised HV component. Operating without PPE.
