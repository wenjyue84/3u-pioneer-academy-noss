# PELAN MENGAJAR TEORI / THEORY LESSON PLAN

**Kod WIM / WIM Code:** G452-010-3:2023-C03/PM(Teori)
**Kertas Warna / Paper Color:** KUNING / YELLOW

---

## Competency Unit

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| CU Code | G452-010-3:2023-C03 |
| CU Title | Perform BEV Thermal Cooling System Rectification |
| CU Weight | 20% |

## Learning Outcomes
1. Explain thermal cooling system workplace preparation, safety, and hazard identification
2. Describe BEV liquid cooling system components, inspection, and servicing procedures
3. Explain BEV HVAC system fundamentals including PTC heater, electric water pump, and 3-way valve

## Reference Documents
| KP 1 | C03/KP(1/3) | Thermal Cooling System Rectification Workplace Preparation |
| KP 2 | C03/KP(2/3) | BEV Liquid Cooling System |
| KP 3 | C03/KP(3/3) | BEV HVAC System |

---

## 4-Step Method

### Langkah 1: PERSEDIAAN (15 min)
- Introduce CU: thermal management is critical to BEV performance and battery longevity
- Without proper cooling, battery capacity degrades, motor efficiency drops, and safety risks increase

### Langkah 2: PENYAMPAIAN (120 min)
| 30 min | KP(1/3) | Workplace preparation: regulations, safety, tools, hazards, waste management |
| 45 min | KP(2/3) | Liquid cooling: electric water pumps, leakage/corrosion/clogging inspection, component replacement |
| 45 min | KP(3/3) | HVAC: PTC heater, electric water pump, 3-way valve, system diagnosis and servicing |

### Langkah 3: PENGGUNAAN (45 min)
| 15 min each | KT(1/3), KT(2/3), KT(3/3) | Assignment exercises |

### Langkah 4: PENGESAHAN (15 min)
- Review, Q&A, preview practical sessions

## Total: approximately 195 minutes
