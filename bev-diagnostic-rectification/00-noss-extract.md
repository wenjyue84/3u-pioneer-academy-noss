**CU Code:** G452-010-3:2023-C01
**Learning Outcomes:** Enable trainees to deliver accurate information regarding statutory law and regulation, safety and health compliance and provide adequate requirement for workshop to perform scheduled maintenance.

#### WA 1: Carry out BEV schedule maintenance workplace preparation

**Related Knowledge:**
- 1.1 BEV rules and regulations: Licensing Legislative, Regulatory requirement, Certification requirement
- 1.2 BEV schedule maintenance safety, health and environment guidelines: Provision of a safe and conducive workplace (anti-static flooring, ergonomic), Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- 1.3 Overview of environmental impact in BEV industry: Guidelines in scheduled waste handling, Guidelines in prescribed activities (environmental impact)
- 1.4 Maintenance tools and equipment: Insulated Tools, Common tools, Equipment, Dedicated BEV work bay which includes anti-static flooring
- 1.5 Hazard Identification in workshop environment: Definition of hazard, Type of hazard, Threat, Control measure
- 1.6 Procedure of schedule waste management: Type of waste, Temporary waste disposal area
- 1.7 Procedure for Scheduled maintenance preparation: Equipment list layout, Tool list location, Toolbox talk, Assembly point/emergency route escape

**Related Skills:**
- 1.1 Identify licensing legislative, regulatory or certification requirement
- 1.2 Identify BEV schedule maintenance safety & health requirement
- 1.3 Identify BEV schedule maintenance tools and equipment
- 1.4 Identify scheduled maintenance job hazard in relation to working within a workshop environment
- 1.5 Identify scheduled waste management procedure
- 1.6 Arrange BEV scheduled maintenance workplace preparation

**Attitude/Safety/Environment:**
- ATTITUDE: 1.1 Responsible in handling task assigned; 1.2 Systematic in organising works; 1.3 Time, cost and effort conscious
- SAFETY: 1.1 Compliance with safety and health guidelines related to the provision of a safe, conducive workplace and ergonomic practice; 1.2 Ensure use of appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE)
- ENVIRONMENT: 1.1 Compliance with environment regulation on schedule waste handling; 1.2 Compliance with environment regulation on prescribed activities

**Assessment Criteria:**
- COGNITIVE: 1.1 BEV rules and regulation explained; 1.2 BEV schedule maintenance safety, health and environment explained; 1.3 BEV schedule maintenance tools and equipment listed; 1.4 BEV schedule maintenance hazard identification described; 1.5 Procedure of schedule waste management explained; 1.6 Procedure for scheduled maintenance preparation described
- PSYCHOMOTOR: 1.1 Licensing, legislative, regulatory or certification requirement determined in accordance with regulatory authority; 1.2 BEV workplace safety & health applied in accordance with manufacturer and company safety & health guideline; 1.3 BEV scheduled maintenance tools and equipment requirement prepared in accordance with manufacturer's service manual; 1.4 BEV schedule maintenance job hazard in relation to working in workshop environment determined in accordance with company JHA form; 1.5 BEV scheduled maintenance waste management procedure applied in accordance with company waste management guideline; 1.6 BEV scheduled maintenance workplace preparation prepared in accordance with company standard operating procedure
- AFFECTIVE: 1.1 Responsibility in handling task assigned demonstrated; 1.2 Systematic in organising works demonstrated; 1.3 Time, cost and effort conscious demonstrated; 1.4-1.7 Safety, PPE, environment compliance adhered to

#### WA 2: Carry out BEV health check activities

**Related Knowledge:**
- 2.1 BEV health check safety: Safety equipment inside the vehicle, Type of PPE and specification, Condition of BEV equipment or parts
- 2.2 BEV health check tools and equipment
- 2.3 Procedure for BEV component active test: Checklist content, Testing procedure, Component condition
- 2.4 Diagnostic test: Function and operating of diagnostic system, Type and category of fault code, Interpret fault code
- 2.5 Procedure for health check result report

**Related Skills:**
- 2.1 Identify BEV health check safety requirement
- 2.2 Identify BEV health check tools and equipment
- 2.3 Execute BEV components active test
- 2.4 Identify diagnostic fault code
- 2.5 Prepare BEV health check report

**Attitude/Safety/Environment:**
- ATTITUDE: 2.1 Details and meticulous in performing health check; 2.2 Handle customer vehicle with care; 2.3 Alert on surrounding while carrying out vehicle health check
- SAFETY: 2.1 Compliance with safety and health guidelines; 2.2 Ensure use of appropriate PPE
- ENVIRONMENT: 2.1 Compliance with environment regulation on schedule waste handling; 2.2 Compliance with environment regulation on prescribed activities

**Assessment Criteria:**
- COGNITIVE: 2.1 BEV health check safety practice explained; 2.2 BEV health check tools and equipment listed; 2.3 Procedure for BEV component active test explained; 2.4 BEV health check diagnostic test procedure explained; 2.5 Procedure for health check report described
- PSYCHOMOTOR: 2.1-2.5 Per performance criteria with manufacturer's service manual
- AFFECTIVE: 2.1-2.7 Attitude, safety, environment compliance demonstrated

#### WA 3: Carry out BEV maintenance activities

**Related Knowledge:**

### Element Content Weightage Notes

- **OSH:** All CUs are rated evenly (20% each) for safety aspect. Safety, health and environment elements are crucial in handling high voltage and battery management to ensure work can be conducted in a safe manner.
- **SD:** The highest CUs for SD element are C02, C03 and C04. BEV is a developing technology which will expect more improvement in terms of battery technology. The lowest is C05 as it emphasizes supervisory related work.
